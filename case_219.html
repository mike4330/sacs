Former Earth science teacher and softball coach at Owen J. Roberts High School, South Coventry Township, Chester County, Pennsylvania. Previously taught in West Philadelphia and Miami. Female defendant, age 38 at arrest. One male student victim, age 16.

Bonkoski had taught the victim during his freshman year. She began offering him rides home from work and initiated contact outside school. She purchased a burner phone specifically to communicate with the victim, believing it could not be traced. Sexual contact began December 2016 at her Pottstown home; ended January 2017 when the victim cut off contact. After he went silent, Bonkoski sent repeated desperate texts: 'Whatever you do, please don't shut me out. Not now. I'm begging you.' In interviews she admitted to 'bombarding' him with messages.

Investigation began when the victim, upset about cheating on his girlfriend, disclosed the relationship; the girlfriend discovered suspicious texts. Bonkoski was initially arrested August 16, 2017, on charges that reflected uncertainty about the extent of the relationship. She re-arrested August 29, 2017, after prosecutors received confirmation from the victim that the relationship included sex. She admitted to intercourse in a second interview.

Pleaded guilty February 26, 2018, before Judge Ann Marie Wheatcraft. Sentenced May 2, 2018: - Count 1 (Intercourse/Sexual Contact with Student, 18 Pa. §3124.2): 11 months 15 days to 23 months confinement; work-release eligible at warden's discretion after 4 months; Megan's Law Tier 2 registration; surrender teaching certificate; forfeiture of seized electronics - Count 6 (Unlawful Contact with Minor, 18 Pa. §6318): 5 years probation, consecutive to Count 1 - Count 11 (Corruption of Minors, 18 Pa. §6301): 5 years probation, concurrent with Count 6

NOTE: Burner phone tactic is documented and explicit in charging materials. Chester County DA Tom Hogan quoted publicly both at arrest and re-arrest. Two-stage arrest (initial charge upgraded after victim confirmation) is structurally notable.
