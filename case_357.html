Former teacher at Fulton County High School, Fulton County, Kentucky. Victim was a 17-year-old male student. Age 25 at time of offense. Netz lived in Union City, Tennessee.

Kentucky State Police received a complaint of inappropriate behavior on January 25, 2017. Netz was arrested in Union City, Tennessee, and extradited to Kentucky on February 3, 2017. Originally charged with felony third-degree rape.

The charge was reduced to sexual misconduct with a minor (misdemeanor). Sentenced in Fulton County District Court to 360 days in jail with 350 days suspended on conditional discharge for two years, or alternatively 30 days on an ankle monitor. Netz chose the ankle monitor. She was ordered to have no contact with the victim or his family, not to enter any Fulton County Schools property, and to provide written proof of sex offender counseling progress at a December 12 review.
