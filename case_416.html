Tara Yvonne Stumph #416 sentenced

State California
Court San Luis Obispo County Superior Court
Incident Date 2014-12-01
Defendant Age 34
Victim Age 16
Prison Sentence 0.5 yrs

Former culinary arts teacher at Arroyo Grande High School, Arroyo Grande (San Luis Obispo County), California. Age 34 at time of offense; married to a firefighter; mother of three. Five-year employee of Lucia Mar Unified School District.

One male victim, age 16. Sexual acts occurred December 2014 through June 2015, including during and after class and off-campus. Stumph also sent nude selfies and sexually explicit videos of herself to the victim. School district was notified in December 2015 and immediately placed Stumph on administrative leave; she was no longer employed by the district by August 17, 2016 (resigned or fired).

Charged August 16, 2016 with two felony counts (oral copulation with a person under 18; sexual intercourse with a minor) and three misdemeanor counts of child molestation. Arraigned August 29, 2016; pled not guilty. Pled no contest April 6, 2017 to felony unlawful sex with a minor more than three years younger (Cal. Penal Code § 261.5(c)). Remaining charges dismissed. Sentenced to 180 days San Luis Obispo County Jail (commenced May 1, 2017) and 4 years felony probation including sex offender counseling program; no contact with minors. Teaching license revoked. No sex offender registration required.

The victim's family filed a civil lawsuit against Stumph, the school district, and her former principal alleging she cultivated the victim's trust over months and exploited it. Stumph counter-sued the victim claiming his statements to classmates, family, and the community defamed her and damaged her reputation and career.

NOTE: No sex offender registration is a notable gap given the conduct. Counter-suit against the victim is unusual and drew press attention. Probation office noted low re-offense risk score and three young children in recommending the lenient sentence. Defense attorney argued jail time was not appropriate.
