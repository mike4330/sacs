Former teacher at South Fremont High School, St. Anthony, Idaho (employed August 2021–June 2023). No longer employed at the school at time of offense. Victim was a 16-year-old male who had been adopted out of the foster system just days before the assault.

On the night of November 6, 2023, Saint Anthony Police pulled over Lawson's vehicle and found the teen driving; Lawson was too drunk to drive. The teen admitted to using marijuana provided by Lawson. He was driven home and subsequently told his parents that Lawson had picked him up, that they had smoked and drunk alcohol together, and that they had sex. Lawson called the parents afterward and admitted to giving the teen alcohol but denied the sexual conduct. Arrested later that week.

Originally charged with felony sexual battery, felony rape of a minor, felony delivery of a controlled substance, and misdemeanor dispensing alcohol to a minor. Accepted a plea deal to felony sexual battery and felony rape of a minor; drug and alcohol charges dropped. Sentenced September 30, 2024 by Senior District Judge Stephen Dunn.

Sentence: 2–20 years (Idaho indeterminate; 2-year fixed minimum), sex offender registration and treatment, 20-year no-contact order with victim. Defendant was 36 at sentencing; estimated 35 at time of offense.
