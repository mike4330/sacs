Kristen Gantt #57 sentenced

State Iowa
Incident Date 2023-02-01
Defendant Age 36
Victim Age 17
Prison Sentence 2.0 yrs

Former English teacher at Dowling Catholic High School, West Des Moines, Iowa. Victim: 17-year-old male student with a difficult home life who confided in Gantt. Contact began Dec 2022 via personal email, Dowling-issued email, and Instagram. Gantt would send messages of encouragement that escalated into concerning exchanges across multiple platforms. Physical intimacy on at least 5 occasions Feb-March 2023, all in Gantt's classroom after school hours. Evidence includes: shirtless photo from student asking 'What do you think about this?' with Gantt replying 'I think you know'; messages scheduling outside-school meetings (yoga; Top Gun Maverick, canceled due to weather); Gantt messaging victim 'you look annoyingly good today.' Anonymous tip led Dowling to place Gantt on administrative leave; school contacted West Des Moines PD. Search warrant yielded journals, computers, Fitbit, Kindle, letters, Dowling desktop and laptop. Arrested April 14, 2023 (turned herself in); charged with felony sexual exploitation by school employee + aggravated misdemeanor sexual contact by school employee. Pled guilty Feb 8, 2024 to one count (aggravated misdemeanor). Sentenced Feb 28, 2024: 2 years prison; no-contact order until Feb 28, 2029; sex offender registration; DNA sample. NOTE: Charge reduced from felony to aggravated misdemeanor at plea — a significant downgrade. Gantt exploited a pastoral role: victim had a hard home life and came to her for support. Pattern of dual-channel communication (official school email alongside personal/social media) is a documented evasion tactic — using the official channel for plausible deniability while escalating on personal channels. Anonymous tip as the discovery mechanism; school acted quickly.
