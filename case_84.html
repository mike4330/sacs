Lillie Bowman #84 sentenced

Judge Andrew Jacobsen
Incident Date 2023-03
Defendant Age 23
Victim Age 17
Prison Sentence 0.0 yrs

Former 9th/10th-grade English teacher and assistant trap shooting coach at Norris High School, Lancaster County, Nebraska. Age 23 at time of offenses. Victim: 17-year-old male student-athlete.

Relationship developed March 2023; sexual encounters occurred approximately 10 times at Bowman's apartment in Lincoln. Student rumors reached a school administrator in early April 2023. Both Bowman and the student denied the relationship; no further action was taken by the school. A parent of another student later reported to the Lancaster County Sheriff's Office.

On May 14, 2023, deputies received the report. By May 18, investigators had obtained phone records showing 306 calls between Bowman and the student between April 1 and May. On May 19, a search of her apartment found open and used condom wrappers. Both Bowman and the student admitted to the relationship and to approximately 10 encounters. Arrested May 2023; bond $5,000 (10% of $50,000). Fired following arrest.

Initial charge: first-degree sexual abuse by a school employee (Class IIA felony, up to 20 years). Pleaded no contest January 31, 2024 to the reduced charge of third-degree sexual abuse by a school employee (Class IV felony, up to 2 years). Sentenced March 29, 2024 by Lancaster County Judge Andrew Jacobsen: 5 years probation, plus 90 days jail served in 30-day annual installments (April 5 in 2024, 2025, and 2026). Sex offender registration required. Prosecuted by Deputy Lancaster County Attorney Morgan Smith.

NOTE: The school administrator's failure to escalate after receiving rumors — then being given a denial — is a documented prior-warning-ignored instance; a parent had to go directly to law enforcement. The 306 documented phone calls are a rare quantitative record of contact frequency. The intermittent 30-day annual jail structure is unusual in this DB. Charge reduction from Class IIA (20-year max) to Class IV (2-year max) with no active prison time continues a plains-state lenient pattern; cf. Gantt #57.
