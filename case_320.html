Former long-term substitute teacher at Springfield School District 186, Springfield, Illinois (Sangamon County). Age 24 at time of offense and arrest. A 2011 graduate of Springfield High School (basketball/volleyball); played basketball at Lincoln Land Community College and SIU-Edwardsville (2011-12 season).

Victim: male juvenile (age not stated in available sources; identified in court records as B.A.).

Arrested May 21, 2018 after Springfield Police received a complaint of inappropriate contact with a juvenile male student in the district. Offense date per complaint: May 9, 2018. Charged May 24, 2018 with criminal sexual assault (Class 1 felony, Count I), aggravated criminal sexual abuse (Class 2 felony, Count II), and contributing to the delinquency of a minor (Class A misdemeanor, Count III). Grand jury indictment June 13, 2018. The case underwent repeated continuances over nearly five years; a fitness evaluation was ordered January 2021 and Korza was held in custody for approximately 65 days in early 2021 before bond was converted to personal recognizance.

Entered a plea of guilty but mentally ill to Count II (aggravated criminal sexual abuse, 720 ILCS 5/11-1.60(d)) on March 3, 2023 before Judge Raylene Grischow; Counts I and III dismissed pursuant to plea. Sentenced to 130 days in Sangamon County Jail with credit for 65 days served; 30 months probation; no contact with victim B.A.; lifetime registration as a sexual predator; fines and costs of $2,139. Probation discharged September 2, 2025. Prosecutor Kendra Hansel (ASA); defense attorney William Reid.
