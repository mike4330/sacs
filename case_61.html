Former special education teacher at Centennial High School, Corona CA (Corona Norco Unified School District). Age 31 at arrest. Five underage male victims ages 14-17; none were her special ed students — she met them at Saturday school, using the program as access to students outside her class. Incidents: May 2012 through May 2013. Locations: her classroom, a utility/storage room at the school, her vehicle parked near a victim's home, and at another victim's home. Also sent nude photos of herself to students. School district notified Corona Police; arrested June 20, 2013. Charged with 16 felony counts: unlawful sexual intercourse with a minor, unlawful oral copulation with a minor, sending harmful matter to a minor. Pled guilty ~March 2015 to all 16 counts. Sentenced April 3, 2015 by Riverside County Superior Court Judge Becky Dugan: 3 years county jail (faced up to 13 years state prison). Prosecutors and victim families opposed the sentence. Victim's mother: 'Three years is a slap on the wrist for this sexual predator. It is a slap in the face for the victims and their families.' Hansen apologized in court: 'I'm not a monster. I made bad choices in life.' Judge Dugan: 'Not just one kid, but multiple kids. That's a plan. You're making a plan and continue to do it. I think you are sorry for the last two years, but frankly you're just sorry that you got caught.' NOTE: Saturday school as an access mechanism is notable — she identified students in a structured but lower-supervision setting outside her classroom and used that contact to develop relationships. Judge Dugan's 'that's a plan' framing is one of the clearest judicial articulations of predatory pattern vs. impulse in this DB. 16 felony counts / 5 victims / 3 years county jail is among the more lenient per-count outcomes recorded. Hansen's tearful courtroom apology contrasts sharply with Dugan's assessment of it.
