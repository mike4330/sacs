Incident Date 2015-06-01

Victim Age 18

23-year veteran teacher and 2005 Gaston County Teacher of the Year at South Point High School, Gaston County, North Carolina. Married with two children in the school system at time of offense. Age 46 at sentencing; estimated 45 at time of offense.

Had sexual contact with an 18-year-old male recent graduate of South Point High. Dispute over timing: Helton claimed contact occurred after graduation; victim stated it occurred while he was still enrolled. Text messages between the two prior to graduation were deemed improper by prosecutors but insufficient to establish when the physical encounter took place. Victim's mother, who was approximately one year older than the defendant, learned of the relationship from a family friend and tipped off authorities.

Deferred sentencing arrangement reached March 8, 2016. DA Locke Bell cited the evidentiary difficulty — a jury unable to determine the date would have resulted in acquittal and no punishment. Helton surrendered her teaching license and did not make a statement in court. Her attorney acknowledged she knew what she did was wrong.

Sentence: 2-year probation, 200 community service hours, surrender of teaching license. Record to be wiped upon completion. No prison time.

NOTE: Adult victim (18). Outcome is a deferred arrangement rather than a conviction — status set to closed. DA explicitly stated the arrangement was preferable to risking a full acquittal.
