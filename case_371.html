State Florida
Court Clay County Circuit Court
Incident Date 2014-01-06
Defendant Age 40
Victim Age 16
Prison Sentence 1.0 yr

Former 2nd grade teacher at W.E. Cherry Elementary School, Orange Park, Clay County, Florida. Previously taught at Greenland Pines Elementary (Duval County) through 2012-2013 school year.

Victim was a male teenager (age 16 or 17 per charge). On January 6, 2014, at approximately 12:30 a.m., Jacksonville Sheriff's Office deputies responded to a call and found Brody half-naked and a teen male naked outside her truck in a cul-de-sac off Skinner Lane in Jacksonville. She was arrested on the scene. Age 40 at time of offense and arrest.

Brody was placed on paid administrative leave by Clay County School District following her arrest. She had no prior criminal history per reporting.

Charged with Unlawful Sexual Activity with a Minor (Suspect 24 or Older, Victim 16 or 17) under Florida F.S. § 794.05. Sentenced August 20, 2014 in Clay County to 1 year in state prison. Additional conditions: mandatory psychosexual counseling, required to pay for victim's counseling, curfew, no internet access, electronic monitoring, 1,000-foot buffer from schools and daycare facilities.

NOTE: Offense occurred in Duval County (Jacksonville cul-de-sac) but prosecution was in Clay County where the school was located. Sentence of exactly 1 year under F.S. § 794.05 — a felony of the second degree, maximum 15 years — is notably lenient. No sex offender registration reported in sources.
