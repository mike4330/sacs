Brynn Larsen #245 sentenced

State Wisconsin
Court Oconto County Circuit Court
Incident Date 2014-01-01
Victim Age 15
Prison Sentence 2.0 yrs

Former volleyball coach and substitute teacher at Oconto Falls High School, Oconto Falls, Wisconsin (Oconto County). Defendant age not reported. Victim was a 15-year-old female student.

In 2014, investigators received an anonymous tip that Larsen was seen holding hands and kissing the student, but the student denied a sexual relationship. Larsen told investigators she did not think the relationship was inappropriate. The high school athletic director was aware of complaints but could not substantiate them; he spoke to Larsen about maintaining appropriate boundaries. No charges were filed at that time.

In 2019, the victim came forward and detailed a sexual relationship with Larsen. Detectives contacted Larsen through Facebook, posing as the victim; Larsen admitted to the sexual relationship during a lengthy online exchange. Arrested 2019.

Originally charged with second-degree sexual assault of a child and child enticement. Pleaded no contest in February 2021 to third-degree sexual assault; the other charges were dismissed but read into the court record at sentencing.

Sentenced approximately April 19, 2021: 2 years state prison, 5 years extended supervision, sex offender registration, no contact with minors without prior approval, no contact with victim.

NOTE: Female victim — one of relatively few same-sex cases in the DB. Five-year delayed reporting; original 2014 tip was not acted upon by school administration (athletic director acknowledged awareness). Facebook undercover operation produced the admission. Sentencing date approximate ('last week' per April 26 article).
