Former general science, chemistry, and physics teacher at Williamsburg Community High School, Williamsburg, Blair County, Pennsylvania. Teaching career of 20 years (since 1997). Female defendant, age 44 at time of offense, 45 at sentencing. Married (husband David Varney present at sentencing). One male student victim, age 15.

Two sexual encounters occurred in July 2017: once at Varney's residence in Hollidaysburg and once at Canoe Creek State Park. School officials learned of the incidents from the student himself and reported to state police, who opened an investigation in late August 2017. Varney was placed on administrative leave; she submitted her resignation a few days later. Defense counsel noted she admitted to and took responsibility for her conduct when first confronted by investigating officers.

Originally charged with Institutional Sexual Assault, Statutory Sexual Assault, Corruption of Minors, and Unlawful Contact with Minors. Pleaded guilty March 10, 2018, to one count of Institutional Sexual Assault (18 Pa. §3124.2) before Judge Wade Kagarise, Blair County Court of Common Pleas. Defense attorney Brian Grabill (Altoona); District Attorney Richard Consiglio.

Sentenced to 9 to 23 months house arrest (no electronic monitoring device required), supervised by Blair County Adult Parole and Probation; 2 years probation. No prison or jail. DA Consiglio agreed to house arrest specifically because the victim's family supported it: 'The family wanted this because she has two children at home and they didn't want her separated from them.' Teaching certificate surrendered; pension likely lost. SOAB evaluation ordered; DA stated he intends to seek Megan's Law registration after reviewing the assessment, but registration was not imposed at sentencing.

NOTE: Victim's family explicitly drove the lenient sentence — the DA acknowledged this on record. No electronic monitoring is unusual even for house arrest. Sex offender registration deferred pending SOAB evaluation rather than imposed at sentencing, which is procedurally distinct from most cases in this DB.
