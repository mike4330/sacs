Former Spanish I teacher at Byrnes High School, Lyman, South Carolina (Spartanburg County School District 5). Age approximately 23 at time of offense; 25 at arrest. Case No. 2016A4210101166 / Indictment 2016GS4202523.

Victim: 16-year-old male student in her Spanish I class. Offense occurred in fall 2014 during and after a Byrnes High vs. J.L. Mann football game. The student initiated first contact by leaving his phone number on her desk; the two then flirted via FaceTime. McClure sent the student topless photographs, which he saved before deleting. During the football game, McClure texted the student to come to her home. That night and on two other occasions that month, McClure performed oral sex on the student at her residence in Lyman.

McClure resigned during the law enforcement investigation. Arrested March 23, 2016, approximately 15 months after the offense. Indicted May 6, 2016 on three counts of sexual battery with a student 16 or 17 years of age (SC Code 3463).

Pled guilty October 20, 2016, to sexual battery with a student. Sentenced by Judge J. Mark Hayes II to 5 years suspended to time served (1 day), 36 months probation concurrent on all three counts, first 90 days under intensive supervision, no contact with victim. No prison time.

NOTE: Per Lyman Police Chief Jay Hayes, the victim was of legal consent age in South Carolina but state law prohibits teacher-student sexual contact regardless. Approximately 15 months elapsed between offense and arrest. The student initiated first contact, but McClure escalated to explicit photos and sexual conduct at her home — three separate incidents. No-prison outcome despite three counts.
