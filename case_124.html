Honors English teacher at Freedom High School, Tampa, FL (Hillsborough County Public Schools). Previously at Chamberlain High School until 2004. Age 45 at offense. Resident of Wesley Chapel, FL.

On March 14, 2008, Spack encountered two male students (ages 17 and 18) at a liquor store, purchased alcohol for them, then drove them to a Howard Johnson Express motel where she rented a room. Three additional teens arrived. Spack had sex with the 17-year-old in the shower while four others were in the adjacent space. The case came to light when the victim began boasting at school and the information reached a school resource officer. Tampa Police publicly asked whether other victims existed; none were confirmed.

Arrested March 21, 2008. Charged with 1 count unlawful sexual conduct with a minor (felony) and 2 counts contributing to the delinquency of a minor. Pleaded guilty to felony battery (charge reduction) approximately March 2009. Sentenced to 5 years probation; no prison time; no sex offender registration.
