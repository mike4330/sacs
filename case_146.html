Former teacher at Nathanael Greene Academy (private school), Siloam, Georgia (Greene County). Age 60 at arrest. Address: 1211 Twin Rivers Road, Greensboro, GA (also listed as Buckhead, Greene County).

The GBI investigation began June 14, 2024, when the Greene County Sheriff's Office asked GBI to investigate allegations of sexual contact between a student and a former Nathanael Greene Academy teacher. That original investigation targeted co-defendant Bonnie Elizabeth Brown (age 25), who was arrested March 21, 2025. Mauldin was identified as a second teacher from the same school involved in similar conduct and was arrested by the GBI March 24, 2025. She was booked into the Greene County Sheriff's Office Jail.

GBI arrest press release listed charges as: Improper Sexual Contact by an Employee, Agent, or Foster Parent; Aggravated Child Molestation; and Statutory Rape. The May 13, 2025 indictment resolved to three counts: Aggravated Child Molestation (OCGA §16-6-4(d)(1)); Child Molestation (OCGA §16-6-4(b)(1)); Sexual Contact by Employee or Agent in the First Degree (OCGA §16-6-5.1(b)). All docket offense dates listed as 01/01/2022 (likely a placeholder; investigation identified conduct during her tenure at the school). Prosecution by Ocmulgee District Attorney's Office (Blayne May on docket). Defense: Adam S. Levin (Athens, GA).

On May 27, 2025, the court granted use and derivative use immunity to Ashton Leach — a cooperating witness whose testimony apparently required immunity protection, suggesting potential co-knowledge of the offense conduct.
