Former teacher at S.O.A.R. (alternative education program housed at Olathe Northwest High School), Olathe, Kansas (Johnson County). Age 31 at time of offense (December 2014). Full name Jeana Marie Fleming.

One male victim, age 16 — above Kansas age of consent (16) but unlawful under Kansas law prohibiting teacher-student sexual contact at the same school. Single encounter in December 2014 at Fleming's apartment. Victim reported to school officials in May 2015; school officials questioned Fleming the following day and she immediately admitted the allegation and resigned.

Arrested approximately July 2015; bond initially set at $100,000 but reduced to $10,000 based on her being seven months pregnant and the primary caretaker of a four-year-old child. Kansas education officials sought permanent surrender of her teaching license following charges.

Pleaded guilty February 3, 2016 to one count of unlawful sexual relations. Sentenced April 12, 2016 by Judge Sara Welch: underlying sentence of 2 years 8 months prison, suspended; 3 years probation; 30 days jail as condition of probation (turned herself in the following day). Sex offender registration required. Sex offender evaluation required under plea agreement. Prosecuted by Johnson County ADA Keith Henderson.

NOTE: Same ADA (Keith Henderson) and same courthouse as Michelle Preston case #446, which also resolved with probation and no prison. Both Kansas teacher-student cases with victims at or above age of consent. Single encounter and immediate admission likely factored in the lenient outcome.
