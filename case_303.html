Former math teacher at Westlake High School, Eanes Independent School District, Austin, Texas. Employed there for approximately three years. Age 27 at time of offense (turned 28 by December 2015 arrest).

Two 17-year-old male victims. Victim 1 met Wey at a student ministry program in June 2015. Wey accompanied the victim's family on a trip to Africa; the relationship became physical during that trip. They had sex more than 10 times after returning; the victim ended the relationship upon learning Wey was also involved with a second student.

Victim 2 had been communicating with Wey via social media since the start of the 2015 school year. The relationship became sexual during a hike in September 2015. Wey subsequently asked the victim to delete their text conversation from his phone; a juvenile witness discovered the exchange on the victim's phone and alerted authorities.

Eanes ISD received an initial report of inappropriate electronic and social media communications on October 23, 2015; Wey was removed from the classroom that day and terminated October 30. She surrendered her teaching certification voluntarily on December 15, 2015. Arrested December 23, 2015 by Travis County Sheriff's Detectives on two counts of improper relationship between an educator and student (2nd degree felonies).

Pleaded guilty February 3, 2017 to both counts. Sentenced March 14, 2017 to 10 years probation; no contact with either victim.

NOTE: Both victims were 17 and at Texas's age of consent, but Texas's educator-specific statute makes teacher-student sexual contact criminal regardless of victim age. Wey asking victim 2 to delete texts = evidence-tampering pattern. Accompanying a student's family on an international trip as a grooming vector is unusual in this DB.
