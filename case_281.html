Also known as: Anna-Maria Andersen

Judge Steven Sharpe
Incident Date 2012-06
Defendant Age 52

Prison Sentence 8.0 yrs

Former sixth-grade teacher at Freedom Elementary School, Cheyenne, Wyoming. One male victim. Andersen had sex with the victim in June 2012 when he was younger than 13, and on three additional occasions between October 20, 2012 and March 12, 2014 when the victim was ages 13–14. Additional conduct included groping the victim's groin through a blanket. Encounters occurred at Andersen's home and in at least two of her vehicles. The ongoing relationship lasted from 2012 to 2014; prosecutors described the sexual component as spanning two years. Investigators had text messages and other evidence; the victim was prepared to testify. Age approximately 52 at time of first offense.

Arrested March 2014; immediately placed on administrative leave by Laramie County School District 1. Arraigned April 29, 2014 before Judge Steven Sharpe; entered not-guilty pleas to seven felony counts: one count third-degree sexual abuse of a minor, five counts second-degree sexual abuse of a minor, and one count first-degree sexual abuse of a minor. One additional second-degree count was dismissed by the prosecution at arraignment. School board elected not to renew her contract. Eventually pleaded no contest to second- and third-degree sexual abuse of a minor. Sentenced August 17, 2015 to 8 to 12 years in prison.

NOTE: First-degree sexual abuse charge (maximum 25–50 years) resolved through no-contest plea to lesser counts — significant reduction from original exposure of up to 165 years. Earliest offense occurred when victim was under 13, placing that conduct in the most serious charge tier; that count was effectively resolved through the plea.
