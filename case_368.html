Former forensic science teacher at Hollenstein Career and Technology Center, Eagle Mountain-Saginaw Independent School District, Fort Worth, Tarrant County, Texas. Previously taught Pre-AP and AP Biology at Boswell High School for eight years. Married mother of two. Victim was a 17-year-old male student, a senior at Hollenstein preparing for a Marine Corps career as a diesel mechanic; the initial police report incorrectly listed him as 18. Age 34 at time of offense.

The relationship began in February 2012 when the victim enrolled in Heenan's forensic science class. The two began sexting; they subsequently met in the parking lot of a private gym in Fort Worth, held conversations in a vehicle, kissed, and checked into a hotel. Sexual encounters continued from approximately February 24 through May 18, 2012, at hotels in Fort Worth after school and on weekends. A school resource officer learned of the allegations, informed the superintendent, and the case was reported to Fort Worth Police on May 25, 2012. Heenan was arrested at her home in Haslet on July 5, 2012, and placed on paid administrative leave. Investigators said there was no reason to believe other students were involved.

Indicted February 2013 on one count of improper relationship between an educator and student (Texas Education Code § 21.12, 2nd degree felony, up to 20 years). Accepted a plea bargain for five years of deferred adjudicated community supervision and relinquished her teaching certificate. Sentencing date approximate.

In March 2014, the victim (pseudonym 'Mark Smith') filed a civil lawsuit against Heenan and Eagle Mountain-Saginaw ISD, represented by attorney Bobbie Edmonds. The lawsuit alleged the relationship caused emotional and financial damage — including delaying Smith's Marine Corps enlistment — and attributed approximately $24,000 in damage to school district property to emotional trauma from the relationship. The district was sued for failing to supervise and protect students and for assaults alleged to have occurred on school premises and at school events.

NOTE: Victim age was initially misreported in the police report (18) and corrected to 17 in civil court filings. Sentencing date is approximate — plea taken between February 2013 indictment and March 2014 civil filing. Deferred adjudication — no formal conviction upon completion of supervision.
