Former girls' basketball coach and PE/computers/elementary teacher at Pickens Academy (private school), Carrollton, Alabama. Age 30 at arrest.

Two male student victims confirmed at plea. Indicted on 13 counts under Ala. Code § 13A-6-81 (school employee having sex with student under 19). Primary victim: at least 11 incidents October 2014 through March 2016, at her home and in a cemetery. A civil suit by one victim alleged additional incidents in her classroom during school hours, disputed by defense. Arrested March 28, 2016. Pleaded guilty to improper sexual contact with two students; sentenced to 3 years prison. Prosecuted by DA Chris McCool (Pickens County).

An appeal was filed February 6, 2018 by attorney M. Virginia Buck (Northport) on three grounds: equal protection (school employees criminalized for sex with 16-18 year olds while doctors, ministers, and attorneys are not); the § 13A-6-81 statute did not cover private schools until a mid-2016 amendment, predating the charges; and a constitutional right to private consensual sexual intimacy. The Alabama Court of Criminal Appeals denied the appeal. A civil suit filed by the victim on the same day the appeal was denied alleged classroom incidents during school hours.

Husband James Franklin Parker, 32 (math teacher, basketball/football coach at the same school) was arrested March 30, 2016 — two days after Charli — on the same charges involving a different female student in a separate and unrelated incident.

NOTE: Second cemetery-venue case in this DB (cf. Aldinger #60). The constitutional appeal is the most legally sophisticated challenge to school-employee statutes recorded here; the equal protection argument has theoretical merit but was rejected. The husband-and-wife simultaneous arrest at the same small private school is statistically extraordinary. The private-school statutory gap (§ 13A-6-81 not covering private schools until mid-2016) nearly created a complete legal defense; the court's rejection preserved the conviction.
