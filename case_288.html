Former physical education teacher at Campus High School, Haysville, Kansas (USD 261). One male victim, age 18, enrolled at the same school. Between April 7 and May 18, 2018, Herrs exchanged sexual text messages with the student and had physical contact of a sexual nature; according to the complaint she was ultimately 'prevented or intercepted' from having sexual relations. Age approximately 35 at time of offense.

Originally charged with unlawful sexual relations (severity level 5 felony). Charge amended via plea to attempted unlawful sexual relations (severity level 7 felony). Pleaded guilty in Sedgwick County District Court. Sentenced November 13, 2019 to two years of supervised probation; a 13-month prison sentence would be imposed upon any violation. Registered as a sex offender prior to sentencing; surrendered teaching license. District Attorney Marc Bennett announced the sentence; USD 261 confirmed Herrs was no longer employed by the district.

NOTE: Victim was 18 — a legal adult and consenting student at the same school. The 'attempted' charge reflects that the conduct stopped short of intercourse. Charge reduced from level 5 to level 7 felony via plea. No-prison outcome.
