Former teacher at Calvary Baptist School (private), Towamencin/Lansdale, Montgomery County, Pennsylvania. Age 24 at onset of offense. Male victim, age 15. After leaving her teaching position Jacques worked for a bank.

Jacques met the victim through her job at Calvary Baptist School and resigned her position in 2011, but continued communicating with the teenager. Between February 2011 and February 2012 she engaged in sexual contact with the victim approximately 70 times at her apartment; the relationship turned sexual around May 2011. The victim described a romantic relationship throughout. In February 2012 the boy's father contacted Hatfield Township police after reviewing a phone bill and observing an excessive number of texts and calls from numbers linked to Jacques. A Verizon statement documented 1,168 text messages between January 23 and February 9, 2012, plus 37 phone calls between her landline and the boy's cell phone.

Pleaded guilty May 22, 2013 to felony corruption of a minor (open plea). Sentenced August 16, 2013 by Judge Wendy Demchick-Alloy to 11.5-23 months county jail (work release eligible) plus 3 years probation. Dismissed at sentencing: involuntary deviate sexual intercourse, statutory sexual assault, unlawful contact with a minor. Pennsylvania Sexual Offender Assessment Board determined Jacques does not meet the sexually violent predator criteria, but she is required to register her address with state police for 15 years under Megan's Law. Convicted felon; will never be able to teach again per prosecutor.
