Former social studies/history teacher at Clifton High School, Clifton, New Jersey (Passaic County). Age 26 at time of offense.

Victim: 16-year-old male student in her class. Two incidents of criminal sexual contact during the 2011–2012 school year: the student placed Leone's hand on his genitals in school and she kept it there for her own gratification; she also placed her hand on his genitals at her apartment. Arrested May 7, 2012 on $100,000 bail. Original charges included sexual assault, endangering the welfare of a child, luring and enticing a child, and two counts of official misconduct, in addition to two counts of criminal sexual contact.

Pleaded guilty April 16, 2013 to one count of criminal sexual contact. All other charges dismissed. Sentenced June 21, 2013 to one year in jail and five years probation. Required to register as a sex offender under Megan's Law, prohibited from state employment, and surrendered teaching certificates.

Leone apologized in court, stating the conduct was completely out of character. The victim's mother told the court that Leone was a good person who made a terrible mistake, but that her son had suffered emotionally.
