Psychology and sociology teacher at Centennial High School, Peoria, Arizona (Maricopa County). Hired by the district in 2020. Age 27 at time of investigation. Fired by the school board following the investigation; did not request a hearing to dispute her termination.

The victim was a male student who turned 18 in December 2024. Detectives believe the sexual misconduct occurred during his sophomore and junior years, when Beck was his psychology and sociology teacher — placing the onset of conduct as early as the 2022–2023 school year when the student was approximately 15–16. The district's statement of charges against Beck stated she began grooming the student before he turned 18 in December 2024.

Beck gave the student special treatment in class, completed his homework, and falsified his grades and attendance records. She gave him access to her car, bought him gifts, food, alcohol, and drugs, and paid him more than $600, calling herself his 'sugar momma' and writing that it 'felt like straight prostitution.' Over approximately six weeks, Beck and the student exchanged more than 4,000 texts about sexual and illegal activity. The victim's mother told investigators she knew her son was 'having sex with a teacher named Haley Beck.'

The victim's family declined to cooperate with detectives, citing concerns that the investigation would interfere with the student's college athletic scholarships. The school's principal provided Beck's name to police after the investigation into co-teacher Angela Burlaka began; the principal stated he had received multiple complaints from students and staff about Beck.

Both Beck and co-defendant Burlaka were placed on administrative leave in August 2025. Police resubmitted the cases to prosecutors in early April 2026. As of April 2026, charges had not yet been formally filed.
