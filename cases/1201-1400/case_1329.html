Former teacher in the Bolivar-Richburg Central School District in New York. At the time of the offense she was a volunteer girls' basketball coach at Port Allegany Junior-Senior High School in McKean County, Pennsylvania, and applied to coach there after leaving her New York teaching position.

The educator met the male victim, a 17-year-old Port Allegany student, on Snapchat and began flirting with him. In approximately late July or early August 2022, she met the victim at a cemetery in the Port Allegany area where she performed a sexual act on him and the two had sexual intercourse. She was charged in McKean County with two counts of institutional sexual assault and two counts of corruption of minors.

She pled guilty to one count of institutional sexual assault on March 28, 2024, and was scheduled to be sentenced July 11, 2024. She surrendered her Pennsylvania teaching certificate, which was revoked September 30, 2024. New York revoked her certification separately.

Born 1978; age 44 at time of offense.
