Former teacher at Thomas Jefferson Middle School, Lodi, New Jersey (Bergen County). Age approximately 32-33 at time of offense; 39 at time of sentencing. Also the daughter of Lodi Mayor Emil Carafa. Engaged in an inappropriate relationship with a male student between 13 and 16 years old. Allowed the victim to touch her breasts and buttocks over clothing and touched his genitals over his clothing. Exchanged sexually explicit photographs and videos via Snapchat; met with the victim in her classroom after school hours.

The investigation originated from an unrelated matter — police were investigating the circulation of sexually explicit photos of high school girls among students at Lodi High School. During that investigation, students disclosed that explicit photos and videos of Carafa were also being circulated. A former student had saved them from Snapchat and disseminated them to peers.

Arrested October 27, 2018. Originally charged with aggravated criminal sexual conduct and endangering the welfare of a child. Pleaded guilty October 30, 2024 to second-degree endangering the welfare of a child (sexual conduct); remaining charges dismissed at sentencing. Teaching licenses formally surrendered April 11, 2025 with the force and effect of revocation; licenses included elementary education, special education, middle school social studies, and supervision.

Sentenced to a five-year suspended sentence, parole supervision for life, and sex offender registration. No contact with victim ordered.

NOTE: Over six years elapsed between arrest (2018) and guilty plea (2024) — a notably protracted case. No-prison outcome on second-degree endangering charge is atypical for NJ; the suspended sentence with parole supervision for life is the negotiated result. After sentencing, posted a public LinkedIn message framing the offense as a "past mistake" and expressing frustration at its impact on future employment.
