Former freshman English teacher at Wall High School, Wall Township, New Jersey (Monmouth County). Age 22 at time of offense. Male victim, age 15, a student in her English class.

Beginning January 2013, Warnick engaged in an inappropriate relationship with the victim across various locations in Monmouth County. In May 2013 she was approved by the school board as one of four teachers for summer in-home student instruction. The relationship culminated in an act of sexual penetration in July 2013 at her Wall Township residence. The incident was reported to Wall Township police July 14, 2013. Arrested and charged July 30, 2013 (initial charges: aggravated criminal sexual contact, 3rd degree; sexual relationship with a minor in her care, 2nd degree). Bail set at $50,000.

Indicted September 2014 by Monmouth County grand jury on three counts: first degree aggravated sexual assault, second degree endangering the welfare of a child, and second degree sexual assault. Case proceeded slowly; Warnick's attorney maintained she was not guilty.

Entered New Jersey Pretrial Intervention (PTI) program May 6, 2019 for 12 months — a first-offender diversion program resulting in dismissal of all charges on successful completion; no formal conviction entered. As a condition, Warnick relinquished her Teacher of English Certificate of Eligibility with Advanced Standing to the NJ State Board of Examiners (Board of Examiners meeting June 28, 2019); the Board accepted the relinquishment with the force and effect of a revocation. No prison, no sex offender registration.

NOTE: PTI diversion = no formal conviction. Charges entered as dismissed. Contact_type not coded (no conviction charge). The in-home tutoring approval (May 2013 board meeting) preceded the July 2013 penetrative offense and may have been used to facilitate access. Timeline from arrest (July 2013) to PTI (May 2019) spanned nearly six years. Prosecutor: Monmouth County Prosecutor's Office (Christopher J. Gramiccioni, Acting).
