Swim coach and pool manager at Riverside High School, Riverside Beaver County School District, Beaver County, Pennsylvania. Age 27 at start of offense.

Beginning in 2023, Marshall engaged in a sexual relationship with a 16-year-old male member of the swim team she coached.
