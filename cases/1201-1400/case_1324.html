Former algebra teacher and soccer coach (boys' head, girls' assistant) at Richland High School in Richland, Mississippi (Rankin County School District). Age 34 at the time of the documented offense in May 2012. The known victim was a 16-year-old male student.

The Rankin County School District and the Richland Police Department opened a joint investigation in early August 2012 after allegations surfaced that Walker had a sexual relationship with one of her students. Walker admitted to investigators that there had been inappropriate contact with the victim. She was arrested on Monday, August 6, 2012 by the Richland Police Department and charged with sexual battery by a person in a position of trust or authority. Richland Police Chief Russel James told reporters at the time of arrest that the case might involve as many as three counts and that the department was investigating whether other students had also been affected; no additional victims were ultimately charged. Walker was released the same day on a $25,000 bond. The school district terminated her employment.

The case proceeded in Rankin County Circuit Court (Twentieth Judicial Circuit District), case number 61CI1:13-cr-25007-JM. A grand jury indictment filed February 6, 2013 charged two counts of sexual battery under Mississippi law. Defense counsel was James Lee Kelly (private retained); the State was represented by Assistant District Attorney Jacque Landes Purnell, with Rankin County District Attorney Michael Guest overseeing.

On January 8, 2014, Walker filed a petition to enter a guilty plea, and on January 9, 2014 the court entered an order of nolle prosequi as to Count One. A judgment of conviction on the remaining count was entered January 15, 2014. Sentencing was deferred and the court conditionally placed Walker in a one-year trusty program.

On December 15, 2014, Circuit Court Judge John H. Emfinger sentenced Walker to ten years in the Mississippi Department of Corrections, with nine years and twenty-one days suspended — leaving 344 days of actual incarceration to serve — followed by five years of supervised probation. The court also ordered sex-offender registration. Court reporter Harvey Rayborn captured the proceedings. The order of sentence was transmitted to the Mississippi state prison system and to the state Office of Licensure on December 17, 2014.
