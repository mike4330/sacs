Former teacher in the Placer County Office of Education's Emotionally Disturbed Program at Sierra Vista School in Newcastle, California — a special-needs program serving students with behavioral and emotional disabilities. Granite Bay resident. Age 34 at the start of the documented relationship; 35 at the time of her January 2013 arrest. Male victim, age 16 at the beginning of the relationship and 17 by the time it ended; the victim was a student in her program when they met but had transferred to another school by the time the relationship became sexual.

According to the Placer County Sheriff's Department, Woods and the victim had a year-long sexual relationship that began when he was 16. Investigators learned of the relationship after the Placer County Office of Education received information about it and contacted the Sheriff's Department directly.

Woods was booked into the Placer County Jail on January 10, 2013 on charges of sex and oral copulation with a minor. She was released the same day after posting a $100,000 bond and made her first court appearance on February 4, 2013. She pleaded guilty in June 2013 to two felony counts and was sentenced in October 2013 to 365 days in county jail and five years of probation. She was not required to register as a sex offender.

NOTE: Special-needs / emotionally-disturbed-program setting amplifies the position-of-trust dimension — the victim was drawn from a population with documented vulnerabilities even though formal institutional contact had ended by the time the relationship became sexual. No sex-offender registration despite a felony plea on penetrative and oral-sex counts is unusual for California and merits cross-case comparison. Discovery vector was the school district itself contacting law enforcement, not a parent or peer.
