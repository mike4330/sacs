Former junior high English teacher and cheerleading coach at Sallisaw Central School, Sequoyah County, Oklahoma. Age 27 at time of first offense (reported as 29 at time of arrest in December 2019; first offense December 2017). Two male victims confirmed. First victim: initial sexual contact December 2017, two additional encounters through June 2019 (including post-graduation contact). Second victim: six to seven sexual encounters beginning summer 2018, just after he turned 16, through March 2019. Sexual battery conviction (federal guilty plea) specifies victim under age 16.

Investigation opened December 2, 2019 by Sequoyah County Sheriff's Office. Foreman was identified by school administrators. She was found to have had sexual contact on multiple occasions with at least two different students, and exchanged explicit photographs via text and Snapchat. Foreman and both students admitted the accusations when questioned. State charges (four counts of sexual battery) were filed December 2019 in Sequoyah County District Court; those charges were dismissed and the case was refiled federally under Indian Country jurisdiction (likely post-McGirt v. Oklahoma, July 2020).

Federal indictment May 12, 2021: one count of second-degree rape in Indian Country. Arraigned May 28, 2021; released on $10,000 unsecured bond. Notice of intent to plead guilty filed July 12, 2021. Guilty plea entered July 23, 2021 to sexual battery in Indian Country (victim under age 16). Original rape indictment dismissed on government motion at sentencing (March 25, 2022). Sentence: 6 months home detention, 5 years probation. No prison.

NOTE: Federal jurisdiction based on Indian Country status of Sequoyah County (McGirt era). Sexual battery conviction specifies victim under 16; original rape charge was reduced at plea.
