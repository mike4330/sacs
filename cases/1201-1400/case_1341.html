Former student teacher and substitute biology instructor in the Erie School District, Pennsylvania. Also known as Erica L. McEldowney. Age at offense not established from available sources.

Baker was completing a student teaching placement at Strong Vincent High School when she began a relationship with a 16-year-old female student. She was subsequently hired as a substitute at Pfeiffer-Burleigh Elementary School. The two had three sexual encounters between August and September 2007 at Sara's Campground near Presque Isle State Park. Baker admitted the relationship in a recorded interview at the Erie County Children's Advocacy Center; letters written during class documented the grooming.

Baker was arrested on November 12, 2007. Originally charged with three counts of Corruption of Minors, she pled guilty on September 3, 2008 to one count; the two remaining counts were dismissed. Judge John Garhart rejected an initial plea deal calling for probation only, stating that a teacher who becomes involved with a student will serve jail time. He sentenced Baker to 3 to 12 months in prison plus 4 years of probation. Her teaching certificate was surrendered in lieu of discipline in September 2008.
