Former substitute teacher working in Stanislaus County (including 58 days at Patterson High School), Los Banos, California. Age 21 at time of offense. Female victim, age 17, a student at Pacheco High School, Los Banos.

Egan was the girlfriend of Gary Bettencourt, a teacher at Pacheco High School who had previously taught at Los Banos High School (where Egan was his student, graduating 2011). Between approximately January and June 2015, Egan and Bettencourt jointly maintained a sexual relationship with the 17-year-old victim, a current Pacheco student of Bettencourt's. The relationship lasted approximately six months, ending when the victim graduated. Egan was not the victim's teacher; access to the victim was through Bettencourt. Police notified the Stanislaus County Office of Education in September 2015 that Egan was under investigation for serious sexual misconduct; officials claim they were not told the allegations involved sex crimes with minors, and Egan continued to work as a substitute until her arrest.

Arrested February 3, 2016. Bail set at $165,000. Five felony charges (unlawful sexual intercourse, oral copulation, contact with a minor with intent to commit offense) were dismissed as part of a plea agreement. Pleaded no contest October 21, 2016 to one misdemeanor count of unlawful sexual intercourse with a minor. Sentence: 1-year jail (misdemeanor maximum), fully served via 500+ days pre-trial custody. No sex offender registration required. Prosecution (Deputy DA Travis Colby) characterized Egan as a victim of Bettencourt's manipulation while acknowledging her conduct was criminal. Bettencourt was separately sentenced to 8 years 4 months.

NOTE: Access to the victim was through Bettencourt, not Egan's own institutional role — she was a substitute at different schools than the victim attended.
