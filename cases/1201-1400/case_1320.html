Former psychology teacher at Borah High School, Boise, Idaho (Ada County). Age 40 at time of offense; married mother of two children. Her husband worked for the same school district.

Male victim age 17, a student at the school. Farley admitted sexual contact to police at the time of arrest.

Indicted on 8 counts of sexual battery of a minor 16 or 17 years of age by lewd conduct. Arrested December 18, 2008. A magistrate judge ordered no-contact with children other than her own. Pleaded guilty April 2009 to one count; remaining 7 counts dismissed via the plea agreement. Sentenced June 19, 2009 by Idaho Fourth Judicial District Court Judge Timothy Hansen to a 10-year suspended sentence, 6 months continuous jail, and 10 years probation. Sex offender registration and treatment ordered.

NOTE: Husband working for the school district is a structural feature — the district had to manage a personnel situation in which one spouse was both an employee and the spouse of an indicted offender-employee. Confession at arrest is unusual and may have contributed to the favorable plea (8 counts → 1).
