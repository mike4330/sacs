Former teacher at Centralia Middle School, Centralia, Lewis County, Washington. Age 30 at time of offense. Moved from Washington to West Sacramento, California in 2021; hired by Davis Joint Unified School District in August 2022, where she was on administrative leave at time of arrest.

Victim was a 13-year-old male student. The relationship began in 2020 while the student was in 8th grade and continued after he finished middle school. Polk drove the student from Centralia to her apartment in Lacey (approximately 30 miles away), where she would provide alcohol before sexual contact. She is also believed to have given the student marijuana and vapes during the relationship. Polk used her status as an educator to visit the student at a Lewis County juvenile detention facility where he was held on unrelated matters. After moving to West Sacramento in 2021, Polk continued traveling to Washington to see the student; on one occasion she rented an Airbnb in Tacoma for that purpose.

Arrested August 21, 2024 after a Lewis County detective learned she was teaching in West Sacramento and requested a warrant the prior day. Extradited from Yolo County jail to Lewis County. Polk pled guilty. Sentenced to 364 days with 359 days suspended — effectively approximately 5 days served. Placed on sex offender registry.
