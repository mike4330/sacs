Former teacher at Cumberland International Early College, Fayetteville, North Carolina (Cumberland County), a public early college program on the campus of Fayetteville State University. Age 30 at time of arrest; approximately 29 at time of offense in fall 2015. Resigned from her position in December 2015. Multiple victims — two separate students.

Two separate arrests: February 8, 2016 (four counts indecent liberties with a student, two counts sexual offense with a student); March 4, 2016 (seven additional counts indecent liberties with a student, six counts sexual activity with a student). The ten resulting cases were consolidated: cases 16CR051741–051743 (first arrest group) and 16CR052844–052850 (second arrest group).

Held on $325,000 bond after the second arrest; served 123 days pretrial confinement (approximately February 8 to early June 2016) before release on a $2,000 unsecured bond on June 9, 2016. Arraigned March 6, 2017. Both sets of cases resolved October 5, 2018 before Judge Mary Ann Tally.

Across the consolidated cases: guilty pleas to multiple counts of indecent liberties with a student (Class I, §14-202.4(A)) and contributing to the delinquency of a juvenile (Class 1 misdemeanor, §14-316.1); Alford plea to one count of indecent liberties with a student (in case 052847). All sexual activity with a student charges (Class G) and sexual offense with a student charges (Class G) were dismissed without leave by the DA. Community dispositions on all charges: 0 days active on each indecent liberties count; 45 days on each contributing charge with 123 days pretrial credit applied. 24 months supervised probation. Not to have unsupervised contact with anyone under 18 except biological children; permanent no-contact order with victim. Mental health evaluation required. $500 fine.
