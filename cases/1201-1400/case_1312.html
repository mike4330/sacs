Michelle Morano #1312 sentenced

Former English teacher at West Essex Regional High School, North Caldwell, New Jersey (Essex County). Age 32 at time of offense onset; 34 at sentencing. Married with two young children at the time of the offenses.

Male victim age 17, a special education student at the school. The offenses began in late 2006; she pleaded guilty to twice having sex with the student. The relationship was discovered when the New Jersey Attorney General's Office was questioning the victim about an auto-theft ring involving students at the school; the victim was never charged.

Arrested March 2007 and suspended without pay. Pleaded guilty in June 2008. Sentenced July 25, 2008 by Essex County Superior Court Judge Donald J. Volkert Jr. to 3 years probation; her teaching certificates were to be stripped. Deputy Attorney General Richard Queen had sought 364 days in county jail. Volkert at sentencing: "There is a difference between justice and vengeance." Defense attorney John Bruno noted that Morano had no previous criminal record and was recognized as an outstanding teacher.

The victim's mother gave an emotional statement at sentencing, recounting that Morano had presented herself as differently invested in the student: "She told me she was different from the other teachers because she cared about the whole child. We believed her. We trusted her."

NOTE: No-prison outcome over the prosecutor's specific request for 364 days county jail is unusual; the judge's framing ("difference between justice and vengeance") is a notable judicial articulation of leniency rationale. The special-education status of the victim is a clear vulnerability factor that did not appear to influence sentencing. The discovery vector (during an unrelated criminal investigation of an auto-theft ring) is unusual. The mother's recounted "cared about the whole child" line is documented manipulative grooming language directed at the parents rather than the victim.
