Former math teacher at Tenino High School, Tenino, Washington (Thurston County). Resident of Sumner, Washington. Age 37 at time of offense, 38 at sentencing.

Female victim, age 16, a student at the school. Welter was charged in Lewis County in connection with email messages sent to the victim and separately in Pacific County where prosecutors said she spent the night with the victim at a motel.

Placed on administrative leave in March 2007 when school officials began investigating. Information filed in Lewis County Superior Court April 20, 2007; arraigned May 4, 2007. Entered Alford pleas August 13, 2007 to reduced charges of communicating with a minor for immoral purposes and second-degree sexual misconduct with a minor. Under Alford pleas, she did not admit guilt but agreed she would likely be convicted at trial. Subsequently resigned.

Sentenced August 13, 2007 by Judge Richard L. Brosey: 1 year in jail with 305 days suspended for 24 months, 60 days served as work release, plus 2 years probation. Judgment and sentence satisfied February 25, 2008.

Defense attorney Robert Hill argued the situation was “horseplay that became sexual” and characterized it as “an attraction between two people over the age of consent,” claiming Welter ultimately did not allow a relationship to form. Defense also filed pre-plea motions to declare the communicating-with-a-minor statute unconstitutionally overbroad and for Knapstad dismissal; both were abandoned at the Alford plea.

NOTE: Two-county prosecution — Lewis County (emails) and Pacific County (motel overnight); sentencing occurred in Lewis County. School is in Thurston County. sentence_years = 0 (work release by convention). The defense “over the age of consent” framing ignores the institutional authority element that triggers the statute. The constitutional overbreadth challenge (equal protection) mirrors the Alabama pattern in Parker #63 and Witt #65 but was dropped at the plea stage rather than litigated.
