Former teacher at Edwards Middle School, Brunswick, Ohio. Age 34 at time of offense. Married; husband Sonny was a firefighter working 24-hour shifts, leaving home unoccupied for 48 hours at a stretch.

During the 2008–2009 school year, Covic taught a special program for at-risk eighth-grade students and had obtained principal permission to share her personal cell number with students for homework and truancy contact. Beginning in May 2009 she invited groups of students to her home to drink alcohol and play poker on nights when her husband was on shift. Two male students, K.C. and J.H., visited repeatedly. K.C. testified he went approximately every third day from May through August 2009 — roughly 30 visits — and that Covic had sex with him during his second visit. J.H. testified she gave him beer and had sex with him on several occasions near the end of the school year in May and June 2009.

Covic confessed to colleague Lori Wagner while driving home from a party in early July 2009, then confessed again at a teachers' gathering at Catawba, telling the group she had done "something very wrong." After the group left the table, she told fellow teacher Janet Dooling specifically that she had had sexual intercourse with K.C. In August 2009, J.H. sent a series of extortion texts demanding $4,000 or he would report her. Covic photographed the texts, consulted a neighbor who was a police officer, and contacted her union rep, who advised her to wait for a formal complaint. Principal Kent Morgan learned of the texts and the Catawba confession through other teachers, interviewed students, and called police.

Arrested June 2009. Indicted on three counts of sexual battery, two counts of child endangering (her own children, ages 2 and 3 at the time), and one count of contributing to the delinquency of a minor. The jury acquitted on both child endangering counts and on the sexual battery count relating to J.H. — likely discounting J.H.'s testimony in light of his extortion attempt — but convicted on sexual battery as to K.C. and contributing to the delinquency of J.H. (alcohol). Sentenced April 4, 2011 by Judge Christopher Collier to three years for sexual battery and a concurrent six months for contributing to the delinquency of a minor. Prosecutor Dean Holman; defense attorneys Richard Lillie and Gretchen Holderman.

Appeal (State v. Covic, 2012-Ohio-3633) affirmed August 13, 2012, Ninth Judicial District Court of Appeals. Sufficiency and manifest weight challenges overruled; bill-of-particulars claim overruled.

NOTE: Classic at-risk-population access pattern — Covic taught a special program for students already on probation or with behavioral histories, providing heightened access and reduced parental oversight. The extortion attempt by J.H. complicated the sexual battery charge on that victim but did not affect the K.C. conviction, which rested on corroborated confessions. Husband's firefighter schedule (predictable 48-hour absences) was operationally central to the offense.
