Former English teacher at Cyprus High School (Granite School District), West Valley City, Utah (Salt Lake County). Age 33 at time of offense. Male victim, age 16. Native of Texas; was residing with parents in Texas while the case was underway. Described as being in a "terrible marriage" at the time of the offense; diagnosed with bipolar disorder.

Between April and July 2007, Brown engaged in sexual contact with the victim on more than 40 occasions. Arrested by West Valley City police after being spotted with the male juvenile in a parked car near 2900 South and 5600 West, West Valley City. Arrested July 2007.

Pleaded guilty August 29, 2007 to all three original counts of second-degree felony forcible sex abuse (no reductions or amendments). Wept during the plea hearing. Sentencing October 19, 2007 by Third District Judge Stephen Henriod. Sentence: 3 concurrent prison terms of 1-15 years each, all suspended; 36 months probation to be served in Texas under an interstate compact; sex offender treatment; no unsupervised contact with children under 16 for the first year of probation; sex offender registration required. No prison time served.

The victim and his family asked that Brown not serve jail time. Pre-sentence report recommended some jail time; prosecutor Paul Parker argued against prison but sought some jail time. The judge concluded Brown was "probably punishing herself more than any means I have at my disposal." Prosecutors noted Brown had characterized the relationship as the victim pressuring her, calling her failure one of not saying no.
