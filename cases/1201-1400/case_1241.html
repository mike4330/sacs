Laura Sieradzki #1241 sentenced

Former special education teacher at McHenry High School West Campus, McHenry County, Illinois. Age 34 at time of sentencing; approximately 33–34 at time of offense. Engaged in a sexual encounter with a 17-year-old male student on March 29, 2006, in a car parked outside a Wonder Lake house party attended by high school students. The student was four days from turning 18 at the time of the offense. Authorities declined to say whether the student was in Sieradzki's class. Also separately pierced two female students — ages 16 and 17 — in "inappropriate locations" on earlier occasions in March 2006 in Wonder Lake.

The teacher-student authority relationship was the operative legal element: Illinois sets the age of consent at 17 for ordinary citizens but at 18 when a teacher-student relationship exists. The sex act would have been legal had Sieradzki not taught at the same school. She was a $41,000-a-year teacher and a former resident of Palatine; at the time of sentencing she lived in Freeport.

Pleaded guilty to criminal sexual assault (teacher-student authority statute) and to two misdemeanor counts of unlawful piercing of a minor. Charges of sexual assault of a minor and providing liquor to minors were dropped per the plea agreement. Prosecutors stated that the male victim preferred not to testify publicly.

Sentenced by Judge Sharon Prather. 180-day jail sentence suspended contingent on completing two years of probation without violations; $5,000 fine; $200 to McHenry County Child Advocacy Center; $100 to Crime Stoppers; no contact with victims; sex offender registration for 10 years. Lost teaching license.
