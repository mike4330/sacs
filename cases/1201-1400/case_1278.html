Former English teacher and assistant softball coach at Dickinson High School in Dickinson, Texas (Galveston County), part of Dickinson Independent School District. Employed by the district from August 2015 through June 2018. Age 26 at the time of the documented offense; 28 at arrest. Male victim, age 15, a tenth-grade student at the school. Source articles vary in spelling between "Kirstin" and "Kristin"; the Houston Chronicle reporting uses "Kristin," retained as an alias.

According to the official complaint released by the Galveston County District Attorney's Office, the documented incident occurred on September 15, 2016, when Pike and the victim had sexual intercourse in a classroom at Dickinson High School. The victim told investigators with the Galveston County Sheriff's Office that the contact was consensual; he disclosed the encounter to four friends shortly afterward, all of whom corroborated his account when investigators interviewed them.

Pike had already left Dickinson ISD by the time the allegations surfaced two years later. District spokeswoman Tammy Dowdy said the district was made aware of the allegations in early September 2018 and immediately referred the matter to Child Protective Services and law enforcement. Pike turned herself in to the Galveston County Sheriff's Office on September 7, 2018; bond was set at $20,000 and she was released the same week.

She was charged with one count of improper relationship between an educator and student, a second-degree felony under Texas law. The case resolved by guilty plea; she was sentenced to 10 years probation with no prison time.
