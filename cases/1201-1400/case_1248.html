Former teacher at Notre Dame-Bishop Gibbons School (private Catholic), Schenectady, New York. Resident of Amsterdam, NY. Age 36 at time of offense (inferred: age 37 at August 2022 plea). Female victim, age 15. Husband was stationed out of New York with the military during the offense period.

Hiltunen first taught the victim in 8th grade and again when she was a sophomore. She cultivated a trusted mentoring relationship, telling the victim she was "the daughter she never had" and asking her to call her "Momma." The victim's mother, who also trusted Hiltunen, allowed her daughter to stay at defendant's home. Between April and August 2021, Hiltunen provided the victim with alcohol, kissed and sexually abused her, and exchanged over 20,000 text messages. Over FaceTime she would sexually touch herself and instruct the victim to do the same on camera, producing sexually explicit material. She referred to the victim as both her "daughter" and "girlfriend." When the victim seemed at risk of disclosing, Hiltunen told her: "If you slip up, I will end up in jail and I can't survive jail." When later questioned by investigators, Hiltunen blamed the victim, calling her "clingy" and claiming she had "just did whatever she wanted" out of fear.

FBI and Rotterdam Police Department investigated. Pleaded guilty August 25, 2022 to sexual exploitation of a child. Sentenced March 2, 2023 to 19 years federal prison with credit for time already served (detained since arrest), plus 20 years supervised release. Required to register as a sex offender. Sentencing guidelines range was 30 years to life; defense requested minimum; court imposed 19 years. U.S. Attorney Carla B. Freedman's office; AUSA Rachel L. Williams.
