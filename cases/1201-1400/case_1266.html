Teacher's aide at Sabine ISD (elementary school campus), Gregg County, Texas. Residence: Diana, Texas. Age 27 at time of arrest (June 2019); offense occurred during the 2018–19 school year. Male victim, age 16–17 (one news account says 17; prosecution's closing argument references 16 — lower bound used per convention). Victim was an older student in the district, not a student at the elementary school where Dowden worked.

Dowden resigned from Sabine ISD in late May 2019, before school officials had knowledge of the allegation. The case came to law enforcement's attention when Dowden confessed to a pastor that she had engaged in sexual contact with the student; the pastor then reported it to authorities. At least one encounter occurred in a Whataburger parking lot, per witness testimony at sentencing. Arrested June 4, 2019.

Pleaded guilty to improper relationship between an educator and a student, a second-degree felony. Sentenced March 24, 2021 to 10 years adjudicated community supervision, $10,000 fine ($7,000 probated), 30 days plus a period of immediate confinement (Wednesday through Sunday at sentencing) in Gregg County Jail, five years of Alcoholics Anonymous attendance, and community service. Counseling required.

During pretrial supervision, Dowden tested positive for alcohol in July 2020; she received a warning after disclosing it voluntarily. Prosecution argued at sentencing that she had not accepted accountability and recommended 8 years imprisonment; defense emphasized remorse, employment stability, and participation in a faith-based recovery program.
