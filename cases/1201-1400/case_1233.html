Former teacher at Turning Point Academy, Shelby, Cleveland County, North Carolina. Age 28 at arrest. Turning Point Academy is a school serving students with special needs and behavioral challenges.

Victim: male student with special needs. Baker engaged in indecent liberties with the student over an approximately 18-month period. Conduct included sending lewd photos and videos to the student via social media, and at least one incident of indecent liberties on school property. Baker was employed at the school throughout the offense period.

Arrested November 20, 2019; first appearance November 21. Originally charged with three counts of indecent liberties with a student. At first appearance, the victim's mother confronted Baker outside the courthouse, shouting: "Why are you covering your face? You weren't covering it when you were sending videos." Superior Court case filed December 17, 2019 after waiver of probable cause. Indicted March 9, 2020.

Pleaded guilty August 20, 2020 to one count of indecent liberties with a student before Judge Forrest D. Bridges. Community sentence: confinement range 5-15 months suspended; no pretrial jail credit; 24 months supervised probation. Required to register as sex offender for 30 years (no satellite-based monitoring required). Permanent no-contact order with victim. Sex offender assessment and treatment. Restitution $600 to Cleveland County Sheriff for digital download.

NOTE: Turning Point Academy likely qualifies as alternative-school given special needs population. Three original counts reduced to one at plea.
