Former teacher at Douglas Byrd Middle School, Fayetteville, North Carolina (Cumberland County). Age 33 at time of offense; age 49 at arrest in 2023. Engaged in a sexual relationship with a female student beginning in 2007, when the victim was 16 years old and in 10th grade. The relationship continued until the victim was approximately 23 years old. Phillips had worked in Cumberland County Schools since 2006, teaching at Douglas Byrd (2006–2007), Long Hill Elementary (2007–2017), and Cape Fear High School (2017–present at time of arrest), where she taught English.

The charges arose from testimony given in a January 2023 custody proceeding. The victim, then 32, testified that Phillips was her former middle school teacher and described the sexual relationship. Phillips took the stand and testified the relationship began when the victim was 16, then later recanted, saying she was confused. The Cumberland County Sheriff's Office noted that an earlier investigation (2007, report Control #2007-04574) had occurred but both parties denied everything and were uncooperative at the time. Arrested July 31, 2023; suspended with pay from Cape Fear High School.

Charged under §14-27.7(B) (sexual activity with a student by school personnel — felony G), §14-177 (crime against nature — felony I), misdemeanor child abuse, and sexual battery. The felony charges were dismissed per the plea agreement. Pleaded guilty to misdemeanor child abuse (Class 1); sexual battery received a Prayer for Judgment Continued. Sentenced May 13, 2025 by Judge Gale M. Adams. Community disposition: 30 days suspended, 18 months supervised probation, 1 day credit for time served. No contact with victim ordered. Required to continue therapy; waived rights to all seized items.

NOTE: Female victim. Charges arose from court testimony in a custody proceeding, not a direct report to police. Phillips initially admitted the relationship under oath then recanted. An earlier 2007 investigation had occurred without prosecution. The WRAL article states the relationship began "in 2000," which is inconsistent with the victim being 16 and 32 in 2023 — 2007 is the correct offense year per the docket and the victim's own testimony. The conviction charge (misdemeanor child abuse) does not reflect the duration or nature of the relationship; the felony sex offense student charge was dismissed entirely.
