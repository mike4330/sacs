Former substitute teacher in Delaware City Schools District (April 2013 to May 2015), Delaware, Ohio. Worked across multiple buildings, including Willis Intermediate School, where the victim was a sixth-grader. Age 42 at onset of offense.

The victim was a 12-year-old male student. Bergandine first contacted him through chat in the gaming app Clash of Clans, recruiting him alongside other Willis students. Cellphone records later showed more than 8,000 text messages between them. In-person meetings began in 2015 at parks, a wooded area, and a construction site, escalating from caressing to kissing to mutual oral sex. At a birthday party, Bergandine isolated the victim in her bedroom and directed him to touch her sexually. The on-again/off-again sexual relationship continued through early 2017, including periods after the victim turned 13 and resumed sexual contact. In 2018, while the victim was in high school, Bergandine supplied him with alcohol and tobacco; the resulting school suspension was the only contemporaneous downstream signal. The victim, then 23, reported the abuse to Delaware police in January 2026.

Delaware City Schools investigated Bergandine in July 2015 for inappropriate communication with a student via text and gaming chats. The victim did not disclose the sexual relationship at that time. The district reported her to police, Children's Services, and the Ohio Department of Education Office of Professional Conduct. Her substitute license expired in June 2015 and she is permanently ineligible to renew or hold a teaching license again.

Indicted on one count of unlawful sexual conduct with a minor, a third-degree felony. Bond set at $50,000 at her March 17, 2026 initial appearance in Delaware County Common Pleas Court; she posted bond the following day. Preliminary hearing scheduled for March 26, 2026 unless indicted before that date. The victim provided police a manuscript allegedly authored by Bergandine containing a first-person narrative paralleling their relationship, plus annotated maps of meeting locations. A February 2026 recorded pretext call captured Bergandine acknowledging the sexual relationship while blaming the victim for pursuing her and attributing her conduct to fear.

NOTE: Classic prior-warning-ignored pattern. The district had a 2015 investigation flagged on inappropriate communication and gaming-chat contact, but the absence of a disclosure at that time meant no charges followed and the abuse continued for nearly two years afterward. The platform of first contact (Clash of Clans) is unusual for the corpus, which more often features Snapchat, Instagram, or Discord. The recorded pretext call functions as an admission, but it is paired with explicit victim-blaming in the same recording. Delayed reporting at age 23 places this case in the small but recurring subset where adult-victim disclosure reopens otherwise stale grooming narratives.
