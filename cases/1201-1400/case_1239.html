Former teacher and assistant boys' soccer coach at Liberty Christian Academy, Cumberland County, North Carolina (private school). Age 40 at time of offense and arrest. Engaged in an inappropriate relationship with a 14-year-old male student, exchanging sexually explicit text messages and photos of her body parts, and sharing a kiss. The offense occurred in fall 2018. The investigation was initiated after a parent found the text messages on the student's phone and reported it to the school and the Cumberland County Sheriff's Office. School administrators fired Nelsen once they learned of the allegations.

Arrested January 4, 2019. Two cases were filed simultaneously: lead case 19CR050247-250 and subordinate case 19CR050244-250. Original charges across both cases included second-degree sexual exploitation of a minor (Class E felony), two counts of indecent liberties with a student (Class I felony), indecent liberties with a child (Class F felony), solicitation of a child by computer (Class H felony), three counts of disseminating obscene material to a minor, and two counts of felony probation violation (indicating a prior offense at the time of the new charges). The second-degree sexual exploitation charge was dismissed in October 2019 per superseding indictment.

Cumberland County DA Billy West characterized the conduct at the time of arrest: "In this particular case — other than a kiss — the only allegation is electronic communication between the two. And yet when it's someone under age, those are felony offenses."

Under the plea agreement (August 5, 2020, Judge Claire V. Hill): pleaded guilty to indecent liberties with a child (Class F) in the lead case and solicitation of a child by computer in the subordinate case. Both counts of indecent liberties with a student and all three obscene material counts were dismissed.

Sentenced August 5, 2020. Intermediate disposition on indecent liberties with a child: presumptive range 16–29 months suspended; 36 months supervised probation; 4 days special probation (time served); sex offender registration; cannot reside with any minor child; permanent no-contact order; psychiatric and psychological counseling; DNA provided.

Two felony probation violations disposed October 24, 2022: both resolved with 7 days active, time served. Additional special condition imposed requiring her to notify her probation officer when her daughter visits and when she leaves.
