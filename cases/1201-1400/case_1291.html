Former 11th-grade teacher at Maumelle High School, Pulaski County Special School District, Maumelle, Arkansas. Age 28 at arrest. Male victim, age 17, a student at the school.

Investigation began May 6, 2021 after multiple students reported a sexual relationship between Almand and a student. Grooming communication involved covert sticky-note exchanges in class — passing notes via a shared trashcan. A student reported witnessing Almand and the victim pass notes this way. A recording captured other students confronting Almand; she was heard saying "things got a little blurry" but did not directly admit to a sexual relationship. A third student reported that Almand handed him her phone to respond to dating app messages, and he found texts from the victim in which Almand wrote "do you wanna breakup?" and the victim replied he "couldn't do it anymore." Almand also reportedly showed the victim a nude photograph of herself on her phone, and the two had kissed at school. The victim stated they had sex twice at her home in Little Rock.

Because the offenses occurred at Almand's Little Rock residence, she was arrested by Little Rock police on July 1, 2021, after a two-month investigation. The Pulaski County Special School District placed her on administrative leave; she chose to resign on July 1, 2021. Convicted at bench trial in 2023 on a charge of sexual assault in the first degree. Sentenced to six years probation; no prison time served.
