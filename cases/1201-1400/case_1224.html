Former assistant coach for the boys and girls soccer teams at Vernon Hills High School, Vernon Hills, Illinois. Employed by District 128 since the 2013-14 school year. Offenses occurred between December 2016 and February 2018; police stated the sexual encounters did not occur on school property.

Two male victims, ages 14 and 15, who previously played on teams she coached. Originally charged with 12 counts of criminal sexual assault following a March 2018 arrest on $1 million bond. Pleaded guilty to one count of aggravated criminal sexual assault and one count of attempted criminal sexual assault.

Judge Victoria Rossetti sentenced Beard to six years in prison followed by two years of probation, community service, and potential treatment programs. Required to register as a sex offender for life. Age 27 at time of offense onset; age 29 at sentencing. Authorities indicated charges involving a third student were possible.
