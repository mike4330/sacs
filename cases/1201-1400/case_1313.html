Former science and special-education teacher and soccer coach at Maine East High School, Park Ridge, Illinois (Cook County). Resident of Park Ridge. Age 35 at offense onset (38 at sentencing); married mother of two daughters at the time.

Male victim age 16, a former special-education student of hers and self-identified gang member, who had been expelled from Maine East in September 2008. Their sexual relationship began after the expulsion and lasted approximately 8 months. Espinosa told police she loved the victim and described herself as having been his teacher and mentor. Sex acts occurred at Espinosa's Park Ridge home and in her vehicle near the school; she stated they had sex many times.

The relationship was discovered May 23, 2009 when Des Plaines police pulled over Espinosa's SUV after smelling marijuana through the open windows. Police recognized the 16-year-old victim and another adult passenger, Alejandro Landi (22, of Niles), as gang members; a search of the vehicle turned up suspected marijuana. Landi was charged with misdemeanor drug possession, and the relationship between Espinosa and the victim emerged during her questioning at sheriff's police headquarters. Held on $50,000 bond after arrest.

Originally charged with two counts of aggravated criminal sexual abuse. Pleaded guilty to one count of aggravated battery. Sentenced December 2, 2010 by Judge Larry Axelrood (Cook County Circuit Court) to 30 months probation; ordered to surrender her teaching certificate.
