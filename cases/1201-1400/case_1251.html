Licensed teacher working at West Afterschool Summer Lunch Program, Lancaster (Fairfield County), Ohio. Age 30 at time of offense. Two male victims, both age 17, both special needs.

Hachat met the first victim through the lunch program. That victim introduced the second victim to her. Sexual contact with at least one victim occurred at the victim's home. The Fairfield County prosecutor acknowledged this would have been a difficult case to try before a jury because both victims had admitted to consensual sex with Hachat.

Pleaded guilty August 22, 2014 to two counts of endangering children. Sentenced to 3 days in jail. Maximum possible was 180 days per count (360 days total).

NOTE: Conviction is for endangering children (non-sex charge), not a sex offense — same charge-reduction pattern as Hawkins #1250. Sentence_years = 0.01 (3 days continuous jail, per convention). The 3-day sentence on two counts of child endangerment with confirmed sexual contact is one of the lowest sentences in the DB. Special-needs victim population is a significant aggravating factor absent from the disposition. Victim's father impact statement noted the victim felt responsible for the family's situation. Article alternates spelling between "Hachat" (photo caption, first reference) and "Hatchet" (body) — the former is the correct spelling.
