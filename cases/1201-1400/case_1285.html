Former staff assistant at Ben Lomond High School in the Ogden City School District, Ogden, Utah (Weber County). Age 21 at time of offense (born 2002). Male victim, age 16, a student at the same school.

Rubio initiated contact with the victim via Instagram and engaged in sexual contact with him in December 2023. The school district stated that she did not work directly with the victim as part of her regular job duties, but she held a position at the school and used it to access him. A video recording of the encounter exists. She was placed on administrative leave and fired on March 4, 2024, and was arrested March 11, 2024.

Initially charged with forcible sodomy, a first-degree felony, and forcible sexual abuse, a second-degree felony. On September 17, 2024 she pleaded guilty in Weber County District Court to two amended counts of unlawful sexual activity with a minor, both third-degree felonies, before Judge Reuben J. Renstrom. On October 29, 2024 the court sentenced her to 120 days in the Weber County Jail with credit for 23 days served — the first 60 days to be served in jail (report by November 1, 2024) and the remaining 60 days on home confinement — followed by state probation supervision.

NOTE: Charges substantially reduced via plea from first- and second-degree felonies to two third-degree felonies. Contact initiated via Instagram from her position at the school; video evidence documented in charging records.
