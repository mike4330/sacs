Former English teacher and substitute at Calvary Christian Academy, a private religious school in Middleburg, Florida (Clay County). Female victim, age 14, a student. Age 25 at time of offense. Resided in Jacksonville.

Crum first encountered the victim while delivering pizza to the school on Long Bay Road, before being hired there as a substitute and later as a full-time English teacher. Sexual relationship occurred from January through May 2021. Meetings took place in Callahan, Nassau County, at one of the victim's relatives' homes after the victim had transferred to a different school. One sexual encounter was recorded on the victim's cellphone, recovered later by investigators. The victim was interviewed by First Coast Child Protection Team in November 2021.

Arrested Wednesday February 23, 2022 by Nassau County authorities and charged with sexual battery (victim over 12) and promoting sexual performance of a child; bail set at $125,000. Pastor Ken Pledger said the school dismissed Crum upon learning of the report and contacted Clay County authorities; the church was not aware of any misconduct on school property.

Pleaded guilty to a single count of willful child abuse; sexual battery and promoting sexual performance counts dismissed. Sentenced January 12, 2023 to 18 months prison plus sex offender probation.
