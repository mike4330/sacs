Janet Hughes #1203 sentenced

StateFloridaCourtBrevard County Circuit Court
JudgeKathryn M. SpeicherIncident Date2007-04-12
Defendant Age32Victim Age16
Prison Sentence2.0 yrs

Former teacher at Jackson Middle School, Titusville, Brevard County, Florida. Resided in Viera, FL. Born 1974 in California. Age 32 at time of offense. Married with children. Male victim, age 16, a student.

On April 23, 2007, defendant was discovered by three plainclothes officers in the parking lot of a church at Golfview Drive and South Park Avenue, Titusville, inside a van with the victim. During a post-Miranda interview, Hughes admitted to engaging in sexual activity with the victim on at least one occasion. She also admitted to kissing the victim while lying on the floor of his van prior to being caught by officers. The affidavit was supported by sworn statements from both victim and defendant and by a recorded conversation between them.

Arrested April 23, 2007 by Titusville PD (Det. J. Edens). Initially charged with Unlawful Sexual Activity With a Minor (F2) and Attempt to Commit Unlawful Sexual Act With Minor (F3). Bond $35,000 per count ($70,000 total). Public defender appointed at initial appearance. Charges later upgraded via information to Sexual Battery Familial or Custodial Victim 12 to 18 (First Degree Felony, offense date 04/19/2007) and Attempted Sex Act by Person in Familial or Custodial Authority (Second Degree Felony, offense date 04/23/2007).

Multiple jury trial continuances (Feb 2008, May 2008, Sept 2008). Pleaded guilty on both counts May 5, 2009. Released on own recognizance pending sentencing. Sentenced July 9, 2009: 5 years state prison on each count with 3 years suspended (2 years to serve), credit 3 days. Sex offender probation ordered on both counts. 90 days county jail with 79 days credit. State filed notice that defendant qualifies as sexual predator; court entered order finding defendant a sexual predator.

Extensive probation violation history: VOP in July 2016 (dismissed but probation modified, jail ordered October 2016); second VOP warrant January 2017 (dismissed April 2017); third VOP arrest November 2020 (multiple hearings through 2021); VOP admission July 26, 2021 — resentenced to 5 years probation on each count, license revoked 6 months each count, vehicle impoundment 240 hours each count. Motion to modify/terminate probation filed March 2024; granted in part, denied in part August 2024. Case still active as of July 2025 with continued hearings before Judge Speicher.

NOTE: Charges upgraded from F2/F3 to F1/F2 — the custodial authority enhancement. Designated a sexual predator, one of the more severe designations in the DB. Four separate probation violations over 12 years indicate persistent noncompliance. Case has been active for 18 years and counting.
