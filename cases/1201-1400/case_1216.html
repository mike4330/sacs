Jennifer Rice #1216 sentenced

StateWashingtonCourtPierce County Superior Court
JudgeGary SteinerIncident Date2007-07-01
Defendant Age31Victim Age10
Prison Sentence25.0 yrs

Teacher at McKinley Elementary School, Tacoma (primary school for offense) and Yelm Community Schools (2005-2006 school year). Wife and mother of three. Age 33 at sentencing; age 31 at time of offense.

Multiple victims: 1. Male student, age 10. Sexual relationship lasted several months at McKinley Elementary. August 2007: Rice sneaked victim out of home, drove to Ellensburg, had sex at rest stop. 2. Victim's older brother, age 15. Two sexual encounters in July 2007.

Charges: First-degree kidnapping (predatory), first-degree child molestation (predatory), two counts third-degree rape. All guilty verdicts April 2009.

Sentenced July 25, 2009 to 25 years to life (mandatory minimum 25 years). Predatory designation on kidnapping and molestation charges required under Washington state law (passed 2006, nine years after Mary Kay Letourneau case). Credited ~2 years jail time served awaiting trial. After 25 years, eligible for release via Indeterminate Sentencing Review Board.

Civil suit filed by families against Tacoma, Bethel, and Yelm school districts alleging negligent hiring/supervision and failure to protect students.

Court: Pierce County Superior Court, Judge Gary Steiner.

Appeal filed by attorney Gary Clower regarding predatory designation.

NOTE: Offense conduct discovered when Rice attempted to remove student from home in August 2007; victim told investigators full extent of sexual abuse. No remorse noted; Rice apologized at sentencing but framed actions as 'careless, impulsive and selfish' rather than acknowledging predatory conduct.
