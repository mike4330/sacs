Former part-time substitute teacher at Crest Middle School, Shelby, Cleveland County, North Carolina. Age 19. Began working as a substitute on November 8, 2021; had been employed for only approximately four days before she allegedly began sending lewd messages to students.

On November 18, 2021, a parent of one of the victims contacted a student resource officer at Crest Middle School reporting that Durden may have sent inappropriate images and videos to their child. The Cleveland County Sheriff's Office found that Durden had initiated conversations and shared sexually explicit materials with several 13- and 14-year-old students. Durden admitted to deputies that she sent sexually explicit videos and pictures. Arrested November 18, 2021; released the following day on $50,000 bond. Four case numbers were filed (21CR054224 through 054227), indicating multiple victims. Indicted January 19, 2022.

Pleaded guilty February 6, 2025 before Judge William Todd Pomeroy to obstructing justice (Class H felony) and displaying material harmful to a minor (misdemeanor). Indecent liberties with a student and second-degree sexual exploitation of a minor (Class E felony, CSAM) dismissed per plea. Community sentence: confinement range 6-17 months suspended; 2 days pretrial credit; 36 months supervised probation. Prohibited from Cleveland County Schools premises (except for own biological children). Counseling required. No sex offender registration. Restitution $600.

NOTE: Disposition took over three years from arrest. The CSAM charge (Class E felony — the most serious in the original charging package) and the indecent liberties charge were both dismissed. Result: no prison, no sex offender registration. Four related case numbers suggest multiple separate victims processed under individual dockets.
