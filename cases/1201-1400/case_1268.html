Physical education, volleyball, and soccer coach at Hillcrest Middle School in Simpsonville, Greenville County, South Carolina. Employed at the school since 2012. Residence: Clinton, South Carolina. Age 36 at time of offense. Female victim, age 14, an eighth-grade student at the school.

The offense period ran January 4 through March 25, 2015, involving multiple instances of sexual battery. The victim's parent reported an inappropriate relationship between Dougherty and her daughter to school authorities. Dougherty resigned March 26, 2015. She was arrested March 27, 2015 and booked at the Greenville County Detention Center. Her teaching license was suspended indefinitely and revoked November 10, 2015.

While released on bond, Dougherty sexually assaulted the same victim again in July 2015. The Greenville County Sheriff's Office was notified of the incident in May 2016 and identified two additional encounters. She was subsequently charged with two additional counts of criminal sexual conduct with a minor between ages 11 and 14, and with intimidating a court official or juror. Bond was denied following the new charges.

Originally charged with second-degree criminal sexual conduct with a minor. Pleaded guilty to criminal sexual conduct with a minor, 3rd degree. Sentenced to 3 years in prison and 5 years probation.
