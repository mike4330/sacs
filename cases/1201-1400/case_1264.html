Substitute teacher's aide at Unionville High School, Unionville-Chadds Ford School District, Chester County, Pennsylvania. Residence: West Chester, Pennsylvania at time of offense. Age 42 at time of offense. Two male victims: Victim 1, under 18 (inferred 17 — high school student), a current student in her class; Victim 2, age 18 but still enrolled as a student, also in her class.

On August 20, 2016, Maufort contacted Victim 1 by text message in the evening stating she wanted to hang out. She picked him up outside his home, gave him alcohol, drove to a restaurant in Birmingham Township, and then went to a nearby field where she kissed him and performed oral sex on him until he asked her to stop, worried about being seen. Maufort's husband arrived at the restaurant during this incident; he drove Victim 1 home. Maufort told the victim not to tell anyone and to get in touch when he turned 18.

Approximately three weeks later, Maufort encountered Victim 2 at a party following a high school football game. She drove him out of the neighborhood, parked the car, kissed him, and touched his penis before returning him to the party.

Both incidents were reported to authorities November 11, 2016. Pennsylvania State Police investigated with Chester County Detectives. Maufort was charged with two counts of institutional sexual assault (school), unlawful contact with a minor, and corruption of minors.

Found guilty at trial November 29, 2017, before Judge Patrick Carmody. Sentenced March 28, 2018: two counts of institutional sexual assault, each 11.5–23 months confinement (concurrent assumed, but not confirmed in docket); unlawful contact with a minor and corruption of minors each 3 years probation concurrent.

An Armstrong County transfer case (CP-03-MD-0000214-2019) carrying identical charges remained open as of 2026; circumstances unclear from available records.
