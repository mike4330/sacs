Former teacher's aide at Hidden Valley High School, Grants Pass, Oregon (Josephine County). Age 24 at conviction.

Offense occurred in early January 2025. Marchand initially faced two felony charges: sexual abuse in the second degree and online sexual corruption of a minor in the first degree. Through a negotiated resolution, the online sexual corruption charge was dismissed and Marchand pleaded guilty to sexual abuse in the second degree. Court records describe the conviction as stemming from her knowingly subjecting a minor to sexual contact, having been the victim's teacher before the offense.

The victim's mother, Jennifer Archy, spoke at sentencing in opposition to the plea deal: 'I'm not okay with it — I don't agree with it. And I know every parent does not agree with this. She is getting to walk away from what she did. She crossed so many lines. When I drop my kids off at school, I'm closing my door, telling them have a great day. I'm hoping that the school protects them and loves them as much as I do.'

Sentenced March 30, 2026: three years of supervised probation (alternatively, 30 days jail or 30 days in a custodial program), sex offender registration, and no knowing contact with the victim.
