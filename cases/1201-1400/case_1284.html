Former teacher in the athletic medicine program at South Kitsap High School, Port Orchard, Kitsap County, Washington. Age 25 at arrest. Female victim, age 17, a student at the school.

Springer developed a sexual relationship with the victim during the 2007–2008 school year, her first year at the school. School Resource Officer Bob MacFann received a complaint regarding suspicious activity between Springer and the victim; when interviewed by MacFann and a Kitsap County Prosecutor's Office Special Assault Unit investigator, the victim admitted to sexual contact on multiple occasions and in multiple locations. Contact included inappropriate touching at the high school and sexual activity at Springer's home in Gig Harbor.

South Kitsap School District placed Springer on administrative leave once school officials learned of the relationship. She was arrested May 20, 2008. At her first hearing before Judge Karlynn Haberly, she pleaded not guilty; bail was reduced from $250,000 to $150,000 over prosecution objection. Defense attorney: Jeanette Dalton.

Pleaded guilty to one count of sexual misconduct with a minor. Sentenced to 6 to 12 months in jail plus one year of community custody. Sex offender registration required. Teaching certificate revocation was initiated.

NOTE: Age 25 at arrest; first year at the school. Female victim. Multiple locations: school campus (touching) and defendant's home in Gig Harbor (sexual activity). Worked alongside mentor Patrick Olsen in the athletic medicine program.
