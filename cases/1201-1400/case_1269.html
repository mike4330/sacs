Kirstie Rosa #1269 sentenced

StateFloridaCourtCollier County Circuit Court
JudgeFoster, Joseph GIncident Date2019-09-01
Defendant Age27Victim Age15
Prison Sentence12.0 yrs

Assistant teacher at Phoenix Naples, a Collier County alternative school serving students who would otherwise face expulsion. Age 27 at time of offense. Male victim, age 15, a student at the school.

Rosa connected with the victim and a group of students via Snapchat. She brought them to a party, where she supplied them with beer and marijuana; the host ejected the group after learning they were high school students. Rosa then took them to her condo in East Naples, where she had sex with the victim. On a separate occasion, the victim brought Rosa to his home while his adult brother was present; she and the victim had sex in his room. A third sexual encounter occurred at a community swimming pool. Rosa regularly supplied the victim and classmates with nicotine for their vaping devices, leaving it in a school bathroom for them to retrieve. Collier County Sheriff's Office investigators reviewed DCF interview footage of the victim and surveillance footage of Rosa's apartment complex showing her being intimate with him. Rosa denied all allegations when interviewed, tested positive for THC, and was arrested December 6, 2019.

Charged with three counts of lewd lascivious battery on a victim age 12–16 and two counts of delivering drug paraphernalia to a minor. Alcohol and nicotine supply charges were dropped December 27, 2019. Pled no contest April 6, 2022 before Judge Joseph G. Foster. Sentenced to 12 years state prison (all counts concurrent), 4 years probation upon release, sex offender registration, no contact with victims, 10 p.m.–6 a.m. curfew.

NOTE: Alternative school setting amplified access — Phoenix Naples serves behaviorally at-risk youth facing expulsion, a population with reduced institutional oversight. Two-year case timeline with multiple counsel changes including a Nelson hearing (self-representation inquiry) resolved in September 2021.
