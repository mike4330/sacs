Paraprofessional (teacher's aide) at Stella Worley Middle School, Westwego, Jefferson Parish, Louisiana. Residence: Kenner, Louisiana. Age 22 at time of arrest; offense occurred spring 2022, when Canales was approximately 21–22. Male victim, age 12, a student at Stella Worley.

Canales met the victim in spring 2022 while working at the school. She told the victim she liked him and began interacting with him more frequently. She groomed him with cash, food, and video games. The relationship was discovered when a friend of the victim's mother overheard the boy talking with a friend about Canales; the friend notified the mother, who confronted her son. The boy admitted to kissing Canales and performing oral sex on her in a vehicle belonging to Canales' mother. Investigators found voicemail messages on the victim's phone in which Canales expressed love for the boy and threatened to kill him if she found out he was with another woman. They also recovered text messages and photos of Canales with the victim taken at school.

The Jefferson Parish Sheriff's Office arrested Canales in August 2022 and booked her with first-degree rape (applicable when victim is under 13; carries a potential life sentence) and indecent behavior with a juvenile. She was held without bail. Formal charges: sexual battery of a child under 13 and indecent behavior with a child under 13.

On January 29, 2024, the sexual battery charge was dismissed. Canales pleaded guilty under Louisiana Code of Criminal Procedure Article 894 to an amended charge of contributing to the delinquency of a juvenile. Judge Nancy Miller deferred sentencing and imposed 12 months of active probation. Article 894 allows expungement of the conviction if probation conditions are completed.

A civil lawsuit against Canales and Jefferson Parish Public Schools was pending at the time of sentencing, brought by the victim's family. The family's attorney stated that prosecutors concluded they could not prove the allegations beyond a reasonable doubt, and expressed frustration that a male perpetrator would have faced a different outcome.

NOTE: Article 894 plea effectively functions as deferred adjudication — no formal sex offense conviction, expungement available on completion.
