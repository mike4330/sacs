Gianna Marie Bednarski #1254 sentenced

StateTexasCourt412th District Court, Brazoria County
JudgeJustin R. GilbertIncident Date2018-11-26
Defendant Age32Victim Age15
Prison Sentence20.0 yrs

Former English 1 teacher at Brazosport High School, Brazosport ISD, Freeport, Texas (Brazoria County). First year with the district (hired August 2018). Age 32 at time of offense. Male victim, age 15.

In early December 2018, a Brazosport ISD officer escorting basketball teams to a game in Wharton observed Bednarski arrive with a toddler and bring food exclusively for the victim, not the other players. He noted Bednarski on multiple occasions standing near the victim in hallways with a visibly upset expression when he talked to other female students. CPS notified the district of an inappropriate relationship in January 2019; investigation began. Bednarski resigned January 17, 2019. She and the victim initially denied the relationship. The victim later gave investigators access to his phone, where Bednarski's number appeared; a search of both phones found emails, texts, and Snapchats including nude photos and video Bednarski had sent. The victim's mother then called investigators to say her son wanted to talk. The victim confirmed the sexual relationship and described tattoos on Bednarski's body in areas normally hidden by clothing. He stated they had sex in her classroom twice, including one occasion when he stayed after school for tutoring. A search warrant for Bednarski's cellphone recovered nude photos confirmed by the victim to be of her.

Arrested February 12, 2019. Indicted April 18, 2019 on 5 counts of improper relationship between educator and student (offense dates 11/26/2018 x2, 12/10/2018 x2, 12/18/2018). Jury trial commenced June 28, 2021. Convicted by jury June 30, 2021 on all 5 counts. Punishment phase jury verdict July 1, 2021: 20 years TDCJ on each count, concurrent, 1 day jail credit. Appeal to 1st Court of Appeals (No. 01-21-00409-CR) affirmed August 4, 2022. Petition for discretionary review refused by Court of Criminal Appeals January 26, 2023. Mandate issued March 1, 2023. Paroled December 30, 2024 (after approximately 3.5 years served).

NOTE: Jury-imposed 20-year sentence is the maximum for a single second-degree felony under Texas law; the jury set all 5 counts concurrent. Early parole after 3.5 years. Defense trial attorney Thomas C. Locke (retained) is deceased as of at least March 2025 per court docket notation. Sex occurred on school campus (classroom), which is an aggravating factor under Texas law for the improper relationship statute.
