Christine Jouini #1201 sentenced

Former 11th and 12th-grade teacher at Center Academy in Coral Springs, Broward County, Florida. Born 1969 in Munich, Germany. Age 36 at time of offense (38 at arrest). Male victim, age 16 when the relationship began. The victim reported that the relationship lasted more than a year and that they had sexual contact more than 100 times.

Arrested June 6, 2008 by Margate PD on three counts of Unlawful Sexual Activity (794.05(1), F2). Fourth count added by capias warrant June 27, 2008. Arraigned July 17, 2008; pleaded not guilty to all counts. Bond posted at $25,000 total surety. Original defense attorney Michael David Orenstein; later replaced by court-appointed Steve Rossi.

Charges amended from Unlawful Sexual Activity (F2) to Child Abuse (827.03(1), F3) on all four counts. After over four years of pre-trial proceedings, changed plea to guilty on Counts 1-3 and nolo contendere on Count 4 on December 11, 2012. Adjudicated guilty on all four counts.

Sentenced December 11, 2012 by Judge Carlos S. Rebollo. Count 1: 2 years community control with electronic monitor followed by 5 years state probation. Counts 2-4: 5 years state probation each, concurrent. Sentenced under sentencing guidelines. Conditions: no contact with victim; no early termination of community control or probation; no employment or volunteer work with minors; psychosexual evaluation and follow-up treatment; surrender all teaching certificates. Restitution $2,043.99 to BSO Crime Lab. Total costs and fines $2,661.99 on Count 1.

Electronic monitor removed April 1, 2014 when community control was converted to probation. DOC closing summary filed December 16, 2020. Still owing court costs as of 2026.

NOTE: The four-and-a-half-year gap between arrest (June 2008) and plea (December 2012) is unusually long. Charge reduction from F2 to F3 significantly lowered the sentencing exposure. More than 100 sexual contacts over a year represents one of the highest-frequency abuse patterns in the DB. No prison despite adjudication on four felony counts.
