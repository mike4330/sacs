Jennifer Jean Hawkins #1250 sentenced

Former cheerleading coach at Moore Public Schools, Moore, Oklahoma (Cleveland County). Age 39 at time of offense (inferred: age 45 at March 2023 arrest). Male victim, age 16 at onset of offense.

The victim met Hawkins through her daughter; he began dating Hawkins' daughter and thereafter moved into Hawkins' home for several years. Hawkins began initiating a sexual relationship with him while he lived in the household. According to the victim's letter and affidavit, the first sexual incident occurred while they were watching a soccer game together; Hawkins reportedly told him he would remember that match for the rest of his life. The victim told investigators they had sex almost every day during his lunch hour during his sophomore and junior years and well over 300 times total between 2017 and October 2022. Hawkins eventually admitted to investigators she had been in a sexual relationship with the victim, stating only "no clue" when asked how many times. She acknowledged awareness that his age would "seem odd or inappropriate to other people." The victim, now 21 and in a rehabilitation program in California, sent a detailed letter to Moore Public Schools triggering the investigation.

Charged March 7, 2023 with second-degree rape and sexual battery. Released on $15,000 bond. Sexual battery dismissed August 28, 2023 at preliminary hearing stage. After multiple continuances and status conferences, pleaded nolo contendere June 26, 2025 to amended Count 1: Compounding a Crime, a non-sex offense. Sentence: 3-year deferred sentence, fines, Oklahoma Court Services supervision, no contact with victim. No prison. No sex offender registration. Court docket entry explicitly states: "CRIME DOES NOT INVOLVE SEXUAL EXPLOITATION OR ABUSE OF A CHILD."

NOTE: Extraordinary charge reduction — second-degree rape down to compounding a crime, a non-sex felony. Court's explicit finding that the crime does not involve sexual exploitation of a child is the legal mechanism that eliminates registration. Deferred expires June 25, 2028; if completed successfully, no conviction formally entered. One of the most severe charge reductions in the DB. Victim disclosed 5+ years after the abuse began; contact with victim's mother and living arrangement with the family are the access mechanism.
