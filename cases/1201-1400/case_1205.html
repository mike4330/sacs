Lauren Cosgrove #1205 sentenced

StateTexasCourt16th Judicial District Court, Denton County
JudgeCarmen Rivera-WorleyIncident Date2007-07-03
Defendant Age28Victim Age13
Prison Sentence5.0 yrs

Former 7th-grade teacher at Strickland Middle School, Denton, Texas. Resided in Sanger, TX. Age 28 at time of offense. Male victim, age 13, a former student. Cosgrove was babysitting the boy at the time of the offense. The relationship allegedly lasted two days in July 2007.

Indicted November 1, 2007 on two counts: Count 1 — Aggravated Sexual Assault of a Child (22.021(a)(2)(B) PC, First Degree Felony); Count 2 — Indecency with Child Sexual Contact (First Degree Felony). Both offense dates July 3, 2007. Bond initially set at $10,000. Arraignment November 26, 2007 before Judge Carmen Rivera-Worley. Defense attorney initially retained, then substituted to Donna Winfield on December 22, 2008. Bond held insufficient December 22, 2008; new bond posted at $150,000.

Multiple jury trial continuances through 2008. Jury trial commenced April 20, 2009. Application for probation filed by defendant April 20, 2009. Pleaded guilty on Count 1 on April 24, 2009; Count 2 convicted same day. Sentenced to 5 years Texas Department of Criminal Justice, confinement to commence April 24, 2009, credit 7 days. Taken into custody same day. Pen packet sent to sheriff's office May 6, 2009. Motion for new trial filed May 22, 2009. Court costs $611 paid in full by May 2012.

NOTE: Victim was only 13 — a former student she was babysitting. The babysitting arrangement gave her access outside the school context, but the institutional trust from the prior teacher-student relationship is the access vector. Aggravated sexual assault of a child is a first-degree felony in Texas carrying 5-99 years; the 5-year sentence is the statutory minimum. Subpoenas to Strickland Middle School staff (Mary Rivers, Cindy Jacobson) and victim's parents suggest the school context was part of the prosecution's case.
