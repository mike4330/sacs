Former sixth-grade teacher at North Schuylkill Elementary School (North Schuylkill School District), Schuylkill County, Pennsylvania. Born 1976; age 39 at time of offense.

The victim was Trezise's own sixth-grade student, a 13-year-old boy. Between February and April 2015 — with some accounts placing the relationship beginning as early as December 2014 — the two had sex at least twelve times in the master bedroom of Trezise's home. The case came to light on April 15, 2015, when a school police officer responding to a minor car accident discovered the student had been a passenger in Trezise's vehicle. The student subsequently disclosed the relationship to school officials. A handwritten letter found in Trezise's classroom — apparently written to be read in the event of her arrest — included the lines: "My mistakes have been many... I need help — a lot of help. I'm seeing how broken I am." The school board accepted her resignation.

Trezise was arrested in July 2015. Original charges included Statutory Sexual Assault, Institutional Sexual Assault, Involuntary Deviate Sexual Intercourse, Sexual Assault, Indecent Assault, Corruption of Minors, Interference with Custody of Children, and Unlawful Contact with a Minor. On September 7, 2016, she pled no contest to Indecent Assault, Corruption of Minors, and Interference with Custody of Children (two counts each); the heavier charges were dismissed. She was sentenced the same day to 2 to 23 months in jail and 3 years of probation. She is registered as a Tier 2 sex offender in Pennsylvania.
