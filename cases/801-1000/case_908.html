Victim was a female student who was in 8th grade (approximately age 13) when text exchanges began in fall 2017 during the 2017-18 school year when Close was her soccer coach. The victim moved on to high school in August 2018 (age ~15); sexting and nude photo exchanges via Snapchat began at that point, and the relationship became physical at the same time. Sexual acts occurred: in Close's classroom at Ruckel (the pair tilted classroom cameras and leaned against the door so no one could enter); during after-school soccer coaching at the school; at both of their homes; and during a school dance team event in Osceola County. The sexual relationship lasted approximately three years, from 2017 to August 2020.

Arrested February 5, 2021 on charges of lewd and lascivious conduct with a student by an authority figure. Additional charges filed March 2021 after it was learned the Osceola County dance team encounter constituted a separate series of offenses: lewd and lascivious battery of a child between 12 and 16, and sexual assault on a minor. Placed on administrative leave the day before arrest; superintendent Marcus Chambers recommended suspension without pay. Released on 0,000 bond.
