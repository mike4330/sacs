Former eleventh-grade English teacher at Deer Park High School South Campus in Deer Park, Texas (Harris County). Hired by Deer Park Independent School District in January 2007. Age 23 at the time of the documented offense conduct. The known victim was an 18-year-old male senior at the same campus.

Janes met the victim during the spring 2007 semester, when he was a student in her eleventh-grade English class. The sexual relationship began later that year and the conduct did not occur on school grounds. On December 22, 2007, Janes performed oral sex on the victim and the two had vaginal intercourse the same day, both incidents at her residence in La Porte. Between December 19, 2007 and January 25, 2008, the two had sexual contact at her residence on six documented occasions. Janes asked the victim to keep the relationship secret.

The case opened on February 8, 2008, when the school's campus administration received a report and immediately escalated to Deer Park ISD administration and the Deer Park Police Department. Janes confessed to Deer Park PD the same day: she admitted consensual sexual intercourse knowing it was inappropriate as the victim's teacher, confirmed both the oral and vaginal incidents, and acknowledged having asked the victim to keep the relationship secret. She resigned from Deer Park ISD on February 11, 2008.

Janes was charged the same day as the resignation in the Harris County 232nd District Court with improper relationship between an educator and a student, a Texas second-degree felony; bond of $30,000 was posted prior to arrest. A grand jury indictment followed on May 1, 2008.

On August 28, 2008, the court granted deferred adjudication on a four-year probation term. Conditions of supervision were amended four times — in November 2008, January 2009, April 2009, and November 2009. On September 28, 2010, the court terminated supervision early, approximately two years into the four-year term, with no final conviction entered.

NOTE: The victim was eighteen years old at the time of the offense, an adult under most general-purpose statutes; this case rests entirely on the Texas educator-student statute, which reaches any student in a public school district where the educator is employed regardless of the general age of consent. Sentence_years is recorded at zero — Texas deferred adjudication followed by early termination produces no final conviction. Janes confessed in a same-day police interview, an instance of pre-counsel acknowledgment that placed the case on a quick path to negotiated disposition.
