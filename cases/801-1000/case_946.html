Former seventh-grade volleyball coach at La Pine Middle School, La Pine, Oregon (Deschutes County). Also coached a traveling U14 baseball team in the La Pine area. Age 22 at time of arrest. Attended La Pine Middle School and graduated from La Pine High School in 2021. Only employed by Bend-La Pine Schools for the six-week volleyball season in fall 2024.

Originally charged with 20 counts in connection with alleged abuse of a boy under 14, covering incidents between May and July 2025. Court documents allege at least three sexual encounters with the minor during that period. Incidents reportedly took place in a vehicle and a hotel room. Stigall's coaching role with the alleged victim occurred after her employment with the school district.

Arrested November 14, 2025. A second victim was subsequently identified. Following a formal grand jury indictment on November 24, 2025, Stigall now faces 39 counts including multiple counts of rape, sexual abuse, sodomy, luring a minor, and online sexual corruption of a child. All are Class B or C felonies except a misdemeanor endangering the welfare of a minor. Prosecutors allege contact with one or both victims may have lasted through November 2025.

At the November 24 hearing, prosecutors read a letter from an alleged victim's parent urging the court not to lower bail. Defense requested bail reduction to $50,000; prosecutors recommended $350,000. Judge Raymond Crutchley reduced bail from $500,000 to $100,000 with conditions: surrender passport, electronic monitoring, no contact with minors. Stigall remains in jail. Plea hearing scheduled for December 15, 2025.
