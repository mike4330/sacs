Alyssa Perry #873 sentenced

Former teacher at Morris Knolls High School in Denville, New Jersey (Morris County); had previously served as a private tutor to the eventual victim before he became a student at the school. Resided in Rockaway Township. Age 35 at the time of the offense conduct. The known victim was a 17-year-old male student at Morris Knolls.

The relationship escalated to two documented incidents of sexual assault at Perry's Rockaway Township residence between February and March 2025. In March 2025 a Morris Knolls High School administrator reported the conduct to the Denville Police Department and the Morris County Prosecutor's Office. Perry was suspended from her teaching position. The investigation was conducted jointly by the Rockaway Township Police Department, the Denville Police Department, and the Morris County Prosecutor's Office Sex Crimes and Child Endangerment Unit.

Perry was charged on June 6, 2025 with two counts of second-degree sexual assault by a supervisor over a student and one third-degree count of endangering the welfare of a child by sexual conduct. She was released pre-trial under Level 3 monitoring with no-contact conditions, and the case proceeded by accusation rather than grand-jury indictment — Perry waived indictment to expedite resolution.

On October 10, 2025, Perry pleaded guilty before Superior Court Judge Ralph Amirata to the single count of endangering the welfare of a child; the two sexual-assault counts were dropped pursuant to the plea. Judge Amirata sentenced her the same day to a three-year state-prison term fully suspended, two days of jail credit for time served, and parole supervision for life. The case was prosecuted by Morris County Prosecutor Robert Carroll's office.

NOTE: Fast resolution — only four months elapsed from charging on June 6, 2025 to sentencing on October 10, 2025. The grand-jury waiver and accusation pathway is unusual within the corpus and is the procedural element that compressed the timeline; it generally signals an early plea agreement and a defendant unwilling to test the State's evidence at the indictment stage. Charge reduction at plea took the conviction down from a pair of second-degree institutional-trust sexual-assault counts to a single third-degree non-physical-conduct framing, with parole supervision for life as the principal long-term consequence. Off-campus conduct at the defendant's residence; the prior tutoring relationship is the access pathway that predates Perry's formal teacher role and would survive the falsification test even absent the school employment, but the institutional employment is what placed Perry in proximate authority during the offense window.
