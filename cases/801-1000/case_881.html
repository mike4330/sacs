Former learning support teacher at Mountain View High School, Susquehanna County, Pennsylvania. Three-year district employee. Age 29 at time of offense. Victim was an 18-year-old male student; per the plea, the student was considered unable to give consent due to the teacher-student relationship and the victim's personal circumstances.

Had sex with the student once at her Great Bend Township home in March 2015. Also sent nude photographs of herself to him via Snapchat; the photos were shared with at least one other student and seen by many more at the school. Investigation initiated after a parent reported the Snapchat photos to the school. Suspended without pay pending resolution. Clough told state police she 'had feelings for him.'

Originally arraigned June 2015 on one count of institutional sexual assault (PA § 3124.2, felony; applies when victim is 18+ but still a student). Also faced a felony count of sexual contact with a student. Pleaded guilty December 18, 2015 before Senior Judge Kenneth Seamans to a misdemeanor count of indecent assault. Sentenced approximately February 18, 2016 to 24 months probation. Voluntarily surrendered teaching credentials to Pennsylvania Department of Education as a condition of the plea. DA: Jason Legg.
