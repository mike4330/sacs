Whitney Leigh Chiles #991 sentenced

Former teacher and tutor at Lima North Middle School in Lima, Ohio (Lima City Schools, Allen County). Age 29 at the start of the documented offense conduct. Two known male student victims, in two different Ohio counties, and the case proceeded as parallel prosecutions in each county.

The first victim was a 13-year-old male student in Allen County. After Chiles agreed to tutor him after school at North Middle School, sexual contact occurred between approximately October 2010 and April 2011. The conduct in this case included offenses on school grounds — inside North Middle School.

The second victim was a 15-year-old male student whose conduct fell in Hardin County. The Ada Police Department investigated reports of inappropriate conduct and a search at Chiles's residence in Ada recovered evidence. The conduct with this victim included Chiles providing him with marijuana, which separately produced corrupting-another-with-drugs and sexual-imposition counts in the Hardin County indictment.

The discovery vector is one of the cleanest documented husband-reports in the corpus: on March 31, 2011, Chiles's husband Drew Chiles reported his concerns about her conduct with a student to Lima City Schools Superintendent Karel Oxley, and the district filed a corresponding report with the Ohio Department of Education in April 2011.

The two county prosecutions ran in parallel. In Hardin County, a grand jury indictment was returned in October 2011 on counts that included sexual battery, unlawful sexual conduct with a minor, corrupting another with drugs, and sexual imposition. Chiles pleaded guilty on March 6, 2012 to one count of unlawful sexual conduct with a minor; on April 16, 2012, Hardin County Judge William Hart sentenced her to four years in state prison.

In Allen County, the original charging instrument carried four counts of unlawful sexual conduct with a minor and four counts of sexual battery. Chiles pleaded guilty on November 5, 2012 to two counts of unlawful sexual conduct with a minor and two counts of sexual battery. On November 7, 2012, Allen County Judge Jeffrey L. Reed sentenced her to six years in state prison (three years per unlawful-sexual-conduct count, consecutive), to run consecutively to the Hardin County four-year term — producing a net ten-year prison sentence.

Chiles was admitted to the Ohio Reformatory for Women on April 19, 2012 and released to state parole supervision on January 26, 2022. She was designated a Tier II sex offender with twenty-five years of registration.

NOTE: Two-county prosecution producing two separate plea events and a stacked ten-year sentence is procedurally distinctive within the corpus. The husband-reported discovery vector is documented through a specific named referral path (husband to superintendent to Ohio Department of Education) and is a clean instance of the pattern. The conduct included drug provision (marijuana) to the Hardin County victim, an additional access lever beyond the institutional teacher relationship; the corrupting-another-with-drugs count and the sexual-imposition count from the Hardin indictment do not have confirmed dispositions in available sources and may have been dismissed in the plea or merged into the unlawful-sexual-conduct conviction. Allen County offense location is confirmed on school grounds; the Hardin County conduct occurred at Chiles's residence in Ada.
