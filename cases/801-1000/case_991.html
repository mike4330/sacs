Former teacher and tutor at Lima North Middle School, Lima, Ohio (Allen County). Age 29 at onset of offense. Husband Drew Chiles reported concerns to Superintendent Karel Oxley on March 31, 2011.

Two male victims. Victim 1 (Allen County): 13-year-old boy she agreed to tutor after school at North Middle School; sexual offenses occurred between October 2010 and April 2011. Victim 2 (Hardin County): 15-year-old male student; Ada Police Department investigated after reports of inappropriate conduct; home search in Ada (Hardin County) recovered evidence. Additional conduct involving marijuana with victim 2.

Two-county prosecution. Hardin County (Docket 20112203CRI, Judge William Hart): indicted October 2011; pleaded guilty March 6, 2012 to 1 count unlawful sexual conduct with a minor (ORC §2907.04, F3); sentenced April 16, 2012 to 4 years prison. Allen County (Docket CR20120248, Judge Jeffrey L. Reed): originally faced 4 counts USCWM + 4 counts sexual battery; pleaded guilty November 5, 2012 to 2 counts USCWM + 2 counts sexual battery; sentenced November 7, 2012 to 6 years prison (3 years per USCWM count, consecutive), consecutive to Hardin County sentence.

Net sentence 10 years. Admitted Ohio Reformatory for Women April 19, 2012. Released to APA supervision January 26, 2022; 5-year supervision period; Tier 2 sex offender registration 25 years. ODRC #W083689.

NOTE: Two-county case with distinct victims. Hardin County offense location appears to be Ada (where Chiles lived), not school campus. Allen County tutoring offense occurred on school grounds ('inside North Middle School'). Drug element (marijuana with victim 2) present but charged separately; corrupting another with drugs (F4) and sexual imposition (M3) charges also indicted in Hardin County case — final disposition of those counts not confirmed from available sources.
