Former agriculture science (Agri-Business) teacher at Selma High School, Selma, Alabama (Selma City School District). Employed at the school for eight months. Married, mother of a small child. From Knoxville, Alabama. Age 33 at arrest. Later known as Charlaina Lee Morales Garcia (married name change prior to sentencing). Prosecution: DA Michael Jackson.

Victim was a 15-year-old male student in her class; relationship reportedly began when the student was 14 (spring 2016). Wilson began exchanging text messages with him in the spring of 2016; texting escalated to sexting. Wilson and the student had sex multiple times at different locations, including on the school's campus and on field trips. Wilson also sent the student nude videos of herself upon his request, which she later admitted.

Discovery: In September 2016, Selma High School's principal was alerted by a concerned parent and two students about a sexually explicit video of Wilson circulating on student social media (Facebook and other platforms; reportedly over 10,000 views on one site). Police identified Wilson as the adult in the video. She was arrested September 2, 2016 and admitted to the sexual relationship and sending the videos. Bond: $1,006,000. The article notes the video depicted Wilson 'engaged in a sexual act' — the simultaneous sodomy charge indicates this was oral sex on the student. She resigned from the school.

Originally charged with: school employee engaging in sex act with student under 19, rape 2nd degree, sodomy 2nd degree, and distributing material harmful to minors. Pleaded guilty to second-degree rape and distributing material harmful to minors.

Plea agreement called for 10 years suspended, 5 years probation, and 14 months jail. Dallas County Circuit Judge Marvin Wiggins changed the sentence to essentially unsupervised probation, eliminating the agreed-upon jail time. DA Jackson publicly condemned the modification and stated the victim's family was 'outraged.' Jackson indicated an appeal.

NOTE: Judge unilaterally reduced the agreed sentence over DA and victim family objection. Sodomy 2nd degree (AL §13A-6-64) was charged (the video showed a sex act on the student consistent with oral sex) but dismissed in plea.
