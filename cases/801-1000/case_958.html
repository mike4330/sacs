Therapeutic staff member at Austin Centers for Exceptional Students (ACES), a private special education school serving K–12 students with disabilities, near 67th Avenue and Cactus Road, Peoria, Arizona (Maricopa County). Worked at the school for six years with no prior complaints. Age 24 at time of arrest.

Allegedly began a sexual relationship with a 17-year-old special needs student diagnosed with schizophrenia and ADHD. They met at the beginning of the 2024 school year when the victim was 16. The relationship lasted approximately eight months, spanning nearly every weekend and most of summer break. They met at a hotel where Huesby recorded them having sex. The video was shared in a student group chat on social media.

Huesby asked the victim to impregnate her because she wanted to marry him, move to Oregon, and start a family. The pair created a cover story — the victim posed as a 22-year-old coworker and Huesby posed as an 18-year-old student at a nearby high school. The victim's father had no idea who Huesby was; he believed his son was dating an 18-year-old named Angie.

Discovered August 28, 2025 when the school superintendent found inappropriate messages between Huesby and the student. A fellow student had also told a counselor about the relationship and a sex video. The victim initially denied the relationship but later admitted it. Huesby was placed on administrative leave and subsequently fired.

Arrested September 3, 2025. During questioning, Huesby said she was concerned the victim would hurt himself if she broke off the relationship. She acknowledged it was wrong but continued dating for eight months. Grand jury indicted her September 10, 2025 on two felony counts of sexual conduct with a minor and one felony count of sexual exploitation of a minor. Held at Maricopa County Jail on $100,000 cash-only bond.
