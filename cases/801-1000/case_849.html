Former paraprofessional (teacher's aide) at Rapid City High School, Rapid City Area Schools, Rapid City, South Dakota (Pennington County). Age 39 at arrest.

Victim was a 15-year-old male student. Felkey sent him naked pictures of herself and arranged to pick him up for a sexual encounter; the meeting fell through. Arrest report mentions two underage juveniles, though sentencing proceedings focused on one victim. The victim's father told the court his son was still affected, was being bullied, and wanted to move away.

Terminated by Rapid City Area Schools on April 18, 2016 after district officials learned of the investigation. Arrested April 19, 2016, on solicitation of a minor (class 5 felony) and dissemination of harmful material to a minor. Pleaded guilty to sexual exploitation of a minor; more serious solicitation felony and a misdemeanor charge dismissed in the plea deal. Sentenced October 14, 2016 by Judge Jane Pfeifle to 180 days in jail with credit for time served plus 2 years probation. Judge Pfeifle reprimanded Felkey for blaming everyone but herself.

Felkey told police she is not a pedophile but was 'simply lonely' due to a tumultuous romantic relationship. Divorced mother of three.
