Former paraprofessional (teacher's aide) in the Holdenville school district in Holdenville, Oklahoma (Hughes County). Born 1995; age 26 at her March 2022 arrest. The known victim was a male student at the school, under the age of sixteen per the surviving conviction count; victim age is recorded as 15 as an inferred estimate.

A Holdenville school district administrator contacted the Hughes County Sheriff's Office in early March 2022 after learning Gibbs had been having sex with a student at the school. The sheriff's office requested assistance from the Oklahoma State Bureau of Investigation's Internet Crimes Against Children task force on March 3, 2022. During the investigation the victim told agents that Gibbs had been sending him nude photographs and asking him to send nude photographs in return. Gibbs was arrested at her home in Holdenville on March 7, 2022.

The original information charged ten counts spanning four statutory groupings: two counts each of second-degree rape, solicitation of a minor, lewd or indecent proposals to a child, displaying harmful material to a minor, and Oklahoma Computer Crimes Act violations. After two recusals from the Hughes County bench, the case was assigned to Judge Steven C. Kessinger of Pontotoc County and heard in Ada. Defense counsel Richard E. Butner of Wewoka was retained; the prosecution was handled by Hughes County District Attorney Tim R. Webster.

Gibbs was arraigned October 27, 2022, waived preliminary hearing, and entered a blind guilty plea on February 27, 2023. Two amended informations reduced the charging structure to three counts at plea — two counts of second-degree rape and one count of lewd or indecent proposals to a child. The remaining seven original counts were dismissed.

On May 16, 2023, Judge Kessinger imposed a five-year split sentence on each of the three convicted counts. Each five-year term was structured as a portion served in state prison followed by a probation tail. Sex-offender special supervision conditions applied across the case.

Gibbs filed a motion to withdraw her plea on May 18, 2023, two days after sentencing; Judge Kessinger denied the motion on June 8, 2023. She appealed to the Oklahoma Court of Criminal Appeals, which on August 5, 2024 issued a summary-opinion mandate affirming the conviction. A second application for judicial review of sentence was filed March 31, 2025 and denied April 14, 2025.

Gibbs was received into Oklahoma state prison custody on May 24, 2023 and assigned to the Mabel Bassett Correctional Center. The May 2025 inmate record shows the incarceration components of both second-degree rape counts were discharged on May 8, 2025; the incarceration component of the lewd-or-indecent-proposals count remained active as of the March 2025 record date, meaning Gibbs continued to serve prison time on that count.
