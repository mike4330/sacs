Former special education teacher at Altoona Area High School, Blair County, Pennsylvania. Age 45 at time of offense.

Male victim age 17. Several weeks after homecoming (approximately October 2018), the student told Metzler via Snapchat that he and other students found her attractive; she was flattered and sent him nude photos. Police discovered two nude photos and one nude video of Metzler on the student's iPhone, all transmitted via Snapchat. A third party reported a possible relationship to the Altoona Area School District in November 2018. Both Metzler and the student initially denied any inappropriate contact. No physical sexual relationship occurred. Arrested January 25, 2019; school placed her on administrative leave. The board accepted her resignation at its March 4, 2019 meeting.

Pled guilty August 28, 2023 to three charges: corruption of minors (M1, § 6301(a)(1)(i)), disseminating explicit sexual material to a minor (F3, § 5903(c)(2)), and unlawful contact with a minor — obscene materials (F3, § 6318(a)(4/5)). Sentenced by Judge Elizabeth Doyle: 5 years probation on each count concurrent, no prison. Case CP-07-CR-0000215-2019.

NOTE: Purely digital offense — no physical contact. Defense attorney Daniel Joseph Kiss. Four-year gap from arrest (January 2019) to disposition (August 2023); docket notes 'Appeal Decided' as case closing status.
