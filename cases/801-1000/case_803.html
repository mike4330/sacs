Former teacher at Mesa View Middle School, Huntington Beach, California (Orange County). Age approximately 57 at onset of offense (age 60 at sentencing October 2012). Co-defendant: husband Daniel Alma Shepard (63 at sentencing), a science teacher at Westminster High School, Westminster, California, who met the victim through his school.

Victim was a 16-year-old male student at Westminster High School (victim's age at onset April 2009; turned 17 December 2009 and 18 December 2010). The Shepards groomed the student by inviting him to their Huntington Beach home for meals, board games, video games, and presents. Sexual abuse began in April 2009 in the Jacuzzi at their home. The couple got the victim drunk on a weekly basis and abused him both individually and as a couple throughout 2009–2010. Gay Davidson-Shepard's abusive contact continued until December 2010. Sexual contact continued after the victim turned 18 in December 2010, but no charges were pressed for post-18 conduct. The couple also took pornographic images of the victim and encouraged him to email nude photographs of himself. The victim disclosed to his mother in March 2011; she contacted police. Following the Shepards' arrest, several additional potential victims came forward; authorities said the response was "more than usual" but did not confirm ages or genders.

Both defendants were arrested April 21, 2011; bond was $150,000 each. Gay Davidson-Shepard was subsequently re-arrested for violating the protective order barring contact with the victim. Originally charged with multiple sexual abuse and child pornography felonies.

On August 31, 2012, Gay Davidson-Shepard pleaded guilty at the West Justice Center (Westminster) to three felony counts of unlawful sexual intercourse with a minor (PC § 261.5), one felony count of sodomy with a minor (PC § 286), one felony count of oral copulation of a minor (PC § 288a), one misdemeanor count of contributing to the delinquency of a minor, and one misdemeanor count of violating a protective order. Child pornography charges were dropped at plea. Daniel Shepard pleaded guilty separately to two counts oral copulation, one count sodomy, and one count contributing to delinquency (not a separate DB entry).

On October 31, 2012, both were sentenced to three years of formal probation, a suspended prison sentence (Davidson-Shepard: 4 years; Shepard: 2 years), 60 days community service, counseling, and restitution. Defense: Salvatore Ciulla (both defendants).
