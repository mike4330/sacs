Amanda Sotelo #812 closed

StateTexasCourt379th District Court
JudgeRon Rangel Incident Date2012-10-01
Defendant Age35 Victim Age14
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former substitute teacher in several San Antonio-area school districts, Bexar County, Texas. Age 35 at time of offense (DOB November 29, 1976), married mother.

Victim was a 14-year-old male student. The offense date listed on the charge is October 1, 2012. Sotelo was arrested by the Bexar County Sheriff's Office on November 1, 2012; initial bond was set at $75,000, later reduced to $40,000. She was indicted by a grand jury on June 19, 2013 and assigned to the 379th District Court.

Charged with indecency with a child — contact (Texas Penal Code § 21.11(a)(1), F2 — 2nd-degree felony), a more serious charge than the standard educator statute (TPC § 21.12). The charge carries an affirmative finding as a 3G offense (Texas Code of Criminal Procedure Art. 42A.054), which would require serving at least 50% of any prison sentence before parole eligibility.

On January 29, 2015 (arraignment/trial date), Sotelo entered a nolo contendere plea. On March 3, 2015, Judge Ron Rangel sentenced her to 5 years deferred adjudication community supervision, a $1,500 fine, and $689 in court costs (Case DC2013CR5293). Defense attorney: David Andre Woodard (retained).

The deferred adjudication period was troubled: a first motion to revoke probation was filed August 18, 2016; after multiple hearings, the motion to adjudicate guilt was denied on November 18, 2016 and Sotelo was continued on probation. A second motion to revoke was filed July 10, 2018; again the motion to adjudicate was denied September 24, 2018, with Sotelo continued on probation; she was briefly jailed October–December 2018 while awaiting a treatment bed. On March 6, 2020 — three days after the scheduled end of the 5-year supervision period — the deferred adjudication was dismissed and the case closed. No formal conviction was entered.
