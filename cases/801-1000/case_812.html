Former substitute teacher in several San Antonio-area school districts in Bexar County, Texas. Married mother. Age 35 at the time of the documented offense. The known victim was a 14-year-old male student. The charge ultimately filed was indecency with a child by contact rather than the standard educator-student improper-relationship statute, suggesting the prosecution framed the case around the conduct itself rather than around the institutional relationship; whether the victim had been in one of Sotelo's classrooms is consistent with her substitute role but is not specifically established in available sources.

The charged offense conduct dates to approximately October 1, 2012. Sotelo was arrested on November 1, 2012 by the Bexar County Sheriff's Office. Initial bond was set at $75,000 and later reduced to $40,000. A grand jury returned an indictment on June 19, 2013 and the case was assigned to the 379th District Court before Judge Ron Rangel. Defense counsel David Andre Woodard was retained.

On January 29, 2015, at the trial setting, Sotelo entered a plea of nolo contendere. The court placed her on five years of deferred adjudication community supervision and imposed an affirmative finding that the offense qualified as a Texas 3G offense — a designation that would have required her to serve at least fifty percent of any prison term before parole eligibility had supervision been later revoked.

The deferred-adjudication period was contested. The State filed a first motion to revoke probation on August 18, 2016 and Sotelo was remanded without bond; the court denied the motion to adjudicate guilt on November 18, 2016 and continued her on supervision. A second motion to revoke followed on July 10, 2018; that motion to adjudicate was again denied on September 24, 2018, with supervision continued. Sotelo was briefly held in jail from October through December 2018 while awaiting a treatment bed. On March 6, 2020 — three days after the scheduled end of her five-year supervision — the deferred adjudication was dismissed and the case was closed with no formal conviction entered.
