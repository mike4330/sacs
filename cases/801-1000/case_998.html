Teacher at Bishop Consolidated Independent School District, Bishop, Kleberg County, Texas. Age 28 at arrest (February 2023).

On January 20, 2023, Bishop CISD administrators reported a possible improper relationship between Pena and a student to Bishop Police. Pena was immediately placed on administrative leave and later resigned. Investigation included interviews of suspect, victim, and witnesses, plus search warrants for electronic devices, phones, computers, and social media accounts; investigators obtained substantial electronic evidence.

Arrest warrants obtained February 3, 2023 for five felony offenses. Arrested at her Corpus Christi residence at 10:45 PM the same day; booked at Nueces County Jail on $210,000 bond.

Pleaded guilty September 27, 2023 to all five charges: sexual assault of a child (PC §22.011), online solicitation of a minor (PC §33.021), improper relationship between educator and student (PC §21.12), indecency with a child by sexual contact (PC §21.11), and tampering with or fabricating physical evidence (PC §37.09). Received deferred adjudication with a five-year community supervision term; 200 hours community service; sex offender registration; no contact with victim or victim's family. May petition to have conviction removed from record upon satisfactory completion of supervision.

NOTE: Evidence tampering charge indicates Pena attempted to destroy or alter evidence during the investigation. Deferred adjudication in Texas still requires sex offender registration for sex offenses regardless of eventual dismissal.
