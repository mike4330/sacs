Former teacher's aide at Fieldston Preparatory School, Titusville, Brevard County, Florida. Fieldston Prep specializes in students with special needs. Age 27 at arrest (late 2020). Thorson's mother was a member of the school's administration.

Victim was a 16-year-old male student (initials E.C. per court documents). Thorson sent him several sexually explicit videos and photos of herself via Instagram. The victim shared screenshots with a friend, who then told school officials. The friend later ran away from home fearing he would get in trouble at school for reporting on Thorson.

Despite the report to school officials, Fieldston Preparatory School Principal Cindy Colletti 'appeared to dismiss the allegations' and did not notify law enforcement. Investigation began only after a parent of the victim's friend contacted police directly. Lead Detective Jessica Cabrera (Titusville PD): 'This case provides an important reminder that school officials, by law, are mandatory reporters of suspected abuse, regardless of whether they believe the allegations or not.'

Case number 05-2020-CF-017114. Charged with transmission of materials harmful to minors (Count 1) and tampering with evidence (Count 2). Defense attorney Kevin J Mawn (Titusville). State Attorney Elena Vazquez. Entered nolo contendere plea to Count 2 only (tampering, F3); adjudication of guilt withheld. Transmission charge dismissed. Sentenced April 12, 2021 by Judge Tesha Scolaro Ballou to 5 years probation. Probation conditions include: sex offender treatment program; psycho-sexual evaluation within 30 days; inpatient drug/alcohol treatment (Glass House, already enrolled at sentencing); 10pm-6am curfew; no unsupervised contact with minors; no contact with victim or victim's family; no contact via social media with victim; internet access restrictions until probation approves a safety plan; 50 hours community service.

NOTE: Withheld adjudication on the tampering count means Thorson is not formally convicted. Transmission charge entirely dropped. Sex offender supervision conditions imposed without a sex offense conviction — court acknowledged the sexual nature of the underlying conduct through conditions even as it avoided a qualifying conviction. Prior-warning-ignored: school was notified directly by a student but principal dismissed allegations; police were notified by a parent. Thorson's mother's administrative role at the school is a plausible factor in the institutional dismissal. sentence_years=0 (probation only).
