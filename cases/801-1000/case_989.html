Former English and Speech teacher at Ayersville High School, Ayersville, Defiance County, Ohio. Age 29 at sentencing. Married, and gave birth approximately one month before sentencing (husband confirmed as father).

The victim was a 17-year-old male student. The offense occurred in the summer of 2005. Long had sex with the student in her home and on a road trip to Cincinnati. Grooming access included calls to the student on the bus during track meets.

Long resigned from Ayersville High School after allegations became public, and the school board accepted the resignation in January 2006. She also surrendered her teaching certificate. The grand jury reviewed the case on January 18, 2006.

Pleaded guilty to sexual battery, a third-degree felony. Sentenced March 13, 2006 to 90 days in the regional jail in Stryker, 4 years of probation, and sex offender registration. The statutory maximum was 5 years.
