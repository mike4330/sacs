Head softball coach at West Lyon High School, Larchwood, Iowa (Lyon County). Resident of rural Larchwood. Age 26 at time of arrest. 2017 Rock Valley High School graduate (maiden name Mousel). Married with a daughter.

Charged with three counts of sexual exploitation by a school employee (Class D felony) and one count of grooming. Incidents occurred with a member of the West Lyon softball team from July 24, 2024 to May 16, 2025. The grooming charge alleges Severson used a cellular phone/device to communicate with the student both in person and through a third party to entice and seduce a child into a relationship resulting in unlawful sex acts, July 24–October 9, 2024. The sexual exploitation charges state Severson had direct supervisory authority over and a long-term relationship with the student that included kissing, touching, and sex acts throughout the student's senior year (August 23, 2024–May 16, 2025).

Arrested October 31, 2025. Posted $10,000 unsecured signature bond and released same day. No contact order issued with the former student. Preliminary hearing scheduled for November 20, 2025.

Severson served as head coach for the 2024 and 2025 seasons after three years as assistant coach. The West Lyon School District Board of Education approved her resignation on September 8, 2025. She is no longer employed by the district.
