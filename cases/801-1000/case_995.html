Physical education teacher at Horse Heaven Hills Middle School, Kennewick, Benton County, Washington. Kennewick School District employee since 1997 in multiple roles: substitute teacher, head gymnastics coach at Southridge High, and math teacher/assistant track, volleyball, and dance coach at Horse Heaven Hills. Age 43 at time of offense; 44 at sentencing.

Four male victims, all under 16, all middle school students. From approximately fall 2015 through June 2016 (up to 10 months), Reiboldt sent sexually explicit nude videos to the boys via Snapchat and solicited nude pictures from them in return. She used her own son's Snapchat account to obtain contact information for his friend, one of the reported victims. She attempted to arrange in-person sexual encounters with at least two of the boys; one refused and another fabricated an excuse while she waited for him at a park near his home.

One victim disclosed in January 2017 that Reiboldt had also raped him. He was the younger of the two sons whose mother gave the victim impact statement at sentencing.

Arrested June 2016 following police investigation of an explicit Snapchat video circulating to underage kids; Reiboldt identified as the woman in the video. Charged January 2017 with third-degree child rape. Pleaded guilty May 2017 to third-degree child rape and three felony counts of communicating with a minor for immoral purposes; each count carried the aggravating circumstance of using a position of trust to commit the crime.

Sentenced July 6, 2017 by Judge Bruce Spanner to 18 months prison under Washington's Special Sex Offender Sentencing Alternative (SSOSA), plus a 3.5-year suspended term. Standard range for the rape charge was 5 years. SSOSA granted on findings of genuine remorse, low reoffending risk, and amenability to treatment. Divorced during proceedings; limited to supervised contact with her own two sons.
