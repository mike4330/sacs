Former sixth-grade teacher, Axtell Park Middle School, Sioux Falls School District (employed 2009–2013). Female victim, age 14. Relationship began 2012 while Drueppel was still employed by the district.

Investigation began January 22, 2014 after a referral from the Department of Social Services. Arrested and charged January 29, 2014 on three counts of 4th-degree rape and two counts of sexual contact with a child under 16; held on $500,000 cash bond.

Charged in a five-count indictment. Pleaded guilty February 2015 to two counts of sexual contact with a child younger than 16 (rape counts dropped as part of plea).

Sentenced May 22, 2015 to 15 years in prison. Both the victim and victim's mother testified at sentencing. Victim stated she intended to forgive Drueppel "because it's the right thing to do."

DOB November 5, 1980; age 31 at offense, 34 at sentencing. SOR name: Sidel, Tonya M (married name). Minnehaha County conviction.
