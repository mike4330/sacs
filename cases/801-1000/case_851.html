Former biology teacher at Patrick Henry High School, Ashland, Virginia (Hanover County). Worked at the school for approximately 15 years: began as a substitute teacher and was promoted to full-time teacher in 2006. Age 35 at plea (October 2018).

Male student victim; age not reported. Another teacher reported concerns about Pauley's interactions with the student to administration. The student later told investigators he had a sexual relationship with Pauley. Arrested at Patrick Henry High School in spring 2018 by the Hanover County Sheriff's Office.

Pleaded guilty October 19, 2018 in Hanover County Circuit Court to four counts of proposing sex with a minor and one count of using a communication device to commit crimes against children (five felony counts). The plea was part of an agreement calling for two years of active incarceration. Prosecutor Elizabeth Linka. Sentenced January 11, 2019 to 25 years in prison with all but two years suspended; required to register as a sex offender.

NOTE: Conviction charges are solicitation/communication offenses; prosecutors disclosed an underlying physical sexual relationship with the student. The reporting teacher's concern is the trigger for the investigation — a prior-warning-ignored pattern was not present; a colleague did act.
