Former social studies teacher at Troy Middle School, Troy ISD, Troy, Texas (Bell County). Age 32 at time of offense.

Victim was a 14-year-old male student. Three incidents of sexual intercourse occurred in Pomerenke's car. On July 19, 2011, she resigned after meeting with Superintendent Neil Jeter. Case filed by information on July 21, 2011 (Case FR68594, 264th Judicial District Court, Bell County).

On August 15, 2011, Pomerenke entered a guilty plea before Judge Trudo to one count of improper relationship between educator and student (Texas Penal Code § 21.12(a), 2nd-degree felony). The court ordered a pre-sentence investigation report. On November 10, 2011, she was sentenced to 7 years deferred adjudication community supervision and 3 days in Bell County Jail as a condition. Upon successful completion of the deferred adjudication period, the charge is dismissed with no formal conviction (7 YRS DA/DISMISSED per docket). Defense attorney: Kurt Glass (retained). Prosecutor: Fred Burns.

Texas deferred adjudication = no formal conviction if 7-year supervision period is completed (cf. NAJG convention). The Houston Press noted the stark disparity between Pomerenke's outcome and a comparable male Texas teacher case that received significantly harsher punishment.
