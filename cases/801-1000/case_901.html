Former owner and instructor of Colette's Dancing Studio, Taylorsville, Salt Lake County, Utah. Former Utah Jazz NBA dancer. Age 33 at time of offense and arrest. Mother of three.

Victim was a 17-year-old male student at her dance studio. The relationship began in September 2014, shortly after Telford filed for divorce from her husband. Grooming included flirting during lessons, purchasing clothing for the victim, and arranging a trip to Disneyland for a dance contest. Sexual activity occurred at Telford's home, her mother's home, inside her vehicle, and at the studio itself; she picked the victim up in the middle of the night on multiple occasions. Relationship spanned September through December 2014.

The relationship was discovered in November 2014 by a private detective hired by Telford's estranged husband to investigate suspected infidelity. The PI found Telford naked with the teenager — his own son — under a blanket, clothing strewn around the room. Telford was arrested in January 2015. Colette's Dancing Studio subsequently closed.

Charged with seven counts of unlawful sexual activity with a minor. Pleaded guilty July 2015 to one count; six identical counts dismissed. Sentenced September 3, 2015: 30 days jail, 150 hours community service, probation, sex offender registration.
