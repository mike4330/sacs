Math teacher and volleyball coach at Silver High School, Silver City, New Mexico. Worked for Silver Consolidated School District for 8 years. Age 32 at sentencing (April 2018). Married; two daughters.

Conduct: In April 2017, had sexual intercourse with a 17-year-old male student approximately four times. Meetings included midnight rendezvous at Bear Canyon Dam (caught there by victim's mother) and driving 45 minutes to Mimbres for sexual encounters. Sexual texts and explicit photos exchanged with the student.

Discovery: Victim's mother caught Sanchez meeting her son at midnight at Bear Canyon Dam, became suspicious, and searched his phone, finding sexual texts and photographs. Reported to New Mexico State Police. Student admitted to sexual intercourse approximately four times. Sanchez arrested May 18, 2017. Placed on paid administrative leave May 12 and voluntarily resigned the same day; surrendered New Mexico teaching and coaching licenses.

Prior warnings: Before arrest, multiple students had reported Sanchez's inappropriate conduct. She was brought in and spoken to by Silver High's principal and was aware she was being closely watched. She continued the relationship regardless.

Charges filed April 5, 2017: 4 counts Criminal Sexual Penetration in the Fourth Degree (School Employee), NMSA §30-9-11(G)(2), F4 felonies (maximum 18 months each; 6 years total). Defense attorney Jim Foy. Chief Deputy DA George Zsoka. No contest plea entered April 2018. Sentenced April 9, 2018 by Judge J.C. Robinson: conditional discharge with 5 years supervised probation (until 04/08/2023); no jail time; no sex offender registration.

Victim's mother read an impact statement at sentencing, disclosing that her son threatened suicide before the case became public and suffered depression for months. She stated approximately 13 other teenage boys' families had declined to come forward due to shame and fear of embarrassment.

Probation terminated early October 4, 2021 by Judge Tom F. Stewart (defense attorney Jim Foy had by then become a judge and recused himself). All charges dismissed upon termination of conditional discharge.
