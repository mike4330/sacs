Former teacher at Lawrenceburg High School, Lawrenceburg, Indiana (Dearborn County). Resident of Greendale. Age not specified in sources.

Accused of grooming and having an inappropriate relationship with a 17-year-old female student approximately 11 years ago, beginning in the summer of 2014. The victim had been a student in Schwarz's class and the daughter of one of Schwarz's friends. The relationship began after the victim joined Schwarz's family at church. The victim was often invited into Schwarz's home when the teacher's husband and children were not there. The relationship included kissing, inappropriate touching, and texting, and lasted about a year. The victim believed she was in a secret relationship with Schwarz. As an adult, the victim realized how inappropriate the relationship was and is concerned it may have happened to other students.

On January 28, 2025, the Lawrenceburg High School principal contacted police after receiving a message from the former student. Schwarz was placed on administrative paid leave and submitted a retirement letter effective February 3, 2025.

Originally charged with two counts of child seduction (filed by Dearborn County Prosecutor Lynn Deddens). Disposition March 12, 2026: convicted of official misconduct (IC 35-44.1-1-1(1), Class F felony) and harassment by means of a telephone call (IC 35-45-2-2(a)(1), Class B misdemeanor). Judge Jonathan Neil Cleary presiding.

Sentenced on Count III (official misconduct): 365 days county jail, 261 days credit, 0 suspended. Consecutive with Count IV. Court costs $189. Count IV (harassment by telephone): 180 days county jail, 90 days credit, consecutive with Count III. Conditions: 2 days home detention, no contact with victim (E.T.).

NOTE: Female victim, age 17 at time of offense. Significant charge reduction — child seduction reduced to official misconduct and harassment. Total sentence 545 days (1.5 years) county jail. Delayed reporting — victim came forward ~11 years later. Grooming through church and family friendship. Victim was daughter of Schwarz's friend. Long-term relationship (~1 year).
