Social studies teacher (7th grade) at Jimmy Carter Middle School, Albuquerque Public Schools, New Mexico. Age 33 at time of offense; mother of three. Began as educational assistant at Afrisco Elementary School in 1998 (two leaves of absence); transferred to Jimmy Carter Middle School as classroom teacher in 2007.

Conduct: In October 2009, Sanchez-Trujillo took her 13-year-old male 7th-grade student on two dates — each time to a movie, then to Petroglyph National Monument, which she referred to as 'our spot.' Both times she had sex with him in the back of her vehicle, then drove him home after midnight; the first incident ended at 5am.

Discovery: Concerned that the victim's brother had seen them at the movies, she confided in a school colleague. That teacher immediately reported to Albuquerque Public Schools Police, who contacted APD. Sanchez-Trujillo confessed to police. She was fired in October 2009. Trial was set for May 2012; she was also under review for violating conditions of pretrial release during the intervening period.

Plea: Guilty April 28, 2012 to one count criminal sexual penetration of a minor, two counts criminal sexual contact, two counts contributing to the delinquency of a minor. Sentence June 29, 2012 by Judge Reed Sheppard: 364 days house arrest, sex offender registration. Victim and family were not present at sentencing. Defendant made a tearful apology in open court.

Statements to police: described the sex as 'loving and caring'; said the boy 'was more experienced than any man she has had sex with'; described him as 'a 25-year-old in a 13-year-old's body.'
