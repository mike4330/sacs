Former teacher at Church Farm School, an all-boys private school in Exton, Chester County, Pennsylvania. Taught there for eight years. Age 35 at arrest. Victim was a 16-year-old male student (17 at time of arrest).

Relationship began January 2014 and lasted approximately 10 months. First physical contact (kissing) at a Motel 6 in King of Prussia, February 2014; returned to the same motel in March 2014 to celebrate O'Neill's birthday. Police identified four incidents occurring in O'Neill's vehicle and at area hotels. O'Neill wrote the student an undated love letter — left at the school — which read in part: 'The thought that I could love so deeply and so wrongly pervaded before you and I even officially began. It was quite a long winter break before my lips even attempted yours and I wondered if you were as affected as I.' Discovery of the note led to her resignation.

Arrested January 13, 2015 on four counts of corruption of minors (18 Pa.C.S. § 6301). Pleaded guilty approximately February 13, 2015 to two counts of corruption of minors. Sentenced to 5 years probation, 100 hours community service, prohibition on unsupervised contact with minors, and mandatory mental health evaluation. No prison; no sex offender registration mentioned.
