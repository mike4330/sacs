Former lunch employee at Ben Franklin Middle School, Fargo Public Schools, Fargo, North Dakota (Cass County). Age approximately 19 at time of offense (December 2021); age 20 at guilty plea (January 2023).

One male victim, age 14. Investigation began December 2021 after a Snapchat video circulated at the school showing Carlson and a shirtless boy brushing their teeth. Court records allege sexual intercourse on December 1, 2021. Video recovered from Carlson's phone showed the victim and Carlson having sex, smoking marijuana, and holding guns; Carlson discussed getting pregnant with the boy on video. Court records also referenced a student challenge circulating at the school to see who could 'get with' Carlson.

Fired by Fargo Public Schools December 20, 2021. Pleaded guilty January 17, 2023 in Cass County District Court to three felony counts: gross sexual imposition, possession of THC with intent to deliver or manufacture, and child neglect. Dismissed at plea: promoting a sexual performance by a minor and possession of certain materials prohibited by North Dakota law (CSAM). Sentenced March 20, 2023: 30 months prison, 5 years supervised probation; credit for 201 days time served.

NOTE: CSAM charge (prohibited materials) and promoting a sexual performance by a minor both dismissed at plea despite video evidence. Child neglect charge relates to marijuana paraphernalia accessible to Carlson's 2-year-old child in the apartment. Gross sexual imposition (ND § 12.1-20-03) requires a sexual act — penetrative by statute.
