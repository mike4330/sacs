Former instructional aide at Chaparral High School in Phelan, California (San Bernardino County), part of the Snowline Joint Unified School District. Hired at Chaparral in November 2017; previously worked at the Eagle Summit Community Day School. Resided in Phelan. Age 43 at arrest. Two known male victims, both students at Chaparral, ages 15 and 16. Investigators publicly stated they believed additional victims might exist.

The case opened on March 1, 2018, when school officials reported the alleged relationships to the San Bernardino County Sheriff's Victor Valley Station. Detectives from the department's Crimes Against Children division interviewed several minors during the investigation. Escobar was arrested the next day, March 2, 2018, and booked into the Central Detention Center in San Bernardino in lieu of a $100,000 bail.

Charges filed by the San Bernardino County District Attorney's Office covered a wide range of conduct: a count of lewd or lascivious acts with a child 14 or 15, two oral-copulation counts (one for a person under 16, one for a person under 18), two counts of arranging a meeting with a minor for lewd purposes, two counts of sending harmful material to seduce a minor, three counts of unlawful sexual intercourse with a minor, one count of possessing child sexual abuse material, and a witness-dissuasion count alleging that Escobar had attempted to deter a victim from reporting.

The case was prosecuted by Deputy District Attorney David Foy; defense counsel Brian Watson was retained. Through plea negotiation, the matter resolved on three counts: the lewd-act-on-14-or-15 count, the oral-copulation-under-18 count, and the witness-dissuasion count. The remaining charges — the second oral-copulation count, both arranging-a-meeting counts, both harmful-material counts, the CSAM-possession count, and all three statutory-rape counts — were dismissed.

On January 10, 2020, San Bernardino County Superior Court Judge Miriam I. Morton (Victorville District) denied probation and sentenced Escobar on the three convicted counts to two years in state prison on each, to run concurrently, for a 2-year determinate aggregate term. The court awarded pretrial credit covering roughly twenty-two months of pre-sentencing custody (331 days of actual custody plus 330 days of conduct credit under California's good-time rules). Escobar was committed to the California Institution for Women at Frontera.

NOTE: Multi-victim case (two known victims plus investigator-flagged possible additional victims) with a CSAM-possession charge filed and an explicit witness-dissuasion conviction documenting concealment conduct after the relationships were exposed. The plea structure is striking: of the original twelve counts, the most serious sex-offense exposure (statutory-rape and CSAM-possession counts) was dismissed and the conviction set centered on the lewd-act count plus the dissuasion count. The two-year aggregate sentence was largely covered by pretrial credit, leaving comparatively little post-sentencing custody. Escobar's prior assignment at Eagle Summit Community Day School is recorded as cross-case access-pattern detail; community day schools serve students with attendance, behavior, or alternative-placement issues, which fits the alternative-school access pattern observed in other corpus cases.
