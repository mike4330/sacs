Former eighth-grade English teacher at Sheffield Junior High School, Sheffield, Alabama (Colbert County). Approximately age 37 at time of offense.

The victim was a 15-year-old male student. In December 2009, Muscle Shoals police discovered Watkins and the student having sexual intercourse in the backseat of her car in a school parking lot, an in-flagrante discovery rather than a delayed-disclosure investigation. Watkins did not accept a plea and was tried by jury.

On November 3, 2011, a Colbert County Circuit Court jury convicted Watkins of second-degree rape and second-degree sodomy. On February 16, 2012, she was sentenced to two 20-year split sentences with 5 years active on each count, to run consecutively, for 10 years total active imprisonment. She was remanded immediately to custody for transfer to Julia Tutwiler Prison for Women, the state's all-female prison, and was later moved to Franklin County due to overcrowding. After approximately two years served, she was released with the consent of the victim and the district attorney, with the remainder of the term served under supervision.

Several witnesses, including her treating psychiatrist, testified at sentencing asking for leniency so Watkins could continue treatment for mental illness. Defense doctors described diagnoses of depression, dissociative disorder, and PTSD; the psychiatrist's central concern was regression if Watkins were sent to prison. The judge took approximately an hour of testimony before imposing sentence. District Attorney Bryce Graham said he was pleased with the disposition and noted that Watkins would be able to seek treatment while incarcerated. Defense attorney Ralph Holt declined to comment on camera and said any decision to appeal would be discussed with the family. Watkins testified for the first time on the afternoon of sentencing.
