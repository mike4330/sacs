Former 8th-grade English teacher at Sheffield Junior High School, Sheffield, Alabama (Colbert County). Age approximately 37 at time of offense.

Victim was a 15-year-old male student. In December 2009, Muscle Shoals police discovered Watkins and the student having sexual intercourse in the backseat of her car in a school parking lot — an in-flagrante discovery. She did not accept a plea and was tried by jury.

On November 3, 2011, a Colbert County Circuit Court jury convicted Watkins of second-degree rape (Alabama) and second-degree sodomy (Alabama). On February 15, 2012, she was sentenced to two 20-year split sentences with 5 years active on each count, to run consecutively — 10 years total active imprisonment. She was transferred from Tutwiler Prison to Franklin County due to overcrowding. After approximately 2 years served, she was released with consent of the victim and district attorney, with the remainder served under supervision.
