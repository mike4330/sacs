Teacher at Basic Academy of International Studies (Basic High School), Henderson, Nevada (Clark County School District). Age 27 at arrest. Hired by CCSD in 2015. Male student victim, age 14-15 per original charge language.

Conduct: Beginning September 2018, Hernandez began messaging a male student on an unspecified app, initially about school matters. They exchanged compliments; she called him handsome. They began meeting in person around mid-October. On October 31 the student walked to her home, where they shared a tongue kiss and he touched her buttocks; her husband returned home during the visit, shook the student's hand, and went inside. She subsequently gave the student rides home daily for nearly two weeks. On November 3 the student visited again and the two spent approximately two hours watching TV and kissing on the couch; husband was not present.

Discovery: On October 31, a female classmate found the victim's account still logged in on her own phone and saw incoming messages. She brought them to police. Among the messages: Hernandez directing the student to 'jump the wall to get in and leave so it doesn't go on the camera.' A probable cause warrant was executed November 8; a search warrant for electronic devices November 27. Hernandez had deleted messages from her devices after the investigation began.

Arrested January 14, 2019 at the law offices of her attorney Richard Tanasi. Original charges: 2 counts lewdness with a child 14/15, 2 counts kidnapping, 2 counts luring. Bail ,000. Defense attorney Richard Tanasi (retained). Prosecutor Steven B. Wolfson (Clark County DA).

Plea: Guilty May 22, 2019 to Sexual Conduct Between Certain Employees or Volunteers of School and Pupil, NRS §201.540.1. Sentenced September 5, 2019 by Judge Michael Villani: 19-48 months Nevada DOC (suspended), 5 years indeterminate probation, sex offender registration, digital device monitoring conditions, 3 days credit for time served.
