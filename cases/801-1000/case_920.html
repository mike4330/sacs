Preschool teacher at Lake Bowen Baptist Church, Spartanburg County, South Carolina. Age approximately 44 at arrest; 46 at sentencing. Married at time of arrest (divorced shortly after); mother of three.

Conduct: Beginning summer 2010, Grabarkiewicz and co-defendant Sarah Jane Lindsay (Case #527) hosted parties for middle school and high school students at which they provided alcohol and marijuana to the teens and had sex with several of them. Multiple teen victims.

Arrested September 2010 along with Lindsay. Lindsay, who taught 4-year-old kindergarten at Boiling Springs Elementary, faced the additional charge of having sex with someone under 14, and was sentenced in April 2012 to serve 40 months of a 10-year prison term.

Charges: 10 counts contributing to the delinquency of a minor (corrupting a minor). Plea: Guilty to all 10 counts. Sentenced July 2, 2012: 3 years probation; 120 hours community service; no prison time.
