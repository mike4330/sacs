Primary victim was a male sophomore (approximately 15) at Crowley County High School. Two incidents of sexual intercourse: (1) approximately April 12, 2024, ~4:15 pm, before drama practice, in the back seat of a red 2011 Mazda CX7 at Conestoga Park, Ordway; (2) approximately April 19, 2024, ~11:00 pm — victim snuck out of his home, Clarke picked him up via Snapchat and drove him to his grandparents' abandoned farm near Crowley, where they had intercourse in the car.
