Teacher's aide and tutor at West De Pere High School, West De Pere, Wisconsin (Brown County). Male victim, age 17. West De Pere School District; superintendent John Zegers.

Jensen invited the student to her home, ostensibly for tutoring, citing his difficult home life. The victim admitted having sexual intercourse with Jensen two or three times at her residence. He also told police she allowed him to drink alcohol (he brought a flask; she acknowledged he may have mixed it into a sports drink without her knowledge). Jensen denied sexual contact with the victim, claiming he was allowed into her bedroom only to watch TV without his glasses, and that they sat on the couch together for movies.

Discovered when Jensen's adult children found her in bed with the boy. Daughter told police she observed Jensen and the teen "cuddling and/or spooning." Jensen was placed on administrative leave immediately.

Police separately investigated reports that Jensen had also had a 15-year-old boy to her residence; no additional charges reported in available sources.

Charged June 2016 with one felony count of child enticement–sexual contact (Wis. Stat. 948.07). Sentenced November 11, 2016: 140 days in jail, 3 years probation, 15-year sex offender registration.

Age 50 at offense. Male victim, age 17.
