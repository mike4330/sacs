Former 8th grade teacher at Renaissance School (alternative school serving grades 6–12), Warren Township Schools, Indianapolis, Indiana. Lived in Fishers, Indiana. Age 36 at sentencing (local arrest report listed 37; AP sentencing report lists 36). Mother of three. Resigned before arrest to avoid termination for poor classroom performance.

Victim was a 15-year-old male student. Relationship began in February 2008, initiated by Vasquez making sexually charged statements to the teenager. Conduct progressed to kissing and fondling in an empty classroom, then to a planned sexual encounter: Vasquez obtained permission from administrators to take the student out of school for lunch as a reward for improved performance. On May 1, 2008, she drove the student to her Fishers home where they had sexual intercourse; a female student she had also brought along stayed downstairs during the encounter.

Victim's parents learned of the relationship and contacted police. Arrested May 2008; originally charged with four counts of felony sexual misconduct with a minor (and initially with child molestation). Pleaded guilty to one count of sexual misconduct with a minor in exchange for a maximum of eight years.

Sentenced October 3, 2008 by Marion Superior Court Commissioner Stan Kroh: 2 years home detention, 4 years sex offender probation, sex offender registration. Prosecutors sought at least four years in prison. Vasquez apologized and asked for leniency.
