Former band director at Lakeland High School, Lackawanna County, Pennsylvania. Age 37 at verdict (June 2019); approximately 34 at onset of offense (2015).

Co-defendant: Nicodemo Baggetta, husband, age 30, special education teacher at Fell Charter School (different institution from victim's school).

Victim: 16-year-old female student at Lakeland High School (victim's school where Ruth worked).

Offense conduct: Nicodemo began having sex with victim at age 16. Ruth not only knew but encouraged it, telling him to "get it out of his system" before they married. Ruth was engaged to Nicodemo when abuse began. On at least one occasion Ruth and victim engaged in threesome with Nicodemo. Victim also slept in same bed as couple. Defendants texted victim approximately 80 times per day; texts described by prosecutors as "clearly inappropriate" though not sexual. Defendants claimed relationship was non-sexual and they were helping victim with mental health issues.

Discovery: Victim disclosed to therapist. Both arrested March 2018.

Trial: Jury verdict June 19, 2019. Both convicted of institutional sexual assault, endangering welfare of child, corruption of minor, furnishing alcohol to minor. Ruth additionally convicted of failure to report child abuse. Both announced intent to appeal.

Sentencing: January 14, 2020 by Judge Michael J. Barrasse. - Institutional Sexual Assault: Min 12 months, Max 36 months confinement (30 days credited) - Endangering Welfare of Child: Min 12 months, Max 24 months confinement + Min-Max 2 years probation - Corruption of Minors: Min 6 months, Max 12 months confinement - Failure to Report: Min-Max 2 years probation - Furnish Liquor to Minor: Min-Max 1 year probation

Lifetime SORNA registration. Ordered to refrain from contact with crime victims.

Defense Attorney: Jason Albert Shrive. Prosecutor: Sara Aurelio Varela, Lackawanna County DA.

NOTE: Scope edge case—Ruth scope-clear as band director at victim's school. Nicodemo worked at different school and had no institutional access to this victim independent of Ruth; his ISA conviction rests on PA §3124.2's broad "school employee" language. Female victim; threesome dynamic with couple unusual in this DB. Sentencing shows concurrent/stacked arrangement across five convictions.
