Former math teacher and sophomore girls' basketball coach at Riverton High School, Riverton, Utah (Jordan School District, Salt Lake County). Employed with the district for less than one year. Age 22 at arrest; 23 at sentencing.

Victim was a 17-year-old female student at Riverton High School. Between February and March 2013, Jarrell had sexual activity with the student on three occasions at Jarrell's home. The district received a complaint about inappropriate behavior and immediately suspended Jarrell; she submitted her resignation on April 19, 2013, the same day charges were filed.

Originally charged April 19, 2013 in 3rd District Court with object rape (first-degree felony) and forcible sexual abuse (second-degree felony). Both felony charges were dismissed as part of a plea deal. Pleaded guilty to misdemeanor sexual battery, acknowledging sexual acts with the teen three times. Surrendered teaching license as a condition of the plea.

Sentenced April 8, 2014 by Judge Bruce Lubeck (West Jordan): 18 months probation, 100 hours community service, $900 fine. No jail. Prosecution: Tyson Hamilton; defense: Kenneth Brown.

At sentencing, victim was present but did not speak. Prosecutor Hamilton stated he was impressed that Jarrell took responsibility and did not minimize events. Judge Lubeck stated he 'struggled mightily' with the punishment, noting 'You were a teacher and she was a student. It's been very troubling.' Jarrell, crying, said losing her teaching license had punished her sufficiently: 'I'll never be in a school again.'

NOTE: Female victim — same-sex offense. Original charges of object rape (first-degree felony, UT §76-5-402.2) reflect penetrative conduct; plea to misdemeanor sexual battery is a major reduction. Prosecution explicitly stated Jarrell acknowledged sexual acts on three occasions. No-prison outcome despite felony-level underlying conduct.
