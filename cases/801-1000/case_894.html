Former science teacher at Doral International Academy of Math & Science, Doral, Florida (Miami-Dade County). Age 46 at onset of offense, 47 at arrest. Defense attorney: Jude Faccidomo.

Victim was a male student in her class; contact began in May 2018 when he was 12 years old. Jimenez sent him increasingly romantic text messages over the summer ('I will go to hell for U. Die for U... My love for u is so real and huge!'). She urged him not to speak with other girls and bought him gifts including clothes, shoes, toys, and electronics. No photos were exchanged. When school resumed, she continued texting while the boy was in her class. The victim expressed no desire to engage with Jimenez. Approximately one month before arrest, Jimenez lured the student into her classroom and attempted to kiss him while grabbing his face.

Discovered when the boy's parents notified school principal Victoria Ramos-Gomez, who called police. Jimenez admitted inappropriate behavior to staff and texted the boy's mother to beg forgiveness. She was fired by the school and turned herself in December 14, 2018.

Originally charged with 2 counts lewd and lascivious conduct with a child under 16 (F.S. 800.04) and 1 count using a computer for prohibited purposes — all three nolle prosequi'd. Pled to 1 count child abuse without great bodily harm (FL §827.03, third-degree felony). Disposition: withheld adjudication, 5 years probation with special conditions (case closed November 20, 2019).

NOTE: Withheld adjudication = no formal conviction entered; probation only. No sex offender registration (§827.03 child abuse is not a qualifying sex offense in Florida). The L&L charges (which require actual sexual contact) were all dismissed — the only physical act documented was an attempted kiss. A non-contact grooming case that stopped short of consummated physical sex acts.
