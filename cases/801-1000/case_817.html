Former special education teacher at Young Men's Leadership Academy at Kennedy Middle School, Grand Prairie, Texas (Dallas County; Grand Prairie ISD). In her second year of teaching at time of arrest.

Victim: male 8th-grade student (~age 13-14). In January 2017, Goerdel sent the student a photo of herself naked in a tanning bed and asked him to draw a portrait of her. Police discovered sexually explicit text conversations between Goerdel and the student, some from third-party apps that had since been deleted. An arrest warrant alleges Goerdel 'sat on his lap in her car and they made out,' but no sex occurred. On March 11, 2017, an officer texted Goerdel from the student's phone; she responded asking him to spend the night.

Arrested March 10, 2017 after school; did not return to the classroom. Indicted by Dallas County grand jury on September 6, 2017. Actual charge per docket: TX §22.011(a)(2) — sexual assault of child, solicitation (3rd degree felony), not the §21.12 improper relationship charge described in early news coverage. Defense attorney: Varghese Benson.

Pleaded guilty March 22, 2019 before Judge Brandon Birmingham. Sentenced to 10 years TDCJ, 9 years suspended, 9 years CSCD probation; $2,000 fine plus costs; sex offender registration required. Filed motions for new trial March 26 and April 22, 2019 (denied June 5, 2019). Appeal filed April 1, 2019; conviction affirmed; appellate mandate enforced February 24, 2021.
