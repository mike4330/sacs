Crystal Burke #862 sentenced

StateOhioCourtWashington County Common Pleas Court
JudgeMark KerenyiIncident Date2020-07-01

Prison Sentence5.0 yrs

Former boys basketball and track coach at Marietta Middle School, Marietta, Ohio. Coaching contract expired at the end of the 2019–20 season; offenses occurred after she left the school. Age 40 at time of offense.

Victims were four male former basketball players, all approximately 14 years of age. One victim had not yet reached puberty at the time of the offenses. Offenses occurred July through October 2020 at Burke's home and at various locations in Washington County. Burke invited the boys over when her own children were not home, provided alcohol (Busch Light and Fireball whiskey), cash, and gifts, and had multiple sexual encounters with three of the four juveniles. The fourth was the subject of the rape count in the original indictment.

Arrested April 11, 2021 on six counts of unlawful sexual conduct with a minor; grand jury indicted April 12 on one count of rape plus eight counts of unlawful sexual conduct, involving four victims. Released on bond. Pled guilty February 2022 via bill of information to nine counts of unlawful sexual conduct with a minor (Ohio RC § 2907.04, third-degree felony — elevated from fourth degree because Burke was more than 10 years older than the victims; rape count not included in plea). Sentenced March 28, 2022 by Judge Mark Kerenyi: 60 months per count, all nine counts concurrent (5 years). Tier II sex offender. Prosecuted by Washington County Prosecutor Nicole Coil; defense attorney Rolf Baumgartel. Prosecution sought 11 years; defense requested 4–5 years.

NOTE: PSI investigator found no remorse; prosecutor stated Burke attempted to re-victimize the boys in her PSI statement, claiming she was scared of their threats and that she was taken advantage of by 14-year-olds. Judge cited multiple victims, multiple incidents, position of trust, and grooming as sentencing factors. One victim's mother testified her son had not yet started puberty and subsequently developed nightmares, panic attacks, and social anxiety.
