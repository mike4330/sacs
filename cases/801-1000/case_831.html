Teacher and cheerleading coach at Loyalton High School, Loyalton, California (Sierra County). Previously employed at Alder Creek Middle School. Full name: Emma Blance Shaffer. Age 35 at time of arrest. Has a child.

Sexual relationship with a male student at Loyalton High School. Incident date approximate (2023; relationship was ongoing prior to November 2023 discovery). On November 29, 2023, Loyalton High School's principal reported a potential sexual assault of a minor to the Sierra County Sheriff's Office. Investigation revealed evidence of a sexual relationship between Shaffer and the student.

Shaffer had developed a plan to blame the victim if discovered: she told police the victim had sexually assaulted her. The victim was initially suspected before further investigation unraveled the story. Arrest warrant issued November 30, 2023 by Sierra County Superior Court. Arrested December 1, 2023 at 3:38 PM at the SCSO Substation; booked into Nevada County Jail. Original charges: sexual intercourse with a minor, perjury, filing a false police report, and contributing to the delinquency of a minor.

Pleaded guilty to unlawful sexual intercourse with a minor and perjury. Plea deal accepted to avoid trial and protect the victim from re-exposure. Sentenced September 10, 2024 by Judge Jane York Punneo to a split sentence of 2 years: 1 year in county jail + 1 year mandatory supervision. Required to pay approximately $4,000 in fines; $2,000 perjury fine waived by judge (reserved for restitution). Restitution amount reserved pending final calculation of victim's ongoing counseling costs. Remanded immediately at sentencing; request to self-surrender Friday denied. DA Sandra Groven prosecuted.

Registered as a Tier 1 Sex Offender for 10 years; may petition for removal after 10 years if no additional offenses. Will not appear on the Megan's Law website but registration is visible to future employers. Victim's parents stated at sentencing that Shaffer received a "slap on the wrist."

NOTE: Defendant age 35 at arrest (December 2023). Victim age not reported. Incident date approximate; relationship predates November 2023 discovery. Victim-blaming false report is the distinctive element of this case — she initially had the victim treated as a suspect. sentence_years = 1.0 (1 year custody portion of split sentence; 1 year mandatory supervision is not custody).
