Former special-education teacher placed at Northgate High School in Coweta County, Georgia. Dixon was not a Coweta County School System employee in the conventional sense — she was on staff at the Burwell Psychoeducational Center in Newnan and was assigned to Northgate under a contract teaching agreement between Burwell and the school system. Married. Age 29 at the time of the offense and arrest. The known victim was a 15-year-old male former student whom Dixon had previously taught in a special-needs classroom at Northgate.

Investigators with the Coweta County Sheriff's Office said the relationship had been ongoing for several months and that Dixon and the victim had met at different locations. The case came to light when Dixon's husband saw sexting messages on her phone. He initially assumed the messages were with another adult man and confronted her; she told him the relationship was actually with a 15-year-old boy who had been her former student in the special-needs class at Northgate. Her husband reported what she had told him to law enforcement, which opened the criminal investigation. Major James Yarbrough described the discovery sequence to reporters at the time of arrest.

Dixon was arrested on May 27, 2011 and charged with child molestation. She was held without bond at the Coweta County Jail. Coweta County School System spokesperson Dean Jackson confirmed that, even though Dixon was not a system employee, the matter remained a district personnel issue but declined to comment further while the investigation was active.

Dixon subsequently pleaded guilty to a single count of sexual assault, the original child-molestation count being absorbed into the plea. She was sentenced to fifteen years probation; no prison time was imposed.
