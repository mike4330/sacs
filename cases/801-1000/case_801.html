Former social sciences teacher at Washington Union High School (Easton area), Fresno County, California. Approximately age 23 at time of offense; age 27 at sentencing.

The victim was a 15-year-old male sophomore. Two incidents of sexual intercourse and at least one incident of oral copulation occurred off-campus in January and February 2009. Diaz was working as a substitute teacher at the school at the time of the offenses and later became a full-time teacher there. She was arrested in approximately September 2011.

Originally charged with three felony counts: two counts of unlawful intercourse with a minor under 16 and one count of oral copulation with a minor under 16. She pleaded no contest to two counts of unlawful intercourse; the oral copulation count was not part of the conviction. Sentenced November 30, 2012 in Fresno County Superior Court to three years of probation and sex offender mental health treatment, plus 30 days in an adult offender work program (an alternative-to-custody community service program rather than continuous confinement). The judge ruled that Diaz would not have to register as a sex offender, which the victim's mother publicly objected to. Diaz was permitted to attend school functions if accompanied by family members. Her teaching certificate, already suspended, will now be revoked.

The now-18-year-old victim addressed the court at sentencing, told the judge he wanted the minimum punishment, said he had been the aggressor, and apologized to Diaz. The victim's mother stated her son and the entire family had suffered since discovering the relationship. Defense attorney Rick Berman, asked whether Diaz was treated differently than a male teacher would have been, replied that "reasonable people would conclude there are differences between men and women in certain circumstances." Diaz spoke only "yesses and nos" in court but submitted a letter to the judge expressing shame, guilt, remorse, and extreme embarrassment.
