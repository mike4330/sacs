Secretary at Cornerstone Christian Academy (private school, closed August 2024) and later part-time library aide at Viroqua Area School District, Viroqua, Wisconsin (Vernon County). Resident of Coon Valley (later Westby). Age 33 at time of charges.

Alleged sexual contact with an underage student over a two-year period from December 2022 to December 2024, when the victim was between 15 and 18 years old. Benson had sexual intercourse with the victim, who was enrolled at Cornerstone Christian Academy where Benson worked as a secretary.

The victim reported the relationship to Hillsboro Police in June 2025. Sexual encounters occurred at multiple locations: an Airbnb in Vernon County, a campground on County Road S, Debello Cemetery near Hillsboro, a Viroqua residence where Benson was babysitting, and in the back seat of Benson's car at a park outside Wonewoc. Encounters spanned Vernon and Sauk counties.

In a recorded phone conversation, Benson said: 'I feel like a horrible monster. I wish I would have waited. I shouldn't have done that to you. I should have taken care of you.' She initially denied meeting the victim outside school but eventually admitted to sexual contact after investigators played the recording.

Arrested July 1, 2025. Released on $5,000 signature bond. Benson had resigned from Viroqua Area School District just before the end of the 2024–25 school year.

Disposition March 26, 2026 before Court Official Timothy Gaskell. Prosecutor Angela Lynn Palmer-Fisher. Count 1: Sex assault of student by school staff (Felony H) — guilty plea, deferred prosecution/sentence. Count 2: 4th degree sexual assault (Misd A) — guilty plea, 2 years probation (sentence withheld), 4 months jail with Huber (work-release for treatment, counseling, employment). Concurrent with Count 3. Count 3: Sexual intercourse with child 16 or older, actor 19 or older (Misd A) — guilty plea, 2 years probation (sentence withheld), concurrent with Count 2.

NOTE: Non-teaching staff (secretary, then library aide). Private school (Cornerstone Christian Academy). Victim age 15–18 over the offense period. Long-term relationship (~2 years). No-prison outcome — Huber (work-release) jail only. Felony charge deferred. Multiple locations across two counties. Recorded phone conversation as key evidence.
