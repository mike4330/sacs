Former English teacher and instructor of an "Ethically Speaking" course at Jeannette High School, Jeannette, Pennsylvania (Westmoreland County). Married with two children. Age 38 at time of charged conduct.

Victim was a male student who first met Chappell in 2013 at age 14. The relationship became sexual during the 2015–2016 school year when the victim was approximately 15–16. The civil complaint alleges approximately 50 instances of sexual contact between January and March 2016, occurring in her classroom, at a hotel, in her car, and at various locations around Jeannette. Chappell covered the window into her classroom to obstruct the view; when ordered to uncover it, she refused. Other students made reports about her behavior and administrators were suspicious prior to the formal investigation.

The Jeannette School District began its investigation approximately March 2016. Chappell turned herself in April 8, 2016. Charged with institutional sexual assault, corruption of minors, and unlawful conduct with minors. Pled guilty October 31, 2018; sentenced to 6 to 23 months in jail and 4 years probation. Prosecution by Westmoreland County DA's Office.

A former student filed a federal civil suit in January 2022 against the Jeannette City School District alleging Title IX violations and negligence, claiming administrators had prior notice of the abuse and responded with deliberate indifference. Attorney Nate Foote represented the plaintiff.

NOTE: Approximately two-year gap between arrest (April 2016) and plea (October 2018). Prior-warning-ignored pattern well-documented: window-covering known to administration; student reports made before formal investigation; district ordered her to uncover windows and she refused.
