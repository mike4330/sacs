Former substitute teacher at Buckeye Local High School in Jefferson County, Ohio, employed on a one-year long-term substitute contract during the 2021-22 school year. Age 24 at arrest. The known victim was a minor student at the school; victim age and sex are not specified in available reporting and are recorded as 17 (high-school inferred estimate).

Hoover engaged in sexual conduct with the victim on nine separate occasions, all of which occurred on Buckeye Local High School grounds. Buckeye Local School District became aware of the relationship on February 2, 2022 and immediately notified the Jefferson County Sheriff's Office and Jefferson County Children Services. The district removed Hoover from the classroom and placed her on leave the same day. Interim Superintendent Scott Celestin issued the public statement on behalf of the district.

Hoover was arrested and charged on February 7, 2022 with nine counts of sexual battery under the Ohio school-employee provision, a felony classification. The case was prosecuted by Jefferson County Prosecuting Attorney Jane Hanlin. Hoover posted bond and was released pending trial.

She was found guilty on all nine counts. On March 6, 2023, the Jefferson County Court of Common Pleas sentenced her to three years of community control commencing with a six-month residential component at the Eastern Ohio Correctional Center, with an eighteen-month suspended prison reserve that would activate on revocation of community control. The court awarded one day of jail credit, designated her a Tier I sex offender, and ordered a mandatory DNA sample.
