Victim was a 17-year-old female student. Petty wrote hall passes excusing the victim from other classes so they could spend time together. She provided the victim a dedicated cell phone for communication; numerous texts professing love were exchanged. Sexual abuse occurred at Petty's home and in her car on multiple occasions (per victim's forensic interview). Petty gave the victim a 'promise ring.'

Petty was placed on administrative leave and subsequently resigned. Charged March 2020 with improper relationship between educator and student (Texas PC § 21.12, second-degree felony). Pleaded guilty May 20, 2021 before Judge Patty Maginnis; sentenced same day to 7 years 6 months TDCJ prison, confinement commencing May 21, 2021 (2 days jail credit).
