Former teacher and girls golf/softball coach, Mesa High School, Mesa Unified School District (Gilbert, AZ). Relationship with female student began 2003 when victim was 15; lasted approximately four years (victim ages 15–18). Relationship ended after the pair had a falling out, at which point the victim came forward to Gilbert police.

Arrested December 2007. Mesa USD placed Turley on paid administrative leave during proceedings; she eventually separated from the district.

Pleaded guilty July 24, 2008 to one count of sexual conduct with a minor in Maricopa County Superior Court. Victim objected to the plea agreement at the hearing. Defense attorney characterized the conduct as occurring during one month in 2003; victim stated abuse spanned ages 15 to 18.

Sentenced September 19, 2008. Received 10 years probation; no prison. Required to register as a sex offender and surrender teaching certificate. No contact with victim.

Age 38 at time of offense (43 at sentencing). Female victim, age 15 at onset.
