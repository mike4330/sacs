Rikki Salzman #871 sentenced

Also known as: Rikki Straley, Rikki Lynn Salzman

StatePennsylvaniaCourtLancaster County Court of Common Pleas
Incident Date2013-07

Prison Sentence3.0 yrs

Former youth advocate at Arborvale Manor, Millersville, Lancaster County, Pennsylvania — a supervised foster care placement home for boys ages 15 to 20 sent there by court order or state custody. Age 32 at time of offense.

Male victim age 17, a resident of the facility. Salzman engaged the victim in sexual contact on multiple occasions between July and December 2013, entirely at the facility. Administrators discovered the relationship by reviewing surveillance video recordings and terminated her. A second male resident witnessed some of the contact.

Pled guilty August 20, 2014 to institutional sexual assault, corruption of minors, and unlawful contact with a minor. Judge Howard Knisely sentenced her to 6 months house arrest, parole, and 3 years probation; no prison. Ordered to register as a sex offender for life and have no contact with the victim. Defense attorney Jeffrey Conrad; ADA Jennifer Blackledge.

Shortly after sentencing, the victim was found at Salzman's home. On March 18, 2016, West Hempfield Township police stopped a vehicle and found Salzman with the victim. While jailed awaiting the violation sentencing, she made at least 25 phone calls to the victim directly or through a third party, and wrote letters describing plans to 'get one over on the system.' ADA Karen Mansfield called her 'as manipulative as any sex offender that has come before this court.'

Sentenced July 20, 2016 for parole and probation violations: 3–9 years in state prison. Judge Knisely specifically ordered the sentence served at the state prison in Cambridge Springs — the facility farthest from the victim.

NOTE: Residential foster care setting, not a school (cf. Blanchard #36). Original 2014 sex offense sentence was no-prison (house arrest + probation); the 3-year minimum prison term resulted from the violation proceedings. Also known as Rikki Straley.
