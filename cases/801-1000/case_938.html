Computer science, business teacher, and business manager at Tidioute Community Charter School, Warren County, Pennsylvania. Age 41 at arraignment. Previously executive director of United Way of the Bradford Area (June 2011–December 2012). Teaching at Tidioute since August 2021; business manager since March 2024.

Victim was a 13-year-old male student in her computer class. The victim had suffered a family loss in October 2022; the relationship began after that. Minich added the victim on Snapchat and they began messaging. She let him spend extensive time in her classroom. She began touching him on his arms and legs and sent nude photos of herself via Snapchat. The victim became familiar with Minich's children (ages 8 and 10) and was asked to help set up a trampoline at her residence. While there, Minich grabbed him by the neck and kissed him. She then took him to her bedroom, removed his clothes, and had intercourse. The victim immediately called his mother to pick him up. Police believe the assault took place April 7, 2023, corroborated by a text from Minich to the victim's mother thanking her for allowing her son to help.

The victim later confronted Minich about molesting him and said he would not keep it secret. Minich threatened to kill herself if he told anyone, showing him a text message corroborating the threat. She offered to pay him for his silence and opened a bank account in December 2024 with $20,000–$30,000 for his use, providing him a debit card. The balance reached $45,000 at one point. Police believe Minich used the school's computer program to access the victim's date of birth and Social Security number to add him to the account.

On August 21, 2025, Warren-based state police were notified of three Childline reports. The victim was forensically interviewed at the Warren County Children's Advocacy Center on September 8, 2025. Minich was arraigned November 17, 2025 before District Judge Laura Bauer; released on $50,000 unsecured bail. Represented by Warren attorney Rob Kinnear.

Charged with statutory sexual assault (victim more than 11 years old, first-degree felony), school-intercourse with a student (third-degree felony), unlawful contact with a minor (felony), and corruption of minors (third-degree felony, two counts reduced to one at preliminary hearing). All charges bound over to court at preliminary hearing January 14, 2026. Formal arraignment set for February 12, 2026 in Court of Common Pleas.

Docket No. CP-62-CR-0000011-2026. Offense date per docket: 10/01/2022 (earlier than the April 2023 date in the complaint — the docket date likely reflects the start of the grooming conduct, not the specific assault). Seven counts at Common Pleas: one F1 statutory sexual assault, three F1 unlawful contact with minor (sexual offenses), one F3 school-intercourse with student, two F3 corruption of minors. Formal arraignment (Feb 12) cancelled. Settlement conference April 6 produced no resolution; case status moved to Awaiting Trial as of April 10, 2026. Calendar call April 20. Next scheduled hearing June 23, 2026 (Jackson Courtroom, Judge Todd A. Woodin). Arresting officer: Trooper James D. Andrews, Warren PSP.
