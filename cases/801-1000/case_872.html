Physical education teacher at Meadowview Middle School, Sparta, Wisconsin (Monroe County). Age 27 at time of offense. Also worked at the Sparta Barney Family Community Center (Boys and Girls Club).

Male victim age 17, a Sparta High School student who also worked at the Boys and Girls Club. They met through their shared work at the club, not through her school position. Sexual encounters occurred at the victim's home, in his car, and at the community center; nude photos were also exchanged. Surveillance video from the Barney Family Community Center captured them kissing, prompting a police investigation. Incidents from November 2017 through March 2018. Arrested March 2018 and released on $1,000 cash bond.

Pled guilty July 2, 2019 to three misdemeanor charges: sexual intercourse with a child 16 or older and two counts of attempted exposing genitals to a child. A fourth count — sexual assault of a child by a person who works or volunteers with children (felony, § 948.02) — was placed under a diversion agreement and dismissed upon completion of terms. Sentenced by Monroe County Judge Mark Goodman: one year jail served from home on electronic monitoring with work release, plus three years probation. DA Kevin Croninger noted that if diversion terms were violated, up to 27 months in prison remained possible.

NOTE: Victim was not Mashak's own student; relationship formed through their shared employment at the Boys and Girls Club. Offense includes non-school venue (community center). Mashak stated in court: 'I can never repair the damage I've caused and the people I've hurt in the process.'
