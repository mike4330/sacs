Former English teacher at Chester County High School in Henderson, Tennessee (Chester County), with a fourteen-year teaching tenure at the school. Age 37 at the time of the offense and arrest. The known victim was a 17-year-old male student.

The relationship arose in connection with a school play in which both Howell and the victim were involved. According to the arrest affidavit, the two became flirtatious during play activities and kissed; the victim subsequently went to Howell's home on multiple occasions, where she performed oral sex on him twice in her bedroom. When questioned by investigators, Howell admitted to the flirting and kissing but denied the allegations of oral sex.

Howell was arrested on Wednesday, December 7, 2011 by the Chester County Sheriff's Department. Sheriff Blair Weaver announced she had been charged with two counts of statutory rape by an authority figure and one count of sexual assault by an authority figure, and confirmed she had no prior criminal history with the department. Howell posted a $10,000 bond and was released the same day. Chester County Schools suspended her without pay; district officials declined to discuss whether the victim had returned to class or whether other students might be involved, but said counseling would be available to him.

On September 24, 2012 in Chester County Criminal Court, Howell pleaded guilty as charged to a single count of aggravated statutory rape; the original two statutory-rape-by-authority-figure counts and the sexual-assault-by-authority-figure count were dismissed in the negotiated plea. The aggravated statutory rape statute is penetrative by definition, so the conviction count is coded contact_type 3 even though the underlying conduct described in the affidavit was oral.
