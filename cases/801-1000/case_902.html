Former teacher at Clear Creek High School, League City, Galveston County, Texas. Age 40 at time of arrest (January 2013); age at offense approximately 39-40. Victim was a 16-year-old male student.

All sexual acts occurred inside the school, some during school hours with other students on the other side of the door. The relationship involved thousands of text messages. The two had a running joke about faking their deaths to run off to Fiji together. The student was a willing participant but was legally a minor.

Clear Creek ISD officials were notified of allegations in December 2012; Forbes resigned from the district when detectives launched an investigation. Arrested January 10, 2013, in League City on charges of sexual assault of a child and improper relationship between educator and student. Released on $20,000 bond.

Convicted at trial (verdict approximately October 16, 2015) on both counts. Sentenced to 2 years prison for improper relationship between educator and student, plus 10 years probation for sexual assault of a child. Released on bond pending appeal of both convictions. Husband Kevin Forbes publicly denied the conduct and called the evidence insufficient.

NOTE: All conduct was on-campus and during/after school hours — no off-campus component documented. Husband's public post-conviction denial is notable. Sentence structure: 2 years prison on the lesser count, probation-only on the more serious count (sexual assault).
