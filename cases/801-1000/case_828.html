Turned conversation to sexual innuendo; sent nude photographs of herself; solicited sex on multiple occasions. Suggested befriending the victim's mother to gain access to him outside of school. Picked the student up in a full-sized van with a bed in the back on multiple occasions. Admitted to having sexual intercourse with him on four separate occasions. Conduct occurred September 18–26, 2016 per prosecutors.

School leaders were informed by other students and reported the matter to authorities. Nasser initially denied any wrongdoing, then confessed.

Sentenced January 10, 2018 to 7 years in prison (the statutory minimum on the charges). Prosecuted by Commonwealth's Attorney Rusty McGuire and Assistant Commonwealth's Attorney Joshua Loren; investigated by Louisa County Sheriff's Office and Virginia Attorney General's Office.
