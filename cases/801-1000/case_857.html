Former substitute teacher at Elkhart High School in Elkhart Community Schools, Elkhart, Indiana. Hired on February 5, 2024 and placed on administrative leave on March 21, 2024 — a tenure of approximately six weeks. Age 25 at the time of the documented offense conduct. The known victim was a 16-year-old male student in her study hall.

Conduct documented in the police investigation included sexual intercourse with the victim and the transmission of nude photographs of herself to him. Investigators also recovered messages on Ringer's phone discussing a sexually transmitted infection — a corroborating data point that surfaced in the search-warrant material.

Ringer was arrested in approximately July 2024 and charged with child seduction by a child care worker. The Indiana statute distinguishes degree by physical-act type: Level 5 felony for intercourse, Level 6 felony for fondling or touching. The original Level 5 charge framing was reduced at plea to the Level 6 fondling-and-touching framing despite the documented underlying intercourse. Defense counsel Christopher Petersen was retained; the case was prosecuted by Deputy Prosecuting Attorney Kaitlynn Campoli.

On January 22, 2025, Elkhart County Superior Court Judge Teresa L. Cataldo sentenced Ringer to two years state prison, served through alternative placement at the Michiana Community Corrections residential facility rather than in the state prison system proper. The sentence carried electronic monitoring and standard sex-offender-specific conditions.
