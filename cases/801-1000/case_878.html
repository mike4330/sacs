Former English teacher at North Monterey County High School in Monterey County, California, during the 2022-23 school year. Approximately age 22 at the time of the documented offense conduct; age 25 at her January 2025 arrest. The known victim was a male student under sixteen at the time of the relationship. Yoneda also offered private tutoring services in Monterey County during the same period; the Monterey County Sheriff's Office publicly raised concerns at arrest about possible additional victims arising from those tutoring engagements.

The sexual relationship took place during the 2022-23 school year. Yoneda subsequently left North Monterey County High School in 2023 and obtained a teaching position at Woodland High School in the Woodland Joint Unified School District in Yolo County. The case opened later when the victim came forward to the Monterey County Sheriff's Office. Detectives identified Yoneda and arrested her on January 7, 2025 at Woodland High School on the first day back from the school's winter break. Woodland Joint USD placed her on administrative leave the same day.

Yoneda was originally booked on sixteen felony counts spanning seven distinct California Penal Code provisions: arranging a meeting with a minor for lewd purposes, contacting a minor to commit a sexual offense, unlawful sexual intercourse with a minor, lewd act on a child (the ten-or-more-years-older provision for a victim aged 14 or 15), sodomy with a minor, oral copulation with a minor, and procuring a minor for a sex act. She was held in lieu of $1,500,000 bail. The case was prosecuted by the District Attorney's Office, with Assistant District Attorney Lana Nassoura speaking publicly at arrest.

Yoneda pleaded guilty to five felony counts of unlawful sexual intercourse with a minor under sixteen. The other six statutes — covering the more serious lewd-act, sodomy, oral-copulation, procurement, and contact-and-arrangement charges — were all dismissed in the plea agreement. On October 29, 2025, the court sentenced her to one year in county jail, two years of felony probation, a ten-year criminal protective order with respect to the victim, and ten-year sex-offender registration.
