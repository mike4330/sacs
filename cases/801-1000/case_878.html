Former English teacher at North Monterey County High School, Monterey County, California, during the 2022–23 school year. Age approximately 22 at time of offense (age 25 at arrest). Victim was a male student under 16.

Engaged in a sexual relationship with the student during the 2022–23 school year. Also provided private tutoring services in Monterey County during this period; the sheriff's department raised concerns about possible additional victims from those tutoring engagements. Left North Monterey County HS in 2023 and obtained a teaching position at Woodland High School, Woodland (Yolo County).

Investigation initiated when the victim came forward to the Monterey County Sheriff's Office. Detectives identified Yoneda and arrested her on January 7, 2025 at Woodland High School on the first day back from winter break; she was placed on administrative leave by Woodland Joint Unified School District. Originally booked on 16 felony counts: PC 288.4 (arranging to meet minor for lewd purpose), PC 288.3 (contacting minor to commit sex offense), PC 261.5(a) (unlawful sexual intercourse with minor), PC 288(c)(1) (lewd act with 14–15yo, 10+ year age gap), PC 286(b)(1) (sodomy with minor), PC 287 (oral copulation with minor), PC 266(j) (procuring minor for sex act). Held on $1,500,000 bail.

Pleaded guilty to five felony counts of unlawful sexual intercourse with a minor under 16 (PC § 261.5). Sentenced October 29, 2025: 1 year county jail, 2 years felony probation, 10-year criminal protective order for victim, 10-year sex offender registration. Case prosecuted by Santa Cruz County District Attorney's Office (Assistant DA Lana Nassoura was spokesperson at arrest).
