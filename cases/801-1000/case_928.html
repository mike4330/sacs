Former teacher's aide at Reagan County High School, Reagan County Independent School District, Big Lake, Texas (Reagan County). Received educational aide certificate in August 2024. Age 31 at arrest.

Accused of having sexual relationships with multiple male students. Reagan County ISD Superintendent Eric Hallmark learned of the allegations from the high school principal, who had heard talk about Shook engaging in sexual relations with several male students. The superintendent reported the alleged misconduct to the Reagan County Sheriff's Office on January 13, 2026. Investigators questioned current and former students. One confirmed having sex with Shook but could not confirm if he was still a student at the time. Shook allegedly told investigators she had sexual relationships with at least two students after they had graduated.

Fired by the district on January 21, 2026 for violating board policies related to inappropriate communications and possible unprofessional personal relationships with students. Arrested January 27, 2026 by the Reagan County Sheriff's Office. Charged with improper relationship between educator and student (Texas PC § 21.12, second-degree felony, 2–20 years).

NOTE: Non-teaching staff (teacher's aide). Multiple victims alleged. Victim ages not reported. Shook's claim that relationships occurred post-graduation may be a defense strategy — the charge requires the victim to be enrolled or the defendant to be employed at time of contact. § 21.12 applies to educational aides, not just credentialed teachers.
