Former teacher's aide in the Mooresville School System, Mooresville, Indiana (Morgan County). Dismissed from the school before charges were filed. Age 38 at sentencing (August 2016); approximately 36 at time of offense (before March 2015).

Multiple male victims across multiple incidents. Youngest victim was 13 years old (14 by time of August 2015 arrest). Key victim contacts:

Youngest victim (13-14): Met on Instagram/Snapchat. Moore sent him nude pictures 3-4 times via Snapchat and Instagram starting April 2015. He had three physical contact incidents: (1) she picked him up, drove around looking for a 'good spot,' returned to his driveway and touched him sexually; (2) invited him to her bedroom, undressed both, and performed sexual acts on him; (3) picked him up from baseball practice, drove to the back of Newby Elementary School, and made sexual contact with him.

Two 17-year-old boys (June 2015): Met one on Instagram, arranged for him and a 17-year-old friend to come to her house; performed sexual acts on both.

14-year-old friend of youngest victim: Sent upwards of 15 nude pictures via Snapchat after he flirted with her on Instagram.

16-year-old from Tri-West High School: Alleged sexual involvement per a friend of Moore who witnessed her showing shirtless photos to high school boys.

Police indicated additional victims were likely. Forensic inspection of Moore's phone revealed nude pictures and explicit sexual content involving Moore.

Arrested August 5, 2015. Prosecutor Steve Sonnega. Originally charged with 3 counts sexual misconduct with a minor and 3 counts dissemination of matter harmful to minors. Pleaded guilty August 24, 2016 to 1 count sexual misconduct with a minor (level 5 felony) and 3 counts dissemination of matter harmful to minors (level 6 felonies). Two additional sexual misconduct counts (1 level 4 felony, 1 level 5 felony) dismissed. Defense presented mental health mitigation; victims' mothers rejected the mental condition argument as 'sounding like an excuse.'

Sentenced August 24, 2016 by Judge Pete Foley to 3 years on the sexual misconduct count with 1 year suspended; 2 years to serve, with at least 18 months before good-time credit applies. One year home detention to follow. Dissemination counts: 1 year each, concurrent. Sex offender registration required upon release.

Post-sentencing: In June 2017 (approximately 10 months into a 2-year sentence), Indiana DOC filed a notice that Moore was ineligible for the Community Transition Program. Judge Foley overrode DOC and granted CTP placement on July 11, 2017. Within two months, Moore violated sex offender residence conditions — her address was too close to the victims' address. Probation petitioned to move her from home detention back to Morgan County Jail on September 1, 2017. At hearing September 7, Judge Foley found the violation but gave her 20 days to find a compliant address. She produced one within a day and was released from jail back to CTP on September 8. Defense attorney Kevin Karimi represented her at the violation hearing.

NOTE: Multiple victims across multiple venues including her home, victims' driveways, a car, and the back of Newby Elementary School (on-campus incident). Level 4 felony charge (sexual intercourse/other sexual conduct) dismissed in plea; conviction is for level 5 (sexual touching only). Victims' mothers spoke at sentencing; three boys named in probable cause affidavit. Judge Foley's override of DOC's CTP ineligibility determination is notable — the prison system itself said she should not be released early, and the judge did it anyway. Effective time served was approximately 11 months of a 2-year sentence.
