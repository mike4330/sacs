Language arts teacher and assistant cheerleading coach at Coahulla Creek High School, Cohutta, Georgia (Whitfield County). Former girls cross country coach. Three-time nominee for local teaching awards; won 2010 "Best of the Best" teaching award. Age 32 at time of arrest.

Ongoing sexual relationship with an 18-year-old student. Relationship was described by defense as consensual; Georgia law prohibits sexual relationships between persons with supervisory or disciplinary authority and those under their charge regardless of age or consent. Scroggs admitted the activity to school administrators; an anonymous tip led to her arrest. Incident date approximate (2014); no specific start date reported.

Arrested January 21, 2015 and held without bond. Indicted March 2015 on felony charge of sexual assault by a person with supervisory or disciplinary authority. Jury selection had been set for August 31, 2015. Pleaded guilty August 28, 2015 under the Georgia First Offender Act.

Sentenced to 10 years probation. No jail time, no sex offender registration required. Prohibited from working or volunteering in a supervisory role with anyone under age 16. If probation is completed successfully, no conviction is entered on her record under the First Offender Act.
