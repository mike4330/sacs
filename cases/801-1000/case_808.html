Former foreign language (Spanish) teacher at Graves County High School, Graves County Schools, Mayfield, Kentucky (Graves County). Age 38 at time of arrest. Resided in Hickory, Kentucky.

Victim was a teenage male exchange student at Graves County High School. Age not confirmed in available sources. According to the arrest citation, the victim reported kissing Noles on three occasions and touching her sexually once — conduct that is non-penetrative contact (contact_type=1 on the sexual abuse charge). Noles also used an electronic device (phone) to communicate with the student in furtherance of the relationship and to tip him off after being told to cease contact.

Principal R.B. Mays learned of the alleged relationship on November 1, 2011 and instructed Noles to have no further contact with the student. After receiving a no-contact instruction, Noles texted the student to alert him of the investigation — forming the basis for the evidence-tampering charge. Kentucky State Police charged Noles with sexual abuse first degree on November 3, 2011. She was suspended that day and terminated November 22, 2011 for ethics violations, conduct unbecoming a teacher, and insubordination. Noles waived her preliminary hearing December 7, 2011.

A Graves County grand jury indicted Noles on February 28, 2012 on three felony counts: sexual abuse first degree, tampering with physical evidence, and using an electronic device to procure sexual activity from a minor. Arraignment was set for March 12. Defense: Andrew Coiner (Paducah, retained). Prosecutor: Seth Hancock.

On May 15, 2012, Noles entered a guilty plea in a pretrial diversion arrangement. The sexual abuse charge was dismissed. The tampering charge was diverted for five years. The electronic device charge was amended to unlawful transaction with a minor (KRS § 530.070) and she received a 12-month sentence conditionally discharged for two years — no incarceration. No contact with victim or family. Teaching license referred to the Education Professional Standards Board for review.
