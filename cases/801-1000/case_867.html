Former teacher at Caldwell High School (Caldwell ISD), Caldwell, Texas (Burleson County). Age 37 at time of offense. Married to Caldwell ISD athletic director and head football coach Bobby Jack Goforth; he retired following the 2018 season. Name later changed to Jamie Bellamy after the case.

Victim was a 15-year-old male student. Relationship began October 2017 and occurred off campus.

School officials notified the Burleson County Sheriff's Office February 2, 2018; investigation began immediately. Warrant issued February 9, 2018; Goforth was initially believed to have left Burleson County. Turned herself in February 11, 2018. Charged with improper relationship between educator and student (Texas PC § 21.12, felony). Released on $10,000 bond. Resigned February 15, 2018.

Pled guilty July 2020 to injury to a child (Texas PC § 22.04); sentenced to 7 years probation; teaching license surrendered.

NOTE: Original charge (improper relationship, PC § 21.12 — requires sexual contact or intercourse) reduced to injury to a child (PC § 22.04) — a non-sexual charge. Husband was the school's athletic director and football coach at the time of offense.
