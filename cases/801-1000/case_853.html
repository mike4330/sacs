Former math teacher at New Braunfels Christian Academy (private Christian school), New Braunfels, Comal County, Texas. Resident of Bulverde, Texas. Age approximately 22 at start of documented offense (April 2012); age 25 at arrest (April 2015).

Female victim; exact age not reported. Beginning around January 2012, Packwood moved into the victim's family home. The victim's family asked Packwood to care for their children and take them to school while parents were away. Packwood and the victim shared a room. An ongoing sexual relationship developed. The victim disclosed to her family on February 13, 2015. The victim's mother informed school officials, who immediately removed Packwood before formally terminating her employment in February 2015.

Investigation conducted jointly by New Braunfels PD and Bulverde PD (school in New Braunfels; victim's home in Bulverde). Packwood turned herself in to the Comal County Sheriff's Office on April 29, 2015. Charged with improper relationship between educator and student, sexual performance of a child, and sexual assault of a child.

Both charges resolved by plea bargain on May 19, 2016 before Judge Dib Waldrip (207th District Court). Defense attorney Gina Motz (retained). Improper relationship: 20 years TDCJ, confinement commencing May 27, 2016; judge specifically barred shock community supervision. Sexual assault of a child (22.011(a)(2)): deferred adjudication — 10 years CSCD probation + 300 community service hours, to begin after TDCJ release. Sexual performance charge was not filed by information and was dropped before indictment. TDCJ ID #2069756; housed at Crain Unit, Gatesville, Texas.

NOTE: Female/female case. Living arrangement — moving into the victim's home and sharing a room — is a distinctive grooming and access pattern. Offense duration: approximately 2012 through early 2015, approximately three years. The sexual assault charge docket lists offense date 07/01/2009, which predates both her employment at NBCA and the January 2012 timeline from the arrest article; the discrepancy is unexplained and may reflect a different or earlier incident, or a placeholder date. Private Christian school. Deferred adjudication on sexual assault count means if she violates probation after TDCJ release, adjudication and sentencing on that count (up to 20 additional years) would proceed.
