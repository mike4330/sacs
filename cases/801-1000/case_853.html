Former mathematics teacher at New Braunfels Christian Academy, a private Christian school in New Braunfels, Texas (Comal County). Resided in Bulverde. Age approximately 22 at the start of the documented offense conduct in early 2012; age 25 at her April 2015 arrest. The known victim was a female student at the school; her exact age was not reported in available sources, and victim age is recorded as 13 as an inferred estimate consistent with the private Christian academy's grade range and the live-in caregiving arrangement described below.

The case has an unusual access pattern. Beginning in approximately January 2012, Packwood moved into the victim's family home. The victim's family asked Packwood to care for their children and take them to school while the parents were away from the home, and during that arrangement Packwood and the victim shared a bedroom. An ongoing sexual relationship developed and continued for approximately three years.

The victim disclosed the relationship to her family on February 13, 2015. The victim's mother promptly informed school officials, who removed Packwood from her teaching role and formally terminated her employment in February 2015. The criminal investigation was conducted jointly by the New Braunfels Police Department (the school's jurisdiction) and the Bulverde Police Department (the victim's home jurisdiction). Packwood turned herself in to the Comal County Sheriff's Office on April 29, 2015. She was charged with improper relationship between an educator and a student, sexual performance of a child, and sexual assault of a child. The sexual-performance count was not ultimately filed by information and was dropped before indictment.

The remaining charges resolved by plea bargain on May 19, 2016 before 207th District Court Judge Dib Waldrip. Defense counsel Gina Motz was retained. Judge Waldrip imposed a twenty-year state prison sentence on the improper-relationship count, with confinement commencing May 27, 2016, and specifically barred any shock community supervision — meaning Packwood would not be eligible for early release back to probation as a sentence-modification mechanism. On the sexual-assault count the court entered a deferred-adjudication order: ten years of probation plus 300 hours of community service, structured to begin only after Packwood's release from the twenty-year prison term. She was committed to the Crain Unit at Gatesville, Texas.

NOTE: Female-female case. The live-in caregiver pattern — moving into the victim's family home, sharing a bedroom with the victim, taking the children to school in the parents' absence — is among the most distinctive grooming-and-access patterns in the corpus, qualitatively different from the typical classroom-only or off-campus-meeting structures. Three-year offense duration. The deferred-adjudication structure on the sexual-assault count means that if Packwood violates probation after release from the twenty-year prison term, that count could be adjudicated and an additional sentence (up to twenty further years) imposed. The twenty-year imposed term plus the explicit shock-supervision bar makes this one of the longer effective Texas educator-student sentences in the corpus. The docket's recorded July 1, 2009 offense date on the sexual-assault count predates both Packwood's school employment and the 2012 cohabitation timeline; the discrepancy is unexplained in available reporting and may reflect a separate earlier incident or a placeholder date in the records.
