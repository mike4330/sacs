Eighth-grade teacher at Connellsville Area Middle School, Connellsville, Pennsylvania. Age 29 at time of offense.

Victim was a 14-year-old male student who had been in Mongell's eighth-grade class during the 2024–25 school year. Conduct included exchanging nude photographs over Snapchat, Instagram, and cellphone text messages. Mongell sent the student a fully nude photograph, found on his cellphone. She demanded messages be set to disappear, and told the student "No texting me for 9 years," referencing his age.

Pennsylvania State Police Computer Crimes Unit opened an investigation September 11, 2025 following a NCMEC cyber tip about communications between an account associated with Mongell and one used by the student. Pennsylvania Attorney General issued a subpoena to obtain account data. Mongell admitted to sending the nude photograph.

Arraigned September 12, 2025; charged with unlawful contact with a minor (18 Pa.C.S. § 6318, felony, up to 7 years). Held at Fayette County Jail on $40,000 bail. Next court date September 24, 2025. Placed on administrative leave by Connellsville Area School District. Prosecution by Fayette County DA Mike Aubele.
