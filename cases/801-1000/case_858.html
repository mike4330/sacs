Former teacher at Central DeWitt High School (Central DeWitt Community School District), DeWitt, Iowa. Age 28 at time of offense.

Victim was a 17-year-old male student. Contact began early 2015 (second semester of victim's junior year) via Snapchat, Twitter, text message, and school email, continuing through August 2016. Communications were sexually explicit; both parties exchanged nude photographs and explicit videos. Hinkle came to the student's home on one occasion to meet but departed without physical contact when the household was disturbed.

Arrested February 2, 2018. Charged with sexual exploitation by a school employee (Class D felony, Iowa Code § 709.15). Pled guilty to sexual exploitation by a school employee (aggravated misdemeanor); the plea agreement stipulated the offense date as March 24–August 2016, when the victim had turned 18, enabling suspended-sentence eligibility under Iowa Code § 907.3. The Class D felony was dismissed. Sentenced February 7, 2019 by District Associate Judge Mark Fowler: 2-year sentence suspended; 2 years supervised probation; $625 fine; sex offender treatment program ($1,500 fee); sex offender registration; no-contact order. Prosecuted by Assistant Clinton County Attorney Ross Barlow.

NOTE: Plea stipulation moved the offense date window to when the victim was 18 specifically to unlock § 907.3 suspended-sentence eligibility — preventing any prison exposure despite conduct that began when the victim was 16–17. A journal recovered in the search warrant documented Hinkle's awareness the relationship was illegal ("17 years, illegal"). Physical contact was apparently never consummated despite repeated planning. Analytically a dual-downgrade: felony → misdemeanor and minor-victim conduct reframed as adult-victim.
