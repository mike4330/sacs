Former freshman English teacher at Bethel High School in Bethel, Oklahoma (Pottawatomie County). Beginning her third year of teaching at the time of the offense. Age 40 at the time of the documented offense conduct. The known victim was a 16-year-old male student whom Hickman had taught during the 2011-12 school year.

Between May 1 and August 21, 2012, Hickman sent the victim sexually explicit text messages and nude photographs of herself from her cellphone. On approximately July 28, 2012, the two had sexual intercourse and oral sex at the victim's home. The victim reported the encounter as having occurred only once.

On August 21, 2012, the victim and his mother went to the Pottawatomie County Sheriff's Office to report a sexual assault. Authorities served a search warrant at Hickman's home the next day and arrested her on August 22, 2012. One of the nude photographs Hickman had sent to the victim, showing her face, was recovered and identified during the investigation. Hickman had resigned from Bethel High School on August 21, the day before her arrest.

She was originally charged with second-degree rape, forcible sodomy, and soliciting sexual conduct with a minor by use of technology. The preliminary hearing was conducted before Special District Judge Dawson Engle in January 2013. In March 2014 Hickman pleaded guilty to forcible sodomy and to soliciting sexual conduct or communication with a minor by use of technology; the second-degree rape charge was dismissed pursuant to the plea.

On May 9, 2014, the court imposed a twenty-year suspended sentence on the forcible-sodomy count and a ten-year suspended sentence on the technology-solicitation count. No active incarceration was imposed. Additional conditions included fines and court costs, 200 hours of community service, two years of sex-offender treatment, and a written apology letter to the victim.

NOTE: Sentence_years recorded at zero — both sentences were fully suspended. The dismissal of the second-degree rape charge means only the oral-sex conduct (sodomy) and the electronic-solicitation conduct were adjudicated in the conviction record. Oklahoma's forcible-sodomy statute applied here through the teacher-student provision under which a student cannot legally consent regardless of age — the statutory framework does the work of converting consensual conduct between an educator and a student into the felony framing. The conduct occurred at the victim's home rather than on school grounds.
