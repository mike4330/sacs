Former math teacher at Space Center Intermediate School (Clear Creek ISD), Texas. Age approximately 27–28 at time of first offense (August 2018); age 29 at arrest. Married at time of arrest. Had worked at the school since 2016.

Two male victims. Victim 1, age 14: a former 8th grade student who had moved to 9th grade. McFerrin obtained his Snapchat username and in August 2018 began messaging him that she missed him. The relationship escalated to exchanging nude photographs via Snapchat and sexual intercourse in her truck at least 15 times. McFerrin purchased the victim a computer, reportedly to keep the relationship quiet. She also confided in a female student about the relationship. Victim 2, age 13: McFerrin invited him to her classroom and performed oral sex on him there. She subsequently texted him that 'next time we should kiss.' The victim had nude photographs of McFerrin on his phone.

Investigators were contacted February 11, 2020 about an inappropriate relationship between McFerrin and a former student. McFerrin resigned during the initial interview. Arrested March 3, 2020 (Galveston County Sheriff, Offense No. 2000000571); initial bond $35,000 per count ($70,000 total across two charges). Emergency protection order issued. Bond revoked November 9, 2021 for condition violation; rebonded at $70,000 on December 8, 2021. Grand jury indictment returned July 22, 2020. Charged with aggravated sexual assault of a child (F1, victim under 14) and sexual assault of a child (F2, victim 14–17). Defense attorney Chris Tritico; ADA Janna Oswald prosecuted for the Harris County DA Child Abuse Division. Superintendent Greg Smith informed parents and referred the case to the State Board of Educator Certification. Protective order granted February 26, 2021.

Entered guilty plea on September 1, 2022 (no jury); convicted of aggravated sexual assault of a child (F1). Sentenced to 15 years TDC. The sexual assault of a child count (victim 1, age 14) does not appear on this docket — likely resolved separately or dismissed as part of plea.
