Former teacher at Thomas E. Mathews School (T.E. Matthews School), Marysville, California (Yuba County) — a public school serving at-risk students. Employed October to December 2014. Age 26 at time of offense. Married; hid the relationship from her husband.

Victim was a 15-year-old male student. Lasek and the victim began texting, which evolved into a sexual relationship over approximately two months in fall 2014. Physical encounters included statutory rape (intercourse) and oral copulation. Lasek also sent the victim sexually graphic messages via Facebook and a sexual photograph. Discovery: on December 4, 2014, a para-educator overheard students gossiping about the teacher sending the victim sexually graphic messages online; the worker notified school officials, who confronted Lasek and contacted police. Lasek was placed on administrative leave, admitted the relationship to her husband, and resigned.

Arrested February 13, 2015 by Marysville Police. Released on $211,000 bail. Convicted of statutory rape (CA PC § 261.5) and oral copulation with a minor child (CA PC § 288a). Sentencing September 14, 2015 by Judge Benjamin Wirtschafter, Yuba County Superior Court.

During sentencing Lasek became emotional, said "I'm truly sorry; I'm ashamed" and attributed the relationship to depression following a miscarriage: "When this young man showed interest, I lost the pain of the baby. It made me feel better." Judge Wirtschafter acknowledged the loss but stated: "You abused a position of trust in a horrible way. This is not what a teacher should have done."

Sentenced to 180 days Yuba County Jail, 5 years probation. Allowed one week to arrange childcare before turning herself in. Warned: violation of probation could result in 4 years 8 months in state prison. Lifetime sex offender registration ordered. Fines: $600 restitution, $1,140 sex offender registration, $40/month probation, $370 probation report, $43 booking fee, $100 court fees.
