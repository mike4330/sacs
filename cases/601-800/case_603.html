Erin Stephens #603 sentenced

StateOhioCourtLorain County Court of Common Pleas
JudgeChristopher R. RothgeryIncident Date2019-01-01
Defendant Age27Victim Age16
Prison Sentence4.0 yrs

Former owner and instructor at ATA Karate for Kids (later Avon Lake ATA Martial Arts), Avon Lake, Lorain County, Ohio. Age 28 at time of offense (32 at original sentencing, March 2023). Note: karate studio, not a traditional school; criminal liability applied under Ohio's position-of-trust statute (ORC 2907.03(A)(9)) prohibiting sexual conduct by a coach, instructor, or person with disciplinary control over a minor.

Taught the victim from the time he was 10 years old. Abuse occurred at her studio in 2019 and at her apartment in Sheffield Lake during the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020, when the victim was 16. The victim, then 19 years old, reported the relationship to law enforcement in March 2022. Avon Lake Police investigated and Stephens was indicted July 6, 2022 on three counts of sexual battery (F3) by a Lorain County grand jury. Arrested on the indictment July 21, 2022; held until posting $10,000 bond.

A Lorain County jury convicted Stephens on all three counts of sexual battery (F3) after less than 40 minutes of deliberation. Original sentence March 15, 2023: six years in prison. Tier III sex offender — lifetime 90-day registration.

Stephens appealed. The 9th District Court of Appeals (Hensal, Sutton, Carr) rejected all claims except one: Judge Rothgery improperly commented on defense trial strategy at sentencing, saying her attorneys argued she was 'in love' with the victim only because she is a woman rather than a man. The court ordered resentencing. On January 15, 2025, Rothgery resentenced Stephens to 4 years aggregate (CT1: 2yr, CT2: 1yr, CT3: 1yr, consecutive) at ORW; 5 years mandatory post-release control; Tier III registration unchanged. Stephens indicated intent to appeal the resentencing.

NOTE: Jury verdict (not plea); defense argued she was unaware the relationship was criminal and that victim had control over her. Prosecutors noted she admitted the sexual acts — the only question was whether it was a crime. Victim-blaming defense strategy noted by court. Offense locations: studio (2019) + defendant's apartment in Sheffield Lake (2020). Sentence reduced from 6 to 4 years on resentencing.
