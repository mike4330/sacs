Dionne Marie Younce #659 sentenced

StateFloridaCourtSt. Johns County Circuit Court, 7th Circuit
JudgeMichael TraynorIncident Date2015-09-11
Defendant Age28Victim Age17
Prison Sentence1.0 yr

Former physical education teacher at Allen D. Nease High School, Ponte Vedra Beach, St. Johns County, Florida. Age 28 at time of offense.

Victim was a 17-year-old male student (one month shy of 18) at Nease High School. Communications began friendly and escalated: Younce borrowed suspenders from the victim for a costume and sent him a photo, which led to explicit exchanges on Instagram and Snapchat including descriptions of desired sexual acts and exchange of nude photos. Younce drove the victim to her Ponte Vedra Boulevard apartment on September 11, 2015 — he left a Menendez vs. Nease football game at halftime to meet her. They watched a film before having oral sex and intercourse. After the encounter she instructed him to keep silent. A second overnight encounter occurred October 2 or 3, 2015. The victim later texted friends bragging about the relationship.

The investigation began January 20, 2016 after deputies received three or four anonymous letters from students alleging inappropriate conduct with at least one student. Three students gave statements describing inappropriate messages from Younce, one reporting a mutual nude photo exchange. A search warrant for Younce's apartment yielded evidence corroborating the victim's account. The district suspended Younce in January and formally fired her in February 2016. She was arrested February 25, 2016 on $200,000 bond.

Charged with 2 counts of unlawful sexual activity with certain minors (FL § 794.05). Defense attorney Raymond Forbess and the 7th Circuit State Attorney's Office did not reach a formal plea agreement but the state dropped one count and agreed to cap incarceration at 364 days. Younce pleaded guilty to one count before Judge Michael Traynor on September 8, 2016. Prosecutor: ASA Chris Ferebee.

Sentenced to 364 days St. Johns County Jail with credit for 2 days served, reporting date September 23, 2016. Five years probation with sex offender conditions. Sex offender registration required.

NOTE: Ferebee described the relationship as "certainly consensual" but argued incarceration was warranted given the teacher-student dynamic. The victim was one month short of the age of consent, making this a narrow statutory violation. Grooming involved social media escalation from costume humor to explicit content exchange before any physical contact.
