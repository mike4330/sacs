Jessica Welch Greene #665 sentenced

JudgeLori HamiltonIncident Date2016-11-01

Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former English teacher at Davie High School, Mocksville, Davie County, North Carolina. Age 27 at time of offense. Married at time of offense with a young child; husband later characterized as abusive (substance abuse, domestic violence).

Victim was a 17-year-old male student at Davie High. On November 29, 2016, students and a school resource officer observed Greene kissing the victim in the school parking lot after a basketball game. When interviewed, the victim said the relationship had begun a few weeks earlier and included meals together and a trip to the movies. He told investigators they had had intercourse on at least three occasions, one of which was in her classroom, and also had sex in her Honda parked in the driveway of a vacant house. When Greene was interrogated, she initially denied the relationship, then admitted it in an emotional state but adamantly denied intercourse; she admitted she had performed oral sex on the victim twice, not on campus. The victim's phone, seized December 7, contained sexually explicit text message screenshots and non-sexual photos of Greene. Students reported the victim bragged at school about the relationship.

Davie High principal reported the incident December 2, 2016; Greene was suspended with pay that day. Arrested December 13, 2016. Charged with 4 counts of sexual activity with a student (NC GS § 14-202.4, Class G felony). Pleaded guilty to 2 counts; 2 dismissed. Represented by Grady McClamrock Jr. Prosecutor: ADA Rob Taylor (same ADA as Davie County case #664, Shannon Handy Wood). Sentenced September 25, 2017 by Judge Lori Hamilton.

Defense mitigation: abusive marriage (husband had serious drug and alcohol problems, was physically violent, called her ugly and worthless), postpartum depression, weight loss, failure to manage diabetes, isolation. Doctor's letter stated Greene was very depressed and very unlikely to reoffend. Judge Hamilton stated Greene was "endowed with a position of trust" and that was her "real sin."

Sentence: 13-25 months suspended, 36 months probation. No incarceration. 100 hours community service within 6 months. DNA sample. 00 fine and court costs. Sex offender registration for 30 years. Sex offender treatment required. Teaching license surrendered. Prohibited from contact with victim by any means; cannot reside with anyone under 18 except her own child; no contact with school staff; cannot attend school, church, or community events involving minors.

NOTE: Cross-case: same ADA (Rob Taylor) and same court as Shannon Handy Wood #664 (Davie County, 2016-2018). Greene admitted oral sex but denied intercourse; conviction charges were for "sex act with a student" encompassing either. contact_type = 2 reflects the admitted conduct; penetrative contact (victim's account) was not adjudicated. The victim was reportedly unbothered, bragged about the relationship, was not punished by school resource officers, and was not present at sentencing — which defense counsel explicitly argued as an equity issue. Judge Hamilton declined to credit that argument.
