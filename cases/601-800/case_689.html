Former English teacher at Cherryville High School, Gaston County Schools, Cherryville, North Carolina (Gaston County). Age 41 at incident and sentencing. In a relationship with Alan Cobb, 27, at the time. Mother of two.

Victim was a 17-year-old male student. Sparrow and the victim communicated on Snapchat throughout October 2016, with the relationship escalating to flirting. On October 22, 2016, Sparrow picked the victim up at a car wash directly across the street from the Cherryville Police Department and drove him to her home, where they had sex. She allowed him to photograph her nude and told him not to tell anyone. The boy subsequently shared the nude photos with his friends; his parents discovered the relationship and alerted police. Investigators seized both Sparrow's and the victim's cell phones.

Sparrow was arrested in October 2016, charged with sexual activity with a student by a teacher (N.C.G.S. § 14-27.7). Gaston County Schools placed her on administrative leave without pay. She appeared in court December 22, 2016 for a probable cause hearing.

On May 30, 2017, Sparrow accepted a deferred prosecution agreement. Terms included surrendering her teaching license and a one-year prohibition on attending events where former students may be present. No incarceration. Under the DPA, she may never be formally convicted.

NOTE: The victim shared Sparrow's nude photos with classmates without her consent -- an unusual secondary disclosure mechanism. Deferred prosecution in NC is not a conviction; completion results in dismissal.
