Former home economics teacher at McLoud High School, McLoud, Oklahoma (Pottawatomie County). Age 43 at time of offense. McLoud is approximately 26 miles east of Oklahoma City.

At least 10 male student victims, ages 14–18. Cator solicited sex using Snapchat, sending and receiving nude photographs with at least three victims. At least one victim said she attempted to arrange a meeting for oral sex. Cator also photographed students in class and at sporting events and sent the photos to their mothers, cultivating parental relationships as a cover for her access to their sons. Cator told investigators she had first approached each victim in person at school before moving the contact to Snapchat.

McLoud Public Schools began investigating in November 2022 after a parent — alerted by her daughter — reported concerns to Principal Bryan Jennings, who contacted Superintendent Rhonda Hockenbury. The school conducted an internal investigation November 14–15, 2022. Cator signed a written statement admitting she had been sending and receiving lewd messages and nude photos with several male students, and voluntarily provided a written list of 10 victims. She told administrators she believed she had a mental illness that caused difficulty distinguishing dreams from reality and that she had been drinking excessively. She was terminated in December 2022. Investigation and documentation were transferred to the McLoud Police Department; investigating detective was Jan Montgomery.

On March 16, 2023, Cator was formally charged in Pottawatomie County District Court with three counts of soliciting sexual conduct or communication with a minor by use of technology. She was released on $15,000 bond. On April 13, 2023, she made her initial appearance and waived her preliminary hearing. She subsequently pleaded guilty to all three counts before Special Judge David Cawthon with the PSI waived. Each count received a 10-year suspended sentence (DOC suspended), to run concurrently, plus a $100 fine and $100 VCA. No prison time imposed.

The three charged counts covered the period August 1 – November 14, 2022: Count 1 involved a 17-year-old victim; Counts 2 and 3 each involved an 18-year-old victim. The broader conduct involved students ranging in age from 14 to 18.
