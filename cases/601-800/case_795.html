Former English teacher at Atlantic Coast High School, Jacksonville, Florida (Duval County). Age 22 at time of offense.

Victim was a student described as at least 12 but under 18 years of age. The offense date is listed as March 1, 2012 — five days before Reed's 23rd birthday.

Reed was arrested November 9, 2012 on two counts of sexual battery — victim 12+ but under 18, custodial authority, sodomy — oral and anal (Florida Statute § 794.011(8)(B), F1 felony each). Bond was set at $75,003 per count; she was released on recognizance November 15, 2012 with conditions including residing with parents, a 9 PM curfew, no unsupervised contact with minors, and no contact with schools.

On November 29, 2012, an information was filed reducing Count 1 to child abuse — intentional act expected to result in physical or mental injury (Florida § 827.03(1)(B), F3 felony). Count 2 (the second sexual battery count) was dropped and abandoned. Reed pleaded guilty to Count 1 in open court before Judge Charles W. Arnold. Adjudication was withheld — no formal conviction entered.

Sentence: 5 years probation; no direct or indirect contact with the victim; no unsupervised contact with children under 18; prohibited from working or volunteering where children congregate; prohibited from teaching at any school; surrender of teaching certificate; pay all victim counseling and related expenses; drug treatment evaluation; mental health evaluation. $151 crime-against-a-minor fine plus court costs.
