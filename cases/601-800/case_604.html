Former principal (from 2015) and later superintendent (from February 2019) of Windham Exempted Schools / Windham Junior/Senior High School, Windham, Portage County, Ohio. Age approximately 32 at time of primary offense (35 at sentencing, February 2020). Prior to Windham, taught English in Mahoning County and served as curriculum supervisor for Mahoning County Educational Services.

Two male victims, both Windham High School students during Amero's tenure as principal. Victim 1 (male, age 16): sexual battery in early 2017. Victim 2 (male, age 16-17): sexual misconduct during the 2015-16 school year.

Charged April 2019 following Windham Police investigation. Pleaded not guilty initially. Suspended without pay; resigned June 2019. Pleaded guilty November 2019 to 2 counts felony sexual battery (F3) and 1 count misdemeanor sexual imposition (amended down from felony sexual battery for Victim 2). Additional felony sexual battery charge and 2 counts misdemeanor intimidation of victim/witness dismissed at plea. Amero apologized at sentencing and cited mental health problems.

Sentenced February 10, 2020 by Judge Laurie J. Pittman to 10 years (5yr + 5yr consecutive, maximum allowed under state law); 6 months concurrent for misdemeanor; Tier III sex offender — lifetime 90-day registration. Amero was 8 months pregnant at sentencing; reporting to prison delayed to April 1, 2020 (baby born March 5, 2020). Appealed to Ohio's 11th District Court of Appeals in March 2020; court stayed sentence and ordered house arrest with electronic monitoring at defendant's expense. 11th District affirmed the judgment. Amero then appealed to the Ohio Supreme Court; Supreme Court denied the motion to continue the stay. Admitted to Ohio Reformatory for Women on April 17. Ohio Supreme Court subsequently denied the appeal.

NOTE: Maximum sentence on both felony counts. Multiple male victims. Pregnant at sentencing — incarceration delayed for birth. Defense argued probation was appropriate due to confession to law enforcement and mental health treatment.
