Former teacher at Graland Country Day School (private K-9 preparatory school, Hilltop neighborhood, Denver, Colorado; founded 1924, ~638 students, tuition $15,000-$16,680). DOB July 20, 1967; age approximately 39-40 at time of offense (prior school year); 40 at arrest (November 2, 2007). No longer teaching at Graland when arrested.

Victim: male, former student, no longer enrolled at time of arrest. Age and grade level not disclosed; Graland serves grades K-9 (ages approximately 5-14). Offense occurred prior to the 2007-08 school year. Police were notified only recently before the November 2007 arrest.

Arrested November 2, 2007; released on $50,000 bond. Charged with 2 counts sexual assault on a child and 1 count sexual assault on a child by a person in a position of trust. School stated it had not been contacted by law enforcement and would cooperate in any investigation; parents notified via newsletter.

Pleaded guilty February 21, 2008 to one count of sexual assault on a child by a person in a position of trust (F4, CRS 18-3-405.3). Immediate sentencing same day. Chief Deputy DA Verna Carpenter (Denver DA's office) noted the plea was supported by the victim and victim's family, who wished 'to protect their privacy and put this case behind them so that they can move on toward healing.' Sentenced to intensive supervision probation (sex offender-specific) for 10 years-to-life; sex offender registration; completion of sexual offender treatment. No prison.

NOTE: Private K-9 prep school; victim age and grade withheld throughout. Colorado '10 years-to-life' probation is an indefinite structure — minimum 10-year term with no fixed end date, standard in CO sex offender sentencing. F4 classification (rather than F3) suggests either victim was 15 or older or conduct was sexual contact rather than penetration. Delayed reporting: offense predated school year 2007-08.
