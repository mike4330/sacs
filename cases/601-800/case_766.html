Former physical education teacher and coach at Mineola High School (also worked at elementary and middle schools in the district), Mineola Independent School District, Mineola, Texas (Wood County). Approximately age 40 at onset of relationship. Husband was the principal at the school.

The victim was a 15-year-old male student. The relationship lasted approximately three years. Grooming was transactional: Stephens provided the student with a prepaid cell phone, candy, gifts, clothing, and money.

Arrested December 16, 2010 on multiple charges. Indicted March 1, 2011 in the 402nd District Court (Cause No. 21,241-2011) before Judge G. Timothy Boswell on a single count of improper relationship between educator and student, with a charged offense date of September 15, 2009. A felony arraignment with guilty plea was entered March 11, 2011, and a motion to amend the indictment was granted the same day. The state was represented by DA Jim Wheeler; defense by retained counsel Dan Wyde of Mineola. A defense motion for continuance was denied April 26, 2011, and the sentencing hearing followed the next morning.

Sentenced April 27, 2011 to 5 years in the Texas Department of Criminal Justice plus 10 years of community supervision (CSCD) with 180 hours of community service, and 180 days in the Wood County Jail beginning May 4, 2011. Conditions of probation were modified once, on March 24, 2014. Community supervision was formally discharged September 3, 2021, approximately four months after the nominal ten-year expiration.
