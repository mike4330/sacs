Former teacher's aide at Southern Elementary School, Pulaski County school system, Somerset, Kentucky. Age 38 at time of offense (39 at sentencing, January 2018).

Victim: male, age 14. Offense occurred November 21 – December 28, 2016. Victim was not a student at Southern Elementary School and the offenses did not occur on school grounds; the nature of the prior relationship between Flynn and the victim was not disclosed by police. Victim's mother discovered the relationship when she overheard her son and friends discussing it during a sleepover and went to police.

Indicted by Pulaski County grand jury in February 2017 on 4 counts third-degree rape, 2 counts sodomy, and 1 count using an electronic communications system to procure a minor to commit a sexual offense. Flynn had traveled to a cheerleading competition in Florida (for a relative, not in official school capacity) knowing she was under investigation. Arrested in Kissimmee, Florida and held at a local jail awaiting extradition to Kentucky. Suspended by Pulaski County schools when the investigation began in December 2016.

Pleaded guilty in 2017 to engaging in sexual conduct with a 14-year-old. Sentenced January 19, 2018 by Pulaski Circuit Court to 3 years in prison. Communication between Flynn and the victim was conducted via iPad and iPhone messages.

NOTE: Victim not a student at Flynn's school; offense not on school grounds — position-of-authority basis for charges derived from her school employment role. Flight to Florida (non-official trip) while under investigation; extradition required.
