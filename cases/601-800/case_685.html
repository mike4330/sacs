Jenna Tarabelsi #685 sentenced

Former career counselor at Medford Vocational Technical High School (MVTHS), Medford, Massachusetts (Middlesex County). Employed at MVTHS from approximately April 2012 to April 2015. Age 33 at conviction (October 2017); approximately 30 at time of offense (January 2014). Of Waltham, MA. Not a classroom teacher.

Victim was a 15-year-old male student at MVTHS. The victim sought out Tarabelsi for advice about problems he was having with his girlfriend. After communicating with the boy for approximately one month, Tarabelsi sexually assaulted him in a parking lot in Stoneham, Massachusetts in January 2014. Allegations came to light in April 2015 when students complained; MVTHS immediately suspended Tarabelsi and fully cooperated with the investigation. Police and DCF launched investigations.

Tarabelsi was tried before a Middlesex Superior Court jury. On October 13, 2017, she was convicted of two counts of rape of a child. Prosecutor: ADA Tom Brant; Victim Witness Advocate: Cheri MacDonald. Judge Merita Hopkins sentenced her on October 26, 2017 to 3.5 years in prison.

Tarabelsi appealed, arguing the trial judge erred in admitting text messages between her and two students (including the victim), challenging other evidentiary rulings, and contesting the prosecutor's closing argument. Her attorneys argued she was framed by a group of students who falsified text messages. The Massachusetts Appeals Court affirmed the conviction on March 19, 2019, finding the victim's testimony coupled with the text messages was sufficient evidence of authenticity, and that the prosecutor's closing argument was proper.
