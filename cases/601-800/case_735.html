Former science teacher and girls basketball coach at Norman North High School, Norman, Oklahoma (Cleveland County). Employed by Norman Public Schools since 1996, initially as girls basketball coach at Norman High School (replacing Sherri Coale). Her 1997 team went 28–0 and won the Class 6A state title. Became the first girls basketball coach at Norman North in 1998; left coaching in 2004 and continued as a science teacher. Age 45 at time of offense.

Victim was a 16-year-old female student at Norman North. One incident occurred on school grounds (reported to authorities by school personnel); additional incidents occurred at Lewallen's home during class hours. Oklahoma DA Greg Mashburn noted that while the age of consent in Oklahoma is 16, the teacher-student relationship removes consent and makes it a crime.

Lewallen was suspended with pay May 4, 2007. On June 13, 2007, she surrendered to authorities and was charged with two counts of forcible sodomy and two counts of rape by instrumentation (four counts total); released on personal recognizance bond (DA had requested $20,000 bond). Defense: Steve Stice.

On March 4, 2009, Lewallen pleaded guilty to Counts I and II. Sentenced the same date by Judge WH to 10 years suspended but 2 years in the Oklahoma Department of Corrections on each count, concurrent. Fees: $100 VCA, $150 lab fee, $60 court cost. Counts III and IV (remaining charges) dismissed at plea.
