Former program inclusion helper at North Harford High School, Harford County, Maryland. Employed January 2012; terminated February 27, 2013. Age 35 at arrest, 36 at sentencing. Mother of three. Not a classroom teacher; role involved supporting the classroom teacher, preparing instructional materials, and charting student performance data. Worked in the victim's classroom.

Victim was a 15-year-old male 10th-grader at North Harford High School. Miller began communicating with the victim outside school in December 2012. The student disclosed the relationship after investigators received a tip in early February 2013. He told investigators they had sex on multiple occasions since December 2012, and that Miller performed oral sex on him in her parked car at a Walmart parking lot. Miller also bought him cigarettes and drove him home from school. The two exchanged more than 500 texts and explicit photographs of each other; the texts and videos were later deleted. When questioned, Miller admitted to having sex with the boy and admitted to performing oral sex on him in the Walmart parking lot.

Charged February 2013 with sexual abuse of a minor, one count third-degree sex offense, and two counts fourth-degree sex offense. Miller pleaded guilty on May 20, 2013 to one count of third-degree sex offense; the other charges were not prosecuted. At sentencing on September 16, 2013, retired Harford Circuit Judge Maurice Baldwin sentenced her to 5 years with all but 1 year suspended: 4 months at the Harford County Detention Center (work release eligible), followed by 8 months home detention, and 3 years probation. As a Tier 2 sex offender, Miller is required to register for 25 years.
