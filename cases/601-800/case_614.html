Cassandra Elfers #614 sentenced

Also known as: Cassie Elfers

StateKentuckyCourtPendleton County Circuit Court
JudgeJay DelaneyIncident Date2013-01-01
Defendant Age21Victim Age13
Prison Sentence1.0 yr

Former part-time assistant volleyball and basketball coach, Sharp Middle School, Pendleton County Schools, Falmouth, Pendleton County, Kentucky. Age 21 at arrest and sentencing. Worked in a paraprofessional coaching capacity alongside her then-boyfriend, who also coached basketball at the school.

Victim: male, age 13, member of the boys basketball team. Spent time with the coaches both at school and away from school property. Defense attorney Eric Deters stated the offense involved sexual contact once but not sexual intercourse; text messages and photos were also exchanged. School administration became aware of rumors January 13, 2013 and immediately launched an investigation.

Arrested January 2013 on 1 count first-degree sexual abuse. In March 2013, two additional sexual abuse counts and 1 count distribution of obscene matter were added. Pleaded guilty July 2013 to 1 count custodial interference with a minor (significant charge reduction from 4 original counts). Sentenced September 4, 2013 by Judge Jay Delaney to 1 year in prison; defense request for probation/house arrest denied. Conditions: no contact with victim or his family; no access to Pendleton County Schools property; no unsupervised contact with anyone under 18 except her own child.

Attorney Deters anticipated Elfers would file for shock probation when eligible and would not serve the full year; actual time served unknown.

NOTE: Part-time volunteer coach, not a classroom teacher. Charge reduced to custodial interference from 1st-degree sexual abuse (3 sexual abuse + 1 distribution of obscene matter counts dismissed). Defense confirmed single incident of sexual contact, no intercourse. Incident date approximate (rumors surfaced and investigation launched January 13, 2013).
