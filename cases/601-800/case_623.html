Former substitute teacher, Eugene School District 4J, Eugene, Lane County, Oregon. Age 24 at time of offense (March 2008). Bachelor's degree from University of Oregon 2005, Master's in Teaching 2006; believed graduate of Thurston High School in Springfield. Had substituted across multiple 4J middle and high schools during 2006-07 (66 days) and 2007-08 (85.5 days total), including Churchill, Sheldon, North Eugene, and South Eugene high schools, and Kelly, Madison, Monroe, and Roosevelt middle schools.

Victim: male, age 15, middle school student and former student of Bridges. Two off-campus encounters in March 2008 involving sexual intercourse and other sexual contact.

A principal came forward with information on June 6, 2008, triggering a mandatory report by Eugene School District 4J to the Eugene Police Department. Bridges was arrested the same evening at EPD and lodged in Lane County Jail. Detectives found no additional victims. District suspended her substitute teaching privileges immediately and reported the matter to the Oregon Teacher Standards and Practices Commission.

Pleaded guilty to Rape in the Third Degree and Sodomy in the Third Degree (Oregon ORS ch. 163). Sentenced July 18, 2008: 60 days jail, 5 years probation, sex offender registration, no contact with minors without probation officer approval. Teaching license automatically revoked upon conviction. Sexual Abuse in the Third Degree charge dismissed via plea.
