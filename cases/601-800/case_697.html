Former special education teacher at Princeton High School, Princeton ISD, Princeton, Texas (Collin County). Age 30 at arrest (April 2014) and offense. Married to another teacher in the Princeton school district; the couple have a son together.

Victim was a 17-year-old male student not enrolled in special education. Portwood began harassing the student earlier in 2014 -- complimenting his appearance and brushing against him inappropriately. The student told Portwood multiple times he was not interested in a relationship, but "she persisted, and he did not know how or who to tell." On approximately March 30, 2014, Portwood called the student to her office. Once there, she pushed him against a desk, pulled down his shorts, and performed oral sex on him. Allegations surfaced when teachers overheard students discussing rumors; school officials immediately contacted law enforcement.

Portwood was arrested April 17, 2014 on a pre-indictment warrant. A Collin County grand jury indicted her on one count of improper relationship between educator/student (Tex. Penal Code § 21.12, F2) on September 30, 2014. On October 15, 2015, Portwood entered a plea of guilty before Judge Webb Biard. The court deferred adjudication of guilt and placed her on six years CSCD (community supervision), with a $500 fine and fees reduced by half. Probation fees were set at $50 per month.

Portwood moved for early termination of deferred adjudication in October 2017; the motion was denied. A second motion was granted on May 17, 2018, terminating the deferred adjudication approximately 2.5 years into the 6-year term, with no finding of guilt.
