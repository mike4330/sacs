Former 6th-grade teacher at Garden Grove Impact Academy (formerly Garden Grove Elementary School), Simi Valley, California (Ventura County). Employed by Simi Valley Unified School District since September 2004; taught 6th grade for eight years. Married mother of two.

Victim was a male 6th-grade student, approximately age 11, on campus. Offense window January 15 – February 16, 2013. Conduct included oral copulation and digital penetration, constituting substantial sexual conduct with a child under 14. School placed Brooks on administrative leave February 21 after a report of suspected inappropriate behavior; district notified police. Brooks resigned June 5, 2013 as the district was taking steps to fire her.

Arrested June 11, 2013 (surrendered to authorities). Bail set at $2 million due to flight risk concerns. Pleaded not guilty June 12; changed plea to guilty June 13. Original charges: one count lewd act on a child + one count oral copulation + three counts sexual penetration — potentially 16 years. Pleaded guilty to three counts lewd act on child under 14 (CA Penal Code § 288(a)) with substantial sexual conduct enhancement. Under the plea, maximum exposure was 12 years; Judge Kevin McGee indicated at the hearing he would sentence to six years. Sentenced September 27, 2013 to six years state prison. Prosecutor: Erin Meister. Defense attorney: Ron Bamieh. Sex offender registration required.

Defense claimed Brooks suffered a 'manic episode' triggered by repressed memories of being abused as a teenager while living in Sudan with her missionary parents.

NOTE: Victim age 11 inferred from 6th-grade enrollment. Plea to three § 288(a) counts represents significant reduction from the original five-count charging document. The $2M bail was unusually high for a first-time offender, reflecting flight-risk assessment. Defense theory of trauma-induced manic episode is unusual mitigation claim.
