Former teacher at White County Central Schools in Searcy, Arkansas (White County). Age 29 at the time of the documented offense. The known victim was a 17-year-old male student at the school; he was not a student in Loy's own classroom and his contact with her at school was limited to the hallways.

In October 2020, Loy met the victim at the Searcy Walmart Supercenter and drove him to the parking lot of the Searcy Cinema 8, where she performed oral sex on him in the back seat of her car. After the encounter, Loy used Snapchat to instruct the victim to keep silent. A screenshot recovered from his phone preserved her messages: 'Do not say shit about me…,' 'This is my career…,' 'I have kids…,' and 'You clearly aren't mature enough to handle this.'

The victim reported the incident to the Searcy Police Department on January 18, 2021, where Sergeant Joshua Chambliss took the report. He was forensically interviewed at the White County Child Safety Center the following day. Detective Laurel Sexton interviewed Loy on March 2, 2021 in the presence of her attorney; Loy denied ever meeting or speaking with the victim outside of school. The Snapchat screenshot was recovered the next day, March 3, 2021, in a court-authorized search of the victim's phone.

The White County Prosecutor's Office filed an information on July 12, 2021 charging Loy with one count of sexual assault in the first degree, an Arkansas Class A felony. Bond was set at $30,000. Through plea negotiation the case resolved to a single count of sexual assault in the second degree under the Arkansas school-employee provision (sexual contact between a school-district employee and a minor student). Prosecutor Norene Smith handled the case for the State; defense counsel Rebecca Ferguson was retained.

On October 17, 2023, Loy was sentenced before White County Circuit Court Judge Mark A. Pate to ninety-six months of probation with no incarceration; the order credited 120 days of pretrial jail time. The court required sex-offender registration, a no-contact order with the victim through the probation period, and a DNA sample. The disposition was structured to run concurrent with a separate Faulkner County matter.

On January 7, 2025, approximately fifteen months after sentencing, Loy was arrested in Mayflower (Faulkner County) on two counts of failure to register as a sex offender and one count of drug-paraphernalia possession involving methamphetamine.
