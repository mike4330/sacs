Former teacher at White County Central Schools, Searcy, Arkansas (White County). Age 29 at time of offense.

Victim was a 17-year-old male student. In October 2020, Loy drove the victim to the Searcy Cinema 8 parking lot (2933 East Race Street) — they had met at the Searcy Walmart Supercenter — where she performed oral sex on him in the back seat of her car. A Snapchat conversation screenshot recovered from the victim's phone showed Loy instructing him to keep silent: "Do not say shit about me...", "This is my career...", "I have kids...", and "You clearly aren't mature enough to handle this."

Report taken January 18, 2021 by Searcy Police Sgt. Joshua Chambliss. The victim was interviewed January 19, 2021 at the White County Child Safety Center. Det. Laurel Sexton interviewed Loy on March 2, 2021; Loy denied ever meeting the victim outside school, claiming she only saw him in hallways. The Snapchat screenshot was found during a March 3, 2021 search of the victim's phone. Information filed July 12, 2021 (Case 73CR-21-584). Bond set at $30,000.

Originally charged with Sexual Assault in the First Degree (ACA § 5-14-124(a)(1)(D), Class A Felony). Negotiated plea to Sexual Assault in the Second Degree (ACA § 5-14-125(a)(4)(A)(iv)) — employee of school district engaging in sexual contact with a minor student. Sentenced October 17, 2023 before Judge Mark A. Pate. Prosecutor: Norene Smith. Defense: Rebecca Ferguson (private). Sentence concurrent with case 43CR22-474 (Faulkner County).

Sentenced to 96 months probation, no incarceration (120 days pretrial jail credit applied). Sex offender registration required. No-contact with victim through probation. DNA sample ordered.

On January 7, 2025, Loy was arrested in Faulkner County (Mayflower, AR) on two counts of failure to register as a sex offender and one count of drug paraphernalia possession (methamphetamine). Booking number 202500074.

NOTE: The Snapchat concealment messages are atypically explicit for the record — direct threats to the victim about silence tied to her career and children.
