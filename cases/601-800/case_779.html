Former biology/science teacher at Spring High School, Spring ISD, Spring, Texas (Harris County). Age 27 at time of offense. Hired as a teacher in January 2008; had worked for Spring ISD since 2002 in a non-teaching role.

Victim was a 16-year-old male student in her biology class. Two sexual encounters occurred in March and April 2009 at Higgins' home; a third encounter occurred in the student's pickup truck parked outside her home while her three children slept inside. The student told investigators he had sex with Higgins on these occasions. Physical evidence: Higgins left a pair of panties in the student's truck, which he turned over to investigators.

Discovery: Higgins gave her cell phone to her 8-year-old son for a weekend visit with his father. Her ex-husband found text messages on the phone from a 16-year-old's number; investigators linked the texts to the victim. Higgins was placed on paid administrative leave in September 2009. After Spring ISD Police and school administration completed their investigation, the DA accepted charges on October 21, 2009: improper relationship between educator and student (TPC § 21.12) and sexual assault of a child (TPC § 22.011).

On September 27, 2010, Higgins entered a no-contest plea to one count of attempted improper relationship between educator and student. The sexual assault and original improper relationship charges were dismissed. She was sentenced to 4 years probation and a $500 fine, and was required to surrender her teaching certificate.
