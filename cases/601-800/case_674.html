Former Level 3 volunteer at East Forsyth High School, Kernersville, North Carolina (Forsyth County). Not a school employee — a background-cleared volunteer since 2013 with Level 3 clearance (permitted to accompany students on overnight trips). Age approximately 43 at time of offense.

Victim was a male student at East Forsyth High School. Between January and August 2017, Pike engaged in sexual contact with the student. Some contact was electronic: Pike sent the student a text message stating "If you want it, you have to come get it right now." The student's mother heard rumors about the relationship, confronted both her son and Pike, and both confirmed the relationship. On October 30, 2017, the Forsyth County Sheriff's Office received a CrimeStoppers tip, initiating a formal investigation. Pike was suspended as a volunteer when school officials learned of the allegations in October 2017.

Originally charged with 4 counts of sex acts with a student and 2 counts of taking indecent liberties with a student. As part of a plea agreement, ADA Pansy Glanton voluntarily dismissed the sex acts and indecent liberties charges. Pike pleaded guilty December 19, 2018 in Forsyth Superior Court to 2 counts of crimes against nature (NC GS § 14-177). Judge David Hall consolidated the two counts and imposed a suspended sentence; no active incarceration was possible because of the class of felony and the absence of a prior record.

Sentenced to 6 to 17 months suspended, 3 years supervised probation, 100 hours community service within one year. Prohibited from any Winston-Salem/Forsyth County Schools property during probation.
