Former gifted education teacher at Mossville Junior High and Elementary School, Illinois Valley Central District 321, Mossville, Peoria County, Illinois. Age 22 at time of offense and arrest; 23 at sentencing (February 2013). First year of teaching at Mossville after earning a degree from Eureka College (2011); also served as junior high scholastic bowl coach.

Victim: male, age 16; not a student of Trueblood's. Trueblood knew one of the victim's siblings. After working with the teen, their relationship grew 'more emotional' and they engaged in sexual activity on three occasions. At least one act occurred on school grounds after hours; the acts did not occur while Trueblood was acting in official capacity. Incident date: November 23, 2012 (per indictment). Trueblood remained cooperative throughout the investigation.

Arrested December 3, 2012; placed on paid administrative leave. Indicted December 23, 2012 on 2 counts aggravated criminal sexual abuse; arraigned January 3, 2013; released on $15,000 bond. Pleaded guilty February 28, 2013 (Peoria County Circuit Court, Judge Steve Kouri) to 1 count aggravated criminal sexual abuse (felony; 1 count dismissed). Sentenced: 90 days jail (all 90 days must be served per offense type; minus 2 days pre-hearing custody; voluntary report March 21, 2013) and 2 years probation. Required to register as sex offender; DNA sample required.

NOTE: Victim not a student of Trueblood's; connection through victim's sibling. At least one incident on school grounds after hours. Statute required all 90 days be served (no early release); 90/365 = 0.25 years straight time.
