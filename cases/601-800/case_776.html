Former second and third-grade teacher at Kiana School, Kiana, Alaska (Northwest Arctic Borough). Age 26. Originally from Washington state; moved to Kiana the prior summer. Buchanan was not the victim's direct teacher — she taught 2nd and 3rd grade; the victim was 16.

Victim: 16-year-old male student. The relationship began in early January 2011 with "talking and texting," per Buchanan's own account to troopers. Sexual intercourse occurred always at her residence; she told troopers it happened 15 to 20 times, while the student said approximately 8 times. The last encounter was February 13, 2011 (the student noted it started around 8:30 p.m. to finish before the 9 p.m. curfew). Buchanan left Kiana for Anchorage on February 13.

Discovered when Kiana principal Scott Warren called Alaska State Troopers and reported the relationship. Warren provided a photograph showing Buchanan and the student kissing, taken by the student on his own cell phone and apparently shared or discovered by the principal.

Arrested February 21, 2011 by Alaska State Troopers at the Alaska Airlines terminal in Kotzebue as she returned from Anchorage. She was transported to Kotzebue Regional Jail and held without bail. Represented by public defender. Superintendent Norm Eck stated the school district had initiated termination proceedings. Principal Warren made the initial report; the assistant superintendent noted district policy "worked as it should."

Guilty plea entered October 4, 2011 to one count of attempted sexual abuse of a minor in the second degree — authority figure (AS 11.41.436(a)(6)) in Kotzebue District Court. The completed offense count was dismissed by the prosecutor. Sex offender registration required. Sentence details in the judgment file (not publicly available in docket).
