Former health teacher and coach at Bessie Coleman Middle School, Cedar Hill ISD, Cedar Hill, Texas (Dallas County). Age 31 at arrest (May 2013).

Two male student victims. A 13-year-old student disclosed to another coach (while being driven home) that he and Mints had sexual contact in January 2013. When told that constituted rape, the student replied that Mints was "just a cougar who likes younger men" and said he was "one of her favorites." The boy also said Mints allowed him and other students to touch her inappropriately in her classroom. During the investigation, police identified a second victim who said Mints had picked him up from his home during spring break and brought him to her home to perform a sex act. Cedar Hill ISD placed Mints on administrative leave April 30, 2013. Police issued arrest warrants May 7; Mints was taken into custody after being stopped for a traffic violation on May 8, 2013, and booked into the DeSoto Jail (then transferred to Dallas County custody).

Charged with two counts of improper relationship between educator/student (2nd degree felony) and one count of aggravated sexual assault. Bond set at $450,000 ($150,000 per charge). Mints posted bond May 24, 2013 and was conditionally released into a special program.

Indicted July 17, 2013 (Case F-13-14126, 265th Judicial District Court). On July 27, 2015, Mints entered an agreed plea to non-adjudication of guilt (deferred adjudication) on one count of improper relationship. Sentenced to 10 years CSCD (community supervision). Jury trial waived. On February 8, 2022, the probation was dismissed and discharged with no finding of guilt -- approximately 6.5 years into the 10-year term (early termination).

Board of trustees voted unanimously June 3, 2013 to propose Mints' termination. A second teacher, Marquita Waites, was also proposed for termination for failing to report allegations about Mints and an 18-year-old student within the required 48-hour window under the Texas Family Code.
