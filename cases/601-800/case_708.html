Lynette Trotta #708 sentenced

Defendant Age32Victim Age17
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former science teacher at Archbishop Curley High School, Archdiocese of Baltimore, Baltimore, Maryland (Baltimore City). Had worked at the school since 2007. Age 33 at arrest (April 2014); approximately 32 at the time of the offense. Married to a fellow teacher at the school.

Victim was a 17-year-old male student at Archbishop Curley. In November 2013, Trotta began showing personal interest in the victim at a children's day care center connected to the school; the two began texting and emailing regularly, with communications eventually turning sexual. During December 2013 and January 2014, Trotta would pull the victim out of study hall during school hours to spend time together in her classroom; on multiple occasions they engaged in kissing and mutual fondling in the classroom. On a separate occasion during the same period, the victim left an after-school activity with Trotta without permission from his guardian; during the drive home Trotta stopped to engage in sexual activity.

The relationship came to light when a school librarian became aware of it, withheld the information for several weeks, and then disclosed it to school officials. The Archdiocese was notified on April 1, 2014; police were alerted and interviewed the victim on April 3. Trotta was arrested and charged with fourth-degree sex offense, sex abuse of a minor, and sexual solicitation of a minor. She was released April 4 on $250,000 bond. The Archdiocese suspended both Trotta and the librarian (the latter for failing to report immediately under state law).

On October 30, 2014, Trotta pleaded guilty before Baltimore City Circuit Court Judge Timothy J. Doory to one count of fourth-degree sex offense — sexual contact (Md. CR § 3-308(b)(1)), a misdemeanor. The remaining charges were dropped. Sentence: 1 year jail with 11 months and 29 days suspended (1 day time served, 10/29/2014); 3 years supervised probation.

NOTE: Private Catholic school (Archbishop Curley High School). The docket disposition is "Probation After Conviction — Guilty," a standard post-conviction sentence (not deferred adjudication).
