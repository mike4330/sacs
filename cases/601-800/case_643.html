Former para-professional (teacher's aide) at Green Bay Southwest High School (Green Bay Area Public School District), Green Bay, Wisconsin (Brown County). Age 31 at time of offense and arrest; 32 at sentencing.

Began a sexual relationship with a 16-year-old male student in mid-July 2019. Roznowski described herself as initially a mentor to the student; their meetings began near his home. Two confirmed sexual encounters, both at the victim's house and off school property. She stated she was drinking heavily and having extensive personal issues at the time. She said she could not remember the first encounter due to intoxication and described being ill during the second (August 5, 2019). Roznowski told the victim he was 'special' and 'deserved special things' and told investigators she loved him 'in the way I didn't want to see anything happen to him.' A school colleague described Roznowski as 'unusually close' with the student and noted she often escorted him through the halls; word of an inappropriate relationship had spread among school staff.

Child Protective Services received a report and notified police in August 2019. Arrested August 2019 and charged with three counts of sexual assault of a student by school staff and two counts of exposing intimate parts to a child. Pleaded no contest to one count of exposing intimate parts to a child. Sentenced September 25, 2020. Judge Donald Zuidmulder sentenced her to six months in jail and three years of probation. The judge called her actions 'appalling' and said she was 'the nightmare of every parent.' Green Bay Area Public School District placed Roznowski on unpaid administrative leave following the arrest.

NOTE: Significant charge reduction — three counts of sexual assault by school staff (penetrative conduct) reduced to one count of exposing intimate parts to a child (non-contact by statute). Actual conduct was penetrative (two sexual encounters confirmed); contact_type coded 0 per conviction charge. Conduct occurred off campus and during summer. Alcohol and personal instability cited by defendant. Victim initially denied the relationship to police, then confirmed it, characterizing it as 'every kid's dream to do a teacher.' CPS was the discovery mechanism, not school staff despite visible warning signs.
