Former teacher at Cross High School, Cross, Berkeley County, South Carolina. Age 29 at time of offense. Married with three children.

On April 11, 2014, Niemiec engaged in sexual conduct with three of her students at a house party in Berkeley County. Two victims were 17 years old; the third was 18. The conduct had been going on for several weeks prior to the school principal notifying law enforcement, according to the Berkeley County Sheriff's Office. Niemiec was placed on administrative leave when school administration learned of concerns; Berkeley School District reported the matter to law enforcement and cooperated fully.

On April 28, 2014, the sheriff's office received a formal complaint. Niemiec turned herself in on May 8, 2014. Bond was set at $21,092 with house arrest conditions. She was terminated from the Berkeley School District and barred from Cross High School campus with a no-contact order for all students.

Charged with 3 counts of sexual battery of a student (SC § 59-25-47). Two counts were felonies (victims age 17) and one was a misdemeanor (victim age 18). Sentenced November 13, 2015 to 5-year suspended sentence and 3 years probation (concurrent). No incarceration.
