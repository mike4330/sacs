Former English language arts intervention specialist at T.L. Handy Middle School, Bay City Public Schools, Michigan (Bay County). Age 29 at time of offense (DOB January 1, 1983); 31 at arraignment (January 15, 2014); 32 at sentencing (November 10, 2014). Had been with the school system for approximately three years. Married Christopher W. Raymo in August 2012 — concurrent with the start of the offense — and used the surname Raymo; maiden name Dominowski. MDOC aliases: Sarah Dominowski, Sarah Maria Raymo, Sarah Marie Dominowski, Sarah Marie Tincani.

Victim was a male student in eighth grade, between the ages of 13 and 15 during the offense period (2012–2013 school year). The relationship began in 2012 and continued into 2013. The victim testified he had sex with Raymo six to eight times, with encounters occurring both at the school and at his home. School staff became aware of possible improper contact on Monday, January 13, 2014 — initially described as a rumor — and contacted police the same day.

Raymo was arraigned January 15, 2014 by Bay County Chief District Judge Timothy J. Kelly on two counts of first-degree criminal sexual conduct (life offense). A total of six counts were eventually filed; four first-degree counts were dropped as part of the plea agreement. On September 26, 2014, Raymo pleaded guilty to two counts of third-degree criminal sexual conduct (MCL 750.520d(1)(a), person 13–15). Defense attorney: Matthew L. Reyes.

On November 10, 2014, Judge Kenneth W. Schmidt sentenced Raymo to 7 years 6 months to 15 years on each count, running concurrently. Schmidt stated that Raymo had betrayed the trust placed in her and deserved a penalty at the high end of the sentencing range. MDOC discharge date: June 10, 2024 (MDOC #947949).
