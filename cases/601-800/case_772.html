Sheral Lee Smith #772 sentenced

StateMississippiCourtRankin County Circuit Court
JudgeSamac Richardson Incident Date2008-06-01
Defendant Age37 Victim Age14
Prison Sentence7.0 yrs

Former teacher at New Summit School, a private K–12 school on Lelia Drive in northeast Jackson, Mississippi (Hinds County). Age 37 at time of arrest. The stub notes she taught at the school for only three weeks before her arrest; she was not the victim's assigned teacher.

Victim was a 14-year-old male student at the same school. Smith was not his teacher. The relationship began in the summer of 2008. Sexual intercourse occurred on at least four occasions between June 1 and August 27, 2008 — three times at Smith's Rankin County residence, once at the victim's residence. Authorities also alleged Smith gave the teen Xanax (an anti-anxiety medication), believing she had transferred a controlled substance to him.

Arrested September 17, 2008 by Rankin County Sheriff's deputies; originally charged with 5 counts of statutory rape and transfer of a controlled substance; released on $50,000 bond; fired from New Summit School. A Rankin County grand jury indicted her in December 2008 on 4 counts of statutory rape only — the DA stated the office presented only the strongest cases and declined to indict on the drug charge. Separate statutory rape and sexual battery charges were also pending in Hinds County against the same victim; their disposition is not confirmed in available sources.

Bond conditions included psychological treatment and prohibition on unsupervised contact with minors. Trial was set for July 2009, but Smith suffered a breakdown and spent approximately 7 months in the psychiatric ward at the Mississippi State Hospital in Whitfield.

On June 7, 2010, Judge Samac Richardson found Smith competent to stand trial. Smith entered a guilty plea to all 4 counts of statutory rape. She was sentenced the same day to 20 years with 13 years suspended, resulting in 7 years actual prison time. Defense attorney: Matt Baldridge. DA: Michael Guest (Madison-Rankin District Attorney).
