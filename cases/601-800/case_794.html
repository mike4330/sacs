Lisa Footer #794 sentenced

StateNew YorkCourtCanandaigua City Court / Manchester Town Court

Defendant Age44Victim Age16
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former Spanish teacher at Canandaigua Academy, Canandaigua, New York (Ontario County). Teaching at the school since 1993 (22 years as of 2012). Married with two sons. Age 44.

Victim was a 16-year-old male student, introduced to Footer through a third party in summer 2012. He was not a student in any of her classes. The relationship lasted several months.

Two separate incidents charged in two courts: (1) November 10, 2012 — Canandaigua city police found Footer engaging in sexual contact with the boy in the parking lot of the Byrne Dairy convenience store, South Main Street, Canandaigua; charged with third-degree sexual abuse, forcible touching, and endangering the welfare of a child. (2) November 16, 2012 — Ontario County sheriff's deputies charged her with an additional count of endangering the welfare of a child for sexual contact at her Manchester residence in July 2012.

Footer was placed on administrative leave immediately. The Canandaigua City School District Board of Education accepted her letter of resignation at a special meeting December 4, 2012.

In early May 2013, Footer pleaded guilty in Manchester Town Court to third-degree sexual abuse (misdemeanor) and endangering the welfare of a child (misdemeanor). On May 8, 2013, she pleaded guilty in Canandaigua City Court to an additional misdemeanor count of endangering the welfare of a child. Neither incident occurred on school grounds.

Sentenced to 3 years probation; teacher's license revoked; Level 1 sex offender registration (NY's lowest tier); $1,000 registration fee; 3-year order of protection barring contact with the victim. No jail time. Defense: Stephen Sercu (Rochester). Prosecution: Ontario County ADA Jeff Taylor.
