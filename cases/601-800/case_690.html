Former first-grade teacher at Adams County Christian School (private K-12), Natchez, Mississippi (Adams County). Age 39 at arrest (August 2014), 40 at sentencing (July 2015). Teaching credentials surrendered.

Victim was a 17-year-old female student at Adams County Christian School. The relationship occurred between January and May 2014. It was discovered when the victim's parents read text messages exchanged between the two. Adams was fired in May 2014 when school leaders learned of the incident, then arrested in August 2014 on a charge of touching a child for lustful purposes (Miss. Code § 97-5-23). An Adams County grand jury returned an indictment in November 2014.

On July 6 or 7, 2015, Adams pleaded guilty in Adams County Circuit Court to simple assault (misdemeanor), a charge reduction from the original felony. Judge Forrest "Al" Johnson sentenced her to six months suspended and one year supervised probation.

NOTE: Female victim. The charge reduction from "touching a child for lustful purposes" (felony) to "simple assault" (misdemeanor) avoided both a sex offense conviction and sex offender registration. The pairing of a first-grade teacher with a 17-year-old victim at a private K-12 school is unusual and suggests contact outside the normal classroom relationship.
