Former PE teacher and head girls basketball coach at Basalt High School, El Jebel / Eagle County, Colorado. Basalt High alumna (2004); basketball player at Fort Lewis College 2004-2008; hired as head girls basketball coach in 2010. Age approximately 25 at start of offense (fall 2011); 25-26 at sentencing (August 2012).

Victim: male, age 17, student at Basalt High School. Approximately four-month relationship involving sexual acts at Redfern's home and the student's home in unincorporated Eagle County.

Charged February 2012 with two counts of sexual assault on a child by one in a position of trust, pattern of abuse (felony). Held without bond initially in Eagle County Detention Facility. Plea agreement in May 2012: DA dropped sexual assault charges; Redfern pleaded guilty to felony contributing to the delinquency of a minor. The plea specifically avoided sex offender registration.

Before sentencing, Redfern left Colorado without permission and in June 2012 was cited by a National Park Service officer at Lake Powell, Utah for prohibited use of a weapon (firing shotguns in a campground while partying). This constituted a bond violation. She voluntarily relinquished bond on July 17, 2012 and remained in Eagle County Jail until sentencing.

Sentenced August 15, 2012 by Judge Katharine Sullivan: 180 days jail, 150 days suspended (30 days served via credit for time since July 17); 4 years supervised probation; enrollment in Sexual Offenders Management Board counseling program; 10 sessions of 12-step program within 75 days; no special events outside work and treatment; avoid drugs, alcohol, and new law violations; $50/month probation supervision fee. Bond violation case (possession of firearm in violation of criminal protection order, misdemeanor): concurrent. No sex offender registration. More serious charges related to Lake Powell incident dismissed via bond violation plea.

Redfern apologized in court to the victim, her family, the community, and teaching professionals. Judge Sullivan warned: 'If you blow it, you're looking at prison.'

NOTE: Charge reduction from sexual assault on child by position of trust (felony) to contributing to delinquency of minor (felony) is significant — avoidance of sex offender registration was explicit goal of plea. DA characterized Redfern as having 'serious issues with boundary issues.' Judge noted bond violation behavior raised question of whether Redfern 'made a mistake' or was 'prone to criminal behavior.'
