Former registrar at Port Arthur Memorial High School, Port Arthur ISD, Port Arthur, Texas (Jefferson County). Age 57 at time of offense. Not a classroom teacher — Hardy worked in the school's registration office; however, Texas Penal Code § 21.12 covers all school employees, not only teachers.

On January 8, 2012, around 8 p.m., a Port Arthur police officer found Hardy and a 17-year-old male student engaged in a sex act — Hardy performing oral sex on the student — in a car parked near the downtown seawall in a publicly accessible parking lot. The student was a football player at Memorial High School. Both were arrested: Hardy was released January 9 on a $75,000 bond; the student was charged with Class A misdemeanor public lewdness and released on a $250 bond. Hardy was no longer employed with Port Arthur ISD following her arrest.

On February 2, 2012, a Jefferson County grand jury indicted Hardy on one count of improper relationship between an educator and a student (Texas Penal Code § 21.12), a second-degree felony carrying up to 20 years in prison.

On September 17, 2012, Hardy was sentenced to six years in prison — the maximum under her plea deal — by 252nd District Court Judge Layne Walker. Hardy was taken into custody immediately after sentencing. Reporting at the time stated she would not be eligible for parole. Judge Walker had harsh words at sentencing: "Maybe you have been a really good person up to this point. I will never bat an eye at holding people like you accountable." He told Hardy that parents "trust people like you to take care of their babies" and called her conduct "despicable."
