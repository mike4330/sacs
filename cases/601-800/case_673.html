Former English teacher and cross-country coach at East Forsyth High School, Kernersville, North Carolina (Forsyth County). 15 years of teaching experience. Age 38 at time of offense.

Two victims, both 17-year-old male seniors at East Forsyth High School during fall semester 2017. Between October and December 2017, Swinson had sexual encounters with both students in her classroom. With the first victim, she performed oral sex on him after class. Months later she performed oral sex on a second student and they also had sex briefly. Swinson exchanged sexually explicit text messages and nude photographs with both students and sent at least one video.

In July 2018, a female student reported to the principal that a male friend had bragged about having sex with Swinson. The principal reported to Kernersville police, who opened an investigation July 30. When interviewed by police, Swinson confessed to the relationship with the first student, then admitted to the second. She was arrested August 21, 2018 on 50,000 bond. Kernersville police charged her with 3 counts of sexual activity with a student and 16 counts of indecent liberties with a student. Winston-Salem/Forsyth County Schools suspended her upon learning of the investigation, then dismissed her.

Pleaded guilty December 19, 2018 in Forsyth Superior Court before Judge David Hall to all 19 counts (3 counts sex act with a student, 16 counts indecent liberties with a student). Prosecutor: ADA Kia Chavious. Defense: James Quander.

Sentenced to 6 months active prison (released June 16, 2019 per jail records), followed by 3 years 6 months supervised probation. Suspended sentence: minimum 3 years 3 months to maximum 6 years 3 months. Sex offender registration for 30 years.
