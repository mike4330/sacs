Former business teacher at Morehead High School, Eden, North Carolina (Rockingham County). Teaching at the school since August 2010. Age 38 at time of arrest.

Victim was a male student under 18. On January 18, 2012, Eden Police received a tip of inappropriate sexual contact and arrested Buck the same day. She resigned from her position that day. A grand jury returned a true bill of indictment on February 6, 2012 on two counts of Sexual Activity with a Student (NCGS § 14-27.7(b), Class G felony). Bond: $15,000 secured.

On July 26, 2012, Buck pleaded guilty to both counts before Judge Richard W. Stone in Rockingham County Superior Court. Sentenced to 36 months supervised probation on each count (concurrent). Count 1 disposition: Intermediate — 30 days special probation (2 days to serve immediately under direction of PO), with the structured sentencing confinement range of 8–19 months suspended. Count 2 disposition: Community, consecutive to Count 1. Additional conditions: sex offender registration 30 years, no SBM, DNA, surrender teacher's certificate, psychological counseling, no contact with victim. Defense: Peter Donald Zellmer (retained). State: Michelle Alcon.

On August 30, 2022, Judge William A. Wood II granted Buck's motion to terminate sex offender registration.
