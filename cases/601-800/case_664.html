Former physical education teacher (and other classes as needed) at William Ellis Middle School, Davie County, North Carolina. Age 33 at time of offense. Married at time of offense (subsequently divorced).

During summer 2016, Wood sent nude and partially nude photographs of herself to at least one male former student, then 14 years old. Some photographs used Snapchat filters with flowers; some contained suggestive wording of disputed origin. Wood initially told officers the photos were intended for her husband, then admitted sending them. When asked why, she said the victim "asked for them." She also told investigators that some students threatened to report her if she did not send pictures; the ADA acknowledged there was evidence this was true for one or two students but characterized it as a poor excuse. The photos were quickly and widely redistributed. At least two male students of approximately the same age received the photos. Wood had previously run a slideshow for an ADA presenting on the dangers of exactly this type of conduct at the middle school.

Received a complaint August 26, 2016; resigned August 30, 2016. Arrested and charged September 22, 2016. Charged with: felony disseminating obscene material to a minor under 16, felony solicitation to commit a statutory sex offense, felony taking indecent liberties with a child (later listed as misdemeanor), and 3 misdemeanor counts of disseminating material harmful to a minor under 16. Under plea arrangement, sentenced on 1 felony and 2 misdemeanors; remaining charges dismissed. Represented by Miranda Mills (Raleigh). Prosecutor: ADA Rob Taylor. Sentenced October 1, 2018 by Judge Mark E. Klass.

Sentence: 5-15 months suspended, 24 months probation. No incarceration. Psychological treatment required; DNA sample ordered. Voluntarily surrendered teaching license. Banned from school events for minors unless her own children have a direct role. Probation supervision fee waived.

No physical contact between Wood and any student was established. Teen witnesses were unwilling to testify, which precluded trial. Wood had undergone a sex offender assessment rated at low risk of reoffending. By sentencing she was divorced and working in a boutique; she had joint custody of her two young children.

NOTE: No sex offender registration was ordered — consistent with the non-contact nature of the offense and NC guidelines. The blackmail/extortion dynamic (students threatening to report her if she did not comply) was partially corroborated but the ADA correctly framed Wood as the initiating actor. This is a non-contact case; contact_type = 0. The case originated from the first photo being sent consensually to a requesting student, then escalating to threat-driven distribution.
