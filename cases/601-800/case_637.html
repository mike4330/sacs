Former 10th grade teacher at Horizon Science Academy (charter school), Columbus, Ohio (Franklin County). Age approximately 27 at time of offense. Age 28 at guilty plea (February 2026).

On April 2, 2025, Columbus police were called to a home on Salado Creek Drive after the victim's aunt and legal guardian found her 15-year-old nephew — a student in Daboubi's class — in Daboubi's car. The boy admitted to police that his relationship with the teacher involved kissing and touching. In a later police interview, he described an ongoing relationship involving texting and sexual activity for a period of time. His phone contained hundreds of calls and thousands of texts between him and Daboubi. Horizon Science Academy confirmed she was no longer employed on April 21, 2025.

Indicted June 2025 on two counts of sexual battery. Pleaded guilty February 2026 to amended charges of gross sexual imposition (ORC 2907.05) and unlawful sexual conduct with a minor (ORC 2907.04). Each amended charge carries a maximum of 18 months prison. Franklin County Prosecutor's Office confirmed the plea. Franklin County Common Pleas Court recommended 5 years community control, continued counseling, surrender of teaching license, and community service. Must register as a Tier II sex offender. Sentencing hearing scheduled March 21, 2026 was continued; new date not set.

NOTE: Open case as of March 21, 2026. Court recommendation (5 years community control, no prison) is not binding; sentencing pending. Original sexual battery charges reduced at plea — GSI covers non-penetrative sexual contact; unlawful sexual conduct with a minor covers penetration under Ohio law. Victim was discovered by his aunt and legal guardian (not a parent), who found him in the defendant's car. Horizon Science Academy is a charter school network.
