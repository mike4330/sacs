Former 10th grade teacher at Horizon Science Academy (charter school), Columbus, Ohio (Franklin County). Full name Jamelah A. Daboubi. Age approximately 27 at time of offense. Age 28 at guilty plea (February 2026).

On April 2, 2025, Columbus police were called to a home on Salado Creek Drive after the victim's aunt and legal guardian found her 15-year-old nephew — a student in Daboubi's class — in Daboubi's car. The boy admitted to police that his relationship with the teacher involved kissing and touching. In a later police interview, he described an ongoing relationship involving texting and sexual activity for a period of time. His phone contained hundreds of calls and thousands of texts between him and Daboubi. Horizon Science Academy confirmed she was no longer employed on April 21, 2025.

Indicted June 2025 on two counts of sexual battery (Franklin County case 25CR2871). Pleaded guilty February 2026 to amended charges: count 1 reduced from sexual battery to gross sexual imposition (fourth-degree felony); count 2 reduced from sexual battery to unlawful sexual conduct with a minor (fourth-degree felony). Prosecutor America Andrade; defense counsel Tamas Tabor and Brian Joslyn.

Sentenced August 10, 2026 by Judge Kimberly Cocroft (joint recommendation). Disposition: five years community control with a six-month residential sanction at the Franklin County Corrections Center, plus non-residential conditions including programming, behavioral-health assessment and recommended treatment, obtain/maintain employment, stay away from the victim, no new convictions, and no possession of marijuana, illicit drugs, or medications not prescribed to her. Direct placement; risk-reduction sex-offender supervision. Must register as a Tier II sex offender. Jail-time credit of two days does not apply toward the local residential sanction.

Reserved prison term if community control is violated: 13 months on each amended count, consecutive to each other (26 months total), with five years post-release control. She was notified in writing and orally of that possible prison term and of post-release control. Fine waived; costs to be paid; not declared indigent.

NOTE: Charge reduction from sexual battery to gross sexual imposition and unlawful sexual conduct with a minor at plea. Six months continuous residential jail counts toward sentence_years (0.5); the 13-month consecutive terms are reserved for a community-control violation and are not time imposed at sentencing. Victim was discovered by his aunt and legal guardian, who found him in the defendant's car. Horizon Science Academy is a charter school network.
