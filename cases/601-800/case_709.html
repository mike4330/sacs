Former first-year teacher at Theodore High School, Mobile County Public School System, Theodore, Alabama (Mobile County). Age 26 at arrest (March 16, 2022).

Victim was a 17-year-old male student at Theodore High School. A detective testified at the April 2022 preliminary hearing that the victim stated the sexual acts took place in Evans's classroom during lunch (on-campus). The school system was made aware of the allegation on March 15, 2022 and immediately placed Evans on administrative leave and contacted police.

Evans was arrested March 16, 2022 and charged with engaging in the sex act with a student under 19 (Ala. Code § 13A-6-81). She was released March 18, 2022 after three days in custody. Defense attorney: Dennis Knizley. On April 23, 2024, Evans pleaded guilty to one count of School Employee Sex Act. She was sentenced to 15 years, all but 3 days suspended (the 3 days representing time served at the initial arrest); 2 years probation; sex offender registration.

A federal civil lawsuit was filed against Mobile County Public School System on February 23, 2024, seeking damages for alleged negligence, ahead of the guilty plea. Evans has since relocated and is a registered sex offender.
