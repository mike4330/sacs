Former math teacher at MacArthur High School, Irving Independent School District, Irving, Texas (Dallas County). Age 24 at arrest (April 2015); approximately 23 at the time of the earliest offense.

Two male student victims at MacArthur High School. Victim 1 reported that Granado kissed him and allowed him to rub her breasts in April 2014. Irving ISD police were first alerted to a possible inappropriate relationship on March 17, 2015; Granado admitted to the kissing and fondling in an interview at the Irving police station on March 18. She was arrested March 19, 2015 on a charge of improper relationship between educator and student (Tex. Penal Code § 21.12) and placed on paid administrative leave. Victim 2 was a male sophomore who reported that Granado had sexual intercourse with him in the back seat of her car on two consecutive nights — January 18 and 19, 2015 — parked in front of a residence in the 800 block of Keeneland Court in Dallas. He was interviewed by the school resource officer on March 24, 2015, shortly after the first arrest. Granado was arrested a second time on April 14, 2015 and booked into the Irving Jail on the second charge; she posted $50,000 bond.

Both cases were adjudicated together in the 203rd Judicial District Court. On April 5, 2018, Granado entered an open guilty plea to both counts of improper relationship between educator and student. The court entered non-adjudication of guilt (NAJG) on both counts and placed her on 4 years of community supervision (CSCD), concurrent, beginning April 5, 2018.
