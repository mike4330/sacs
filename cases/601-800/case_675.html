Angela Barbosa #675 sentenced

StateTexasCourtNueces County District Court
Incident Date2020-09-01
Defendant Age45Victim Age11
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former fifth-grade teacher at Galvan Elementary School, Corpus Christi ISD, Corpus Christi, Texas (Nueces County). Age 45 at time of offense and arrest.

Victim was an 11-year-old male student in her fifth-grade class. Barbosa sent nude photographs of herself to the victim and received a nude image from him in return. She admitted to investigators that she engaged in the communications knowing the victim was a minor, and confessed to sending the nude images.

Arrested November 2, 2020 by Nueces County Sheriff's Office. Placed on paid administrative leave by CCISD upon arrest; later lost her teaching license permanently. Charged with possession with intent to promote child pornography and distributing harmful material to a minor. Released on bond and remained under supervision for approximately four years while awaiting trial.

Plea deal accepted May 30, 2024. Sentenced to 10 years probation, no prison time. If probation conditions are violated, she faces up to 20 years in prison. Lifetime sex offender registration required. Prosecutor: Nueces County DA Jimmy Granberry, who cited four years of supervision and low ongoing threat assessment. Victim's mother Sapphire Rodriguez publicly stated she felt justice was not served.

NOTE: Non-contact case (contact_type = 0); no physical contact alleged. The exchange of nude images with an 11-year-old constitutes CSAM production/receipt on top of distributing harmful material. The victim's age (11) is among the youngest in this DB for a non-contact case. The 4-year delay between arrest and sentencing reflects the extended pre-trial supervision period cited by the DA.
