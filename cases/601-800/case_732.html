Former teacher at Warner Christian Academy, South Daytona/Port Orange, Florida. Married mother of four. Age 45 at time of offense (DOB 08/12/1963). Florida SOR aliases: Cynthia Chapogas Horvath, Cynthia Marie Horvath, Cynthia C Horvath. DOC #V32708.

Victim was a 17-year-old male student at Warner Christian Academy. Relationship began spring 2008; escalated over the summer. Contact included long drives, physical contact at the school, explicit text messages, sexual encounters in store parking lots, under a bridge in Port Orange, and at motels in Daytona Beach. The teen provided police with personal mementos as evidence.

South Daytona Police received information from the school about the relationship and contacted Port Orange PD. Arrest warrant signed October 22, 2008; Horvath arrested at a Walmart on Beville Road in Daytona Beach. Taken to Volusia County Branch Jail. Case number: 0835468.

Pleaded no contest to one count of Unlawful Sexual Activity with Certain Minors (FS § 794.05(1), 2nd-degree felony). Adjudication entered (SOR: "Guilty/convict"). Adjudication date February 26, 2009. Sentenced to 1 year community control (house arrest) and 7 years sex-offender probation. Required to surrender teaching license and register as sex offender. No contact with minors except own children. Defense: Aaron Delgado.

May 2009: Charged with probation violation for Facebook contact with a teenager — Port Orange police had set up a fake profile for "Steve Warren," a purported 16-year-old. Horvath accepted a friend request and chatted with the account. Chief Circuit Judge J. David Walsh dismissed the violation, ruling her activity was not "willful." Her husband testified she thought "Steve" might be a son from a prior marriage whom she had been barred from contacting since her arrest.

Florida SOR status (2026): Released — Subject to Registration; address Lake County, FL.
