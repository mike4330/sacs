Former teacher at Spain Park High School in Hoover City Schools (Hoover, Alabama, Shelby County). Married. Resided in Moody, Alabama (St. Clair County). Age 33 at the time of the documented offense conduct in spring 2012. The known victim was a 17-year-old male student; the defense argued at sentencing that the victim was actually 18 at the time.

Millard had sexual intercourse with the victim between March 9 and May 14, 2012. The school system discovered the matter and reported it to law enforcement. Millard resigned from Spain Park High School on December 13, 2012, and arrest warrants were issued shortly afterward. She surrendered at the Hoover Police Department on December 27, 2012 at 3:45 p.m. and was held on a $30,000 bond on two counts of school employee engaging in a sex act with a student. The investigation was handled by the Hoover Police Department's Family Services Division under Lieutenant Harry Long.

On December 2, 2013, Millard entered a best-interest plea — an Alford-type plea acknowledging that the State had sufficient evidence to convict without formally admitting guilt — before Shelby County Circuit Court Judge Dan Reeves to one count of school employee engaging in a sex act with a student. The second count was dismissed pursuant to the plea. Defense counsel Brett Bloomston was retained.

Judge Reeves sentenced Millard to ten years in state prison fully suspended in favor of five years of probation under state supervision, with mandatory sex-offender registration and a sex-offender treatment program. The defense had publicly characterized the Alabama school-employee statute as a "very poorly written law" for applying to a victim who was, on the defense's account, eighteen years old at the time.
