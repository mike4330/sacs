Former teacher at Spain Park High School, Hoover City Schools, Hoover, Alabama (Shelby County). Age 33 at time of offense (spring 2012). Resides in Moody, Alabama (St. Clair County).

Victim was a 17-year-old male student (defense claimed the victim was actually 18 at the time). Millard engaged in sexual intercourse with the student between March 9 and May 14, 2012. The school system discovered the matter and reported it to law enforcement; Millard resigned on December 13, 2012. Warrants were issued and she surrendered to Hoover police on December 27, 2012, held on $30,000 bond on two counts of school employee sex act (Ala. Code § 13A-6-81). Investigation conducted by Hoover PD Family Services Division.

On December 2, 2013, Millard entered a best-interest plea (Alford-type, acknowledging the state had sufficient evidence to convict) to Count 1 before Judge Dan Reeves in Shelby County Circuit Court. Count 2 was dismissed. She was sentenced to 10 years in prison, suspended, with 5 years of probation under state supervision, mandatory sex offender registration, and completion of a sex offender treatment program. Defense attorney: Brett Bloomston.

NOTE: Best-interest plea — Millard did not formally admit guilt; defense counsel challenged the victim's age (arguing he was 18, not 17) and characterized § 13A-6-81 as "a very poorly written law" for applying to an 18-year-old. contact_type = 3 (§ 13A-6-81 is specifically "sexual intercourse" — penetrative by statutory definition). sentence_years = 0 (10 years suspended; probation only).
