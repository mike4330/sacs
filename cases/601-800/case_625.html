StateTexasCourtSmith County District Court

Former elementary school counselor at Arp Elementary School, Arp Independent School District, Smith County, Texas. Age 31 at arrest (October 2017). Married.

Allegations received by Arp High School principal Shannon Arrington on October 11, 2017, from two parents and a student reporting an improper relationship with a student. Superintendent Dwight Thomas placed Orpineda on administrative leave immediately, filed a CPS report, and submitted a report to the State Board of Educator Certifications. Arrested October 25, 2017 by Arp Police Department in coordination with Smith County Sheriff's Office and the Child Advocacy Center of Smith County. Charged with 2 counts sexual assault of a child and 2 counts improper relationship between an educator and student. Released on $200,000 bond.

Trial conviction on Count 1 of sexual assault of a child. Sentenced October 11, 2018 by Judge Christi Kennedy: 5 years Texas Department of Corrections.

As of November 2018, Count 2 (sexual assault of a child) and 2 counts improper relationship between educator and student remained pending. Orpineda pleaded no contest to the improper relationship charges on October 23, 2018; Judge Kennedy rejected that plea on November 1, 2018 and gave both sides 10 days to reach agreement on how to proceed. Status hearing scheduled November 12, 2018.

NOTE: Multiple victims — Arp PD confirmed involvement with two students. Resolution of Count 2 and improper relationship counts unknown beyond November 2018 (further sources needed). The AHS principal's involvement in receiving allegations suggests at least one victim was a high school student despite Orpineda's role as elementary counselor. sentence_years = 5 per trial sentence; pending counts may add additional exposure.
