Former substitute teacher at Citrus High School, Citrus County Schools, Inverness, Florida (Citrus County). DOB November 16, 1968; age 50 at time of offense. Aliases: Angela Jean Cone, Angela J. Stanton, Angela Cone-Stanton. FDOC #W60417.

Three male student victims at Citrus High School. One 15-year-old victim: Stanton performed oral sex on him. Two additional victims received nude photographs of Stanton sent via the Snapchat mobile application. Stanton had been heavily involved in community activities working with youth in Citrus County; she met the students through those activities before her brief employment as a substitute teacher. The school principal learned of possible inappropriate activity between Stanton and a minor and contacted the Citrus County Sheriff's Office, which responded and interviewed three possible victims. When questioned by investigators, Stanton gave statements that "corroborated the specific details given by the victims."

The offenses occurred before Stanton was hired as a substitute teacher on January 3, 2019, and not on school property. She was arrested on or about January 25, 2019 (approximately 20 days after hire) and held on a $12,000 bond.

On June 19, 2019, Stanton was convicted on two counts: lewd or lascivious battery — victim 12–15 (Fla. Stat. § 800.04(4)(a)(1)) and providing obscene material to a minor (Fla. Stat. § 847.0133). Sentenced to 8 years in the Florida Department of Corrections followed by 1 year of probation; lifetime sex offender registration required. FDOC received date: April 3, 2020. As of May 2025, Stanton remains in FDOC confinement.
