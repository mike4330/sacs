Former substitute teacher at Citrus High School in the Citrus County School District (Inverness, Florida). Age 50 at the time of the documented offense conduct. Three known male student victims, all enrolled at Citrus High School. Stanton has been documented under several name variations (Angela Jean Cone, Angela Cone-Stanton, Angela J. Stanton), retained in the aliases field.

Prior to her brief stint as a substitute teacher, Stanton had been heavily involved in community activities working with youth in Citrus County, and she met the eventual victims through those activities. The institutional connection to the school was therefore established before her teacher employment: the victims were already enrolled at Citrus High and Stanton became a substitute teacher there only after the offense conduct had begun.

The documented conduct involved three victims separately. With one 15-year-old victim, Stanton performed oral sex on him. Two other victims received nude photographs of Stanton transmitted through the Snapchat application. None of the conduct occurred on school property, and the offenses pre-dated Stanton's January 3, 2019 hire as a substitute teacher.

The case opened when the school principal learned of possible inappropriate activity between Stanton and a minor and contacted the Citrus County Sheriff's Office. Detectives responded and interviewed three possible victims. When investigators questioned Stanton, her statements corroborated the specific details given by the victims — a same-day investigative-interview admission that produced corroboration rather than denial.

Stanton was arrested on or about January 25, 2019 — approximately twenty days after her substitute-teacher hire — and held on a $12,000 bond. On June 19, 2019, in the Citrus County Circuit Court, she was convicted on two counts: lewd or lascivious battery on a victim 12 to 15, and providing obscene material to a minor. The court sentenced her to eight years in state prison followed by one year of probation, with lifetime sex-offender registration. She was received into state-prison custody on April 3, 2020.
