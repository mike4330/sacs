Melissa Ann Andreini #770 sentenced

StateUtahCourt7th District Court, Carbon County
JudgeDouglas ThomasIncident Date2009-06-01
Defendant Age28Victim Age15
Prison Sentence0.33 yrs

Former special education teacher at Helper Junior High School, Helper, Utah (Carbon County). Hired fall 2006; had been teaching for three years. Age 28 at time of offense.

Victim was a 15-year-old male student (not enrolled in Andreini's classes). Three separate sexual episodes occurred at Andreini's home, approximately three blocks from the school, in June 2009. Andreini often brought coffee before the school day and gave it to the victim and his friends in her classroom. Following the three incidents, she paid the victim between $1,400 and $1,500; court documents do not specify whether the payment was for the sexual contact or to purchase silence.

The victim's mother confronted Andreini at her home in early July 2009; Andreini admitted the sexual relationship and confirmed the payment. Charges surfaced in July 2009. Andreini submitted her letter of resignation August 3, 2009.

Originally charged with three counts of unlawful sexual activity with a minor (third-degree felony, up to 5 years each). Pleaded guilty to one count; the other two were dismissed. Sentenced March 8, 2010 by 7th District Judge Douglas Thomas.

Sentence: 120 days in jail, followed by 120 days home confinement, 36 months probation, sex offender registration (Utah), DNA testing, sex offender treatment, no contact with minors, restitution for victim's counseling costs. County Attorney: Gene Strate.
