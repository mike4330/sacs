Lauren Coyle-Mitchell #620 sentenced

Also known as: Lauren Mitchell

StateNew JerseyCourtBergen County Superior Court
Incident Date2015-06-01
Defendant Age32Victim Age15
Prison Sentence5.0 yrs

Former second-grade teacher at Dr. Lena Edwards Academic Charter School, Jersey City, Hudson County, New Jersey. Age 32 at arrest (June 2015); 36 at sentencing (August 2019). Married to a firefighter; resident of Lyndhurst, Bergen County.

Victim: female, age 15, 8th grade student at Dr. Lena Edwards Academic Charter School — same school but not one of Mitchell's direct students. Relationship began with explicit text messages. Teachers at the school became suspicious during a class trip to Washington, DC. The school reported to the Hudson County Prosecutor's Office, who referred to Bergen County law enforcement (offense occurred at Mitchell's Lyndhurst home).

Arrested June 22, 2015 on charges of aggravated sexual assault and endangering the welfare of a child; held on $150,000 bail; no-contact order issued. Investigators believed at least one sexual encounter occurred at the Mitchells' home. July 18, 2015: Re-arrested for violating the no-contact order; charged with contempt of court. Mitchell had downloaded a call-blocking app and searched online for the student's phone number; the victim received calls from restricted numbers and a text message using a pet name Mitchell had for her.

Pleaded guilty January 2019 in Bergen County Superior Court to aggravated sexual assault and endangering the welfare of a child. Admitted performing oral sex on the 15-year-old at her home in 2015; characterized the affair as 'emotionally and morally damaging.' Husband present in the courtroom. Sentenced August 2, 2019: 5 years state prison; eligible for parole after 4 years 3 months (85% of sentence, NJ No Early Release Act); lifetime parole supervision; Megan's Law registration. Permanently prohibited from contact with victim and from holding any government position. NJ State Board of Examiners suspended teaching certificates May 12, 2017.

NOTE: Female victim — uncommon in this DB. Victim was at same school but in a different grade (8th vs. 2nd). Explicit texts initiated the relationship. Violation of no-contact order using call-blocking app — concealment. Husband present at plea hearing. Nearly 4-year gap from arrest to sentencing. County field reflects school county (Hudson); prosecution and offense were in Bergen County.
