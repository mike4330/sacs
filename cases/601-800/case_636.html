Former teacher at Salem Attendance Center, Walthall County, Mississippi. Age approximately 39–40 at time of offense (October 2013 – February 2014); age 40 at sentencing. Resident of Tylertown, Mississippi. Married to Chad Griffin at time of offense; Griffin filed for divorce more than a year after her incarceration and was awarded physical and legal custody of their four daughters.

Engaged in sexual activity with four students — one age 15, two age 16, and one age 17 — on multiple occasions between October 1, 2013 and February 14, 2014. Sex of victims not reported.

Pleaded guilty to four counts of sexual battery against children under 18 by a person of trust or authority (MS § 97-3-95). Sentenced to 25 years with 10 suspended and 5 years post-release supervision on each count, all concurrent. Actual time to serve: 15 years. Sentencing date approximately April 22, 2014 (AP article published April 23 reports plea 'this week').

In 2018, the Mississippi Supreme Court affirmed Chancellor Wayne Smith's ruling (Walthall County Chancery Court) allowing Chad Griffin to determine visitation for their four daughters, citing the nine-hour round trip to the prison and potential environmental harm to the children.

Post-divorce custody ruling provides a rare case record of family consequences of incarceration. Defendant age at offense is approximate — born approximately 1973–1974.
