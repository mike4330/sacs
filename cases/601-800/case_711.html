Former special education teacher at Annapolis High School, Anne Arundel County Public Schools (AACPS), Annapolis, Maryland (Anne Arundel County). Age 41 at arrest (October 1, 2021). Resides in Arnold, Maryland.

Victim was a 17-year-old male student who had attended Annapolis High School and, at some point, lived in Arnold's home — Arnold had acted as his guardian. Arnold sexually abused the victim multiple times while he was under her care. None of the incidents occurred on school property. The victim had since moved out of state by the time of the July 2021 disclosure.

Anne Arundel County Police and the Department of Social Services were notified on July 6, 2021 of a possible sex offense. The Board of Education was notified the same month and Arnold was immediately banned from returning to the school. The victim was interviewed on July 29, 2021 and disclosed the abuse. Police also interviewed Arnold; she claimed the victim had raped her, but investigators found multiple inconsistencies in her account. Arnold was arrested without incident on October 1, 2021, charged with sex abuse of a minor (Md. CR § 3-602), third-degree sex offense, and fourth-degree sex offense involving a person in a position of authority. She was released on her own recognizance.

On September 2, 2022, Arnold pleaded guilty in Anne Arundel County Circuit Court to the felony charge of sexual abuse of a minor. On January 5, 2023, she was sentenced to six years in prison.

NOTE: Guardian relationship — victim lived in Arnold's home and Arnold acted as his guardian, a significant aggravating factor beyond the teacher-student relationship alone. Arnold's claim that the victim raped her was contradicted by police evidence.
