Paraprofessional ("Ms. K") at Paetow High School, Katy ISD, Fort Bend County, Texas. Age approximately 25 at time of charges. Not a classroom teacher; supervised students during lunch period.

Victim was a 15-year-old male student. Koepke met the victim at a school event in October 2017 and gave him her Snapchat username ("Momma K"), telling him not to save any of their chats. On homecoming night shortly after, she sent him nude photos of her breasts and two explicit videos of herself. The following day she approached the student at school, said "I can't believe I did that," and apologized. A few days later she sent additional nude photos and videos.

When questioned by investigators, Koepke claimed she initially thought the student was a person from a dating site to whom she had already been sending explicit material. She acknowledged that when she realized it was the student, she continued sending photos to "keep the peace." She admitted knowing the victim from school because she supervised his lunch period.

Charged with improper relationship between educator and student (Tex. Penal Code § 21.12, F2) and online solicitation of a minor. On September 3, 2020, Koepke received deferred adjudication: 4 years community supervision ending September 2, 2024. A motion for early termination was filed November 7, 2023; early termination was granted and the deferred adjudication was terminated September 17, 2024. No formal conviction entered.

NOTE: Deferred adjudication in Texas is not a conviction; successful completion results in case dismissal. The online solicitation charge does not appear in the available docket entries and may have been resolved separately or dismissed. Incident date is approximate (October 2017 homecoming).
