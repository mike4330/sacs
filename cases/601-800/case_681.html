Paraprofessional (Testing Center supervisor, “Ms. K”) at Paetow High School, 23111 Stockdick School Rd, Katy, Harris County, Texas (Katy ISD). Age 23 at time of offense. Not a classroom teacher; supervised students in the Testing Center during lunch period.

Victim was a 15-year-old male student. Defendant met victim during “Power Hour” at school in October 2017. A couple of weeks before Homecoming, she started chatting with him on Snapchat (username “kelsie_koepke,” screen name “Momma K”) and instructed him not to save any of their chats. On Homecoming night, she sent him the first set of nude photos and videos of herself. The following day at school she approached him and said “I can’t believe I did that” and apologized. A few days after the apology, she began sending him more nude photos and videos.

Forensic evidence: Victim’s phone examined using Cellebrite UFED (Katy ISD PD). Two files recovered: (1) IMG_5385.MP4 — a screen-recording of the Snapchat conversation thread showing a thumbnail of a masturbation video sent from “Momma K” on May 1, 2018 (thumbnail shows fingers partially inserted into vagina); (2) IMG_5386.MOV — the full masturbation video, in which the woman removes her middle finger from her vagina and brings it to her mouth; defendant’s face is clearly visible and was confirmed against her Texas Driver’s License. Snapchat contact “Momma K” was #1813 in victim’s contact list with username “kelsie_koepke.”

Defendant’s statement (March 7, 2019): Claimed she initially thought the victim was a contact from a dating site and had already been sending explicit content to multiple people. After realizing it was the student, she continued sending to “keep the peace.” Admitted knowing the victim from Paetow HS because she supervised him in the Testing Center during his lunch period. Confirmed her Snapchat credentials and acknowledged the masturbation video with her face visible.

Charged March 18, 2019 (Katy ISD PD, Affiant Frank Muniz; cause no. 1625024, 184th District Court): improper relationship (§21.12, non-contact sexting variant) and online solicitation of a minor; SAME DEF-2 FEL. Deferred adjudication granted September 3, 2020: 4 years. Early termination granted September 17, 2024.
