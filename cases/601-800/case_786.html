Former substitute teacher in Timberlake Public Schools, based in Cherokee, Oklahoma (Alfalfa County). Age 34 at the time of arrest. The known victim was a 15-year-old male student. The conduct documented in the charging instrument involved both physical contact and electronic grooming.

Redding was arrested on August 30, 2012 by the Alfalfa County Sheriff's Office and made her initial appearance the same day. The Alfalfa County District Attorney filed five felony counts against her: two counts of lewd molestation, one count of forcible sodomy, and two counts of transmitting information by computer for sexual solicitation. The two molestation counts and the sodomy count described physical conduct with the victim; the computer-solicitation counts reflected the electronic side of the relationship.

On November 5, 2014, before Alfalfa County District Court Judge Loren E. Angle, Redding entered a guilty plea to four of the five counts (the two molestation counts, the sodomy count, and one of the two computer-solicitation counts). The remaining computer-solicitation count was dismissed at plea. The court sentenced her to ten years in prison on each convicted count, to run concurrently, with the entire sentence suspended — no period of incarceration was imposed at sentencing. Defense counsel was Fritz McGee of the Oklahoma Indigent Defense System, court-appointed; the prosecution was handled by Assistant District Attorney Westline Ritter.

On January 26, 2017, the State filed an Application to Revoke Suspension of Sentence. The application was dismissed on May 2, 2018 and Redding remained on the suspended sentence.
