Former school counselor and mentor at Sanders School, Indianapolis, Indiana (Marion County; Wayne Township). Sanders School serves troubled and behaviorally challenged youth. Age 32 at arrest.

Victim: 15-year-old autistic male student (described as highly functional). Hill was assigned as his mentor through the school. Multiple sexual encounters occurred; when Hill had money they stayed in hotels; other encounters in her car at remote locations. On July 6, 2006, Airport Police spotted Hill's car pulled over in a remote area near the Indianapolis Airport and found Hill and the boy undressed inside. Hill was arrested that day and held July 6–12 (7 days, credited at sentencing).

Charged with two counts: sexual misconduct with a minor (Class C felony, Count 1) and child solicitation (Count 2). Plea agreement filed November 13, 2006. Pleaded guilty January 10, 2007, to Count 1. Count 2 (child solicitation) dismissed at sentencing. Prosecutors: Maureen Devlin and Eric Schmadke; Lisa Borges was the deputy prosecutor named at arrest. Defense attorney: Steven Litz.

Sentenced January 10, 2007: 6 years total; 730 days (2 years) executed to the Indiana Department of Correction; 4 years suspended; 3 years probation. Good time credit applicable; 7 days jail credit (July 6–12, 2006).
