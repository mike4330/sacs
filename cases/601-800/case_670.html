Former teacher employed by Cherry Creek School District and assigned to teach inmates at the Marvin W. Foote Youth Services Center (juvenile detention facility for youth ages 10-17), located at 13500 East Fremont Place, Englewood, Colorado (Arapahoe County). Age 32 at time of offense (2019). Stastny also had prior teaching assignments with Adams County, Aurora Public Schools, and Jefferson County Schools.

Victim was an underage male inmate at the Foote Center (exact age not reported; facility houses youth 10-17). Stastny gave the victim money and arranged meetings outside the facility. In May 2019, she met him at the Denver Public Library off Quebec Street, where they had sex in a car and in a library bathroom. According to the probable cause affidavit, they had sex in a car on multiple occasions and once in the library bathroom.

Discovery came through a Facebook Messenger conversation Stastny had with a former coworker, in which she wrote: "Let's just say I fu**ed up a bit" and "I definitely slept with him." Screenshots of this conversation were included in the arrest affidavit. Cherry Creek School District had already decided in April not to renew Stastny's contract for unrelated reasons. In May 2019, the district learned of an allegation of inappropriate contact and placed her on administrative leave, cooperating with law enforcement throughout their investigation. Stastny was arrested September 19, 2019 on a charge of sexual assault on a child by a person in a position of trust. Bond was set at $25,000. Investigators noted there may be additional victims.

Pleaded guilty March 2021 to sexual exploitation of a child (Colorado). Other charges dismissed as part of plea. Sentenced May 10, 2021 to 7 years Colorado state prison.
