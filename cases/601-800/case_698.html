Former math teacher at Crowley High School, Acadia Parish School Board, Crowley, Louisiana (Acadia Parish). Age 34 at arrest (October 2014). Resided in Duson, Louisiana.

Three 16-year-old male student victims at Crowley High School. All three received nude photos of Barras through Kik, a text-messaging app. One student said Barras performed oral sex on him in her classroom during the homecoming pep rally. The Acadia Parish School Board received the initial complaint on October 10, 2014 from a school board member about possible inappropriate behavior between a teacher and several students. The three victims admitted their contact with Barras to a coach.

Barras was arrested on October 14, 2014 at her home in Duson by Lafayette Parish deputies acting on Acadia Parish warrants. She was booked into the Acadia Parish Jail on three counts of indecent behavior with a juvenile (La. R.S. § 14:81), with bond set at $60,000 by District Judge Kristian Earles. She resigned from her teaching position the following day.

Barras pleaded guilty to all three counts of indecent behavior with a juvenile. District Judge Earles sentenced her to seven years at hard labor, with the entire sentence suspended in lieu of five years of supervised probation, a fine, and court-ordered sex offender treatment.
