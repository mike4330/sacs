Former English teacher and cheerleading coach at Paradise Valley High School (Paradise Valley Unified School District), Phoenix, Arizona (Maricopa County). Age approximately 25–26 at time of offense. Married; husband Andy Mally.

Engaged in a sexual relationship with a 16-year-old male student from October 2006 to April 2007 — approximately seven months. The student reported the relationship to school officials himself. During the police investigation, Mally was recorded in a confrontation call urging the student to deny the relationship: 'If you make your parents think it's ridiculous, then it will go away.' She warned him she 'could go to f---ing jail.' Mally was also recorded on video at the police station calling her husband; unaware she was being recorded, she acknowledged the situation obliquely while avoiding specifics, telling him she would not discuss it over the phone. Phoenix Police Department released both recordings publicly. Arrested June 2007.

Originally indicted on 17 counts of sexual misconduct with a minor; later recharged on 29 criminal counts. Pleaded guilty in March 2008 to three felony counts of sexual conduct with a minor (ARS 13-1405). Sentenced May 9, 2008 to six months in jail. Original sentencing exposure up to six years. Defense attorney Mel McDonald.

NOTE: Massive charge reduction — 29 counts to 3. Six-month jail sentence on three felony sex-offense counts is a very light outcome. Victim reported to school officials despite Mally's recorded attempts to suppress disclosure. The public release of the confrontation call and police station video generated significant media coverage. Defendant age at offense is approximate (25–26); she is described as 26 at June 2007 arrest and 27 at May 2008 sentencing.
