Former physical education teacher at Sandcreek Middle School, Ammon, Idaho (Bonneville County). Age 28 at time of arrest. Two daughters ages 4 and 6 at time of sentencing.

Victim was a 14-year-old male student. Bonneville County Sheriff's detectives received an anonymous tip, interviewed the victim and Atkinson, and arrested her on September 9, 2009. Placed on paid administrative leave.

Pleaded guilty to two felony counts of lewd conduct with a child under 16 (Idaho Code § 18-1508). Case CR09-17219. Sentenced December 17, 2009 by 7th District Court Judge Jon S. Shindurling to 18 years in prison (indeterminate maximum) with a 2-year fixed minimum before parole eligibility. Atkinson requested probation at sentencing so she could continue counseling and remain with her family. Judge Shindurling rejected that request, noting that teachers have a special responsibility to protect children.

IDOC records: released to supervision (parole) December 8, 2011 (at the 2-year minimum); sentence satisfaction date December 7, 2027.
