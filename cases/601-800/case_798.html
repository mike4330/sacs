Former special education teacher at Andalusia Middle School, Andalusia, Alabama (Covington County).

Two male student victims. The offenses occurred between November 2010 and February 2011. The Chief Assistant District Attorney stated the state expected to prove Caudle "performed oral sex on two different victims." Originally arrested in March 2011 on charges of first-degree rape and first-degree sodomy (Class A felonies). Three students were originally involved.

On June 1, 2011, Caudle pleaded guilty to two counts of second-degree sodomy (Ala. Code § 13A-6-62, Class B felony) pursuant to a plea agreement. She was sentenced the same day: 15 years on each count (concurrent), with 3 years actual incarceration and 12 years supervised probation upon release. She was required to register as a sex offender under the Alabama Community Notification Act and to surrender her teaching certificate. A DNA sample was ordered.
