Former dance teacher at Jordan High School, Durham Public Schools, Durham, North Carolina (Durham County). Married to Phillip White; three young children. Age approximately 35 at start of offense (September 2012); 37 at arrest (July 2, 2014); 39 at sentencing. Resides on Penrith Drive, Durham.

Victim was a 14-year-old female freshman student (referred to as "Jan" in court records) during the 2011–2012 school year. After Jan turned fifteen, White began texting and emailing her daily. Jan's father complained about the abnormal attention and requested White maintain a professional relationship. White repeatedly agreed but continued contact. Early in Jan's sophomore year, her father discovered a "highly inappropriate love letter" from White in Jan's pocket. Over the following years, White and Jan met secretly at Jan's home, White's home, Jordan High School, and White's vehicle. Three separate school district investigations were conducted. During Jan's junior year, her parents discovered a photograph of White and Jan in an intimate kiss. White resigned in April 2014 following the third investigation.

After termination, White continued contact despite a cease-and-desist demand from Jan's family's attorney. They continued to meet in person at a CVS restroom and a Michael's craft store where Jan worked. Jan's father later learned that White had gotten a tattoo of Jan's face on her back, was soliciting Jan for threesomes with White's husband, and was discussing a "lesbian trip" to San Francisco together. A search warrant executed at White's home yielded a VHS tape labeled with the victim's name, framed photos of White with the victim, and handwritten notes from White's husband Phillip addressed to the victim. Phillip White was not charged.

On July 2, 2014, White was arrested and charged with felony sex offense with a student and felony indecent liberties. On March 16, 2015, she was indicted for engaging in sexual activity with a student and taking indecent liberties with a student; a subsequent indictment added two counts of taking indecent liberties with a minor and second-degree kidnapping. On September 2, 2016, White pleaded guilty to all charges before Judge Orlando F. Hudson in Durham County Superior Court. The court consolidated three offenses and sentenced her to 20–33 months, then consolidated the remaining offenses and imposed a consecutive 25–90 months. Total: 45–123 months imprisonment. Required to register as a sex offender for 30 years. A permanent no-contact order prohibited White from contacting Jan or her family for the remainder of White's life.
