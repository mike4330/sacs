Prison Sentence0.25 yrs

Former Spanish teacher at Northridge High School, Greeley, Colorado (Weld County). Taught for more than 20 years.

Victim was a male student, age 17 at time of conception, in Eisenberg's class. He had been placed in her legal guardianship after his parents had to return to Mexico. The relationship occurred in 2010 while the victim was living in her home. Eisenberg later stated they had sex 20 to 30 times. The relationship produced a baby girl.

Formally charged July 28, 2012 with two felony counts of sexual assault on a child. Pleaded guilty January 30, 2013 to attempted sexual assault on a child — position of trust, a Class 5 felony. Sentenced March 27, 2013 to 90 days jail and four years supervised probation. Sex offender registration required; may petition to deregister 10 years after completion of sentence.

At sentencing, Eisenberg stated: 'I somehow want to find redemption and make up for the hurt that I have caused.' The court allowed written and phone contact between Eisenberg and the victim at the victim's request, in recognition of their shared child.
