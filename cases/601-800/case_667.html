Former teacher at Navarre High School, Navarre, Santa Rosa County, Florida. Age approximately 36 at time of offense (summer 2019).

Victim was a 17-year-old male student. Frequent platonic text message exchanges between Rodriguez and the victim turned sexual. In summer 2019, after the victim attended a summer practice, he went to Rodriguez's classroom to go over something and they had sex. The victim told law enforcement the encounter occurred only once and he regretted it. He said Rodriguez initiated. He told his parents he was worried she would fail him if he refused.

On March 3, 2020, the Santa Rosa County Sheriff's Office received an anonymous tip that Rodriguez had sex with a student the previous summer. An investigator met Rodriguez the same day at Navarre High principal's office. Rodriguez initially declined to speak without a lawyer, but called the investigator back as he was leaving. She asked what the investigation's possible outcomes were, including asking if there was a "middle ground." Rodriguez had separately admitted to the principal that she kissed the student, but denied having sex with him. The student's mother said her son disclosed the investigation to his parents on March 3.

Rodriguez resigned from Santa Rosa County School District on May 28, 2020. She was arrested June 11, 2020 and charged with sexual assault; booked into the Santa Rosa County Jail without bond.

Sentenced July 28, 2022 by Judge Clifton Alan Drake to 8 years Florida Department of Corrections, with credit for 2 days time served. Plea or trial verdict not specified in available records.

NOTE: The 8-year sentence for a single confirmed incident is among the higher sentences in this DB for a one-time offense. The victim's statement — that he feared Rodriguez would fail him if he refused — is a documented coercive element rarely stated so explicitly in arrest records. Rodriguez's question about "the middle ground" suggests she understood the severity of her exposure before invoking counsel, then sought to manage the outcome informally.
