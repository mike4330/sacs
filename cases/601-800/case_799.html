Former English/language arts teacher at Gar-Field High School, Woodbridge (Dale City), Virginia (Prince William County). Age 25 at onset of relationship (DOB May 1983; offense onset September 2008).

Victim was a 15-year-old male student (freshman, 2008–2009 school year). The sexual relationship began during the 2008–2009 school year and continued approximately two years. Sexual contact included intercourse at a Woodbridge hotel on two occasions and at AMC Cineplex at Potomac Mills. The victim stated Amato "fell in love with" him. The relationship was discovered on April 1, 2011 when a school administrator confiscated the student's cell phone for texting in class and found sexually explicit messages from Amato. Amato had been employed at Gar-Field since 2008.

Indicted by grand jury August 1, 2011 (Prince William County Circuit Court, CR11001949-00). On December 15, 2011, Amato pleaded guilty to three counts of taking indecent liberties with a minor by a custodian and one count of crimes against nature. Separate Stafford County charges were also pending (CR11000743-00, offense date March 31, 2011 — one day before discovery). Defense attorney for Prince William: Sarah Buskirk; defense attorney for Stafford: Tracey Lennox.

Sentenced April 12, 2012 (Prince William) and June 12, 2012 (Stafford), both to 5 years with 4 years 9 months suspended (3 months active), concurrent. Prince William: 2 years supervised probation, $1,650 costs. Stafford: 10 years indefinite supervision, $865 costs. The Stafford probation term governs the longer supervision period. In Stafford, the jury trial set for February 8, 2012 was withdrawn before proceeding to a guilty plea in April.

In July 2024, a post-trial motion was filed and set for hearing; it was withdrawn at the August 15, 2024 hearing. Nature of the motion not documented in available sources.
