Former choir director at DeSoto Central High School (DeSoto County Schools), Southaven, Mississippi. Hired in 2008. Named MMEE Young Music Educator of the Year in 2013 by the Mississippi Music Educators Association. Age approximately 28 at time of offense (2015); age 29 at January 2016 arrest.

One victim, a student at DeSoto Central High School. Sex and age not publicly disclosed (under 18). Victim's parents contacted police after learning of the relationship. Southaven police declined to disclose the student's name or age.

Turned herself in January 8, 2016 after learning a warrant had been issued; charged with two counts of sexual battery. Bond set at $50,000. Indicted March 10, 2016. Mississippi Attorney General's Office (Public Integrity Division, Luke Williamson) prosecuted rather than local DA. Defense attorneys Jerry Wesley Hisaw and James Holland. Pleaded guilty June 30, 2016.

Count 1 (§ 97-3-95(2)): 7 years imposed, 2 years MDOC (with 1-day credit), 5 years post-release supervision; $591.50 court costs, $1,000 fine, $1,000 MSCTF; $100/month payments beginning 60 days after release; no contact with victim; sex offender registration. Count 2 dismissed. Released approximately June 29, 2018 (sentence expiration per imminent release notice).
