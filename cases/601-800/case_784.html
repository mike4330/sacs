Former art teacher (first year) at Norwalk High School, Norwalk, Ohio (Huron County). Age 25 at time of offense.

Victim was a 17-year-old male student at Norwalk High School. On March 31, 2012, Nasonti had sexual intercourse with the student at a home in Bellevue. On April 2, 2012, a patrol officer discovered Nasonti and the student together in a parked car at Lion's Park — this discovery triggered the investigation. Investigators subsequently obtained hundreds of text messages between Nasonti and the student. Nasonti resigned on May 2, 2012.

On August 26, 2012, she pleaded guilty to one count of sexual battery with a sexual motivation specification (Ohio Rev. Code § 2907.03) in Sandusky County Common Pleas Court.

On October 29, 2012, she was sentenced to 3 years community control (no prison), mandatory counseling, and Tier III sex offender registration (every 90 days for life). The plea agreement had capped any prison recommendation at 18 months; the judge imposed no prison.
