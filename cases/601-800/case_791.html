StateFloridaCourt—

Prison Sentence15.0 yrs

Former reading teacher at Okaloosa Academy Charter School, Fort Walton Beach/Crestview area, Okaloosa County, Florida. Age 25 at time of offense (DOB July 10, 1986).

Victim was a 16-year-old former student. In November 2011, Cooney left a resignation note on her classroom desk and fled Florida with the teenager. The pair were apprehended in Riverside, Ohio after committing a series of crimes — including use of stolen credit cards, theft of a 9mm firearm (recovered under a pillow), and drug paraphernalia. The OCSO investigated the sexual relationship; Crestview PD handled the property crime charges separately.

The sexual relationship is described as ongoing. Cooney was convicted under Florida Statute § 794.05(1), which prohibits sexual activity with 16–17-year-old minors regardless of consent. On August 20, 2012, she was adjudicated guilty and sentenced to 15 years in prison and designated a sexual offender (not sexual predator tier). She remained under FL DOC supervision as of offender registry records.
