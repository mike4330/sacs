Sherri Lynn Baffa #788 sentenced

StateTennesseeCourtSullivan County Criminal Court
JudgeR. Jerry BeckIncident Date2007-02-18
Defendant Age30Victim Age16
Prison Sentence8.0 yrs

Former math teacher at Sullivan East High School, Bluff City area, Sullivan County, Tennessee. Employed by Sullivan County school system since 2005. Age approximately 30 at time of offense.

Victim was a 16-year-old male (a former student at the time of discovery, now an adult). On February 18, 2007, Baffa and the student were photographed together in sexually explicit images. The photographs were discovered in May 2011 when Baffa's ex-husband, Michael S. Campbell, found them on his camera and reported them to the Sullivan County Sheriff's Office. The FBI performed digital forensic analysis confirming the authenticity and date of the images. Campbell was separately charged as an accomplice and was pending trial as of Baffa's sentencing. Baffa formally resigned on February 23, 2012.

Originally indicted on 7 counts of especially aggravated sexual exploitation of a minor (Tenn. Code Ann. § 39-17-1005, a CSAM production statute). She pleaded guilty at the last minute to a plea deal offering the minimum punishment on all seven counts.

On August 28, 2012, Judge R. Jerry Beck sentenced Baffa to 8 years in prison. Under Tennessee law, sex offenders must serve the full term without suspension. She was also fined $2,000 and required to register as a sex offender.
