English teacher and teacher's aide supervisor at Hemet High School, Hemet, California (Riverside County). Hemet Unified School District. Married mother of two.

Victim was a 16-year-old male student who served as Ramirez's classroom aide. Grooming began at the end of his sophomore year when Ramirez kissed him and told him he resembled Twilight actor Taylor Lautner, promising they would be together after he graduated. His junior year he again served as her classroom aide; their relationship escalated to sexual contact that fall. Three incidents total, all in the locked classroom — including sexual intercourse and oral sex acts. Ramirez shared her marital unhappiness with the student and monitored his text messages, requiring him to delete all communications between them.

Student reported the relationship to school officials on February 29, 2012. Search warrant issued; Ramirez arrested March 1, 2012 at the Hemet USD offices. She resigned May 1, 2012.

Waived right to jury trial and pleaded guilty May 3, 2012 in Riverside County Superior Court to two counts of oral copulation with a person under 18 and one count of unlawful intercourse with a minor more than three years younger. Sentenced immediately to five years probation and 120 days of electronically monitored house arrest. Court order does not require sex offender registration.
