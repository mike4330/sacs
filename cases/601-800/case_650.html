English teacher at Hillcrest High School, Dallas Independent School District, Texas (Dallas County). Certified by the Texas Board of Education in 2012; hired in October 2012 — arrested before completing her first full school year. Age 24 at time of offense and arrest.

Victim was a 17-year-old male freshman in her 9th-grade English class. Offense period May–July 2013; police documented more than 25 sexual encounters in her car, her apartment, and his apartment when his parents were not home. Law enforcement documented 15,371 text messages between Sharp and the student, including explicit photos exchanged between May and July.

Victim's mother discovered Sharp with her son at their apartment complex swimming pool, prompting investigation. Three days before her arrest, Sharp was informed of the pending investigation; she then instructed the student to delete all photos and messages. She had synced her iPhone with her school laptop and deleted directories on the laptop before surrendering it to investigators, but the deleted content was recovered elsewhere on the hard drive.

Sharp denied the sexual relationship in a written statement to Dallas ISD. Arrested August 8, 2013; held in Collin County Jail and released on $29,500 bond. Original charges: improper relationship between educator and student (second degree felony), possession or promotion of child pornography (third degree felony), perjury, and tampering with physical evidence.

Sharp confessed in 2014. Plea finalized July 2015: guilty to second degree felony (TX Penal Code § 21.12 — improper relationship between educator and student). Sentenced to 10 years probation and a $2,500 fine. No prison. Third degree CP charges dismissed.
