Amy Hamilton #611 sentenced

Also known as: Amy M Hamilton

StateIndianaCourtHarrison County Circuit Court
Incident Date2018-03-03
Defendant Age40Victim Age15
Prison Sentence2.0 yrs

Former teacher at a Meade County middle school, Meade County Schools, Kentucky; transferred to South Oldham High School (Oldham County Schools), Kentucky in December 2017. Age approximately 40 at arrest (February 2019); resident of Corydon, Harrison County, Indiana.

Victim: male, age 15, former student from Meade County. Hamilton met the victim while teaching in Meade County; the two remained in contact via text after she transferred to Oldham County. Offense occurred March 3, 2018 at Hamilton's home in Corydon, Indiana. Victim stayed overnight; Hamilton provided approximately nine beers to the victim while consuming multiple bottles of wine herself. According to the victim's statement, he initiated physical contact earlier in the day and Hamilton resisted; Hamilton later re-initiated contact and led him to her bedroom where they had sexual intercourse. Hamilton denied to investigators providing alcohol or having any sexual contact. Forensic examination of the victim's phone yielded messages from Hamilton alluding to an intimate relationship.

Charged February 11, 2019 in Harrison County, Indiana (Case #31D01-1902F4000122) with Level 4 felony sexual misconduct with a minor (IC 35-42-4-9) and misdemeanor contributing to the delinquency of a minor. Harrison County Prosecutor J. Otto Schalk declined child seduction charges because the teacher-student relationship had ended prior to the offense. Hamilton turned herself in February 11, 2019; released on $5,000 bond.

Convicted January 6, 2020. Sentenced: 2 years DOC + 3 years suspended. Sex offender registration: April 4, 2021 – April 4, 2031 (10 years, not lifetime).

NOTE: Offense at defendant's home; victim was former student with ongoing contact via text after school relationship ended. Prosecutor explicitly declined child seduction charges — charged under age-of-consent statute only (victim under 16). Community members brought case to law enforcement. Alcohol: ~9 beers to victim, multiple bottles of wine consumed by Hamilton. Hamilton denied both alcohol provision and sexual contact to investigators.
