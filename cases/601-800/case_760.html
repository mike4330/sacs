Former special-education non-instructional aide at Waterford Mott High School in Waterford, Michigan (Oakland County). Independence Township resident. Age 37 at the time of the documented offense conduct. Three known male student victims, all teenagers; specific ages were not reported in available sources, and victim age is recorded as 16 as a high-school inferred estimate.

Ellsworth had sexual contact with all three victims both on the school campus and off campus. The investigation opened in October 2009 after allegations were brought forward to police; Ellsworth resigned from the district effective October 27, 2009.

She was arraigned on November 5, 2009 in the 51st District Court in Waterford before Judge Richard Kuhn on six counts of criminal sexual conduct: four counts at the first-degree grade under the Michigan authority-figure-relationship provision (a life felony) and two counts at the third-degree grade under the student-victim provision (a fifteen-year felony). Bond was set at $25,000 cash or surety with a ten-percent provision; the court ordered no contact with anyone under 18 except her own children.

At a January 7, 2010 hearing, defense attorney Todd Kaluzny — standing in for Lawrence Kaluzny — requested a forensic examination to determine competency and criminal responsibility. Oakland County Circuit Court Judge Michael Warren granted the request. The case record reflects that Ellsworth had been hospitalized twice for mental-health issues and was diagnosed as bipolar with depression.

On March 11, 2010, Ellsworth was found guilty on a no-contest plea to all six counts. On April 15, 2010, Judge Warren sentenced her to 62 months minimum with a 30-year maximum on the four first-degree counts and 62 months minimum with a 15-year maximum on the two third-degree counts, all six counts running concurrently. The court awarded one day of jail credit and imposed lifetime electronic GPS tether on release, ongoing mental-health treatment and medication compliance, a suicide-watch hold pending state-prison medical clearance, DNA testing, and sex-offender registration.
