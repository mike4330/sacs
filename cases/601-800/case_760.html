Amy Rita Ellsworth #760 sentenced

StateMichiganCourtOakland County Circuit Court (6th Circuit)
JudgeMichael Warren Incident Date2009-01-01
Defendant Age37
Prison Sentence5.17 yrs

Former special education non-instructional aide at Waterford Mott High School, Waterford, Michigan (Oakland County). Age 37. Independence Township resident.

Three male teenage student victims. Ellsworth had sexual relations with all three students both on and off school campus. Police investigation began in October 2009 after allegations were brought forward; Ellsworth resigned effective October 27, 2009.

Arraigned November 5, 2009 at 51st District Court in Waterford before Judge Richard Kuhn on six counts of criminal sexual conduct: four counts of 1st degree CSC (MCL § 750.520b(1)(b), authority figure relationship, life felony) and two counts of 3rd degree CSC (MCL § 750.520d(1)(e), student, 15-year felony). Bond: $25,000 cash or surety with 10% provision; ordered no contact with anyone under 18 except her own children.

At a hearing January 7, 2010, defense attorney Todd Kaluzny (standing in for Lawrence Kaluzny) requested a forensic exam to determine competency and criminal responsibility. Oakland Circuit Judge Michael Warren granted the request. Ellsworth had been hospitalized twice for mental health issues and was diagnosed as bipolar with depression.

On March 11, 2010, Ellsworth was found guilty (via no contest plea) on all six counts. Sentenced April 15, 2010 by Judge Warren. All six counts sentenced concurrently: 4 counts CSC 1st degree — minimum 62 months, maximum 30 years; 2 counts CSC 3rd degree — minimum 62 months, maximum 15 years. Credit: 1 day. Additional conditions: lifetime electronic tether (GPS) upon release from prison, mental health treatment and medication compliance, suicide watch pending MDOC medical clearance, DNA testing, HIV testing, sex offender registration. Fees: $60 victims rights fee, $408 state minimum costs.
