Erica DePalo #649 sentenced

StateNew JerseyCourtEssex County Superior Court
Incident Date2012-06-15
Defendant Age33 Victim Age15
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

English teacher and JV tennis coach at West Orange High School, West Orange, New Jersey (Essex County). Montclair resident. Had been named Teacher of the Year prior to her arrest.

Victim was a 15-year-old male student in her honors English class. The relationship began on or about June 15, 2012 and continued until August 28, 2012. Offense occurred during summer break.

Arrested September 12, 2012 by West Orange Police Detective Louis Mignone; arrest warrant signed by Judge Martin G. Cronin; bail set at $100,000. Indefinitely suspended by the school district. Original charges: first degree aggravated sexual assault, second degree sexual assault, and child endangerment — up to 10 years potential exposure.

Pleaded guilty in February 2013 to child endangerment (NJ § 2C:24-4). Sentenced April 29, 2013 to lifetime parole supervision plus sex offender registration and forfeiture of teaching certificate. No prison time. At sentencing, a tearful DePalo expressed regret and stated she is trying to rebuild her life. Prosecuted by Acting Essex County Prosecutor Carolyn A. Murray. Defense attorney: Anthony Alfano.

Defense argued DePalo suffers from bipolar disorder and was on medication that can cause errors in judgment.

NOTE: The aggravated sexual assault charge (NJ § 2C:14-2) strongly implies penetrative contact, but the conviction charge (child endangerment) does not map to a specific contact type per the DB taxonomy — contact_type left NULL on conviction charge. The charge reduction from first degree aggravated sexual assault to child endangerment is extreme. Lifetime parole supervision is a significant collateral consequence despite the no-prison outcome. The Teacher of the Year status prior to arrest is analytically notable — high-standing offenders may be more likely to receive favorable plea terms.
