Math teacher at West Career and Technical Academy, Clark County School District (CCSD), Las Vegas, Nevada (Clark County). Hired August 2015.

Victim was a male student, age 15 at the start of the offense during the 2015-16 school year; victim was 17 by January 2018 when arrest was made after students reported seeing sexually explicit photos and text messages on the victim's phone. Relationship spanned approximately two school years. Conduct included sexting and explicit photos.

Arrested January 26, 2018. Original charges: attempted kidnapping (for attempting to lure the victim to a hotel), lewdness with a minor, child abuse, sexual misconduct, and unlawful contact with a minor (5 charges total). Bail set at $50,000. Pleaded not guilty January 30, 2018.

Pleaded guilty (date not specified in available sources) to Count 1 (child abuse, neglect, or endangerment) and Count 2 (attempted sexual conduct between certain employees or volunteers of a school and a pupil — NRS 201.540). Sentenced December 12, 2019 by Judge Eric Johnson to suspended terms (12–32 months DOC on Count 2; concurrent), 5 years probation.

On June 27, 2023, after successfully completing probation, Judge Johnson granted an honorable discharge pursuant to NRS 176A.850: Count 2 (attempted sexual conduct) plea withdrawn, adjudication set aside, sex offender registration requirement vacated. Count 1 (child abuse) sentence was simultaneously restructured to 24–72 months Nevada DOC, suspended (5 years probation) — this stands. Net result: defendant is not required to register as a sex offender.

NOTE: Defendant age 34 estimated at offense start (2015-16 school year); she was 36 at arrest in January 2018 (born approximately 1981). Incident date 2015-09-01 is the approximate start of the 2015-16 school year. The SORA vacatur via NRS 176A.850 is the primary legal mechanism of interest — it allowed complete removal of the sexual conduct conviction and registration requirement after probation completion. The attempted kidnapping charge (hotel luring) was apparently dismissed before plea. Rine was identified as the 9th CCSD employee arrested for sexual misconduct in the 2017-18 school year.
