Former mathematics teacher at West Career and Technical Academy in the Clark County School District, Las Vegas, Nevada. Hired by the district in August 2015. Born approximately 1981; age 34 at the start of the documented offense conduct in the 2015-16 school year, age 36 at her January 2018 arrest. The known victim was a male student who was 15 at the start of the relationship and 17 by the time arrest was made.

The relationship spanned approximately two school years. Documented conduct included sexting and the exchange of explicit photographs. Investigators learned of the relationship in January 2018 after fellow students reported seeing sexually explicit photographs and text messages on the victim's phone.

Rine was arrested by Las Vegas Metropolitan Police on January 26, 2018. The original charging instrument carried five counts: attempted kidnapping (alleging an attempt to lure the victim to a hotel), lewdness with a minor, child abuse, sexual misconduct, and unlawful contact with a minor. Bail was set at $50,000. Rine pleaded not guilty on January 30, 2018.

She subsequently pleaded guilty to two counts: child abuse, neglect, or endangerment (Count 1) and attempted sexual conduct between a school employee and a pupil (Count 2). The original attempted-kidnapping count and the lewdness, sexual-misconduct, and unlawful-contact allegations were dismissed before plea. On December 12, 2019, Clark County District Court Judge Eric Johnson sentenced Rine to suspended state-prison terms on both convicted counts (12 to 32 months on Count 2, concurrent), with five years of probation.

On June 27, 2023, after successful completion of probation, Judge Johnson granted an honorable discharge under Nevada's post-probation honorable-discharge mechanism. The Count 2 plea was withdrawn and that adjudication set aside, and the sex-offender registration requirement was vacated. The Count 1 child-abuse sentence was simultaneously restructured to 24 to 72 months in state prison, suspended in favor of five years of probation; that conviction stands.

NOTE: The post-probation honorable-discharge mechanism is the case's distinctive feature. Nevada's framework allowed Rine, upon successful probation completion, to withdraw her plea on the school-employee-and-pupil count and have the adjudication set aside, vacating the sex-offender registration requirement; the child-abuse conviction remains, but it is a non-sex offense for registration purposes. This is the cleanest documented instance in the corpus of a state-statutory mechanism producing complete sex-offense and registration removal after probation rather than a deferred-adjudication-from-the-start structure. Sentence_years recorded at zero — both sentences were suspended at imposition. Rine was identified at the time of her arrest as the ninth Clark County School District employee arrested for sexual misconduct during the 2017-18 school year.
