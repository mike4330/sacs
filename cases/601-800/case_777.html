Former English teacher at Union Grove High School, Union Grove ISD, Upshur County, Texas (employed since 2007). Age 38 at time of offense. Married. Resided in White Oak, Texas.

Victim was an 18-year-old male student. The alleged offense occurred April 22, 2011 at a Gladewater residence. The school principal was notified April 25; New was placed on administrative leave April 27. The case was referred to Gladewater Police Department (the city straddles the Upshur/Gregg County line) because the offense occurred within Gladewater city limits. New was arrested May 19, 2011 by White Oak police at her home and booked into Gregg County Jail on a $20,000 bond, from which she was released the same evening. She resigned her teaching position the day of arrest.

The original arrest charge was improper relationship between educator and student (TPC § 21.12). However, the case was filed February 16, 2012 in Gregg County Court at Law No. 1 (Case 2012-0496) as Assault Causes Bodily Injury (TPC § 22.01(a)(1)), a Class A Misdemeanor — not the original educator felony. On March 13, 2012, New entered a nolo contendere plea before Judge Rebecca Simpson. She was sentenced to 15 months community supervision (CSCD) with 60 hours of community service, a $300 fine, and a 1-year county jail term suspended in favor of probation. On January 18, 2013, the probation period was reduced by court order.

Defense attorney: Joe Black (retained). Prosecutor: Jenny Carol Huckle.
