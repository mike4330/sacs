Abbie Jane Swogger #740 sentenced

Former teacher's aide to a special needs student in the Highlands School District, Natrona Heights, Pennsylvania (Allegheny County). Also a former exotic dancer. Age 34 at time of offense (DOB May 14, 1973). Resided in Natrona Heights. The stub incorrectly listed her as a teacher at Highlands Senior High School and gave an incorrect age of 28.

Multiple male victims spanning two incident clusters in January–February 2008. In January 2008: Swogger allegedly performed a sex act on a 16-year-old boy at his home; she also stripped for three adults and three boys (including a 15-year-old) at the Natrona residence of a 15-year-old, allowed those present to touch her inappropriately, and marijuana was used. A 17-year-old Harrison Township boy acknowledged a sexual relationship with Swogger lasting several weeks beginning in January. In February 2008: police investigating two teenage runaways found Swogger in a hotel room with crack cocaine, alcohol, and several teens. Swogger resigned as a teacher's aide a few days after the hotel raid.

At the time of the NBC article (April 2008), Swogger had been charged with 33 counts across multiple counties, including indecent assault, corruption of minors, and related offenses; police in neighboring counties consolidated charges. The Allegheny County case (CP-02-CR-0007310-2008, 380th District Court) focuses on the February 2008 incidents.

Complaint filed April 16, 2008; bail set at $50,000. Information filed September 24, 2008. On May 26, 2009, Swogger entered a guilty plea to seven counts before Judge Kevin G. Sasinoski in a non-jury trial. Two counts (obstruction, criminal solicitation to intimidate witness) were dismissed. Sentence on the controlling count — IDSI Person Less Than 16 Years Age (F1, 18 Pa.C.S. § 3123(a)(7)) — was 3 years minimum to 6 years maximum confinement, with 392 days credit for time served, plus 14 years probation consecutive to confinement. Two Unlawful Contact counts (F3) received 7 years probation each, consecutive to the IDSI probation, creating a total probation tail of up to 28 years. No-contact order with victim. Defense: James Depasquale (private). DA: Laura Ann Ditka (Allegheny County).

Probation/parole violation hearings were held in 2019 (January, March, April). A motion to lift detainer was granted December 18, 2018. Case interest was formally closed October 30, 2025 on motion by defense counsel.
