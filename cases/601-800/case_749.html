Melanie K. Yusko #749 sentenced

StateOhioCourtPortage County Common Pleas Court
JudgeLaurie J. Pittman Incident Date2010-04-07
Defendant Age22 Victim Age15
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former student teacher at Southeast High School, Portage County, Ohio; concurrently enrolled as an education major at Kent State University. Age 22 at time of offense. Resided in Highland Heights, Ohio.

Victim was a 15-year-old male student (DOB 05/24/1994) enrolled at Southeast High School. On April 7, 2010, Yusko performed oral sex on the victim at his home while he was off from school on spring break; she had been helping him with schoolwork. Yusko later told a psychologist she performed the act because she "felt bad" for the student. The incident came to light after the victim told a friend; the school district was informed and Yusko was dismissed as a student teacher.

Portage County grand jury indicted Yusko on Sexual Battery — Person in Authority (ORC § 2907.03(A)(9), F3), Case 2010CR00284. In October 2010, she pleaded guilty to the reduced charge of Unlawful Sexual Conduct with a Minor (ORC § 2907.04, F4). On December 20, 2010, Judge Laurie J. Pittman sentenced Yusko to 300 days in the Portage County Jail (work release eligible, beginning January 1, 2011), five years of probation, and designation as a Tier II sex offender requiring address registration every six months for 25 years. Additional conditions: $500 fine; no teaching license; mental health evaluation; full-time employment within 15 months; no contact with victim or family.

The Portage County Prosecutor's Office recommended prison time, arguing Yusko initially denied the relationship to school officials and sheriff's detectives. Defense argued she admitted involvement during a psychological evaluation two weeks after the offense. Defense attorney: Kenneth Bossin. Assistant Prosecutor: Eric Finnegan.
