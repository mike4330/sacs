Former special education teacher at Sashabaw Middle School, Clarkston, Michigan (Oakland County; Independence Township). Age 28. Oxford Township resident. Employed at Sashabaw Middle School for two years.

Victim: 14-year-old cognitively impaired male student who had been in Simonson's special education class during the 2008–2009 school year. Simonson sent approximately 50 text messages from late fall 2008 through the end of the 2009 school year. Messages initially framed as academic help, then escalated in sexual content. One message included a partially nude self-photograph. The student told police the texts made him increasingly uncomfortable and he asked her to stop; he reported no physical contact or inappropriate in-person conversations. Discovered when the victim's foster parent (another source says his mother) found the messages and turned his cell phone over to police in Independence Township.

Arraigned August 2009 on charges of enticing a minor for immoral purposes and distributing sexually explicit visual or verbal material to a minor; posted ,500 bail. Waived preliminary examination and was bound over to Oakland County Circuit Court. No prior criminal record.

Pleaded guilty. Sentenced December 11, 2009 by Oakland County Circuit Court Judge Mark Goldsmith: 90 days jail (tether request denied), 5 years probation, sex offender registration, treatment, no contact with victim, fines and court costs.
