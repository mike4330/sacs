Javelin coach for the Northampton Area High School track and field team; concurrently employed as a teacher at Easton Area School District, Northampton County, Pennsylvania. Age 24 at time of offense (May 2021); DOB December 6, 1996. Residence: Lehigh Township (later Walnutport), Northampton County. Marth was not an employee of Northampton Area SD — she coached there as an outside contractor while teaching at Easton Area.

Victim: male student-athlete, Northampton Area High School, under age 18 in May 2021. Around 2 a.m. on May 22, 2021, Marth texted the victim and invited him to her residence on Hunsicker Drive in Lehigh Township, where she initiated sexual contact. Marth confessed to police she had 'romantic involvement' with the victim in May 2021. The victim stated the relationship continued until October 2022 (~17 months).

Charged April 13, 2023 by Northampton County DA Terry Houck with institutional sexual assault (F3, 18 Pa.C.S. § 3124.2(a)(2)) and sexual assault by a sports official (F3); arraigned April 13, 2023. Placed on leave from coaching at Northampton Area; Easton Area SD launched internal investigation. Pleaded guilty September 27, 2023 (Case CP-48-CR-0001271-2023) to one count: institutional sexual assault — school (intercourse/sexual contact with student), 18 Pa.C.S. § 3124.2(a)(2), F3; sports official charge dismissed. Sentenced February 14, 2024 by Judge Koury: 3 months minimum to 23 months 29 days maximum confinement; 1 year probation. Defense attorney: Sara Alison Moyer.
