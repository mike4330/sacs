Former kindergarten teacher at Upson-Lee South Elementary School, Thomaston, Georgia (Upson County). Taught in the Thomaston-Upson County school system for approximately 20 years.

Victim was a male student, age 14, at Upson-Lee Middle School — not Robinson's student; the two knew each other through their families. Offense period July 1, 2015 to February 21, 2016. Sexual acts occurred in Robinson's kindergarten classroom (over summer break), in her car in a parking lot near the school playground, and at her home. Arrest warrants documented five separate incidents. Robinson confessed upon arrest.

Discovery: Robinson called in sick on Monday, February 22, 2016, leaving her school computer on with her personal Facebook page open. A paraprofessional noticed incoming notification beeps, checked the screen, and found evidence of the relationship. The parapro reported to her supervisor, who contacted law enforcement. Robinson was arrested that evening.

Originally charged with five counts of aggravated child molestation and five counts of child molestation (10 counts total). Arrested February 22, 2016; held without bond. Pleaded guilty on November 14, 2016 to one count of child molestation. Sentenced by Judge Tommy Hankinson to 15 years — 3 to serve in prison, 12 years probation — plus a $1,500 fine and sex offender registration (Level 2).
