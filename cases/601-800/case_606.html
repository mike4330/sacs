Former physical education teacher at Coleman High School, Coleman Public Schools, Johnston County, Oklahoma. Age 24 at time of offense (also 24 at sentencing, March 2019).

Victim: male, age 16. Sexual intercourse occurred May 11–15, 2017. District reported the alleged crime to the Oklahoma State Department of Education in May 2017; Harris's teaching license was suspended in June 2017. Oklahoma State Bureau of Investigation worked the case. Charged November 29, 2017. Turned herself in December 18, 2017; appeared before judge and was released on own recognizance. Second court appearance February 26, 2018.

Pleaded guilty March 15, 2019 to second-degree rape. Sentenced to 1 year in prison and 9 years probation. Both teaching certifications suspended at sentencing; revocation hearing scheduled to follow.

NOTE: Short offense window (5 days, May 11–15). Second educator in Johnston County sentenced to prison for student relationship in 2019 (per KXII). Affidavit sealed by judge — reason not stated in available coverage. Superintendent Sheridan confirmed school cooperation with law enforcement.
