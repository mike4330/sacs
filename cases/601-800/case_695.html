Former teacher at Hastings High School, Alief ISD, Houston, Texas (Harris County). Age 29 at arrest (August 2013).

Victim was a 16-year-old female who was not in Garcia's classroom; the two met through a mutual friend. Garcia used the pretext of mentoring the girl to establish a relationship, staying with her after school while she worked on a classroom project and taking her to Garcia's apartment on numerous occasions. The girl's father caught the two together at a public park and notified authorities. A prosecutor described one encounter in which Garcia drove the victim to a sex shop, purchased a sex toy, and returned to her apartment where they engaged in sexual activity. Court documents reportedly contained additional graphic details.

Garcia was initially charged with indecency with a child and sexual assault of a child; bond was set at $60,000. She was suspended from her teaching position and ordered to have no contact with the victim. A Harris County grand jury indicted her on August 8, 2013 on one count of improper relationship between educator and student (Tex. Penal Code § 21.12, F2). On September 4, 2013, Garcia entered a deferred adjudication and was placed on eight years' community supervision ending September 3, 2021. Conditions of supervision were amended on three occasions (May 2015, September 2015, July 2016). The deferred adjudication was terminated on September 7, 2021 with no finding of guilt.

NOTE: Female victim. Deferred adjudication in Texas is not a conviction; termination results in dismissal with no formal adjudication. sentence_years = 0. Initial charges (indecency/sexual assault) were reduced to the single § 21.12 count at indictment.
