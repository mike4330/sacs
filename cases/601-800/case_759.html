Former preschool teacher at Central Valley Christian School, a Seventh-day Adventist institution serving preschool through ninth grade in Tangent, Oregon (Linn County). Married. Age 34 at the time of the offense, with no prior criminal history. The known victim was a 14-year-old male eighth-grader at the same school.

The relationship became sexual in the autumn of 2009. Stitzel kissed the victim and engaged in sexual contact with him both on and off the school campus, and she sent him sexually explicit text messages and nude photographs of herself by cellphone. The conduct came to light after the victim's parents grounded him in late November 2009 and recovered his cellphone; the messages they discovered were described by Linn County Sheriff Tim Mueller as 'the kind of thing that you might read in Penthouse Forum — X-rated.'

Stitzel was arrested on December 3, 2009 by the Linn County Sheriff's Office and booked into the county jail; she was released on her own recognizance, with the court noting her absence of prior history and a documented diabetic condition. The release order barred contact with the victim. She was arraigned before Judge Glen D. Baisinger on December 23, 2009; the bond conditions were amended to bar contact with any minor male except with written approval from the Oregon Department of Human Services.

Charges were ultimately filed in six counts: two counts of sexual abuse in the third degree, two counts of furnishing sexually explicit material to a child (both Class A misdemeanors under Oregon law), and two counts of luring a minor (Class C felonies, added in late November 2009). Defense counsel Forrest Reid was retained; the case was prosecuted by Heidi Sternhagen for the Linn County District Attorney's Office. On February 22, 2010 Stitzel pleaded guilty by petition to one count of sexual abuse in the third degree and one count of furnishing sexually explicit material; the remaining four counts, including both Class C felony luring counts, were dismissed.

On March 29, 2010 Judge Baisinger imposed a Suspended Imposition of Sentence on each conviction count under Oregon's deferred-sentencing statute — no period of incarceration was imposed, and Stitzel was placed on probation through Linn County Community Corrections. Sex-offender registration was required.

NOTE: Private religious-school setting (Seventh-day Adventist preschool-through-ninth-grade institution). Distinctive Oregon-statute moment: Sheriff Mueller publicly observed at arrest that 'had this happened after January 1st, it would have been a Class C felony because of the sexting part of it' — Oregon HB 2641, which added text and video messaging to the felony online-sex-abuse statute, took effect January 1, 2010, just weeks after this case was charged. The Class C felony luring counts that were filed were ultimately dismissed at plea, leaving only two Class A misdemeanor convictions; the sentence_years value of 0.0 reflects the Suspended Imposition disposition.
