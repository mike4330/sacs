Former teacher's aide at Western Valley Middle School, Phoenix, Arizona (Maricopa County). Age 20 at arrest.

Two male victims. Victim 1, age 13: Compton groped the student. Victim 2, age 14: engaged in sexual intercourse with the student in the back of her van, parked at an industrial park near 67th Avenue and Van Buren Road in Phoenix. Compton also sent a nude photograph of her breasts to male students; the image was circulated throughout the school. School principal and teachers alerted police. During the investigation, Compton admitted to sending the nude photo and to a sexual relationship with one student during a confrontation call.

Arrested March 2011; booked into Maricopa County Jail. Charged with four counts of sexual conduct with a minor (ARS 13-1405, penetrative), two counts of sexual abuse (ARS 13-1404, non-penetrative), and one count of furnishing obscene material to a minor. Took a plea deal in March 2012, pleading guilty to three counts of sexual abuse. Sentenced May 8, 2012 to lifetime probation with sex-offender terms. No prison time.
