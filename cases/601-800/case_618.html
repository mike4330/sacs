Hospitality teacher at Gulf Breeze High School, Gulf Breeze, Santa Rosa County, Florida. Age 35 at start of offense (turned 36 two days before arrest July 29, 2025). Navarre, FL resident.

Victim: student, age 17, Gulf Breeze High School. Offense period: February 1 – July 18, 2025 (per warrant). School administration had previously investigated Mersereau and the student before the end of the 2024-25 school year based on reports of the student riding in Mersereau's personal vehicle and spending time in it before school; both provided written statements during the internal investigation, which confirmed Mersereau frequently texted the student from her personal phone (policy violation) and gave the student rides in her vehicle.

Grooming detail from warrant: Love notes handwritten by Mersereau found hidden in victim's bedroom, numbered as a countdown to victim's 18th birthday; some hidden inside a hollowed-out copy of Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince. A hidden iPad (bearing Mersereau's image as background) was found in victim's possession — victim's parents had not purchased it. Victim visited Mersereau's apartment (Reserve Apartments, Navarre) multiple times; victim lied to parents about location. Parents used Life360 family locator app to identify visits. Forensic extractions of seized devices yielded intimate text messages and group notes, grooming speech from Mersereau directed at victim, photographs of Mersereau and victim on outings outside school, and text messages about evading the investigation.

After victim's devices were seized as evidence, Mersereau purchased a prepaid Android burner phone and coordinated via Discord with victim's best friend (a 17-year-old third party) to smuggle the device to the victim. The friend admitted to smuggling the phone and to lying in an earlier police interview to cover for Mersereau. Investigators recovered the burner phone, confirmed as matching the device model seized from Mersereau. Physical contact confirmed: victim's best friend stated that on at least one occasion, Mersereau gave the victim a hickey.

Case originated from anonymous Crime Stoppers tip (from Mersereau's personal friend who stated Mersereau admitted the relationship directly) forwarded to SRSO July 8, 2025. Arrested July 29, 2025 at her residence (Warrant SRSO25WAR002250; Deputy Tyler Lee Mabire). Bond: $41,000.

Charges: engagement in romantic relationship with student by authority figure (F2); use of two-way communication device to facilitate a felony (F3) × 2; criminal solicitation (F2); contributing to the delinquency of a minor. Placed on administrative leave; unpaid suspension recommended to school board.

NOTE: Open case as of July 2025. Burner phone purchased and smuggled to victim after investigation began — clear evidence of concealment/obstruction. Love notes with countdown to 18th birthday.
