Former special education teacher at Carpenter Middle School, Plano ISD, Plano, Texas (Collin County). Age 38 at time of offense. Earned special education certification in 2004 and general classroom certification in 2008.

Victim was a 14-year-old boy enrolled at Carpenter Middle School but not one of Kieffer's students. Kieffer got the boy drunk and had sex with him multiple times at her home. Two specific offense dates confirmed from the indictment: March 1, 2009 and June 15, 2009.

Kieffer turned herself in to Collin County authorities in August 2009 and was released on bond; placed on administrative leave and subsequently terminated by Plano ISD. A Collin County grand jury indicted her on March 30, 2010 on four counts: two counts of Sexual Assault of a Child (TPC § 22.011(a)(2), F2) and two counts of Improper Relationship Between Educator and Student (TPC § 21.12, F2).

On January 31, 2011, Kieffer entered a guilty (no-contest) plea to all four counts before the 380th District Court. Jury trial was canceled. She received 10 years of deferred adjudication community supervision on each count (concurrent), sex offender registration, and was ordered to surrender her teaching credentials (surrendered April 2011). Defense attorney at plea: Ward Maedgen (retained).

On October 3, 2012, the state filed a Motion to Adjudicate Probation on all counts; a warrant was issued and executed December 27, 2012. On February 22, 2013, Kieffer appeared with court-appointed counsel Darlina Crowder, pleaded TRUE to all violations, and was admonished. The motion to adjudicate was denied; deferred adjudication was continued with all original terms plus additional conditions: reside with parents, report to Plano CSCD, continue sex offender treatment. On February 9, 2021, the court entered an Order Discharging Probation on Counts I through IV. No formal conviction was entered.
