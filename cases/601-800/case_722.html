Former teacher at South Iredell High School in Iredell-Statesville Schools (Troutman, North Carolina, Iredell County). Resided in Mooresville. Age 37 at the time of the documented offense conduct. The known victim was a male student at South Iredell High School described as over 16; victim age is recorded here as 17, consistent with the arrest report description.

Mills had sexual contact with the victim at multiple locations in Iredell County; none of the conduct occurred on school property.

The case opened in an unusual way. On approximately January 21, 2015, an Iredell County deputy on patrol encountered Mills standing outside a suspicious vehicle parked at the dead end of Moose Club Road in Troutman; the student was sitting inside the vehicle. The student was subsequently interviewed at a child-advocacy center in Statesville, and Mills was arrested following that interview and held on a $10,000 secured bond.

On October 9, 2015, Mills pleaded guilty before Iredell County Superior Court Judge Mark E. Klass to one count of indecent liberties with a student. The more serious sexual-activity-with-a-student count, which would have established penetrative conduct, was dismissed pursuant to the plea. Judge Klass imposed a community-supervision disposition under North Carolina structured sentencing: twelve months of supervised probation, with a 3-to-13-month confinement range suspended, plus a mental-health evaluation, a substance-abuse assessment, and a DNA sample.
