Former substitute teacher, Carlisle County Schools, Bardwell, Carlisle County, Kentucky. Age 28 at time of offense and sentencing (February 2017). Hired January 2016; began teaching March 1, 2016; designated long-term substitute toward end of 2015-16 school year.

Two male victims, both age 16, met through school. Sexual contact occurred between March 1 and June 28, 2016. Kentucky State Police noted that while both victims were 16 (Kentucky's age of consent), Warren's position of authority precluded legal consent — KSP: 'It's the same charge that would go to a jail guard for having sex with an inmate.' Case began with a KSP tip; two-month investigation followed.

Arrested August 15, 2016 following grand jury indictment on 3 counts third-degree rape + 3 counts third-degree sodomy. Held on $10,000 cash bond; released. Arraigned McCracken Circuit Court June 27, 2016; pleaded not guilty. Pleaded guilty November/December 2016 to 3 counts third-degree rape + 2 counts third-degree sodomy (1 count dismissed). Requested to withdraw plea; denied. At sentencing February 9, 2017, defense again moved to withdraw guilty plea; denied by Judge Clymer. Warren apologized in court to victims, their families, and her own family including her five-year-old daughter.

Sentenced to 3 years per plea agreement (Prosecutor Leigh Ann Dycus). Must complete sex offender treatment program before release. Sex offender registration for life. Five-year conditional discharge upon release.

NOTE: Multiple male victims (2). Prosecution venue McCracken County, not Carlisle (school county). Defense sought plea withdrawal twice; denied both times. KSP confirmed position-of-authority as statutory basis for charges. WPSD notes offenses occurred in summer when Warren was no longer employed by the district, though KSP estimated contact window beginning March 1.
