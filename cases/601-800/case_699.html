Former French teacher at Madison High School and Churchill High School, Northeast Independent School District (NEISD), San Antonio, Texas (Bexar County). Age 23 at arrest (January 2015), 24 at sentencing (October 2015). Taught in NEISD from January 2014 until resignation December 9, 2014.

Victim was a 15-year-old male student. According to phone records, Gonzalez and the student exchanged texts and spoke by phone for lengthy periods, including late at night. On December 5, 2014, the boy's parents notified the district after finding inappropriate text messages on their son's phone. The district placed Gonzalez on administrative leave and began termination proceedings; she resigned before the process was completed.

On January 20, 2015, a police officer responding to a burglar alarm on the Northeast Side drove past a parked Mazda with fogged windows. Looking inside, he found Gonzalez straddling the 15-year-old in the passenger seat; the two had been engaged in "heavy petting" and the student's shirt was partially lifted. The teen initially told the officer he was 18 before the officer determined his true age. The boy's parents, called to the scene, informed the officer of Gonzalez's prior resignation under similar allegations. Gonzalez admitted to having a sexual relationship with the student when taken into custody.

Gonzalez was arrested January 23, 2015 and charged with sexual assault of a minor. On October 19, 2015, she accepted a plea deal and was sentenced to five years of deferred adjudication probation, a $2,000 fine, seven weekends in jail, and sex offender registration.
