Former special education instructional aide at Land O'Lakes High School, Pasco County School District, Land O'Lakes, Florida (Pasco County). Age 35 at arrest (May 2016) and offense. Had worked in the district since 2012 with no prior demerits.

Two male student victims. Victim 1 was a 15-year-old student in Borsch's special education class; victim 2 was a 17-year-old Land O'Lakes High student who was friends with victim 1 but not in Borsch's class. Borsch had befriended the 15-year-old and his mother. On April 30, 2016, while the 15-year-old was home alone at his house in Wesley Chapel, Borsch went to the residence and had intercourse with him. On May 3, 2016, Borsch was spending the night at the same Wesley Chapel home when she had intercourse with the 17-year-old who was also staying over. Borsch admitted to both incidents in calls to the 15-year-old's mother that were recorded by detectives.

Borsch was arrested May 6, 2016 and booked into the Land O'Lakes Detention Center on $60,000 bail. She was suspended with pay pending School Board action. Initial charges were unlawful sexual activity with a minor (Fla. Stat. § 794.05(1)) for the 17-year-old victim and sexual battery by a person in custodial authority (Fla. Stat. § 794.011(8)(b)) for the 15-year-old victim; the custodial sexual battery charge was later reduced to lewd or lascivious battery (Fla. Stat. § 800.04(4)(b)) when filed by the prosecutor.

On June 6, 2019, both counts were adjudicated guilty without trial. Borsch was sentenced to three years in state prison concurrent on both counts, with credit for approximately two months and sixteen days time served.

NOTE: Both incidents occurred off campus at the same private residence. Borsch admitted to both on recorded calls -- an unusual self-incrimination mechanism. The three-year gap between arrest (May 2016) and adjudication (June 2019) is notable.
