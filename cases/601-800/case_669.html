Former physical education teacher at Apopka Memorial Middle School, Apopka, Florida (Orange County). Age 44 at onset of offense (school year 2015-2016).

Victim was a 14-year-old female student (at time of arrest, October 2016) who had attended Apopka Memorial Middle School the prior school year and was now enrolled at Apopka High School. London communicated with the victim through Snapchat and Kik messaging apps, accumulating approximately 129,000 messages over the course of the relationship. Messages were described as sexually explicit, discussing sex, rape, drugs, and abuse. London referred to the victim as her 'daughter' and sent messages saying 'I love you, baby girl.' She told the victim she first developed feelings for her in 7th grade and 'fell in love' with her in 8th grade. In one August 13 exchange, London explained orgasms to the teen and instructed her how to pleasure herself. London also confided she had cheated on her husband Victor with 10 different men. School administrators ordered London to cease contact with the victim; she allegedly continued relaying messages through third parties. The victim's mother had removed her from London's PE class during the prior school year. Both the victim and London denied any physical or sexual relationship in police interviews, characterizing the relationship as mentorship. London claimed someone had hacked her messaging accounts. The victim's mother had expressed concern the prior school year about her daughter communicating with London outside school.

London also learned from the victim that the victim was addicted to drugs and alcohol, had been raped on multiple occasions, and was being beaten at home by her mother — all facts triggering mandatory reporting obligations as a teacher under FL § 39.205.

DCF received an anonymous report and investigated; on September 27, 2016, Apopka Police were notified of the DCF investigation. London was arrested October 28, 2016 at Apopka Memorial Middle School.

Original charges: 4 counts solicitation to commit sexual battery (FL § 794.011(4)(A), 2nd degree felony), 5 counts failure to report child abuse (FL § 39.205(1), 3rd degree felony), 1 count transmission of material harmful to minors (FL § 847.0138(2), 3rd degree felony), 1 count unlawful use of two-way communication device (FL § 934.215, 3rd degree felony). All solicitation, transmission, and 4 of the 5 failure-to-report counts were nolle prosequi. Adjudicated guilty on Count 5 (unlawful use of two-way communication device) and Count 6 (failure to report child abuse). Prosecutor: ASA Sean Wiggins. Defense: Kimberly Lasure.

Sentenced December 8, 2017 by Judge Mark S. Blechman to 5 months Orange County Jail (concurrent on both counts, credit for 1 day time served; to report January 3, 2018), 4 years supervised probation, ,238 fines and costs. Permanent revocation of educator certification. Prohibited from employment or volunteering with any entity providing contact with minors. Psycho-sexual evaluation and treatment required within 30 days of release.

NOTE: The 4 solicitation-to-commit-sexual-battery counts (the primary sex offense charges) were all nolle prosequi'd. The conviction was on the communication device and mandatory-reporting charges only. No sex offender registration was imposed. The case is included in the DB because the 129,000-message communication record and the solicitation-level charges establish a grooming pattern, even absent a conviction for contact offenses. The failure-to-report conviction is analytically significant: London knew the victim was experiencing abuse, rape, and addiction, and did not report — a compound failure of the custodial/mandatory-reporter role. The female victim/female teacher dynamic is notable.
