Former physical education teacher and coach at Arlington High School, Arlington, Tennessee (Shelby County). Age 29 at time of offense.

Multiple victims — at least two minor students. In addition to physical sexual conduct, Hopkins sent nude photographs of herself to students. Original charges (Shelby County): 3 counts of statutory rape by an authority figure (TCA § 39-13-532) and 2 counts of soliciting sexual exploitation of a minor. One alleged victim was determined to be 18 at the time of the offense; that count was dropped.

In approximately July 2012, Hopkins pleaded guilty to 2 counts of aggravated statutory rape (a reduced charge). On August 24, 2012, she was sentenced to 4 years probation with no jail time. She was required to register as a sex offender within 48 hours of sentencing, prohibited from teaching, and prohibited from employment involving anyone under 18. No-contact orders were issued for the two student victims.
