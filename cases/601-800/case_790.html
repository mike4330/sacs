Former physical education teacher and coach at Arlington High School, Arlington, Tennessee (Shelby County). Hopkins had taught at Arlington for nearly six years at the time of her arrest. Age 29 at time of offense.

Multiple victims, at least two minor students. Hopkins texted nude pictures of herself to the students, and the students admitted to having sex with her. The third student to come forward described a sexual relationship beginning in early 2011 with each incident occurring at Hopkins' Cordova home.

Initial arrest in May 2011 on charges of statutory rape by an authority figure. After the first arrest, Hopkins' ex-boyfriend approached investigators with information that led a third student to come forward. Charges expanded to three counts of statutory rape by an authority figure and two counts of soliciting sexual exploitation of a minor. Shelby County Schools confirmed Hopkins' termination on May 26, 2011. One alleged victim was later determined to have been 18 at the time of the offense and that count was dropped.

In approximately July 2012, Hopkins pleaded guilty to two counts of aggravated statutory rape. On August 24, 2012, she was sentenced to four years of probation with no jail time. She was required to register as a sex offender within 48 hours of sentencing, was prohibited from teaching, and was prohibited from any employment involving people under 18. No-contact orders were issued for the two student victims.
