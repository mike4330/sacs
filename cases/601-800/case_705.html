Former drama teacher at Weaver Academy (a magnet school), Guilford County Schools, Greensboro, North Carolina (Guilford County). Taught in Guilford County from August 2005 to June 2008. Age 25 at time of offense (2007); 26 at arrest (July 23, 2008). Victim age not recorded in available sources.

Victim was a male student at Weaver Academy. Macklin and the student engaged in both oral and vaginal intercourse three to five times at Macklin's home. Police received a complaint on July 1, 2008; five warrants were issued July 21, 2008. A letter apparently written by Macklin to the victim was referenced in charging documents; in it she wrote: "I was doomed from the moment I met you. You made me feel needed and trusted and loved."

Macklin turned herself in and was arrested July 23, 2008, charged across three consolidated case numbers with five counts of sexual activity between school personnel and a student (NC GS § 14-27.7(B)). The case was transferred to Guilford Superior Court in December 2008. On March 23, 2009, Macklin pleaded guilty to one count of sex offense — student (Felony G); the remaining counts were disposed in the consolidated cases. Guilford Superior Court sentenced her to 36 months of intensive supervised adult probation, with a suspended confinement sentence of 13–16 months. Additional conditions: 100 hours of community service, sex offender registration, and costs and fines. Final discharge from probation: May 22, 2012. Case No. 08CR095414-400. As of 2026, Macklin is listed on the NC sex offender registry as residing out-of-state in Arlington, Virginia (SRN: 017629S3).
