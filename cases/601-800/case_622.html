Former Director of College Counseling and Assistant Swim Coach at Malvern Preparatory School (private Catholic prep school), Malvern, Chester County, Pennsylvania. Age approximately 39 at start of offense (spring 2014); 41 at sentencing (March 2016). Harvard alumna (1997) and Columbia MA.

Victim: male, age 16, junior on the swim team. Feeney exploited his collegiate aspirations, intimating she could secure him a Harvard athletic scholarship. Beginning spring/summer 2014, sent thousands of sexually explicit emails and texts from her school email account, including a topless photo. Victim explicitly rejected her advances but she continued pursuit. December 7, 2014: Feeney escorted victim to her office, locked the door, sat in his lap, kissed him, and attempted to initiate sexual intercourse; victim refused and returned to class. Victim reported to parents January 2015. Father notified school administrators; victim received a new academic counselor but Feeney remained swim coach. School administrator emailed Feeney minimizing the complaint as 'one negative experience' and expressed personal trust in her. Chester County CPS received report May 13, 2015; investigation opened by Chester County Detective Child Abuse Unit.

Charges filed August 2015: Institutional Sexual Assault, Corruption of a Minor, Unlawful Contact with a Minor, and Indecent Assault; released on $75,000 bail. Pleaded guilty March 7, 2016 to Corruption of Minors (§ 6301(a)(1)(i)(3), M1) and Indecent Assault Without Consent (§ 3126(a)(14), M2). Sentenced same day by Chester County Judge Thomas G. Gavin: 5 years probation on count 1, 2 years probation on count 2 (concurrent); 15-year sex offender registration; $3,710 restitution (victim's ongoing counseling costs); $200 fines; $250 DNA test. No prison. Probation conditions: no unsupervised contact with males under 15, no contact with youth ages 15–17, no contact with victim or his family, continued therapy. Defense attorney Michael J. Engle. A parallel misdemeanor case on identical charges was filed in Delaware County (CP-23-MD-0000906-2016); no disposition recorded.

Civil suit filed June 2, 2016 by the victim against Malvern Preparatory School (Chester County Court of Common Pleas) for assault, battery, negligence, and emotional distress; represented by Anapol Weiss / Sol Weiss and James Pepper. School head Talbot stated no procedural changes resulted from the criminal case.

NOTE: Private Catholic boys' prep school; male victim. Clear institutional failure — school administrator explicitly minimized father's January 2015 complaint and emailed Feeney reassurance of personal trust. Coercive dynamic leveraged Ivy League admissions influence; victim rejected advances repeatedly in writing. Attempted intercourse (thwarted by victim) is the most serious physical contact; convicted charges involved kissing/lap-sitting and the ongoing harassment campaign. No prison despite physical contact incident. sentence_years=0 (probation only).
