Former math teacher at Perrin High School, Perrin-Whitt CISD, Perrin, Texas (Jack County). Age 29 at time of offense. Currently registered as Kimme Annette Moss (Mineral Wells, TX, as of May 2025).

Two male victims: a 16-year-old and an 18-year-old, both students at Perrin High School. The victims reportedly initiated contact after hearing a rumor that Woolf had left her prior district (Mineral Wells ISD) for similar conduct. According to the arrest warrant affidavit, Woolf initially declined their advances but eventually agreed. With the 16-year-old, sexual intercourse occurred on multiple occasions, including at least once at the victim's residence (Woolf was seen leaving by the victim's father on approximately October 20, 2010). With the 18-year-old, Woolf performed oral sex on two occasions. The relationships were discovered when the 16-year-old's mother found text messages on her son's phone; both victims' families initially sent Woolf a private letter asking her to resign before eventually contacting the principal. Woolf's husband confirmed his suspicions when Woolf herself admitted to having sexual relations with students.

Arrested November 25, 2010 after surrendering to the Parker County Sheriff's Department on two warrants; released on $30,000 bail. Charged April 2011 on seven counts: two counts sexual assault of a child (TPC § 22.011(a)(2), F2), two counts improper relationship with a student under 17 (TPC § 21.12, F2), and three counts improper relationship with a student over 17. Cases filed in two separate cause numbers.

On June 24, 2011, Woolf pleaded guilty to four counts of improper relationship between educator and student across both cases. The sexual assault counts were dismissed at plea. Sentencing occurred July 1, 2011. Defense attorney: Bob Glasgow. Prosecutor: Jay Lapham (Jack County DA).

Sentenced to prison; on parole/mandatory supervision per Texas SOR registry (disposition reflected as "PAROLE/MANDATORY SUPERVISION," lifetime registration). Exact sentence length not confirmed in available sources. Woolf was advised she would register as a sex offender for the rest of her life. Registered in Mineral Wells TX (Palo Pinto/Parker County area) as of May 2025, consistent with prior employment at Mineral Wells ISD before her hire at Perrin.
