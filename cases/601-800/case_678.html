Tiffany Ann Blair #678 sentenced

StateAlabamaCourtCovington County Circuit Court
Defendant Age35 Victim Age15
Prison Sentence10.0 yrs

Opp, Alabama (Covington County). Not a school employee; included for Covington County cluster context (see Hall, Opp HS English teacher, same era). Age 35 at arrest (January 2015), 36 at sentencing (November 2015).

Victim was the 15-year-old male friend of Blair's own son. Investigation began after Blair told friends she was in a sexual relationship with the boy. During the investigation, both Blair and the victim told investigators they had sexual intercourse several times. Blair was arrested in January 2015 and initially charged with four counts of chemical endangerment of a child, rape second degree, and sodomy second degree.

Blair pleaded guilty to second degree rape before the case was presented to a grand jury. Sentenced to 10 years in the Alabama Department of Corrections. Sex offender registration required upon release.

NOTE: Two other Opp women -- Kristina Burroughs and Mandi Brianna Harrison -- were arrested the same week as Blair on rape second degree and sodomy second degree charges; Chelsea Alexandria Calhoun (20) was charged with rape second degree. None are educators. The cluster of four sex-crime arrests in Opp in early 2015, plus the Hall teacher case in May 2015, represents an unusual concentration for a small town.
