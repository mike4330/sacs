Former special education teacher at Habersham Ninth Grade Academy, Habersham County, Georgia. Age 34 at resignation.

School officials launched an investigation into text messages Terrell sent to a student. She resigned March 8, 2021. On March 19, 2021, the school system contacted the Habersham County Sheriff's Office about the texts, which had crossed the professional threshold. Investigation revealed that during or after the ethics investigation, Terrell became physically involved with the student. Victim sex not stated in sources; victim was a student at Habersham Ninth Grade Academy (9th grade). Victim age approximately 14 inferred from 9th-grade enrollment.

Terrell turned herself in March 26, 2021 on a warrant; bond set at $71,400. She was rearrested May 21, 2021 for violating bond by contacting the student; bond was revoked and four additional charges were added. The physical relationship continued after the initial arrest; at sentencing the state noted she had again had sex with the student while on bond.

Pleaded not guilty November 2021 and demanded trial by jury. Defense claimed an unlawful stop and search without Miranda warnings and moved to suppress resulting evidence. Pleaded guilty October 3, 2022 to two counts of child molestation. Sentenced October 28, 2022 by Judge Chan Caudell to 30 years total — 16 to serve in prison, remainder on probation (including 4 years supervised). Sex offender registration required. Prohibited from communicating with, working with, or residing with children under 18 (other than her own) during probation. Required to complete sex offender evaluation and treatment. Twelve additional charges dismissed as part of the plea.

Dismissed charges included: 2 counts statutory rape, 2 counts enticing a child for indecent purposes, sexual battery against a child under 16, influencing a witness, making a false statement, sexual contact by employee or agent (first degree), exhibiting pornography to minors, interference with custody, obstruction of an officer, and electronically furnishing obscene material to minors.
