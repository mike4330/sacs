Former karate instructor at Next-Gen Martial Arts, Goldenrod Road, Orlando, Florida (Orange County). DOB June 19, 1995; age 20 at time of offense. Not a school employee — private martial arts studio setting (cf. Blanchard #36, behavioral health).

Victim was an 11-year-old male student at the karate school. Beginning in February 2016, Figueroa made physical contact with the victim (placing her hand on his leg, cuddling, kissing) when they were alone in the studio office. She sent him more than a dozen nude photographs via the Kik messaging app and had explicit sexual conversations, telling him she "could not wait for another sleepover at the karate school so she could have sex with him in a hard way." She also invited the victim to her home to have sex with her on her 21st birthday (June 19, 2016). The victim's mother discovered the messages on her son's phone in June 2016 and contacted police. Officers examined the phone but were unable to recover the messages or photographs. Police indicated they were investigating possible additional victims.

On June 17, 2016, Figueroa was charged with six felony counts. On June 20, 2017, she entered a nolo contendere plea to Counts 2–6; Count 1 (attempted lewd or lascivious conduct, Fla. Stat. § 800.04(6)(B)) was dismissed via Nolle Prosequi. She was adjudicated guilty on all five remaining counts and sentenced to sex offender probation with no prison time. Defense attorney: Michael Panella. Judge: Eric J. Netcher, Division 16, Orange County Circuit Court (Case No. 2016-CF-007907-A-O).

Figueroa was subsequently found in violation of probation on two separate occasions. First VOP (affidavit filed January 16, 2019): she admitted to violations involving possession of two internet-connected devices; on March 18, 2019, the court ordered 60 days in Orange County Jail (credit for 55 days time served) and reinstated probation, finding she was not a danger to the community. Second VOP (affidavit filed August 30, 2019): she admitted to violations on September 13, 2019; the court ordered 30 days in Orange County Jail (credit for 9 days time served) and again reinstated probation. On December 18, 2020, a motion for early termination of probation was denied.
