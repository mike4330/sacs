Taylor Janell Anderson #653 sentenced

StateFloridaCourtManatee County Circuit Court (12th Judicial Circuit)
JudgeFrederick P. Mercurio Incident Date2021-07-01
Defendant Age38 Victim Age17
Prison Sentence1.0 yr

English teacher at IMG Academy, a preparatory boarding school and sports training facility in Bradenton, Florida (Manatee County). Hired January 2021. Married mother of a 6-year-old son. Previously nominated for Teacher of the Year in Missouri in 2016; was working on a second master's degree. Had a prior 2017 DUI charge in Kansas City, Missouri.

Victim was a 17-year-old male IMG Academy student who was set to turn 18 in March. Anderson and the victim began communicating via Instagram after the victim learned she had communicated with another student that way. At some point in July or August 2021, they agreed to meet; Anderson picked him up, took him to Culver's, then drove to a Manatee County beach where they had sex in the backseat of her car. Communication continued via Instagram, Snapchat, and FaceTime throughout July and August. FaceTime videos recovered from the victim's phone (July 24 and August 24, 2021) showed Anderson masturbating and exposing her breasts on her bed while the victim watched, and showed both of them masturbating during the first call. Additional videos from the backseat of Anderson's car were recovered. The victim stopped contact when Anderson asked him to watch videos depicting older females with younger males. Rumors circulated among students since July.

IMG Academy administration confronted the student on September 14, 2021; the student admitted the relationship. Anderson was banned from campus immediately; terminated the next day. Detectives obtained search warrants and seized her phone September 15. Anderson evaded detectives for days and left the state before surrendering at Manatee County Jail on September 20, 2021. Case number 2021 CF 2530.

Jury trial held December 19–20, 2022. State prosecutors: Ashley Dusnik and Lauren Benson. Defense attorney: Jennifer Fury. Verdict: acquitted Count I (sexual battery by custodial authority); convicted Count II of lesser included offense — soliciting a child for unlawful sexual conduct using computer services (FL § 847.0135(3)(a), 3rd degree felony); convicted Count III — giving obscene material to a minor as charged.

Sentenced March 6, 2023 by Judge Frederick P. Mercurio to downward departure: 11 months 29 days county jail (concurrent across counts, concurrent with related case) + 2 years community control + 2 years probation. Sex offender registration required. Special conditions: no contact with victim; no social media accounts; no attending school functions (may pick up/drop off own child at school); psychological and drug evaluations; participate in therapeutic treatment per Dr. Yamout's report; random urinalysis twice per month; no alcohol.

In September 2024, Anderson filed a pro se civil lawsuit (Case No. 2024CA001455AX, Manatee County Circuit Court) against the victim and his parents, claiming the victim unlawfully intercepted and recorded two FaceTime communications on July 24 and August 25, 2021 without her consent, and that his parents negligently supervised his phone use. She sought compensatory damages exceeding $50,000 plus punitive damages.

NOTE: Defendant age not stated in sources. Acquittal on the primary sexual battery charge (Count I) despite victim testimony of intercourse in her car — the jury apparently required the physical evidence (which was limited on her phone) rather than the victim's account. The civil suit against the victim is an extreme victim-blaming tactic — Anderson is arguing she was harmed by the recording of the FaceTime sessions that formed part of the prosecution evidence. The downward departure sentence on a sex offender registration-triggering conviction is unusual.
