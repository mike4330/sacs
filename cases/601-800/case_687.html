Former teacher at Sakai Intermediate School and coach at Bainbridge High School, Bainbridge Island School District, Kitsap County, Washington. Age 35 at charges (August 2017), 36 at sentencing (February 2018). Engaged at time of offense; told victim she was "unhappy in her relationship with her fiance and wanted to leave him and live with the victim."

Victim was an 18-year-old female athlete who played on at least two teams Hebner coached. The sexual relationship occurred during the 2015-2016 school year, often at Hebner's home located down the street from the high school. Evidence from the victim's phone included numerous messages between Hebner and the student about being together and meeting places, as well as photos of the two together.

Hebner disclosed the relationship in July 2016, telling friends she had an "inappropriate relationship with a student." School administrators placed her on leave; she then resigned. The Bainbridge Island Police Department investigated for approximately one year. The investigation was delayed because the agency was also involved in the presidential election and the initial detective assigned to the case left the department.

On August 24, 2017, Hebner was charged with first-degree sexual misconduct with a minor. She appeared at the Kitsap County Jail for processing on a summons to appear. On February 1, 2018, Hebner pleaded guilty to fourth-degree assault with sexual motivation, a misdemeanor charge reduction. She was sentenced to 30 days in custody, convertible to electronic home monitoring if found eligible.

NOTE: Victim was 18 years old at the time -- an adult victim. Washington's sexual misconduct statute applies to school employees who engage in sexual contact with enrolled students regardless of age. The charge reduction from first-degree sexual misconduct (felony) to fourth-degree assault with sexual motivation (misdemeanor) is consistent with two similar Kitsap County cases resolved the same week (Carnegie, Iversen -- both male perpetrators, both received 30-day sentences). Second Bainbridge Island School District case within two years; see Fuchs #686 (same district, 2015).
