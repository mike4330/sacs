Former teacher's aide at White House Middle School, Sumner County Schools, White House, Tennessee (Sumner County). Age approximately 40 at onset of offense. Married ("Mrs. Cartwright" per news reports).

Victim was a male student at a Sumner County high school (not at White House Middle School where Cartwright worked). He was 15 years old when the sexual relationship began; he was 17 at the time of the investigation in June 2022. Communication began on Snapchat. Cartwright and the victim had sexual intercourse approximately 15 times. The docket lists the offense window beginning January 1, 2019, though arrest-era reporting states the relationship began approximately two years prior to June 2022 (i.e., approximately 2020); victim age of 15 at onset is consistent with either start date.

On June 8, 2022, a concerned family member of the 17-year-old victim contacted Millersville Police Department. Detectives investigated and presented the case to the Sumner County Grand Jury in August 2022. Sealed indictments were returned for statutory rape by an authority figure and continuous sexual abuse of a child. Cartwright was arrested August 9, 2022 and booked into the Sumner County Jail. Sumner County Schools suspended her without pay pending the criminal case.

Indicted March 9, 2023 (Sumner County Criminal Court) on 14 counts: Count 1 continuous sexual abuse of a child (TCA § 39-13-518), Counts 2-6 statutory rape by authority figure (TCA § 39-13-532), Counts 7-11 aggravated statutory rape (TCA § 39-13-506), Counts 12-14 solicitation of a minor — aggravated statutory rape (TCA § 39-13-528). Judge: Dee David Gay. Case was continued multiple times through 2023-2025.

On December 2, 2025, Cartwright entered a guilty plea as charged on Counts 7-11 (5 counts aggravated statutory rape). Counts 1-6 and 12-14 were nolle prosequi'd. Sentencing hearing was scheduled March 6, 2026 but rescheduled; a review hearing is set for April 23, 2026. As of entry date, sentencing has not occurred.

NOTE: Aggravated statutory rape under TCA § 39-13-506 applies when the victim is 13-17 and the defendant is 10 or more years older — a Class D felony for each count. The victim was a high school student, not a student at WHMS; the authority-figure charge applies because Cartwright was a school employee and the victim was enrolled in the same school district. Sentencing to be updated when imposed.
