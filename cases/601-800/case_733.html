Former teacher at the Maurice A. Donahue Elementary School (K-8), Holyoke, Massachusetts (Hampden County). Age 24 at time of offense. Ludlow resident.

Victim: 15-year-old male 8th-grade student. Relationship began in October 2008 when the boy obtained Lavoie's cell phone number; they began texting and the relationship became sexual. Lavoie warned the boy that if it were discovered she would lose her job, but the relationship continued. After approximately a year, the boy feared Lavoie might harm herself and they decided to flee. The boy's mother discovered him missing from their home on October 14, 2009 at approximately 1:30 a.m. Bank card records tracked Lavoie through Vermont, New York, and Delaware. West Virginia state police located Lavoie and the boy at a motel in Morgantown, West Virginia, and she was arrested there. FBI and U.S. Border Patrol were involved in the search. Lavoie was held 10 days before release on $25,000 bail. (Note: the January 2011 sentencing article incorrectly states the flight occurred in "February 2009.")

Originally charged with 6 counts of statutory rape, 3 counts of aggravated statutory rape under Massachusetts "Jessica's Law" (10-year mandatory minimum; the aggravated counts applied because, as a mandated reporter, Lavoie was required to report suspected abuse of her students), and 3 counts of enticement. The aggravated charges were dropped in the plea agreement. Pleaded guilty December 15, 2010, in Hampden Superior Court before Judge Cornelius J. Moriarty, to 3 counts of statutory rape (illegal intercourse with a child under 16) and 1 count of enticement of a child under 16. Prosecution recommended 3–5 years state prison. The victim submitted an impact statement requesting leniency: "She means everything to me. So please don't send her away."

Sentenced January 26, 2011: 5 years probation with 10 conditions, including no contact with the victim or his immediate family. Moriarty stated he did not believe Lavoie was "a sexual predator likely to reoffend." ADA Patrick S. Sabbs had sought 3–5 years state prison; defense attorney David P. Hoose asked for probation and time served (10 days in West Virginia).

Probation violation, May 30, 2012: on March 9, 2012, Probation Officer Kevin Horne made a routine unannounced home visit to Lavoie's Ludlow apartment and found the (now 18-year-old) victim hiding in her closet. Lavoie was subsequently seen with him in a car on April 17, and a prosecutor reported seeing them together at a local mall. Hoose argued the no-contact condition became unconstitutional once the victim turned 18; Moriarty denied the motion to dismiss. The young man appeared at the violation hearing and pleaded to spare Lavoie: "Please don't take her away from me." Moriarty sentenced Lavoie to 18 months at Western Massachusetts Regional Women's Correctional Center, Chicopee, stating she "has no intent of abiding by court orders."
