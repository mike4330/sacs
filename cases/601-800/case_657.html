Teacher at Brazoswood High School, Brazosport ISD, Clute, Texas (Brazoria County). Former recipient of Teacher of the Year award. Defendant age at offense not stated in sources; still employed at Brazoswood at time of arrest (September 2023).

Victim was a 15-year-old female student. The relationship occurred off campus over approximately two years, beginning around 2006. Because the offense predated Texas's 2007 improper-relationship-with-a-student statute (TX PC § 21.12), charges were brought under sexual assault of a child (§ 22.011) and indecency with a child (§ 21.11).

Victim walked into the Clute Police Department in September 2023 to report the offense -- approximately 17 years after it occurred. Clute PD Chief James Fitch stated that witness testimony was critical to the investigation given the elapsed time; detectives tracked down former classmates and individuals the victim had made initial outcries to at the time. Hargrove was placed on administrative leave by Brazosport ISD in September 2023 upon notification. Indicted December 2023 on 12 counts: 6 counts sexual assault of a child (2nd degree felonies, 2-20 years each), 4 counts indecency with a child by contact (2nd/3rd degree felonies), and 2 counts indecency with a child by exposure (3rd degree felonies, 2-10 years each). Bond set at $240,000. Brazosport ISD fired Hargrove in early 2024.

Pleaded guilty August 27, 2025 to all 12 counts. Sentenced to 10 years probation with a 10-year probated/suspended sentence on all counts; no prison. Victim spoke at the sentencing hearing. Lifetime sex offender registration required per Chief Fitch. The Brazoria County DA declined to comment to media.

NOTE: Defendant age at offense unknown -- no birth year or age stated in available sources. Incident date 2006-09-01 is approximate (start of 2006-07 school year). The 17-year gap between offense and report is one of the longest delayed-reporting cases in the DB. Chief Fitch noted the victim had hesitations for years before coming forward. The no-prison outcome on 12 felony counts -- including six sexual assault counts with a combined maximum exposure well over 100 years -- is analytically significant.
