Former English teacher (10th and 11th grade) at Currituck County High School, Currituck County, North Carolina. Age 24 at offense and sentencing. Native of Gates, NC; East Carolina University graduate (2013); employed at CCHS since August 2013.

Victim was a male high school junior (approximately 16) at Currituck County High School. Sexual contact occurred in April and early May 2015. The relationship ended on the day the victim disclosed it to another teacher at the school; Umphlett was suspended with pay and resigned May 18, 2015. The offense was formally reported May 12, 2015.

Indicted June 29, 2015 on two counts: sex offense with a student (N.C.G.S. § 14-27.7(B), Class G felony) and crime against nature (N.C.G.S. § 14-177, Class I felony). Arrested June 30, 2015; released on $5,000 secured bond. Defense attorney: Kris J. Felthousen (retained). Prosecutor: Emily Rosemann Davis.

On April 11, 2016, Umphlett entered a no-contest plea to crime against nature before Judge Jerry R. Tillett. The sex offense with a student count was dismissed without leave by the DA (no plea agreement). Sentenced to a community sentence: 24 months supervised probation; confinement range 5-15 months suspended. No active incarceration. Special condition: no contact with or presence near the victim during probation period. DNA sample required.

NOTE: The conviction was on crime against nature (§ 14-177) rather than the educator-specific sex offense statute (§ 14-27.7(B)), which was dismissed. The plea was no-contest (nolo contendere), not a guilty plea, though the legal effect is the same. No sex offender registration is explicitly noted in the docket; § 14-177 involving a minor is a reportable offense under NC SORNA but registration was not mentioned as a condition. Docket spelling is "Kimberly Lynn" (without terminal e); articles consistently use "Kimberly Lynne." Case No. 15CR000343-260.
