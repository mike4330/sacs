Former special education teacher at Warren East High School (WEHS), Bowling Green, Kentucky (Warren County). Age 35 at arrest. Married; two children. Goes by "Niki."

Victim: 17-year-old male student at WEHS, not enrolled in Sumner's class. Relationship began approximately mid-October 2009 and continued for several months. Sumner admitted to police that she and the student had sexual intercourse in a motel room in Bowling Green, and engaged in kissing and touching to the point of sex in her car and apartment on other occasions. She also provided the student a cell phone on which she sent sexually explicit text messages.

Husband Timothy Sumner filed a domestic violence petition against her on December 15, 2009, stating he had witnessed her with the 17-year-old boy and that the relationship had been ongoing since mid-October. She filed a counter-petition the following day. She was fired from WEHS on January 28, 2010, and arrested the same day by Bowling Green Police. Arraigned January 29, 2010, in Warren District Court before Judge Sam Potter on 3 counts of first-degree sexual abuse; pleaded not guilty; released on $10,000 bond. Defense attorney at arraignment: Kyle Roby.

Pleaded guilty in July 2010 to 6 counts of first-degree sexual abuse (count increased from original 3). Sentenced October 11, 2010, in Warren Circuit Court by Judge Steve Wilson: 6 years prison and lifetime sex offender registration. Defense attorney Jason Hays argued for probation, citing no prior record and a sex offender evaluation showing low risk of reoffense; Commonwealth's Attorney Chris Cohron argued against probation. Wilson imposed the Commonwealth's recommended sentence.

Sumner acknowledged at sentencing that the affair ended her marriage and cost her custody of her two children. "I had a lot of issues with depression and was not thinking clearly about anything. I wish with everything in me I would've asked for help during the time this was taking place."
