Former paraprofessional teacher's aide at Fort McCoy School, Marion County Public Schools, Fort McCoy, Florida. Age 36 at time of offense. Married at time of offense; had worked in the district approximately one year.

Male victim, age 14. In the early morning hours of July 6, 2017, a Marion County Sheriff's Office deputy encountered Carsey partially clothed in a church parking lot; she claimed to be meeting an adult male friend. Months later, detectives received information that she had told someone she narrowly escaped being caught having sex with a student before the deputy arrived. When investigators questioned her, Carsey admitted to undressing and having sexual contact with the victim in her back seat. The victim confirmed he had been present and fled when he spotted the patrol car, telling investigators he touched her breast, that they kissed, and that she fondled him — but that the encounter was interrupted.

Arrested September 27, 2017 on an original charge of lewd or lascivious molestation. Placed on paid administrative leave, then terminated in October 2017 and banned from school campuses. Bond: $10,000. Prosecutors subsequently amended the charge to lewd or lascivious battery on a child (requiring "sexual activity" under Florida law); molestation counts nolle prosequi. Adjudicated guilty November 6, 2018 (no trial). Sentenced by Judge Jonathan Daniel Ohlman to five years in state prison plus five years of probation; five days credit for time served. Designated a sexual offender; electronic monitor required during probation. Prohibited from contact with the victim or his family; must complete a sex offender treatment program.
