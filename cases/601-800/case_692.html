Former paraprofessional teacher's aide at Fort McCoy School, Marion County Public Schools, Fort McCoy, Florida (Marion County). Age 36 at offense (July 2017), 37 at sentencing (November 2018). DOB January 30, 1981. Married at time of offense; had worked in the district approximately one year.

Victim was a 14-year-old male student. On July 6, 2017, a Marion County Sheriff's Office deputy investigating a suspicious vehicle found Carsey partially clothed in a church parking lot; she claimed to be meeting an adult male friend. Months later, detectives received information that Carsey had told someone she had narrowly escaped being caught having sex with her student before the deputy arrived. When investigators spoke to Carsey, she admitted to undressing and having inappropriate contact with the boy in her back seat. The victim confirmed he had been present and fled when he spotted the deputy's patrol car. The teen reported that Carsey had fondled him, that they had kissed, and that he had touched her breast, but the encounter was interrupted before anything further occurred.

Carsey was arrested in late September 2017 on a charge of lewd or lascivious molestation and held on a $10,000 bond. She was placed on paid administrative leave, then lost her job in October 2017 and was banned from school campuses.

On November 7, 2018, Carsey pleaded guilty to lewd or lascivious battery of a child 12 to 16 years old (Fla. Stat. § 800.04(4)) and was sentenced to five years in state prison followed by five years of probation. She was designated a sexual offender and will be equipped with an electronic monitor during probation. She is prohibited from contact with the victim or his family and must complete a sex offender treatment program.

NOTE: The conviction charge (lewd or lascivious battery, Fla.
