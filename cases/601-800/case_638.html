Lindsey Whiteside #638 sentenced

Former assistant girls' basketball coach at DeSoto Central High School (DeSoto County Schools), Mississippi. Left DeSoto Central in August 2022 to join Getwell Church Hernando as student and outreach coordinator and youth ministry leader. Defendant age not reported.

Victim was an underage female and member of Getwell Church Hernando. Approximately 16 years old at start of offense (described as 'now 18' at October 2025 sentencing; offense began May 14, 2024). Sexual activity occurred on multiple occasions between May 14 and November 6, 2024, including during church trips that crossed state lines. Evidence included 64,000 pages of printed text messages and handwritten notes.

Arrested November 21, 2024. Indicted by DeSoto County grand jury December 13, 2024, on one count of sexual battery of a child by a person of trust or authority (MS § 97-3-95(2)). Pleaded guilty October 13, 2025. Judge Andrew K. Howorth sentenced Whiteside to three years of house arrest followed by seven years of post-release supervision; sex offender registration required. No prison time. DA Matthew Barton publicly condemned the sentence, called it an 'absolute abomination of justice,' and filed a resentencing motion October 16 citing a Mississippi prohibition on house arrest for violent crimes. Nine DeSoto County school teachers and one vice principal submitted character references; school superintendent Cory Uselton stated these were personal actions not sanctioned by the district. School board member Michele Henley was subpoenaed by the DA and had to publicly clarify she did not support Whiteside. Judge Howorth recused October 21; Judge Jeff Weill appointed November 14, 2025. On February 9, 2026, Weill denied the resentencing motion, ruling the state cannot legally seek resentencing merely because it is 'unhappy with the result.' State sentence stands.

Federal case: Indicted in late October 2025 on two counts — transporting a minor across state lines for sexual purposes (18 U.S.C. § 2423(a)) and coercion and enticement of a minor (18 U.S.C. § 2422). Pleaded not guilty; remanded to custody after release was revoked. Federal trial delayed as of February 2026 to allow defense additional preparation time.

NOTE: State sentence finalized as house arrest — resentencing avenue exhausted. Federal case remains active as of February 2026. Whiteside's educator connection is her prior role as a public school basketball coach; offense occurred during subsequent church employment. Abuse crossing state lines triggered federal jurisdiction.
