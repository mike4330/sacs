Former school counselor and yoga instructor at Ascension Episcopal School, a private Episcopal school in Lafayette, Louisiana (Lafayette Parish). Holds a doctorate. Age 40 at sentencing (approximately 38–39 at onset of offense).

Victim: 14-year-old female student whom Hargrave began counseling in spring 2009. The counseling relationship continued through summer 2009. In fall 2009, Hargrave began texting and emailing the victim graphically about sexual activities and made plans to meet. In fall 2009, Hargrave raped the victim in a medical office in Lafayette. She continued to sexually exploit the victim throughout 2009 and into January 2010, and continued to send graphic text and email communications throughout.

Charged federally with attempting to entice a minor to engage in criminal sexual activity (18 U.S.C. § 2422(b)). Investigated by the FBI Lafayette Resident Agency under Project Safe Childhood. Prosecuted by AUSA J. Luke Walker, Western District of Louisiana. U.S. Attorney Stephanie A. Finley.

Sentenced August 3, 2011 by U.S. District Judge Richard T. Haik to 30 years in federal prison followed by 20 years supervised release. Ordered to pay $279,454 in restitution to the victim.

NOTE: Female victim. Rape occurred in a medical office — not on school property. "Doctor" title indicates advanced degree. Restitution of $279,454 is among the highest in the DB. Sentence of 30 years federal (no parole) is one of the longest in the DB. Hargrave exploited the counseling relationship — a direct misuse of a therapeutic trust structure. Private school.
