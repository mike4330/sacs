Former teacher at Willow Creek Positive Education Program (PEP), Eaton Township, Ohio (Lorain County). Age 24 at time of offense. Resides in Sheffield Lake, Ohio. PEP is a behavioral/alternative education program serving troubled students (cf. Blanchard #36).

Victim was a 17-year-old male student at the time the relationship began; he was 18 by the time Conway filed the harassment complaint.

On March 21, 2018, Conway went to the Lorain County Sheriff's Office to file a harassment complaint against the student — who had broken up with her. During the complaint process, she disclosed that she had been in a sexual relationship with him, which had begun when he was 17. Text messages between Conway and the student confirmed the relationship as well as the breakup and harassment. School officials reported the matter to law enforcement the same day and placed Conway on administrative leave. Charges against the student were investigated but not pursued.

On April 26, 2018, Conway turned herself in at the Lorain County Sheriff's Office and was charged with four counts of sexual battery (Ohio Rev. Code § 2907.03). Bond was set at $40,000 ($10,000 per count). The school announced termination proceedings.

On February 13, 2019, Conway pleaded guilty to the reduced charge of disseminating matter harmful to juveniles (Ohio Rev. Code § 2907.31). All four sexual battery counts were dismissed. Lorain County Common Pleas Court Judge John Miraldo sentenced her to 1 year probation.
