Former teacher at Willow Creek Positive Education Program in Eaton Township, Ohio (Lorain County). Resided in Sheffield Lake. Age 24 at the time of the documented offense conduct. Willow Creek operates as a behavioral and alternative-education program serving troubled students, an institutional setting with paired population vulnerability comparable to other alternative-school cases in the corpus. The known victim was a 17-year-old male student at the time the relationship began; he was 18 by the time the matter came to police attention.

The discovery vector is the case's most distinctive feature. On March 21, 2018, Conway went to the Lorain County Sheriff's Office to file a harassment complaint against the former student, who had broken up with her. During the complaint process she disclosed to investigators that she had been in a prior sexual relationship with him, beginning when he was 17. Text messages between Conway and the student preserved both the relationship and the breakup. The school district reported the matter to law enforcement the same day and placed Conway on administrative leave; charges against the student were investigated but not pursued.

On April 26, 2018, Conway turned herself in at the Lorain County Sheriff's Office and was charged with four counts of sexual battery under Ohio's teacher-student provision. Bond was set at $40,000 ($10,000 per count); the district announced termination proceedings.

On February 13, 2019, before Lorain County Common Pleas Court Judge John Miraldo, Conway pleaded guilty to a reduced charge of disseminating matter harmful to juveniles — a non-contact offense. The four sexual-battery counts were dismissed pursuant to the plea. Judge Miraldo sentenced her to one year of probation.
