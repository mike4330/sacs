Former English teacher and softball assistant coach at Jennings High School, Jefferson Davis Parish, Louisiana. Age 26 at arrest (August 2017), 28 at sentencing (September 2019). Married to Eric Cade Gillespie (married July 10, 2015 at Our Lady Help of Christians Catholic Church, Jennings); formerly Rachel Marie Bourne. Originally from Los Angeles.

Victim was a male student of Jennings High School; victim age not reported. A complaint was filed by the Jefferson Davis Parish School Board on August 22, 2017. The Jefferson Davis Parish Sheriff's Office initiated an investigation immediately, which led detectives to request an arrest warrant. Gillespie was arrested and booked into the parish jail on a $25,000 bond.

Conflicts of interest required both District Attorney Michael Cassidy and District Judge Steve Gunnell to recuse themselves from the case. The Louisiana Supreme Court appointed the Louisiana Attorney General's Office to handle prosecution and retired Lafayette Parish judge Durwood Conque as special judge.
