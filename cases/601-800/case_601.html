Former 8th grade literature teacher at Monmouth-Roseville Junior High School, Roseville/Monmouth, Warren County, Illinois. Age 39 at time of arrest (April 2015).

Used text messaging and Instagram to communicate with a 15-year-old male student over a period of several weeks, soliciting him to engage in oral sex. No evidence a sex act occurred. The student's parents complained to the district, prompting investigation. The district was notified April 6, 2015; deputies arrested Rexroat on April 13, 2015 at a Monmouth convenience store at 3:15 p.m. Placed on paid administrative leave pending investigation.

Originally charged with felony indecent solicitation of a child (Illinois statute carries 3–7 years prison and up to $25,000 fine). Held on $100,000 bond; released after posting 10%.

Pleaded guilty May 18, 2016 to the lesser charge of endangering the life or health of a child in Warren County Circuit Court. Sentenced to two years probation, $2,500 fine, surrender of teaching license, sex offender evaluation, and no contact with the victim or his family. No prison.

NOTE: Non-contact/digital offense — solicitation via Instagram; no physical contact. Original felony reduced to lesser charge at plea. No sex offender registration requirement noted for the plea charge. sentence_years = 0.
