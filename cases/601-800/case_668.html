Former 7th/8th grade language arts teacher at Williston Middle School, New Hanover County, NC (suspended Oct-Nov 2008, resigned Dec 31, 2008) and subsequently at Pactolus Elementary School, Pitt County, NC (hired Feb 2, 2009; resigned May 25, 2009). Separated from husband; had a young daughter. Age 27 at time of offense.

Victim was a 15-year-old male. The relationship predated the May 23, 2009 arrest. At 3:49 a.m. on May 23, 2009, a Wilmington police officer found Wishnask and the victim in "intimate contact" in a 2003 Pontiac Grand Am parked at 200 N. Fifth St. in Wilmington. She was arrested and charged with taking indecent liberties with a minor. Released on 0,000 secured bond.

On June 5, 2009, following continued investigation by the New Hanover County DA's Office and Wilmington police, Wishnask was arrested a second time in Martin County on additional charges of felony statutory rape (victim more than six years younger) and misdemeanor dissemination of harmful material, stemming from contact with the same victim before the May 23 arrest. She was held on 25,000 bond. DA Ben David stated further charges were pending.

Pleaded guilty January 13, 2010 to one count of taking indecent liberties with a minor. Sentenced to 3.5 years North Carolina state prison. Statutory rape and dissemination of harmful material charges resolved as part of plea.

Prior conduct: Wishnask was suspended at Williston Middle School in October and November 2008 — two separate suspensions — before resigning. When she was hired by Pitt County schools, she received recommendation letters from her New Hanover County references. The school board member Elizabeth Redenbaugh publicly stated the district had a responsibility to ensure someone with two suspensions did not receive recommendation letters. New Hanover County officials never released the reason for the suspensions.
