Lauren Kennamore #633 sentenced

StateMississippiCourtUnion County Circuit Court
Incident Date2024-02
Defendant Age36 Victim Age17
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former math and algebra teacher at West Union Attendance Center, Myrtle, Mississippi (Union County). Age approximately 36 at time of offense (February–March 2024); age 37 at sentencing. Resident of Pontotoc, Mississippi. Later employed as academic counselor for the University of Mississippi School of Journalism and New Media, Oxford, after being fired from her teaching position.

Accused of intentionally enticing a 17-year-old student to meet her for the purpose of engaging in sexually explicit conduct. Offense occurred in February or March 2024 while she was employed as a teacher. Union County Sheriff Jimmy Edwards stated Kennamore was fired as a result of the incident. She subsequently obtained employment at the University of Mississippi before the indictment was made public.

Indicted by Union County grand jury October 8, 2024; arrested and booked into Union County Jail the same day; released on $20,000 bond. Initially pleaded not guilty. University of Mississippi placed her on administrative leave October 9, 2024; assistant dean Kareem Russell temporarily assumed her counseling duties.

Changed plea to guilty September 8, 2025. Sentenced to five years suspended; five years supervised probation; sex offender registration required; $2,842 in court costs and fees. No prison time served.

NOTE: No-prison outcome on a single enticement count. Reporting notes the charge typically stops short of intercourse and that sexual battery by a person of trust would have applied if intercourse had occurred, suggesting physical contact was absent or limited. Defendant moved from K-12 teaching to university counseling after the firing — she was serving in a non-school institutional role at time of indictment.
