Former math teacher at Granada High School, Livermore, California (Alameda County). Age 40 at time of offense. Married.

Victim: 14-year-old male student who had finished taking Johnson's class in December 2010. Relationship developed through text messages, Facebook, and the Words with Friends smartphone app. Johnson allegedly sexually assaulted the boy 24 times from December 2010 through May 2011, in her car and at other undisclosed locations in Livermore.

Arrested January 11, 2012, after the victim told a high school coach on January 10 that he had been repeatedly molested, providing a detailed statement about a "long term sexual relationship." Johnson admitted the conduct when interviewed by Livermore police Detective Joseph Draghi. Bail set at $950,000; remained in custody at Alameda County Jail throughout proceedings.

Originally charged with 24 felony counts: 4 counts unlawful sexual intercourse with a minor under 16, 10 counts oral copulation of a person under 16, and 10 counts performing lewd acts on a child. Arraigned and pleaded not guilty approximately February 23, 2012. Amended complaint reduced the total to 9 counts. Pleaded no contest April 16, 2013 to the reduced counts.

Sentenced August 13, 2013, by Alameda County Superior Court Judge Joseph Hurley: 3 years state prison. Prior to sentencing, Judge Hurley sent Johnson to California Institution for Women at Chowchilla for a 90-day diagnostic study. Prosecutor Glenn Kim sought 5 years; Hurley imposed 3, citing the victim's statement at a May 15 hearing. Released same day as sentencing — had been in continuous custody since January 11, 2012 (approximately 19 months) and received extra good-time credits. Surrendered teaching credentials; lifetime sex offender registration. Defense attorney: Elizabeth Grossman. Prosecutor: Glenn Kim.
