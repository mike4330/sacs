Former math teacher at Shallotte Middle School, Brunswick County, North Carolina; previously a student-teacher intern at an elementary school in Herndon, Fairfax County, Virginia (2014-15 school year). Age 24 at arrest (April 2016).

Male victim age 13, a former student from the Herndon internship. Capitano first met the victim in 2015 while working as a student teacher. A physical relationship developed. On April 16, 2016, Capitano drove from North Carolina to Herndon, Virginia to meet the 13-year-old at a hotel for sex. Investigators had met with the boy and his parents prior to Capitano's arrival; she was arrested upon arrival. Brunswick County, NC schools placed her on paid leave.

Pleaded guilty October 14, 2016 to four felony counts in Fairfax County Circuit Court (case FE-16-878): three counts of taking indecent liberties with a child under 15 and one count of use of a communications system to propose sex to a child under 15. Sentenced February 17–22, 2017 (NC SOR records the conviction as 2017-02-17; local docket reflects 2017-02-22 sentencing).

Docket sentences: CT1 indecent liberties: 5yr imposed, 4yr suspended (1yr active) CT2 indecent liberties: 5yr imposed, 5yr suspended (0yr active) CT3 indecent liberties: 5yr imposed, 4yr suspended (1yr active) CT4 communications: 10yr imposed, 5yr suspended (5yr active) plus 17yr supervised probation

Released from prison May 19, 2022 after 6 years 1 month total confinement per the North Carolina Sex Offender Registry — this resolves the concurrent-vs-consecutive ambiguity in the original docket reading: the active sentences ran consecutively (1+0+1+5 = 7 years active, less ~11 months credit for pre-trial detention from the April 2016 arrest). Now registered with the Brunswick County Sheriff's Office as a Regular sex offender (10-year minimum registration period); registration date 2022-05-19, last address verification 2025-11-24, no violations reported. Current residence: Shallotte, NC.

NOTE: Relationship originated during the 2014-15 student-teaching placement at Herndon elementary school — distinctive pattern of student-teacher (intern) using the placement as the access vector, with the relationship continuing after she left for North Carolina employment. Prosecution venue was Virginia because the travel-to-meet offense crossed into Fairfax County; her then-current employer (Shallotte Middle School) was in NC.
