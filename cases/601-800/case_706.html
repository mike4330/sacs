Lynn Michelle Jensen #706 sentenced

StateMarylandCourtWicomico Circuit Court
JudgeLawrence R. DanielsIncident Date2017-04-21
Defendant Age36
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former special education teacher at Wicomico High School, Wicomico County Public Schools, Salisbury, Maryland (Wicomico County). DOB February 6, 1981; age 36 at offense. Had been employed by the Wicomico County Board of Education since 2012, a five-year district educator. Resides in Hebron, Maryland.

Victim was a male student at Wicomico High School. The offense occurred April 21, 2017 at a residence in Salisbury (off-campus). Victim age not stated in available sources. School administration was notified of the alleged offenses on April 27, 2017 and immediately placed Jensen on administrative leave; law enforcement and Child Protective Services were notified the same day.

Jensen was arrested May 17, 2017 and charged with sex abuse of a minor (Md. CR § 3-602) and fourth-degree sex offense — vaginal intercourse by person in position of authority (Md. CR § 3-308(c)(2)). She was initially held without bond. Initial appearance before Wicomico Circuit Court Judge Leah Seaton; Jensen posted bond the following day. On September 19, 2017, Jensen pleaded guilty to the misdemeanor 4th-degree sex offense count before Judge Lawrence R. Daniels; the sex abuse of a minor felony count was dropped. Sentence: 12 months jail, entire sentence suspended; 2 years supervised probation; Tier I sex offender registration. Case No. C-22-CR-17-000341.
