Former English teacher at Central York High School, York, Pennsylvania (Central York School District). Five years at the school before resigning in December 2005 after being charged by York City Police. Two known male victims: a 16-year-old in a sexual relationship from January through November 2005 (approximately 11 months), and a 17-year-old in October and November 2005. Age 29 at start of contact with the younger victim; 30 at plea. Victim age recorded as 16 to reflect the youngest victim per scope rules.

Sexual contact occurred in Winkis' car, in her apartment in York, and in a hotel room. She furnished alcohol to at least one of the victims. Charges originally filed by York City Police were merged in part at her preliminary hearing in January 2006.

In a plea deal with the York County DA's Office on April 5, 2006, Winkis pleaded guilty to two counts each of corruption of minors and unlawful contact with minors and one count of furnishing alcohol to a minor; she also entered a no-contest plea to two counts of indecent assault. All counts were misdemeanors. The agreement called for five years probation on the corruption counts; sentencing on the remaining counts was capped at the county-prison maximum of two years minus one day, to be determined by Judge Stephen P. Linebaugh. Sentencing was deferred to June 22, 2006 to allow the Pennsylvania Sexual Offenders Assessment Board to determine whether Winkis qualified as a sexually violent predator subject to lifetime registration and community notification.

Final disposition recorded in the DB: 2 to 23 months in jail (sentence_years reflects the Pennsylvania minimum of 2 months).

NOTE: Multi-victim case (two male students); 11-month long-term relationship with the younger victim; alcohol-to-victim grooming vector; all convictions are misdemeanors despite combined statutory maximum of 25 years. Prosecution rationale for plea explicitly cited sparing victims from testifying about intimate details.
