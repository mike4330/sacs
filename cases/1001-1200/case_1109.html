Maria Zurita #1109 appeal

StateNew YorkCourtOrange County Court
JudgeNicholas DeRosaIncident Date2007-05-01
Defendant Age28Victim Age16
Prison Sentence1.0 yr

Former teacher's aide at McQuade Children's Services, a residential private school for children with behavioral problems in New Windsor, Orange County, New York. Age 28 at time of offense.

One male student victim, age 16, a resident at McQuade. During Memorial Day weekend in May 2007, Zurita invited co-defendant Rebecca Becker to her New Windsor home; when Becker arrived, Zurita was already in the company of two 16-year-old McQuade students. Becker admitted to having sex with one of the boys; Becker testified that Zurita took the other boy into a bedroom and later bragged about having sex with him. The contact also extended to a Brooklyn hotel stay with both boys the same weekend. McQuade fired both women shortly after their arrests.

Zurita's initial plea bargain collapsed in February 2008 when she alleged Becker was lying. Tried by jury; convicted of endangering the welfare of a child (misdemeanor). Rape charge dismissed after the victim did not appear at trial. Prosecutors noted Zurita had 'repeatedly, blatantly lied to the grand jury about the events of that weekend'; the trial jury received copies of her grand jury testimony and did not credit her account. She flatly denied having sex with anyone that weekend. Sentenced May 9, 2008 by Judge Nicholas DeRosa to the maximum: one year in Orange County Jail (approximately eight months with good behavior); Zurita appealed.

NOTE: Non-teaching-staff role (teacher's aide) with access to residential behavioral-health students. She initiated the contact by inviting the students to her home. Sentence (1 year) exceeded co-defendant Becker's (3 months) despite Becker's felony conviction — Zurita's attorney called it 'basically backwards.' The offense cost both women their marriages and their McQuade positions. See also Rebecca Becker (#1108, co-defendant, same incident).
