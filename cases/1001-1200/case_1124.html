Jennifer Dawn Liskey #1124 sentenced

Former paraprofessional in the gifted program at Robinson Middle School, Topeka, Kansas. Also a close friend of the victim's mother. Age 35 at onset of offense (2005).

Victim was a 13-year-old male student enrolled in the gifted program where Liskey worked. The relationship escalated from kissing to oral sex and sexual intercourse, occurring at Liskey's home, the victim's home, in a car, and at motels and hotels over more than three years (victim ages 13 through 16). In 2007, while Liskey was serving with the Kansas Army National Guard along the New Mexico border, a separate incident occurred involving the same victim (then 15) in New Mexico.

Arrested November 10, 2008. Entered no contest pleas January 23, 2009 to two counts of aggravated indecent liberties with a child and one count of aggravated criminal sodomy. A fourth count of aggravated indecent liberties was dismissed as part of the plea agreement.

Sentenced September 9, 2009 by District Judge Jan Leuenberger to three concurrent 30-month terms (each approximately half the 59-month presumptive), both suspended, with 36 months of intensive supervised probation. An additional 60 days jail brought total incarceration to approximately one year (mostly pretrial). The judge cited 11 downward departure factors including Liskey's dependent personality disorder and chronic depression, emotional immaturity, and the victim's alleged participation. DA Chad Taylor appealed the same day.

Kansas Court of Appeals affirmed November 19, 2010, addressing only two of the 11 departure factors: (1) the boy was a willing participant, and (2) Liskey lacked substantial capacity for judgment due to diagnosed mental impairments.

Separately, Liskey was indicted October 29, 2009 by a grand jury in Doña Ana County, New Mexico on one count of fourth-degree criminal penetration of a child aged 13-16, arising from the 2007 New Mexico incident. DA Susana Martinez prosecuted. New Mexico case resolved: no contest plea entered June 29, 2011 before Judge Fernando R. Macias. Sentenced to 1 year 6 months + 1 year parole with 25 days credit; remainder suspended to supervised probation (1 year 155 days, September 2012 – February 2014). Community service 120 hours, $2,500 fine, sex offender registration required.

NOTE: The victim was enrolled in the gifted program where Liskey worked; the judge then cited the victim's intellectual giftedness as a departure factor. Victim's father: 'How does a 13-year-old boy coerce a 34-year-old woman into kissing and groping?' Victim wrote in a letter: 'To put it blunt, your honor, it was hell.' Victim wanted 3 years prison and received none. Supporters applauded in court as she was led away. Appeal affirmed with court endorsing victim-participation framing.
