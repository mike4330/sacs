Katherine Tew #1164 sentenced

Former English teacher at South Central High School, Winterville/Greenville area, Pitt County, North Carolina. Male victim, age 17, a student. Age 30 at time of offense. Married at time of offense; husband is assistant football coach.

Offense date December 11, 2004. Investigation began January 25, 2005 after school resource officer reported student disclosure of sexual contact. Victim had saved multiple handwritten letters from Tew and text messages on his cell phone. Letters referenced "hooking up" and "getting together." Last text message dated December 15, 2004: "Can you or would you want to see me tomorrow night? Home alone would love to see you. Call cell and leave a message. Y or N." Victim reported meeting Tew one night in school parking lot where she wore pajamas with cartoon moons and stars and carried bottle of tequila.

Search warrant executed February 1, 2005 at Tew's home; seized pajamas with cartoon moons/stars and handwritten letter referencing student. Arrested February 7, 2005; released on $10,000 bond. On leave with pay since investigation began January 25. Bond conditions: cannot communicate with or threaten witness, stay off school property, not alone with anyone under 18.

Charged with sex offense of student (N.C. 14-27.7(B), felony) and taking indecent liberties with a student (N.C. 14-202.4(A), felony intermediate). Arraigned February 7, 2005; transferred to Superior Court February 14, 2005. Scheduled hearing April 15, 2005 in Pitt County District Court. Jury trial January 17, 2006. Found not guilty on more serious sex offense charge; guilty on indecent liberties with a student.

Sentenced January 23, 2006: 6-8 months suspended jail time (intermediate disposition per NC structured sentencing), 24 months supervised probation, 36 hours community service (within 90 days), substance abuse assessment required. Sex offender registration. Court costs $200 (withdrawn on appeal 2-1-06). Appealed to Superior Court and Appellate Division January 23-24, 2006; appeal withdrawn.

NOTE: Detailed grooming evidence in letters and text messages. Acquittal on more serious sex offense charge reflects jury finding insufficient evidence on that count. NC intermediate disposition means probation supervision without actual confinement.
