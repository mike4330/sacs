Former special education teacher and track coach at Northeast High School, Pasadena, Anne Arundel County, Maryland. Age 28 at start of offense (29 at arrest).

Victim: 17-year-old male student, his senior year. Relationship began in spring 2009 and continued through December 2009. Defendant told police she and the student had sex at a park-and-ride lot in Severna Park, at her Severna Park home, and at Queenstown Park — up to 10 times over the course of his senior year.

Arrested January 2010; initially held on 500,000 dollar bail, released on recognizance at bail review hearing before Judge Thomas J. Pryal. Charged with three counts of fourth-degree sex offense. Superintendent Kevin M. Maxwell suspended defendant with pay at the time of arrest. Victim did not graduate; subsequently pursued a GED and enrolled in community college.

Pleaded guilty to one count of fourth-degree sex offense. Anne Arundel District Court granted probation before judgment — no criminal conviction entered. Defense: Thomas Morrow. Prosecutor: Michelle Smith.

NOTE: sentence_years = 0. Probation before judgment (PBJ) in Maryland = no final conviction if probation is completed. Defendant was both special education teacher and track coach at the school; article does not specify whether victim was her student, her athlete, or both. Prosecutor cited desire to avoid retraumatizing the victim as reason for not contesting the no-conviction outcome.
