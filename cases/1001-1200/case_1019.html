Former paraprofessional at Labay Middle School, Cypress Fairbanks ISD, Harris County, Texas. Age 23 at time of offense.

The victim was a 15-year-old male student (and former student) at Labay Middle School. Multiple witnesses described the relationship as boyfriend-girlfriend in nature; the victim's sister confirmed the victim had told her about the sexual relationship and that she had seen them together on multiple occasions.

On April 5, 2018, an anonymous student tip to AP Kathryn Robinson reported that the student had been at the victim's home, observed the defendant and victim lying in bed together, heard noises from the room, and saw them posting photos together on Snapchat.

The defendant was interviewed at school (audio and video recorded, non-custodial) and gave a full confession. She stated she and the victim kissed and held hands, that he had seen her naked and she him (from the back), and that he fondled her breast and touched her vaginal area. She admitted she "lost her virginity" to the victim in the middle of summer 2017, described the relationship as "one mistake after another," and stated it was her fault because she was the grownup and older. She said her period was late and she was scared, admitted she loved him "with all of her heart," and disclosed sexual intercourse occurred at the victim's house, at a park, and at the Palace Inn.

Hotel records from the Palace Inn on West Road, Houston show the defendant rented a room July 17, 2017 and July 20, 2017, two hours each date. The affiant confirmed the victim was age 15 on both dates. A consent search of her phone using Oxygen Forensics produced several explicit images of the defendant and the complainant together and a video the affiant believes depicts the two having sexual intercourse.

Complaint filed September 4, 2018 (Cypress Fairbanks ISD PD, Affiant D. Keele). Arrested September 7, 2018. No-contact order September 10. Bond $10,000. Two bond condition violations on November 28 and 30, 2018 prompted a protective order with limited access on December 17, 2018. Indicted March 8, 2019. Harris County Cause Nos. 160406301010 (improper relationship) and 160406401010 (sexual assault, child 14 to 17). Defense: Robert O. Pelton (hired). ADA Williams.

Deferred adjudication granted June 20, 2019 (Judge Herb H. Ritchie): 10 years and a $950 fine. The sexual assault charge was dismissed simultaneously. Early termination was granted June 10, 2024, roughly five years into the ten-year deferred adjudication.
