Former teacher at Plainfield High School, Plainfield, New Jersey (Union County). Age 28 at time of offense and arrest.

Victim was a 16-year-old male student. Sexual relationship began December 2012. Authorities were notified after the victim told a family member.

Upon learning she was under investigation, Garcia-Calle fled to Florida. Charged January 22, 2013; arrested January 24, 2013 in Davie, Florida by the US Marshals Service, held in Broward County Jail on $200,000 bail. Extradited to New Jersey February 7, 2013 by Union County Sheriff's officers Kenneth Randazza and Joann Huss. Plainfield Board of Education placed her on administrative suspension pending the investigation.

Originally charged with second-degree sexual assault (NJ 2C:14-2) and fourth-degree child abuse. Filed by Union County Prosecutor Theodore J. Romankow. Pleaded guilty to fourth-degree criminal sexual contact (NJ 2C:14-3B). Sentenced November 22, 2013: 3 years probation, 23 days jail (non-custodial; jail days reflect time served). Accusation no. 13-09-01115-A.

NOTE: Significant charge reduction — 2nd degree sexual assault to 4th degree criminal sexual contact. Flight to Florida on learning of investigation required US Marshals extradition. Victim disclosure to family member triggered the investigation.
