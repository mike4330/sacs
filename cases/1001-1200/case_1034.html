Former English teacher at Londonderry High School, Londonderry, Rockingham County, New Hampshire. Age 41 at time of offense. Taught sophomore English at the school for three years.

Male victim, 15 years old, a student at Londonderry High School. Approximately two months before her March 2010 arrest, defendant emailed four nude images of herself — genitals exposed — to the victim's email account and sent text messages offering oral sex. She also kissed him twice in a classroom. The victim forwarded the images to two friends; co-workers identified Dennehy as the woman in the photographs. School administrators and police were notified on March 3, 2010, when the images were circulating at the school; Dennehy was placed on paid administrative leave that day and turned herself in on March 5.

Originally charged with a felony; faced up to seven years in prison if convicted at trial. Pleaded guilty July 26, 2010 to a misdemeanor count of indecent exposure before Derry District Court Judge John Coughlin (Prosecutor: Kevin Coyle; Defense: Cathy Green). One-year jail sentence suspended for two years, conditioned on good behavior and no contact with the victim, his family, or Londonderry High School. Not required to register as a sex offender — misdemeanor indecent exposure not a qualifying offense under NH law. Surrendered teaching certificate as part of the plea. Resigned three weeks after the March charge.
