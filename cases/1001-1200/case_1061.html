Mary Schnell #1061 sentenced

Former substitute teacher at Aurora High School, Aurora City School District, Aurora, Portage County, Ohio. Age 25 at time of offense. Male victim, 16 years old, a student at Aurora High School.

Schnell engaged in sexual activity with the victim on at least two occasions between May 1 and June 9, 2011. Encounters took place in a hotel parking lot near the intersection of State Routes 14 and 303 in Streetsboro, and in the parking lot of a bar in Mantua Township. The relationship came to light when Streetsboro Police, conducting surveillance of the hotel parking lot, observed Schnell and the student in a car together. Further investigation by Portage County Sheriff's Office, Aurora Police, and Streetsboro Police confirmed the sexual activity. Schnell immediately confessed when confronted by investigators.

Arrested July 18, 2011. Indicted July 2011 on two felony counts of sexual battery. Pleaded guilty January 18, 2012 to a single count of sexual battery (third-degree felony); second count dismissed. Sentenced May 11, 2012 by Judge Laurie Pittman: 2 years in state prison; 5 years post-release supervision; Tier III sex offender registration (every 90 days for life); no contact with victim; permanent surrender of teaching license. Judicial release denied March 5, 2013 after approximately 10 months served — Judge Pittman stated the court must send a clear message that such conduct will not be tolerated from either male or female teachers.
