Charlene Schmitz #1110 sentenced

Former teacher at Leroy High School, Leroy, Washington County, Alabama. Age 54 at time of first arrest.

One male student victim, age 14. Schmitz used a cell phone and computer to entice the student and engaged in sexual intercourse with him on at least 10 occasions, per the victim's grandmother's trial testimony. The relationship was discovered when the victim's parent found sexually explicit emails allegedly sent by Schmitz on the student's computer. Schmitz was arrested June 22, 2007 at her residence in Leroy by Washington County deputies.

During bond, Schmitz made illegal contact with the victim at a Friday night football game in Leroy (November 23, 2007), resulting in a second arrest and an additional charge of tampering with a witness. Following a bond revocation hearing November 27, 2007, she was transferred to federal custody after being indicted by a federal grand jury on enticement charges. Convicted in U.S. District Court February 14, 2008. Sentenced June 2, 2008 by Judge Kristi DuBose: 10 years 1 month federal prison; lifetime sex offender registration on release; $2,240 restitution to victim's mother; $200 Specific Assessment fines. Prosecutors had sought 13 years.

Schmitz also faces state charges of second-degree rape and sodomy in Washington County; Judge DuBose declined to address those, leaving them to state proceedings.

NOTE: Federal prosecution under enticement statute; contact by cell phone and email. The football game bond violation and witness tampering charge are unusual — Schmitz reestablished contact during pretrial release rather than severing it.
