Former computer teacher at Union Middle School in Union, Missouri (Franklin County). Age 36 or 37 at the time of the documented offense conduct in February 2012; 37 at the July 2012 indictment. The known victim was a 13-year-old male student at the school. The victim was not enrolled in any of Storie's classes but attended the same school where she worked.

Sexual contact occurred off school property in February 2012. Storie was placed on administrative leave by Union School District in February 2012 and resigned the following month.

A Franklin County grand jury indicted Storie in July 2012 on two counts of first-degree statutory sodomy and one count of witness tampering. The case took nearly six years to resolve, suggesting extensive motion practice and probable continuances over that span.

A pretrial bond condition entered on June 26, 2012 originally prohibited Storie from contacting a separate minor, possibly her own child with Brian Storie. On August 15, 2012, over the State's objection, the court amended that condition to allow Storie to cohabitate with the minor only if Brian Storie also resided in the same home, and prohibited her from communicating with the minor about the case.

On January 30, 2018, Storie pleaded guilty to two non-sex Class C felony counts: endangering the welfare of a child in the first degree and tampering with a victim or witness in a felony prosecution. The two original first-degree statutory sodomy counts were dismissed pursuant to the plea. The court imposed seven years state prison on the endangering count and three years on the tampering count, with execution of both sentences suspended and five years of supervised probation imposed concurrently. A no-contact order with the victim was entered, and Storie was ordered to surrender her teaching certificate. No active prison time was served.

NOTE: Both conviction counts are non-sex statutes. The original first-degree statutory sodomy counts under Missouri law would have carried 10 years to life on conviction; both were resolved away in the plea, leaving only the endangering and tampering convictions, both contact_type 888.
