Computer teacher at Union Middle School, Union, Franklin County, Missouri. Age 37 at time of indictment (July 2012); age at offense (February 2012) was 36 or 37.

One male victim (T.R.), age 13. The victim was not enrolled in any of Storie's classes but was a student at the school where she worked. Sexual contact occurred off school property in February 2012. Storie was placed on administrative leave in February 2012 and resigned the following month.

Originally indicted by Franklin County grand jury on two counts of first-degree statutory sodomy (RSMo §566.062) and one count of witness tampering. Case took nearly six years to resolve.

Bond condition (June 26, 2012) originally prohibited contact with K.S. (apparent minor, possibly Storie's child with Brian Storie). On August 15, 2012, over the state's objection, the court amended the condition to allow Storie and K.S. to cohabitate only if Brian Storie also resided in the same home, and prohibited Storie from communicating with K.S. about the case.

Pleaded guilty January 30, 2018 to endangering welfare of a child, first degree (RSMo §568.045, Class C felony) and tampering with a victim/witness in a felony prosecution (RSMo §575.270, Class C felony). Original statutory sodomy charges dismissed. Sentenced same day to 7 years DOC suspended execution of sentence, 5 years supervised probation (Count I); 3 years DOC SES, 5 years probation (Count: tampering), concurrent. No contact with T.R. Ordered to surrender teaching certificate. No active prison time served.

NOTE: Both conviction charges are non-sex statutes — endangering welfare and witness tampering. The statutory sodomy charges (Class A felony, carrying 10 years to life in Missouri) were completely resolved away. Contact_type 888 on both conviction charges.
