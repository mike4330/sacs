Former contracted athletic trainer at Tustin High School, Tustin, Orange County, California. Jacoby was employed by a private company contracted by the school to train athletes; she was not a direct school employee. Age 22 at time of first offense.

One male student victim, age 16–17 during the relationship. Jacoby had a sexual relationship with the victim from January 1 through December 19, 2008 — nearly one year. Sexual encounters occurred in her car and at her home in San Juan Capistrano. Case came to light in December 2008 when another student saw text message images on the victim's phone believed to have been sent by Jacoby. Arrested December 19, 2008; released on $100,000 bail.

Arraigned on 8 felony counts: 4 counts unlawful sexual intercourse, 3 counts oral copulation of a minor, and 1 count distributing pornography to a minor (facing up to 7 years 8 months if convicted). Pleaded guilty July 1, 2009 to 7 counts (intercourse and oral copulation; pornography count not included in plea). Over the prosecution's objection, Judge Gregory Jones reduced all felonies to misdemeanors at sentencing on July 29, 2009, and sentenced Jacoby to 240 hours of community service only. Prosecuted by Deputy DA Nikki Buracchio, Orange County DA Sexual Assault Unit.

NOTE: Contractor-not-employee status is an access-pattern distinction — Jacoby was placed at the school by an outside training company, creating a supervision gap. The felony-to-misdemeanor reduction over the DA's objection is among the more dramatic no-prison outcomes in the dataset.
