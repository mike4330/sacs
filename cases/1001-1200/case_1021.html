Former teacher at Aldine Ninth Grade School (Aldine ISD), Houston, Harris County, Texas. Victim J.R. had been in defendant’s business class the prior school year. Age 49 at time of offense. Resided in Humble, Texas. Also known as Helen Roberta Page; prior surname Thompson. Non-US citizen.

Male victim J.R., 15 years old. Relationship began approximately mid-August 2020 as friends; defendant and victim began following each other on Instagram, then transitioned to Snapchat, then calls and text messages. During September 2020, defendant began sending nude photos, predominantly of her breasts and tattoos (including a tattoo on her lower center back). Defendant told victim she would perform oral sex on him and they exchanged messages describing sexual acts they desired to have with each other. No physical meeting is established in the complaint. Defendant denied ever having sexual intercourse with the victim or being alone with him, and denied sending breast photos. Forensic downloads of both phones (January 8, 2021) revealed images of nudity of both the victim and defendant on each phone; screenshots from the victim’s phone showed defendant in black lace lingerie. Snapchat and Instagram messages were irretrievable from the phones but screenshots were preserved.

Incident reported January 2, 2021 by Aldine ISD educator Maria Regas, who had seen text messages between defendant and victim. Affiant: Det Renee Hall, Aldine ISD PD Investigation Division (Case #250001). Forensic interview of complainant conducted January 20, 2021 at the Children’s Assessment Center. Complaint filed April 15, 2021 (two counts: July 1–December 15, 2020 and January 5, 2021). Arrested April 19, 2021; bond $30,000 surety. Indicted June 29, 2021, 351st Criminal District Court. TDFPS records subject to in camera inspection during pretrial proceedings, suggesting child protective services involvement.

Pleaded guilty to one count; second count dismissed at plea. 5-year deferred adjudication granted May 31, 2022 (Judge Natalia Cornelio, 351st Criminal District Court). DADJ projected through approximately May 31, 2027. Defense: Dustan Orlando Neyland (hired). Motion for early termination filed June 4, 2024; second motion filed July 16, 2025; pending as of April 2026.

NOTE: Deferred adjudication in Texas = no final conviction unless probation is violated; sentence_years = 0. Victim attended the school’s virtual program at the time of the offense (2020–2021 school year), with defendant teaching in the same district. ADA Oswald accepted the charge.
