Former teacher at Riverside Elementary School, Cleveland, Cuyahoga County, Ohio. Age 40 at time of offense. Male victim, 14 years old (8th grade), a student at the school.

The victim reported that he had sexual intercourse with Ross in her classroom after school on January 15 and January 31, 2010. During this period Ross texted and phoned the victim nearly 4,000 times at all hours of the day and night. After the victim boasted about the relationship to friends who did not believe him, he asked Ross to send photographs of her naked breasts to his cellphone as proof; she complied. The relationship came to light through a blackmail attempt: several students sent Ross a letter threatening to expose the relationship unless she paid them 40 dollars. Ross confronted the students, who backed down. However, a copy of the letter was found by one student's mother, who brought it to school administrators. Ross was removed from teaching duties April 13, 2010 and arrested June 2010.

Pleaded guilty to one count of felonious assault arising from psychological harm to the victim; multiple sex-related charges were dismissed as part of the plea deal. The prosecutor stated in court that the victim had sex with Ross and would have testified to that at trial; Ross denied sexual relations at sentencing. Sentenced February 4, 2011 by Judge John Sutula: 2 years in prison (maximum was 8 years; request for probation denied without discussion). The victim did not attend his 8th-grade graduation due to the impact of the relationship.
