Former fine arts (visual arts) teacher at Zionsville Community High School, Zionsville, Boone County, Indiana. Age 33 at time of offense. Female victim, age not documented, a student at the school.

Incidents occurred on school property and were initiated by Jared. The female student confided in another teacher, who reported to school administrators; administrators confronted Jared and she resigned December 9, 2010 — the same day the school was made aware of the allegations. The district stated officials had no prior knowledge of inappropriate conduct and that investigation revealed no other victims.

Case investigated by the Zionsville Police Department; charging information filed February 10, 2011 on two counts of child seduction (Class D felony). Arrested on bench warrant February 15, 2011; bonded out. Initial hearing February 22, 2011 with no-contact order issued. Jury trial originally set June 15, 2011; continued to October 2011. Pleaded guilty September 30, 2011 to one count of child seduction (Class D felony) before Judge Matthew C. Kincaid; second count dismissed. Sentenced the same day to probation with Indiana Recommended Special Probation Conditions for Adult Sex Offenders; barred from teaching. Released from probation March 26, 2013. Conviction reduced to Class A misdemeanor June 7, 2013 under Indiana alternative misdemeanor sentencing. No-contact order dismissed February 8, 2021. Boone County Cause No. 06D01-1102-FD-000037.
