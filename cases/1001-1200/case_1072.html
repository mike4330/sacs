Former band director at El Modena High School, Orange Unified School District, Orange, Orange County, California. Age 28 at time of offense. Male victim, 15 years old, a student in her band program.

Between November 2007 and September 2008, Attebury engaged in sexual intercourse with the victim at her home in Orange on multiple occasions, and exchanged hundreds of sexually explicit text messages with him. She groomed the victim and his family by befriending them, including having dinner at their home. The relationship came to light in October 2008 when the El Modena principal contacted police after witnesses observed Attebury sitting behind the student in school bleachers with her legs straddled around him, caressing his hair, in view of parents and other students.

Separately, a former student (not the victim in this case) attempted to extort Attebury, threatening to report both a prior relationship with an 18-year-old former student and the relationship with the 15-year-old victim unless she paid him 3,500 dollars and sent him explicit photos. That individual was separately charged, pleaded guilty, and received three years of probation.

Charged November 2008 with eight felony counts of unlawful sexual intercourse and dissuading a witness from reporting a crime; placed on unpaid leave by Orange Unified School District. Sentenced March 4, 2011 in Orange County Superior Court: 16 months in state prison; sex offender registration; stay-away order from victim.
