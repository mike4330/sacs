Former substitute science teacher ("permanent substitute") at San Pedro High School, Los Angeles Unified School District, San Pedro, California (Los Angeles County). Age 28 at time of offense. Also conducted online tutoring providing additional access to minors.

Three male student victims (ages 15, 15, and 16). Yeh began a temporary assignment at San Pedro High in February 2015. At the end of the 2015 school year, she reached out to students and arranged private meetings. With one 15-year-old victim: Yeh first gained trust by buying him lunch, giving him cash, and ultimately an Xbox 360 and games (approximately $400). After returning to the victim's home to play the Xbox, she initiated physical contact, kissing him and initiating sex. The relationship continued for one to two months in a pattern the victim described as "eat, go somewhere, come back, have sex." She treated him as a boyfriend; he described feeling obligated and manipulated. His mother discovered the relationship and called police. Yeh also took one victim to Disneyland and met another at a hotel. Yeh had held multiple temporary teaching assignments across the district.

Arrested July 17, 2015; released July 18 on $100,000 bail. DA filed charges July 2015. Pleaded no contest January 15, 2016 to: unlawful sexual intercourse with a minor under 16 (felony); oral copulation of a minor under 16 (felony); lewd act on a child (felony); child molesting involving 16-year-old victim (misdemeanor); and violating a court order by texting the 16-year-old victim (misdemeanor). Sentenced to 3 years 8 months state prison by Judge Richard Romero, Los Angeles Superior Court. Lifetime sex offender registration ordered. Civil lawsuit filed against Yeh and LAUSD by one victim's attorney, David Ring.

NOTE: Three confirmed victims; attorney and LAPD both stated belief in additional unreported victims. Victim age set to 15 (two of three confirmed victims were 15). Intercourse and oral copulation both confirmed; contact_type=3.
