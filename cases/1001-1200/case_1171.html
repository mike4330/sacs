Former social studies teacher and cheerleading coach at Ponderosa High School, Highlands Ranch (Parker), Colorado. With Douglas County School District since 1995; moved to Ponderosa in August 2002 after previously teaching eighth-grade social studies at Ranchview Middle School. Married, mother of two children. Male victim, age 16, a 10th-grade student in her class. Age 35 at time of offense.

Barnhart was counseling the victim, who was abusing drugs and alcohol and in conflict with his mother. She told police she kissed him and ultimately submitted to sex after "he begged me." Sexual contact occurred on March 10 and March 15, 2005 in the back seat of her van. Per her statement to police, the victim told her he loved her.

Students reported a reputation for wearing revealing clothing, public affection toward the victim, and frequent visits to her home — where she cooked him lobster dinners while her husband and children were away. The victim's 13-year-old brother and the victim's friends told investigators he had bragged about having sex numerous times with a teacher.

The relationship was discovered on March 18, 2005 when a Douglas County sheriff's deputy found Barnhart and the victim together in her van at the end of a dirt road in Castlewood Canyon. Defense attorney Iris Eytan attempted in late November 2005 to forestall arraignment by offering documentation to prosecutors; Judge Paul King refused the postponement and ordered the parties to either announce a plea or set a trial date at the December 20 hearing. Pleaded guilty December 20, 2005 to one count of sexual assault on a child and one count of criminal attempt to commit sexual assault on a child. Sentenced January 3, 2006 to two years in prison plus a minimum of 10 years in a sex-offender probation program; sex offender registration required. The victim's parents notified the school district of intent to sue for $2 million.

NOTE: Pre-existing reprimand history at Ponderosa — removed as cheerleading coach over parent complaints; previously reprimanded for hosting cheerleaders at her house playing "Truth or Dare." Husband absent from her court appearances suggests marital strain prior to the case.
