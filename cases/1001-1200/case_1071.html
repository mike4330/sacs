Former school secretary and attendance clerk at Madison Central High School, Richmond, Madison County, Kentucky, employed at the school for at least 10 years. Also served as a volunteer statistician for the boys' basketball teams. Age 37 at time of offense. Male victim, 15 years old, a student at the school. Chase uses a wheelchair due to paralysis from a car crash at age 5.

Chase engaged in sexual intercourse and oral sex with the victim, including at her home. The prosecution described Chase as having come to view the boy as her soulmate. Following her February 2011 arrest, Chase sent a Facebook message to the victim in violation of a no-contact court order.

Arrested February 2011. Pleaded guilty May 2011 to one count of third-degree rape and four counts of third-degree sodomy. Sentenced August 25, 2011 by Madison Circuit Judge William G. Clouse: 3 years in state prison; sex offender registration; sex offender treatment. Defense argued for probation or continued home incarceration on the basis of Chase's disability; prosecution and court rejected this. Judge Clouse stated: 'There is a victim in this case, and the victim is a child. The only adult in this relationship was you.' Held at the Kentucky Correctional Institution for Women in Pewee Valley. Shock probation denied November 8, 2011.
