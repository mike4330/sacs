Former nurse's aide (Educational Aide 1) at Taylor High School, Houston, Texas (Alief ISD). Certified by the Texas Education Agency under the name Calvillo, Amalia. Age 37 at time of offense. Also known as Maria Calvillo, Amalia Calvillo, Mo Ortega, and Molly Ortega.

The victim was a 17-year-old male twelfth-grade student at Taylor High School who knew the defendant as the school's nurse's aide. Conduct occurred in the Taylor High School clinic storage room on two occasions before the school day. On September 24, 2012, around 6:45 AM, the defendant and victim entered the clinic storage room and engaged in vaginal intercourse on the floor with clothing removed. A second incident in the same clinic storage room on September 26, 2012 also involved vaginal intercourse.

The defendant communicated with the victim on Facebook under the alias "Mo Ortega," conducting sexually explicit conversations. An October 4, 2012 Facebook message from the defendant described an explicit sexual demand for the next day, and the victim replied affirmatively.

On October 31, 2012, the victim's mother found the sexually explicit Facebook conversations between her son and "Mo Ortega" on his account. The victim identified Mo Ortega as the school nurse's aide, Maria Calvillo. The mother preserved the dialog to a thumb drive and provided it to Alief ISD PD (Affiant Officer M. Wilson). The victim was interviewed November 1, 2012, with a forensic interview at the Harris County Children's Assessment Center on November 6, 2012.

In statements on October 31, 2012 and February 14, 2013, the defendant confirmed Facebook communications had turned "somewhat sexual" under the acknowledged name Mo Ortega but denied sexual intercourse.

A no-contact order was issued March 2, 2013, nine days before the criminal complaint. The complaint was filed March 11, 2013, with bond at $10,000. Indicted June 7, 2013. ADA A. Mecca (child abuse specialist) prosecuted. Defense was initially appointed (Alexis Bruegger), then Samuel Raymond Cammack III was hired from April 5, 2013. Judge Vanessa Velasquez presided.

Two improper relationship counts; no companion sexual assault charge. Deferred adjudication was granted October 18, 2013: 5 years and a $500 fine (Harris County Cause No. 138031501010). The second count was dismissed simultaneously. Early termination was granted November 15, 2017, roughly four years into the five-year probation.
