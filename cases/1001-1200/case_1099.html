Former instructional assistant in the Alternative School Program, Maconaquah School Corporation, Bunker Hill, Indiana (Miami County). Worked in the district for approximately six years — initially at the high school, most recently at the middle school. Age 36 at time of first offense (inferred: described as "41" at February 2015 arrest; first offense December 2010).

Two male student victims, both age 17. The first relationship began in December 2010. Ladd allowed the first student — who was on in-home detention — to stay at her farm for two weeks, and permitted him to work there to earn money for license reinstatement. Their relationship began during that period. The second relationship began in April 2013; Ladd had initiated contact with the second student while he was held at the Kinsey Youth Center on a residential entry conviction, and the relationship began after his release. Both relationships continued after each victim turned 18 — the first until summer 2013, the second until November 2014. Ladd provided both students with thousands of dollars in gifts, alcohol, vehicles, and firearms.

In January 2015, deputies interviewed Ladd, who admitted meeting both juveniles through Maconaquah's alternative school and engaging in sexual relationships with them. Arrested February 2, 2015; bond $56,000. First court appearance February 19, 2015. Placed on paid administrative leave pending termination proceedings.

Originally charged with two counts of felony child seduction and three counts of illegal transfer of firearms (six counts total). Pleaded guilty; sentenced March 2, 2017 by order signed February 28, 2017. Found guilty of Count II (child seduction) and Amended Count III (firearms-related); sentenced to 6 years IDOC on each count, concurrent, with 4 years suspended and 2 years to serve. Credit for 1 day served (2/2/2015). Four years probation to follow. Counts I, IV, V, and VI dismissed on state motion. No contact order as to victim A.P. Defense: Mark Dabrowski. Prosecution: Christian Sands.
