Former theater teacher at Dekaney High School, Spring ISD, Houston, Harris County, Texas. Age 26 at time of offense. Male victim, age 18, a student enrolled in her theater class.

Grooming began during the victim's junior year when Mata invited him to join the theater class and obtained his phone number through the class. She initiated private contact via text, sending explicit messages, nude photographs, and a video of herself masturbating with a sex toy. She instructed him to delete their conversations and save her phone number under the alias "Perry." On February 29, 2024, returning from a UIL competition, she pressed her chest against his hand on the bus. On March 8, 2024 — Mata's birthday — she summoned the victim to the rehearsal theater after school hours, where she performed oral sex on him near the stage curtains. Security footage confirmed both entered the rehearsal theater that evening. Mata later admitted to police she performed oral sex on the victim, characterizing the act as "mutual and consensual." Forensic download of her phone confirmed the text history with the victim, whose contact was saved under the name "Mason Little."

Felony complaint filed May 1, 2024; emergency protection order granted May 4, 2024; bond $20,000. Grand jury indictment September 25, 2024 (Harris County GJ Court 262). Harris County Cause No. 186637401010-3. Defense: Luis Stephen Baez. Multiple continuances through mid-2025.

Pleaded guilty July 14, 2025 before Judge Caroline Dozier; no agreed sentencing recommendation — PSI ordered. ADA Shalynn Arbour at plea. PSI hearing October 21-22, 2025; both sides presented testimony and argument. ADA Tieranee Jones at sentencing. Court found defendant guilty and sentenced her to 5 years TDCJ-ID (Texas Department of Criminal Justice — Institutional Division); remanded to custody October 22, 2025. Nunc pro tunc order November 25, 2025 (clerical correction to judgment).

Notice of appeal filed November 11, 2025. Trial counsel withdrew; public defender Fernanda Benavides appointed on appeal. Appeal bond denied — defendant serving sentence in TDCJ awaiting mandate. Assigned to 1st Court of Appeals (case no. 01-25-01002-CR). 1st COA opinion filed January 29, 2026. Case now on petition to Texas Court of Criminal Appeals (CCA).

NOTE: No agreed sentencing recommendation; contested PSI hearing with testimony suggests disputed facts at sentencing. sentence_years = 5 (TDCJ-ID, actual prison). Appeal moving unusually quickly — 1st COA opinion less than 2 months after assignment.
