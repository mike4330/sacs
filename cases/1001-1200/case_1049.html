Lianne Trout #1049 sentenced

StateCaliforniaCourtTulare County Superior Court

Prison Sentence0.5 yrs

Former special education teacher at Mount Whitney High School, Visalia Unified School District, Visalia, Tulare County, California. Newly hired at the time; began teaching at Mount Whitney in August 2011. Age 27 at time of offense.

Victim was a 16-year-old student. The relationship began shortly after Trout started at the school in August 2011 and continued through December 2011. Sexual intercourse occurred on multiple occasions: near the Casa Grande Restaurant on Mooney Boulevard, at the Fairfield Inn, and after a high school football game. The relationship came to the attention of police and the school in December 2011; Trout resigned shortly after and was arrested. Prosecutors alleged she continued attempting to contact the victim through third parties after her arrest.

Charged with five felony counts: four counts of unlawful sexual intercourse with a minor and one sexual assault count. Pleaded not guilty March 2012; later pleaded no contest to five felony counts in July 2012. Sentenced February 23, 2013 by Judge Gary Paden to 185 days in county jail and five years of probation; five-year sex offender registration (not lifetime, over prosecution's objection). Ordered to report to jail April 29, 2013. Prosecutors requested prison and lifetime registration; both requests were denied. Defense: Jeff Hammerschmidt; Prosecutors: ADA Anthony Fultz and DA Shani Jenkins.
