Christina Butler #1120 sentenced

StateFloridaCourtHillsborough County Circuit Court
JudgeJ. Rogers PadgettIncident Date2007-08-01
Defendant Age33Victim Age16
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former 9th-grade special education teacher (temporary certification) at Middleton High School, Tampa, Florida. Hired August 2007, her first year teaching, at a salary of $35,000/year. Age 33 at time of offense.

Victim was a 16-year-old male student in her special education class. Prosecutors described him as borderline intellectually disabled. Butler invited the victim to her apartment on several occasions; up to 12 sexual encounters occurred over a three-week period in August–October 2007.

Discovery occurred October 23, 2007, when police conducting a stakeout at an apartment complex followed a group of students on bicycles and stopped a car being driven without a license. The driver told police his 'friend' Butler had let him use her Jeep. Another student disclosed the sexual relationship. Butler was called to the scene and admitted to sexual activity with the victim.

Charged at arrest with lewd or lascivious battery. Final charges: contributing to the delinquency of a minor and two counts of unlawful sexual activity with a minor.

Entered a guilty plea October 23, 2008 with no plea deal, throwing herself on the mercy of the court. Sentenced December 10, 2008 by Judge J. Rogers Padgett to 5 years sex offender probation with no prison time. She faced up to 30+ years. The judge reasoned the victim was more mature and less vulnerable than typical, stated 'he exercised authority over her,' and noted a sex offender evaluation found recidivism unlikely. Neither the victim nor his family appeared at sentencing.

The Hillsborough County State Attorney's Office filed an appeal of the sentence. Attorney Darryl Rouson threatened a civil suit against Hillsborough County School District. DNA evidence was recovered from Butler's home.

NOTE: No-prison outcome despite special-needs victim and DNA evidence; prosecution appeal filed. Judge Padgett also sentenced Jaymee Wallace (#1121, Wharton High School teacher/coach) to 3 years prison + 3 years SOPR for a comparable offense — Butler's sentence was substantially lighter.
