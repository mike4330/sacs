Former substitute teacher at Cosby High School, Chesterfield County, Virginia. Age 24 at time of offense.

Victim was an underage male student still attending Cosby High School at time of investigation; age not established from available sources. A single incident of sexual contact occurred in a classroom after school hours in spring 2012 (March–April). Text messages had been exchanged between Walters and the student prior to the physical encounter.

Discovery: In October 2012, a former student — by then 18 and in college — posted graphic text messages and nude photos he claimed Walters had sent him on Twitter, prompting an investigation. Because that student was 18 at the time of his interactions with Walters, no charges were pursued for that relationship. However, interviews at the school surfaced the underage student, who was then interviewed and confirmed the classroom encounter.

Walters turned herself in October 25, 2012. Originally charged with two felonies: taking indecent liberties with an underage student while in a supervisory role and carnal knowledge of a minor. Pleaded guilty January 3, 2013 to two misdemeanors: sexual battery and contributing to the delinquency of a minor. Sentenced to 1 year jail with possible release after 6 months for good behavior; required to continue counseling and register as a sex offender. Prosecution: Duncan Minton. Defense: Craig Cooley.

NOTE: The adult former student who initially posted the photos commented on social media after the plea: 'Only reason I'm angry is because if this was a guy teacher and a girl at a high school, the guy would never see daylight again...Predator.' Felony charges reduced to misdemeanors at plea. Second Chesterfield school employee in about a year charged with student sex offense — see Tracy Allanson (#1128, school bus driver).
