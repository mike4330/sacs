Former substitute teacher at Sam Rayburn High School in Pasadena Independent School District, Harris County, Texas. Resided in Pasadena. Age 21 at the time of the documented offense conduct in March 2018; 22 at her November 2019 arrest. The known victim was a 16-year-old male student. Huerta had substituted for the victim's social studies class and had also been a substitute at the school the prior year.

Huerta and the victim exchanged phone numbers and communicated through Snapchat in advance of the offense. On or about March 1, 2018, Huerta walked the victim from the school to her parents' home in Pasadena and the two had vaginal intercourse there. Huerta later confessed the encounter to the affiant detective and acknowledged that she had previously had the victim in one of her substitute classes. iCloud messages recovered from her phone confirmed the sexual relationship; the victim later sent her a message stating 'I remember the day you raped me' and continuing to solicit further contact, a striking record-line preserved in the complaint.

The investigation opened in November 2019 after a student reported the relationship to a teacher, who relayed it to Sam Rayburn assistant principal Chad Cox. Cox confirmed Huerta was on campus as a substitute that day and had her removed from the classroom for interview. Pasadena ISD Police Department Detective A. Murphy led the investigation and identified the parents' home through corroborating physical detail (a Green Bay Packers sign on the door matched the victim's description) before searching Huerta's phone with written consent.

The complaint was filed November 5, 2019 and Huerta was arrested the following day. She was initially represented by appointed defense counsel Luis Alberto Garcia Cruz before retaining Dorian Claude Cotlar. Bond was set at $20,000 with later amendment. The Harris County 232nd Criminal District Court returned a felony indictment on June 5, 2020 charging both improper relationship between an educator and a student and sexual assault of a child; the case was reset three times during spring and summer 2020 for COVID-related docket disruption.

On November 5, 2020, the court granted deferred adjudication on the educator-relationship count: eight years of community supervision running through approximately November 2028. The sexual-assault count was dismissed pursuant to the plea.

NOTE: Deferred adjudication in Texas produces no formal conviction unless probation is later violated, and sentence_years is recorded at zero accordingly. The victim's own subsequent characterization of the March 2018 encounter as rape — preserved in iCloud messages recovered from Huerta's phone — is documented in the complaint record alongside the same messages' continuing solicitation for further contact, an unusual evidentiary combination worth noting. The defendant's pre-counsel acknowledgment to Detective Murphy, in which she conceded the encounter, places this case in the corpus pattern of pre-counsel admissions during investigative interview.
