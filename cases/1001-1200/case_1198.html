Teacher at Garden City High School, Garden City, Kansas. Age 40 at time of offense (born 1965). Male victim, age 16, was a student at the school.\n\nCharged with five counts of unlawful sexual relations between teacher/student (K.S.A. 21-3520). Allegations involved five sexual encounters at Nunnelee's home in December 2005. Arrested at school on January 5, 2006. Suspended with pay pending investigation. Garden City Police Department investigated; Officer Brian Ruder filed the case.\n\nPublic defender appointed (Western Regional). Preliminary hearing waived March 14, 2006. Arraigned April 10, 2006, pled not guilty to all five counts. Changed plea at pretrial conference on July 14, 2006: nolo contendere on counts 1 and 2, counts 3-5 dismissed.\n\nSentenced August 18, 2006 to 6 months jail on each conviction count, concurrent. Credited 10 days time served. Placed on intensive supervision; probation initially set to expire August 2007, later extended to August 18, 2008. Supervision terminated early on December 11, 2007.\n\nNOTE: Charge-reduction case. Five felony counts reduced to two via nolo contendere plea with three dismissals. Six months concurrent jail on two Level 10 felonies is a light sentence. Kansas statute 21-3520 specifically criminalizes teacher-student sexual relations where the student is 16-17 (otherwise age of consent in Kansas is 16).
