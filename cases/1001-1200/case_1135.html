Former biology/science teacher at Messmer High School, Milwaukee, Wisconsin (Milwaukee County), in her first year of teaching (started fall 2012). Age 28 at arrest; 29 at sentencing. When the boys resisted her advances, she lied to a basketball coach about their behavior to have them suspended from the team.

Caught by a witness on May 23, 2013, performing oral sex on the 14-year-old in the parking lot of Destiny Church and School in Milwaukee. Separately charged with groping the 15-year-old while giving him a ride in her car earlier that month and having him touch her sexually. Arrested May 2013; Messmer suspended her.

ADA Karshen stated Garland 'meticulously planned' the acts and was jealous and vindictive throughout. Victims' mothers addressed the court; one noted her son's B+ average and basketball dreams: 'You saw his potential and tried to kill it.' Defense cited low female recidivism rate (~3%) and Garland's quick acceptance of responsibility. Garland offered a brief apology.
