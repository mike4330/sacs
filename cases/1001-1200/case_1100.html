Former history teacher at Cardinal McCarrick High School, South Amboy, New Jersey (Middlesex County). A private Catholic school in the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Metuchen. Age 24 at time of offense. Held a Teacher of Social Studies Certificate of Eligibility with Advanced Standing, issued June 2013.

One 17-year-old male student victim. Grupico had sexual relations with the student several times at the school between May 4 and May 30, 2015. School officials initiated the investigation by notifying police. The school closed permanently in June 2015 for financial reasons; Grupico was unemployed at the time of her arrest.

Arrested July 9, 2015 and held without bail at Middlesex County Jail. Originally charged with sexual assault and endangering the welfare of a child. Pleaded guilty April 25, 2016 to endangering the welfare of a child — sexual contact (3rd degree). Sentenced to three years probation and a fine; no prison. Teaching certificate relinquished with the force and effect of a revocation, accepted by the New Jersey State Board of Examiners on September 16, 2016 (Docket No. 1415-264). Investigators: Det. George Stilwell (Middlesex County Prosecutor's Office) and Det. Matthew Barcheski (South Amboy PD). Prosecutor: Andrew C. Carey.

NOTE: Offense occurred on school premises on multiple occasions. Prosecution did not confirm whether the victim was specifically one of Grupico's students. Charge reduction from sexual assault to 3rd-degree endangering welfare; no-prison outcome.
