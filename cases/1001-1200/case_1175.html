Former 8th-grade science teacher at Blanchard Middle School, Westford, Massachusetts. Groton resident. Married with two children (ages 17 and 16); husband Stephen, an engineer. Male victim, age 14, a student. Age 46 at time of offense.

Sexual intercourse with victim in defendant's car on April 8, 2005. Victim disclosed to parents in July; family reported to Westford police. Arrested August 31, 2005. Fired from position.

Superintendent Stephen Foster knew of the statutory rape charge the day after arrest but failed to notify the public for over two weeks, until contacted by the Lowell Sun after indictment. Foster told parents at a public forum he delayed because he did not know the specific charge, contradicted by his own email to the School Committee the morning after the arrest stating the charge was statutory rape of a child. Westford police omitted defendant's name and charge from the public police log, citing an ongoing-investigation exemption. The DA's office said it never instructed police to withhold the name.

Charged with statutory rape of a child (resolved to indecent assault and battery on a person 14 or over). Sentenced to 18 months in prison. Sex offender registration required.

NOTE: Significant institutional concealment — both the school superintendent and police actively suppressed public disclosure of the arrest. Incident occurred in vehicle off-campus. Defendant expressed no remorse in press interview, focused on impact to her own family and said she missed teaching.
