Former math teacher at New Hope High School, Lowndes County School District, Lowndes County, Mississippi. Married with two young children. Male victim, 17 years old (born 1993), a student at the school.

The indictment charges sexual penetration with the victim between November 19, 2010 and January 3, 2011, while Robbins occupied a position of trust or authority as the victim's teacher. Allegations of an inappropriate relationship surfaced in March 2011. Robbins was placed on administrative leave. One student told investigators that Robbins frequently flirted with male classmates and had given hugs and inappropriately touched other students. Lowndes County School District Superintendent Mike Halford stated termination was likely given the indictment.

Indicted April 26, 2011; arraigned May 10, 2011 before Judge James T. Kitchens Jr., pleaded not guilty; bond set at 25,000 dollars. Trial continued twice (August 2011, then December 2011). Pleaded guilty February 27, 2012; sentenced March 6, 2012: 10 years MDOC, all 10 years suspended, 5 years probation; 500-dollar fine; court costs. Commitment issued March 13, 2012. Motion for early release filed March 20, 2015; probation discharged February 16, 2016 — approximately 4 years into the 5-year term. Lowndes County Cause No. 44CI1:11-cr-00248-K.
