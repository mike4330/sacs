Former drama teacher at Old Bridge High School, Old Bridge, Middlesex County, New Jersey. Organized school plays and productions; taught at the school since September 2004. Previously taught in Marlboro Township (2001-2004) and Jersey City (2000-2001) school districts, and substitute taught in Weehawken (1999-2000). Age 34 at time of offense.

Victim: 17-year-old male drama student. Relationship lasted approximately four months, February to May 2008. Sexual encounters occurred on two occasions, neither on school property. Victim graduated in June 2008.

Investigators learned of the relationship November 25, 2008. Arrested December 3, 2008 at Old Bridge Police Headquarters; held at Middlesex County Adult Correctional Center. Superintendent Simon Bosco sent a letter to parents; internal investigation found no evidence any staff member had prior knowledge.

Pleaded guilty to fourth-degree endangering the welfare of a child, June 2, 2009, before Middlesex County Superior Court Judge Frederick De Vesa. State dropped second-degree sexual assault charge and reduced endangering charge from third degree to fourth degree; prosecutor stated the victim 'wanted to be spared having to testify.' Sentenced August 10, 2009: probation (no jail time). Judge cited defendant's depression and cancer diagnosis, letters from mental health professionals stating jail could be life-threatening, and a letter from the victim (then a college sophomore) who wrote that he was the initiator, did not feel victimized, and considered the case 'blown out of proportion.' Defendant forfeited her teaching certificate and right to hold any public job. Prosecutor Christie Bevacqua argued for the jail term; defense: Michael Nolan.
