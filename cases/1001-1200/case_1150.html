Former girls' varsity volleyball coach and counselor at Xenia High School, Xenia, Ohio (Xenia Community Schools); also previously coached basketball. Worked in the district 17 years, initially as a gym and health teacher before becoming a counselor. Female teenage victim, a student and member of her volleyball team. Age 43 at time of offense. Also known as Brenda Baillargeon.

Police investigation began with a tip; documented incidents occurred July 1 and September 2, 2009, off school grounds. Arrested September 3, 2009 and charged with three third-degree felony counts of sexual battery; posted $7,500 bond. Pleaded not guilty at arraignment in Greene County Common Pleas Court October 2, 2009. Changed plea to guilty in March 2010 to a single count of sexual battery; the other two counts were dismissed.

Sentenced June 30, 2010 by Judge Stephen A. Wolaver to six months in jail, five years probation, and lifetime sex-offender registration. Defendant declined to speak at sentencing but apologized to the victim through her attorney. Defense council submitted dozens of letters in support, and many supporters were present in the courtroom. Resigned from the district fall 2009 and lost her teaching certificate.

Prosecutor Nicole Burke described the impact on the victim: 'Her entire senior year was pretty much destroyed. She had to literally hide in a place where she should have felt safe.'

NOTE: Same-sex case; coach-on-team-member access pattern; charge reduction from 3 counts to 1 in plea.
