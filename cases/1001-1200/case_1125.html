Danielle Walls #1125 sentenced

StateCaliforniaCourtSan Diego Superior Court
JudgeFrank BrownIncident Date2004-04-01

Prison Sentence1.0 yr

Former 10th-grade history teacher at Clairemont High School, San Diego, California. Age 26 at time of offense.

Victim was a 16-year-old male student in her history class. Sexual contact occurred 10 to 25 times at four or five hotels around San Diego County and at the victim's car over an approximately eight-month period from April to December 2004. Walls provided cocaine and alcohol to the victim. Prosecutor described her as 'an out-of-control coke fiend' during the offense period. Walls told the court she felt her life did not matter at the time.

Originally charged with 16 counts. Arrested June 8, 2006 at her North Park home. Pleaded guilty in July 2006 to 5 counts including multiple counts of unlawful sexual contact, furnishing alcohol to a minor, and giving a controlled substance to a minor. Sentenced September 26, 2006 by Judge Frank Brown to 1 year jail and 5 years probation. Faced up to 5.5 years on the plea charges; faced up to 16 years on all original charges. Served approximately 6.5 months in jail before release.

In November 2009, Walls filed a motion to end probation two years early, reduce felonies to misdemeanors, and expunge her record. The DA's office opposed. The victim's mother opposed, stating: 'She gave him drugs, she gave him alcohol, she did unspeakable things to my son and it wasn't just one time. At some point she has to take responsibility for that and just serve the sentence.' Expungement hearing set December 3, 2009; outcome not established from available sources.

NOTE: Drugs and alcohol provision are integral to the conduct, not incidental. Deputy DA Moring emphasized the substance element in both arrest and sentencing proceedings.
