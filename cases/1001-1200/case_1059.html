Former special needs (special education) teacher at Martha Layne Collins High School, Shelby County Public Schools, Shelbyville, Kentucky. Transferred to Shelby County from Oldham County Public Schools approximately one year before arrest. Age 41 at time of offense.

Two male victims, both 17 years old, neither enrolled in Hirchert's class. Hirchert had five sexual encounters with one student and one encounter with the other, between September and December 2010. Encounters occurred at various locations across the county, including at Martha Layne Collins High School. She also sent pornographic photographs to both boys from her cell phone. The case came to light when a school employee overheard conversations and began asking questions; Detective Jason Rice conducted interviews leading to the arrest.

Arrested August 3, 2011. Charged with six counts of sexual abuse and two counts of distribution of obscene material to a minor. Originally pleaded not guilty; withdrew that plea and accepted a plea deal to sexual abuse and distributing obscene material to minors. Plea deal offered in part to protect victims' identities by avoiding testimony. Sentenced June 18, 2012: seven years probation; sex offender registration; prohibited from teaching in Kentucky; no contact with minors without probation officer permission.
