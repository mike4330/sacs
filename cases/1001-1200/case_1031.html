Former language arts teacher at Columbia High School, South Orange-Maplewood School District, Maplewood, Essex County, New Jersey. Employed at the school for approximately nine years; previously taught in Passaic and Bergen counties. Originally from Bloomfield; residing in Caldwell at time of arrest. Age 34 at time of offense; 35 at arrest. Divorced, two young sons. SBI No. 372429D.

Six male victims, ages 14–15, all students at Columbia High School. Conduct occurred during the regular school year and summer school in 2013 and 2014. Sex acts took place on school property and in her car on multiple occasions; conduct included oral sex and vaginal intercourse. A video of defendant performing oral sex on a minor went viral on social media, triggering the investigation. Civil litigation later revealed additional grooming conduct: defendant recruited victims through a summer educational program for students with learning disabilities, hand-picked favored students for her class, gave gifts including expensive sneakers, altered grades and attendance records of preferred students, and on at least one occasion showed a group of male students a video of herself having sex with an adult male school employee before asking them about their penis sizes.

Arrested September 17, 2014 on charges involving three students. Two additional victims charged September 19, 2014 (total five). Grand jury indicted February 17, 2015 on 40 counts of aggravated sexual assault and endangering the welfare of a child; a sixth victim was identified by the time of indictment. Defendant remained free on $500,000 bail. Pleaded not guilty at arraignment. Initial defense theory was 'frontal lobe syndrome' — counsel argued a brain shunt installed after her first pregnancy impaired her judgment and inhibition centers; this theory was subsequently abandoned. Court downgraded four charges due to insufficient evidence on two victims.

Pleaded guilty January 2020 (eve of trial) to three counts of aggravated criminal sexual contact, resolving the full 40-count indictment. Sentenced March 2021: 3-year suspended prison term (no incarceration); forfeiture of teaching certificates and bar from future public employment; parole supervision for life; lifetime sex offender registration and state supervision. Prosecuting APA: Gina Iosim. Defense: Timothy Smith.

Civil lawsuits filed in 2021 against the South Orange-Maplewood School District by at least four former students, alleging the district knew or should have known of the conduct based on grade manipulation, attendance irregularities, gifts to students, and rumors circulating among students.
