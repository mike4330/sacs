Special education teacher at Lexington Middle School, Lexington, Lee County, Texas. Pflugerville (Travis County) resident. Age 32 at time of offense.

Two male victims, both 8th grade students: a 13-year-old whose math tutoring Lopez had taken on, and his 14-year-old friend. For several days in February 2012, Lopez had sex with both boys at the older boy's home and in her car. She provided condoms. One of the boys contacted police because he 'began to feel weird about the situation.' Lopez resigned from Lexington Middle School after the investigation began.

Charged with two counts of aggravated sexual assault, two counts of indecency with a child by sexual contact, two counts of indecency with a child by exposure, and one count of improper relationship between educator and student. Pleaded guilty October 31, 2012 to one count of indecency with a child by sexual contact (PC §21.11). Counts 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, and 7 (aggravated sexual assault x2, indecency/contact x1, indecency/exposure x2, improper relationship x1) all waived. Sentenced December 7, 2012 by 390th District Court to 4 years TDCJ (Huntsville); no jail credit. Consecutive with prior sentence per order.
