Former teacher at Cardington Lincoln Local Schools, Cardington, Morrow County, Ohio. Age 41 at time of offense (DOB August 1982; offense fall 2023).

Male victim, a high school student at Cardington Lincoln High School but not a student in Malcolm's own classroom. Contact was conducted through Snapchat, during which Malcolm sent explicit images (identified as hers by distinctive fingernail patterns recognized by other students) and developed plans to engage in sexual activity with the victim. The victim reciprocated by exchanging images. Before law enforcement involvement, the victim deleted an image of Malcolm from his phone out of fear of repercussions; neither that image nor the Snapchat conversation was recoverable due to delays in Snapchat's legal compliance response. Screenshots of the conversation captured on the victim's phone showed Malcolm making sexual advances; discovery of those screenshots apparently alerted Malcolm, who abandoned her plan to meet the victim after school at either the school or his residence.

A second suspected victim was identified in summer 2024 but denied any solicitation or inappropriate contact. Prosecutor noted credible rumors of additional victims who may now be adults.

Indicted March 2024 (24 CR 27) on 2 counts illegal use of minor in nudity-oriented material (ORC 2907.323), 2 counts attempted sexual battery (ORC 2907.03 + 2923.02), and 2 counts disseminating matter harmful to juveniles. None of the original charges were viable for trial: the nudity counts required the victim to testify about the image and the family declined; the attempted sexual battery counts lacked the required 'significant step' under Ohio law; and the disseminating matter counts were misdemeanors that would have merged at sentencing with no sex offender registration requirement. Prosecutor Andrew Wick offered and Malcolm pleaded to Attempted Pandering, misdemeanor 1st degree — the charge that triggered Tier I sex offender registration.

Sentenced May 29, 2025 to 180 days jail with 60 days suspended (120 days continuous), 2 years community control, Tier I sex offender registration for 15 years (annual reporting), and permanent teaching license revocation. Defense attorney not named in sources. Prosecutor: Andrew Wick (Morrow County).

Prosecutor's statement strongly criticized Ohio's grooming law and advocated for legislative change; cited ORC 2907.071 (effective April 9, 2025) as partial but insufficient remedy. Victim was not one of Malcolm's own classroom students.
