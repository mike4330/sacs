Brenda Baillargeon #1200 sentenced

Former teacher's aide at Amery High School, Amery School District, Amery, Polk County, Wisconsin. Born 1968. Also known as Brenda Schaefer and Brenda Kae Schafer. Age 40 at sentencing; 39 at time of offense. Married; filed for divorce jointly with husband in August 2008. Male victim, age 16, a student whom Baillargeon tutored. Employed by the district for a number of years; resigned September 27, 2007, shortly before the investigation began.

Prosecutors alleged she had sex with the victim at least 10 times beginning in May 2007. Baillargeon allegedly believed she would marry the boy. The victim's mother filed a restraining order in October 2007. Four misdemeanor charges of violating that restraining order were dismissed as part of the plea deal.

Filed November 15, 2007. Defendant in custody at initial appearance before Judge Rasmussen. Bond set at $10,000 cash with no-contact order; reduced to $5,000 next day. Bond conditions included ban from County Hearth facility (Amery) and no contact with any employees there. Preliminary hearing waived November 21, 2007; bound over for trial. Arraigned December 28, 2007 before Judge GaleWyrick; not guilty pleas to all five counts. Defense attorney Owen R. Williams; prosecutor Daniel Patrick Steffen.

Pleaded no contest March 28, 2008 to Count 1 (Sex Assault of Student by School Staff, Felony H) and Count 4 (Child Enticement-Sexual Contact, Felony D). Counts 2, 3, and 5 dismissed but read in for purposes of restitution and sentencing.

Sentenced July 10, 2008 by Judge GaleWyrick. Count 1: 2 years state prison and 3 years extended supervision, imposed and stayed; 3 years probation. Count 4: 5 years state prison and 5 years extended supervision, imposed and stayed; 15 years probation. Sentences concurrent. Conditions: 1 year county jail subject to Huber (work-release), not subject to good time; banished from city limits of Amery and within one mile of victim's residence for 15 years; no contact with males under 18 without agent permission; sex offender treatment including polygraph within first six months; sex offender registration; DNA sample; restitution ordered. Not eligible for Challenge Incarceration or Earned Release programs. Victim impact statement authored and read by victim's mother. Court adopted PSI recommendations in totality.

Amended judgment of conviction issued November 25, 2008. Furlough from jail granted by joint stipulation November 21, 2008. In 2021-2022, defendant (now going by Schaefer) wrote letters requesting partial leniency of sentence. Relocated from Amery to Luck, WI after sentencing; later to Somerset, WI.

NOTE: The 15-year banishment from her own hometown is an unusual sentencing condition. Tutoring relationship gave one-on-one access; defendant believed she would marry the 16-year-old victim — textbook emotional fixation. Restraining order violations show continued contact after initial discovery. Work-release jail means sentence_years is 0 per convention. Five original counts across three offense dates (May, August, September 2007) plus four misdemeanor restraining order violations indicate sustained, escalating contact. Companion misdemeanor case 07CM619 dismissed as part of plea.
