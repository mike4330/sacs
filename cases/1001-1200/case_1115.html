Former English teacher at Newark Early College High School, a college preparatory program housed at Louise A. Spencer School, Newark, Essex County, New Jersey. Partnership program with Essex County College and Rutgers University. Age 26 at time of first sexual contact. California native; graduated California State University 2008; Master's in Education from Kean University; hired by Newark Public Schools 2009 at $50,000 per year. Cromwell described the position on social media as 'I LOVE MY JOB!'

One male student victim, age 14 at time of sexual conduct. Prosecutor documented a two-year grooming campaign (beginning approximately 2010-2011 when victim was 12-13) in which Cromwell gained the victim's trust before sending text messages stating she wanted to have sex. Cromwell also supplied the victim with marijuana. Multiple sexual encounters at her Jersey City home during summer 2012.

Arrested January 30, 2013; held at Hudson County jail in lieu of $300,000 bail; barred from contact with minors. Investigation initiated by Newark Police and referred to Hudson County Prosecutor's Office. Assistant Prosecutor Gene Rubino at arrest; Assistant Prosecutor Lynne Seborowski at sentencing.

Pleaded guilty February 18, 2015 to sexual assault and official misconduct. Sentenced May 29, 2015 in Hudson County Superior Court: five years in prison (mandatory sentence under the official misconduct charge). At sentencing, Cromwell said she was 'sorry for any pain or harm I caused.' Seborowski said Cromwell did not just cross the line, she 'obliterated' it. New Jersey Board of Examiners revoked her Teacher of English Certificate of Eligibility effective July 30, 2015.

NOTE: Two-year grooming period is documented by the prosecutor, beginning when the victim was approximately 12. Marijuana supply to victim is charged-conduct context, not a standalone charge. Mandatory sentence driven by the Official Misconduct count, not the sex offense count.
