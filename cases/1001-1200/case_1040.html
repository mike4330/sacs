Former special education teacher at Veterans Memorial Middle School, Muscogee County School District, Columbus, Georgia. Employed by the school district since 1993. Age 44 at initiation of the offense.

Male victim, 13 years old at the start of the relationship (Halloween 2011); turned 14 five days later. Preston initiated the relationship on October 31, 2011. Sexual encounters continued after the victim's birthday and ended January 30, 2012, when Muscogee County Sheriff's deputies found the pair parked together in Columbus's Cooper Creek Park in the pre-dawn hours. Police had been called to the victim's home earlier that evening.

Arrested January 31, 2012 at the Columbus Public Safety Center. Charged with two counts of child molestation, enticing a child for indecent purposes, and sexual assault against a person in custody. Placed on paid administrative leave; resigned January 2012. Bond set at $70,000. Defense: Clark Adams.

Jury found Preston guilty on all charges June 5, 2013: aggravated child molestation, child molestation, statutory rape, and sexual assault on a person in custody. Sentenced June 6, 2013 by Superior Court Judge John Allen to life in prison with 25 years before parole eligibility; lifetime probation thereafter; sex offender registration; no contact with minors other than her own children or with the victim.
