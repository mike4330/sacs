Former ninth-grade science teacher at Carter-Riverside High School, Fort Worth Independent School District, Fort Worth, Tarrant County, Texas. Age 27 at time of offense. Male victim, 16 years old, a football player who had been a student in her class the prior school year.

Riojas engaged in a sexual relationship with the victim during the prior school year when he was enrolled in her class. The victim came forward to police because he was concerned he might be the father of Riojas's unborn child. Riojas resigned from FWISD in October 2011, citing stress from her pregnancy; her resignation letter denied any wrongdoing and described the pregnancy rumors as false gossip.

Charged with improper relationship between educator and student. Pleaded guilty January 17, 2012 in Tarrant County District Court: 6 years deferred adjudication probation; teaching certificate forfeited; sex offender evaluation required; treatment if determined necessary.
