Former teacher at Oologah-Talala High School, Oologah-Talala Public Schools (School District I-004), Rogers County, Oklahoma. Age 28 at time of first sexual contact.

One male student victim, age 17 at onset of sexual contact (turned 18 in December 2017). Inappropriate behavior began in October 2017 — Smart rubbed the student's back, placed her head on his shoulder, and hugged him in front of other students on multiple occasions. Sexual contact began in November 2017. Smart and the student had sex both after class at the school and at his home. During the two-week statewide Oklahoma teacher walkout in April 2018, Smart continued to have sex with the student at his home. Regardless of the student's age at any point, sexual contact between a teacher and student is prohibited under Oklahoma state law. In May 2018, Smart texted a friend about the relationship and acknowledged that she believed the school would find sufficient evidence to terminate her.

Smart was suspended from the school district in May 2018; she resigned several days later. Employment ended May 29, 2018. Arrest warrant issued August 17, 2018. Smart turned herself in August 24, 2018 and was released on $25,000 bond. Charged with second-degree rape (Rogers County Sheriff's Office). Case filed January 31, 2020 (CF-2020, Rogers County District Court).

Pleaded nolo contendere January 30, 2020 to assault with intent to commit a felony (21 O.S. 681), a significant charge reduction from second-degree rape. Received a 5-year deferred sentence; $500 fine, $250 victim compensation assessment, and court costs; 250 hours community service; psychological evaluation required; no contact with victim; no employment at any school. Supervised DOC probation. Judge Kassie McCoy; prosecutor Kali Strain; defense attorney J. Price.

NOTE: Charge reduction from second-degree rape to assault with intent to commit a felony removes this from sex offense territory — Smart likely avoids SORA registration entirely.
