Former substitute teacher at Ella Lewis School (grades K-8), Steuben, RSU 24, Washington County, Maine. Age 47 at time of offense. Male victim, approximately 13 years old (8th-grade student).

The case came to light in April 2011 when another teacher confiscated the victim's cellphone during class and saw a text message from Leach that raised concerns. Maine State Police launched an investigation; Leach was arrested in May 2011. The complaint alleged Leach engaged in sexual acts with the victim in December 2010 and March 2011. RSU 24 subsequently obtained a protection order requiring Leach to stay off all school properties.

Pleaded no contest February 6, 2012 to two counts of gross sexual assault in Machias. Sentenced July 13, 2012 by Justice Alan Hunter in Washington County Superior Court: 1 year in state prison; 2 years probation upon release; sex offender registration; restitution of 2,100 dollars to the victim's family.
