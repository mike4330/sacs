Former math teacher at Burke High School, Charleston County School District, Charleston, South Carolina. Age 27 at time of arrest. Married with at least one infant child at time of offense.

One male student victim, age 17. Aro-Olajire initiated sexual conversations in her math class, then coerced the student into sexual intercourse on multiple occasions — on school grounds, in her vehicle, and at her residence. She pressured the student by reminding him that she controlled his grades; when he refused her advances she changed his grade from 98 to 89. The student disclosed to his mother after receiving his final math grade for the term; his mother contacted authorities. Aro-Olajire remained a paid district employee following her arrest.

Bond hearing December 19, 2017; indicted June 4, 2018. Two counts of sexual battery with a student 16 or 17 years of age, no aggravated force or coercion (CDR 3463). Sentenced December 10, 2019: five years, served upon 1 day (credit time served); balance suspended with 2 years probation. Active probation December 11, 2019.

The student and his mother filed a civil lawsuit against Charleston County School District in September 2018, alleging the district had a 'history of employees engaging in romantic relationships with students' and failed to properly supervise Aro-Olajire. The victim's mother at a December 2017 hearing: 'He is 17. He is not an adult. Emotionally, he is not ready for this.'

NOTE: CDR 3463. Grade coercion as pressure mechanism is explicit in the civil filing. The presence of a 10-month-old infant during sex acts is documented in the lawsuit.
