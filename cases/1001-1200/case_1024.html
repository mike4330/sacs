Former Wrap Around Specialist (student support counselor) at Eastwood Academy (Houston ISD), 1315 Dumble St., Houston TX 77023, Harris County. Age 31 at time of offense.

One female student victim, age 18. The victim had been referred to Demetriou for counseling regarding personal issues she was experiencing. In September or October 2021, the victim expressed intimate feelings for Demetriou; Demetriou initially said nothing could happen due to the counselor/student dynamic, then began calling the victim while intoxicated approximately one to two weeks later. The two communicated via Instagram and Google Numbers phone calls, including calls at 4 AM. On one visit to Demetriou's residence in the Montrose area, Demetriou took photos of the victim with her personal camera, drank with the victim, and attempted to convince her to stay longer. When the victim tried to leave, Demetriou was too intoxicated to drive; the victim drove Demetriou's vehicle, and Demetriou directed her to a friend's house to "show off" the victim, where Demetriou's vehicle struck another car. On the third visit, Demetriou digitally penetrated the victim's vagina. Attempted oral sex on the victim did not occur. Demetriou required the victim to delete messages between them after each visit. Demetriou told the victim: "Don't tell anyone I have feelings for you too."

On January 31, 2022, principal Brandi Lira contacted Houston ISD Police Department Officer A. Contreras after the victim reported the relationship. Assigned investigator: HISD Detective J. Quirin. Digital forensic extraction of victim's phone revealed images of Demetriou and phone call records; victim also provided Instagram message screenshots. ADA J. Oswald accepted the charge. Complaint filed September 2, 2022; Harris County cause no. 1785466, 177th Criminal District Court.

No arrest or booking — appeared voluntarily; bond $10,000 surety posted 9/8/2022. Emergency protective order sought at filing; protective order on case materials obtained 11/16/2022. Bond conditions violation report filed 11/28/2022; amended bond conditions ordered 11/30/2022. Student records and department records subpoenaed by state (multiple rounds). Indicted 4/28/2023. Plea entered 6/1/2023. 3-year deferred adjudication community supervision granted 6/23/2023 (Judge Emily DeToto, 177th Criminal District Court). Fees paid in full. Post-judgment supervision modification orders 2/5/2025 and 3/17/2025. DADJ projected through ~6/23/2026. Defense: Matthew David Sharp (hired, through 11/2022), then Henry Khoa Nguyen (hired, from 11/30/2022). Born 04/03/1990; age 32 at filing.

NOTE: Prior Harris County criminal record includes felony theft (filed 2015, DADJ) and misdemeanor theft (filed 2010, DADJ) — both involving theft from acquaintances for pawn — plus a 20-day sentence for assault of a family member (2015).
