Former color guard and dance/music instructor at Sharpstown High School and Bellaire High School, Houston, Texas (HISD). Age 31 at first offense.

Victim: male student who met defendant as a freshman at Sharpstown High School during the 2005–2006 school year, when defendant was the color guard instructor. A sexual relationship began in 2006. Defendant transferred from Sharpstown to Bellaire High School around the same time the victim transferred in 2007–2008, following the victim to his new school.

First incident (on or about August 19, 2007, victim age 16): Victim was in the backseat of defendant’s vehicle in the parking lot of his mother’s apartment complex, in Houston. Defendant removed victim’s penis from his pants and placed it in her vagina while she was positioned on top of him; no condom used; victim ejaculated into defendant’s vagina. Defendant subsequently gave birth to a daughter (born approximately 2008). HPD DNA paternity testing (Analyst Priscilla Ancira) confirmed victim cannot be excluded as the biological father; probability of paternity >99.99%.

Second incident (on or about January 17, 2009, victim age 18): Victim and defendant had vaginal intercourse at a residence in Harris County. Victim placed his penis inside defendant’s vagina. Defendant subsequently gave birth to a second daughter (born 2009). HPD DNA paternity testing confirmed victim cannot be excluded as the biological father; probability of paternity >99.99%.

Defendant’s employment terminated January 21, 2009.

Complaint filed June 13, 2011 (HPD Juvenile Sex Crimes Unit, Affiant J.B. Colburn); defendant absent at preliminary hearing; bond $30,000. Indicted July 19, 2011. Bond revoked September 8, 2011 (failure to appear August 26). Harris County Cause Nos. 130971301010 and 130971201010 (SA child 14–17). Defense: Erinn Genelle Brown (hired). Jury trial set and continued multiple times (August 2012, October 2012, March 2013). Last-minute deferred adjudication granted March 22, 2013: 10 years on both counts. Both cases terminated March 2023.

Brady notice regarding DNA evidence recalculation filed April 4, 2017 (post-conviction; likely relates to HPD Crime Lab DNA mixture analysis methodology review, though paternity evidence here was buccal swab—not crime scene mixture DNA).

NOTE: sentence_years = 0; DADJ completed March 2023. Improper relationship charged for the 2009 incident (victim age 18, adult student); SA charged for the 2007 incident (victim age 16, minor). Paternity of two children is the physical evidence establishing the long-term relationship. Defendant transferred schools to follow the victim—an unusual and deliberate grooming pattern.
