Former behavioral teacher at Manual High School, Peoria, Illinois. Employed for approximately four years. Male victim, age 16, a student. Born 1980; age 26 at time of offense.

On December 9, 2006, Church used MySpace to initiate contact and solicit a minor for sexual activity. Arrested March 8, 2007 in federal criminal complaint. Pled guilty August 17, 2007 to federal enticement of a minor. Sentenced February 21, 2008 to 84 months (7 years) in federal prison plus lifetime supervised release.

Federal prosecution under Project Safe Childhood (nationwide initiative created February 2006 to protect children from online exploitation). Case prosecuted by Assistant U.S. Attorney Thomas A. Keith, U.S. Attorney's Office, Central District of Illinois. Investigation conducted by CyberCrime Unit (CICU), multi-agency group, with cooperation of Peoria Public School District 150 and Illinois Department of Children and Family Services.
