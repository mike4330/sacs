Former teacher at Cypress-Fairbanks Independent School District in Harris County, Texas. Age 26 at arrest. The known victim was a 15-year-old male student.

Communication between Mobley and the victim began in April 2015 through text and Facebook messages, initially about a class project, and escalated into personal and sexual content. Sexual encounters occurred both on and off campus.

The case opened when the victim's mother noticed an unusual spike in her son's phone usage to a specific number, researched the number, identified it as Mobley's, and went to school administrators. The district questioned Mobley at school on May 19, 2015. During that interview Mobley admitted to being "in love" with the student but denied that anything sexual had occurred. Administrators contacted law enforcement.

Two counts of indecency with a child by sexual contact were filed on July 2, 2015; Mobley was arrested July 3, 2015 and held in lieu of a $30,000 bond on each count. Defense counsel David Allen Nachtigall was retained.

The case resolved through an unusually compressed procedural sequence. On December 16, 2015, the State filed a fresh complaint charging improper relationship between an educator and a student. The next day, December 17, 2015, the court accepted Mobley's plea on that newly filed count and granted deferred adjudication; both original indecency counts were dismissed simultaneously. Mobley successfully completed approximately three and a quarter years of community supervision before the court granted early termination on March 13, 2019.
