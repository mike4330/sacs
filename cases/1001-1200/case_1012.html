Former school secretary (Clerk 2) at James Madison High School, Houston, Texas (HISD). Age 20 at time of offense — among the youngest defendants in this corpus.

Victim: 15-year-old male student at James Madison High School. Defendant met victim while working in the main office of the school; victim visited the office regularly. On or about June 10, 2010, during the summer school session, defendant picked up victim and his friend (witness Juan Montes) from the James Madison campus in her vehicle, drove to a nearby restaurant, then allowed the friend to drive her car while she and victim had sex in the backseat. Victim placed his penis inside defendant's vagina while wearing a condom, while Montes drove and observed them in the rearview mirror. Offense location: 14600 block of Hiram Clarke Rd., Houston, Harris County.

Defendant gave a voluntary, non-custodial statement to HPD: admitted sexual intercourse with victim, confirmed it occurred in the backseat of her vehicle while victim's friend drove, and stated she met victim while working as Clerk 2 at James Madison High School.

Complaint filed March 28, 2011 (HPD Juvenile Sex Crimes Unit, Affiant J.B. Colburn); probable cause found without arrest at initial hearing. Arrested April 10, 2011. Felony indictment July 21, 2011 (bond $20,000 on §21.12 count; $30,000 on SA count). Harris County Cause Nos. 130056301010 (improper relationship) and 130056201010 (sexual assault of child 14–17). Defense: Jeffery Louis Greco (hired).

Deferred adjudication granted September 29, 2011 for 5 years. Both cause numbers terminated October 18, 2016 (successful completion).
