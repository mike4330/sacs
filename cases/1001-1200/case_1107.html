Former math teacher at East Hartford High School, East Hartford, Hartford County, Connecticut. Age 28 at time of offense.

One female student victim, age 18. Ongoing sexual relationship with the student, including at least one encounter at the school and additional encounters at Motulski's home at Cooper Drive, Glastonbury. Relationship reported to the East Hartford Board of Education on May 29, 2012, by Motulski's estranged husband Joshua Motulski; she was placed on administrative leave the following day and resigned within the week. Her husband subsequently provided police with letters she had written to the victim. In a letter dated May 31, 2012 — after her employment had ended — Motulski wrote: 'I know that I face jail time and that my education career is over but with all said and done I would do it all again.'

Arrested July 26, 2012 on two counts of fourth-degree sexual assault; a second warrant followed August 9, 2012 adding six additional counts. Pleaded guilty February 27, 2013 to three counts of fourth-degree sexual assault. Sentenced approximately May 3, 2013: three years total, execution suspended after 75 days in jail, followed by three years probation.

NOTE: Victim was 18, making this a same-sex adult-victim case under CT's school employee prohibition. The letter produced by the estranged husband was the primary documentary evidence; he is effectively the reporting party.
