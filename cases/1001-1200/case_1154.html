Deanna Bobo #1154 closed

Also known as: Deanna Jean Marino, Deanna Jean Mathis

Special-education teacher at Raymond E. Wells Junior High School, Greenwood School District, Greenwood, Arkansas. Age 37 at time of offense. Male victim, age 14, was one of defendant's students; she served as his math co-teacher in eighth grade. Defendant lived with husband Rusty Bobo, an oilfield mechanic, and their five children.

Offense conduct occurred between February 1 and March 15, 2005, involving sexual intercourse with the victim on two occasions: once in February 2005 when Bobo picked the victim up from church and drove to a loading dock where intercourse occurred in her vehicle, and again a few weeks later at the victim's home while his parents were away.

Discovery: On November 3, 2005, the victim's mother found the victim's email account open on the family computer and read sexually explicit exchanges between the victim and Bobo. She forwarded the emails to her husband, who contacted the prosecutor's office and was referred to the Greenwood Police Department. Detective Heather McCaslin received the emails; Sergeant James Flynn issued subpoenas to internet providers, obtained consent to search the victim's computer, and secured a warrant to search Bobo's computer. Forensic examination recovered 19 emails. Bobo's computer had crashed in June 2005; the victim had deleted his copies. The State used forwarded printouts authenticated via IP address matching and expert testimony.

Arrested November 2005. Released on $10,000 surety bond with no-contact order. Suspended with pay from school. Case filed 11/17/2005 in Sebastian Greenwood Circuit Court, case number 66GCR-05-168A. Charged with four counts of sexual assault in the first degree, Class A felony. Pled not guilty on all counts. Jury trial before Judge Norman Wilkinson resulted in guilty verdict on counts 3 and 4 on September 29, 2006. Sentenced to 72 months (6 years) on each count, to run consecutively, for a total of 12 years in the Arkansas Department of Correction. Sex offender registration required.

Appeal (CACR07-401) decided May 28, 2008: Arkansas Court of Appeals affirmed on all three grounds — admission of prior grooming conduct (victim touching defendant's breast at school, admitted under pedophile exception), admission of the 19 forwarded emails, and failure to preserve Fifth Amendment objection to jury hearing her request for counsel during recorded statement.

Subsequently charged with failure to register as sex offender or report address change (case 66GCR-18-246, offense date September 26, 2018, age 50). Arrested October 8, 2018. Negotiated guilty plea on March 14, 2019 before Judge Delay. Sentenced to 24 months Department of Corrections plus 96 months (8 years) suspended. Case closed March 14, 2019.

NOTE: Verdict case — convicted by jury on two of four counts. The consecutive 12-year sentence is the correct total; prior DB entry of 6.0 reflected per-count sentence only. The pedophile exception ruling is notable: the court found a student-teacher relationship qualifies as an "intimate relationship" for 404(b) purposes, allowing antecedent grooming conduct (victim touching defendant's breast twice in class, which Bobo did not report or discipline) as corroboration of the victim's testimony. Court record shows aliases Marino and Mathis, indicating prior marriages.
