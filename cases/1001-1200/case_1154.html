Special-education teacher at Raymond E. Wells Junior High School, Greenwood School District, Greenwood, Arkansas. Age 37 at time of offense (born 1967). Male victim, age 14, was one of defendant's students.

Offense conduct occurred between February 1 and March 15, 2005, involving sexual intercourse with the victim. Greenwood Police Department executed a search warrant on defendant's home computer and located sexually explicit e-mails between defendant and the victim. Defendant lived with husband Rusty Bobo, an oilfield mechanic, and their five children.

Arrested November 2005. Released on $10,000 surety bond with no-contact order. Suspended with pay from school. Case filed 11/17/2005 in Sebastian Greenwood Circuit Court, case number 66GCR-05-168A. Charged with four counts of sexual assault in the first degree, Class A felony, under A.C.A. 5-14-124 (employee of victim's school engaging in sexual intercourse with a person under 18). Pled not guilty on all counts. Jury trial resulted in guilty verdict on counts 3 and 4 on September 29, 2006. Sentenced to 72 months (6 years) imposed on each count.

NOTE: Verdict case — defendant went to trial and was convicted by jury on two of four counts. Court record shows aliases Marino and Mathis, indicating prior marriages. The 6-year sentence per count is moderate for a Class A felony (max 30 years in Arkansas). Sexually explicit e-mails provided key physical evidence. Special-education teacher role amplifies the trust violation given vulnerable student population.

Subsequently charged with failure to register as sex offender or report address change (case 66GCR-18-246, offense date September 26, 2018, age 50). Arrested October 8, 2018. Negotiated guilty plea on March 14, 2019 before Judge Delay. Sentenced to 24 months Department of Corrections plus 96 months (8 years) suspended. Case closed March 14, 2019.
