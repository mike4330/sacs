Sarah Drown #1044 sentenced

Also known as: Sarah Elizabeth Drown

Former part-time after-school aide and tutor at Lillian Larsen Elementary School, San Miguel, San Luis Obispo County, California. First year of employment with the district. Age 20 at time of offense.

Male victim, 14 years old, an 8th-grade student. The victim got to know Drown in October 2011 while helping at the after-school program. The relationship progressed over subsequent weeks. During Christmas break, Drown received a call from the victim while feeling depressed about a conflict with her boyfriend. They took a walk, talked, and returned to her home to watch a movie, during which they had sex. Drown admitted to investigators that sex occurred once at her home; the victim stated it had occurred several times starting in October.

Discovery: In January 2012, a teacher at the school saw Drown walking home with the 8th-grade student and later noticed what appeared to be hickey marks on his neck. The teacher reported her concerns to another employee, who brought them to the district superintendent. Drown was placed on administrative leave and directed to stay away from school grounds and avoid contact with students, staff, and parents. Arrested January 27, 2012.

Originally charged with four counts of statutory rape. Pleaded not guilty February 1, 2012. Later pleaded no contest to one count of statutory rape. Sentenced June 26, 2012 by Judge Barry LaBarbera: 180 days in San Luis Obispo County Jail and three years of formal probation; no contact with the victim or other underage boys during probation. Not required to register as a sex offender — statutory rape does not carry mandatory registration under California law. Prosecutor: Dan Dow; Defense: Christine Cummings.

NOTE: sentence_years = 0.5 (180 days county jail). No sex offender registration. Position of trust as after-school aide was an aggravating factor at sentencing per the DA.
