Former special education teacher at Garden City Middle School and Garden City High School, Garden City Public Schools, Garden City, Wayne County, Michigan. Age 43 at time of offense.

Male victim, age 14 when conduct began and 16 at the last contact. The victim was a student in Homberg's special education class in 8th grade at the middle school and again in 10th grade at the high school. Homberg was also a family friend who assisted the family when the victim's mother was ill. She regularly gave students lunch money, bottled water, her personal cell phone number, and other support.

First sexual contact occurred when the victim was in 8th grade: Homberg came to his home, entered his bedroom, and initiated vaginal intercourse. Additional incidents occurred in a school office adjacent to her classroom — both while the victim was under 16 (8th grade) and again at age 16 (10th grade). The victim repeatedly denied any impropriety when asked by the school principal, his girlfriend, friends, a family attorney, and his parents; he disclosed only to the lead detective after a forensic interview.

Homberg resigned on March 8, 2010. Arraigned March 29, 2010 before Judge Richard Hammer (21st District Court); bond set at $50,000. In May 2010, bond was increased to $100,000 and she was taken into custody after calling another student in violation of a no-contact order.

Originally charged with 2 counts CSC I, 2 counts CSC III, and distributing sexually explicit material to a minor. Convicted by jury of 1 count CSC I (MCL 750.520b(1)(b), victim age 13–16, defendant a teacher) and 2 counts CSC III (MCL 750.520d(1)(e), victim age 16–18, defendant a teacher). Sentenced to 10–25 years on CSC I and 3–15 years on each CSC III conviction, all concurrent. Conviction affirmed on appeal October 18, 2011. Defense: Raymond Correll. Prosecutor: Kym Worthy (Wayne County Prosecutor's Office).

NOTE: Conduct spanned December 2007 through February 2010 per prosecutor. Distributing sexually explicit material charge outcome not reflected in the appeal record; likely acquitted or dismissed at trial. Bond violation in May 2010 after calling a second student unrelated to the charged victim. Victim's special ed placement documented but specific disability not stated in sources.
