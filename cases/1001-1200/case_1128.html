Former school bus driver for Chesterfield County Public Schools, Virginia. Drove bus for Chester Middle School.

Victim was a 15-year-old male student, the boy she drove on her bus route. The relationship began when Allanson was the victim's bus driver and continued for two years. After a sexual encounter in the woods on May 6, 2009, Allanson continued to communicate with the student via text messages and emails over the following two years, including after her arrest. The victim's mother discovered the relationship after finding fully clothed photos of Allanson on her son's computer flash drive labeled 'The love of my life.'

Convicted of taking indecent liberties with a minor while in a custodial role. Sentenced in July 2012 (approximately) to 5 years with 4 years suspended (1-year active term). In an unusual sentencing arrangement, the Chesterfield judge then stayed the 1-year active term — tantamount to suspending the entire 5-year sentence. No prison time served.
