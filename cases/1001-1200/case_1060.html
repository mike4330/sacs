Former volunteer junior varsity softball coach at Napa High School, Napa, California; also coached for the Napa Valley Express girls travel team. Age 34 at time of offense. Female victim, 16 years old, a player on both teams.

Tharp separated from her husband in early 2011 and moved in with the victim family. A sexual relationship developed between March and July 2011. The relationship came to light when parents of other players reported suspicions about the unusual closeness between coach and player to authorities on July 11, 2011. Tharp was arrested July 22, 2011 on four felony charges. Released on bail, she violated a civil restraining order by meeting with the victim three times; she was taken back into custody and misdemeanor charges of contributing to the delinquency of a minor and contempt of court were added.

Pleaded no contest October 14, 2011 — the date of her scheduled preliminary hearing — to felony oral copulation and sexual penetration with a foreign object (both with a person under 18) and two misdemeanor counts of child molestation. Sentenced November 10, 2011 by Judge Mark Boessenecker: 3 years 8 months in state prison. Two felony charges, contributing-to-delinquency misdemeanors, and contempt charges dismissed at sentencing.

NOTE: Volunteer coaching role provided direct access to the victim. Tharp moved into the victim home following her marital separation, amplifying dependency and access. Restraining-order violations while on bail added charges but did not materially affect the ultimate plea outcome.
