Former middle-school language-arts instructor at Buttonwillow School in Buttonwillow, California (Kern County). Male victim, age 12, a student in the school. Age 41 at time of offense — a 29-year age gap.

The investigation began at least a week before the arrest after the victim's mother discovered a letter in her son's backpack indicating he and Brians had a romantic and physical relationship at the school. Sgt. Richard Wood of the Kern County Sheriff's Department led the investigation.

Brians was arrested January 4, 2006 on felony suspicion of lewd acts with a child under 14 and held on $100,000 bail; the felony carried a three-year minimum on conviction. On March 12, 2007, she pleaded no contest to reduced charges of misdemeanor battery and contributing to the delinquency of a minor; the lewd-acts felony was dismissed. No prison time.
