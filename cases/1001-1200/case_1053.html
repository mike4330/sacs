Former employee at Devereux Glenholme School, a residential therapeutic boarding school in Washington, Litchfield County, Connecticut. The school serves students ages 10–18 with Asperger's, ADHD, PDD, OCD, Tourette's, depression, anxiety, and other learning differences. Age 27 at time of offense.

Male victim, 16 years old, a special-needs student at the residential school. Larson initiated contact via computer, which led to a sexual relationship that lasted from May 2011 to December 2011. The school terminated Larson's employment after learning of the allegations.

Arrested October 13, 2011 on a warrant charging second-degree sexual assault; released on $25,000 bond. State's Attorney Terri Sonnemann described the relationship as non-coercive and consensual. Neither the victim nor his parents appeared at sentencing.

Original charge: second-degree sexual assault. Pleaded guilty to third-degree sexual assault. Sentenced May 21, 2013 by Litchfield Superior Court: 10-year prison term suspended after nine months, followed by 10 years of probation; sex offender registration required.
