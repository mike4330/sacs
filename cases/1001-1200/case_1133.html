Former paraprofessional (teacher's aide) at Merrill Middle School, Oshkosh, Winnebago County, Wisconsin. Beginning her fourth year working with special education students at the time of offense. Age 26 at arrest; 27 at sentencing.

Victim was a 14-year-old male student at the school. Quednow had known the victim for approximately two years before the relationship became sexual in July 2014. No direct school-related connection with the victim — the superintendent stated 'they had no connection other than being in the same building' and that the relationship developed 'purely in the community over the summer.' Sexual activity occurred July and August 2014, off school property. Discovery mechanism: teachers from another school observed Quednow with the student and reported to their supervisor, triggering the mandatory report.

Arrested September 10, 2014 by Oshkosh Police following investigation by school and district; fired the same day. Charged with repeated sexual assault of a child, three counts of child enticement, and one count of sexual exploitation of a child. Pleaded guilty February 2015. Sentenced May 26, 2015 by Judge Thomas Gritton to five years in prison, 15 years extended supervision, and 10 years probation. Lifetime sex offender registration. ADA Scott Ceman; DA Christian Gossett.

Prosecutor Ceman stated Quednow groomed the victim and his family and cited the 'predatory nature of this offense.' The victim suffered social media harassment and his school attendance dropped. Judge Gritton: 'You understood what you were doing was wrong and you couldn't stop it. You were more concerned about whether he liked you than doing the right thing.' Quednow's statement: 'When I think about myself at that age and how innocent I was, it kills me to know that I've taken away that youthful innocence from him.'
