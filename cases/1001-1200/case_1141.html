Former substitute teacher in the Towanda Area School District (Towanda, Bradford County, Pennsylvania). Also worked as an assistant varsity softball coach at Pembroke Academy in New Hampshire and as the director of a summer softball program; was living in New Hampshire at the time of sentencing. Marital status not established. Age 25 at the time of the documented offense conduct. The known victim was a 17-year-old male student in the Towanda Area School District.

The conduct began at a non-school setting. In November 2017, Roberts attended a party where she suggestively approached the victim, kissed him, and exchanged social-media contact information with him. The following day, Roberts picked the victim up from a friend's house and drove him to her residence in Franklin Township, where the sexual contact occurred.

The Pennsylvania State Police at Towanda charged Roberts in March 2018 with two counts of institutional sexual assault and one count of corruption of minors. She was arraigned on March 13, 2018 with a $10,000 unsecured bail.

On March 12, 2019, Roberts pleaded guilty to one count of institutional sexual assault, a third-degree felony under Pennsylvania's educator-specific statute. The second institutional-sexual-assault count and the corruption-of-minors count were dismissed pursuant to the plea. In June 2019, the Bradford County Court of Common Pleas sentenced her to twelve months of probation supervision, court costs, and sex-offender registration. No incarceration was imposed.
