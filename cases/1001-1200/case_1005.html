Former agriculture teacher in Henderson Independent School District (Henderson, Texas, Rusk County). Age 36 at her December 2024 arrest. The known victim was a student in the district whose age and sex were not reported in available sources; victim age is recorded as 16 as a high-school inferred estimate (agriculture is typically a high-school assignment).

Henderson Police were tipped that Prior had exchanged inappropriate photos and text messages with the victim and had touched the victim inappropriately. The investigation began on that tip and led to her arrest in December 2024.

The original charging instrument carried three counts: possession of child sexual abuse material, indecency with a child, and improper relationship between an educator and a student. The Rusk County District Attorney's Office prosecuted.

Prior pleaded guilty to the educator-relationship count. The child-sexual-abuse-material possession count was dropped pursuant to the plea. The indecency-with-a-child count was disposed of through Texas's "taken into consideration" mechanism — Prior admitted guilt and the underlying conduct was weighed at sentencing, but no separate conviction was entered on that charge.

On March 30, 2026, Judge David Hill sentenced Prior to 180 days in county jail to be served before probation begins, followed by probation, with surrender of her Texas teaching license.

NOTE: Sentence_years recorded at 0.5 to reflect the 180-day pre-probation jail term. The available reporting describes that period as mandatory time before probation begins with no work-release qualification, so it counts as continuous custody. Texas's "taken into consideration" disposition is procedurally distinctive: the defendant's admission of guilt is weighed at sentencing without producing a separate conviction or registration consequence on the indecency count, even though the underlying conduct is acknowledged in the record. The original possession-of-CSAM count was dropped at plea, removing that registration-and-public-record exposure entirely.
