Toni Allexy #1197 sentenced

StateCaliforniaCourtButte County Superior Court
JudgeunknownIncident Date2007-01-01
Defendant Age38Victim Age14
Prison Sentence2.0 yrs

Former cheerleading coach at Paradise High School, Paradise, California. Male victim, age 14, a student on the football team. Age 38 at time of offense.

Defendant invited the victim to her home, where she encouraged him to drink alcohol with her until he was severely intoxicated. She drew her initials on his cheek, removed his football jersey and wore it herself, then photographed herself sitting in his lap. She sent the photo to the victim's girlfriend with the message "Don't you wish you were here?" Over her husband's objection, she insisted on driving the victim home, requiring him to sit in the front seat, where she inserted her hand into his pants and stroked his penis during the drive.

Charged in Butte County Superior Court with six counts including committing a lewd act on a minor. Accepted plea bargain to felony child endangerment, May 6, 2008.

Sentenced: four years in state prison, execution suspended; four years felony probation with warning that probation violation would trigger sex offender registration. Approximately one year later, Allexy violated probation twice — first for unauthorized contact with a minor at a skate park, then for embezzling over $10,000 from her employer. Probation revoked; four-year sentence executed. Court also ordered sex offender registration.

Allexy appealed the registration order. The Third Appellate District (People v. Toni Allexy (2012) 204 Cal.App.4th 1358) held the trial court erred procedurally in imposing registration at revocation rather than at original sentencing, but found the error harmless because defense counsel had failed to object at sentencing (forfeiture/invited error). Registration upheld. Under California Penal Code, registration requires the court to find at sentencing that the offense was committed for sexual gratification or due to sexual compulsion — which the facts here supported.

Victim impact: the victim quit school, began drinking heavily and using drugs, and ran away from home following the incident.
