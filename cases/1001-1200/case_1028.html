Former English teacher at University High School, Orange City, Volusia County, Florida. Age 26 at time of offense. Married.

Male victim, 16 years old, enrolled at University High School. Relationship developed August–September 2011 via text messages (thousands exchanged); one sexual encounter took place in a car at a park. Defendant was listed as 'My Precious Angel' in the victim's phone. Defendant ended the relationship in December 2011 amid the investigation.

Discovery: In December 2011, defendant called 911 anonymously claiming a student was suicidal and had access to a gun. Deputies responded to the school, found the student upset but not suicidal, and he disclosed the relationship. Investigators subsequently identified defendant as the anonymous caller through her cell phone. She resigned mid-December 2011 after the investigation began.

Arrested approximately February 28, 2012; charged with unlawful sexual activity with a minor. Sentenced February 20, 2013: 30 months state prison (credit 1 day), $720 court costs; sentence concurrent with any active case.

NOTE: The 911 call originated from the defendant herself — an unusual self-implicating discovery. The victim had reportedly been sending suicidal texts after she ended the relationship; her concern for his wellbeing led directly to the investigation that charged her.
