Gabrielle Marvelli #1063 sentenced

Former business teacher at William Allen High School, Allentown, Lehigh County, Pennsylvania, employed by the district since 2007. Age 38 at time of offense. Male victim, 17 years old (one week shy of his 18th birthday), a senior at the school.

On May 28, 2011 — prom weekend — Marvelli met the victim at a motel in Allentown in the early morning hours and had sexual intercourse with him. Charged June 2011 with a single count of corruption of a minor. Entered a not guilty plea at formal arraignment August 18, 2011 before Judge Maria Dantos. Marvelli was suspended with pay shortly after the incident; the district conducted an internal investigation and she no longer worked for Allentown School District by the time of arraignment.

Pleaded guilty November 2011 to the single count of corruption of a minor. Sought probation at sentencing. Sentenced December 21, 2011: 9 months to 5 years in state prison. First Deputy District Attorney Matthew Falk argued for a custodial sentence, asking that Marvelli be treated the same as a male teacher who had sex with a female student. The Lehigh County District Attorney noted the case could not have been prosecuted if the incident had occurred after the victim's 18th birthday.
