Former teacher at Bangor Area High School, Bangor Area School District, Northampton County, Pennsylvania. Taught under her maiden name Rachel Werner. Age 26 at time of offense.

Male victim, 17-year-old junior whom Farrell taught in 11th grade. The relationship developed through Facebook communication and text messages before becoming physical. Farrell repeatedly booked rooms at the Tannersville Ramada Inn for sex and also had sex with the victim in cars at secluded locations, including the Wal-Mart parking lot off Route 248 in Lower Nazareth Township. District Attorney John Morganelli stated he believed Farrell maintained sexual relationships with four boys at the school; only one (identified in court documents as G.R.) cooperated with the investigation.

The school district contacted the DA's office in December 2011 after Farrell admitted to police and school officials that she had sex with several 17-year-old students. Placed on administrative leave and then resigned in December 2011. Turned herself in January 31, 2012; arraigned on three counts of corruption of minors and released on $500 bail (10% of $5,000). Defense: Steven Mills; ADA Lara Kash Mammana prosecuted.

Convicted on one count of corruption of minors. Sentenced June 8, 2012 by Judge Emil Giordano to five to 23 months in Northampton County Prison and three years' probation; reported to jail the same day. Giordano stated society demanded a prison sentence despite acknowledging she was unlikely to re-offend and would never teach again.
