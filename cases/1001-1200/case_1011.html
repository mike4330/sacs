Former 9th-grade geography teacher at Spring High School, Spring ISD, Harris County, Texas. Age 23 at time of offense.

Victim: 16-year-old male student at Spring High School, who had been in defendant's class during the 2007–2008 school year. Offenses occurred during summer 2008 at defendant's residence, 2418 Daneswood Court, Spring, Harris County.

First incident (on or about July 1, 2008): Victim came to defendant's residence with a friend (a 15-year-old female classmate). After the friend left, they kissed, went to defendant's bedroom, and defendant told him "there was something in the dresser." Victim found a condom, put it on, and they had vaginal intercourse. Second incident (on or about July 23, 2008): Victim returned; defendant told him to "suit up." Victim retrieved a condom from the dresser. They had vaginal intercourse. Victim spent the night.

Defendant admitted to Spring ISD PD Officer Randall Richards on September 24, 2008 that she had sexual intercourse with the victim on three separate occasions in July 2008 at her residence. Disclosure reached Spring ISD Asst. Superintendent of Human Resources through the victim's friend and the friend's mother.

Two sexual assault of a child 14–17 charges filed September 30, 2008 (cause nos. 118551801010, 118551901010; bond $30,000 each). Defense: Michael Fontaine Westbrook II (hired).

§ 21.12 complaint filed September 25, 2009. Defendant arrested September 28, 2009; entire case resolved the same day — waived felony indictment, pleaded guilty to §21.12, sentenced to 2 years TDCJ. Both SA charges dismissed simultaneously. Remanded directly to TDCJ; no bond posted.
