StateTexasCourt118th Judicial District Court, Howard County
Incident Date2022-06-01
Defendant Age23 Victim Age15
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former teacher and coach at Forsan High School, Forsan ISD, Howard County, Texas. Age 23 at arrest (November 2022).

An anonymous tip to Forsan ISD on November 14, 2022 prompted an internal investigation and immediate law enforcement notification. Phillips admitted to investigators that she and a 15-year-old female student had grown to love each other, that she had sent and received nude images, and that they had engaged in sexual intercourse multiple times — beginning over the summer and continuing into the school year. Phillips was arrested November 14, 2022 by the Howard County Sheriff's Office and terminated from the district. Grand jury indictment approximately May 2023.

Charged with improper relationship between educator and student (second-degree felony, Texas Penal Code; carries 2–20 years). Pleaded guilty to the same charge. Sentenced January 29, 2025: 7-year prison sentence fully suspended; 10 years community supervision (probation); 300 hours community service; sex offender education courses; teaching certificate surrendered. No sex offender registration required — improper relationship between educator and student is not a Chapter 62 registration offense in Texas. DA Josh Hamby cited a pre-sentence investigation in support of the suspended sentence.

NOTE: Female victim. No sex offender registration despite second-degree felony involving intercourse and explicit images — consistent with Texas Chapter 62 exclusion of this offense (see also Cockrell #493). Prosecution by DA Josh Hamby, 118th Judicial District.
