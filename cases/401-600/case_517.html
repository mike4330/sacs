Former teacher's aide at Mendota High School, Mendota, Fresno County, California. Firebaugh resident. Age 41 at time of offense.

Victim was a 16-year-old male student. Lopez met the boy near his Mendota home in summer 2019 (July or August) and initiated sexual contact; the victim ran away afterward. Lopez texted an apology the next day. The victim later dropped out of school due to trauma from the offense.

Mendota Police were alerted on January 29, 2020, by Firebaugh Police after Lopez was arrested on an unrelated domestic violence charge. During a police interview Lopez confessed to the relationship. Charged with two felony counts: oral copulation and sexual intercourse with a minor.

Sentenced September 1, 2022, by Judge Wayne Ellison: 180 days in jail, 2 years probation, sex offender registration, continued psychological treatment. Judge noted Lopez suffered her own sexual trauma at ages 14–15, but found she abused a position of trust. Prosecutor Kristin Maxwell noted Lopez made no mention of the victim in her courtroom statement, speaking only of her daughters.

NOTE: Police suspected at least one additional victim, possibly outside the Mendota area, but no second victim was confirmed. Confession obtained without forensic corroboration — noted by police as unusual given typical evidentiary challenges in these cases.
