Former student support aide at Mar Vista High School, Imperial Beach, San Diego County, California. Contracted through staffing agency Ro Health. Victim was a 17-year-old male student. Relationship spanned approximately one year and took place off campus. Defendant age 32 at time of offense.

Arrested May 21, 2025. Pled guilty September 11, 2025 to unlawful sexual intercourse with a minor and oral copulation with a minor. Sentenced December 11, 2025 by Judge Michael Popkins to 6 months work furlough plus probation and a 10-year protective order. No prison.

Defendant attempted to dissuade the victim from reporting and asked him to delete evidence. Allegedly also tried to persuade the victim to flee to Mexico to avoid prosecution.

NOTE: Work furlough = no-prison per convention. Concealment efforts (evidence deletion, flee attempt) are notable.
