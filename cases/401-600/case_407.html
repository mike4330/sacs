Kandace Marie Christopher #407 appeal

State Alabama
Court U.S. District Court, Northern District of Alabama (Huntsville)
Judge R. David Proctor
Incident Date 2014-10-01
Defendant Age 26
Prison Sentence 3.0 yrs

Former history teacher and JV softball and basketball coach at Scottsboro High School, Scottsboro, Alabama (Jackson County). Age 26 at time of offense.

Victim was a female student at Scottsboro High School; exact age not reported in sources. A three-month sexual relationship ended when the student ran away from home after being confronted by her parents about the relationship; she was found safe several hours later. That report led to Christopher's identification and arrest January 26, 2015.

State charges in Jackson County (DC 15-186 and DC 15-187): felony school employee engaging in a sex act with a student under 19 and misdemeanor school employee having sexual contact with a student under 19. Federal charge in the Northern District of Alabama: transportation with intent to engage in criminal sexual activity (reflecting the interstate or transport element of the offense). Pleaded guilty to the federal charge. Sentenced November 3, 2015 by Judge R. David Proctor to 36 months in the Bureau of Prisons, concurrent with the yet-to-be-imposed state sentences, followed by 180 months (15 years) supervised release. Released from federal custody September 2018.

February 11, 2026: Marshall County sheriff's deputies arrested Christopher (now 37, residing in Glencoe) for being an adult sex offender loitering in certain prohibited areas. Released the same day on $10,000 bond. On February 12, 2026, the federal government filed a petition to revoke her supervised release; she appeared before Magistrate Judge Herman N. Johnson Jr. on February 26, waived the preliminary hearing, and was released on a $5,000 unsecured bond. Final revocation hearing set for April 15, 2026 before Judge Harold D. Mooty III.

NOTE: Female victim. The federal transportation charge indicates she moved the student across a jurisdictional line as part of the offense or the running-away incident. Supervised release revocation proceedings are pending as of the most recent docket entry (March 2026). State case outcomes not available from sources.
