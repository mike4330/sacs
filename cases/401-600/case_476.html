Brittany von Stein #476 closed

Former choir director and music teacher at Basalt High School and Basalt Middle School, Basalt, Colorado (Roaring Fork School District). Taught there from 2015; named 2018 Outstanding Young Educator by the Colorado Music Educators Association. Age 25 at time of offense.

Engaged in an ongoing sexual relationship with a 17-year-old male student. The victim regularly drove to von Stein's apartment in Carbondale on Saturdays. Prosecution showed 19.68% of her calls over the relevant period were to the victim and she initiated most. Von Stein had appointed 2–3 boys as teacher aides across her tenure; prosecution alleged this was a grooming pattern. A second former student was identified who had a sexual relationship with von Stein after he graduated. Defense disputed grooming, claiming the student initiated contact and von Stein initially declined before an eventual relationship.

Arrested September 4, 2019. Pleaded guilty July 14, 2020 to one count of sexual assault on a child by one in a position of trust. Four counts of sexual assault on a child showing a pattern were dismissed. Sentenced September 16, 2020 by District Chief Judge James Boyd to 90 days Garfield County Jail (work-release and weekend release denied), 10 years probation (or longer per therapist recommendation), 500 hours community service, sex offender registration, and $3,235.11 restitution. Prosecution sought 12 years DOC; defense sought minimum 10-year probation. Judge described the sentencing as a close call.

NOTE: School county is Eagle County (Basalt); proceedings in Garfield County because offenses occurred at defendant's Carbondale apartment. 90 days continuous jail counted as 0.25 years per DB convention (work-release denied). Second victim identified by prosecution but no additional charges filed. Defense attorney argued no grooming; prosecution countered with call records and pattern of teacher-aide appointments. Von Stein expressed remorse and disclosed her own prior sexual trauma at ages 14 and 17.
