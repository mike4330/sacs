Former teacher at Don Jeter Elementary School, Alvin ISD, Brazoria County, Texas. Later employed at Leonetti Elementary School, Fort Bend ISD (Fort Bend County). Age approximately 31 at time of offense (2009); 45 at time of arrest (February 2023). Missouri City, Fort Bend County resident. Texas State University alumna; TEA-certified to teach early childhood through 4th grade since May 2003, also certified for ESL through 6th grade.

Victim was a student at Alvin ISD during the 2009 school year; age and sex not specified in sources. Offense date of record is May 1, 2009 (approximate per indictment). The case remained dormant until April 2022, when Alvin ISD received an anonymous tip about an inappropriate relationship involving Masi. Alvin ISD investigated, Masi resigned, and the district provided evidence to the Brazoria County DA and the Texas Education Agency.

Despite the active investigation, Masi was subsequently hired by Fort Bend ISD at Leonetti Elementary School. Fort Bend ISD stated it had checked TEA certificate records (no sanctions) and the Texas Do Not Hire registry (no listing) before hiring her. Fort Bend ISD also called a reference at Alvin ISD; an assistant principal gave a 'superb reference.' Masi was arrested on the Leonetti Elementary campus in February 2023 after being charged in Brazoria County.

Initially reported as charged with aggravated sexual assault of a child in Brazoria County (February 2023); the docket reflects a charge of improper relationship between educator and student (Texas PC 21.12, Second Degree Felony). Case filed January 19, 2023 in the 461st District Court. On January 12, 2024, Masi pleaded guilty; adjudication was deferred; sentenced to 6 years community supervision (CSCD) and 60 hours community service. Defense attorney Jeff W. Purvis; prosecutor Thomas J. Selleck (Brazoria County DA's office).

NOTE: Offense date approximately 13 years before reporting — delayed disclosure enabled by inter-district hiring failure.
