Former math teacher at Lincoln High School, Tacoma, Washington (Pierce County). Hired by Tacoma Public Schools in September 2012. Age 24 at time of offense.

Two male victims, both students at Lincoln High School, ages 15 and 15-16. The relationships began in January 2014 when Powell, while intoxicated, sent text messages to the students calling them 'hot.' Sex acts occurred inside her locked classroom; Powell also sent at least one victim a Snapchat photo of herself in the bathtub.

Investigation began after Tacoma police learned Powell had written a letter to one victim's girlfriend apologizing for 'promiscuous' and 'unprofessional' drunken text messages. After interviewing the victims — who confirmed sexual contact in her locked classroom — police re-interviewed Powell, who admitted to sex acts with both students. She resigned and surrendered her teaching certificate shortly after her February 2014 arrest.

Pleaded guilty July 2014 to two counts of third-degree rape of a child and one count of communicating with a minor for immoral purposes (all felonies); a fourth count of sexual misconduct of a minor was dismissed. Sentenced August 29, 2014 by Judge Frank Cuthbertson to a five-year sentence with all but six months suspended; ordered into a sex offender treatment program for first-time offenders. Families of the victims were upset by the short sentence; one stormed out of the courtroom. Tacoma Public Schools General Counsel Shannon McMinimee stated: 'Ms. Powell repeatedly talked about letting her students down. The reality is she committed child rape.'

NOTE: Six months served on a five-year suspended sentence for two counts of rape of a child. The investigation was triggered not by victims coming forward but by Powell's own apology letter to a victim's girlfriend. Judge called the conduct 'out of character' — defendant's remorse was explicitly cited as a factor in the lenient sentence.
