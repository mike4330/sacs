Andee Lantz #550 sentenced

StateOklahomaCourtCaddo County District Court
Incident Date2020-10-01
Defendant Age26 Victim Age16
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former special education teacher at Carnegie Public Schools, Carnegie, Oklahoma (Caddo County).

Victim was a 16-year-old male student. Relationship began at the homecoming game (fall 2020). Lantz admitted to riding around with the student after the game; she alleged the teen 'leaned in and kissed her, and it went from there.' They ended up at Lantz's home and had sex. She admitted to having sex with him on more than one occasion.

The relationship came to light when a nude photo of Lantz began circulating at the school, becoming 'the talk of the town.' When confronted by the superintendent about the photo, both Lantz and the student initially denied the relationship, but Lantz ultimately confessed. She resigned in November 2020.

Charged with second-degree rape (Caddo County case 2020-248). Guilty plea. Sentenced October 19, 2021.

Sentence: 10 years fully suspended. Fine $500 + $75 VCA.

NOTE: Defendant age not reported. Nude photo circulating among students triggered the investigation — an unusual discovery mechanism. Victim allegedly initiated the first kiss per defendant's account.
