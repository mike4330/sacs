Former teacher's aide at Licking Heights High School, Pataskala, Ohio (Licking County). Superintendent: Dr. Phillip Wagner.

Victim was a male student (age not reported). Between December 31, 2021 and January 12, 2022, Webb had sexual intercourse with the student four times — in the parking lot of Licking Heights Middle School and the parking lot of a Pataskala church. The relationship was documented through Instagram messages and phone texts described as romantic in nature. Before submitting her phone to investigators, Webb erased Instagram messages and cell phone messages between herself and the student; the detective nonetheless recovered text messages documenting the relationship.

Licking Heights High School reported concerns about a possible relationship between Webb and the student on January 12, 2022. A Pataskala police detective responded and interviewed Webb at the school; she admitted to the sexual encounters. The student was also interviewed and confirmed all four incidents and their locations. Webb was charged with sexual battery; terminated by the district after charges were filed that day. Superintendent Dr. Wagner wrote to district families: 'The District is grateful to those who brought this matter to official attention.'

Pleaded guilty to two counts of sexual battery (Ohio Rev. Code 2907.03, third-degree felonies) in Licking County Common Pleas Court. Sentenced November 28, 2022 by Judge Thomas Marcelain to three years in prison. Five years post-release control upon release. Tier III sex offender registration (lifetime, every 90 days). Credit for one day previously served.

NOTE: Victim age not reported. Evidence deletion before phone surrender, but messages recovered. Tier III is Ohio's highest sex offender tier — lifetime registration.
