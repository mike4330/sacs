Former English and special education teacher at Valley High School, Las Vegas, Nevada (Clark County School District). Age 25 at time of offense. Married.

The victim was a 16-year-old male student with a learning disability; school sources described him as having the IQ of a fifth grader. The two exchanged more than 13,000 text messages, many sexual in nature. The student hung out with LaFave almost every day after school and called her 'mom'; she called him 'son,' but in texts told him not to use that term when they were discussing sex. The student said he loved her and was upset when the investigation began. LaFave was caught in the act with the student by a hall monitor during school hours in the classroom. She admitted to police to kissing the student and touching his penis; denied intercourse.

Arrested early January 2016; charged June 28, 2016. Case tried in Department 32. On February 17, 2017, Judge Eric Johnson found guilty on all three counts. Sentenced to: Count 1 (sexual conduct) — 14–36 months Nevada DOC; Count 2 (sexual conduct, consecutive) — 24–60 months Nevada DOC suspended, probation up to 3 years, lifetime supervision, sex offender registration; Count 3 (open or gross lewdness) — 364 days CCDC concurrent with Count 1, 1 day credit. Minimum DOC time before parole eligibility: 14 months (Count 1 only active).

NOTE: Victim's intellectual disability makes this a particularly aggravated case. Caught in the act on campus by staff. Unusual mom/son dynamic used as cover relationship. Clark County DA Steven B. Wolfson (same as Glover #478). No connection to Debra LaFave (FL, 2004) despite shared surname.
