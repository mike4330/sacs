Former 7th-grade P.E. coach at Loranger school, Tangipahoa Parish, Louisiana.

Victim was a male student. Family states grooming began in 2020 when the victim was approximately 13–14. Freche gave birth to a baby fathered by the victim. The sheriff's office learned of the rape and the baby approximately two months after the child was born. Family attorney George R. Tucker noted the case came to light partly because 'it's a black baby' — the child's race made the relationship visible as a rumor before it was formally reported. Tucker stated that family members of superintendent Melissa Stilley had knowledge of the ongoing abuse, potentially implicating mandatory reporting violations.

Freche turned herself in October 3, 2023 and was charged with several felonies including third-degree rape (La. R.S. 14:43). Released on $155,000 bond with ankle monitor. Bill of Information filed January 10, 2024. Arraigned January 29, 2024; not guilty plea entered. Defense counsel: Garrison Jordan. ADA: Kaitlyn McMorris. Alternative Sentencing Solutions (Brian Easley) has appeared at all pretrial conferences since arraignment, indicating pretrial monitoring in effect.

As of February 2026, the state moved to amend the Bill of Information to felony carnal knowledge of a juvenile (La. R.S. 14:80) but withdrew the amendment to allow defense time to prepare supplemental motions. Defense has subpoenaed Principals Amber LeBlanc (Anthony school) and Amanda Gomez (Montalbano school) multiple times, and Superintendent Melissa Martin. A sealed 313 protective order was entered October 2023. Motion to Quash filed by defense July 2025. Next pretrial conference: May 5, 2026.
