Former part-time secretary and teacher's aide at Unionville High School, East Marlborough Township, Chester County, Pennsylvania. Age 43 at time of offense and sentencing. Resident of Birmingham, Pennsylvania. Married to Rocky Maufort; her daughter was a student at the school and was friends with one of the victims.

All conduct occurred on a single night, September 16, 2016. Maufort made contact with two male seniors she knew through her work at the school:

(1) At a post-football-game party held at a student's home, Maufort drove an 18-year-old student to a secluded spot away from the house, kissed him, and touched his groin through his pants. The youth returned to the party and told friends about the encounter, though he exaggerated the level of sexual contact.

(2) Later that same night, Maufort contacted a 17-year-old student (her daughter's friend, whom she had planned to hire to play music at a party) and drove him from his home to the parking lot of the Blue Pear Restaurant in Dilworthtown. There they drank beer and she performed oral sex on him. The episode ended when Maufort's husband, Rocky Maufort, drove to the scene after finding his wife was not at home; Rocky Maufort gave the youth a ride home.

Arrested November 2016 by Pennsylvania State Police Trooper Jason Pennington.

Tried by jury November 2017 (three-day trial). Jury of eight women and four men (seven of whom were current or former teachers or had relatives who were teachers) deliberated less than two hours. Convicted of: two counts institutional sexual assault (18 Pa.C.S. § 3124.2(a.2)(1), third-degree felony); one count unlawful contact with a minor (§ 6318(a)(1)); one count corruption of minors (§ 6301(a)(1)(i)). Acquitted of two counts of furnishing alcohol to minors. Remained free on bail pending sentencing.

Sentenced March 28, 2018: 11.5 to 23 months Chester County Prison (led away in handcuffs at sentencing); 3 years probation; 15-year sex offender registration; no unsupervised contact with minors except own children. Conviction affirmed on appeal January 28, 2021.

At sentencing Maufort denied criminal culpability, claiming her only mistake was 'being in the wrong place at the wrong time with the wrong people.' Judge Carmody said her denials led him to impose a longer sentence than originally planned, stating she lived in a 'fantasy world' and had 'Peter Pan syndrome.' He observed: 'The problem was, you were the wrong crowd.' Prosecution had requested 2 to 4 years state prison; defense sought probation.

Defense attributed the conduct to bipolar disorder, PTSD, drug and alcohol abuse. Defense memo noted the prosecution's pre-trial plea offer would have required only about 6 months of jail time.

NOTE: Paraprofessional, not a certified teacher. Both encounters occurred on a single night, not an ongoing relationship. One victim was 18 (adult) at the time. Husband directly witnessed the second incident. Alcohol present at both encounters.
