Former art teacher at Independence High School, Independence, Iowa (Buchanan County). At time of arrest, employed as a special education teacher at Peet Junior High School in Cedar Falls (Black Hawk County). Defendant age at time of offense not specified in sources.

Victim was a female student (identified as A.S. in court documents) attending Independence High School during the fall 2009 school year. Schwartz began a normal teacher-student relationship that escalated within the first month. She approached the victim's table more than other students. She visited other teachers' rooms specifically to talk to her. She repeatedly complimented her appearance and gave her "chest-to-chest" hugs. Schwartz texted "I am probably not suppose[d] to love my students, but I do you" and sent emails inviting her to confide. Schwartz attended events outside of school where the victim was present, including once at the victim's home.

The victim printed the emails and reported them to a teacher, who promised to forward them to the principal. Shortly after this report, Schwartz guided the victim to a stairwell, sat immediately behind her, wrapped one arm around her face and moved the other to her hip, then moved her hand above the victim's clothing to her chest and below her clothing toward her pubic area. The assault stopped only when two other students began walking up the stairs. During the school's investigation, Schwartz admitted to texting, emailing, and hugging the victim. She left her position shortly after. The victim did not report the stairwell incident at the time.

In 2018, the victim emailed the school district where Schwartz was then employed to warn them to investigate any complaints against her. In January 2020, after learning Schwartz was teaching middle school special education at a new school, the victim reported the sexual abuse to police, stating she felt partially responsible for not having reported sooner. Independence Police arrested Schwartz on July 3, 2020.

Trial held in Buchanan County. Jury returned a guilty verdict on November 10, 2021. Schwartz was heard sobbing after the verdict. Convicted of sexual exploitation by a school employee (Class D felony). Sentenced to 5 years in prison and a special 10-year sentence under Iowa supervised release. Iowa law bars deferred judgment or suspended sentence for school employees convicted of this offense, requiring actual incarceration.

Schwartz appealed to the Iowa Court of Appeals and then the Iowa Supreme Court, arguing the jury was incorrectly instructed that hugs constitute sexual conduct and that a school investigation report was wrongly excluded. On June 7, 2024, the Iowa Supreme Court upheld the conviction 4-3. The majority held the jury instruction on hugging, even if imperfect, was not prejudicial enough to overturn the verdict. Chief Justice Susan Christensen, joined by Justices Mansfield and Waterman, dissented, arguing the instructional error may have allowed the jury to convict based on non-sexual hugging without finding the stairwell touching beyond a reasonable doubt.

NOTE: The Iowa Legislature specifically extended the statute of limitations for delayed-disclosure sexual offenses. This case was prosecuted over a decade after the offense. Schwartz moved to a new school district (special education, middle school) after the 2009 incident. The victim's concern about younger vulnerable students was a motivating factor in her decision to finally report.
