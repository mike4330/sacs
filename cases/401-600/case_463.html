Kari Jean Schwartz #463 sentenced

StateIowaCourtBuchanan County District Court
Incident Date2009-09-01
Defendant Age26 Victim Age16
Prison Sentence5.0 yrs

Former art teacher at Independence High School, Independence, Iowa (Buchanan County). Was employed as a special education teacher at Peet Junior High School in Cedar Falls (Black Hawk County) at time of arrest. Defendant age not specified in sources.

One female victim (identified as A.S. in court documents), a student at Independence High School during the fall 2009 school year. Schwartz began a normal teacher-student relationship but within the first month began giving A.S. unusual attention: approaching A.S.'s table more than other students, visiting other teachers' rooms specifically to talk to A.S., repeatedly complimenting her appearance, giving "chest-to-chest" hugs, texting "I am probably not suppose[d] to love my students, but I do you," sending emails inviting A.S. to confide in her, and attending events outside of school where A.S. was present, including once at A.S.'s home. A.S. printed out the emails and reported them to a teacher, who promised to forward them to the principal. Shortly after this report, Schwartz guided A.S. to a stairwell, where A.S. sat on a step and Schwartz sat immediately behind her. Schwartz then wrapped one arm around A.S.'s face and the other toward her hip, moved her hand above A.S.'s clothing to her chest, then below A.S.'s clothing toward her pubic area. The assault stopped only when two other students began walking up the stairs. Schwartz admitted to texting, emailing, and hugging A.S. during the school's investigation and left her position shortly after. A.S. never reported the stairwell incident at the time.

In 2018, A.S. emailed the school district where Schwartz was then employed to warn them to investigate any complaints against her. In January 2020, after learning Schwartz was teaching middle school special education at a new school, A.S. reported the sexual abuse to police, stating she felt partially responsible for not having reported sooner. Independence Police arrested Schwartz on July 3, 2020.

Trial held in Buchanan County. Jury returned a guilty verdict on November 10, 2021; Schwartz was heard sobbing after the verdict. Convicted of Sexual Exploitation by a School Employee (Class D felony). Sentenced to 5 years in prison and a special 10-year sentence (Iowa Code supervised release). Iowa law bars deferred judgment or suspended sentence for school employees (mandatory reporters) convicted of this offense, requiring actual incarceration.

Schwartz appealed to the Iowa Court of Appeals and then the Iowa Supreme Court, arguing the jury was incorrectly instructed that hugs constitute sexual conduct and that a school investigation report was wrongly excluded. On June 7, 2024, the Iowa Supreme Court upheld the conviction 4-3. The majority held that even if the jury instruction on hugging was imperfect, it was not prejudicial enough to overturn the verdict. Chief Justice Susan Christensen, joined by Justices Mansfield and Waterman, dissented, arguing the instructional error may have allowed the jury to convict based on non-sexual hugging without finding the stairwell touching beyond a reasonable doubt.

NOTE: The Iowa Legislature specifically extended the statute of limitations for delayed-disclosure sexual offenses; this case was prosecuted over a decade after the offense. Schwartz moved to a new school district (special education, middle school) after the 2009 incident — A.S.'s concern about younger vulnerable students was a motivating factor in her decision to finally report.
