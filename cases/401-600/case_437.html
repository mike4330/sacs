Ethel Anderson #437 sentenced

StateFloridaCourtHillsborough County Circuit Court
JudgeChet Tharpe Incident Date2011-09-01
Defendant Age30 Victim Age12
Prison Sentence38.0 yrs

Former elementary school teacher at Mango Elementary School, Seffner, Hillsborough County, Florida (near Tampa). Had been the victim's first-grade teacher before the abuse began. Victim was a 12-year-old male student tutored by Anderson on weekends at her Riverview home. Defendant age approximately 29-30 at time of offense.

Abuse began four to five weeks into weekend tutoring sessions at her home, where she lived with her husband and children. Acts included kissing, touching, and oral sex. Anderson also sent more than 230 pages of sexually explicit text messages to the boy. She was recorded secretly admitting to the boy's mother that she had performed oral sex on him.

Named teacher of the year at her school in 2011. Arrested March 2012; suspended without pay; resigned later that year.

At trial (September 2013), Anderson claimed the explicit texts were a sexual therapy technique to motivate a troubled student. She denied the physical acts but admitted sending the messages, saying she was trying to gain the student's attention in an inappropriate way. Jury rejected her defense and convicted her on nine child sex-abuse charges.

Sentenced December 9, 2013 by Circuit Judge Chet Tharpe to 38 years in prison. Judge Tharpe explicitly addressed gender equity, citing Hillsborough County's history of lenient outcomes for female educators (Lafave, Ragusa) and stating the court does not recognize gender: if an adult has sex with a child, they can expect to go to prison. Victim's mother was present and stated she was satisfied with the sentence.

NOTE: One of the highest sentences in the DB. Cross-case context: Hillsborough County cluster — Lafave (no prison, 2005), Ragusa (10 years, 2010), Anderson (38 years, 2013). Judge's sentencing remarks directly address the gender disparity narrative. Black defendant — one of very few in the DB; relevant context for any sentencing disparity analysis. Recording of defendant admitting oral sex to victim's mother was key evidence.
