Gabriela Neufeld #453 sentenced

Also known as: Gabriela Cartaya-Neufeld

State North Carolina
Court Mecklenburg County Superior Court
Incident Date 2023-10-01
Defendant Age 26
Victim Age 18
Prison Sentence 1.42 yrs

Former biology and AP biology teacher (9th–12th grade) at South Mecklenburg High School, Charlotte, North Carolina (Mecklenburg County). DOB May 7, 1997; age 26 at time of offense. Originally from Boone, North Carolina; graduated Appalachian State University. Started with Charlotte-Mecklenburg Schools in 2020. Also known as Gabriela Cartaya-Neufeld.

One male victim, age 18, a student at South Mecklenburg High School. Sexual activity occurred on at least five occasions between October and November 2023 in multiple locations: inside the student's mother's home, Neufeld's home, a vehicle, and Park Road Park (south Charlotte). Campus rumors about the relationship had circulated for some time; school administrators had questioned both the student and Neufeld about it prior to the arrest but took no action.

Discovery: The student's mother grew suspicious over Thanksgiving when her son missed rugby practice. She tracked his location via the Life360 app, found him at Park Road Park, drove there, and found Neufeld and her son engaged in sexual activity in a car. She photographed the car and license plate and called police. Neufeld was arrested at the scene November 29, 2023. CMS suspended her with pay that day; she subsequently resigned.

Originally charged with five counts of felony sexual activity with a student by a teacher. Electronic monitoring ordered at arraignment due to prosecutor concerns about flight risk (fear she might run off with the student). Pleaded guilty November 14, 2024 to one count of crime against nature. Sentenced to a maximum of 17 months in prison and 36 months of probation. Required to surrender teaching license.

NOTE: Administrators were warned by campus rumors and directly questioned student and Neufeld before the arrest — no action was taken. Discovery was entirely due to the victim's mother using Life360 geolocation. Charge reduction from 5 felony counts to 1 count crime against nature. Victim was 18 and a legal adult; NC law prohibits teacher-student sexual contact regardless of age or consent.
