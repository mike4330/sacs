Former teacher at Murrieta Valley High School, Murrieta, Riverside County, California (Murrieta Valley Unified School District). Age 43–44 at time of offense.

A 16-year-old male student told authorities that Fosgett began showing interest in him approximately one year before her arrest. The relationship started with after-hours texting and escalated to oral sex and ultimately intercourse. Authorities were tipped off in late November 2015 about the relationship. Fosgett resigned from her teaching position on Wednesday, November 2015, and turned herself in to authorities on Friday, December 4, 2015. Investigations revealed she also had inappropriate contact with a second underage male student.

Originally charged with one count of unlawful sexual intercourse with a minor under 18 and two counts of oral copulation with a minor. On May 26, 2016, pleaded guilty to seven charges (five felonies) encompassing conduct with both students. Sentenced to two years in prison and required to register as a sex offender.

NOTE: Two male student victims. Victim's mother stated at sentencing that 'justice was not served completely.' Relationship progression from after-hours texting to physical contact is a documented grooming pattern.
