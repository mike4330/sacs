Former substitute teacher and head field hockey coach at Muhlenberg Junior High School, Muhlenberg School District, Berks County, Pennsylvania. Age 43 at onset of offense (2008). Resided in Reading.

One female victim, age 16 at start of abuse. Kleckner was a family friend of the victim. First incident occurred on a camping trip in 2008, where Kleckner supplied the teenager with alcohol before initiating inappropriate sexual contact. Abuse then continued on a regular basis at Kleckner's home through 2009.

Kleckner surrendered to authorities and was arraigned September 13, 2012 on corruption of minors and endangering welfare of a child. She admitted to detectives in a written statement that she had inappropriate sexual contact with the victim. Bail set at $50,000 unsecured. Muhlenberg School District superintendent suspended her upon arrest.

Pleaded guilty to one count of corruption of minors (M1). Sentenced January 8, 2013 by Judge Stephen B. Lieberman: 5 years probation. Defense attorney David R. Eshelman.
