Valynne Bowers #524 sentenced

State Utah
Court 2nd District Court
Judge John Morris
Incident Date 2008-12-01
Defendant Age 40
Victim Age 14
Prison Sentence 30.0 yrs

Former math teacher at Bountiful Junior High School, Bountiful, Davis County, Utah. Age 40 at time of offense. Same victim as case #523 (Linda Nef).

Victim was a 14-year-old male student who had previously been sexually molested as a younger child. In December 2008, the boy began attending Bowers' after-school math study sessions, obtained her cell phone number, and began texting and calling her. Communications turned sexual and progressed to phone sex. The two had intercourse in January 2009; beginning then, the boy came to Bowers' home for guitar lessons every Friday night, where they had sex through the end of February 2009 — approximately two months.

Bowers was reported to police in March 2009 when Linda Nef (case #523), another Bountiful Junior High teacher who had a separate prior sexual relationship with the same boy, confessed her own crimes to police and identified Bowers.

Originally charged with 5 counts rape and 3 counts forcible sodomy (all 1st degree felonies, up to life in prison), based on prosecutors' claim that Bowers occupied a position of special trust as a teacher. Defense disputed the position-of-special-trust element since she was the boy's former teacher and no sex occurred at school. A preliminary hearing proceeded in 2009; plea negotiations over the trust element broke down before resolution.

Pleaded guilty to 2 counts forcible sexual abuse (2nd degree felony, up to 15 years each). Sentenced April 4, 2011, by Judge John Morris to the maximum on each count, run consecutively — up to 30 years in prison. Prosecutor Rick Westmoreland; defense attorney Rich Gallegos.

NOTE: Judge Morris imposed consecutive maximum terms due to 'repeated conduct.' Defense requested probation and one year jail; had submitted evaluations showing low recidivism risk and letters of support from students and parents. 'Society needs to know it can trust teachers' — prosecutor. Charge reduction from rape/sodomy (1st degree, life) to forcible sexual abuse (2nd degree, 15 years).
