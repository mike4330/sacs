Former paraprofessional (educational professional) at Elcho School District, Elcho, Langlade County, Wisconsin. Age 31 at time of offense.

Victim was a child under 16 (victim's sex not specified in reporting). In February 2023, King and the victim had sex in a truck at the Otter Lake boat landing in Elcho. A witness told investigators that King had admitted the sexual contact occurred and described the Otter Lake location consistently with the victim's account.

Arrested late March 2023; initial appearance March 27, 2023, before Judge Brent DeBord, who set cash bond at $250,000. Conditions included sobriety, no contact with victim or victim's school, no contact with minors (except monitored phone contact with her own children), surrender of passport, no leaving the state. District Administrator Rachel Lemanczyk confirmed a criminal investigation and stated the district was cooperating fully.

Original charges: 2 counts 2nd-degree sexual assault of a child. Pleaded guilty in July 2024 to amended charges: 3rd-degree sexual assault and felony sexual assault of a student by school staff.

Sentenced October 8, 2024: 3 years prison + 4 years extended supervision (3rd-degree sexual assault); 2 years prison + 2 years supervision (felony sexual assault of student by school staff) — concurrent. Lifetime sex offender registration. Supervision conditions include sobriety and treatment.

NOTE: Victim age reported as 'under 16'; 15 used per DB convention for upper bound of the statutory range. Off-campus offense. Charge reduction from 2nd-degree to 3rd-degree sexual assault.
