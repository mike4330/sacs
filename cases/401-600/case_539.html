StateOklahomaCourtLogan County District Court

Former Algebra teacher at Guthrie High School, Guthrie, Oklahoma.

Victim was a 16-year-old male student. During the semester Kathrein routinely flirted with the student in class, complimented his looks, sat on his desk while helping him with work, and repeatedly offered him rides home. She spoke openly about her strained marriage and told the student she 'needed' him, asking if 'they could get away.' Over Christmas break (offense date 12/15/2021) she sent a message saying she was high followed by three explicit (nude or semi-nude) photos via Instagram. The student took screenshots and blocked her. Another student in class also noticed and was uncomfortable with the behavior.

School officials reported to Guthrie Police Department on January 14, 2022. Investigated by Sgt. Anthony Gibbs, GPD. Kathrein did not return to campus between report and arrest. Arrested and booked into Logan County Jail January 18, 2022.

Original charge: engaging in sexual communication with a minor by use of technology (21 O.S. 1040.13a, felony). Amended on plea to violation of Oklahoma Computer Crimes Act (21 O.S. 1953(A)(10)). Guilty plea 8/16/2024.

Sentence: 10 years DOC custody, fully suspended during good behavior. Fine $300 + $200 VCA + $960 DA prosecution fees + court costs. 250 hours community service within 2 years. DOC supervision 2 years. No contact with victim. Prohibited from holding teaching certificate or any teaching position.

NOTE: Charge reduction from sexual communication with minor to computer crimes act — significant downgrade. No prison, no sex offender registration mentioned in docket. Married at time of offense.
