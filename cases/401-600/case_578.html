Former special education teacher (life-skills class) at Danville High School, Danville, Illinois (Vermilion County). Started with the district in 2013; age 27 at time of offense; 30 at sentencing. Resident of 233 Poland Road, Danville. Had been reassigned in spring 2018 and was scheduled to move to North Ridge Middle School when the offense came to light.

Engaged in a sexual relationship with a 17-year-old male student from June 2018 to August 13, 2018. Confessed to Danville Police detectives that she and the victim engaged in sexual intercourse on at least three occasions at her Danville home.

Arrested August 2018. Indicted on three counts of criminal sexual assault by a person in a position of trust (Class 1 felony; 4–15 years each); bond 00,000. Pleaded not guilty; jury trial initially set for November 13, 2018. School board voted unanimously to terminate her employment September 12, 2018.

Pleaded guilty July 2020 to aggravated criminal sexual abuse (Class 2 felony). Three counts of criminal sexual assault dismissed as part of the plea.

Sentenced December 3, 2020: 48 months (4 years) specialized sex offender probation; lifetime sex offender registration. Prosecution had requested 5 years in the Illinois Department of Corrections. Judge Nancy S. Fahey imposed probation.

At the sentencing hearing, defense witnesses included Robinson's mother and pastor. Under cross-examination, it was revealed that Robinson to that day believed she had been sexually assaulted by the minor. She stated through questioning by her attorney that while she was 'taken advantage of,' she pleaded guilty because she 'was the adult in the situation.' The victim's mother delivered an impassioned impact statement.

NOTE: Robinson's claim at sentencing that she was sexually assaulted by the victim is a documented victim-blaming posture, made while simultaneously maintaining a guilty plea. No-prison outcome despite prosecution requesting 5 years and a Class 1 felony original charge. Lifetime sex offender registration is the most significant lasting consequence.
