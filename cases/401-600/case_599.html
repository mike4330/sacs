Former teacher at Southern Local High School, Salineville, Columbiana County, Ohio. Age approximately 28 at time of offense (29 at sentencing, May 2011).

Charged with unlawful sexual conduct with a 15-year-old student. Indicted by secret grand jury April 29, 2010; warrant served and arrested May 13–14, 2010. Bond set at $20,000 own recognizance. Arraigned May 19, 2010 and entered not-guilty plea. Two jury trial dates (December 14, 2010 and March 22, 2011) were cancelled.

On March 16, 2011, entered an Alford plea (did not admit guilt, acknowledged sufficient evidence for conviction) to the lesser included offense of sexual imposition (ORC 2907.06A4), a misdemeanor of the third degree. Sentenced May 13, 2011 to three years of regular probation and a maximum fine of $500. Designated a 'sexual predator' under Ohio's pre-AWA classification system. Community control successfully terminated May 13, 2014.

NOTE: Original felony charge (unlawful sexual conduct with a minor) reduced to misdemeanor sexual imposition at Alford plea. The official court docket designates Duncan as a 'sexual predator' (Ohio's pre-AWA highest classification tier, requiring quarterly lifetime reporting). Some secondary sources describe this as a 'Tier 1' designation — the docket is authoritative. sentence_years = 0 (probation only; no jail or prison). Incident date approximate (school year preceding April 2010 indictment).
