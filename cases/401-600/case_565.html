Cara L. Dickey #565 sentenced

Former 8th grade social studies teacher at South Buffalo Charter School, Buffalo, New York (Erie County). Age 30 at time of offense. Mother of three.

On June 16, 2008, Dickey picked up the 14-year-old male student, drove him to multiple locations, and supplied him with rum, NyQuil, and Tylenol. The two had entered a suicide pact. Dickey had also been sending the student inappropriate text messages. Both were found the next morning — the student at a Hamburg mall, Dickey asleep in her vehicle. Dickey was fired from South Buffalo Charter School.

Dickey surrendered to Buffalo police on July 10, 2008, charged with four counts including felony promoting a suicide attempt and unlawful imprisonment. Released on $5,000 bail.

Pleaded guilty to two counts of second-degree rape in March 2009. Sentenced April 2009 by State Supreme Court Justice John Michalski: 2–5 years in state prison; up to 8 years post-release supervision; sex offender designation; teaching certificate surrendered. Served approximately 3 years; released on parole February 2012 with ankle monitoring.

On January 9, 2013, Dickey cut off her ankle bracelet, one day after a parole officer questioned her about contact with a minor. A warrant was issued. A woman calling TV stations claiming to be Dickey said she had only been with her girlfriend's younger brother over Christmas and refused to turn herself in. She was located by the U.S. Marshals Service in mid-February 2013 at a motel in Elk Grove Village, Illinois (near O'Hare Airport), with her girlfriend Candice Brighton, 42, whom she had met in prison.

Pleaded guilty May 2, 2013 to failure to register as a sex offender (failure to notify within 10 days of address change). Sentenced July 25, 2013: 1 year, concurrent with parole violation — no additional prison time. Released August 2014.

On May 20, 2015, Dickey was arrested again for violating parole conditions (relationship with another person on parole; failure to comply with parole officer). Held at Erie County Holding Center.

NOTE: The suicide pact — rum, NyQuil, and Tylenol supplied to a 14-year-old student — is one of the most unusual behavioral profiles in this DB. Three parole violations across three years of post-release supervision demonstrate sustained non-compliance.
