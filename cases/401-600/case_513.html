Kari Jo Boll #513 closed

StateSouth DakotaCourtMinnehaha County Circuit Court

Defendant Age29Victim Age15
Prison Sentence20.0 yrs

Former special education teacher at West Central High School, Hartford, Minnehaha County, South Dakota. Employed at the school approximately three years prior to her arrest. Age 29 at time of offense (summer 2016).

The victim, a 15-year-old male special education student and one of Boll's own students, worked on Boll's Hartford farm during summer 2016. He disclosed romantic feelings for Boll; the relationship became sexual. Boll initially told investigators she waited until the victim turned 16 (South Dakota's age of consent) to engage in sexual contact. She later admitted intercourse occurred before his 16th birthday. The victim reported approximately 10 sexual encounters before his 16th birthday and 10–15 encounters after — more than 20 total, primarily during summer 2016.

Arrested August 18, 2016 by the Minnehaha County Sheriff's Office; resigned from West Central the same night. Grand jury indictment September 2, 2016 on 21 felony counts including multiple counts of fourth-degree rape, sexual contact with a child under 16, sexual exploitation of a minor, and solicitation of a minor. In spring 2017, while out on bond and in treatment, Boll violated bond conditions by contacting the victim.

Pleaded guilty September 12, 2017 to one count of fourth-degree rape and one count of sexual contact with a minor; 19 remaining counts dismissed. Plea agreement capped the maximum at 30 years. Sentenced February 12, 2018 by Judge Robin Houwman: 15 years on the rape count plus 5 years consecutive on the sexual contact count — 20 years total in the South Dakota State Penitentiary. Judge Houwman noted the victim was a special education student as an aggravating factor.

NOTE: Victim was Boll's own special education student. Farm access was the grooming vector — victim doing agricultural work at her property. Boll initially lied to investigators about timing of first contact. Bond violation by contacting victim during treatment. Victim's mother: he was 'robbed of reaching maturity at a normal rate.'
