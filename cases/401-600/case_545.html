Kristin McKee Brown #545 sentenced

StateTennesseeCourtKnox County Criminal Court
Incident Date2023-12-01
Defendant Age32 Victim Age12
Prison Sentence1.0 yr

Former teacher at Karns Middle School, Knox County, Tennessee. Had been with Knox County Schools since 2013, initially as a substitute teacher.

Victim was a 12-year-old male student. Between December 1, 2023 and March 5, 2024, Brown sent him multiple sexually explicit photos via Snapchat. The boy's mother discovered the communications and alerted school authorities. Brown resigned from Knox County Schools on March 18, 2024.

Indicted November 2024 by Knox County grand jury on five counts of exposing a minor under 13 to material containing sexual activity or simulated sexual activity (TCA 39-13-529). Pleaded guilty in April 2025 to several counts of sexual exploitation of a minor by electronic means. Formally sentenced and taken into custody May 19, 2025.

Victim's mother addressed the court at sentencing and released a statement: 'She went out of her way to groom him — this wasn't a mistake or a lapse in judgment, it was deliberate and manipulative.'

Sentence: 10 years; 1 year in jail (active), supervised probation through 2035.

NOTE: Victim age (12) and defendant age (32) make this among the more extreme age-differential cases in the DB. Snapchat platform; explicit photo-sending rather than physical contact charged.
