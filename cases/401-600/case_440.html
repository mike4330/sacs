Former 3rd-grade teacher at Solomon Elementary School and dance team sponsor at Solomon High School, Solomon, Dickinson County, Kansas. Resigned October 2012. Defendant age approximately 27 at time of offense.

Originally accused of sexual relations with two different male Solomon High School students, ages reported between 14 and 18. Originally charged with six counts of engaging in consensual sex with a student at least 16 years old. Arrested February 2013.

Pled guilty August 2013 to three felony counts of unlawful sexual relations (sex with a 17-year-old student, occurring at his home). Prosecutors dropped the remaining three counts in exchange. Sentenced November 13, 2013 in Dickinson County District Court to 15 weekends in jail plus 3 years probation. No state prison.

NOTE: Victim was 17 (legal consent age in Kansas) — charged under the teacher-student sexual relations prohibition. Weekend jail per convention = no-prison. Possible second victim (charges dropped in plea).
