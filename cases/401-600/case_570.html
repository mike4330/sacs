Anessa Paige Gower #570 sentenced

StateCaliforniaCourtContra Costa County Superior Court

Prison Sentence4.7 yrs

Former biology teacher at Making Waves Academy (charter school), 3045 Research Drive, Richmond, California (Contra Costa County). Age 35 at time of offense. Employed at the school approximately 3.5 months, October 2021 through February 2022.

Sexually abused seven male students ages 15–16 between October 2021 and February 2022. Conduct included sex acts, inappropriate touching, and sharing sexually graphic images over digital platforms. Students reported Gower to administrators, who alerted Richmond Police. Investigation opened February 18, 2022; number of known victims grew from two to seven as investigation expanded. Investigators executed search warrants to access electronic data exchanged between Gower and students.

Arrested April 6, 2022 at Sacramento International Airport upon returning from Hawaii with a companion. Filed on 29 counts April 8, 2022; bail set at $1.2 million. Original charges included lewd acts upon a child (PC 288(c)(1)), statutory rape (PC 261.5(c)/(d)), sodomy of person under 16/18 (PC 287(b)), meeting a minor for lewd purposes (PC 288.4(b)), contacting a minor for lewd purposes (PC 288.3(a)), distributing lewd matter to a minor (PC 288.2(a)), and child molesting (PC 647.6(a)(1)).

Pleaded guilty October 2, 2024 to two felony counts of unlawful sexual intercourse (PC 261.5(d) — victim under 16, and PC 261.5(c) — victim under 18) and four misdemeanor counts of child molesting (PC 647.6(a)(1)). Twenty-seven remaining counts dismissed.

Sentenced October 2, 2024: 4 years 8 months state prison (PC 1170). Credit of 1,812 days (Tier 1) applied — consisting of approximately 906 days actual pretrial custody × 2 for conduct credits (PC 4019). Credit exceeded the sentence length of approximately 1,700 days; Gower was released at sentencing, having served the full sentence in pretrial county jail detention.

NOTE: Sentence_years set to 4.7 (the formally imposed state prison term), acknowledging that actual future incarceration at sentencing was zero due to excess credit. Gower served approximately 30 months in county jail pretrial. Making Waves Academy had a prior teacher abuse case: Ronald Guinto (math/science teacher, middle school) convicted 2017 of molesting 15 students and sentenced to life.
