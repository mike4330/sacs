Former health and sex education teacher at North Central Charter Essential School, Fitchburg, Massachusetts. Age 25–26 at time of offense (incidents occurred over approximately two years, 2011–2013).

One male victim, age 14. Engaged in a sexual relationship with the student. Evidence included text messages and photos of Gendron in various states of undress recovered from the victim's and her own cell phones.

Indicted August 23, 2013 on five counts of aggravated rape of a child (aggravated by age difference) and one count of enticing a child under 16. Pleaded not guilty at arraignment; bail set at $2,000. Aggravated rape of a child carries a mandatory minimum of 10 years in Massachusetts. Plea entered March 13, 2015: guilty to enticing a child under 16. Sentenced to 2.5 years in prison with 10 years of probation to follow. Victim and his parents signed off on the plea agreement. Prosecuted by Worcester County DA.

NOTE: Significant charge reduction — original charges carried 10-year mandatory minimum; resolved to 2.5 years. She graduated with honors from Wheaton College in 2004.
