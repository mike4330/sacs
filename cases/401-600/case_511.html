Holly A. Smith #511 sentenced

Also known as: Holly Anne Smith

Former Spanish teacher at Hamburg High School, Hamburg School District, Berks County, Pennsylvania. Age approximately 46 at onset of offense (February 2017); 48 at sentencing.

Developed an intimate relationship with a 15-year-old male student she was tutoring, who came to her home several times for lessons. The relationship became sexual; intercourse occurred at her home on at least one occasion. The relationship spanned February 1, 2017 through September 30, 2018, when the victim was 15 and 16. An investigation was triggered by a tip to the Berks County DA's Child Abuse Unit on September 13, 2018. The teen's parents discovered intimate messages on his phone and arranged a confrontation with Smith at a public park, where she apologized. Smith and the student continued to communicate by cellphone and social media even after the relationship was discovered.

Charged October 1, 2018; suspended without pay and later resigned. Pleaded guilty March 2019 to institutional sexual assault before Judge Paul Yatron; the original deal was expected to carry approximately six months in prison, with a no-contact order.

After the guilty plea, Smith resumed contact with the victim via WhatsApp using the alias 'Alexis,' communicating in Spanish. The messages included plans to meet secretly and professions of love. Two witnesses notified a Berks County detective on May 15, 2019. The teen had been sending messages over Hamburg High School's public Wi-Fi and borrowing a friend's phone; in April the victim's mother had identified 'Alexis' as Smith through a photo Smith sent. When investigators requested her phone, Smith turned over a different device. Re-arrested on new felony counts of unlawful contact with a minor, criminal use of a communication device, corruption of minors, and other offenses. Bail revoked; sent to prison.

The original plea was amended. Sentenced July 8, 2019 by Judge Paul M. Yatron to 364–729 days in Berks County Prison (sentence doubled from original deal), followed by five years of probation; credit for 35 days served. 25-year sex offender registration; no contact with victim or unrelated minors; barred from school property; must remain 250 feet from extracurricular activities (except those involving her own children).

NOTE: Post-plea contact using a foreign-language alias on a separate communication channel is a calculated concealment strategy. Turning over a different phone when the actual device was requested constitutes additional evidence tampering. Sentence doubling due to post-plea contact violations is unusual in this DB.

In May 2020, less than a week after her release from Berks County Jail on April 18, Smith was re-arrested for violating the protection from abuse order. She had sent Facebook Messenger messages to the victim (then 17) that were sexual in nature and asked to meet him. The victim replied telling her to leave him alone and reminding her of the restraining order; Smith continued messaging. She surrendered to Berks County DA detectives May 22, 2020 and was arraigned on a charge of indirect criminal contempt. This was her third documented instance of prohibited contact with the victim after being charged.
