Erin Sayar #575 sentenced

Former teacher at James Madison High School, Marine Park, Brooklyn, New York (Kings County). Age 36 at time of offense; 37 at sentencing. Married with a 3-year-old daughter at time of arrest; husband Jimmy Lathrop filed for divorce in January 2013.

Engaged in a sexual relationship with a 16-year-old male student and football player (Kevin Eng) from late 2011 to January 2012. Conducted oral sex and sexual intercourse with the student on eight occasions in her school office and in her SUV. Supplied the student with marijuana kept hidden in her school office filing cabinet. Exchanged 3,856 text messages with him over a 17-day period in 2011, averaging 227 messages per day, according to school investigators.

Turned herself in to Brooklyn's Special Victims Unit and charged June 2012 with eight counts of statutory rape, 16 counts of sexual abuse of a minor, and related charges; released on $10,000 bail.

Pleaded guilty June 2013 to statutory rape. Sentenced August 1, 2013 by Justice Elizabeth Foley: 10 years probation; revocation of teaching license; Level 1 sex offender designation (low risk of recidivism; not required to appear in public database, but must register with NY State Division of Criminal Justice Services for 20 years). Ordered to have no contact with the victim, including no electronic communication or third-party contact.

At sentencing Sayar appeared thin and frail; sources said she was suffering from cancer. Defense attorney James Culleton stated she accepted responsibility and wanted to move on.

NOTE: Drug supply (marijuana from filing cabinet) and the extraordinary volume of text messages (227/day over 17 days) are documented grooming factors. The case was mentioned alongside similar contemporaneous Brooklyn teacher cases by Mayor Bloomberg and Schools Chancellor Dennis Wolcott, who publicly criticized the difficulty of removing teachers involved in such conduct. No-prison outcome.
