Jessica Saienni #458 sentenced

State Pennsylvania
Court Berks County Court of Common Pleas
Judge Thomas G. Parisi
Incident Date 2012-06-01
Defendant Age 29
Victim Age 14
Prison Sentence 0.5 yrs

Former 7th-grade science teacher at Southwest Middle School, Reading School District, Berks County, Pennsylvania. Age 29 at time of offense (June 2012). Resided in Landenberg, Chester County; also had a home in Bethlehem, Northampton County.

One male victim, age 14, a student in her 7th-grade science class. Relationship was initiated via text message. Initial sexual encounter occurred near the Body Zone Sports and Wellness Complex on Papermill Road in Wyomissing (Spring Township); subsequent encounters took place at Saienni's home in Bethlehem. Saienni sent nude photographs to the victim's cell phone and instructed him not to tell anyone about the relationship. Offenses occurred June through August 2012.

Investigation began in January 2014 after a teacher overheard students discussing the relationship. The victim then confirmed the relationship to authorities. Northampton County Detectives searched the Bethlehem home January 29, 2014. Charges filed February 19, 2014; Saienni surrendered accompanied by her attorney on February 21, 2014. Originally charged with statutory sexual assault, involuntary deviate sexual intercourse, institutional sexual assault, corruption of minors, unlawful contact with a minor, open lewdness, and intimidation of a witness or victim (7 counts).

Pleaded guilty to one felony count of institutional sexual assault. Sentenced May 12, 2014 by Judge Thomas G. Parisi: 6–23 months county prison followed by 4 years probation; prohibited from entering school property or attending school events. Saienni resigned her position after the investigation began and surrendered her teaching license. Prosecuted by ADA Meg McCallum; defense attorney Dwight L. Danser.

NOTE: Same county (Berks) as Risell #456 and Kleckner #457 — three Berks County educator cases within a two-year window (2012–2014). ADA McCallum noted that institutional sexual assault was added to PA law in February 2012, making this one of its early applications. Sentence of 6–23 months county prison; sentence_years reflects 6-month minimum.
