Former math teacher at Bastrop High School, Bastrop, Texas. Age 23 at time of offense. First year of teaching; graduated Texas State University May 2018 with a bachelor's degree in mathematics.

One male victim, age 15. Single alleged incident of sexual intercourse on December 1, 2018. School administrators received an anonymous tip on August 19, 2019; incident was reported to TDFPS, Bastrop County Sheriff, and the State Board for Educator Certification. Pardy was fired November 1, 2019.

Indicted by Bastrop County grand jury; arrested July/August 2020. Case filed June 23, 2020 in 21st District Court. Pled guilty January 26, 2022 to Improper Relationship Between Educator/Student (Tex. Penal Code § 21.12), a second-degree felony. Disposition: deferred adjudication. Sentenced to 2 years CSCD (community supervision). Ordered to be evaluated by Dr. Matthew Ferrara within 60 days; no unsupervised contact with anyone under 17 except biological/family members.
