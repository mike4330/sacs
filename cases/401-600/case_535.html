Kalyn Thompson #535 sentenced

StateOklahomaCourtCreek County District Court
Incident Date2014-01-01
Victim Age18
Prison Sentence1.0 yr

Former English teacher at Kellyville High School, Kellyville, Creek County, Oklahoma. Glenpool resident. Resigned from her teaching position in spring 2014 after reports of the relationship surfaced.

Victim was a male student, stated to be 18 years old. Creek County detectives reported the relationship had been ongoing for months; during this period the student's grade in Thompson's English course improved from failing to 98%. Other students photographed Thompson with him off campus. The student's mother confirmed the relationship.

Charged May 9, 2014, with second-degree rape by Creek County prosecutors. Bonded out of Creek County Jail on $35,000.

Pleaded guilty January 7, 2015. Sentenced January 12, 2015: 6 years in prison with 5 years on probation (1 year active); $1,500 fine; sex offender registration.

NOTE: Grade improvement (F to 98%) is a documented grooming indicator. Mother confirmed the relationship. Victim age of 18 stated at time of charge; relationship may have begun while he was 16-17 given the 'months-long' duration extending back into the school year.
