Former English teacher at Kellyville High School, Kellyville, Creek County, Oklahoma (approximately 20 miles southwest of Tulsa). Age 25 at sentencing. Address in Rockwall, Texas at time of sentencing.

Victim: 18-year-old male junior at Kellyville High School. The relationship was ongoing for months. During this period the student's grade in Thompson's English course improved from failing to 98%. Text messages recovered from both phones included sexual content, references to drug use, and messages about the student's grade. Investigators tracked down a hotel room receipt booked in Thompson's name at a Tulsa hotel; the student reported they had sex twice — once at a lake and once at the hotel.

Kellyville High School Principal Danny Wood told investigators that in January 2014 Thompson had been warned not to contact the student outside of school after she was discovered having dinner with him. She resigned April 16, 2014 after reports of the relationship surfaced.

Charged May 9, 2014 with second-degree rape; bonded out of Creek County Jail on 5,000. Pleaded guilty January 7, 2015. Sentenced January 12, 2015 by Creek County District Court, Bristow: 1 year in state Corrections Department custody, with an additional 5 years suspended; sex offender registration required. Allowed to serve probation in Texas. Thompson's teaching license was revoked June 26, 2014.
