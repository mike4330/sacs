Former special education teacher and girls' sports coach at Page Middle School, San Antonio Independent School District (Bexar County). Age 29 at time of offense.

Victim was a 15-year-old female former student. Page Middle School serves 7th and 8th grade students. Rodriguez was arrested approximately February 18, 2018 after being found in a hotel room with the victim. She admitted to having a sexual relationship with the teen when questioned by authorities. Released on a $20,000 bond. SAISD stated it would report Rodriguez's arrest to the Texas State Board of Education.

Entered a nolo contendere plea May 3, 2018. Sentenced June 5, 2018 to a 5-year deferred adjudication term on one count of improper relationship between educator and student. On October 28, 2021, the charge was dismissed after successful completion of the deferred adjudication period. No formal conviction entered.
