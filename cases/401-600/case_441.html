Former paraprofessional at Lexington Trails Middle School, De Soto School District (USD 232), Johnson County, Kansas. The victim was a high school student (not at the middle school where Alexander worked). Defendant age 26 at time of offense.

Relationship occurred between July 25, 2023 and March 19, 2024. Originally charged August 2024 with three counts of unlawful sexual relations. District suspended Alexander upon learning of the allegations; she immediately resigned. Pled guilty September 11, 2025 to one count of unlawful sexual relations with a student 16 years of age or older; two counts dismissed as part of plea agreement.

Sentenced November 13, 2025 by Judge Jacquelyn Rokusek in Johnson County District Court to 34 months probation; court applied a dispositional departure placing her on 36 months probation. Sex offender registration required. No contact with victim. No prison.

NOTE: Third Kansas case in DB using the teacher-student unlawful sexual relations prohibition on a victim of legal consent age (cf. Young #438, Stroda #440). Paraprofessional role; victim was a high school student despite Alexander being employed at a middle school.
