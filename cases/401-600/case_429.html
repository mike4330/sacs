Former 6th-grade math teacher at Lexington Middle School, Lexington, Dawson County, Nebraska. Victim was a male student in Peterson's class during the 2005-06 school year; sexual relationship began November 2006 when the boy was 12 years old. Defendant age approximately 24 at time of offense.

After the school district superintendent confronted Peterson about allegations of an inappropriate relationship, she fled to Mexico with the boy (then 13) in October 2007. Arrested in Mexico approximately one week later after the boy called his mother.

Federal case: Pled guilty July 2008 to transporting a minor across state lines to have sex. Sentenced September 29, 2008 in U.S. District Court (D. Neb.) to 6 years federal prison with credit for time served; no parole eligibility. An avoided second federal charge would have carried a mandatory 10-year minimum.

State case: Pled guilty January 2009 to two counts of first-degree sexual assault of a minor. Sentenced April 23, 2009 in Dawson County District Court to 8-10 years state prison, to run concurrently with federal sentence. Credited for nearly a year already served; eligible for up to one year early release for good behavior. Sex offender registration required. Teaching license revoked by the state in 2009.

Victim was an undocumented immigrant; granted humanitarian parole by DHS after returning from Mexico, and subsequently applied for a special visa for crime victims. Victim's attorney stated the family did not wish Peterson to serve more than the federal sentence. Defense attorney publicly disputed the boy's age, claiming he was at least 16 and the aggressor.

NOTE: Prior-warning-ignored — superintendent confronted Peterson before she fled. Victim age 12 at start of relationship, 13 at flight to Mexico. Federal charge is the defining feature; state sentence (8-10 yrs concurrent) is the controlling term. Defense victim-blaming of a 12-year-old is notable.
