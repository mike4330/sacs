Former 4K (4-year-old child development) teacher at Boiling Springs Elementary School, Boiling Springs, Spartanburg County, South Carolina. Resigned effective September 20, 2010 (superintendent accepted resignation day of arrest). Age 42 at time of arrest.

Lindsay and co-offender Audrey Grabarkiewicz (former preschool teacher at Lake Bowen Baptist Church Daycare) hosted parties for at least 10 underage teenagers involving alcohol, marijuana, and sex. Offenses occurred off school or church property, beginning July 2010. A parent of one of the teens reported someone was providing alcohol to minors; a weeks-long investigation followed before the September 2010 arrests. Sheriff Wright indicated there could be more victims.

Victims were not Lindsay's or Grabarkiewicz's students; the teacher-student sex statute therefore did not apply, but the criminal sexual conduct statute (carrying a stiffer sentence) did. Grabarkiewicz was charged only with contributing to delinquency (sexual conduct charges were declined because her victims were of legal consent age in South Carolina); Lindsay faced one count of criminal sexual conduct with a minor aged 11–14 and nine counts of contributing to delinquency.

Lindsay remained in jail at $50,000 bond following arrest; received credit for 473 days served (~15.8 months).

Pleaded guilty to one count 2nd-degree criminal sexual conduct with a minor aged 11–14, and nine counts contributing to delinquency of a minor. Sentenced February 7, 2012: 10 years suspended to 40 months active; must serve 80% (approximately 32 months = 2.7 years); 5 years probation; sex offender registry; mental health counseling.

NOTE: Victim age based on statutory definition of the charge (11–14); actual victim age not specified in reporting — 11 used per DB convention for lower bound. Lindsay has sons; the co-defendant Grabarkiewicz was served with divorce papers while in jail the day after arrest.
