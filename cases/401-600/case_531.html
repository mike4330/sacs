Tera Johnson-Swartz #531 sentenced

StateColoradoCourtDouglas County District Court (23rd Judicial District)
Incident Date2024-12-18
Defendant Age44Victim Age16
Prison Sentence14.0 yrs

Former English teacher at STEM School Highlands Ranch, Douglas County, Colorado. Taught there 2022–February 14, 2025. Married with three children. Was a 2025 Colorado Teacher of the Year finalist. Age 44 at time of offense.

Victim was a 16-year-old male student. Johnson-Swartz taught the victim and the two began discussing music. She surreptitiously entered her number into his phone — ostensibly to share a playlist — and the two began texting frequently, accumulating over 2,000 texts. On January 11, 2025, she bought him cigarettes and kissed him. Texts (including ~2,400 subsequently deleted) referenced that she was married with three kids and that she smoked marijuana. In one meeting they discussed that their conduct was inappropriate and that they would need to 'wait until [he] was 18.' Subsequent meetings included additional sexual assaults.

On January 22, 2025, the student's parents discovered via phone tracking that the student had gone to a movie theater with Johnson-Swartz instead of a school sports event. Parents found the deleted texts. The school was notified of an investigation January 21, 2025; Johnson-Swartz was placed on paid administrative leave and denied campus access.

On February 18, 2025, Johnson-Swartz picked the student up from school without parental permission and drove around discussing the investigation. This conduct led to the kidnapping charge. Arrested February 20, 2025.

Original charges: kidnapping and contributing to delinquency of a minor; later added sexual assault on a child by one in a position of trust. Pleaded guilty in January 2026 to sexual exploitation of a child and one count of felony cybercrime. Sentenced March 19, 2026, by the 23rd Judicial District to 14 years in prison. DA George Brauchler.

NOTE: Teacher of the Year finalist — one of the more prominently credentialed defendants in the DB. The surreptitious phone number entry and deleted text cache are notable investigative elements. Continued contact after attorney retained and investigation began.
