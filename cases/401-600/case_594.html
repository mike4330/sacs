Former teacher's aide at Washington Court House City Schools, Washington Court House, Fayette County, Ohio. Age approximately 51 at time of offense (circa September 2021); age 54 at sentencing (September 2024). There was a familial relationship between Vernier and the victim's family.

Engaged in sexual conduct with a 13-year-old male student multiple times approximately two years before discovery, placing the offense circa 2021. Sexual activity occurred away from school. Vernier routinely messaged the student and came to his family's residence to attempt to explain the messages away as general involvement in his life.

On September 7, 2023, the victim's parents found text messages on his phone suggesting a sexual relationship with Vernier and reported to the Washington Police Department. The student told his parents the sexual conduct had occurred approximately two years prior and happened several times. Washington Court House City Schools officials were notified; Vernier was placed on administrative leave and later dismissed.

Indicted March 8, 2024. Arraigned April 1, 2024 on bond of $11,000. Pleaded guilty July 29, 2024 to one count of unlawful sexual conduct with a minor (F3). No agreed sentence — prosecutor argued for prison time; defense requested community control. Judge David Bender sentenced Vernier on September 23, 2024 to three years in prison at the Ohio Reformatory for Women. Designated a Tier II sex offender requiring registration every 180 days for 25 years.

NOTE: Approximately two-year delay between offense and discovery (parents found text messages). Familial connection to victim's family. Offense occurred away from school. No agreed sentence — outcome driven by court, not plea agreement.
