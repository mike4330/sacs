Erin Hamilton-Foley #521 sentenced

StateIllinoisCourtMadison County Circuit Court
Incident Date2023-05-22
Defendant Age50 Victim Age17
Prison Sentence4.0 yrs

Former assistant principal for 11th-grade students at Edwardsville High School, Edwardsville, Illinois. Also served as the victim's assigned mentor. Age 50 at time of arrest.

Victim was a 17-year-old male student. Edwardsville police received a tip on May 22, 2023, alleging Hamilton-Foley had a sexual relationship with a student under 18. Arrested May 23, 2023. Charging documents alleged two specific acts of sexual penetration (vaginal and oral sex). Hamilton-Foley posted $25,000 bail on May 25 and was released.

After her arrest she obtained a new phone. On June 12, 2023, her defense attorney brought the new phone to police, disclosing that the victim had sent a video to Hamilton-Foley via Instagram. Police determined the video constituted child pornography under Illinois law. The prosecution filed a motion to compel the passcode to the new phone; a no-contact order was reinforced with additional bail conditions. No replies from Hamilton-Foley to the victim's messages were confirmed.

Original charges: two counts criminal sexual assault (Class 1 felony). Pleaded guilty September 22, 2025, to one count felony aggravated criminal sexual abuse. Sentenced to four years in prison; sex offender registration and mandatory supervised release required post-sentence. Resigned effective July 21, 2023; resignation accepted by District 7 board July 24.

NOTE: Potential CSAM on defendant's phone — video sent by victim post-arrest classified as child pornography under IL law; no additional CSAM charges reported. Charge reduction from two Class 1 felony criminal sexual assault counts to one count of aggravated criminal sexual abuse. Post-arrest contact by victim via Instagram complicates the case record but no charges against victim. State's Attorney: Thomas Haine; ASA Alison Foley; defense: Jessica Koester.
