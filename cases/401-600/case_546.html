Whitney Blair Fetters #546 sentenced

StateOklahomaCourtOklahoma County District Court
JudgeRay Elliott Incident Date2014-12-01
Defendant Age28 Victim Age12
Prison Sentence0.375 yrs

Former Spanish teacher at Hefner Middle School, Putnam City Schools, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma.

Victim was a male student. Texting began when he was 12 and enrolled in Fetters' class. By the time he moved on to high school (age 14) the messages had become sexual. The victim sent the first explicit message — telling Fetters he thought she was 'hot' and sending a partial nude photo of himself — but Fetters reciprocated. The two exchanged at least 20 nude photos and videos of themselves performing sex acts, plus Snapchats. Fetters also invited the victim to spend the night and engage in sexual intercourse, but authorities were notified before this occurred. The victim attempted to delete images from his phone, including through an app called 'Snap Save' (used to preserve Snapchat photos before auto-deletion), but investigators recovered everything. A search of Fetters' home produced her phone, computers, and undergarments matched to photos found on the victim's iPad.

The victim's parents filed the report January 31, 2015. Fetters had not been on campus since the end of January 2015. She turned herself in February 11, 2015 and was released the same day on $10,000 bail. ADA Suzanne Lavenue prosecuted; defense attorneys Mack Martin and Amber Martin.

Original charges: soliciting sexual conduct with a minor (21 O.S. 1040.13a), indecent exposure, lewd proposal to a child under 16, Computer Crimes Act violation, and marijuana possession. Negotiated guilty plea March 21, 2016. Count 1 amended to indecent or lewd acts with a minor (21 O.S. 1173(A)).

Sentence: Count 1: 20 years; Count 2 (marijuana): 1 year — concurrent, under DOC supervision, first 4.5 months in county jail flat time, day-for-day, no earned credits. Restitution $1,680. Fine $100 + $80 VCA + $150 DNA fee + court costs.

NOTE: Victim age set to 12 (age at which contact began). Sentence_years reflects the 4.5-month flat county jail term; the 20-year DOC figure uses 'supervision' language (not 'custody') suggesting the bulk is community supervision/probation rather than imprisonment. Same Judge Ray Elliott and ADA Suzanne Lavenue as case #540 (Lisa Kays, Putnam City High School, 2013). Third Putnam City Schools case in this DB alongside #539 (Kathrein) and #540 (Kays).
