Jeana Wesson #552 sentenced

StateTexasCourtDenton County District Court
Incident Date2017-05-01
Defendant Age44 Victim Age17
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former chemistry teacher and aquatics coach at Guyer High School, Denton Independent School District, Denton, Texas (Denton County).

Victim was a 17-year-old male student. Wesson had been his chemistry teacher during his sophomore year. Two years later, in 2017, they began exchanging sexually explicit text messages and photos/videos. In May 2017, Wesson waited until all other students had left her classroom, locked the door, and performed a sexual act on the student.

The victim disclosed the relationship to a mandatory reporter in December 2018. Denton Police executed a search warrant and interviewed Wesson, obtaining sufficient evidence for an arrest warrant. Wesson agreed to turn herself in and was arrested February 28, 2019. Placed on administrative leave by Denton ISD that day; resigned the following month. Surrendered her Texas teaching licenses to the State Board of Educator Certification to avoid formal disciplinary proceedings.

Charge: improper relationship between educator and student (Tex. Penal Code 21.12), second-degree felony. Guilty plea June 2021.

Sentence: 10 years deferred adjudication probation, $1,000 fine. No incarceration.

NOTE: Defendant age estimated as ~44 at time of offense (46 at arrest in 2019; offense was 2017). Deferred adjudication = no formal conviction if probation completed successfully. Disclosure to a mandatory reporter (not parents or school) triggered the report.
