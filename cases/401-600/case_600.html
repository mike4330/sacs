Heather Robinson #600 sentenced

StateKansasCourtAtchison County District Court
Incident Date2012-10-21
Defendant Age27 Victim Age17
Prison Sentence2.67 yrs

Former truancy reduction officer at Atchison High School (Atchison Unified School District 409), employed by Leavenworth County Juvenile Services under contract to the district. Troy, Kansas resident. Also known as Heather Duncan Robinson. Age approximately 27 at start of offense (29 at time of charges, July 2014).

Engaged in an unlawful sexual relationship with a 17-year-old male high school senior in a position-of-trust capacity. Although Kansas age of consent is 16, state law prohibits school employees and contract workers from sexual relations with students. The offenses occurred between October 21, 2012 and May 31, 2014 (per original complaint) in multiple locations: Robinson's office at Atchison High School, Warnock Lake, Atchison Watershed Dam No. 23, and her home. The Kansas Bureau of Investigation handled the investigation due to Robinson having a family member employed by the Atchison Police Department.

Arrested May 2014; placed on administrative leave and released on bond hours after arrest. Originally charged with 11 felony counts of unlawful sexual relations. Pleaded no contest to two counts of unlawful sexual relations; nine of eleven counts dismissed. Pre-sentencing hearing scheduled September 15, 2014.

Sentenced November 10, 2014. Motion for dispositional or durational departure denied. Sentenced to 32 months to the Secretary of Corrections on each count, concurrent. Post-release supervision period of 24 months. Court costs and DNA fee imposed.

Multiple offense locations including on-campus and defendant's home. Relationship covered plea window of October 2012 – May 2013 (the original complaint extended through May 2014). KBI conducted investigation.
