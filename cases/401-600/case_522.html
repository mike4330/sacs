Alyssia Reddy #522 sentenced

StatePennsylvaniaCourtBucks County Court of Common Pleas
JudgeRea B. Boylan Incident Date3/1/2017
Defendant Age28 Victim Age16
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former teacher at the Pennington School (private, Pennington, New Jersey), where the victim was a student. At the time of her arrest was employed as a teacher at St. Paul's School for Girls, Baltimore County, Maryland. Age 28 at time of arrest.

Victim was a 16-year-old student from the Pennington School. Reddy engaged in sexual intercourse with the victim at a park in Solebury Township, Bucks County, Pennsylvania. Solebury Township Police were notified of the assault on November 20, 2017.

Arrested December 1, 2017, by Baltimore County Police with assistance from Solebury Township PD, on a felony warrant charging institutional sexual assault, unlawful contact with a minor, criminal use of a communication facility, and corruption of a minor. Held in Baltimore City awaiting extradition to Pennsylvania. St. Paul's School for Girls terminated Reddy immediately upon her arrest, noting no prior knowledge of improper conduct.

Entered negotiated guilty plea January 7, 2019, to Count 3 (criminal use of a communication facility, F3, 18 Pa.C.S. § 7512) and Count 4 (corruption of minors, M1, 18 Pa.C.S. § 6301(a)(1)(i)). Sentenced to 5 years probation on each count, consecutive (10 years total probation). No prison. Conditions: no unsupervised contact with minors or victim (except family); supervision transferred to Maryland.

NOTE: No-prison outcome despite original felony institutional sexual assault charge, which was not among the counts of conviction. Both schools involved are private. Offense occurred off-campus (a park in Solebury Township). 'Mrs. Reddy' — married at time of offense.
