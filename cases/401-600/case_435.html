Former health teacher at Corning East High School, Corning, Steuben County, New York. Victim was a 16-year-old male student. Defendant age 40 at time of offense. Relationship occurred in February 2011 and included thousands of sexually explicit text messages over more than three weeks beginning February 1. Physical contact — kissing and groping at school on Valentine's Day (February 14) — was documented in Verizon records obtained by local press but was not charged. The victim's mother was tipped off to the relationship that same day.

Pled guilty April 2011 in Corning City Court to one count of first-degree endangering the welfare of a child (misdemeanor). Sentenced September 20, 2011 by Judge Robert Cole to 15 days Steuben County Jail (beginning September 28) plus 3 years probation. Jail term was unexpected — plea agreement had called for probation only. Cole said he was setting a standard and making an example of Decker's behavior. Pre-sentence investigations by both Steuben and Chemung counties found Decker minimized her conduct and both recommended incarceration. No sex offender registration required. Decker resigned her teaching position.

As a tenured teacher convicted of a misdemeanor rather than a sex offense, state law required a 3020-a hearing before termination. Estimated to take 520+ days and cost the district $128,000-$200,000; Decker remained on payroll during this period.

NOTE: Physical contact documented but not charged — plea to sexting only. Misdemeanor conviction. No sex offender registration. One of the most lenient outcomes in the DB relative to documented physical contact. New York tenure law created significant additional cost and delay for the district.
