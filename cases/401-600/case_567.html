Caroline Nicole Haney-Johnson #567 sentenced

Also known as: Nicole Johnson, Nikki Johnson, Nikki Haney-Johnson, Caroline Haney-Johnson

Former intervention specialist and track coach at Bloom-Vernon Local Schools / South Webster High School, Scioto County, Ohio. Age 37 at time of offenses; 36 at arrest. Also known as Nicole 'Nikki' Johnson. Wife of a local magistrate, which required a visiting judge (Scott Nusbaum) and a special prosecutor from the Ohio AG's Bureau of Criminal Investigation.

Investigation by Ohio AG BCI on request of Scioto County Prosecutor Shane Tieman. Case broke when law enforcement took the victim's phone and texted Haney-Johnson; she responded quickly to what she believed was the victim's number.

Victim: 17-year-old male student attending an area vocational program. Not her direct student or a student athlete connected with her coaching. Indicted December 1, 2023; arrested December 5, 2023 on three counts of sexual battery (third-degree felonies).

Pleaded guilty October 2024 to two counts of sexual battery; third count dismissed. Sentenced December 2024 by visiting Judge Scott Nusbaum: two concurrent 1-year prison terms (effective 1 year); mandatory 5 years post-release control; sex offender registration. Special prosecutor: Kara Keating (Ohio AG's Office); defense attorney: Rick Faulkner. Victim's family requested prison time; defense requested community control. Reported to Scioto County Sheriff's Office for transport to prison January 9, 2025; expected release December 23, 2025.

Granted judicial release April 24, 2025, after approximately 3.5 months served. Conditions: sex offender counseling; register as sex offender with Scioto County Sheriff's Office; intensive supervised probation for first 6 months of community control. Intervention specialist and student activity credentials permanently revoked by Ohio Department of Education, January 2025.

NOTE: Judge Nusbaum stated she was 'not the typical defendant' — single victim, no drugs or alcohol, not the victim's direct teacher or coach, highly educated. The visiting judge and AG special prosecutor were required due to her marriage to a local magistrate — a conflict-of-interest recusal pattern. Judicial release was anticipated: Nusbaum had flagged at sentencing that he would consider it. Sentence_years (1) reflects the imposed concurrent term; she served approximately 3.5 months.
