Erin Kathleen Queen #541 sentenced

StateOklahomaCourtTulsa County District Court
Incident Date2012-03-17
Defendant Age27Victim Age17
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former English teacher at Charles Page High School, Sand Springs, Oklahoma.

Victim was a 17-year-old male student in Queen's 5th-hour English class. On the night of March 17, 2012 the victim told his parents he was staying at a friend's house. His mother called and he admitted he was at a Candlewood Suites motel room with Queen, whom he still referred to as 'Ms. Queen' and 'my 5th hour English teacher.' Parents called police. Officers caught Queen with the student in the hotel room at approximately 5:30 a.m. Queen had also provided the teen with vodka.

Initially booked on a complaint of first-degree rape. Formally charged with second-degree rape (felony) and contributing to the delinquency of a minor (misdemeanor, related to the alcohol). That case was subsequently dismissed and refiled. Queen resigned from Charles Page High School shortly after the arrest.

Pleaded guilty March 21, 2014 to a single felony count of contributing to the delinquency of a minor. The second-degree rape charge was dismissed as part of the plea. The Sand Springs DA's office stated: 'We believe a felony conviction holds this teacher accountable under the circumstances of this case.'

Sentence: 3 years fully suspended (no prison). Fine $600. Teaching privileges surrendered.

NOTE: Victim's parents publicly criticized the outcome as a 'slap on the wrist.' Mother Shasta Belty stated the prosecutor led the family to believe they were agreeable to a conviction as long as Queen could never teach again, but the family expected the rape charge to stick. The Oklahoma statute makes sex with a student aged 16–18 rape when the perpetrator is a school system employee regardless of the general age of consent (16). Charge reduction from second-degree rape to contributing to delinquency of minor is a significant downgrade.
