Former first-year culinary arts teacher and track and volleyball coach at New Caney High School, New Caney ISD, Montgomery County, Texas. Age 35 at time of offense (2022–2023 school year). Teaching certificate issued June 2022; placed under TEA review following arrest.

One female victim, age 17, a student at New Caney High School. Sexual relationship occurred at the school. Misconduct allegations were reported and the district placed Cummings on administrative leave before allowing her to resign. New Caney ISD Police issued an arrest warrant on January 13, 2023; Cummings was arrested the same day.

Convicted of improper relationship between educator and student (Texas PC 21.12, Second Degree Felony, punishable by 2–20 years). Sentenced February 18, 2025 in Montgomery County District Court: 4 years in Texas Department of Criminal Justice.

NOTE: The sentencing article noted that two other female Texas educators recently received probated sentences for the same offense, prompting public commentary about gender disparity in sentencing.
