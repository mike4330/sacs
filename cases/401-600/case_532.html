Former teacher at Colorado West Christian School, Montrose, Colorado (private Christian school). Age 44 at time of arrest. Married with one child at time of offense; her marriage ended as a consequence of the case.

Victim was a male minor student at the school. In late December 2024, the student's father alerted police to 'ongoing, inappropriate' communications between Cianflone Thyre and his son. She was arrested on a warrant January 3, 2025. The school fired her promptly. Evidence included text messages and a taped recording (affidavit sealed). After the investigation began, Cianflone Thyre sent additional 'salacious' texts to a deputy, apparently believing she was communicating with the student.

Original charges: unlawful electronic sexual communications (persuade to meet), unlawful sexual communications (expose or touch), conspiracy to tamper with physical evidence, and tampering with physical evidence — all felonies. Pleaded guilty to attempted unlawful electronic sexual communication by a person in a position of trust; all other charges dismissed. Sentenced November 19, 2025, by Judge Keri Yoder: 2 years supervised probation, sex offender registration, offense-specific treatment, no contact with victim or anyone under 18 (except own child), over $2,300 in fines and costs. No prison.

Pre-sentence evaluation cited significant cognitive distortions: Cianflone Thyre viewed the case as entrapment, saw herself as 'rescuing' the student, and expressed more concern about her own losses (job, license, marriage) than harm to the victim. DDA Stacey Haase; defense attorney Brandon Luna.

NOTE: No-prison outcome. Attempted charge (not completed sexual offense). Judge noted 'jail would not have been inappropriate' but accepted the plea agreement. Evidence-tampering charges (conspiracy and tampering) dismissed via plea. Private Christian school context. victim_age = 18 (estimated — high school student (max 18); specific age not documented).
