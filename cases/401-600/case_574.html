Former English teacher at Campus Magnet Educational Complex (Campus Magnet High School), Queens, New York City. Age 33 at time of offense; 34 at sentencing. Resident of Bay Shore, Long Island.

In March 2011, drove a 16-year-old male student to the Capri Motel in Lynbrook, Nassau County, where she performed sex acts on him and filmed the encounter. The victim remained quiet until his mother discovered what had happened and contacted authorities. Nassau County Police recovered the video footage.

Arrested August 5, 2011; surrendered to police. Arraigned in Nassau County District Court in Hempstead on charges of third-degree rape and criminal sexual act (felonies); bail set at $5,000. Removed from classroom by New York City Board of Education in May 2011.

Pleaded guilty March 2012 to sexual misconduct (Class A misdemeanor, New York Penal Law § 130.20) — a significant downgrade from the original felony charges. Sentenced June 15, 2012: 6 years probation with sex offender conditions; registered as Level 1 sex offender; teaching license surrendered; required to relocate from her Bay Shore home due to its proximity to a school.
