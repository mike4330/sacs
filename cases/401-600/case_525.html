Melinda DeLuca #525 sentenced

StateUtahCourt3rd District Court
JudgeStephen RothIncident Date2005-10-01
Defendant Age29Victim Age16
Prison Sentence0.25 yrs

Former sign-language teacher at Copper Hills High School, West Jordan, Salt Lake County, Utah. Had taught there since 2001. Married, two daughters ages 3 and 5 at sentencing. Age 29 at time of offense.

Victim was a 16-year-old male student in her sign-language class. In October and November 2005, the two had sex at least twice — at DeLuca's home and in the boy's car at a park near the school. Witnesses also saw them kissing in the boy's car. DeLuca was fired in December 2005 following a school district investigation. Criminal charges filed February 2006.

In summer 2006, DeLuca claimed she had been beaten by two teenage boys as payback and was airlifted to the hospital. The story turned out to be fabricated, apparently to generate sympathy.

Pleaded guilty in October 2006 to one count of third-degree felony attempted forcible sexual abuse (reduced charge). Sentenced January 10, 2007, by Judge Stephen Roth: 90 days Salt Lake County jail, 3 years probation, $1,000 fine, sex offender treatment, reimbursement of victim's counseling costs. Sex offender registration ordered in written probation conditions (not stated in open court). Judge suspended the potential five years in prison.

Defense attorney Earl Xaiz noted DeLuca had been prescribed Topamax (an anti-seizure medication) following seizures after her second child's birth, and argued the drug could cause behavioral and cognitive changes. Judge Roth acknowledged it might partly explain her actions but said it did not excuse them. Prosecutor: Andrea Martinez.

NOTE: Fabricated the assault story to manipulate the proceedings — a striking deception. Victim was harassed by teachers and students and dropped out of school. Victim's mother ultimately expressed forgiveness after a personal meeting with DeLuca. Judge cited 'sincere remorse,' no prior record, and family forgiveness as reasons for leniency.
