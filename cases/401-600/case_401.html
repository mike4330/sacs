Former English teacher at Grant County High School, Walton, Kentucky (Grant County), employed during the 2016-17 and 2017-18 school years; also assisted with the dance team and cheerleading squad. Previously employed at Walton-Verona Independent School District. Age 24 at time of first offense. Verkamp was married at the time.

Victim was a 17-year-old male GCHS student. Snapchat communication began in summer 2017 prior to his senior year. The relationship progressed to sexual activity at Verkamp's Boone County apartment and also at the victim's Grant County home while his parents were present. Verkamp and the student reportedly researched Kentucky's age of consent laws together; Verkamp believed the relationship was legal because he was 16 or older. A GCHS guidance counselor spoke to Verkamp on at least two occasions about the student spending mornings alone with her in the classroom before school.

Investigation began in 2019 when Walton-Verona ISD reported Verkamp for sending sexually explicit texts and videos to an 18-year-old student at that school. That case was closed (victim was 18; no charges). During that investigation, Verkamp admitted to similar conduct with the 17-year-old GCHS student. Arrested July 9, 2020. Also separately indicted by a Grant County grand jury September 16, 2020 on third-degree rape, sodomy, and two counts of first-degree unlawful transaction with a minor for acts at the victim's Grant County home.

Pleaded guilty in Boone County Circuit Court (April 2021) to two counts of second-degree unlawful transaction with a minor; original charges of third-degree rape and sodomy were amended. Sentenced to five years probation on each count, concurrent. Conditions: no alcohol or drugs, drug testing, sexual offender evaluation and treatment compliance, no contact with victim, surrender all teaching licenses, no professional or volunteer work with children. The plea agreement was made with victim and investigating officer approval. Plea with the Grant County charges pending; hearing scheduled June 16, 2021 (outcome unknown from available sources).

NOTE: Rape and sodomy charges reduced to second-degree unlawful transaction in plea. A guidance counselor had prior knowledge of Verkamp's unusual access to the student. Sentencing date approximate (plea entered April 2021; article published May 26, 2021). Incident date approximate (summer 2017).
