Former special education aide at Seguin High School, Seguin ISD, Guadalupe County, Texas. Age 27 at arrest (January 2022). Had children at the time of the offense; grooming included presenting the victim as a prospective father figure for her children.

The Guadalupe County Sheriff's Office received a report of an improper relationship between an educator and student at Seguin High School on January 4, 2022. Investigation confirmed a sexual relationship with a 16-year-old male student. Nelson was arrested January 5, 2022 and booked into Guadalupe County Jail on bonds totaling $175,000. Principal Hector Esquivel placed Nelson on administrative leave; she resigned and was barred from returning to Seguin ISD. Grand jury indictment on March 3, 2022.

Pleaded guilty February 24, 2025 to one count each of sexual assault of a child, improper relationship between educator and student, and tampering with or fabricating physical evidence with intent to impair prosecution. Prosecutors offered a plea cap of eight years TDCJ. Sentenced May 19, 2025 by Judge Gary L. Steel to six years in prison; request for deferred adjudication denied. Credit granted for 605 days previously served; must serve at least half the sentence; prosecutor estimated parole eligibility approximately 16 months after sentencing.

A separate charge in Comal County involving the same victim was set for trial May 27, 2025 as of the date of sentencing.

Prosecutor Jonathan Fischer: 'She groomed this kid into believing he was going to be the new dad to her kids and was going to take care of the family. She made him fall in love with her.'
