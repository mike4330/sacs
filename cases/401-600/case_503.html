Former social studies teacher and cheerleading coach at Brighton Charter High School, Brighton, Adams County, Colorado. Age 29 at time of offense (October 2006).

During an overnight school camping trip to Estes Park, Larimer County, in October 2006, McCandless made sexual contact with and provided alcohol to a 17-year-old male student, Tommy Clay. Witnesses — other students on the trip — observed McCandless and Clay making out on the cabin floor. Court records noted 76 text messages exchanged in a single day between the two. Arrested November 14, 2006; first court appearance December 8, 2006, Larimer County Justice Center, Fort Collins.

Originally charged with two felonies: sexual assault on a child by a person in a position of trust, and contributing to the delinquency of a minor. Pleaded guilty April 24, 2007 to misdemeanor unlawful sexual contact; two additional charges — contributing to the delinquency of a minor (Class 4 felony) and tampering with physical evidence (Class 6 felony) — were accepted as deferred sentences. Sentenced June 8, 2007: 45 days in Larimer County Jail; 5 years intensive supervised sex offender probation; sex offender registration; sex offender treatment program.

First probation violation March 2008: found in bed at the apartment of a parolee; sentenced to 30 days jail, served beginning March 31, 2008. Second probation violation 2011: tested positive for morphine on a court-ordered drug test. The deferred felony sentence for contributing to the delinquency of a minor was revoked and converted to an actual felony conviction; sentenced to 60 days jail plus 60 days unsupervised probation.

NOTE: Jurisdiction attached in Larimer County (offense location, Estes Park) though school was in Adams County; court was Larimer County District Court. Victim Tommy Clay publicly discussed the relationship. Two separate probation violations ultimately resulted in a felony record despite the original plea to a misdemeanor. Last registered on sex offender registry in Georgia.
