Former teacher's aide, special education teacher, and marching band director at Riverside High School, Delanco Township, Burlington County, New Jersey. Age 27 at arrest (May 2023); approximately 25 at onset of offense.

Engaged in a sexual relationship with a male student beginning when he was a freshman (approximately age 14). The relationship involved multiple encounters over approximately two years, most of which took place at Jacoby's home. The relationship had ended before it was disclosed. The student revealed the relationship to another teacher, prompting investigation by the Burlington County Prosecutor's Office and the Delanco Township Police Department. Jacoby was suspended by Riverside superintendent Michael Adams pending investigation.

Arrested May 2, 2023; originally charged with aggravated sexual assault and endangering the welfare of a child. Pleaded guilty via accusation (waiving grand jury indictment) to one count of second-degree endangering welfare of a child by caretaker (N.J.S.A. 2C:24-4a(2)). Sentenced July 24, 2024 to three years in NJ state prison. No parole ineligibility period and no community supervision for life noted on docket.

NOTE: Charge reduction from aggravated sexual assault to endangering welfare (caretaker variant, 2C:24-4a(2)). The caretaker abuse/neglect variant of 2C:24-4 may not trigger Megan's Law registration, in contrast to the sexual conduct variant (2C:24-4a(1)); CSL/PSL field blank on docket. Incident date is approximate — relationship began when victim was a freshman, approximately two years before May 2023 arrest.
