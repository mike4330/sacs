Jessica Storer #588 sentenced

StateOhioCourtPutnam County Court of Common Pleas
JudgeRandall Basinger Incident Date2016-11-01
Defendant Age29 Victim Age18
Prison Sentence2.0 yrs

Former substitute teacher through the Putnam County Educational Service Center, Ottawa area, Ohio. Had only been on the substitute list since September 2016. Age 29 at sentencing (May 2017); approximately 28–29 at time of offense (November 2016). Married mother of two.

Invited an 18-year-old male student to her home, ostensibly to discuss a difficult breakup the teen was going through. Provided alcohol on multiple occasions when the teen was at her home. Had sex with the student once in November 2016. Victim later stated: 'Jessica Storer has really made the last six months a living hell for me. I hated it and I hated myself for doing it.'

Tip received by Pandora Police Department on December 12, 2016. Investigators contacted the victim, who confirmed sex had occurred and that he had been given alcohol. Storer was interviewed December 13 and admitted to the sexual encounter. Arrested December 21, 2016.

Pleaded guilty to sexual battery. Charged under the position-of-trust statute; Ohio's age of consent is 16 but criminal charges applied because Storer was in a position of trust as the student's teacher. Sentenced May 15, 2017 to two years in prison and lifetime sex offender registration. Removed from the substitute teacher list following the allegation.

At sentencing, Storer initially deflected responsibility ('he helped himself to alcohol already at my home'), then partially acknowledged fault only when pressed by the judge ('I accept full responsibility for him drinking and for not saying no'). Also stated the matter 'should be between my husband and I, my priest and God.' The victim's father told the court his son had attempted suicide twice since the incident. The victim's statement described harassment and online bullying from peers and adults following the incident.

NOTE: Adult victim (18). Position-of-trust statute applied despite victim being a legal adult. Storer's behavior at sentencing — repeatedly minimizing and deflecting — drew rebuke from the judge, who noted she was 'shifting responsibility to the teenager.'
