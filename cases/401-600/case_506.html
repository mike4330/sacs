Former fourth-grade teacher and girls JV and varsity softball and basketball coach at Aitkin Public Schools, Aitkin, Aitkin County, Minnesota. A hometown hire, she began teaching in fall 2015 after playing softball at the University of Minnesota-Morris. Age 24 at time of complaint (May 2016).

Developed a friendship with a 17-year-old female student after the girl sought her out for coaching advice. The relationship became intimate; they met at least six times, including twice at Hoge's home and several times at the local cemetery. In March 2016, the girl suggested Hoge spend the night at the family home while the parents were on vacation; though the mother told the daughter this was inappropriate, Hoge nonetheless spent three nights and slept in the girl's bed. The victim's mother was informed of the relationship by another coach who said 'some of the coaches had concerns.'

Criminal complaint filed May 4, 2016; Hoge was removed from her teaching position that week. Approximately 40 minutes after investigators contacted Hoge to come to the Sheriff's Office for questioning, a report came in of a suicide attempt: Hoge drove off the road attempting to roll her car, then used a filet knife to slash her throat. Taken to Riverwood Healthcare Center and placed on a 72-hour hold; subsequently taken to jail and released the next day.

Originally charged with four felony criminal sexual conduct charges. Pleaded guilty to fifth-degree criminal sexual conduct (gross misdemeanor). Sentenced approximately April 13, 2017: two years supervised probation; $1,095 restitution to victim; predatory offender registration; no contact with victim; subject to searches.

NOTE: Female victim. Prior warning from other coaches — 'some of the coaches had concerns' — not escalated before charges. Dramatic self-harm attempt immediately upon investigator contact. Gross misdemeanor plea from four felony charges is a substantial reduction. Incident date approximate; relationship developed between fall 2015 and May 2016 complaint.
