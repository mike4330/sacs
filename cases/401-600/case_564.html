Maggie Laughlin #564 sentenced

StateOhioCourtLake County Common Pleas Court
JudgePaul MitrovichIncident Date2008-05-01
Defendant Age23Victim Age15
Prison Sentence3.0 yrs

Former first-year math teacher at Shore Junior High School, Mentor, Ohio (Lake County). Age 23 at arrest; Willowick, Ohio resident. Had planned to resign and relocate to Michigan to be closer to her fiancé when the investigation began.

Conducted a sexual relationship with a 15-year-old male student (ninth grade) during the final weeks of the 2007–08 school year, beginning in May 2008. Laughlin and the student met in her classroom before school; she performed oral sex on him on eight occasions. She submitted her resignation on June 6, 2008.

The relationship came to light when the victim's ex-girlfriend's mother contacted a school official. Laughlin was arrested June 18, 2008 and arraigned on charges of sexual battery and improper sexual conduct with a minor. She admitted to conduct beyond what the victim had reported. Lake County Chief Assistant Prosecutor Karen Kowall later noted 'without her admission, this case would have been difficult to prosecute.'

Pleaded guilty to 8 counts of unlawful sexual conduct with a juvenile. Sentenced August 27, 2008 by Judge Paul Mitrovich: 3 years in prison. Teaching license revoked; Tier II sexual offender status — address registration every 6 months for 25 years.

Mitrovich denied Laughlin's first request for early release in November 2008 (approximately three months into sentence). Following Mitrovich's term expiration, successor Judge Joseph Gibson granted judicial release on September 9, 2009, after approximately 13 months served. Prosecutor Kowall agreed to release; defense attorney Richard Perez noted she was a model prisoner who completed every available treatment program. Conditions of release: sex offender counseling, 200 hours community service, no contact with victim or family.

NOTE: Eight counts reflect eight separate classroom incidents during the final school week(s). Maximum sentence would have been 18 months per count (up to 12 years total); 3 years was well below statutory maximum. Laughlin's self-incriminating admissions were essential to prosecution. Sentence_years reflects the 3-year imposed sentence; actual time served was approximately 13 months (judicial release, akin to early parole — not a suspended term).
