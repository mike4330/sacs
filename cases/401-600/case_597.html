Former 7th grade language arts teacher and track coach at West Union High School (West Union Junior and Senior High School), Ohio Valley School District, Adams County, Ohio. Age approximately 25–26 at time of offense (articles describe her as 26 at the time of the August 2018 plea); offense likely occurred during the 2017–18 school year.

Accused of an inappropriate sexual relationship with a 17-year-old male student. A school resource officer was alerted to the situation and worked with the prosecutor's office to secure an indictment. Welfley resigned from the district on April 20, 2018 after being confronted with the allegations. Superintendent Richard Seas made an immediate report to law enforcement and the Ohio Department of Education.

Indicted May 3, 2018 on two counts of sexual battery. Arraigned shortly after; bond set at $7,500 (10%). Withdrew her not-guilty plea and entered a guilty plea on August 23, 2018 in Adams County Common Pleas Court to one count of sexual battery, F3, ORC 2907.03(A)(7).

Sentenced October 22, 2018 to three years of community control. Additional conditions per judgment entry: 60 days county jail commencing November 21, 2018; 240 hours community service; $5,000 fine; sex offender counseling with successful completion; DNA testing. Designated a Tier III sex offender for life with 90-day in-person verification. Stated prison term of 48 months if community control is violated.
