Michelle McCutchan #537 sentenced

StateOklahomaCourtMcIntosh County District Court
Incident Date2011-01-01
Defendant Age38 Victim Age16
Prison Sentence15.0 yrs

Former 5th-grade teacher at Marshall Elementary School, Checotah, McIntosh County, Oklahoma. Described as a veteran of the school system. Age 38 at time of offense. Married.

Victim(s) were 16-year-old male high school students (sophomores at Checotah High School — not McCutchan's own students). By sentencing there were two identified victims. Initial investigation focused on one victim: approximately five sex acts between January and February 2011, at McCutchan's home and the teen's home. McCutchan also sent nude photos to the teen's cell phone.

Charged May 2011 with 2 counts second-degree rape and 1 count sodomy. Additional charges added by sentencing: 2 counts second-degree rape, 2 counts sodomy, 1 count child neglect, 3 counts providing alcohol to minors. Suspended with pay upon investigation.

Convicted on all counts. Sentenced September 5, 2012: 15 years (rape, concurrent); 10 years (sodomy, concurrent with rape); 15 years (child neglect, concurrent); 5 years (providing alcohol to minors, 3 counts, consecutive). Oklahoma's 85% rule applies to the rape and sodomy counts, requiring approximately 13 years served before parole eligibility. At sentencing McCutchan took the stand and attributed her conduct to childhood sexual abuse she suffered from age 12 to 15 at the hands of a family member. 'There's no one in this room that can hate me more than I hate myself.'

NOTE: Victims were not McCutchan's students — they attended Checotah High School while she taught elementary school. Multiple victims; alcohol and explicit photos as grooming tools. 85% rule minimum ~13 years; 5-year consecutive alcohol sentence runs after primary term.
