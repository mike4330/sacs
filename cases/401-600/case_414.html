Olivia Sondheim #414 sentenced

Former choir teacher at Lynnwood High School, Lynnwood (Snohomish County), Washington. Age 24 at time of offense; married at the time.

One male victim, age 16. Relationship began with messages about the choir Facebook page and escalated over time. First sexual contact April 9, 2016. Victim reported 5–10 additional encounters; locations included her home (victim recalled the couple's initials displayed on the master bedroom wall), her classroom, a field trip, and a local park. Sondheim ended the relationship at the close of the 2015–16 school year, telling the victim she wanted to focus on her marriage. She has since divorced.

A second male student was also investigated; no charges were filed because that student stated sexual contact did not begin until he was 18 and had graduated (summer 2016).

School officials learned of both students' allegations in fall 2016 and contacted the Snohomish County Sheriff's Office. Sondheim was arrested October 2016 but charges were not filed until March 2019. Tried by bench trial; found guilty September 2019 on three counts of first-degree sexual misconduct with a minor. Sentenced November 4, 2019 by Judge Linda Krese to one year in jail — the higher end of state sentencing guidelines. Post-release conditions: no contact with victim, no employment involving minors, sexual deviancy evaluation.

NOTE: Washington age of consent is 16 generally; the additional prohibition for teachers in positions of public trust is what created criminal liability here. Sondheim wrote a letter to the judge requesting leniency, acknowledging the harm: "My betrayal of him and his family is absolutely inexcusable." Nearly three years elapsed between arrest and formal charges.
