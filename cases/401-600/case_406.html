Toni Nicole Sutton #406 sentenced

State California
Court San Diego Superior Court
Judge Daniel Link
Incident Date 2015-06-01
Defendant Age 37
Victim Age 15
Prison Sentence 2.0 yrs

Former Spanish teacher and volleyball coach at Crawford High School, San Diego, California (San Diego County). Had been a teacher for 11 years, starting in West Virginia. Age 37 at time of offense.

Victim was a 15-year-old male student. A six-month sexual relationship developed beginning summer 2015. Encounters occurred in Sutton's classroom, her car, and her home. The victim's mother discovered graphic text messages between Sutton and her son and contacted police. Sutton was not married; she lived with a boyfriend near the school.

Arrested January 28, 2016. Charged with unlawful sexual intercourse with a minor and oral copulation with a minor. San Diego Unified School District placed Sutton on paid administrative leave; she did not return to campus. Pleaded guilty; sentenced July 29, 2016 by Judge Daniel Link to two years in prison. Lifetime sex offender registration required.

Defense attorney Kerry Armstrong stated Sutton had suffered physical, mental, and sexual abuse and had been in a toxic relationship with a former boyfriend; she had been diagnosed with major depressive disorder and had a 'great need to be liked and loved.' Judge Link told the weeping defendant: 'You desecrated the sanctity of the classroom.' Sutton expressed remorse. The victim's mother read a letter from her son at sentencing: 'My whole life has been turned upside down. She was my first... and she broke my heart. I just want my life back.'
