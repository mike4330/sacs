Former head varsity volleyball coach at Green Mountain Garwin (GMG) School District and paraeducator at South Tama Elementary, Toledo, Iowa (Tama County). Defense attorney: Scott Hunter, Public Defender, Marshalltown.

Victim was a 17-year-old male junior. Haughey met him in September 2020 when he backed into her car in the school parking lot; they exchanged numbers ostensibly for vehicle repair. When he told her he was 17 and a junior, she responded: 'close enough.' Text messages became flirtatious; the student 'just went with it cause he thought she was joking around.' From September to December 2020, they had sex several times. Haughey sent the student seductive photos including at least one nude. She also provided alcohol to the student on multiple occasions.

The school district began investigating in September 2021, months after the abuse ended, and alerted the Tama County Sheriff's Office. Authorities interviewed the student in December 2021. Arrested December 15, 2021.

Originally charged with 5 counts of sexual exploitation by a school employee and 1 count of dissemination of obscene material to a minor (up to 20 years exposure). Pleaded guilty November 10, 2022 to Count 6: sexual exploitation by a school employee, aggravated misdemeanor (Iowa Code) — the obscene material count. Counts 1–5 dismissed per plea agreement.

Sentence: 364 days jail, all but 90 days suspended; 2 years supervised probation; sex offender registration 10 years; $260 civil penalty + $25 SOR fee + $855 fine (fine subsequently suspended); victim pecuniary damages. No contact order through November 10, 2027.

NOTE: Defendant age estimated as 21 at time of offense (22 at arrest December 2021). 'Close enough' remark when learning victim was 17 is explicit evidence of predatory intent. Significant charge reduction: from 5 sexual exploitation counts to 1 aggravated misdemeanor obscene material count.
