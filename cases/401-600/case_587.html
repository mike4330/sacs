Former teacher's aide at Bentonville High School, Bentonville, Arkansas. Age approximately 31 at time of offense (reported as 33 at sentencing in August 2012; offense began late 2010 or early January 2011).

Engaged in sexual intercourse and other sexual conduct with a 17-year-old male special education student on multiple occasions. The relationship began just before the start of 2011. Flynn later admitted the conduct to police when interviewed.

Flynn's husband told his father about the relationship, and the father-in-law went to police in January 2011 to report it. Police interviewed Flynn, who admitted having sex with the student; the victim confirmed the conduct in a separate interview. Flynn was arrested January 30, 2011 and booked into Benton County Jail on $35,000 bond. She resigned from the Bentonville School District at the time of her arrest.

Originally charged with felony first degree sexual assault. Pleaded guilty August 15, 2012 to a lesser felony charge offered by prosecutors. Sentenced to 28 days of house arrest with ankle monitor, 4 years probation, and sex offender registration. Prosecutors had requested jail time; the court imposed house arrest instead.

NOTE: Special education victim. Family (husband via father-in-law) initiated the report. Charge reduced from first-degree sexual assault to lesser felony as part of plea. No-prison outcome despite repeated offenses and special-needs victim.
