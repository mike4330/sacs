Former English teacher at Lanphier High School, Springfield, Sangamon County, Illinois. Hired as an emergency hire two days before the start of the 2012-13 school year. Age 36 at time of offense (September 2012). A post-arrest review found Tyree lacked the credentials to teach high school English, resulting in the resignation of the Springfield School District's human resources director.

In September 2012, engaged in sexual contact with a 17-year-old male student, allegedly transporting him through multiple counties including Rock Island County. Lawsuits filed by two Springfield students alleged inappropriate relationships with both began early in the 2012-13 school year and continued until November 2012. Tyree's husband alerted authorities; she was arrested in November 2012. The Springfield School District settled a lawsuit brought by one student for $100,000 in September 2013; a second lawsuit by another student was ongoing as of 2015. Tyree was dismissed from the district in January 2013.

Charged in Sangamon County with two felony counts of criminal sexual assault. Separately charged in Rock Island County with two counts of criminal sexual assault for alleged contact with a student in Rock Island County between September 22–23, 2012 (Rock Island County charges pending as of May 2015). In Sangamon County, pleaded guilty May 2015 to one count of felony aggravated criminal sexual abuse; two sexual assault charges dropped as part of the plea deal. Sentenced to seven years in prison. Sangamon County State's Attorney John Milhiser stated the sentence was appropriate given the 'unique set of facts in the case.'
