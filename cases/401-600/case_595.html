Katie Fazekas #595 sentenced

StateOhioCourtSeneca County Court of Common Pleas
JudgeSteve Shuff Incident Date2013-06-01
Defendant Age31 Victim Age17
Prison Sentence1.42 yrs

Former health and physical education teacher at Carey High School (Carey, Wyandot County, Ohio) for nearly ten years; also girls' basketball coach at Tiffin Columbian High School for two years. Age approximately 31 at time of offense (summer 2013); 32 at sentencing. Tiffin, Ohio (Seneca County) resident. Note: prosecution was in Seneca County where offense occurred (at defendant's home), though the school is in Wyandot County.

Engaged in a sexual relationship with a 17-year-old female student at Carey High School during the summer of 2013 and continuing into the 2013–14 school year. Conduct occurred at Fazekas' home in Tiffin.

Allegations surfaced in March 2014. Fazekas resigned from both positions immediately upon being confronted with the allegation; the Carey school board accepted her resignation in April 2014. School officials investigated and reported to law enforcement immediately.

Pleaded guilty August 5, 2014 to one count of attempted sexual battery (F4), presented via a bill of information (bypassing grand jury). Sentenced August 6, 2014 to 17 months in prison. Surrendered her state teaching license. Classified as a lifetime sex offender. Defense counsel indicated intent to file for Ohio judicial release after the mandatory 30-day minimum; the prosecution agreed to remain silent on the question.

NOTE: County field set to Wyandot (school county); court and prosecution were in Seneca County (offense location). Charge was 'attempted' sexual battery via bill of information rather than direct indictment — common plea reduction mechanism in Ohio. Female victim. In September 2021, Tiffin Columbian High School's Hall of Fame committee drew public outrage by inviting Fazekas (as a member of the 1998 Girls Basketball Team being inducted) to school events, including a football game and induction banquet. After community backlash, students were uninvited; the team plaque was changed to omit a team photo or individual names.
