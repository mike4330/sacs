StateOhioCourtButler County Common Pleas Court
Incident Date2017-11-01
Defendant Age30
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former assistant varsity girls volleyball coach and JV head volleyball coach at Hamilton High School, Hamilton, Ohio (Butler County). Also employed as a teacher in the district. Age 30 at time of offense.

Victim was a female high school student; exact age not reported in sources. The relationship developed between approximately August and December 2017. The student's mother discovered the relationship and reported it to school administrators, who alerted Hamilton police. The specific sexual offense was alleged on November 1, 2017; police stated the criminal conduct occurred November 1-22, 2017, with a related contributing to delinquency charge covering October 1 through December 18.

Arraigned January 9, 2018 in Butler County Common Pleas Court on one count of third-degree felony sexual battery and one count of first-degree misdemeanor contributing to the delinquency of a minor. Pleaded not guilty; posted 10% of a $5,000 bond. Placed on unpaid administrative leave; the district initiated termination proceedings and reported the arrest to the Ohio Department of Education's Office of Professional Conduct for possible license revocation.

Pleaded guilty May 30, 2018 to gross sexual imposition; original sexual battery charge reduced in plea. Sentenced July 31, 2018 to five years probation, rehab, and community service; no jail time. Required to register as a sex offender.

NOTE: Charge reduced from third-degree felony sexual battery to gross sexual imposition. Female victim; relationship discovered by victim's mother. Victim age not reported in any located source.
