Former math teacher at Molasky Junior High School, Las Vegas, Nevada (Clark County School District). Also served as club soccer coach; had coached the victim since he was in 7th grade (~2014). DOB 12/9/1978; age 36 at first sexual contact. Victim was 14 when the sexual relationship began.

Wilfinger befriended the victim and his family — they were mutual babysitters of each other's 7-year-old daughters and she was a family friend. Contact escalated via phone and Snapchat; she sent him a 'BF rules' list via Snapchat. The two wore matching 'BFF' bracelets. She documented the relationship in a journal kept on her iPhone, recording a chronological log of milestones beginning April 17, 2015 (first time hanging out outside soccer) through the spring of 2016. Notable journal entries: May 22 — 'first time talking and friendship changes forever'; July 4 — 'first time I knew I was in love with him'; July 11 — 'first time we had sex'; August 10 — 'first time he kissed me in my classroom'; November 4 — 'first time having make-up sex'; March 23 — 'first time having sex in his bed' (Wilfinger later stated this occurred in her van in a Von's parking lot at Cheyenne and Jones). Contact included approximately 1,970 phone calls, 156 recovered text messages, 325 images, and 19 videos. She saved the victim as 'Best friend' in her phone.

Sexual contact occurred at her residence (first time 7/11/2015), in the victim's bedroom, in her minivan at a soccer field parking lot, and in the Von's parking lot. Oral sex occurred in the van sometime between January and March 2016. Victim confirmed three incidents of intercourse; Wilfinger acknowledged the same in her police interview. She initially denied sexual contact, then admitted it in a custodial interview on 4/29/2016.

Arrested April 29, 2016 by Clark County School District Police (Detective M. Caldwell; declarant G. Hibner P#243). Originally charged with statutory sexual seduction, sexual conduct with a student aged 14–15, sexual conduct with a student aged 16–17, and three counts of lewdness with a child. Pleaded guilty in August 2016 to statutory sexual seduction and sexual conduct between a school employee and pupil. Sentenced November 30, 2016 in Clark County District Court to 28 months to 6 years Nevada DOC (minimum 28 months before parole eligibility).

NOTE: Husband referenced in arrest report evidence ('don't call me because [husband] is on his way home'). Registered in Florida post-release as Nicole Suzanne Wright (also known as Nicole Mercurio, Nicole Wilfinger). Journal documentation is among the most detailed in this DB — defendant's own contemporaneous account of grooming and sexual milestones. Kidnapping was noted on arrest report as charge recommended for consideration (not filed). Victim first met defendant in 7th grade via soccer; transferred to her class mid-year 2015.
