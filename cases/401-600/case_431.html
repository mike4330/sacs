Meghan Baumann #431 sentenced

State Tennessee
Court Anderson County Criminal Court
Judge Don Elledge
Incident Date 2009-12-01
Defendant Age 27
Prison Sentence 3.0 yrs

Former social studies teacher at Clinton High School, Clinton, Anderson County, Tennessee. Began teaching at CHS in August 2008; had interned there in 2007-08 as a University of Tennessee student. Three male victims, all older than 13 but younger than 18. Defendant age approximately 27 at time of offense.

Offenses occurred December 2009 and January 2010: fondled one student outside his clothing (sexual battery); had sex with a second student (January 29, 2010; statutory rape); sent nude cell phone photos of her chest and pubic region to two students (displaying sexually explicit material). Suspended without pay February 4, 2010 after being confronted with allegations. Anderson County Schools Director Larry Foster notified her of intent to dismiss in mid-February, calling the conduct flagrant, egregious, and reprehensible. TBI and Clinton police investigated.

Pled guilty November 15, 2010 in Anderson County Criminal Court (Judge Don Elledge) to: one count statutory rape by an authority figure; one count sexual battery by an authority figure; two counts displaying sexually explicit material to a minor. Sentenced to 3 years prison for statutory rape (concurrent with two 11-month, 29-day terms for the display counts); 3 years probation consecutive for sexual battery. Must serve 30% of sentence before parole eligibility. Life sex offender registration; teaching certificate surrendered; $5,533 court costs.

June 30, 2015: Pled guilty to probation violation — had dated a man with a child under 18, and visited public ballparks where children were present four times in spring 2014. Probation extended by one year (to four years total).

NOTE: Multiple victims at same school across multiple offense types (intercourse, fondling, sexting). Defense attorney publicly argued comparable cases received probation or diversion with no registry requirement.
