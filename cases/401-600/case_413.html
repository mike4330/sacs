Multiple male victims. Off-campus sexual encounters in November 2019. Initially charged March 6, 2020 with one count of sex act with a student and one count of indecent liberties. Original charge of statutory rape of child ≤15 reduced to attempted statutory rape of a child (N.C.G.S. § 14-27.54(A)), Class B2 felony; also pled guilty to indecent liberties with a student (Class I felony). Sentenced to 120–204 months (10–17 years) active prison, mitigated range, with 85 days credit for time served. Required to register as sex offender, provide DNA, complete sex offender treatment and psychological counseling.
