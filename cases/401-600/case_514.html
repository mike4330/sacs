Former history teacher and volleyball coach at Burke Central High School, Lignite, Burke County, North Dakota. Employed at the school since 2016; also served as a substitute bus driver. Age 33 at time of offense (early 2021). At the time she resided in a home owned by the Burke Central School District on school grounds in Lignite.

In early 2021, had sexual contact with a male student (age 15–17 at time of offense). The student reported the encounter to authorities on December 2, 2021; North Dakota BCI responded to the school. Erdahl admitted the conduct to investigators. Charged December 6, 2021 with a Class C felony (corruption of a minor, victim over 15, defendant 22 or older). Placed on administrative leave December 1, 2021; resigned December 7; resignation accepted unanimously by school board December 8.

Pleaded guilty to the Class C felony charge. Sentenced July 25, 2022 by North Central District Court Judge Richard Hagar to two years in prison, all suspended except the approximately 80 days already served in jail (credit for 10 days good time; 1 day remaining at sentencing); five years supervised probation; sex offender registration; teaching license surrendered; no contact with victim; court costs and restitution. Erdahl: 'I would just like to apologize for my actions.' Prosecution by Burke County State's Attorney Amber Fiesel.

NOTE: Residence was school district-owned housing on school property. Victim age spans 15–17 per prosecution; youngest used. Incident date approximate — early months of 2021. Erdahl has since relocated to Flasher, ND.
