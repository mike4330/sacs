Megan Selfe #519 sentenced

StateNorth CarolinaCourtGranville County Superior Court
JudgeJohn M. DunlowIncident Date2016-01-01
Defendant Age23Victim Age10
Prison Sentence1.7 yrs

Former fifth-grade teacher at Creedmoor Elementary School, Creedmoor, Granville County, North Carolina. Taught there from January to July 2016, then resigned to take another position. At the time of her arrest she was working as a kindergarten teacher at Henderson Collegiate, a charter school in Henderson, NC. Age 23 at time of offense.

Victim was a male student, 10 or 11 years old during the January–July 2016 period when Selfe taught him in 5th grade. Law enforcement believed the sexual conduct occurred more than once. It is not established whether the relationship began while Selfe was still at Creedmoor Elementary or after her departure.

Arrested February 6, 2018, after turning herself in to the Granville County Sheriff. Original charges: felony statutory rape of a child ≤15 and felony indecent liberties with a child. Henderson Collegiate fired Selfe after police contacted the school; she subsequently moved to Pennsylvania. Bail set at $150,000 secured bond.

Entered an Alford guilty plea on February 24, 2020, to two counts of indecent liberties with a child (Class F felony, offense code 1118); statutory rape charge dismissed. Sentenced to aggravated-range active sentence of 20–33 months (minimum 20 months) on each count, concurrent; credit for 1 day time served. Sex offender registration and DNA collection ordered.

NOTE: Alford plea — defendant did not admit guilt but acknowledged prosecution could prove the charges. Charge reduction from statutory rape of child ≤15 to indecent liberties. Victim was approximately 10–11, one of the youngest in the DB.
