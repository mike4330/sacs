Former music teacher at Daniel Webster Middle School, Waukegan, District #60, Lake County, Illinois. Age 33 at arrest (October 2015).

On October 15, 2015, Waukegan Police began an investigation after a concerned parent reported inappropriate texting between a different student and Travis. During detective interviews, Travis revealed details of a separate sexual relationship with a 13-year-old student — not the student who prompted the original complaint. The original texting incident remained under separate investigation. Travis and the victim had allegedly begun their relationship at the end of the 2014-2015 school year; it continued through summer 2015 until her arrest on October 15, 2015. Appeared before Judge George Strickland on October 16; bond set at $1,000,000. Held in Lake County Jail from arrest through sentencing.

Initially charged with 3 counts of criminal sexual assault (Class 1 felony); charges later expanded to 13 counts of criminal sexual assault and 1 count of aggravated criminal sexual abuse, covering conduct between June 1 and October 15, 2015 with a victim between ages 13 and 17. Pleaded guilty September 28, 2016 before Judge James Booras to one count of criminal sexual assault; remaining 13 counts dismissed. Prosecution by Assistant State's Attorney Jason Grindel. Sentenced to 14 years in prison; must serve at least 85 percent before parole eligibility; minimum three years on parole after release; lifetime registration as a sexual predator.

NOTE: Two-student investigation — initial complaint involved a different student; Travis disclosed the separate victim during interrogation. Disposition of the original texting student's case unknown. Illinois 'sexual predator' designation (higher tier than sex offender) applies. 85% truth-in-sentencing provision means minimum approximately 11.9 years actual service.
