Former paraeducator at Beatrice Public Schools, Beatrice, Nebraska (Gage County). Age 28 at time of offense (2020–2021 school year). Resided in DeWitt, Nebraska. Dismissed from employment after investigators discovered inappropriate messaging with a student.

One male victim, age 14. Schaefer communicated with the student via social media; officers obtained screenshots showing enticing and questionable messages. The pair had kissed on several occasions and video chatted while in the shower. Schaefer stated her behavior did not extend beyond communications. Initially charged with enticement by electronic communication device (felony), negligent child abuse (felony), and contributing to the delinquency of a child (misdemeanor).

Pleaded guilty to negligent child abuse and contributing to the delinquency of a child; felony enticement charge dismissed. Sentenced November 3, 2021 by District Judge Rick Schreiner: 90 days Gage County Jail (work release potentially authorized; to report December 3, 2021), 4 years probation. Judge rejected a house arrest request, stating confinement was necessary 'to depreciate the seriousness of the crime.' Schaefer had no prior criminal history and had undergone counseling. Gage County Attorney Roger Harris; defense attorney Lee Timan (Gage County Public Defender).
