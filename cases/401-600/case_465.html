Loren Leoni #465 sentenced

Judge Joe McGraw
Incident Date 2007-12-01
Defendant Age 23

Prison Sentence 0.0 yrs

Former English teacher and freshman girls volleyball coach at Belvidere High School, Belvidere, Illinois (Boone County). Age 23 at time of offense (December 2007). Had taught at Belvidere for at least one year; left the district in January 2008 and did not return after Christmas break.

One male victim, age 15, a student at Belvidere High School. Described as a months-long relationship; the bill of indictment specified sexual conduct between December 1 and December 9, 2007, at Leoni's apartment in Rockford (Winnebago County). School officials reported inappropriate contact between a teacher and a student to Belvidere police in late December 2007; the case was transferred to Winnebago County in January 2008 because the incidents occurred in that jurisdiction.

Leoni surrendered to police in February 2008 and was arraigned February 28, 2008 in Winnebago County, pleading not guilty to criminal sexual assault and aggravated criminal sexual abuse. Defense attorney Michael Fleming of Elmhurst; moved to her parents' bed and breakfast in Cambridge, Wisconsin pending trial. Pleaded guilty to aggravated criminal sexual abuse (reduced charge) in March 2009.

Sentenced May 8, 2009 by Judge Joe McGraw: 48 months probation, mandatory sex offender counseling, lifetime sex offender registration; no prison. Prosecutor Kate Kurtz had requested a prison sentence. Judge McGraw cited a sex offender evaluation finding recidivism unlikely as a factor. Defense attorney Fleming noted the lifelong restrictions would prevent Leoni from dropping her own future children off at school or coaching youth sports.

NOTE: Offenses occurred in Winnebago County (Rockford apartment) though Leoni was employed in Boone County (Belvidere High School); county field set to Boone per school-county convention; court was Winnebago County. Same school district superintendent (Michael Houselog) and same defense attorney (Michael Fleming) as Blackert #464, though Blackert was five years later. Charge reduced from criminal sexual assault to aggravated criminal sexual abuse.
