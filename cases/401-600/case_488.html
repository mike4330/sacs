Former math teacher and after-school program director at Hill Gustat Middle School, Sebring, Highlands County, Florida. Had taught with Highlands County schools for 15 years; at Hill Gustat since 2012. Age 38 at arrest.

Engaged in sexual activity with a 13-year-old student who was enrolled in one of her classes. Shared inappropriate images of herself with the victim through Instagram and the Kik messaging app. Approximately one month before her arrest, Hooks was reprimanded by school administrators after being observed eating lunch with students and sharing a drink with the victim; her disciplinary record was otherwise spotless. On February 15, 2016, the victim's mother found the teacher's name and messages on her child's phone and contacted authorities; investigators found inappropriate texts from Hooks to the student and established probable cause within three days.

Arrested February 19, 2016. Charged with five counts of lewd battery, three counts of transmission of material harmful to a minor by electronic device, and one count of traveling to meet a minor to engage in unlawful sexual conduct; booked into Highlands County Jail on $625,000 bond. Placed on unpaid administrative leave. Sentenced to 20 years in state prison. Teaching certificate permanently revoked by the Florida Education Practices Commission in February 2019; barred from reapplying.

NOTE: 13-year-old victim — among the youngest in this DB. Prior reprimand for inappropriate closeness with the same victim constitutes an institutional warning ignored before escalation. Authorities raised concern about additional victims. Kik and Instagram used for explicit image sharing.
