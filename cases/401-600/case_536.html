Elizabeth Lenaburg #536 sentenced

Former history teacher and JV basketball coach at Yukon High School, Yukon, Canadian County, Oklahoma. Age 24 at time of offense.

Victim was a 15-year-old female student who played on the varsity basketball team. The two became close after meeting in fall 2018 when Lenaburg coached JV. A complaint about the relationship was made to the school district; Lenaburg resigned following the district's investigation. The two did not communicate for over a month after her resignation. The victim then called Lenaburg and left a voicemail expressing a desire to be in a relationship. Though Lenaburg was apprehensive, they began a relationship. In late June or early July 2019, they met at AirBnBs and a hotel to perform sex acts on each other. When police questioned Lenaburg, she confirmed the victim's account and acknowledged she had known at the time that she should not be doing it.

Investigators were first made aware of the relationship in August 2019 when a statutory rape report was filed. Charged October 2019 with 5 counts: 2 counts indecent/lewd acts with a child under 16 (21 O.S. 1123), 2 counts forcible oral sodomy (21 O.S. 888), and 1 count rape in the first degree by instrumentation (21 O.S. 1111-1116); all offenses dated June 30, 2019.

Appeared in custody with attorney Miller; ADA McMahan prosecuted. Pleaded guilty and sentenced November 15, 2019, by Judge Savage: 5 years DOC (active), followed by 10 years outside supervision; all counts concurrent. Fines: $100 + $225 VCA + $250 DA + $150 DNA + court costs.

July 2025: State filed application to revoke supervision. Lenaburg pleaded guilty to revocation on July 7, 2025, before Judge Green: revoked in part to 2 additional years in DOC (credit for time served); public defender S. Webb; ADA D. Gros.

NOTE: Female victim — same-sex offense. Lenaburg explicitly admitted to police she knew the conduct was wrong at the time. Original sentence appears to have been based on a single offense date (June 30) consolidating the entire encounter into one date. 2025 revocation suggests supervision violation approximately 5–6 years post-sentencing.
