Ann Marie Barrett #554 sentenced

StateVirginiaCourtLoudoun County Circuit Court
Incident Date2013-08-01
Defendant Age34 Victim Age16
Prison Sentence2.0 yrs

Former school counselor at Freedom High School, South Riding, Virginia (Loudoun County). Subsequently employed at Stone Hill Middle School, Ashburn. Defense attorney: William Mann.

Victim was a 16-year-old male student. Barrett began grooming him after his sophomore year, inviting him to a playground in Brambleton with her young child, framing it as 'wholesome' because he was 'hanging out with the wrong crowd.' The relationship progressed through dinner at her home, cuddling on the couch while watching television ('two or three times'), then kissing, then sexual activity. Barrett also issued the student a hall pass that could get him out of any class at any time while he remained her student. She repeatedly told him he needed more 'wholesomeness' in his life. The relationship was controlling: the victim later said he felt isolated from his mother, best friend, and peers. Barrett acted, in his words, 'like my mother and my lover all wrapped into one.' The relationship continued past his 18th birthday, through graduation, and into his freshman year of college, when Barrett ended it (June 2016).

In May 2021 — approximately 5 years later — the victim disclosed to a former Freedom High School teacher to prevent Barrett from harming others. That teacher reported to school administration. Barrett was placed on administrative leave May 19, 2021 and suspended without pay November 9, 2021. Arrested November 2021. Indicted by grand jury February 14, 2022. First guilty plea entered September 16, 2022 without a sentencing arrangement; the judge recused himself on the scheduled sentencing date (December 15, 2022), requiring Barrett to re-enter her plea. Re-entered guilty plea and sentenced January 6, 2023. Charge: taking indecent liberties with a child by a person in a custodial relationship (Virginia Code, Class 6 felony, max 5 years).

Sentence: 5 years, 3 suspended; 2 years active incarceration; 3 years probation.

Victim impact statement: 'You manipulated and convinced me that what we were doing was okay... You acted like my mother and my lover all wrapped into one... You no longer control me. I'm stronger than you thought. I survived you.'

NOTE: Relationship spanned 2013–2016 — over 3 years and crossing the victim's 18th birthday. Loudoun County Public Schools sought termination and license revocation after arrest. No prior criminal record. The defendant had been emailing other teachers to excuse the victim from class — creating an institutional paper trail that went unremarked at the time.
