Former math teacher at Mead Middle School, Wichita Public Schools (USD 259), Sedgwick County, Kansas. Employed 2015-2020; left on her own. At the time of arrest, was working as a math teacher at Edison Preparatory School, Tulsa, Oklahoma. Victim was a 13-year-old male student at Mead Middle School. Defendant age approximately 28 at time of offense.

Victim described Covel viewing their relationship as a lover relationship. She communicated with him on Snapchat; detectives retrieved nearly 10,000 text messages between the two. Incidents occurred between 2018 and 2019 but were not reported to police until December 2021.

Arrested by Tulsa police January 4, 2022 at her Tulsa school; transported to Wichita January 11. Charged January 12, 2022 with three counts of aggravated indecent liberties with a child (off-grid person felonies) and one count of unlawful sexual relations (severity level 5 felony). Bond set at $150,000.

Pled no contest February 9, 2023 to two counts of severity level 5 aggravated indecent solicitation of a child. Original two counts (off-grid aggravated indecent liberties + unlawful sexual relations) dismissed in plea agreement. Sentenced June 15, 2023 in Sedgwick County District Court: 34 months per count, consecutive — 68 months total (5 years 8 months). Lifetime post-release supervision; lifetime sex offender registration.

Covel argued for probation at sentencing, citing employment, active therapy participation, and a therapist evaluation recommending community-based outpatient treatment. Court declined, citing extensive contacts with the victim, the special relationship with a 13-year-old, and deterrence. Covel appealed the sentence; appeal was dismissed.

NOTE: Charge reduction from off-grid aggravated indecent liberties (potentially decades) to severity level 5 aggravated indecent solicitation. Nearly 10,000 Snapchat messages constitute one of the most extensive documented grooming communication records in the DB. Delayed reporting of approximately two to three years. Teaching at a new school in another state at time of arrest.
