Randi Lynn Zurenko #584 sentenced

StatePennsylvaniaCourtDauphin County Court of Common Pleas
JudgeRichard A. LewisIncident Date2013-01
Defendant Age29Victim Age16
Prison Sentence0.96 yrs

Former social studies teacher at Bishop McDevitt High School, Harrisburg, Pennsylvania (Dauphin County; Catholic school). Age approximately 29–30 at start of offense; 33 at arrest and sentencing. Married with five children; resident of Millerstown, Perry County.

Cultivated inappropriate relationships with two separate female students between January 2013 and October 14, 2016:

Victim #1: Student at Bishop McDevitt who graduated in 2013. Prior to graduation, Zurenko met the victim at Fort Hunter Park in Susquehanna Township, massaged her back, and undid her bra. Provided alcohol to the victim on multiple occasions both before and after graduation (victim was underage throughout). Victim interviewed by police October 17–18, 2016.

Victim #2: Current student at the time of arrest. Relationship began early 2015 and continued through October 14, 2016. Zurenko engaged in multiple sexual acts with the victim. Photographed her in various stages of undress and during sexual activity; also sent the victim nude selfie photos. Sexual encounters occurred at multiple locations: Swatara Township, Fort Hunter Park in Susquehanna Township, parking lots near Union Deposit and Rutherford Roads in Lower Paxton Township, Zurenko's home in Perry County, and Myrtle Beach, South Carolina. Also provided victim alcohol while a minor.

Investigators seized Zurenko's phone and electronic devices and found numerous nude photographs of Victim #2. During questioning October 18, 2016, Zurenko admitted to providing alcohol to both victims; admitted to a sexual relationship with Victim #2; and admitted to taking nude photographs of Victim #2.

School officials were advised of the investigation October 18, 2016 and immediately placed Zurenko on administrative leave.

Originally charged with 232 counts: institutional sexual assault (13), sexual abuse of children (153), unlawful contact with minors (33), dissemination of obscene materials to a minor (20), corruption of minors (13).

Pleaded guilty to institutional sexual assault, unlawful contact with minors, corruption of minors, possession of child pornography, and disseminating obscene materials to minors. Sentenced July 14, 2017 by President Judge Richard A. Lewis: 11.5 to 23 months in prison; 4 years probation; 500 hours community service; 25-year sex offender registration.

At sentencing, defense attorney said Zurenko was 'truly in love with her victims.' Zurenko told the judge: 'I am ashamed to be here. I have never coerced anyone. I have never taken advantage of anyone.' She asked for grace and mercy and cited her five children.

NOTE: Same-sex victims (two female students). CSAM (photographed both victims including during sex acts). Multi-state conduct (Pennsylvania + Myrtle Beach, SC). 232 original counts reduced to plea on fewer counts. Defense's 'truly in love' framing and Zurenko's denial of coercion contrast sharply with the factual record of CSAM and alcohol supply.
