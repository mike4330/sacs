Allanah Benton-Wells #432 sentenced

StateMichiganCourtGenesee County Circuit Court
JudgeGeoffrey Neithercut Incident Date2007-10-31
Defendant Age41 Victim Age12
Prison Sentence25.0 yrs

Former 6th-grade teacher at Williams Elementary School, Flint, Genesee County, Michigan. Had sex with a 12-year-old male student while supposedly tutoring him at her home around Halloween 2007. Victim was a troubled child who came to consider Benton-Wells his girlfriend. Defendant age approximately 41 at time of offense.

Placed on paid leave November 2007; fired August 2008. Arraigned August 19, 2008 on three counts of first-degree criminal sexual conduct. Convicted by jury December 2009 on two of three counts; acquitted on the third. Sentenced February 11, 2010 by Judge Geoffrey L. Neithercut to 25 to 38 years prison — the mandatory minimum under a 2008 Michigan law requiring 25-year minimums for first-degree CSC involving victims under 13. Judge noted the mandatory sentence constrained his discretion, saying it made him more like a clerk than a judge, but applied it nonetheless.

Benton-Wells maintained her innocence throughout trial and sentencing, expressing no remorse. Attorney Michael Cronkright announced intent to appeal and argued the mandatory minimum constituted cruel and unusual punishment under the Eighth Amendment.

Victim, age 14 at sentencing, had run away from home during the trial and remained missing at sentencing. Prosecutor attributed the disappearance to psychological damage caused by the relationship.

NOTE: Mandatory 25-year minimum was decisive — judge would have sentenced lower without it. Victim disappeared mid-trial, a rare occurrence. Victim-in-home (tutoring context). Benton-Wells had previously married a man who was a recent graduate of Beecher HS where she had substitute taught, though defense attorney said they met after his graduation at a shoe store where she also worked. One of very few Black defendants in the DB — relevant context for any sentencing disparity analysis.
