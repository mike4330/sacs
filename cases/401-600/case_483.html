StateCaliforniaCourtSanta Clara County Superior Court
Incident Date2007-09-01
Defendant Age23 Victim Age17
Prison Sentence0.5 yrs

Former first-year PE teacher and assistant soccer coach at Mount Pleasant High School, East San Jose, California (East Side Union High School District). At the time she was an intern earning her teaching credential at San Jose State University. Age ~23 at onset of offense.

Met the victim, a 17-year-old female student, early in the 2007–08 school year. The relationship grew over the following months; they kissed on campus a couple of times and eventually began a sexual relationship. The relationship ended when the victim's mother found a love letter from Brum in her daughter's room. The mother confronted her daughter, who disclosed the relationship, then confronted Brum at HP Pavilion and pulled her hair during an altercation; officers at the arena responded and were told about the relationship.

Arrested February 26, 2009. East Side Union High School District voted to fire Brum shortly thereafter. Pleaded no contest June 11, 2009 to one count of sexual penetration of a person under 18. Sentenced August 5, 2009 to six months in county jail and three years formal probation.

NOTE: Investigating police stated they believed there may be more victims who had not come forward. Brum was described by students as popular and well-liked, unusually accessible compared to other teachers — consistent with grooming dynamics. Female victim. Love letter was the trigger for discovery.
