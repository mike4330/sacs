Former child nutrition program employee (food service worker) at Lauderdale County High School, Lauderdale County, Alabama. Age 41 at time of offense and sentencing. Married with several children. Resident of Rogersville, Alabama.

Had sexual intercourse with a student (under age 19) on school grounds on two occasions: March 7 and March 31, 2025. When interviewed by police, Wigginton waived her Miranda rights and confessed to both encounters.

Arrested April 4, 2025 on two counts of school employee engaging in a sex act (Alabama Code § 13A-6-82, Class B felony); bond set at $100,000, posted shortly after arrest. Placed on administrative leave. Lauderdale County Board of Education voted unanimously to terminate her employment April 21, 2025.

Pleaded guilty to misdemeanor sexual misconduct; sentenced February 5, 2026 to probation.

NOTE: Non-teaching school employee (child nutrition / food service). Victim age not specified in available sources beyond 'under age 19'; Alabama law (§ 13A-6-82) criminalizes school employee sexual contact with students under 19 regardless of consent, while the general age of consent in Alabama is 16. Confession at arrest. Original felony charges (two counts) resolved via misdemeanor plea. No-prison outcome.
