Former substitute teacher and secretary at Arkansas Christian Academy, Alexander, Arkansas (Saline County). Age 37 at time of offense (DOB March 5, 1978); age 38 at arrest. Turned herself in to the Benton Police Department on August 7, 2016; charged with first-degree sexual assault (A.C.A. 5-14-124(a)(2), Class A felony) for an alleged improper relationship with a 16-year-old student. Booked into Saline County Jail. Offense date per sentencing order: November 1, 2015.

A second charge — first-degree stalking (A.C.A. 5-71-229(a)(1), offense date September 7, 2016) — suggests conduct directed at the victim or another party following her initial arrest. That charge was nolle prossed at sentencing.

Negotiated guilty plea entered April 5, 2018, before Judge Grisham Phillips. Sentenced to 10 years (120 months) probation; no incarceration; sex offender registration required; 60 days electronic monitoring at her expense with a curfew consistent with her work schedule; no contact with victim (J.H.); sex offender-specific treatment; DNA sample required. Financial: $5,000 fine, $150 court costs, $250 DNA sample fee, $20 booking fee. Defense attorney: James Law (private); prosecutor: Rebecca Bush.

NOTE: Presumptive sentence on the sexual assault count was 42 months (Community Corrections Center or alternative sanction); the actual sentence of 10 years probation with no incarceration is a substantial departure below presumptive, though the sentencing order does not formally check 'departure.' First-degree stalking charge — filed with a post-arrest offense date — suggests Knight contacted the victim or another person in violation of investigation conditions; dismissed at plea.
