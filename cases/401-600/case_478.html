Kaitlin Ann Glover #478 closed

StateNevadaCourtEighth Judicial District Court, Clark County
JudgeJasmin Lilly-Spells Incident Date2017-12-01
Defendant Age28
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former teacher at Mountain View Christian Schools, a private K-12 Christian school in Las Vegas, Nevada. Employed there since August 2014. Age ~28 at onset of offense. Victim was a female student.

Beginning in December 2017, groomed a female student via Snapchat (username 'MissG-lover'), positioning herself as a mentor and mother figure. Sent the victim nude photos; communicated daily for nearly four years. The victim's phone was contacted 430 times between August 2020 and January 2022; a second phone showed 232 contacts July 2021–June 2022. Glover told the victim she would teach her to masturbate when she turned 18. The two told each other they loved each other approximately 500 times over the four-year period. First in-person sexual contact occurred in Glover's vehicle (a green van) during spring break 2018; contact continued approximately every two weeks at the same location near the victim's home through approximately September 2021. Glover threatened to kill herself if the victim left her or reported the abuse. The victim came forward after learning Glover was in a relationship with another student.

Case filed December 5, 2023. Pleaded guilty February 14, 2024 to Count 1 (coercion with physical force or immediate threat of physical force, sexually motivated, NRS 207.190.2a). Sentenced April 17, 2024 by Judge Jasmin Lilly-Spells: 24–60 months Nevada DOC suspended; probation up to 2 years; 4 weekends intermittent custody; sex offender registration; internet devices subject to P&P monitoring; barred from positions of authority over minors. On August 27, 2025, Count 2 (coercion sexually motivated) sentenced separately: 24–60 months DOC suspended, probation 1 day (effectively concurrent/nominal). No prison time served.

NOTE: Original expected charges were statutory sexual seduction and sexual conduct between teacher and student; resolved as coercion (sexually motivated). Four-year relationship — among the longer in this DB. Female victim; age not reported (under 18 at offense onset per context). Private religious school. Glover denied all allegations and her attorney characterized the relationship as mentorship. Possible additional victim prompted the complainant to come forward.
