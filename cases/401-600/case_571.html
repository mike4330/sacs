Former music and choir teacher at Henry Sibley High School, Mendota Heights, Minnesota (Dakota County). Age 31 at time of offense; 32 at sentencing. Resident of New Hope, Minnesota.

Engaged in sexual intercourse with a 17-year-old male student on three occasions in February 2017, at her home, at the school, and in the student's car. Also sent several partially nude photos of herself to the boy via text message. Initially arrested in late February 2017 — at that time also accused of possessing child pornography (disposition of that charge not reported). Denied the allegations initially, claiming any sexual texts were a 'fantasy,' but admitted to the conduct after police showed her the photos from the boy's phone. Resigned from Henry Sibley High School in March 2017.

Charged July 2017 with three felony counts of criminal sexual conduct (maximum 15 years and $30,000 fine per count); bail set at $75,000. Pleaded guilty to one count; two counts dismissed. Sentenced June 25, 2018: 6 months jail, 10 years probation.

NOTE: The child pornography accusation at the time of initial arrest is not reflected in the final charge resolution as reported. Sentence (6 months jail + 10 years probation) is a substantial reduction from the 15-year maximum faced on three counts.
