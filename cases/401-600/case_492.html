Former art teacher at Singer High School, Singer, Beauregard Parish, Louisiana. Age 28 at arrest (December 2013); offense age approximately 27–28.

Charged with 18 counts of felony carnal knowledge of a juvenile following a sexual relationship with a 15-year-old student. Arrested December 23, 2013; bond set at $540,000.

Pleaded guilty to 4 counts of carnal knowledge of a juvenile. Sentenced August 10, 2015 by Judge Kerry Anderson: five years per count on each of the four counts, four-and-a-half years suspended on each, sentences to run concurrent — resulting in six months to serve. Upon release, two years of home incarceration to follow. Prohibited from teaching again.
