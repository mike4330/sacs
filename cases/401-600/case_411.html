Former English teacher at Aspire East Palo Alto Phoenix Academy, East Palo Alto, California. Age 32 at time of offense.

Two male victims, both 15 years old. Between September and November 2015, committed lewd acts in her locked classroom: showed both boys tattoos of a planet and stars on her bare breasts and encouraged them to touch her. The second victim accompanied the first to her classroom on one occasion. Also engaged in inappropriate conduct with the first victim after school hours on two additional occasions, including oral copulation.

Students told their friends; one eventually reported to a local legal aid lawyer who called police. No video evidence or independent witnesses; case relied on victim testimony. Convicted by jury in 2017 of three lewd acts and oral copulation (victim 1) and one lewd act (victim 2). Sentenced to 5 years state prison by Judge Jonathan Karesh. Three-judge Court of Appeal panel unanimously upheld conviction and sentence in February 2019.

NOTE: Charter school case. District Attorney noted case relied entirely on victim credibility with no corroborating physical evidence.
