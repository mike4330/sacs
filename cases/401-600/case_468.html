Former substitute teacher at Boyd ISD, Boyd, Texas (Wise County). Age 28 at time of offense (January 2023). Not certified by the State Board for Educator Certification; not listed in the Texas Do Not Hire Registry for non-certified employees at time of arrest.

One victim, age 15, a Boyd ISD student; sex not specified in sources. Ferguson communicated with the student via Snapchat and sent multiple nude 'snaps' outside of school hours. A school resource officer assigned to Boyd ISD learned of the conduct on February 6, 2023. Boyd ISD placed Ferguson on administrative leave and terminated her employment on February 7. She was arrested February 10, 2023.

Indicted January 18, 2024 on two counts: (1) improper relationship between educator and student (Texas PC 21.12, 2nd degree felony), offense date January 10, 2023; (2) online solicitation of a minor (Texas PC 33.021(b), 3rd degree felony), offense date January 20, 2023. Arraigned March 18, 2024 before Judge Brock R. Smith in the 271st Judicial District Court. Case has been reset multiple times; jury trial currently set for June 23, 2026. Defense attorney Paul Belew; prosecutor Jay Lapham (Wise County DA).

NOTE: As a non-certified substitute teacher, Ferguson had no SBEC record and was not on the Do Not Hire Registry — a gap in the pre-employment screening system noted by media coverage at the time.
