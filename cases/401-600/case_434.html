Former paraprofessional special education assistant at Jefferson Junior High School, Greeley, Weld County, Colorado (Greeley-Evans School District 6). Hired by District 6 in August 2011; transferred to Jefferson Junior High in August 2016. Victim was a 14-year-old male student. Defendant age 24 at time of offense.

Relationship began in fall 2016 through cool-off walks — a school program for students needing emotional breaks, with Enriquez substituting as chaperone when the regular security guard was busy. She helped the victim with his math homework and, after breaking his cellphone, bought him a replacement. Texting, social media, and video chatting began October 15, 2016. Within a week they kissed and documented it with selfie photos. Shortly after, they had sex twice in the parking lot of a Greeley park in the back seat of her red Saturn. Victim eventually ended the relationship, saying it started to feel weird because she was his teacher. Enriquez also sent nude photos of herself to at least two other juvenile males (ages 14 and 15).

Charged December 2016 with two counts of sexual assault on a child by a person in a position of trust (including pattern of abuse), promotion of obscenity to a minor, and child sexual assault. A second case filed in 2017 added charges of internet sexual exploitation of a child, sexual exploitation of a child, and attempted child sex assault.

Jury trial before Judge Shannon Lyons in Weld District Court, May 2018. Convicted on all counts. Sentenced August 9, 2018 to 18 years to life in prison. Enriquez, who had given birth to a daughter while incarcerated, asked for the minimum sentence; she acknowledged her criminal actions and apologized to the victim's family.

NOTE: Multiple victims — nude selfies sent to at least two other juveniles; second case added additional exploitation charges. Grooming pattern: trusted chaperone role → replaced broken phone (gift) → escalated to physical contact. Special ed paraprofessional working with behaviorally at-risk student.\n\nUpdate (October 2020): Colorado Court of Appeals vacated the promotion of obscenity conviction — photo evidence too blurry to establish lewd exhibition. Primary sexual assault convictions and 18-year-to-life sentence upheld. Case remanded to correct sentence. Currently incarcerated at La Vista Correctional Facility; parole eligibility September 21, 2033; life sentence.
