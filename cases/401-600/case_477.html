StateColoradoCourtJefferson County District Court
Incident Date2002-01-01
Defendant Age36 Victim Age14
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former AP English teacher and assistant principal at Collegiate Academy, a K-12 charter school in Littleton, Jefferson County, Colorado. Taught there since 2000. Age ~36 at onset of offense.

Engaged in a three-year sexual relationship with a male student beginning when he was a 9th grader (approximately age 14). The victim said he met Trabucco in 7th grade and was in her English class when the relationship began in 9th grade. Trabucco told the teen she was in love with him. They engaged in sexual activity numerous times at his parents' home. Trabucco was married and living with her husband and children during part of the relationship; she later divorced and purchased a home in Highlands Ranch, where the relationship continued. The victim, then 21, reported the abuse to police in 2009.

Arrested August 19, 2009 at her Douglas County home. Charged with felony sexual assault on a child by a person in a position of trust. Held in Jefferson County Jail on $50,000 bond. Pleaded guilty to sexual assault of a child by a person in a position of trust. Sentenced April 27, 2010 to five years in community corrections followed by sex offender intensive supervision probation (SOISP) for five years to life; sex offender registration required. No DOC prison time.

NOTE: Three-year relationship spanning victim's full high school tenure — among the longer relationships in this DB. Defendant expressed love for victim. Married at onset; divorced mid-relationship. Community corrections (residential halfway-house placement) is not counted as prison per DB convention; sentence_years = 0.
