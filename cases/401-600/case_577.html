Erica Ann Ginnetti #577 sentenced

StatePennsylvaniaCourtMontgomery County Court of Common Pleas
JudgeGarrett PageIncident Date2013-05
Defendant Age32Victim Age17
Prison Sentence0.08 yrs

Former AP math teacher at Lower Moreland High School, Lower Moreland Township, Montgomery County, Pennsylvania. Age approximately 32 at time of offense; 34–35 at sentencing. Resident of Philadelphia.

Grooming began in May 2013 when Ginnetti approached the 17-year-old student at prom and asked if he would like to work out at her gym. A few days later the student emailed her; she replied with her cellphone number. They communicated regularly thereafter. Ginnetti sent a series of sexually-charged messages including videos of herself undressing and performing sex acts on herself, as well as sexually-suggestive photos depicting her in underwear and a bikini.

In July 2013, Ginnetti had sex with the student in her car in an industrial park near the border of Lower Moreland Township.

Arrested January 2014. Charged with institutional sexual assault and disseminating obscene and other sexual materials to a minor (combined maximum: 7 years). Pleaded guilty December 2014 to both counts.

Sentenced April 3, 2015 by Judge Garrett Page: 30 days in Montgomery County Correctional Facility (report date April 17, 2015); 60 days house arrest; approximately 18 months parole; 3 years probation; 25-year sex offender registration with state police. Court found she did not meet the criteria of a sexually violent predator. Teaching career permanently ended.

At sentencing Ginnetti said she should have exercised better judgment as the adult in the situation and apologized to family and friends. She noted she had since found work as a fitness instructor and volunteered at her church. Judge Page stated he believed she was 'a good person who did a bad thing,' and added: 'One bad day of sexual hunger resulted in this avalanche of harm.'

The victim submitted a letter (read aloud by the judge) stating he should have been able to look back on his senior year with fond memories but instead was bullied, his grades slipped, and he required counseling.

NOTE: Sentence (30 days jail) is dramatically below the 7-year maximum. Grooming vector was gym/fitness connection established at prom. Videos of herself performing sex acts sent to a student — documented CSAM-adjacent conduct. Victim impact statement described sustained secondary harm from public exposure.
