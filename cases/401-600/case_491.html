Former history teacher at River Ridge High School, New Port Richey, and Sunlake High School, Land O'Lakes, Pasco County, Florida. Began teaching in 1996 after being an at-risk teen herself. Age 40 at onset of offense.

In December 2009, while teaching at River Ridge High School, initiated a sexual relationship with a 15-year-old male student. Victim's mother stated Verdi introduced her son to drugs and that he became addicted. The relationship continued through at least 2011, by which time Verdi had transferred to Sunlake High School. On September 29, 2011, Verdi's boyfriend — who suspected she was cheating — followed her and rammed her car on County Line Road while the 17-year-old victim was a passenger; deputies responding to the crash discovered the relationship. Verdi was suspended and on unpaid leave for unrelated professional conduct violations at the time; she resigned October 3, 2011 upon commencement of the formal investigation.

Arrested October 28, 2011 on charges of lewd or lascivious battery and unlawful sexual activity with a minor; booked at the Land O'Lakes jail on 0,000 bail. In 2012, while out on bail and forbidden from contact with the victim, Verdi sent him a Facebook message through another person's account — resulting in an additional charge of harassing a witness. She was incarcerated for approximately one year prior to sentencing. Pleaded guilty September 2013 to unlawful sexual activity with a minor and harassing a witness. Sentenced October 1, 2013 by Circuit Court Judge Mary Handsel to two years in prison (with credit for ~1 year served), two years of house arrest, and three additional years of sex offender probation; required to register as a sex offender and relinquish her teaching certificate.

NOTE: Victim suffered drug addiction attributed to defendant's introduction of drugs; was sent to court-mandated rehabilitation. Victim's mother: 'She deserves to burn in hell.' Verdi had a prior March 2010 domestic battery arrest (charges dropped via pretrial intervention) and extensive disciplinary history including unprofessional conduct, substance use references in class, and profanity. Facebook contact via third-party account to circumvent no-contact order resulted in witness harassment charge — one of the few such charges in this DB.
