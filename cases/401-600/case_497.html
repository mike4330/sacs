Former English and English support teacher and head girls' basketball coach (since 2018) at Thurston High School, Springfield, Lane County, Oregon. Age 31 at arrest (December 2020); approximately 29–30 at onset of offense.

Sexually abused a 16-year-old female student. Springfield School District placed DeFluri on paid administrative leave December 8, 2020 in coordination with a Springfield Police Department investigation. Arrested December 14, 2020; booked into Lane County Jail. Initially three charges filed. Thurston High School fired DeFluri shortly after arraignment.

Pleaded guilty to 11 counts of second-degree sexual abuse of a minor. Sentenced May 2021 to seven years in prison. On appeal, an Oregon appellate court remanded to the trial court; three convictions were merged into one. At resentencing, defense argued for 2.5 years with five years post-prison probation, citing emotional distress and mental illness at time of offense and subsequent rehabilitation efforts. Prosecution opposed any reduction, citing ongoing harm to the victim and family. Resentenced May 25, 2023 to the same term: just over seven years in prison, with three years of post-prison probation.

NOTE: Incident date approximate — DeFluri became girls' basketball coach in 2018; offense occurred before December 2020 arrest. Appeal resulted in merger of three counts but no reduction in sentence length. Female victim.
