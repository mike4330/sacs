Rhonda Michelle Ford #538 sentenced

StateOklahomaCourtPushmataha County District Court
JudgeGary L. Brock Incident Date2012-02-14
Defendant Age29 Victim Age15
Prison Sentence3.5 yrs

Former teacher and coach at Clayton Public Schools, Clayton, Oklahoma. Resigned from Clayton shortly before charges. Had previously taught at Cleveland High School (fall 2009 only).

Victim was a 15-year-old male student. Ford had sexual intercourse with him more than 10 times at her home beginning around Valentine's Day 2012 (February 14). Conduct also included kissing and genital touching. Separately, Ford provided a 30-pack of beer and mixed cocktails to three students (two boys, one girl) who helped her move.

Investigated by OSBI. First Assistant DA was Johnny Loard. Tattoo on Ford's pelvic area was cited as corroborating physical evidence — victim described it in detail.

Trial by judge (bench trial). Verdict 2/6/2013. Convicted on counts 2, 3, 6, 7: second-degree rape (x2) and sodomy (x2). Counts 1, 4, 5, 8, 9, 10, 11 dismissed (additional rape/sodomy counts and alcohol-furnishing charges).

Sentenced to 10 years; all but first 42 months suspended. Active time: 42 months (3.5 years). Fines: $100 VCA + $150 OIDS + $250 PSI.

NOTE: Oklahoma 85% rule applies to sex offenses — Ford would serve at least 85% of the 42-month active term before parole eligibility.
