Samantha Fisher #596 sentenced

Former substitute teacher at Wellston High School, Wellston, Jackson County, Ohio. Age approximately 22 at time of offense (23 at sentencing, May 2016). Note: school is in Jackson County but prosecution was in Ross County, where Fisher lived and where the offense occurred.

Engaged in sexual conduct with a 16-year-old male high school student between February 1 and March 10, 2015, at her home in Ross County. The victim was not a direct student of hers. Defense counsel stated the relationship was consensual and that both parties were going through difficult times; Fisher realized within days it was wrong. The victim addressed the court at sentencing in support of Fisher, asking the judge not to impose the sex offender classification.

Pleaded guilty April 7, 2016 to sexual battery, a third-degree felony. Filed a motion to withdraw her plea, which Judge Michael Ater denied at a May 23, 2016 hearing immediately before sentencing. Defense argued Fisher had not been fully informed of the lifetime notification and registration requirement when she entered her plea; Ater characterized the motion as 'buyer's remorse' and found no evidence she had not committed the crime.

Sentenced May 23, 2016 to three years of community control sanctions, 60 days in the Ross County Jail (work release permitted, one day credit), followed by 90 days house arrest. No prison imposed on the F3. Also ordered: 200 hours community service within one year, sex offender class, substance abuse testing, no contact with anyone under 18 while on community control. Designated Tier III sex offender — 90-day lifetime registration with mandatory neighborhood notification. Teaching license lost.

Defense challenged the Tier III classification as cruel and unusual punishment, noting the victim was 16 (Ohio age of consent) and the relationship was consensual; argued the statute penalizes Fisher solely for being a teacher in a position of trust. Ater agreed his hands were tied by statute. The county prosecutor stated community control was 'appropriate' and the case was 'clearly consensual, but clearly illegal.'

NOTE: sentence_years = 0 (work-release jail and house arrest; no prison). Ohio's age of consent is 16; position-of-trust statute applied. Victim spoke at sentencing in support of defendant. Tier III classification for an F3 no-prison case drew explicit constitutional challenge from defense.
