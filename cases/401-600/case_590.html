Former science teacher at Wheeling Park High School and former track coach at Bridge Street Middle School in Wheeling, West Virginia (Ohio County School District). Resided in Moundsville. Age 30 at the time of the first sexual offense in summer 2005; age 42 at sentencing in August 2019. The known victim was a male student who was first her student in eighth grade at Bridge Street and then continued at Wheeling Park High School where she also taught.

The relationship began during the summer of 2005, when the victim — then 14 years old — was living with Harbert and her then-husband. The first sexual encounter occurred that summer. Harbert subsequently divorced her husband, and the relationship continued as the victim moved on to Wheeling Park High School the following fall. In 2007, when the victim was 16, Harbert became pregnant with the first of four children fathered by him. The relationship continued for approximately thirteen years and ended in 2018.

The investigative record is unusually well corroborated. Medical records contemporaneous with the relationship reflect a contraceptive failure that required medical follow-up. DNA evidence establishes the victim's paternity of all four children at a 99.99% confidence level. The civil complaint and criminal complaint both alleged that school employees within the Ohio County district had received multiple reports during the relationship that Harbert was sexually abusing the victim and had failed to investigate; one cited indicator was that Harbert appeared visibly pregnant while a single divorcee.

The criminal investigation opened on January 28, 2019, when the victim — by then an adult who had graduated from Wheeling Park in 2009 — filed a sexual-abuse complaint with current Ohio County Schools Superintendent Kim Miller. Miller contacted the West Virginia State Police, who interviewed the victim on January 31, 2019. Harbert resigned her teaching position at Wheeling Park at the start of the 2018-19 school year. She was arrested in April 2019 on a charge of sexual abuse by a parent, guardian, or custodian to a child.

On August 13, 2019, before Ohio County Circuit Court Judge Michael Olejasz, Harbert entered a Kennedy/Alford plea to one count of sexual assault in the third degree. Under the Kennedy/Alford structure she did not admit guilt but acknowledged that the prosecution had sufficient evidence to obtain a conviction. The original sexual-abuse-by-custodian charge was dismissed in the plea. Judge Olejasz imposed an indeterminate sentence of one to five years in the West Virginia Division of Corrections, ten years of supervised release following imprisonment, and lifetime sex-offender registration. Harbert had no prior criminal history and was permitted to self-report by September 3, 2019. Prosecuting Attorney Shawn Turak appeared for the State.

A parallel civil action filed by the victim against Harbert and the Ohio County Board of Education in 2018 was the catalyst for the criminal prosecution. The civil claim against Harbert herself was dismissed on statute-of-limitations grounds; the claim against the Board of Education remained on track for a civil trial set for August 10, 2020. Harbert at one point publicly alleged that the now-adult victim had instead abused her — a victim-blaming counter-posture that informed the post-sentencing media coverage.

NOTE: Among the longest-running relationships documented in the corpus (approximately thirteen years from inception in 2005 through termination in 2018) and one of the most evidentially corroborated (medical records contemporaneous with the relationship plus DNA paternity at 99.99% on all four children).
