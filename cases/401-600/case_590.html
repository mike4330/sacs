Former science teacher at Wheeling Park High School and track coach at Bridge Street Middle School, Wheeling, West Virginia (Ohio County). Age 30 at time of first sexual offense (summer 2005); age 42 at sentencing (August 2019).

Began a relationship with Christopher Birch (publicly identified through his civil lawsuit) while he was her student in 8th grade at Bridge Street Middle School. Birch was living with Harbert and her then-husband during the summer of 2005. First sexual encounter occurred that summer when Birch was 14 and Harbert was 30. The sexual relationship continued as Birch became an incoming freshman at Wheeling Park High School, where Harbert was also a teacher. Harbert became pregnant with the first of their four children in 2007 when Birch was 16. She later divorced her husband. The relationship continued until 2018, spanning approximately 13 years.

Medical records corroborate the relationship; DNA evidence establishes Birch's paternity of the children at 99.99%. School employees within the Ohio County district received multiple reports alleging Harbert was sexually abusing Birch but allegedly failed to investigate. When the relationship ended in 2018, Birch filed a civil lawsuit against Harbert and the Ohio County Board of Education. Criminal charges followed. The civil case against Harbert was dismissed on statute-of-limitations grounds; the suit against the board remained pending with a trial date of August 10, 2020.

Arrested April 2019 on charges of sexual abuse by a parent, guardian, or custodian to a child. West Virginia State Police had interviewed Birch on January 31, 2019; Ohio County Schools Superintendent Kim Miller had contacted police after receiving Birch's complaint on January 28, 2019. Harbert resigned her position at Wheeling Park High School at the start of the 2018–19 school year.

Entered a Kennedy/Alford plea on August 13, 2019 to one count of sexual assault in the third degree. Did not admit guilt but acknowledged sufficient evidence for conviction. Sentenced to 1 to 5 years in the West Virginia Division of Corrections (indeterminate), 10 years supervised release following imprisonment, and lifetime sex offender registration. Allowed to self-report by September 3, 2019.
