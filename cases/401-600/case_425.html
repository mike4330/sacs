Amanda Carol Phillips #425 sentenced

StateKentuckyCourtHarrison Circuit Court
Incident Date2018-07-31
Defendant Age37 Victim Age10
Prison Sentence6.0 yrs

Former special education teacher (4th/5th grade) in Harrison County, Kentucky. Victim was a male student in her special needs class. Abuse occurred at Phillips' home over at least six months beginning in 2018. Defendant age approximately 37 at time of offense. Victim age approximately 10 (inferred: 4th/5th grade special needs student).

Originally indicted by grand jury in 2019 on charges of rape, sex abuse, and sodomy involving a juvenile. Pled guilty August 2021 to four counts of first-degree sexual abuse (KRS 510.110). Sentenced November 20, 2021 in Harrison Circuit Court to six years; ordered to serve at least 85% before parole eligibility. Required to complete sex offender treatment program before release. Surrendered teaching certificate and barred from working in any school capacity. Sex offender registration for life; five years post-release supervision.

Phillips admitted to the abuse per a civil lawsuit filed on behalf of the victim. Parole supervision began December 1, 2025 (released after approximately four years served).

NOTE: Charge reduction from rape/sodomy to sexual abuse. Victim in special needs class — particularly vulnerable victim. Abuse location was defendant's home.
