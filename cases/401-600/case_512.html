Former English Language Arts teacher (middle and high school) at Henry School District, Henry, Codington County, South Dakota. Resided in Watertown at time of arrest. Age 41 at time of offense (February 28, 2023).

Had sexual contact with a 16-year-old male student. Ries self-reported the relationship to authorities. Arrested March 5–6, 2023; resigned from the Henry School District after being charged.

Originally charged with 2 counts of sexual contact with a child under 18 by a person of authority (Class 6 felony) and 3 counts of sexual exploitation of a minor (Class 6 felony). Pleaded guilty July 26, 2023 to 1 count of sexual contact with a child under 18 by a person of authority; 4 remaining counts dismissed. Sentenced August 31, 2023 by Judge Carmen Means to 2 years in the South Dakota State Penitentiary with 1 year suspended; taken into custody immediately by Codington County deputies.

NOTE: Self-reporting cited by Judge Means as a mitigating factor at sentencing. Prosecution argued contact occurred multiple times; court documents reflected a single incident.
