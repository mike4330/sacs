Former instructional aide at Kilgore High School, Kilgore ISD, Kilgore, Texas (Gregg County). Certified educational aide (TEA certification April 2024). Employed August 2023; resigned January 7, 2025 amid a district investigation into possible educator misconduct. Age 34 at arrest.

Investigation initiated by Kilgore ISD; results turned over to law enforcement following O'Dell's resignation. Arrested February 19, 2025 by the Gregg County Sheriff's Office; booked into Gregg County Jail. Released February 20 on a $20,000 bond. Victim described as 17 or older.

Charged with improper relationship between educator and student (second-degree felony, 2–20 years). Pleaded guilty November 4, 2025 before Judge Novy. Sentenced to 10 years deferred adjudication probation; 2–20 year exposure if revoked. No sex offender registration required, citing victim age (17 or older) and O'Dell's clean prior record. Teaching certificate revoked for life by TEA. Court fees: $1,000.

As of March 2026, O'Dell was jailed on a motion to adjudicate guilt filed by the state: alleged theft of property ($100–$750) from Dillard's (arrested February 19, 2026), failure to complete counseling and community service, and failure to pay probation fees. Held without bond; revocation hearing not yet set.

NOTE: Texas law exempts educators from sex offender registration for improper relationship offenses when the victim is 17 or older and the defendant has no prior criminal history — a statutory carve-out that drew notice here. Deferred adjudication, if completed, results in case dismissal with no formal finding of guilt, but the March 2026 motion to adjudicate puts that outcome at risk. If found guilty on revocation, O'Dell faces 2–20 years.
