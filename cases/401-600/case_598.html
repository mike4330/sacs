Elizabeth Giesler #598 sentenced

StateMissouriCourtSte. Genevieve County Circuit Court
Incident Date2018-04-07
Defendant Age39Victim Age16
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former assistant principal at Ste. Genevieve Middle School (also described in some reports as Ste. Genevieve High School), Ste. Genevieve R-II School District, Ste. Genevieve, Missouri (approximately 65 miles southeast of St. Louis). Hired by the district in 2014 after previously teaching in Liberty, Missouri. Age 39 at time of charges (May 2018).

A 16-year-old male student (9th grade, football player) had reportedly been staying at Giesler's home during a regional baseball tournament. The victim's family had known Giesler since the student was in 6th grade and considered her a trusted mentor — the father told media she had promised to help ensure the boy "got on the right path" athletically and academically. Following the student's disclosure, he was placed in state protective custody for up to 90 days. A friend of the student told police after the student disclosed sexual activity with Giesler. A Missouri state trooper took the student's statement; the boy reported Giesler had performed sex acts on him during the period of April 7–8, 2018, at her home, and that they had engaged in sexual activity on two other occasions, all at her home.

Charged in May 2018 with three Class E felony counts of sexual contact with a student, three Class D felony counts of statutory sodomy in the second degree, and two counts of statutory rape in the second degree (eight total counts). Giesler was placed on leave by the district at that time. In October 2018, two counts (one sexual contact with a student, one statutory sodomy) were dismissed following a preliminary hearing based on testimony about the three alleged incidents. A separation agreement with the district was signed in November 2018.

A jury trial on the remaining six felony counts was scheduled for late April 2020. On February 11, 2020, Giesler entered an Alford plea to one misdemeanor count of harassment in the second degree. Under the plea agreement: two years of unsupervised probation, no contact with the victim or non-law-enforcement witnesses, travel out of state permitted on specified dates only, and voluntary surrender of her teaching license. No prison, no jail, no sex offender registration.

Prosecuting Attorney Wayne Williams acknowledged the sentence appeared light but noted the surrender of Giesler's teaching license was the key consequence: 'Some people might not understand the bargain that was struck in this case unless they really understand what she had to give up voluntarily to get that bargain. That was basically her livelihood.'

NOTE: One of the most severe charge reductions in the DB — 8 felony counts (including rape and sodomy) reduced to 1 misdemeanor, 2 years unsupervised probation, no registration, no incarceration. Victim-in-home (student staying at defendant's residence during baseball tournament). Friend-reported.
