Former teacher at Waynoka Public Schools, Waynoka, Woods County, Oklahoma. Had taught at the school for six years. Age 32 at time of offense. Married mother.

Beginning in December 2014, engaged in a sexual relationship with an 18-year-old male student. The two had sex in Poe's home, in the student's pickup truck, and multiple times in the school teacher's lounge. Oklahoma State Bureau of Investigation received an anonymous tip and questioned both Poe and the student on January 20, 2015 — hours after the two had last had sex in the student's pickup. Poe provided a written confession: 'I am Daresa Poe, a wife, mother and teacher. I have made a horrible mistake. I have let my judgment falter and I am embarrassed and mortified for the decisions I have made in the last two weeks. I have had sexual intercourse with a student.' She was suspended with pay pending investigation.

Originally charged with one count of sexual battery and six counts of second-degree rape. Charge was amended in Woods County District Court to assault with intent to commit a felony. Pleaded guilty August 21, 2015; sentenced to five years suspended, with the first two years supervised. No prison time.

NOTE: Victim was 18 (legal adult) but the relationship is illegal under Oklahoma law, which prohibits sexual conduct between a teacher and student until the student is 21. On-campus sex in the teacher's lounge is notable. Written confession included in the affidavit. Relationship lasted approximately two weeks — among the shortest documented in this DB. Charge-reduction from second-degree rape to assault with intent to commit a felony is significant.
