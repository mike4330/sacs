Former math teacher and assistant Scholar's Bowl coach at El Dorado High School, El Dorado, Kansas (Butler County, USD 490). Name at time of arrest: Jelinda Dawn Keeling (changed to Smith by marriage prior to sentencing).

Victim was an 18-year-old male student, identified in court documents as N.A.F. Arrested March 14, 2024 by El Dorado Police Department on one felony count of unlawful sexual relations with a student over the age of 16. She submitted her resignation to USD 490 on April 1, 2024. On March 18, 2024, she was arrested a second time for attempting contact with the victim in violation of a protection order.

Struck a plea agreement with the district; jury trial originally scheduled September 24, 2024 was canceled. Sentenced December 4, 2024 to 36 months probation. Conditions: if probation is violated she will serve 32 months in prison followed by 24 months post-release supervision. Required to register as a sex offender for 25 years.

NOTE: Victim was 18 — the offense is the institutional teacher-student relationship, not age of consent. Defendant attempted to contact the victim after arrest in violation of a protection order, resulting in a second arrest four days after the first.
