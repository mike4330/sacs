Former math teacher, coach, and athletics director at Noble Academy Cleveland, a public K–8 charter school in Euclid, Ohio (Cuyahoga County). Age approximately 26 at time of first offense (2017); age 34 at sentencing. LinkedIn listed employment beginning August 2018, though the indictment covers abuse from 2017.

Sexually abused a 14-year-old female middle school basketball player multiple times between 2017 and 2019. Abuse occurred in the back of the school bus on the way home from basketball games, in the locker room at Noble Academy, and at Polzner's home. The victim, an adult at the time of reporting, disclosed the abuse to Euclid Police.

Arrested December 4, 2024. Indicted by a Cuyahoga County grand jury on several third-degree felonies including sexual battery. School principal placed Polzner on unpaid administrative leave and she was listed as no longer employed as of December 2024.

Pleaded guilty June 2, 2025 to four counts of gross sexual imposition (4th-degree felony) and one count of unlawful sexual conduct with a minor (misdemeanor), as part of a plea agreement that dismissed the more serious felony charges. Originally set for jury trial that day before the agreement was reached.

Sentenced July 10, 2025 to one year in prison. Designated a Tier 1 sex offender requiring annual registration for 15 years. Required to surrender her state teaching license and may not reside within 1,000 feet of any school, preschool, or daycare with visible signage.

NOTE: Delayed reporting — approximately five years elapsed between the end of the abuse (2019) and arrest (December 2024). Female victim. School stated no evidence of misconduct after 2019 or involving other students.
