Former teacher at Choctaw Middle School, Choctaw-Nicoma Park School District, Oklahoma County, Oklahoma.

Victim was a 14-year-old male student. Multiple teachers and staff at Choctaw Middle School reported contact between Heslip and the teen to the school; the school contacted the family, who found text messages on the boy's phone and called police. Heslip had moved the conversation from text to Snapchat, where nude photos were exchanged. An image of Heslip's nude breast dated October 10, 2021 was found on the victim's phone.

On October 18, 2021, Heslip picked the teen up from a basketball court, took him hiking and for ice cream, then brought him to her home. In the garage, she sexually assaulted him. Arrested November 2021 by Oklahoma County Sheriff's Office; charges were not filed until December 21, 2022 — more than a year later.

Pleaded guilty April 3, 2024, to both counts before Judge Coyle. ADA Cleveland prosecuted; defense attorney Jonathan Herrera. Sentenced: Count 1 (technology communication for sexual interest with a minor, 21 O.S. 1040.13a) — 10 years fully suspended; Count 2 (indecent or lewd acts with a child under 16, 21 O.S. 1123(A)) — 15 years fully suspended; concurrent. Supervised by Oklahoma DOC. Fines: $1,000 + $100 VCA + $100 DA + court costs. No prison.

NOTE: No-prison outcome — both sentences fully suspended, DOC supervision only. The 15-year concurrent suspended sentences represent significant charge severity without incarceration. Offense date for Count 2 is listed as 10/18/2022 in court records but is clearly 10/18/2021 based on news reporting and the November 2021 arrest; apparent docket data entry error.
