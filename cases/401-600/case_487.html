Samantha Fisher #487 closed

Former substitute teacher at Wellston High School, Wellston, Jackson County, Ohio. Age 22 at time of offense.

Engaged in sexual conduct with a 16-year-old male student between February 1 and March 10, 2015, at her home in Ross County. The victim was not a direct student of Fisher's. Both parties described as going through 'difficult times'; defense said Fisher recognized the relationship was 'bad' within days but it had already occurred. Relationship was described by all parties — including the prosecution — as consensual.

Pleaded guilty April 7, 2016 to sexual battery (Ohio third-degree felony). Filed a motion to withdraw her plea, which Judge Michael Ater denied at a hearing on the day of sentencing. Ater classified Fisher as a Tier III sex offender — the highest Ohio tier — requiring registration every 90 days for the rest of her life with community notification of her address. Sentenced May 23, 2016 to three years community control, 60 days Ross County Jail (work release permitted), 90 days house arrest, 200 hours community service, sex offender class, and substance abuse testing. Judge declined to impose prison, noting Fisher's life had been 'exemplary.' She lost her teaching license.

At sentencing the victim appeared in court and addressed the judge in support of Fisher, arguing she should not have to be a sex offender for the rest of her life. Defense attorney Mark Miller challenged the Tier III classification as cruel and unusual punishment, arguing the age of consent is 16 and Fisher was being penalized for her status as a teacher. Ater acknowledged his hands were tied by statute.

NOTE: Substitute teacher; victim not her direct student. 37-day offense window — among the shorter relationships in this DB. Prosecutor Jeff Marks: 'It was clearly consensual, but clearly illegal.' Defense indicated an appeal of the SOR classification was likely. Offense occurred at Ross County residence; school in Jackson County — court jurisdiction follows offense location.
