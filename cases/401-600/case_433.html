Former religious studies teacher at Archbishop Stepinac High School (Catholic), White Plains, Westchester County, New York. Taught during the 2012-2013 school year and resigned at the end of the year (last day of school June 7, 2013). Sexual encounters with a 14-year-old male student occurred after her resignation, at her home in Eastchester, between June 11 and July 14, 2013. Defendant age 27 at time of offense (DOB 2/28/1986).

Victim's parents discovered the encounters and contacted Yonkers police; investigation determined the incidents took place in Eastchester. Iles had relocated to California but returned to surrender to Eastchester police November 6, 2013. Charged with two counts of rape (max 7 years each); bail set at $25,000 cash or $75,000 bond.

Pled guilty March 2014 per agreement with prosecutors. Sentenced May 27, 2014: 6 months Westchester County Jail, approximately 10 years probation, sex offender registration required.

NOTE: All incidents occurred after Iles had already resigned from the school; school emphasized this in its public statement. Private Catholic school. Iles announced her resignation in spring 2013 citing plans to move to California.
