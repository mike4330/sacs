Former hip-hop teacher at Cornerstone Academy Middle School, Spring Branch ISD, Houston, Harris County, Texas. Victim was a 14-year-old female student. Defendant age 32 at time of offense.

Communicated with victim via phone, Instagram, and Snapchat. Told the girl she had feelings for her at the end of the victim's 7th grade year. Repeatedly showed up at victim's apartment after her mother left for work, asking her to come to her vehicle. Kissed and touched victim inappropriately in the back seat of her car on multiple occasions (August 1 and August 14, 2018). Sent the victim explicit photos of herself and solicited explicit photos and video in return; victim refused. Father discovered sexually explicit messages on the victim's phone and notified police. School principal had already been informed of the situation by the victim's mother.

Arrested September 13, 2018. Charged with sexual assault of a child under 17 and improper relationship with a student. Pled guilty October 14, 2019 in 339th District Court (Judge Belinda Hill) to improper relationship between educator/student (2nd degree felony; cause no. 160475101010). Deferred adjudication community supervision for 10 years. Conditions: 180 days Harris County Jail served off work hours beginning 10/18/2019; $5,000 fine; sex offender registration required; no contact with any minor under 17; cannot reside within 1,000 feet of areas where children gather.

NOTE: Sexual assault charge reduced to improper relationship via plea. 180 days jail off work hours = no-prison per convention. Deferred adjudication = no formal conviction entered if supervision successfully completed. Principal was aware before police arrest — prior-warning-ignored.
