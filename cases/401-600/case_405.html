Wendy Diane Fox #405 sentenced

JudgeJeff McKnightIncident Date2011-05-01
Defendant Age31Victim Age18
Prison Sentence9.0 yrs

Former substitute teacher at Iowa Park High School, Iowa Park, Texas (Wichita County). Age 31 at time of offense.

Victim was an 18-year-old male student. Fox became acquainted with the student while substituting in January 2011. Between January and May 2011 she regularly met him at a local lake; the relationship progressed to sexual intercourse beginning in May 2011. Four encounters in total; last documented encounter June 10, 2011, at the victim's home.

Indicted by grand jury in 2011. Pleaded guilty in 2012 pursuant to a plea bargain; trial court deferred adjudication and placed Fox on 8 years community supervision. In 2016 the state moved to adjudicate guilt after Fox violated supervision terms; she agreed to a jail sanction and continued in sex offender treatment, remaining on deferred adjudication. In 2018 the state again moved to adjudicate: Fox had failed to report to her probation officer, tested positive for opiates, and had been discharged from the sex offender treatment program twice. The trial court revoked her community supervision and sentenced her to 9 years in prison on April 1, 2019.

On October 3, 2019, Fox sought shock probation before Judge Jeff McKnight, who denied the motion. The prosecution cited her two discharges from sex offender treatment and continued denial of responsibility. Fox contended the student had pursued her and blamed him for the offense. She was parole-eligible March 3, 2020. Fox appealed the revocation and 9-year sentence.

NOTE: Victim was 18 — the offense is the institutional educator-student relationship, not age of consent. The 9-year prison sentence was not the original disposition; Fox initially received 8 years deferred adjudication, which was revoked after repeated supervision failures and double discharge from sex offender treatment.
