Former special education teacher at Cross County High School, Cross County, Arkansas. Age 26 at time of offense (November-December 2023).

Victim: 14-year-old male student. Lee engaged in inappropriate physical touching and sexual intercourse with the student on multiple occasions in November and December 2023. A community member noted the victim had been communicating with her digitally (email or chat).

In December 2023, Cross County School District administrators received information about inappropriate conduct and reported it to the Arkansas Child Maltreatment Hotline. The Crimes Against Children Division investigated. Lee resigned. Arrested January 4, 2024 on three counts of second-degree sexual assault and one count of contributing to the delinquency of a minor; bond set at 40,000 dollars.

Negotiated plea to one count of third-degree sexual assault (A.C.A. 5-14-126), reduced from second-degree (A.C.A. 5-14-124). Original sentencing May 12, 2025; amended sentencing order entered September 9, 2025. Sentence: 36 months Arkansas Department of Corrections + 36 months suspended imposition of sentence; sex offender registration required upon release. Judge Danny Glover (1st Circuit, Division 2). Prosecutor: Kevin Watts. Defense: Adam B. Holman. Cross County case no. 19CR-24-52. The sentencing order notes the defendant "conspired to commit the offense."

NOTE: Charge reduction from three counts of second-degree sexual assault to one count of third-degree. Arkansas third-degree sexual assault (A.C.A. 5-14-126) covers sexual contact (touching) with a 14-17 year old by a person in a position of trust — contact_type = 1 on the conviction charge reflects the statutory act; actual conduct per the investigation included intercourse. The amended sentencing order (September 2025) lists an offense date of 11/4/2024, inconsistent with the January 2024 arrest and November-December 2023 incident window — likely a form artifact. Reporting made through the Arkansas Child Maltreatment Hotline rather than direct victim disclosure.

Divorced Zachary Alan Lee; decree entered August 21, 2025 (8th Circuit Division 2, Judge Brent Haltom).
