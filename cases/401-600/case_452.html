Kindergarten teacher at Calera Elementary School, Calera, Alabama (Shelby County). Named 2024-2025 Teacher of the Year for the school. Age 49 at time of offense. Full name Heather Lynn Ozment.

One victim, age 13–15 (indictment specifies 'older than age 12 and younger than age 16'). On April 4, 2025, Ozment is accused of enticing the child into a bathroom for sexual purposes at or near 4979 Highway 31, Calera. Conduct occurred after school hours and not at a school facility; victim was not one of her students. Shelby County Schools was notified of the allegations in early April 2025 and immediately placed Ozment on administrative leave. Calera Police investigated with assistance from Owens House, a children's advocacy nonprofit that conducted forensic interviews. Arrested and booked June 6, 2025; released same day on $45,000 bond. Calera Police Chief David Hyche. Police publicly requested additional information, suggesting possible additional victims.

Indicted on: sexual abuse first degree, sexual abuse second degree (2nd or subsequent offense), and enticing a child to enter a vehicle or house for immoral purposes.

NOTE: The '2nd or subsequent offense' charge on the sexual abuse second degree count means she carried a prior sexual abuse conviction while employed as a teacher and honored as 2024-2025 Teacher of the Year. The district apparently failed to screen for or discover this prior record.
