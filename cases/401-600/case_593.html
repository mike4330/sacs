Former teacher's aide at Licking Heights school, Licking County, Ohio; later became legal custodian of the victim. Age 31 at time of offense (2024); 32 at sentencing (October 2025). Heath, Ohio address. Also spelled 'Brittany McKenna' in some reports.

First met the victim in fifth grade when she was his teacher's aide at Licking Heights. The victim, a boy, later became a runaway and drug user. McKenna took him in, describing the arrangement as kinship care; she eventually became his legal custodian. The sexual relationship occurred while he was in her legal custody in 2024, when he was 15.

Sexual conduct occurred between March and December 2024 across multiple occasions in areas chosen to avoid detection. The victim told police he and McKenna had sex many times. Heath police responded on December 12, 2024 to a report of McKenna fighting with the boy along Woodland Drive after a neighbor called police following the boy's disclosure of the sexual relationship. McKenna was interviewed by police and denied the sexual relationship, claiming he slept in her bed sometimes and that she was providing kinship care; she downplayed references to 'cuddling' found in the phone records.

DNA evidence and text messages retrieved from the victim's phone corroborated the boy's statements. McKenna was indicted by a Licking County grand jury in February 2025 and initially pleaded not guilty; she was scheduled for jury trial multiple times before changing her plea. After posting bond, McKenna picked up the victim and concealed him in her home in violation of a no-contact order.

Entered a no-contact plea on October 8, 2025 to two counts of sexual battery and two counts of unlawful sexual conduct with a minor (court records; one news outlet incorrectly reported a guilty plea). Sentenced the same day to 10 years in prison with credit for 154 days served, followed by five years of probation. Designated a Tier III sex offender requiring registration every 90 days for life.

At sentencing, McKenna minimized her actions and blamed the victim.

NOTE: Hybrid educator/custodian case — the educator relationship preceded the abuse by several years; actual abuse occurred in a custodial context. Violation of bond no-contact order by concealing victim in her home is a significant aggravating factor. DNA evidence. No-remorse/victim-blaming at sentencing.
