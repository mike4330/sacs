Former language arts teacher and girls swimming and water polo coach at Valencia High School, Placentia, Orange County, California. Had been teaching at the school since 2003. Age ~25 at onset of offense.

In 2004, established an illegal sexual relationship with a then-15-year-old female student. On multiple occasions between 2004 and 2008, engaged in sexual acts with the victim in Diebolt's classroom at the high school and at her home after school and on weekends. The sexual relationship continued after the victim graduated from high school and began attending college.

Victim reported the sex acts to the Placentia Police Department on June 4, 2014 — approximately ten years after the abuse began. Diebolt was arrested approximately one week later, on June 11, 2014. Pleaded guilty January 15, 2016 to one felony count of oral copulation with a minor under 16 and one felony count of sexual penetration with a foreign object of a minor. Sentenced August 26, 2016: 3 years 8 months state prison stayed pending successful completion of 5 years formal supervised probation; 180 days county jail; mandatory lifetime sex offender registration.
