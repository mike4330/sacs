Lisa Kays #540 sentenced

StateOklahomaCourtOklahoma County District Court
JudgeRay ElliottIncident Date2011-11-01
Defendant Age46Victim Age17
Prison Sentence4.0 yrs

Former Spanish teacher at Putnam City High School, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma. Had taught at the school since 2002.

Victim was a 17-year-old male student. Kays had a sexual relationship with him over more than three months. Five sexual encounters total: two occurred in her classroom, three elsewhere. Relationship surfaced via a campus safety hotline tip. Kays was removed from campus the next day; parents were notified by phone that Friday and met with administrators Monday morning.

Arrested February 13, 2012 on three counts of second-degree rape and two counts of forcible oral sodomy. Released on bond.

Entered a blind guilty plea July 15, 2013. ADA Suzanne Lavenue prosecuted; defense attorney Billy Bock. In allocution, Kays said 'I've learned my lesson and I never intended to hurt anyone.' Judge Ray Elliott — himself a former high school teacher — called her actions 'disgusting' and denied the defense request for probation.

Sentence: 50 years (10 per count), all concurrent, more than half suspended; active time 4 years. Sex offender registration required. Teaching license revoked. Kays was led from the courtroom in handcuffs in front of approximately two dozen supporters.

NOTE: 'Blind guilty plea' (open plea without agreement; judge set sentence independently). Judge explicitly noted his own teaching background in rejecting probation. Two classroom encounters cited by prosecution as aggravating.

NOTE: Same ADA Suzanne Lavenue and Judge Ray Elliott as case #546 (Whitney Fetters, Hefner Middle School, Putnam City Schools). Both #540 and #546 are Putnam City Schools cases prosecuted by the same ADA before the same judge.
