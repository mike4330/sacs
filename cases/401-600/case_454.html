Incident Date 2015-10-01

Victim Age 16
Prison Sentence 10.0 yrs

Former education aide at Snook High School, Snook ISD, Burleson County, Texas. Age 32 at time of offense (October 2015). Full name Deanna Kathryn Lara.

Four student victims, some under age 17. Sexual relations began October 2015. Lara handpicked each victim and groomed them with cash, food, and clothing to prevent them from telling parents or other teachers.

Arrested December 2015 on two counts of improper relationship with a student; bond $10,000 per count. Resigned from Snook ISD following initial allegations; prohibited from returning. Indicted June 2016 on seven counts of improper relationship with a student and three counts of sexual assault of a child; bonds totaling $110,000. Burleson County later abandoned two charges, leaving eight counts for trial.

Jury verdict September 13, 2017: guilty on all eight counts (three counts sexual assault + five counts improper relationship between teacher and student). Jury sentenced her to 10 years per count, all concurrent; parole eligible after 5 years.

NOTE: Teacher's aide (not certified teacher) — access to students through aide role rather than classroom instruction. Cash-grooming cluster: compare Bardfield #46, Rockensies #47, Smith #35.
