Former English teacher at Tyrone Public Schools, Tyrone, Texas County, Oklahoma. Age 27 at arrest (January 2016); approximately 24 at onset of offense.

Engaged in an approximately 2.5-year relationship with a 16-year-old male student that began through Snapchat. Groomed the victim for several months before the relationship became sexual. On December 15, 2015, Tyrone school superintendent contacted Texas County Sheriff Matt Boley after becoming aware of the relationship; teacher was placed on administrative leave. Investigation conducted by Sheriff's Investigator Andrea Walters. Felony arrest warrant issued January 21, 2016.

Torres was residing in Liberal, Kansas at the time. Arrested January 22, 2016 in Seward County, Kansas; declined to waive extradition and was released on a $25,000 fugitive bond. Oklahoma governor's warrant pursued. Torres appeared before District Judge Jon Parsley at Texas County Courthouse on February 11, 2016; bond maintained at $100,000. Torres was allowed to remain free on bond throughout proceedings with conditions prohibiting passport possession and contact with males under 18.

Originally charged with 5 counts of rape in the second degree and 22 counts of indecent exposure. Sentenced October 13, 2017: counts 1–5 at 15 years and $2,000 fine each; counts 6–25 at 10 years with costs only; all counts suspended except five years to serve; credit for time served. Placed on Oklahoma Department of Corrections probation.

NOTE: Incident date is approximate — relationship lasted approximately 2.5 years before December 2015 discovery, placing onset around mid-2013. Sentencing covered 25 counts vs. 27 originally charged; 2 counts appear to have been dismissed or consolidated. Extradition resistance from Kansas is unusual.
