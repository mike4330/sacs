Former teacher at Tipton High School, Tipton, Tillman County, Oklahoma. Age 34 at time of offense. Surrendered teaching license prior to sentencing.

Two male victims, both students. Victim 1 (January 2014): sex in a motel and in Cowan's car. Victim 2 (spring break 2014): sex in her classroom and in her car in the country outside Tipton. Contact with both victims initiated via text messages. Cowan admitted the conduct in court documents.
