Laura Cowan #533 sentenced

StateOklahomaCourtTillman County District Court
Incident Date2014-01-01
Defendant Age34 Victim Age16
Prison Sentence1.0 yr

Former teacher at Tipton High School, Tipton, Tillman County, Oklahoma. Age 34 at time of offense. Surrendered teaching license prior to sentencing.

Two male victims, both students. Victim 1 (January 2014): sex in a motel and in Cowan's car. Victim 2 (spring break 2014): sex in her classroom and in her car in the country outside Tipton. Contact with both victims initiated via text messages. Cowan admitted the conduct in court documents.

Originally charged during the 2014–2015 period; charges were dismissed when authorities could not locate the students to testify. Cowan was re-charged approximately six weeks later after the students were subpoenaed and located.

Entered a blind plea (no plea agreement) to two counts of second-degree rape. Judge imposed two 15-year prison sentences, concurrent, with 14 years of each suspended — leaving 1 year active, to be served in Tillman County Jail. Lifetime sex offender registration ordered.

NOTE: 'Blind plea' means no plea deal; judge set the sentence independently. Two concurrent 15-year sentences effectively reduced to 1 year active by suspending 14 years of each. Charges originally dismissed due to witness unavailability — an unusual procedural sequence. Sex in a classroom is explicitly stated in court documents.
