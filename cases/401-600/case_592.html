Former English teacher at Northridge High School, Northridge Local School District, Licking County, Ohio. Frazeysburg/Newark address at time of offense. Age approximately 38 at time of offense (39 at sentencing, January 2019).

Engaged in a sexual relationship with an 18-year-old male senior at Northridge High School between February and May 2018. Sexual activity took place at Grow's home.

Superintendent Scott Schmidt notified district families of the allegations and stated the school had given full cooperation to the Licking County Sheriff's Office. Grow was placed on leave pending termination, barred from school grounds and contact with students, had email and building access revoked, and a report was filed with the Ohio Department of Education.

Appeared in court on October 6, 2018 on a charge of sexual battery, third-degree felony. Pleaded guilty in 2018. Sentenced January 3, 2019 to two years in prison. Designated a Tier III sex offender.

NOTE: Adult victim (18). Ohio position-of-trust statute applied despite victim being a legal adult. Tier III designation (Ohio's most serious sex offender tier, requiring quarterly lifetime registration) is unusually severe relative to other adult-victim cases in the DB.
