Former teacher's aide in an after-school care program, including supervision of study hall at St. Francis High School, St. Francis, Wisconsin (Milwaukee County). Age 23 at onset of offenses (August 2016). Resigned May 19, 2017.

Two male victims, both age 16, from different high schools. Victim 1 attended Menomonee Falls High School (Waukesha County); Gersonde met him in fall 2015 while picking up his younger brothers. Sexual contact began August 2016; they had intercourse at least nine times at Gersonde's apartment. When Victim 1 attempted to end the relationship, Gersonde continually contacted him via calls and texts. Victim 1 stated he had been a willing participant. Victim 2 attended St. Francis High School (Milwaukee County); Gersonde met him in March 2017 while supervising study hall. They communicated via email and Twitter; sexual contact occurred after a football tryout and a track meet in May 2017. After the investigation began, Gersonde texted Victim 2: 'I know I'm screwed. Maybe jail isn't the worst thing in the world.'

On October 26, 2017, Gersonde entered a no-contest plea to two counts of sexual assault of a student by school staff; four additional charges were dismissed. Sentencing was scheduled for December 2017.

Sentenced December 13, 2017: 2 years in prison, 3 years extended supervision. Gersonde stated at sentencing: "I am wholeheartedly, sincerely remorseful. I take full responsibility for everything that occurred." Gersonde discussed deleting evidence with one of the victims via text message.

NOTE: Gersonde was employed by St. Francis School District March 21–May 19, 2017; resigned via email. Two victims at two different schools (Menomonee Falls HS and St. Francis HS); county field set to Milwaukee per school-county convention. Victim 1 contact: August–October 2016 (at least 9 times). Victim 2 contact: April 29–May 5, 2017.
