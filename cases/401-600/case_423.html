Paraprofessional at Woodmore Local School District, Woodville Township (Sandusky County), Ohio. Worked directly with the victim at school. Defendant age unknown.

One male victim, age 15, autistic with an IQ of 58 and the mental capacity of a 9-year-old. On January 6, 2025 — a school closure day due to inclement weather — Coble allegedly went to the victim's home and raped him. The boy's mother walked in during the assault. Prosecutors noted Coble was not at school that day but allegedly entered the home uninvited; an aggravated burglary specification (R.C. 2909.02(B)) was attached to the rape counts, reflecting the unlawful entry.

First court appearance January 8, 2025; bond set at $100,000. Released on bond January 10, 2025. Grand jury indicted Coble May 16, 2025 on 2 counts of rape and 2 counts of sexual battery. Not guilty plea entered June 2, 2025. Bond revoked December 8, 2025 after Coble was found in violation of bond conditions (which included no contact with victim, victim's family, or the Woodville Public Pool); remanded to custody.

Family retained Ruck & Wright Law Ltd.; attorneys indicated they will pursue civil action against the district after the criminal case resolves.

On May 19, 2026, the court granted the prosecution's motion to amend the indictment as to counts 3 and 4 (sexual battery). Jury trial ran May 20–21, 2026. On May 26, 2026, the jury returned guilty verdicts on all four counts: two counts of rape and two counts of sexual battery. Coble was remanded to the custody of the Sandusky County Sheriff pending sentencing.

Sentenced July 10, 2026. The court imposed a mandatory 8-year term on each of the two rape counts, to run consecutively, for an aggregate mandatory minimum of 16 years and a maximum indefinite term of 20 years under Ohio's Reagan Tokes sentencing law. Coble was found to be a Tier III sex offender and given 217 days of jail time credit. She was remanded to the Ohio Department of Rehabilitation and Correction. Defense counsel Karin Coble filed a notice of appeal on July 20, 2026 and moved for preparation of transcripts at state expense, which the court granted.

NOTE: Victim's disability (autism, IQ 58, mental capacity of 9) is a severe aggravating factor. The aggravated burglary specification on the rape counts elevates the sentencing exposure. Paraprofessional role — not a classroom teacher — illustrates that the DB scope appropriately includes all school-based educator roles. The no-contact bond condition referencing the Woodville Public Pool suggests possible prior off-campus contact there. Case is under appeal as of August 2026; sentencing outcome should be treated as non-final pending disposition of the appeal.
