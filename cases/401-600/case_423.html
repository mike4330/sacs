Christin Coble #423 active

StateOhioCourtSandusky County Court of Common Pleas
Incident Date2025-01-06
Victim Age15

Paraprofessional at Woodmore Local School District, Woodville Township (Sandusky County), Ohio. Worked directly with the victim at school. Defendant age unknown.

One male victim, age 15, autistic with an IQ of 58 and the mental capacity of a 9-year-old. On January 6, 2025 — a school closure day due to inclement weather — Coble allegedly went to the victim's home and raped him. The boy's mother walked in during the assault. Prosecutors noted Coble was not at school that day but allegedly entered the home uninvited; a burglary charge was also under consideration.

First court appearance January 8, 2025; bond set at $100,000. Released on bond January 10, 2025. Grand jury indicted Coble May 16, 2025 on 2 counts of rape and 2 counts of sexual battery. Not guilty plea entered June 2, 2025. Bond revoked December 8, 2025 after Coble was found in violation of bond conditions (which included no contact with victim, victim's family, or the Woodville Public Pool); remanded to custody pending further order.

Family retained Ruck & Wright Law Ltd.; attorneys indicated they will pursue civil action against the district after the criminal case resolves.

NOTE: Victim's disability (autism, IQ 58, mental capacity of 9) is a severe aggravating factor. Paraprofessional role — not a classroom teacher — illustrates that the DB scope appropriately includes all school-based educator roles. Bond revocation reason not specified in public records; no contact with the Woodville Public Pool as a condition suggests possible prior off-campus contact there.
