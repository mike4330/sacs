Former teacher at New Smyrna Beach High School, Volusia County, Florida. Age 28 at time of offense. Married mother of two.

In 2019, began a relationship with a 16-year-old male student that progressed to sexual encounters in her car at a beachfront park. Parris told at least two friends about the relationship: she told one she was having marital troubles and needed attention, and told the other she loved the 16-year-old. A student reported information about the relationship to school authorities in March 2019; Parris was placed on paid administrative leave and did not return to the classroom.

Arrested October 17, 2019 after turning herself in on a judge-signed warrant; booked into Volusia County Branch Jail on $25,000 bond. Pleaded no contest August 11, 2021 to unlawful sexual activity with a minor. Sentenced October 25, 2021 by Judge Raul Zambrano to 2.5 years in prison, 10 years sex offender probation, and sex offender registration. Could have received 5.5–15 years. The victim, who dropped out of school after Parris's arrest and moved out of the area, asked for a lesser sentence. Parris cited undiagnosed bipolar disorder at the time of the offense; told the court she has since received treatment.

NOTE: Investigation initiated by a student tip to school authorities. Victim asked for leniency at sentencing. Encounters occurred off campus (car at beachfront park). Parris told the court she still could not explain 'how mentoring somebody got from here to crossing that line.'
