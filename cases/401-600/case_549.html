Sandra G. Mayfield #549 sentenced

StateOklahomaCourtCleveland County District Court
Incident Date2016-02-27
Defendant Age51 Victim Age17
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former teacher at Moore High School, Moore, Oklahoma (Cleveland County). DA: Greg Mashburn.

Victim was a 17-year-old male student. Late on February 27, 2016, Mayfield and the student drove around the Oklahoma City metro together; surveillance cameras captured them kissing and hugging at multiple locations. In the early morning hours of February 28, Mayfield performed oral sex on the student in the backseat of her vehicle while parked in the northeast corner of Buck Thomas Park.

Moore Public Schools received information about possible improper behavior March 4, 2016. Mayfield was immediately suspended and placed on administrative leave; law enforcement was contacted the same day. Cleveland County DA Greg Mashburn filed one count of forcible sodomy. Bail requested at $20,000 but judge set it at $1,000.

Pleaded no contest June 2, 2017. Sentence: 7 years fully suspended (no prison, no jail).

NOTE: Surveillance video of kissing and hugging in Oklahoma City was cited as key evidence. No-prison outcome on forcible sodomy charge is notable — bail set far below DA's request ($1,000 vs. $20,000 requested) foreshadowed the lenient trajectory.
