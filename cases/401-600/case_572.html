Former health and physical education teacher and volleyball coach at Moon Area High School, Moon Township, Pennsylvania (Allegheny County). Age 26 at time of offense; 27 at sentencing. Married at time of offense; divorce pending at sentencing. Resident of Scott Township (2034 Swallow Hill Road).

Began exchanging text messages with a 14-year-old male freshman in October 2007 after obtaining his cell phone number from a member of her volleyball squad. The relationship escalated over two months; messages included sexually explicit content and nude photographs. Chester also wrote the boy excuses for being late to class and hall passes to allow them extra time together on campus. On December 22, she sent three photos of herself by cellphone, two of them nude; the boy replied with a nude photo of himself. The two also had physical sexual contact, including in the school parking lot.

Parents discovered the text messages and met with Chester on December 26 in Robinson Township; she agreed to resign and they agreed not to press charges. Chester resigned December 27, citing personal reasons. A second teacher later informed the principal that she believed the resignation was related to messages Chester had sent a student. School officials referred the matter to police. Chester was arrested in early January 2008 after confessing to having sex with the boy.

Initially charged with child sexual abuse, statutory sexual assault, and related counts; bail 0,000. At preliminary hearing January 21, 2008, sexual abuse of children charge was withdrawn; five counts added (statutory sexual assault, indecent deviate sexual intercourse, indecent assault, aggravated indecent assault x2) and all waived to Allegheny County Court. Chester sought counseling following arrest.

Pleaded guilty to statutory sexual assault, corruption of a minor, and criminal use of a cell phone. Sentenced August 20, 2008: 1½ to 3 years in prison (Pennsylvania indeterminate). Led away in handcuffs at sentencing, stating 'I know this is the punishment I deserve.'

NOTE: The victim's family had been willing to accept a non-prison sentence until Chester contacted the boy in violation of her bond conditions. That contact changed their position and drove the prison outcome. Prior to this, the parents had kept the matter private on the condition of Chester's resignation — it was a teacher colleague who ultimately triggered the police referral.
