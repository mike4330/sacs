Lindsey P. Anderson #568 sentenced

StateIllinoisCourtKane County Circuit Court
Incident Date2013-07-01
Defendant Age24Victim Age17
Prison Sentence10.0 yrs

Former teacher at Carpentersville Middle School and coach at Dundee-Crown High School, Community Unit School District 300, Carpentersville, Illinois (Kane County). Age approximately 24 at start of offense (July 2013); 26 at first arrest (May 2015); 31 at sentencing. Resident of Geneva, Illinois. Ended employment with District 300 September 29, 2014.

Sexually abused two male students under age 18 between July 2013 and May 2015. The victims were not her students at the time of the abuse but she had previously been their teacher and leveraged that prior relationship to access them. The victims' parents discovered the abuse and contacted East Dundee Police.

First arrested May 15, 2015 on one count of criminal sexual assault (Class 1 felony) and one count of aggravated criminal sexual abuse (Class 2 felony), covering contact with one victim between April 1 and May 5, 2015. Bail set at $100,000; released on $10,000 bond. Conditions: no contact with youth, no proximity within 150 feet of his residence, no unsupervised contact with minors under 18.

Pleaded guilty September 2019 to criminal sexual assault and aggravated criminal sexual abuse (full offense period July 2013–May 2015, both victims). Sentenced January 31, 2020: 10 years in prison; Illinois 85% rule applies — minimum 8.5 years to be served; lifetime sex offender registration; credit for 2 days served. Taken into custody at sentencing; held at Kane County Jail pending transfer to Illinois DOC.

NOTE: 4+ year gap between first arrest (May 2015) and guilty plea (September 2019). Both victims were former students she re-contacted after leaving the classroom — leveraging prior institutional relationships for access. The 85% rule means actual minimum prison time is 8.5 years.
