Brittany Stanek #557 active

StateNebraskaCourtCuming County District Court
Defendant Age34

Former teacher and 'success coordinator' (support staff) at Bancroft-Rosalie Schools, Cuming County, Nebraska. Ten-year employee. Age 35 at time of article (February 2026); approximately 34 at arrest.

On April 30, 2025, the school superintendent alerted the Cuming County Sheriff's Office that an employee may have had a sexual relationship with a student. Stanek had reportedly admitted to other staff that she bought alcohol and tobacco for two students. Text messages between Stanek and the students implied a relationship; both students confirmed she provided alcohol and nicotine pouches, and one admitted to a sexual relationship. Arrested May 1, 2025 at her home; booked into Thurston County Jail (Stanek's county of residence). Victim age not stated in available sources.

The district placed Stanek on administrative leave upon learning of the allegations. Officials noted that in her later 'success coordinator' role she would not have had one-on-one student contact.

Originally charged with first-degree sexual assault of a child and two counts of contributing to the delinquency of a child. Plea entered February 2026: guilty to first-degree sexual assault (amended from 'of a child'); two contributing counts dismissed. Sentencing set for April 2, 2026.

NOTE: Charge was amended downward from 'sexual assault of a child' to 'sexual assault' — significant reduction, as Nebraska law distinguishes victim age in the statutory framework. Victim age not stated in available sources; the amendment may reflect the victim's actual age above the child-specific threshold, or a negotiated reduction.
