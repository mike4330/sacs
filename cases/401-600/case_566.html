Carrie Smith Perkins #566 sentenced

StateAlabamaCourtHouston County Circuit Court
JudgeButch Binford Incident Date2009-12-01
Defendant Age29 Victim Age15
Prison Sentence14.0 yrs

Former 5th grade teacher at Parkview Christian School, Eufaula, Alabama (Barbour County). Also previously the same student's 7th grade science teacher. Age 29 at arrest. Resident of Eufaula.

Conducted a five-month sexual relationship with a 15-year-old male student (9th grade). The victim told his parents, then police, that he had sexual intercourse and oral sex with Perkins approximately 4–5 times over five months. Some incidents occurred on school property (reportedly not during school hours). Perkins also brought the victim to Dothan (Houston County), where additional crimes were committed.

Arrested by Eufaula police in May 2010; released on bond, then re-arrested on additional charges the same week. Originally charged in Barbour County with 4 counts rape and 4 counts sodomy. Prosecution was pursued in Houston County based on the Dothan incidents. Convicted by a Houston County jury of rape and sodomy on January 14, 2011; defense attorney Susan James (Montgomery); prosecutor Assistant Houston County DA Banks Smith. Sentenced February 23, 2011 by Judge Butch Binford: 14 years in prison, no parole.

NOTE: Prosecution in Houston County (Dothan) rather than Barbour County (Eufaula/school location) because the Dothan incidents formed the basis of indictment — a venue quirk. Perkins was the victim's educator across two grade levels (7th and 9th), suggesting a long-standing school relationship before the offense. Private Christian school; principal stated publicly he had no idea and called it 'a shock.' School's post-arrest response included new door locks and upgraded digital security cameras.
