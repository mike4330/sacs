Sonya Ann Wilks Bailey #496 closed

StateAlabamaCourtEtowah County Circuit Court
JudgeSonny SteenIncident Date2017-01-01
Defendant Age51Victim Age14
Prison Sentence20.0 yrs

Former computer technology instructor and webmaster at West End High School, Etowah County, Alabama. Age 51 at arrest (August 2017).

Engaged in sexual acts with a 14-year-old student and sent the student sexually explicit photographs while employed with the Etowah County Board of Education. Investigation conducted by the Etowah County Sheriff's Office. Arrested August 28, 2017; the Etowah County School Board held an emergency meeting the same evening to accept Bailey's resignation. Bond set at $60,000; as a condition of bond, Bailey was required to report to Community Corrections and have no contact with the victim or victim's family.

Charged in August 2017 with second-degree rape, second-degree sodomy, and school employee engaging in a sex act with a student, all felonies. Entered a blind plea in October 2022, pleading guilty to all three charges. Sentenced December 9, 2022 by Judge Sonny Steen to 20 years in prison — the maximum sentence on each count, to be served concurrently. Prosecution by Etowah County Deputy District Attorney Brynn Crain.

NOTE: Five-year gap between arrest (2017) and sentencing (2022). Blind plea means no plea agreement — judge determined the sentence. Prosecutor Crain described the maximum sentence as precedent-setting for the community. The article notes Bailey was the third Etowah County educator charged for inappropriate contacts with students in the two years preceding her August 2017 arrest; one of the others was Andrea Elliot Means (not yet in DB), arrested January 2016 for alleged offense in March 2013.
