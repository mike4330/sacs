Jenee A. Blackert #464 sentenced

StateIllinoisCourtBoone County Circuit Court
Incident Date2013-03-01
Defendant Age30 Victim Age17
Prison Sentence4.0 yrs

Former social studies teacher at Belvidere North High School, Belvidere, Illinois (Boone County). Age 30 at time of offense (2013). Resided in Poplar Grove. Had worked for the Belvidere School District since 2008.

One female victim, age 17. Four alleged encounters, all off school grounds: two between March 1 and May 16, 2013, and two on June 28, 2013. The victim described Blackert as her favorite teacher and someone she looked up to and trusted. School district superintendent placed Blackert on unpaid leave following her August 2013 arrest. The Belvidere School Board accepted her resignation effective September 23, 2013.

Arrested August 9, 2013. Originally charged with four counts of criminal sexual assault (Class 1 felony, 4–15 years, not eligible for probation). Pleaded guilty to one count; three charges dropped by the state. Sentenced October 20, 2014 to 4 years in prison. Required to register as a sex offender. Following completion of the prison sentence, Blackert was placed on parole for a minimum of 3 years, with the DOC retaining authority to extend supervision for the rest of her life based on behavior during the initial parole period. Prosecuted by Boone County Deputy State's Attorney Victor Escarcida.

The victim wrote a letter to Blackert, released by the State's Attorney after sentencing: "You were my favorite teacher; one of the adults I really looked up to and trusted. You totally warped my thinking and made me someone I wasn't ... I am so thankful we got caught, and I am also proud of myself for having the courage to tell the truth to the cops and detectives. I really hope you learn from this and choose to never prey on anyone else ever again ... At the end of all of this, it is your life that is ruined, not mine."

NOTE: Charge reduction from 4 counts to 1 via plea. Post-release supervision potentially lifelong under Illinois DOC discretion.
