Sonya Horn #528 sentenced

StateOhioCourtLicking County Court of Common Pleas
JudgeThomas MarcelainIncident Date2011-06-01
Defendant Age30Victim Age18
Prison Sentence2.2 yrs

Former intervention specialist (special education teacher) at Johnstown High School, Johnstown, Licking County, Ohio. Worked for Johnstown-Monroe Local Schools since 2008. Age 30 at time of offense.

Victim was an 18-year-old male student with developmental disabilities attending summer school. Assaults occurred on school property and at a Johnstown residence between June 1 and October 1, 2011. The victim reported the assault to police and the school the following fall. Horn was excused from duty and subsequently provided false records to authorities to hinder the investigation.

Indicted May 11, 2012, on 8 counts of sexual battery (F3) and 1 count of tampering with evidence (F3). Pleaded guilty in November 2012 to tampering with evidence; entered an Alford plea to 4 counts of sexual battery (reduced from 8 counts). Ohio revoked Horn's teaching license January 12, 2013, days before sentencing.

Sentenced mid-January 2013 to 4 years in prison. On March 12, 2015, a judge granted Horn's request to suspend the last 22 months of her sentence, putting her on probation after approximately 26 months served (per the plea agreement condition that prosecutors would not oppose early release). Probation conditions: no internet access, no unsupervised contact with minors.

NOTE: Victim was an adult (18) with developmental disabilities — qualifies under Ohio's sexual battery statute because of the institutional relationship. Evidence-tampering charge (false records) alongside the sexual offenses is unusual. Early release built into plea deal — effective time served ~2.2 years of 4-year sentence.
