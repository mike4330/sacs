Victim Age15
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former eighth-grade earth science teacher at Gatesville Junior High School, Gatesville ISD, Gatesville, Coryell County, Texas. Defendant age not stated in available sources.

Beginning approximately January 2023, engaged in sexually explicit messaging with a 15-year-old male high school student via Instagram, including sending nude photographs of herself and soliciting nude photographs from the student. A second victim, a 17-year-old former student who had since moved to Ohio, received nude photographs from Cockrell via Instagram between March 5, 2023 and July 30, 2023. Explicit messaging with the Texas victim continued through at least October 2023. No allegations of physical contact with either victim. Cockrell admitted to school administrators that she had messaged students on Instagram but could not recall whether she had sent breast photographs. When the Ohio victim told Cockrell he was still a high school student, she replied 'no one has to know.'

On April 11, 2024, Gatesville ISD was notified of the inappropriate communications; Cockrell was placed on administrative leave and subsequently resigned. Warrants for two counts of improper relationship between educator and student issued May 2, 2024; arrested by Gatesville ISD Police on May 3, 2024. Original charges at arrest were indecency with a child and possession of child pornography with intent to distribute. The Coryell County DA's Office was recused; case prosecuted by Lampasas County Attorney Jessica Hanes Guy in 52nd District Court.

Pleaded guilty to two counts of improper relationship between educator and student (electronic communication). Sentenced September 9, 2025 to eight years probation; no prison. No sex offender registration required — improper relationship between educator and student is not a Chapter 62 registration offense in Texas. Teaching certificate permanently surrendered.

NOTE: Charge reduction from indecency with a child and CSAM charges to two counts of improper relationship is significant given the explicit photograph conduct. Texas law on sex offender registration explicitly excludes this offense, creating a sentencing anomaly relative to the underlying conduct. Two-victim case spanning Texas and Ohio.
