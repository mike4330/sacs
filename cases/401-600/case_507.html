Rochelle L. Cressman #507 closed

State Pennsylvania
Court Crawford County Court of Common Pleas
Judge Mark Stevens
Incident Date 2018-09-01
Defendant Age 32
Victim Age 14
Prison Sentence 5.0 yrs

Former middle school teacher at Titusville Area School District, Titusville, Crawford County, Pennsylvania (resided in Oil Creek Township). Age 32 at arrest (May 2019).

Engaged in a sexual relationship with a 14-year-old male student from September 2018 through approximately April 2019, with sexual contact approximately twice a week at multiple locations. No contact was alleged to have occurred on school property. Cressman repeatedly threatened the victim not to disclose the relationship, telling him she would harm herself and him if anyone found out.

The investigation was launched after the boy revealed the relationship. Arrested May 8, 2019; arraigned before District Justice Rita Marwood and taken to Crawford County Jail after failing to post $75,000 bail. Originally charged with 10 counts of statutory sexual assault, 10 counts of IDSI (person less than 16), unlawful contact with a minor, endangering the welfare of children, corruption of minors, 42 counts of indecent assault, and terroristic threats.

Pleaded guilty in November 2020 to three first-degree felony counts of statutory sexual assault; all other charges dismissed. Sentenced February 16, 2021 by Judge Mark Stevens to consecutive terms of 3–6 years and 2–4 years on counts one and two (aggregate 5–10 years), with a concurrent 3–6 year term on the third count; plus 10 years of probation.

NOTE: Threats to harm herself and the victim as a coercive concealment strategy is unusual in this DB — see also terroristic threats original charge. Charge reduction from 60+ counts (including 42 indecent assault and 10 IDSI counts) to 3 statutory sexual assault counts at plea is substantial.
