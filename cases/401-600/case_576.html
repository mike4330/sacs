Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former social studies teacher at Middle School/Intermediate School 8 (MS/IS 8), Jamaica, Queens, New York (Queens County). Age 28 at time of offense and sentencing. Resident of West Babylon, Long Island (later Bellport, L.I.).

Had sexual intercourse with a 14-year-old male student on seven occasions in an empty second-floor classroom at the school between April 13 and May 14, 2009, all between 3:00 p.m. and 6:00 p.m. (after school hours). Also exchanged hundreds of calls and text messages with the student; the final message to him read 'Erase your phone.'

The victim's mother, a member of the school's Parent Teacher Association, was tipped off about the relationship. She inspected her son's cell phone and found hundreds of calls and texts from Weber. The family reported the matter.

Pleaded guilty August 4, 2009 to second-degree rape. Sentenced the same day: time served, 10 years probation, 20-year sex offender registration, surrender of teaching license, mandatory sex offender treatment, and barred from social networking sites frequented by minors. Cried during the hearing. Defense attorney Donald Vogelman attributed the behavior to a dysfunctional upbringing. Victim's family agreed to the plea deal to avoid requiring the teenager to testify in a lengthy trial.

Civil suit filed August 2010 by the victim and his mother in Queens Supreme Court against Weber and the New York City Department of Education seeking unspecified damages for physical and psychological injuries. The suit alleged abuse between January and May 2009.

After the criminal case, Weber joined the U.S. Air Force and was training to become a nurse.

NOTE: All seven sexual encounters occurred on school grounds in a second-floor classroom, during after-school hours. The 'Erase your phone' text message is a documented concealment act. No-prison outcome (time served + probation) on a second-degree rape guilty plea involving a 14-year-old victim.
