Former substitute teacher at St. Cloud Middle School, St. Cloud High School, and Harmony High School, Osceola County, Florida. Age 34–35 at time of offense. Full name Sara Beth Moore.

One male victim, age 14, who was a friend of Moore's children. Victim met Moore at school and gained access through her family. Moore had up to 25 sexual encounters with the victim. She would pick the victim up from his home even after his family had told her he was not allowed to leave. The victim accompanied Moore's family on a vacation to California. Investigation triggered when the victim's family found explicit messages on his Instagram account and a box of condoms in his bedroom.

Originally charged with 10 counts of sexual battery on a minor. Re-arrested January 13, 2016 for tampering with physical evidence after going to a Verizon store to inquire how to remotely delete data from her cell phone, which had been designated as evidence; the phone was subsequently found with a new passcode preventing law enforcement access. Held without bail following re-arrest.

Plea entered June 29, 2017: guilty to five counts of lewd or lascivious battery (F2, FS 800.04(4)(a)) and one count of tampering with physical evidence (F3, FS 918.13). Counts 6–7 nolle prosequi. Sentenced August 11, 2017: 10 years DOC (181 days credit for time served), community control, 10 months probation; all counts concurrent. Designated a Sexual Offender. Defense witnesses at sentencing included family members; victim's father's letter read in open court by the State.

NOTE: Re-arrest for evidence tampering is distinctive — the Verizon store reported her inquiry to police. Charge reduction from sexual battery to lewd/lascivious battery (F2) significantly lowered sentencing exposure.
