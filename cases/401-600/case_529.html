Former English teacher at Lincoln High School, Tallahassee, Leon County, Florida. Crawfordville resident. Age 27 at time of offense and arrest.

Victim was a 15-year-old girl. In 2021, Webb exchanged nearly 9,000 text messages with the victim, many of a sexual nature; some were sent during school hours. Webb was placed on administrative leave by Leon County School District in late December 2021 after police began investigating.

Arrested late December 2021 on 24 counts of soliciting a minor using a computer. School district terminated her employment during the winter break and referred the matter to the Florida Department of Education Office of Professional Practices.

Entered a no-contest plea to charges related to the sexting conduct. Sentenced September 2025 to 8 years in prison plus 7 years sex offender probation. Arrived at Florida Women's Reception Center, Marion County, September 29, 2025. Court records show Webb is appealing the sentence.

NOTE: Female victim — one of few female-victim cases in the DB. Offense was entirely electronic (text messages); no physical sexual contact charged. 9,000 messages is an exceptionally large volume of documented contact.
