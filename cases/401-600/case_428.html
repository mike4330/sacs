Christina Eve Oliver #428 sentenced

StateCaliforniaCourtSan Joaquin County Superior Court
JudgeFranklin StephensonIncident Date2009-01-30
Defendant Age24Victim Age16
Prison Sentence0.41 yrs

Former sophomore English teacher at McNair High School, Stockton, San Joaquin County, California (Lodi Unified School District). Hired July 2007; in her second year of teaching at arrest. Victim was a 16-year-old male student in her sophomore English class. Defendant age 24 at time of offense.

Relationship involved multiple sexual encounters between January 30 and February 10, 2009. Father of victim contacted police after learning of the relationship. Oliver was arrested February 10, 2009 near the victim's home — apparently en route to meet him again.

Pled guilty June 2010 to four counts (two counts unlawful sex with a minor, two counts oral copulation). Sentenced November 1, 2010 by Judge Franklin Stephenson to 150 days San Joaquin County Jail (to begin May 16, 2011 after spring semester) plus 5 years probation. Judge declined to require sex offender registration, citing victim's age (16, not under 16) and defense expert reports that Oliver had no predisposition for such conduct. Defense argued the relationship was an aberration; Oliver had a master's degree and was enrolled in law school at time of sentencing.

Teaching credential suspended by California Commission on Teacher Credentialing. Deputy DA had argued for state prison and mandatory registration; judge rejected both.

NOTE: No sex offender registration despite four counts and multiple encounters — judge's discretion under California law (registration not mandatory when victim is 16 and no force used). Oliver was on her way to meet the victim again when arrested, undermining the defense's aberration argument.
