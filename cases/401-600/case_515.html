Former substitute teacher at a St. Paul public charter school (middle school), St. Paul, Ramsey County, Minnesota. Age 24 at time of offense (February 2024).

Communicated with a 17-year-old male student via messenger apps and purchased items for him and his friends. In February 2024, directed the student to meet her in an empty middle school classroom where she was substitute teaching, then performed sexual acts on him. She subsequently solicited him for additional encounters, which he declined. The school's executive director was aware she had pulled the boy from class without reason or authority, and there had been prior complaints about her inappropriate behavior with students. When called to meet with a school principal, Thao immediately resigned on February 26, 2024.

Thao's husband discovered the conduct and informed the victim's parents on March 8, 2024. On March 9, Thao completed a child maltreatment form at Regions Hospital, self-reporting the sexual relationship with the student.

Charged May 2024 with one count of third-degree criminal sexual conduct. Pleaded guilty July 2024 to an amended charge of fourth-degree criminal sexual conduct in exchange for a lesser sentence. Sentenced September 10, 2024: five years probation; 160 hours community work service; predatory offender registration; no contact with minors or vulnerable adults; may not hold a position of power over minors; no contact with victim.

NOTE: Husband-reported to victim's parents; defendant self-reported at hospital two days later. Prior complaints of inappropriate behavior with students known to school administration but not acted upon before the sexual offense. On-campus offense (empty classroom).
