Former social studies teacher at The New School for Leadership and the Arts, Kingsbridge (Bronx), New York. Age 29 at time of offense. Married to a Rockland County sheriff's deputy; had been teaching since 2014; also established the school's track team.

One male victim, age 14, believed to be one of her students. Performed oral sex on the victim on multiple occasions and in multiple locations; first known incident November 1, 2017. A fellow teacher witnessed Myers massaging the victim. Victim confided in a classmate who notified the school principal; police were alerted.

Arrested January 19, 2018 at the site of the offense; charged with criminal sexual act in the second degree (D felony) and endangering the welfare of a child. Released on own recognizance (prosecutors had requested $50,000 bail). Initially pled not guilty; attorney stated "it just takes one person's accusation to make an arrest." Pled guilty August 1, 2018 to criminal sexual act (D felony) before Justice Michael Orbus in Manhattan Supreme Court. Formal sentencing September 12, 2018: 10 years probation; sex offender registration required; fired by NYC Department of Education. Teaching license not ordered surrendered at defense request.

NOTE: Prosecutors requested 2 years prison and license surrender; Judge Orbus declined both, citing no prior history. Defense argument for license retention — "she still is a talented teacher" with the possibility of teaching adults — drew scrutiny given offense involved a 14-year-old student. No-prison outcome on multiple acts of oral sex with a 14-year-old is notable. Myers deactivated social media after photos in provocative-slogan T-shirts surfaced publicly.
