Alberta Fern Padilla #459 sentenced

StateTexasCourtBexar County District Court
Incident Date2016-06-01
Defendant Age51 Victim Age17
Prison Sentence3.0 yrs

Former math teacher and case manager at Claudia Taylor Johnson High School, North East ISD, San Antonio, Texas (Bexar County). Age 51 at time of offense (June 2016). Also tutored the victim in math at a local public library.

One male victim, age 17. Padilla was the victim's teacher, case manager, and tutor. In June 2016 she allowed the victim to move in with her and began developing romantic feelings; she provided him housing, food, and clothing. Sexual relationship began at that time and continued approximately 15–20 times over the following year, including in a public park off West Avenue. The last encounter was the week of August 20, 2017. Both Padilla and the victim told detectives they had feelings for each other. The victim allegedly attempted to intimidate Padilla's relatives when they suspected the relationship and threatened to report it.

NEISD placed Padilla on administrative leave when allegations surfaced and proposed termination to the school board. Arrested approximately August 31, 2017; held on $20,000 bond. Entered a plea of nolo contendere to one count of improper relationship between an educator and student (Texas Penal Code). Sentenced October 8, 2018: 3 years TDCJ-ID, 403 days jail credit, $2,000 fine, no-contact order with victim (identified in court records as Mathew Rodriguez); concurrent with prior sentence. Plea agreement capped sentence at 10 years.

NOTE: Grooming vector was dependency-based — housing, food, and clothing — rather than gifts or social media. At age 51–52, Padilla is among the older defendants in this DB.
