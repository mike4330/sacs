Former science teacher at Stambaugh Middle School, Auburndale (Polk County), Florida. Age 31 at time of offense. Had taught approximately 10 years, beginning at Lake Alfred Middle School.

Four male victims, ages 14–16. Sexual activity occurred from June through August 2007 at Jones's Auburndale apartment. Jones reconnected with two former students via MySpace; they introduced her to two additional boys. She messaged one initial contact: "Hey baby. I just miss my boys. Ya'll need to come see your momma soon." She met the boys at locations in Winter Haven and drove them to her apartment. Sexual activity included simultaneous encounters with two of the victims (brothers); one victim estimated at least eight separate encounters. The relationship ended when one boy grew tired of contact and cut it off.

School district placed Jones on paid administrative leave in May 2008 after learning of potential misconduct; one victim told a teacher that month and the Polk County Sheriff's Office opened an investigation. Arrested June 5, 2008.

Convicted by jury August 20, 2009 of 5 counts of lewd battery and 3 counts of unlawful sexual activity with a minor. Faced a possible life sentence. Sentenced October 20, 2009 by Circuit Judge Donald Jacobsen to 10 years prison and 15 years sex offender probation.

NOTE: Judge explicitly cited the boys' willingness as a factor in departing below the maximum. Three of the four victims' families filed a civil lawsuit against the Polk County School District. MySpace was the initial contact vector.
