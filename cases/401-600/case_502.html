Sarah Jones #502 closed

State Kentucky
Court Kenton County Circuit Court
Judge Patricia Summe
Incident Date 2011-02-01
Defendant Age 26
Victim Age 17
Prison Sentence 0.0 yrs

Former freshman English teacher at Dixie Heights High School, Crestview Hills, Kenton County, Kentucky, and former Cincinnati Bengals Ben-Gals cheerleader. Approximately age 26 at onset of offense (February 2011).

The victim, male, had been in Jones' freshman English class in 2008, then served as her peer tutee in 2010–2011. A sexual relationship began in February 2011 when the victim was 17. The relationship was discovered in November 2011; Jones resigned. A grand jury indicted Jones in March 2012 on felony charges. Jones pleaded not guilty in April 2012.

Pleaded guilty October 8, 2012 in Kenton County Circuit Court before Judge Patricia Summe to sexual misconduct (misdemeanor) and custodial interference (Class D felony); original felony charges of first-degree sexual abuse and unlawful use of electronic means to induce a minor were dismissed. Sentenced the same day to a 2-year suspended sentence with a 5-year diversion program; no jail, no prison, no sex offender registration. Permanently barred from teaching. Text messages between Jones and the victim were sealed by the court as part of the plea deal.

On the same date, Jones' mother, Cheryl Armstrong Jones (a former school principal), pleaded guilty to misdemeanor attempted tampering with evidence for texting the victim directing him to discard his phone.

Jones later became engaged to the victim (reported June 2013).

NOTE: Charge reduction from felony sexual abuse to misdemeanor sexual misconduct and Class D custodial interference is substantial. No sex offender registration. The case received extensive national media attention due to Jones' profile as a former NFL cheerleader. Mother's evidence-tampering plea is rare collateral fact in this DB.
