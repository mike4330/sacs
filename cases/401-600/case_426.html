Former reading and special education teacher and assistant volleyball coach at Jay High School, Jay, Santa Rosa County, Florida. Defendant age approximately 29 at time of offense. Victim age unknown (male high school students; age not specified in reporting).

Sexual activity with male students occurred at Burkett's residence and Watson's residence between July and October 2007. Three victims total across both cases.
