Kimberly Larkin #526 sentenced

Former teacher at Paradise School for Boys, Paradise Township, York County, Pennsylvania (along West Canal Road). Employed by Lincoln Intermediate Unit No. 12 and assigned to Paradise School; not a direct employee of the school or the Roman Catholic Diocese of Harrisburg that runs it. Paradise School is a residential treatment facility for boys ages 12–17 who have gone through the juvenile probation system. Age 38 at time of offense.

Victim was a 16-year-old male student (or recently departed student — disputed). The school maintained the victim had successfully completed the program at the end of the 2009–2010 school year and been placed in a foster home before the offenses. The victim testified at trial, however, that he was still a student when the relationship began. The two had sex at least five times — in Larkin's car, at her ex-spouse's home, and at motels. Larkin provided the victim with alcohol and marijuana and gave him a cell phone to facilitate frequent contact.

Arrested December 16, 2010. Tried April 2012: acquitted of institutional sexual assault (3rd degree felony) — the school-student relationship element was disputed — but convicted of corruption of minors and furnishing alcohol to a minor (both misdemeanors). Corruption of minors conviction carries a lifetime ban from teaching.

Sentenced July 16, 2012, by Judge Craig Trebilcock to 6–12 months in prison — the maximum penalty allowed for the misdemeanor convictions. Both the prosecutor (who requested probation only) and the defense (who requested no prison) were surprised by the sentence. Judge described Larkin's actions as 'despicable,' said she was 'a huge discredit to her profession,' and cited the chilling effect such conduct has on all teachers. Larkin claimed at sentencing that the student had manipulated her, and sobbed in court.

NOTE: Behavioral-health residential facility, not a traditional school. Acquitted of felony but received maximum misdemeanor sentence — an unusual outcome where judge exceeded what prosecution requested. Two teen-aged children. The marijuana provision is uncommon in this DB.
