Amanda Ludwig #520 sentenced

StateIllinoisCourtPeoria County Circuit Court
JudgeKevin Lyons Incident Date2012-08-04
Defendant Age27 Victim Age15
Prison Sentence2.5 yrs

Former art teacher at Manual Academy (Manual High School), Peoria, Illinois. Had taught there since August 2009. Age 27 at start of offense conduct.

Victim was a 15-year-old male student. Ludwig had been his art teacher the prior year; at the time of the offense she was helping him with school work, not assigned as his teacher. Text message contact began approximately August 4, 2012, and escalated to sexually explicit messages over several months. The two had sex in a classroom at Manual on January 28, 2013.

Discovered when another student told district police the victim had bragged about the relationship and showed a video of Ludwig on his cellphone. Ludwig allegedly admitted to sexting the boy and then having sex with him.

Charged federally on February 4, 2013, with enticement of a minor (10 years to life). Indicted by federal grand jury February 21, 2013; appeared before U.S. District Judge Michael M. Mihm and released to third-party custodian under home incarceration. Defense attorney Thomas Penn Jr. prosecuted by AUSA Thomas A. Keith (federal).

Waived indictment and entered a guilty plea in Peoria County Circuit Court to one count of aggravated criminal sexual abuse. Sentenced August 1, 2013, to 5 years in prison (middle of 3–7 year range); defense attorney indicated the pending federal enticement charge was expected to be dismissed as a result of the state plea. With day-for-day good-time credit, effective prison term approximately 2.5 years.

NOTE: Federal charge (10 years–life) dropped in exchange for state plea — significant charge reduction. Sex in a classroom; video evidence. The federal nexus was the text messages involving interstate commerce.
