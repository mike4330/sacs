Kristina Rhoden Hartless #439 sentenced

StateFloridaCourtFourth Judicial Circuit Court, Duval County
JudgeThomas Beverly Incident Date2010-06-01
Defendant Age39 Victim Age16
Prison Sentence1.08 yrs

Former 5th-grade teacher at Beauclerc Elementary School, Jacksonville, Duval County, Florida. Also served as youth counselor at her church, where the relationship with the victim originated. Victim was a male student, age 16 or 17 (per conviction charge). Defendant age 39 at time of offense (DOB 08/02/1971).

Relationship involved kissing, texting, and Facebook chats over several months in 2010. Both Hartless and the victim admitted to the sexual relationship. Investigation began when the victim's mother contacted police about an inappropriate relationship between her son and his church youth counselor. Original arrest charge was capital sexual battery on a child older than 12.

Pled guilty September 29, 2011 in Fourth Judicial Circuit Court, Duval County (Judge Thomas Beverly; case 16-2010-CF-013813). Conviction charge: unlawful sexual activity with certain minors 16/17 years old (F.S. 794.05, 2nd degree felony). Sentenced to 13 months state prison, 5 years probation, sex offender registration. Jail credit: 2 days. Judge handwrote a recommendation that defendant's own children be allowed to visit her while incarcerated.

Released by at least January 2020 per FDLE registration (status: Released — Subject to Registration).

NOTE: Charge reduction from capital sexual battery to unlawful sexual activity with 16/17-year-old. Dual-role access: school teacher and church youth counselor. The relationship was initially reported through the church context, not the school.
