First-year teacher at Conestoga High School, Berwyn, Tredyffrin/Easttown School District, Chester County, Pennsylvania, where she served as an academic support teacher for grades 9–12. Previously taught at Hillside Elementary School (2019–2024) and worked as a paraprofessional at Hillside and Valley Forge Elementary Schools (2014–2018). Also held an adjunct position teaching Italian at Immaculata University. Age 35 at arrest.

Beginning in February 2025, Mercogliano initiated surreptitious communication with a 16-year-old male student via Snapchat, and a sexual relationship followed. Sex acts occurred approximately twelve times over several months, with most incidents at her parents' home in Malvern. She provided marijuana to the student in connection with the encounters. She was placed on administrative leave upon arrest and lost access to Tredyffrin/Easttown schools.

Arrested April 30, 2025 by Tredyffrin Township Police Department following an investigation by the Chester County District Attorney's Office. Initially charged with sixty-three counts including multiple counts each of institutional sexual assault, unlawful contact with a minor, and endangering the welfare of children. Chester County District Attorney Christopher L. de Barrena-Sarobe said she had 'destroyed the trust' of parents and students at the school; Tredyffrin Township Police Chief Michael Beaty called her conduct 'calculated' and 'shocking and disturbing.'

On May 1, 2026 Mercogliano pleaded guilty to two counts of institutional sexual assault and one count of corruption of minors, with the remaining charges resolved through the plea. The same day she was sentenced to a minimum of fifteen months in Chester County Prison followed by at least eighty months of probation, and classified as a Tier II sex offender. As part of the plea she admitted to having sex with the student and to providing the student marijuana. Mercogliano surrendered her teaching license before entering the plea and is barred from working with children while on probation.
