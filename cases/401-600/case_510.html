First-year teacher at Conestoga High School, Berwyn, Tredyffrin/Easttown School District, Chester County, Pennsylvania. Previously taught at Hillside Elementary School (2019–2024) and served as a paraprofessional at Hillside and Valley Forge Elementary Schools (2014–2018). Age 35 at arrest (April 30, 2025).

Beginning in February 2025, initiated surreptitious communication with a 16-year-old male student via Snapchat. A sexual relationship followed, with sex acts occurring approximately 12 times over several months, many at Mercogliano's parent's home in Wayne. Arrested April 30, 2025 by Tredyffrin Township Police Department following an investigation by the Chester County District Attorney's Office. Faces 63 charges including multiple counts each of sexual contact with a student, unlawful contact with a minor, and endangering the welfare of children.

Chester County DA Christopher L. de Barrena-Sarobe: she 'destroyed the trust' of parents and students at the school. Tredyffrin Township Police Chief Michael Beaty called her actions 'calculated' and 'shocking and disturbing.'
