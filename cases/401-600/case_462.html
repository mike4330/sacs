Former American Sign Language teacher at Hagerty High School, Oviedo, Florida (Seminole County), where she served as a long-term substitute from November 2015 to May 2016. Age 28 at time of offense (March 2016). Had previously worked as an instructional assistant and then teacher at Lyman High School (2005–2013), and as Coordinator for Mentored and Pastoral Counseling at Asbury Theological Seminary (2008–2013). Was employed part-time as an ASL teacher at Lake Howell High School at the time of her arrest.

One female victim, age 15, a student at Hagerty High School. Sexual relationship began in March 2016 and lasted approximately two months; 5–10 sexual encounters occurred in a school classroom. The victim disclosed the relationship to investigators in October 2017. Truman cooperated with investigators in a recorded interview and turned herself in.

School district placed Truman on administrative leave October 2, 2017 upon notification by child protective services; she resigned later that month. Arrested October 19, 2017; booked into John E. Polk Correctional Facility, Sanford; bond set at $40,000. Investigators noted concern about possible additional victims. Truman had no prior criminal history or employee misconduct.

Pleaded guilty to two counts of lewd and lascivious battery on a minor (ages 12–16). Sentenced May 2018: 15 years probation, sex offender registration; no incarceration. Defense attorney Jeff Levkulich.

NOTE: Offense occurred at Hagerty High School but Truman was employed at Lake Howell High at time of arrest. Reported as the 5th Hagerty High teacher arrested since 2014.
