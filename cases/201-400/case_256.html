Former teacher at Rufus King High School (a north side magnet school), Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Age 40 at sentencing. Known to students as 'Mrs. A.' Employed at Rufus King since the 2013–14 school year, teaching since 2008.

Four male student athletes on the basketball team were victims, ages 16–18 during the 2024–25 school year. Misconduct included sexually explicit text messages, offering a sexual favor to one student (declined), caressing a student's inner thigh during class, sending over $700 via Cash App as leverage, attempting to sit on a student's lap, requesting phone numbers and GPS locations, and late-night texting about a 'weird feeling.' One student reported she apologized for an explicit text but then offered a sex act; another said she responded 'I'm an affectionate person' when told her touching was unwelcome.

Students reported to a school resource officer on April 15, 2025. Allemang-Reinke learned of charges from news media and turned herself in; she spent four days in custody and consented to a phone search by Milwaukee PD. Charged April 21, 2025 with four felony counts of sexual misconduct by school staff or volunteer.

Pled guilty July 15, 2025 to one felony count of sexual misconduct by school staff and one misdemeanor count of disorderly conduct. Two additional felony sexual misconduct counts dismissed but read into the record for sentencing. Teaching license forfeited as part of the plea. Sentenced to 18 months initial confinement plus 2 years extended supervision (felony) and 90 days in Community Reintegration Center (misdemeanor), both stayed. Placed on 24 months probation with conditions: sex offender assessment, no unsupervised contact with minors under 18 except relatives, no contact with victims or Rufus King HS, must maintain employment. Restitution of $3,004 to three victims and relatives.

NOTE: FOX6 reported 'six months jail with Huber release' but Milwaukee Journal Sentinel (more detailed) states sentences were stayed; sentence_years set to 0 accordingly. Allemang-Reinke stated during sentencing that she 'had an issue with boundaries' and that students 'seem to have more social needs this past year' — partial-minimization framing.
