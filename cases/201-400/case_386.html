Former teacher at Pocola High School, Pocola, Leflore County, Oklahoma (rural area near Arkansas border). Previously employed at Smithville Public Schools. Married at time of offense.

Victims were three male students at Pocola High School, ages 16-19. Two were minors (victims of second-degree rape in separate incidents between January 2014 and November 2016); the third was an adult student (victim of sexual battery in a separate incident). Under Oklahoma law, teachers may not have sexual contact with students regardless of age.

An investigation began in late 2016 after police received a tip alleging inappropriate conduct with multiple students. Barnes was suspended; her contract was not renewed and her Oklahoma teaching certificate lapsed.

Grand jury indicted Barnes in Leflore County in May 2019 on two counts of second-degree rape and one count of sexual battery; warrant issued and bond set at $250,000. Pleaded guilty December 3, 2019 to all three counts. Sentenced to 40 years total: two 15-year rape sentences running concurrently, plus a 10-year sexual battery sentence suspended as part of probation terms. Expected to serve 15 years at Mabel Bassett Correctional Center, McCloud, Oklahoma. Required to register as a sex offender.

Prior record: In 2010, while at Smithville Public Schools, a student under 16 reported Barnes had propositioned him for sex over months and sent him nude photos; he told police he feared she was 'stalking him.' Barnes pleaded guilty to outraging public decency involving a minor, receiving a 9-month suspended sentence and fine. Approximately 2012: pleaded guilty to violating a protection order (no jail time). She continued teaching and offending for approximately 6 more years after the 2010 conviction.

Husband Clayton Barnes filed for divorce December 1, 2021; consent decree of divorce entered September 26, 2022 (Leflore County case FD-2021-00186).

NOTE: Three victims across the same school (at least); prior conviction at a different school in 2010 did not end her teaching career.
