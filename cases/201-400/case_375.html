State North Carolina
Court Cleveland County Superior Court
Judge James W. Morgan
Incident Date 2018-06-01
Defendant Age 24
Prison Sentence 0.0 yrs

Former math teacher at Crest High School, Cleveland County, North Carolina. Hired August 16, 2017; resigned September 25, 2018.

Charged with an inappropriate relationship with a current student. Age 24-25 at time of offense (25 at arrest). Arrested October 3, 2018 on a $10,000 secured bond. Originally charged with 2 felony counts of sex act with a student.

December 10, 2019: One sex act count dismissed without leave by DA (no plea agreement); the other dismissed per plea agreement. Pleaded guilty to one count of crime against nature (GS 14-177). Judge James W. Morgan sentenced Costner under mitigated-range Community punishment: suspended sentence of 3-13 months, 18 months supervised probation, 50 hours community service within 180 days, mandatory DNA sample, and no contact with victim. No credit for time served.

NOTE: Charge reduction from sex act with a student to crime against nature. Community punishment means no active incarceration. Victim age not identified in sources; high school student. offense date of June 1, 2018 taken from court record filing notation.
