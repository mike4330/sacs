Also known as: Ashley Hurst, Ashley Hurst Casiday

Prison Sentence 0.5 yrs

Former 8th grade teacher at Leeds Middle School, Leeds, Alabama (St. Clair County). Also known as Ashley Hurst (maiden name). Age 31 at time of offense.

Resigned from Leeds Middle School February 2014 amid allegations of inappropriate contact with a 14-year-old student. Arrested March 2014. A St. Clair County grand jury indicted her on one count of transmitting obscene material to a child and four counts of possession of obscene matter.

Pleaded guilty October 2014 to transmitting obscene material to a child (Class B felony); four remaining charges dismissed. Sentenced to 5 years in prison with 6 months to serve; began sentence January 2015, released July 2015. Post-release: 3 years supervised probation, no contact with victim, and compliance with Alabama's Community Notification Sex Offender Law (no residence or employment within 2,000 feet of a school or childcare facility; must register name, address, and employment).

Re-arrested May 4, 2016 by Jefferson County Sheriff's Office on SORA violations — working within 2,000 feet of a daycare and failure to register employment (she was working as a contractor for Good Grit magazine in Birmingham). Both charges dismissed July 27, 2016 by the Jefferson County DA after review of actual facts; St. Clair County probation revocation proceeding also dropped August 8, 2016.

NOTE: Victim age/sex not specified in available coverage; middle school context and '14-year-old student' language suggests male victim but unconfirmed. Offense is sexting/obscene material transmission — no reported physical contact. Sentence was 6 months actual on a 5-year suspended sentence, a significant leniency for a Class B felony. SORA re-arrest resolved without conviction.
