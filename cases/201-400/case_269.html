State Florida
Court St. Johns County Circuit Court
Judge Howard Maltz
Incident Date 2016-09-01
Defendant Age 26

Prison Sentence 0.5 yrs

Former English and reading teacher at St. Augustine High School, St. Johns County, Florida. Age 26 at arrest; 28 at sentencing. Taught 9th-grade English and 9th- and 10th-grade intensive reading. Resided in Jacksonville.

Victim: 16-year-old male student in her class. Sexual contact included kissing and fondling in a classroom, and sexual intercourse in her vehicle at Treaty Park on Wildwood Drive in St. Augustine. They also exchanged nude photographs by text.

Investigation began October 20, 2016 after allegations surfaced; Hinckley suspended with pay the same day. Arrest warrant issued; Hinckley turned herself in at Nassau County Sheriff's Office on November 25, 2016. Released on $10,000 bond. Charged with unlawful sexual activity with a minor (F.S. 794.05, second-degree felony).

Entered no contest plea May 8, 2017 as part of plea agreement. Sentenced June 7, 2017 by Judge Howard Maltz to 6 months in jail, 72 months probation (first 12 months under community control), and classified as a sex offender.
