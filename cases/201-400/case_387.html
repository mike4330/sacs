Molly Wray #387 sentenced

State Mississippi
Court Lafayette County Circuit Court
Judge Kent Smith
Incident Date 2017-11-01
Victim Age 17
Prison Sentence 0.0 yrs

Former math teacher at Lafayette High School, Oxford, Lafayette County, Mississippi. Resigned following the investigation.

Victim was a 17-year-old male student. In November 2017, Wray allegedly enticed the student to meet for sexual purposes. The incident was not reported until June 4, 2018, when Lafayette County School District Superintendent Adam Pugh filed a complaint with the Lafayette County Sheriff's Department.

Arrested in June 2018; charged with one count of enticement of a child with intent to meet for sexual purposes. Bond set at $10,000. Appeared with attorney Tony Farese on July 16, 2018 and waived probable cause hearing. Grand jury indicted Wray on the enticement charge approximately October 2018. Over the following three years, defense attorney Farese filed nine continuances.

On July 13, 2021, Lafayette County Circuit Court Judge Kent Smith granted a state motion to retire the case. The retirement order requires Wray to complete 5 years of good behavior from the date of the order. No jail time served; no conviction entered.

NOTE: Mississippi 'retired' disposition is a conditional dismissal — no conviction absent a violation of the good-behavior term. Functionally equivalent to deferred adjudication in other states. Defendant age not identified in sources. The case took 3 years and 9 defense-requested continuances to resolve. Statute (child enticement, Miss. Code) carried 2-10 years if convicted.
