Stephanie Mariskanish #292 sentenced

State Nevada
Court First Judicial District Court, Carson City
Judge Todd Russell
Incident Date 2014-02-01
Defendant Age 28
Victim Age 14
Prison Sentence 1.0 yr

Former teacher's aide (paraprofessional) at Eagle Valley Middle School, Carson City, Nevada, assigned to work with special needs students. Age 28 at time of offense.

Victim was a 14-year-old male student. Mariskanish invited students to her apartment, provided alcohol, and initiated a sexting relationship with the victim. Four sexual encounters occurred at her apartment between February 1 and March 30, 2014, each involving unclothed touching in genital areas; one incident involved ejaculation. The school principal reported her to investigators after the victim alleged she put her hands down his pants. She was relieved of her duties in late January or early February 2014.

Charged with four Category B felony counts of Sexual Conduct with a Pupil (each carrying 1–6 years). At arraignment she stated to her father: 'I did some sexual stuff with a kid' and 'I knew what I was doing.'

Defense sought probation at sentencing, arguing conduct occurred off-campus, did not involve intercourse, and that Mariskanish was a volunteer rather than a direct teacher. Judge Todd Russell rejected probation: 'A teacher's aide in my opinion served in the same trust position as a teacher.' Victim's mother testified grades fell from As/Bs to Cs following the incidents.

Sentenced September 15, 2014 to 1–3 years in state prison (indeterminate; minimum 1 year).

NOTE: Defense characterized her as a 'volunteer' though district statements and reporting describe her as a paid paraprofessional. The indeterminate sentencing structure (1–3 years) means actual release depends on parole board.
