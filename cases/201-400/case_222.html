Lisa Kelly #222 sentenced

StatePennsylvaniaCourtAllegheny County Court of Common Pleas
JudgeAlexander P. BicketIncident Date2014-03-01
Defendant Age51Victim Age24
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former job coach at Allegheny Intermediate Unit (AIU) Lifehouse program, Bethel Park area, Allegheny County, Pennsylvania. Female defendant, age 51 at time of offense; born October 26, 1962. One male victim, age 24, with an IQ of 48 and the mental capacity of a 5-to-8-year-old child; a former student/client of the AIU program.

Kelly worked at AIU Lifehouse during the 2013-14 school year. The AIU opened an investigation into inappropriate communications; Kelly abruptly resigned on February 4, 2014, and had no further contact with students or AIU property. Both Kelly and the victim subsequently obtained employment at South Hills Village mall in Pittsburgh. Alleged sexual conduct occurred in a storage room at the mall on more than 12 occasions over approximately one year. The victim's father discovered the relationship after speaking with his son about concerns, then contacted police.

When approached by media, Kelly denied a sexual relationship; police stated she had admitted the relationship during her interview, describing it as a 'weird friendship.' Arrested March 8, 2017; charged with rape of a person incapable of consent.

Negotiated guilty plea to two counts of Indecent Assault of a Person with Mental Disability (18 Pa. §3126(a)(6), M1). Sentenced June 6, 2018, by Judge Alexander P. Bicket: 5 years probation on each count (concurrent). No confinement imposed. Defense attorney Michael J. Deriso.

NOTE: Charge reduction from rape (F1) to two misdemeanor counts of indecent assault. Boundary case — victim is an adult (age 24) with severe intellectual disability (IQ 48; mental capacity of 5-8 year old). Kelly's professional authority derived from educator/job-coach role at an educational intermediate unit, not a traditional classroom. Conduct occurred off-campus after Kelly resigned, but both parties' connection originated in the educational relationship. See behavioral-health tag convention.
