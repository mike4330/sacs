Former algebra teacher at Franklin Central High School, Indianapolis, Marion County, Indiana (Franklin Township Community School Corp.). Age approximately 37 at time of offense (age 38 at arrest May 2017; age 40 at sentencing November 2019). Employed at the school for only a few months prior to her arrest; had cleared a full criminal history check. Victim was a 17-year-old male student.

Hagan provided the victim with Xanax and had a sexual relationship with him. She also supplied a gun to a different student whom she knew planned to use it to commit crimes — specifically, allegedly to rob other students who may have had incriminating evidence on their phones.

Franklin Central administrators received an anonymous tip April 7, 2017; alerted police and the Department of Child Services. Hagan was suspended, then resigned. Arrested May 2, 2017 on two counts of child seduction, one count each of dealing in a controlled substance, conspiracy to commit armed robbery, and dangerous control of a firearm.

Pleaded guilty November 8, 2019 in Marion Superior Criminal Court to: child seduction (Level 5 felony); dangerous control of a firearm (Level 5 felony); dealing in Schedule IV controlled substance / Xanax (Level 6 felony); child seduction (Level 6 felony). State dismissed conspiracy to commit armed robbery and one additional child seduction count in exchange. Sentence: 10 years total; 2 years in the Department of Correction (actual prison); 8 years home detention; sex offender registration for 10 years.

NOTE: Provision of a gun to a second student to facilitate robbery of peers who might have incriminating evidence on their phones is a highly unusual criminal scheme — among the most complex aggravating conduct in this DB. Xanax provision to primary victim adds a drug-grooming dimension.
