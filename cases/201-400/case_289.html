Former music teacher at South Gwinnett High School, Gwinnett County, Georgia (employed since 2002). One male victim, age 17. Between March and approximately May 2016, Gunn engaged in a sexual relationship with the student lasting less than two months. Sexual activity occurred at the school, at Gunn's residence in Grayson, and at Lenora Park; some activity also occurred in a neighboring county. During this period, Gunn hosted a party at her home and allowed the victim and two other female students to smoke marijuana under her supervision; Gunn also smoked. Age approximately 53 at time of offense.

April 19, 2016: Victim's mother discovered the relationship, visited the school to inform administration; School Resource Officer and Department of Family and Children Services contacted; Gunn resigned the same day. April 20, 2016: Victim's mother filed a police report with Gwinnett County Police Department. May 6, 2016: Gunn turned herself in; charged with one count of sexual assault and three counts of contributing to the delinquency of a minor. Pleaded guilty. Sentenced July 14, 2017 by Judge Warren Davis to 90 days in prison; defense request for house arrest denied. Defense attorney Jim Wall.
