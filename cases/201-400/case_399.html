Former teacher and girls' lacrosse coach at Camden Catholic High School, Cherry Hill, New Jersey (Camden County). Age 34 at time of first offense (January 2019).

Victim was a 17-year-old male student in her class. The relationship began January 2019 and continued through March 2020 — approximately 14 months — with roughly 60 sexual encounters. Locations included the victim's car, the victim's home, Sipera's home, and the home of Sipera's mother; crimes occurred across Cherry Hill and Gloucester Township. The victim, a recent graduate by the time of the report, disclosed the abuse to authorities on August 6, 2020.

Charged August 2020 with two counts of second-degree sexual assault and two counts of second-degree endangering the welfare of a child. Held at Camden County Correctional Facility pending first appearance. Pleaded guilty and sentenced May 14, 2021 to one count of third-degree endangering the welfare of a child; sentenced to 4 years in New Jersey State Prison. Conditions: forfeiture of teaching license and credentials, Megan's Law registration, lifelong supervised parole, no contact with victim or victim's family.

NOTE: Charges reduced from second-degree sexual assault to third-degree endangering the welfare of a child in plea deal — a significant grade reduction. Sipera had been voted 'coolest teacher' at Camden Catholic in 2013 by Philadelphia radio program The Preston and Steve Show.
