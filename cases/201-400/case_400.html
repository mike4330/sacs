Former instructional aide at Texas High School, Texarkana Independent School District (Bowie County). Age 22 at time of offense.

Three male victims: two boys aged 17 and one aged 16, all in fall 2019. Bell arranged meetups through social media, picking boys up at a truck stop after school, having sex in her car with her 3-year-old daughter asleep in the back seat, and also taking them to her home. She had joined TISD as an instructional aide in 2019.

Arrested July 2020; initially charged with one count sexual assault of a child and two counts improper relationship between educator and student. Indicted by a Bowie County grand jury October 9, 2020 on two counts improper relationship (involving the 17-year-old boys) and two counts sexual assault of a child (involving the 16-year-old).

Pleaded guilty April 28, 2021 in a punishment-only jury trial. The jury sentenced Bell to 4 years in prison on one count of sexual assault of a child, with concurrent probation terms on the remaining counts. Judge Tidwell imposed 10 years probation on the second sexual assault count and two 5-year probation terms on the improper relationship counts, all concurrent. Formally sentenced May 10, 2021. Required to register as a sex offender upon release.

NOTE: Bell testified she did not know TISD policy prohibited aides from having sex with students and believed the students were all 18. She also claimed the students were the sexual aggressors. Prosecutor: 'All you saw in here was a liar when the truth would have been better. I've heard about all the victim blaming I want to hear.'
