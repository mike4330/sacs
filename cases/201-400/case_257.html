North Carolina v. Doll #257 sentenced

StateNorth CarolinaCourtJohnston County Superior Court
JudgeThomas H. Lock Incident Date2022-04-01
Defendant Age37 Victim Age14
Prison Sentence1.0 yr

Former English teacher at West Johnston High School, Johnston County, North Carolina. Age 37 at arrest. Previously nominated for Teacher of the Year. Had worked as a substitute teacher in Johnston County Schools before being hired full-time in August 2021.

Victim was a male student younger than 15 (one news report stated 15, but the arrest warrant specifies under 15; victim_age set to 14). Offense date per judgment: April 1, 2022. Arrested approximately September 2, 2022; terminated from her position the same day. Originally charged with statutory rape of a child under 15, sexual act with a student, and indecent liberties with a student. Bond initially set at $1 million; reduced to $75,000 in late September 2022 after Doll cited four children to support. Clayton, NC resident.

Pled guilty to one count of indecent liberties with a child (G.S. 14-202.1, Class F felony). Sentenced December 19, 2025 by Judge Thomas H. Lock, Johnston County Superior Court. Active punishment: 12 months minimum, 24 months maximum in DAC custody; 13 days credit for prior confinement. Prior record level I (0 points). Sex offender registration required (G.S. 14-208.6 reportable conviction). No restitution, fines, or costs.

NOTE: Over three years elapsed between arrest (September 2022) and sentencing (December 2025). Charge reduction from statutory rape of a child under 15 (Class B1 felony, minimum 144 months under NC structured sentencing) to indecent liberties with a child (Class F, 12–24 months active) is dramatic. The judgment references a count 54 with waived jail fees, suggesting at least one additional count was resolved separately but details are not in available documents. Defense attorney James R. Lawrence, Jr. (retained); prosecutor Paul T. Jackson.
