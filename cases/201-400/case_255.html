United States v. Carazas-Pinez #255 sentenced

StateNew YorkCourtU.S. District Court, Southern District of New York
JudgeJohn P. CronanIncident Date2022-11
Defendant Age33Victim Age16
Prison Sentence25.0 yrs

High school biology teacher at The Biondi School, a K-12 special needs boarding school in Yonkers, New York, operated by Rising Ground Inc. and funded by the NYC Department of Education. Age 33 at time of offense. Married mother of three; had previously taught at two NYC public schools.

From November 2022 through February 2023, engaged in sexual exploitation of a 16-year-old schizophrenic male student who had previously attempted suicide. Carazas-Pinez singled the victim out for personal attention, induced him to believe they were in a romantic relationship, and engaged in sexual intercourse with him on dozens of occasions in her vehicle at locations in the Bronx, Yonkers, and Staten Island. The encounters were facilitated by school day passes allowing the victim to leave campus without parental consent; she met him at a nearby Dunkin' Donuts and drove him to other locations. She used her cell phone to send explicit photos and messages, induced the victim to perform sexual acts on live-streamed video calls, and instructed him repeatedly to delete all communications. She referred to their sexual encounters and his on-camera performances as gifts.

School cameras captured Carazas-Pinez cuddling with the victim in her classroom and directing him to touch her genitals, but no one monitored the video. The abuse came to light when the victim disclosed to his mother; the principal then reviewed the footage. Carazas-Pinez was fired upon the school's discovery. When the victim attempted to stop the relationship, Carazas-Pinez threatened to fail him in class and threatened to kill herself — coercive threats made to a student with schizophrenia and prior suicide attempts.

Arrested July 12, 2023 by FBI and NYPD; charged in Manhattan federal court with enticing a minor to engage in illegal sexual activity and production of child pornography. Jailed pre-trial after violating bail conditions while staying with her parents in Staten Island.

Pled guilty to enticing a minor to engage in illegal sexual activity. Sentenced September 19, 2025 by Judge John P. Cronan to 25 years federal imprisonment and 5 years supervised release. No parole in the federal system. Prosecution: AUSA Mitzi S. Steiner, Civil Rights and Human Trafficking Unit, SDNY.

NOTE: The Biondi School closed permanently several months after her arrest, cited as not financially viable by Rising Ground Inc. The victim's family filed a civil lawsuit (Peters Brovner LLP) alleging failure to monitor classroom cameras, failure to obtain parental consent for day passes, and systemic supervisory failures. Rising Ground Inc. has collected $374 million in NYC funds since 2018 across various services. The victim is identified as special needs (schizophrenic, prior suicide attempts) — one of the most vulnerable victims documented in this DB.
