Former physical education teacher at Washington High School, Fremont, California. Age 32 at time of offense.

Victim was a 16-year-old male student. Audiat began communicating with the student in early 2016; conversations escalated over several months. Had sexual intercourse with the student on June 15, 2016. Arrested November 24, 2016 after Fremont police received a report the previous day. Original charges: unlawful sexual intercourse with a minor, oral copulation of a person under 18, three counts of contacting a minor for sex, one count of sending harmful material to a minor with sexual intent, and misdemeanor child molestation. Teaching credential was suspended following arrest.

Pleaded no contest December 19, 2017 to unlawful sexual intercourse with a minor and oral copulation of a person under 18; remaining counts dismissed. Victim and his family supported the plea agreement.

Sentenced February 9, 2018 by Judge Jon Rolefson to 5 years probation with 6 months county jail served through electronic monitoring (home confinement). Additional conditions: lifetime sex offender registration, surrender of teaching credential, 150 hours community service, DNA sample, 10-year stay-away order from victim, 1 year sex offender counseling, no unsupervised contact with minors during probation.
