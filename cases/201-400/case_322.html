Former teacher at Hudson High School, Hudson, Ohio (Portage County). Age approximately 52 at time of offense; 57 at sentencing. Also owner and chief instructor of Kent Karate and Family Fitness Martial Arts Dojo, West College Avenue, Kent.

Victim: 17-year-old female student; defense described the relationship as 'consensual and ongoing.' Sexual contact occurred January 22, 2017 at Hallstein's West College Avenue residence in Franklin Township, Kent. Hudson police contacted the Portage County Sheriff's Office August 4, 2020 following a report of sexual battery involving an underage student; Hallstein arrested August 20, 2020.

Originally charged with two counts of sexual battery (third-degree felony). Pled guilty February 2021 to gross sexual imposition and attempted unlawful sexual conduct with a minor, both fourth-degree felonies, pursuant to a plea deal amending both charges from the original third-degree felonies. Sentenced April 5, 2021 by Judge Laurie J. Pittman to 180 days in jail; Tier II sex offender (biannual registration for 25 years); 4 years probation, first year intensive supervision; mental health evaluation; $300 fine; $2,000+ court costs. Hallstein voluntarily surrendered her teaching license. Defense attorney Donald Malarcik.

NOTE: No prior criminal record per defense. Three-year gap between offense (January 2017) and report (August 2020) likely attributable to the victim coming forward as an adult. Formerly known as Heidi Gauntner (maiden name used in 2020 arrest article).
