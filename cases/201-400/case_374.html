Former 6th-grade language arts teacher at Ledford Middle School, Thomasville, Davidson County, North Carolina.

Victim was a 14-year-old male former student. Williams was also described as a close family friend of the victim. The victim reported that between the ages of 14 and 15 Williams engaged in a sexual relationship with him. All sexual conduct occurred outside the school setting. Age approximately 42 at time of offense (2009).

In July 2017, the victim reported the abuse to the Davidson County Sheriff's Office. Investigation began July 19, 2017; investigators found evidence supporting the allegations. Williams was arrested in August 2017. Originally charged with 2 counts statutory rape, 4 counts statutory sex offense, and 4 counts indecent liberties with a minor.

In May 2018, all 10 original counts were dismissed in exchange for a guilty plea to one count of felony crime against nature. Judge Anna Mills Wagoner imposed a suspended sentence of 5-6 months with 24 months supervised probation. No-contact order with victim and family. Mental health evaluation ordered.

NOTE: Approximately 9 years elapsed between the offense and the arrest — victim disclosed as an adult. No-prison outcome despite original statutory rape charges. Williams was a close family friend in addition to being a former teacher, suggesting access predated or extended beyond the school relationship. Offense did not occur on school grounds per investigators.
