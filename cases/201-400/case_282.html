Former English teacher at Lakewood High School, Lakewood, California (Long Beach Unified School District). Had been teaching at the school since 2006. One female victim. Jahn engaged in a sexual relationship with a 15-year-old female student beginning July 2008; the relationship continued for approximately three years, through 2011 when the victim turned 18. Jahn and the victim remained in intermittent contact until late April or early May 2017. In 2017, the now-adult victim reported the abuse to Long Beach Police Department. Age approximately 43 at time of first offense.

LBUSD placed Jahn on leave in May 2017 after police began investigating. Arrested September 13, 2017; bail set at $750,000. Arraigned September 19, 2017 in Long Beach; charged with nine felony counts: four counts of sexual penetration by a foreign object, three counts of lewd act upon a child, and two counts of oral copulation of a person under 18. Defense attorney Leonard B. Levine stated Jahn 'adamantly denies the charges.' Pleaded no contest November 9, 2018 to one count of lewd act upon a child (case NA107331). Sentenced November 30, 2018 to three years in state prison; required to register as a sex offender for life.

NOTE: Female victim — comparatively rare in this DB. Delayed reporting of approximately nine years; victim came forward as an adult. Original exposure of 8 years 4 months on nine counts resolved through no-contest plea to a single count — significant charge reduction. Investigated by LBPD; prosecuted by LA County DA's office.
