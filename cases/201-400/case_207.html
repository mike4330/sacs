Former American civics and economics teacher at Thomson High School, Thomson, McDuffie County, Georgia. Male victim, age 16. Defendant age 37 at arrest.

GBI called to assist McDuffie County Sheriff's Office on November 9, 2015. Investigation by Special Agent in Charge Pat Morgan revealed Hubert and the 16-year-old student had been engaging in sexual conduct since early September 2015. Hubert arrested at the high school on November 10, 2015 and booked into McDuffie County Detention Center on felony charge of sexual assault by a person with supervisory or disciplinary authority. Bond set at $25,000. McDuffie County Board of Education issued statement saying she was no longer affiliated with the school system.

Sentenced July 27, 2016: 10 years total under the First Offender Act — 1 year in McDuffie County Jail followed by remainder on probation. Fine of $2,500. Required to register as sex offender. Teaching credentials revoked; not allowed to reapply for a teaching certificate.

NOTE: Sentenced under Georgia's First Offender Act — formal conviction entry withheld pending completion of probation. One year actual incarceration; sentence_years reflects jail time only. Relatively short period between onset (September 2015) and arrest (November 2015) — approximately two months.
