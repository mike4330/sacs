State Missouri
Court Jefferson County Circuit Court
Incident Date 2015-01-01
Defendant Age 43

Former school nurse at Fox Elementary School in Arnold, Missouri (Fox School District). Age 43 at time of first offense (DOB 03/18/1972; offenses began 2015).

Victim was a male student; age not reported in sources. Sumner had sex with the victim at least eight times between 2015 and 2018, at her home, the victim's home, and in a Fox School District building. Arrested in 2017; immediately resigned. After the arrest, Sumner offered the victim $2,000 to pay off his car in an apparent attempt to purchase his silence; a copy of a check confirmed payment.
