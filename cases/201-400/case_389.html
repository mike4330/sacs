Former math teacher (2009-2020) and math coach (2020-2022) at Cedar Lee Middle School, Bealeton, Fauquier County, Virginia. Also conducted SAT prep classes through Warrenton Baptist Church (2015, 2019, 2020, 2022) and led youth mission trips to Haiti through Friends of Fort Liberté. Age 32 at time of offense.

Victim was a 13-14-year-old male student at Cedar Lee Middle School. Incidents involving sexual intercourse and oral sex occurred between May and September 2018. The victim came forward with allegations approximately summer 2022, approximately four years after the incidents.

Fauquier County Public Schools placed Grimley on administrative leave August 1, 2022. Resigned early November 2022. A grand jury indicted Grimley November 28, 2022 on 2 counts of carnal knowledge/sexual intercourse and 2 counts of carnal knowledge/oral sex — each a felony carrying up to 10 years. Released on $5,000 unsecured bond; barred from contact with the school system. Trial was scheduled for April, then August 2023.

Pleaded guilty July 31, 2023 to 4 misdemeanor counts of contributing to the delinquency of a minor. Each count: 12 months incarceration with all but 14 days suspended. The 56 total days served as home confinement administered by Fauquier County Detention Center. $1,260 net fine per count; 1 year supervised probation; 56 hours community service (refuse pickup, 14 hours per count); no contact with victim/family; restorative justice program; mental health evaluation; barred from employment as tutor or at any school or childcare facility. Not required to register as a sex offender — this was explicit in the plea agreement.

NOTE: Charge reduction from 4 felony carnal knowledge counts to 4 misdemeanor contributing-to-delinquency counts — a significant downgrade given victim was 13-14 and conduct included intercourse and oral sex. The plea agreement explicitly stated she would not be ordered to register as a sex offender. Home confinement is the sole confinement modality; sentence_years = 0. Victim came forward approximately 4 years after the incidents. Grimley's continued access to youth through church programs during the intervening years was noted in reporting but not publicly investigated.
