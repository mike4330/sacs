Sarah Heskin #366 sentenced

StateWisconsinCourtPepin County Circuit Court
Incident Date2019-02-01
Defendant Age24 Victim Age14
Prison Sentence5.0 yrs

Former 8th grade English teacher at Durand-Arkansaw Middle School, Durand-Arkansaw School District, Pepin County, Wisconsin. Victim was a 14-year-old male student. Age 24 at time of offense.

Beginning in February 2019, Heskin created a secret Instagram account for communication with the student. Through it she sent two or three topless photos and a video of herself taken from her bed. She also admitted to kissing the student on school grounds. School officials learned of the conduct when multiple students reported an 'inappropriate relationship' to an administrator, who then reported to police. During the investigation, the student initially denied social media contact; Heskin admitted to the Instagram communication when questioned and eventually to sending explicit photos. She claimed to have been intoxicated when she sent the explicit materials, but acknowledged full awareness of the student's age.

Arrested May 2, 2019 on charges of using a computer to facilitate a child sex crime; booked at Pepin County Jail, released on $10,000 bond. Pleaded guilty December 2019 to second-degree sexual assault (felony) as part of a plea deal. Sentenced April 20, 2020 to five years in prison followed by 10 years extended supervision. Ordered to pay $2,058 in restitution and to have no contact with the victim, his family, or any minors without prior probation officer approval. Required to register as a sex offender.

NOTE: Heskin created a secret account specifically to avoid detection — deliberate platform concealment. She claimed intoxication when sending explicit photos but acknowledged knowing his age. Charge reduced from computer facilitation of a child sex crime to second-degree sexual assault at plea. No other victims identified. Peers reported the relationship to school officials — same reporting dynamic as Porter #364 (Safe2Tell).
