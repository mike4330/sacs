Keri Ann Brekne #226 sentenced

StateNew JerseyCourtNorthampton County Court of Common Pleas (PA)
JudgeF.P. Kimberly McFadden Incident Date2007-06-01
Defendant Age28 Victim Age13
Prison Sentence15.0 yrs

Former social studies teacher at Lopatcong Township Middle School, Lopatcong Township, Warren County, New Jersey. Resided in Bethlehem, Northampton County, Pennsylvania. Female defendant, age ~28 at time of offense. One female victim, age 13–14 (June 2007 through January 2008).

Brekne systematically groomed the victim over an extended period: befriended the victim's family, drove the student places even after being told not to, spent nights at the victim's home, gave the victim a 'secret' cell phone (to replace one the parents confiscated at bedtime), gave her a wedding-style ring, discouraged her from friendships with peers, told the victim she 'owned' her, and helped her sneak out of her home at night. Staff at the school told Brekne her relationships with students were too casual; her colleagues advised her to be more professional. Despite these warnings, the conduct continued.

Crimes occurred at Brekne's Bethlehem, PA home and in Lopatcong, NJ, involving digital penetration, oral sex in both directions, and sexual assault with an artificial penis. Discovered when the victim's parents found sexually explicit text messages on the secret cell phone.

Pennsylvania case: Brekne pleaded guilty July 9, 2008, in Northampton County (Easton, PA) to 5 counts of Aggravated Indecent Assault (18 Pa.C.S.A. §3125(a)(8); victim under 16, offender 4+ years older; F2). Sentenced October 10, 2008, by Judge F.P. Kimberly McFadden to 36–72 months state prison per count, all 5 consecutive: aggregate 180–360 months (15 to 30 years). Judge McFadden stated it was the most heinous case she had seen in two decades on the bench and told Brekne: 'You are dangerous.' PA appeal filed (J-A06029-13, 2013); outcome not yet determined from available sources.

New Jersey case: Warren County; APA Kevin Brotzman; defense attorney Scott Wilhelm; Judge John Pursel. April 18, 2009 — Pursel rejected Brekne's guilty plea after hearing victim impact statements he had not previously seen, saying he felt 'bushwhacked.' Remanded to trial. Victim's supporters had hoped for a consecutive 10-year NJ sentence. NJ case outcome unknown from available sources.

Civil suit: Filed 2013 against Brekne, the Lopatcong Township School District, school board, middle school, and 10 current/former teachers and administrators. Federal counts partially dismissed July 2015; state-level suit revived November 2015, seeking at least $75,000 damages.

NOTE: Dual-jurisdiction case (NJ school, PA crimes, PA conviction). County field uses NJ school county (Warren, NJ) per convention; court field reflects PA conviction. Female victim — unusual in this DB. Among the longest sentences recorded here. Prior-warning-ignored: colleagues explicitly warned Brekne her relationships with students were too casual before the abuse.
