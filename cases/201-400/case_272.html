Lindsey Limbaugh #272 sentenced

Former teacher at Kelly High School, Kelly R-IV School District, Benton, MO (Scott County). Age 38 at arrest. Relationship with a male student began in April 2023 when Limbaugh, while intoxicated at a concert, contacted the student via TikTok. Subsequent contact included kissing at a car wash, kissing and sexual touching during a school field trip to a Cape Girardeau park (inside a men's restroom), and intercourse at Limbaugh's home during the final weeks of the 2022-2023 school year. The victim, a current high school student, later stated he also had sex with Limbaugh at her home before the school year ended. Victim age approximately 17 (high school student in final weeks of school year; NOTE: age inferred, not stated in sources). Limbaugh claimed she did not have sex with the student until after he had graduated; victim's account contradicted this.

The case was prosecuted in two counties. In Cape Girardeau County, Limbaugh pleaded guilty to Class C misdemeanor fourth-degree assault; the two felony sexual contact counts were dismissed. Sentenced November 5, 2025: 10 days county jail with credit for time served. In Scott County, she pleaded guilty on February 24, 2026 to Class A misdemeanor fourth-degree assault (RSMo 565.056 subd. 6, special victims); the two felony sexual contact counts were again dismissed. Sentenced to 5 days county jail, no credit for time served.

NOTE: Both counties accepted misdemeanor assault pleas in exchange for dismissal of the felony sexual contact with a student charges — a significant charge reduction across both jurisdictions. The dual-county prosecution was procedurally unusual; Cape Girardeau County covered the field trip/park offense while Scott County covered the school-district-based offenses in Benton.
