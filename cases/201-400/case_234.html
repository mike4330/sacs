Former boys' basketball coach and employee of Gooding School District at Gooding High School, Gooding, Idaho. Hired October 2017. Had a sexual relationship with a 17-year-old male student. Age 26 at arrest; 27 at sentencing.

Arrested January 5, 2018 following a tip received by Gooding police. Initially charged with felony sexual abuse of a minor aged 16 to 17 (maximum 25 years). Gooding Police Chief Dave Fisher confirmed she was suspended Thursday and the school board had her termination on the agenda for the following Tuesday.

At sentencing June 27, 2018, Kuroki pleaded guilty to two felony counts of injury to a child and one misdemeanor count of dispensing alcohol to a minor under a plea deal. The judge complied with the plea agreement. Victim's mother Aubrey Cheney gave an impact statement.

Sentence: 90 days Gooding County jail (work release), 4 years probation, sex offender treatment. No sex offender registration required.
