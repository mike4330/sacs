Noelle Devlin #290 sentenced

StateOhioCourtRichland County Court of Common Pleas
JudgeJames DeWeese Incident Date2014-10
Defendant Age30 Victim Age16
Prison Sentence1.0 yr

Former third-grade teacher at Mansfield Choice Academies (Interactive Media and Construction School), a public charter school housed at Ocie Hill Neighborhood Center, Mansfield, Ohio (employed since 2014). One male victim, age 16, enrolled at the school. Sexual relationship occurred October 1, 2014 through June 5, 2015. Age approximately 30 at time of offense. Devlin was also described as a mother.

Fired October 2015 after Mansfield Police Department began an investigation. Arrested December 7, 2015 on a warrant; transported to Richland County Jail on $15,000 bond. Indicted November 16, 2015 on 15 counts of sexual battery and 14 counts of child endangering. Pleaded guilty to three counts of sexual battery and three counts of child endangering; 12 counts of each were dismissed as part of the plea agreement. Sentenced April 25, 2016 by Judge James DeWeese to one year in prison followed by four years of probation. Required to register as a Tier III sex offender for life. No-contact order with victim.

At sentencing, Devlin submitted an eight- or nine-page letter to the court in which she claimed she was not thinking clearly and stated she felt the victim 'was in charge of the relationship.' Judge DeWeese stated he did not believe she was taking responsibility and read excerpts of the letter in open court. 'In your letter you substantially blame him. I have a hard time believing you are taking responsibility,' DeWeese said, noting Devlin took advantage of the victim's age, mental state, and her position of trust as a teacher. The victim's father submitted a written statement: 'You took a vulnerable, impressionable mind and twisted it to believe this was a normal and adult-style relationship.'

NOTE: Explicit victim-blaming by defendant in a court submission — one of the more documented examples of this pattern in the DB. Judge DeWeese specifically rejected the remorse framing on the record. Tier III classification (Ohio's highest) reflects the seriousness of the conduct. Notable that Devlin was a 3rd-grade teacher with a 16-year-old victim — consistent with a charter school serving non-traditional or multi-age enrollment.
