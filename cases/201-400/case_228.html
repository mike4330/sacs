Former seventh-grade math/science teacher at Catasauqua Middle School (Catasauqua Area School District), Catasauqua, Lehigh County, Pennsylvania. Age 42 at arrest (September 2019). Foster mother to the victim, a 15-year-old male, at the time of the offenses.

Race had previously taught the victim in seventh grade years before the abuse began. She is a Catasauqua Area SD teacher; the school district suspended her upon her arrest. No allegations arose from the school context — all conduct occurred at her home.

Lehigh County DA received a report June 27, 2019 of sexual assaults occurring at Race's Catasauqua home in July and August 2018. A detective interviewed the victim and viewed video evidence. Race was arrested and arraigned September 19, 2019 on charges of statutory sexual assault and corruption of minors.

Pleaded guilty to statutory sexual assault. Sentenced to 12–24 months state prison; 7 years supervision; lifetime Megan's Law registration. Aggravating factors: victim blaming, risk to children. Mitigating: no prior record, cooperation with investigation.

NOTE: Dual-role case — Race was simultaneously the victim's foster mother and a teacher who had previously taught him. The sexual relationship occurred in the foster-care context, not the school context. Video evidence confirmed by detective. Victim blaming cited as an aggravating factor at sentencing.
