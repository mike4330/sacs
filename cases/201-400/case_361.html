Laura Ramos #361 sentenced

State Connecticut
Court Fairfield County Superior Court
Judge Robert Devlin
Incident Date 2016-12-23
Defendant Age 30
Victim Age 17
Prison Sentence 5.0 yrs

Former special education teacher at Central High School, Bridgeport, Fairfield County, Connecticut. Both victims were male special education students. Age 30 at time of first offense. Defendant was married and the mother of a young daughter.

Victim 1 (age 18 at time of offense): Ramos initiated a sexual relationship beginning December 23, 2016 that continued through April 2017. She admitted to police in a recorded statement to having sex with him 'a handful of times,' mostly in her car. Following her first arrest, a court protective order barred her from contact with victim 1. On June 22, 2017 — while the case was pending — she was pulled over by police in Waterford while driving him to the beach, violating the protective order.

Victim 2 (approximately 17 at time of offense): In April 2017, he accompanied Ramos to a restaurant in Fairfield; afterward they had sex in her car. On a separate occasion she took him to a restaurant in Shelton where they had sex in the restaurant's electrical room. Ramos texted him afterward that it was 'the best sex she ever had.' A second male student disclosed the relationship to police on June 23, 2017.

Arrested June 20, 2017 on one count of second-degree sexual assault; bond $50,000. Re-arrested August 2, 2017 on additional charges after victim 2 came forward; bond increased to $250,000. Pleaded no contest in November 2018 to two counts of second-degree sexual assault and one count of violating a protective order. Sentenced January 25, 2019 by Judge Robert Devlin to 10 years suspended after 5, plus 10 years probation. Required to register as a sex offender. Resigned her teaching position after her arrests.

NOTE: Prosecutor stated Ramos showed no remorse and continued to sexually abuse victim 1 while the case was pending. Defense minimized the offenses by noting the victims' near-adult ages; the judge rebuked this directly — 'These were not two valedictorians. These are special education students and they need more support.' The rebuke is a notable explicit rejection of the near-adult mitigation argument. Victim 2's age estimated at approximately 17 based on defense's 'nearly 18' reference at sentencing.
