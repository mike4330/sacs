South Carolina v. Wentzky #261 sentenced

StateSouth CarolinaCourtRichland County Court of General Sessions
Incident Date2011-05-01
Defendant Age32Victim Age17
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former English teacher at Dreher High School, Columbia, South Carolina (Richland School District 1). Age approximately 32 at time of first offense; 34 at arrest; 36 at sentencing. Had taught at Dreher for seven years. Married with two children at time of arrest.

Two separate male victims, both 17-year-old students. Victim 1 (Columbia PD case): sexual intercourse at a home on Wilmot Street in Columbia in May 2011; both Wentzky and the student gave sworn statements admitting to the relationship. Victim 2 (Forest Acres PD case): sexual intercourse at her home on Fernwood Road in Forest Acres on December 20, 2012.

Arrested twice in early January 2013 within three days: first by Columbia Police Department (~January 4), then by Forest Acres Police Department (January 7). Placed on paid administrative leave by Richland School District 1; teaching certificate suspended by the SC Department of Education in January 2013; ultimately terminated.

Pled guilty in late October 2014 to two counts of sexual battery with a student 16-17 years of age (SC Code 3463), one count per victim. Sentenced to 5 years suspended, 3 years probation (beginning approximately October 27, 2014; ending October 27, 2017), placement on South Carolina Central Registry of Child Abuse/Neglect, and mental health counseling. No prison time.

NOTE: Two separate victims, two separate police jurisdictions, incidents spanning approximately 19 months. The WIS sentencing article cites 'May 2012' for the CPD incident; the 2013 arrest warrants specify 'May 2011' (CPD) and 'December 20, 2012' (Forest Acres). Warrant dates used as more contemporaneous. Both students consented; police noted the law was specifically enacted (2010) to address teacher authority over students of legal consent age.
