Kyle Damato-Kushel #318 sentenced

State Connecticut
Court Bridgeport Superior Court
Judge Robert Devlin
Incident Date 2014-08-12
Defendant Age 44
Victim Age 15
Prison Sentence 1.5 yrs

Former teacher's aide at Wooster Middle School, Stratford, Connecticut (Fairfield County). Age approximately 44 at time of offense; 48 at sentencing.

Two male victims: (1) a 15-year-old boy she had known since serving as his preschool teacher — the relationship became sexual and was ongoing; (2) a 16-year-old friend of the first victim.

On August 12, 2014, following a fight between the first victim and Damato-Kushel's husband at her home, Damato-Kushel picked up both boys and drove them, along with her two daughters, to the Trumbull Marriott Hotel, where she had sexual contact with both boys in a hotel room with her daughters present.

Arrested late 2015 for the relationship with the first victim. Charged January 13, 2016 with additional counts related to the second victim: two counts fourth-degree sexual assault, three counts risk of injury to a minor, and one count second-degree sexual assault; bond set at $50,000.

Pled guilty January 2018 before Judge Robert Devlin to second-degree sexual assault and risk of injury to a child as the case was being prepared for trial. Sentenced April 13, 2018 to 10 years, suspended after 18 months, followed by 10 years' probation; sex offender registration required.

NOTE: Long-term connection to primary victim — she had been his preschool teacher, establishing deep family trust years before the offense. Hotel assault occurred with Damato-Kushel's own minor daughters present.
