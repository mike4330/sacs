Former substitute teacher with Maysville Local Schools, near Zanesville, Ohio (Muskingum County). Hired through Renhill Group staffing company; background check was clear prior to hire. Age 22 at start of offenses; 23 at arrest. A 2012 graduate of Maysville High School — teaching at her own alma mater.

Two male victims: ages 16 and 14 at start of offenses. Relationship spanned approximately February 1 through November 1, 2017.

Arrested December 21, 2017; posted $200,000 bond same day. Initially charged with nine counts of sexual battery; pled not guilty. Subsequently charged with unlawful sexual conduct with a minor (Count 1, Ohio 2907.04(A), F/4) and four counts of gross sexual imposition (Counts 3-6, Ohio 2907.05(A)(5), F/4).

Sentenced June 6, 2018 in Muskingum County: Count 1 — 1 year; Counts 3-6 — 1 year each concurrent; Count 1 consecutive to Counts 3-6; aggregate 2 years in Ohio Dept of Corrections. 1 day jail credit. Remanded to Muskingum County Sheriff for transport to appropriate state institution.

NOTE: Charge reduction from nine counts of sexual battery to five counts (one unlawful sexual conduct with a minor + four gross sexual imposition). Renhill Group terminated Lake's contract; superintendent noted the background check had returned clear.
