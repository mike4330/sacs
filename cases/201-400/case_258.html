Former math and science teacher at Meridian Middle School, Meridian, Idaho. Age 37 at arrest. Ada County case CR09-17816.

Victim was a 12-year-old male student in her 6th-grade class when grooming began in April 2009. Beach targeted him from first contact, buying him shoes and clothes, calling him the 'light of her life,' and arranging for him to mow her lawn and babysit her three children. They had sex in her classroom with the door locked. The relationship continued through the summer. Arrested September 17, 2009, when police found Beach and the boy lying in bushes near Cherry Lane and Vineyards in Meridian (a few blocks from the school) around 9:30 p.m. Victim was 13 at time of arrest.

Separately, husband Shawn Beach, a former Ada County Sheriff's employee, was arrested for taking nude photographs of female inmates and sending them to his wife. Prosecutors said he acted out of jealousy over his wife's relationship with the boy.

Beach admitted the relationship. Charged with lewd conduct with a child under 16. Sentenced December 29, 2009 by Judge Ronald Wilper to 20 years in prison with parole eligibility after four years. Prosecutor Jean Fisher: 'She began grooming him when he was in 6th grade and she was his teacher. The grooming was methodical and it was calculated.' Defense attorney John Meienhofer argued she was not a pedophile and sought treatment over prison. Judge Wilper: 'You cashed in your family, your husband and your children, your reputation. And you did this for the sake of lust.'

Released on parole May 15, 2014 (approximately 4.5 years served). Sentence satisfaction date December 25, 2029 per Idaho DOC.

NOTE: Husband's parallel arrest (video voyeurism of jail inmates) is an unusual collateral element. Beach's 'light of her life' language and sustained grooming from age 12 — including using him for domestic labor — is consistent with predatory fixation rather than situational offense. Early parole release at minimum eligible date (~4 years).
