Former teacher at Bedford High School, Bedford, Cuyahoga County, Ohio. Two male student victims. Defendant age 28 at onset of conduct; 31 at sentencing.

Sexual conduct with two students occurred from November 1, 2018 to October 19, 2019. Four incidents with one student; two with the other. In one instance, Dunker picked up a student before school, drove to a park, and engaged in intercourse in her car. Additional incidents occurred at Dunker's home. Investigation concluded December 4, 2019; determination made that sexual activity had occurred.

Indicted on six counts of sexual battery and one count of tampering with evidence (alleged tampering on approximately November 6, 2019, knowing an official proceeding or investigation was in progress). Bond set at $5,000 cash surety; ordered to provide DNA specimen and have no contact with the victim. Pleaded guilty in October 2020 to two counts of sexual battery; remaining four sexual battery counts and tampering charge dismissed. Sentenced January 21, 2021 to two years in prison on each count, to run concurrently. Five years probation after release. Registered as Tier III sex offender; required to re-register every 90 days for the rest of her life.

Prosecutor Michael O'Malley: 'Teachers are expected to be role models to their students and communities which they serve. She took advantage of these students and now must face the consequences.'

NOTE: Tampering with evidence charge dropped at plea. Tier III designation (most serious tier in Ohio) triggers lifetime registration at 90-day intervals. Late-night/early-morning car meeting (before school) is an unusual logistical pattern.
