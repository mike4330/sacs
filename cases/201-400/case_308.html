Victim was a 13-year-old male student. Nowaczyk sent the victim a Snapchat photo of her bare breasts; the photo also showed a distinctive watch she always wore. The two communicated via Snapchat and OPS email and text services. Sexual contact began in August 2017 and occurred multiple times in her classroom: she performed oral sex on the victim and allowed him to digitally penetrate her.
