Laura V. Bucy #211 sentenced

StateOhioCourtSummit County Court of Common Pleas

Former family and consumer sciences (home economics) teacher at Twinsburg High School, Twinsburg, Summit County, Ohio. Male victim, age 17. Defendant age approximately 31 at time of offense; 32 at arrest. Previously taught in Ravenna, Akron, Strongsville, and other Ohio districts.

Bucy began working in Twinsburg in August 2017. Grooming conduct included allowing the student to vape in her office and purchasing vaping supplies for him. Student helped clean her classroom and pantry; Bucy stated that one day in February or March 2018, the boy came to her classroom after lunch and they had sex once. Student's account placed the incident before Christmas 2017. Bucy also exchanged explicit videos and photographs with the student via Snapchat. Bucy reportedly also had illicit correspondence with a second student at a prior employer (details redacted from police report).

Bucy confessed to her ex-father-in-law on August 2, 2018, then fled the area — drove to Cuyahoga County, abandoned her vehicle on the highway, and was located walking barefoot by an Ohio State Highway Patrol trooper; taken to hospital for evaluation. Ex-husband contacted Twinsburg police September 6. Police met with Bucy September 11; she admitted the relationship and confirmed explicit photo exchange. Warrant issued September 12 after she left a secured facility; traveled to Arizona. Father brought her back; turned herself in September 22, 2018. Arraigned in Stow Municipal Court; released on personal recognizance bond.

Charged with sexual battery (3rd-degree felony) and disseminating matter harmful to juveniles (5th-degree felony). Pleaded guilty to sexual battery in June 2019; disseminating charge dismissed. Sentenced January 8, 2020 by Judge Susan Baker Ross to 2 years in prison; Tier III sex offender registration required. Granted early judicial release October 15, 2020 after approximately 9 months served; 3 years probation.

NOTE: Actual time served approximately 9 months (judicial release); sentence_years reflects imposed sentence of 2 years. Defense argued mental illness played a role. Prosecutors opposed early release citing emotional toll on victim. Tier III in Ohio triggers 90-day re-registration for life.
