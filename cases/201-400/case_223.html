Former paraeducator (teacher's aide) and coach at Conestoga High School, Tredyffrin Township, Chester County, Pennsylvania. Worked in Tredyffrin/Easttown School District from August 2014 until April 2016; coached girls' soccer, basketball, softball, and track at middle and high school levels. Female defendant, age 26. One male student victim, age 16, with learning disabilities; she had tutored him privately in math and other subjects.

Relationship began March 17, 2016, when Towers kissed the victim. Over approximately one month, she took him out to eat, to the mall, and on trips to Philadelphia. They had sex four times. Victim later told police he was 'mostly just confused' about the direction of the relationship: 'She made me think I was her everything and she would go crazy if I left her.' The victim cooperated with detectives and described it as a way out. Investigation triggered by a child-abuse report filed through Chester County Department of Children, Youth and Families in April 2016; Towers arrested April 2016.

Pleaded guilty March 6, 2017, at the Chester County Justice Center to Institutional Sexual Assault and Corruption of Minors. Sentenced the same day by Judge Patrick Carmody: 11 to 23 months jail (left in handcuffs), 5 years probation, lifetime sex offender registration. Defense attorney Vincent P. DiFabio; prosecutor Emily Provencher.

At sentencing, three family members (victim, mother, grandmother) described the 'devastation' caused. Victim quit the basketball team after classmates and opposing players mocked him; he entered therapy and said Towers ruined his senior year.

NOTE: Judge Carmody explicitly addressed gender double standard at sentencing: 'If you were a male and it was a 16-year-old female, people would be outraged and screaming for blood.' Victim described as having learning disabilities — special-needs-victim tag applies. Grooming pattern includes repeated off-campus outings (meals, mall, Philadelphia). Defense attorney at arraignment (Cary McClain) was replaced by Vincent DiFabio at sentencing.
