Melody Lippert #304 sentenced

Also known as: Melody Suzanne Lippert

State California
Court Orange County Superior Court, Harbor Justice Center
Incident Date 2014-12-27
Defendant Age 38
Victim Age 17
Prison Sentence 0.0 yrs

Former teacher at South Hills High School, West Covina, California (Covina-Valley Unified School District). Age 38 at time of offense.

Organized an unauthorized December 27–29, 2014 camping trip to San Clemente State Beach by sending a group text message inviting co-defendant Michelle Ghirelli and five male high school students. Alcohol and cocaine were distributed to the underage students throughout the trip. Lippert was later described as the 'matchmaker' in facilitating Ghirelli's sexual contact with a 17-year-old boy during the trip; she was charged with aiding and abetting that encounter rather than direct sexual contact.

Initially charged February 2015 with misdemeanor contributing to the delinquency of a minor. Orange County prosecutors declined to file felony sex charges, citing insufficient evidence. After a school-related investigation revealed cocaine use and cellphone records confirmed Ghirelli knew the victim was 17, detectives reinterviewed witnesses and felony charges were filed in March 2015: unlawful sexual intercourse, furnishing controlled substance to a minor, and a sentencing enhancement for being more than four years older than the victim.

Pleaded guilty June 5, 2015 to one felony count of unlawful sexual intercourse. Sentenced to 3 years formal probation, 3 months jail (served as 15 days Caltrans work in lieu of jail). Teaching credentials revoked for life. Co-defendant: Michelle Ghirelli (see separate case).

NOTE: County convention: school in LA County (West Covina); offense and prosecution in Orange County (San Clemente). Defense attorney Leonard Levine stated Lippert did not personally have sex with the victim but aided and abetted the encounter.
