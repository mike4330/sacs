Brooke L. Rosendale #212 sentenced

StateOhioCourtHancock County Common Pleas Court
Incident Date2017-01-04
Defendant Age26Victim Age13
Prison Sentence3.0 yrs

Former 5th grade intervention specialist (special education teacher) and girls volleyball and basketball coach at Riverdale Local Schools, Bloomdale/Mount Blanchard area, Hancock County, Ohio. Female victim, age 13. Defendant age 26 at time of offense; was engaged to her long-term boyfriend. Employed by the district for three years.

Victim was not in Rosendale's class but the two knew each other through school extracurricular programs. Incidents occurred off school grounds on January 4 and February 3, 2017. Victim's parents reported incidents to the school district on March 12, 2017. Hancock County Sheriff's Office launched investigation. Rosendale resigned March 27, 2017 and relinquished her teaching certificate.

Grand jury indicted June 27, 2017 on three counts of sexual battery (3rd-degree felonies). Pleaded guilty to one count of sexual battery January 26, 2018; two counts dismissed. Sentenced March 7, 2018 to three years in prison. Tier III sex offender registration; re-register every 90 days for life; cannot work with children. Ordered to pay $225 restitution to victim's family.

In court, Rosendale apologized: 'I want to apologize to the family. I know I hurt them and I apologize.' Victim's parents appeared in court but did not make a statement.

NOTE: Female victim. Defendant was engaged at time of offense. Coach-player relationship (not classroom teacher-student) as grooming vector. Incidents off-campus.
