Former Spanish teacher and assistant volleyball and track coach at Arapahoe High School, Littleton Public Schools, Arapahoe County, Colorado. Victim was a 16-year-old student; gender not reported in available sources. Age 23 at time of offense.

Porter worked at Arapahoe High School from August 2018. On Thursday, March 21, 2019, she was placed on administrative leave; the following day, after Arapahoe County Sheriff's Office investigators responded to a tip received through Colorado's Safe2Tell anonymous student reporting system, she was arrested at the school on a no-bond hold. The tip came from a student peer, not the victim or school staff. Investigators determined probable cause that Porter had sexually assaulted the 16-year-old student. She was terminated upon arrest and did not return to Littleton Public Schools.

Original charges: sexual assault on a child by someone in a position of trust; sexual exploitation of children/pornography/obscene material. Under a plea deal, Porter pleaded guilty to attempted sexual assault on a child by one in a position of trust; all other charges were dismissed. Sentenced January 6, 2020 to 90 days in jail and 10 years of sex offender intensive supervised probation. Required to register as a sex offender.

NOTE: The tip came from a student through Safe2Tell — an unusual reporting vector (peer-reported rather than victim or staff). The sexual exploitation of children charge (pornography/obscene material) was dismissed as part of the plea; the conduct underlying it is not detailed in available sources but likely involved explicit images. Charge reduced from sexual assault to attempted sexual assault; the CSAM-related charge dropped entirely.
