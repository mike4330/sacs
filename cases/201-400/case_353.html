Former substitute teacher at Fairmont High School (Kettering City Schools) and Oakwood Schools, Montgomery County, Ohio. Age 23 at time of first offense (age 24 at sentencing April 2018). At least three male student victims, ages 16-17. Held a K-12 education intervention specialist license; had graduated from University of Dayton in 2016 with an education degree.

Two documented incidents: (1) July 19, 2017 — oral sex with a 17-year-old student in the parking lot of Big Lots on Wilmington Pike, Kettering; (2) September 21, 2017 — intercourse with a 16-year-old student in an apartment complex parking lot on Smithville Road. A third victim was identified in court. Marx also confessed to sending multiple nude photos via Snapchat and Instagram. She admitted to having sexual relationships with multiple students when questioned by police. Encounters occurred in parking lots, not on school premises.

Staff at Fairmont became aware of the inappropriate relationships November 8, 2017; Marx was removed from the building by police the same day. Ruled competent to stand trial February 21, 2018.

Pleaded guilty to two counts of sexual battery. Sentenced April 19, 2018 by Judge Steven Dankof: up to 5 years probation (both counts); no jail time. Designated Tier 3 sex offender (highest Ohio tier); lifetime registration every 90 days; lifetime teaching ban. AP Dylan Smearcheck. Marx cried in court and apologized: 'I cannot express how sorry I am for what I've done especially to the victims and their families.' Judge cited lack of prior record, cooperation with authorities, genuine remorse, 38 letters of support, and requests from two victims' parents for probation.
