Former teacher at Camden-Frontier High School, Camden, Hillsdale County, Michigan. Age approximately 34 at start of offense (age 39 at Indiana sentencing April 2011; 1990 Quincy HS graduate). Victim was a 14-year-old male student. Relationship was years-long, beginning fall 2006, and involved sexual activity on both sides of the Michigan-Indiana state line as well as along Michigan and Indiana roadways.

Camden-Frontier administration became aware of a pending Michigan criminal investigation in November 2009 and suspended Schwartzengraber from teaching. Indiana allegations followed shortly thereafter. Arrested February 8, 2010 at her Angola, Indiana residence by Indiana State Police.

Two separate prosecutions. Michigan (Hillsdale County Circuit Court, Judge Mike Smith): charged with criminal sexual conduct first degree (multiple variables) and CSC third degree (multiple variables); trial scheduled September 8-9, 2010. Michigan case outcome not reported in available sources. Indiana (Steuben County Superior Court): charged with Class D felony sexual misconduct with a minor plus four additional counts including a Class B felony. Pleaded guilty February 1, 2011 to one Class D felony count; remaining four counts dismissed by Judge William Fee as part of plea agreement. Special prosecutor Greg Kenner. Defense: Linda Wagoner (Angola). Sentenced April 8, 2011: three years prison, all but one year suspended; 1 year actually served.

NOTE: School county is Hillsdale (Michigan) per convention; sentencing court was Indiana. Michigan criminal case outcome unknown. Charge reduction from Class B to Class D felony on Indiana plea is significant (Class B maximum 20 years vs. Class D maximum 3 years). Years-long cross-state relationship; victim was her own student.
