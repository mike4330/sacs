Former upper school teacher at Evangelical Christian School (ECS), a private Christian school in Fort Myers, Lee County, Florida. Victim was a male student; age not reported, but the resolved charge (Sexual Activity with a Child, F.S. 794.011(8)(b)) typically applies to victims aged 16-17. Age 35 at time of offense.

For several weeks prior to the incident, Owen communicated with the student via personal text messages. Approximately one week before her arrest, the two met off campus for a single sexual encounter. On April 11, 2018, LCSO Special Victims Unit detectives responded to ECS and met with Owen; she admitted the sexual misconduct. Headmaster John Hunte Ed.D. notified the school community that evening and terminated Owen's employment immediately.

Owen was charged with custodial sexual battery (first-degree felony) and booked into the Lee County Jail. She initially entered a plea of nolo contendere, then withdrew it; an open plea was entered April 29, 2019, and a plea agreement was reached June 24, 2019. Pleaded guilty to one count of Sexual Activity with a Child by a Person in Familial or Custodial Authority. Sentenced June 24, 2019 to two and a half years in prison followed by 20 years of sex offender probation. Judge: J Frank Porter. Defense: Sawyer Charles Smith. Designated a lifetime Sexual Predator (Florida's highest SORN tier, distinct from the Tier I/II/III registration system). Additional conditions: GPS monitoring during probation, curfew, no contact with anyone under 18, mandatory sex offender treatment program. No prior criminal history.

NOTE: Charge reduced from custodial sexual battery (1st degree felony) to Sexual Activity with a Child by a Person in Familial or Custodial Authority; plea developed in consultation with the victim's family.
