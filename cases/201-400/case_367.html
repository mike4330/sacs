State Florida
Court Lee County Circuit Court
Incident Date 2018-04-04
Defendant Age 35
Prison Sentence 2.5 yrs

Former upper school teacher at Evangelical Christian School (ECS), a private Christian school at 8237 Beacon Boulevard, Fort Myers, Lee County, Florida. Victim was a male student; age not reported, but the resolved charge (Sexual Activity with a Child, F.S. § 794.05) typically applies to victims aged 16–17. Age 35 at time of offense (DOB 07/09/82).

For several weeks prior to the incident, Owen communicated with the student via personal text messages. Approximately one week before her arrest, the two met off campus for a single sexual encounter. On April 11, 2018, LCSO Special Victims Unit detectives responded to ECS and met with Owen; she admitted the sexual misconduct. Headmaster John Hunte Ed.D. notified the school community that evening and terminated Owen's employment immediately.

Owen was charged with custodial sexual battery (first-degree felony) and booked into the Lee County Jail. She initially entered an open plea, then withdrew it; a plea agreement was reached in consultation with the victim's family. Pleaded guilty to one count of Sexual Activity with a Child. Sentenced June 25, 2019 to two and a half years in prison followed by 10 years of sex offender probation. Designated a lifetime Sexual Predator (Florida's highest SORN tier, distinct from the Tier I/II/III registration system). Additional conditions: GPS monitoring during probation, curfew, no contact with anyone under 18, mandatory sex offender treatment program. No prior criminal history.
