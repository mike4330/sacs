Defendant Age 21

Prison Sentence 0.0 yrs

Former Spanish teacher, head cross country coach, and assistant track coach at Steubenville Catholic Central High School, Steubenville, Ohio (Jefferson County). Age 21 at time of offense.

Victim was a 15-year-old male student. On March 30, 2021, McCalister was driving the victim home from a track meet at Edison High School when she stopped at the Harrison County Fairgrounds in Cadiz and had sex with him. The victim told his mother, who notified the school on April 21.

Placed on administrative leave and eventually fired. Arrested April 23, 2021 on two counts of sexual battery; held on $100,000 bond. McCalister had received her one-year temporary teaching license in June 2020, just months before the offense.

Pleaded guilty July 15, 2021 to one count of sexual battery; a second count was dropped. Sentenced to two years of probation, 80 hours of community service, and a reserved 2.5-year prison term. Her Ohio teaching license was permanently revoked by the state Department of Education. Lifetime Tier III sex offender registration required; no contact with victim.

NOTE: Probation-only outcome on a reserved 2.5-year sentence. Judge acknowledged her youth (age 22 at sentencing) and noted she could 'put this behind her someday.' Defense highlighted permanent loss of teaching license as a significant sanction.
