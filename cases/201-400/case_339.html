Former technology teacher at Roselle Catholic High School, Roselle, Union County, New Jersey; also an alumna of the school. Age 25 at arrest, approximately 24 at time of offense. Victim was a student at Roselle Catholic. Sexual acts occurred at various times and locations in Union County between December 2016 and February 2017. Hrindo also deleted incriminating images and files from the student's phone.

Three-week investigation initiated by a referral from the Catholic Archdiocese of Newark, which holds jurisdiction over Roselle Catholic. Arrested March 30, 2017. Originally charged with two counts of second-degree sexual assault, two counts of third-degree endangering the welfare of a child (NJ 2C:24-4), and one count of third-degree hindering apprehension (re: deletion of files from student's phone). Arrested without incident at her home; lodged in Union County Jail pending first appearance. Prosecutor: ACA Caroline Lawlor (Union County Prosecutor's Office, Special Victims Unit), under Acting Prosecutor Grace H. Park.

Pleaded guilty via accusation (no indictment) to one count of third-degree endangering welfare of a child — sexual conduct with child by non-caretaker (NJ 2C:24-4a(1)). Sentenced March 9, 2018 by Judge Regina Caulfield: non-custodial disposition; 2 days jail; Parole Supervision for Life (PSL). Original second-degree sexual assault charges dropped as part of plea.
