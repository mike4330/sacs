Former teacher at Rivera Middle School, Trenton, New Jersey. Age approximately 42 at time of offense (age 43 at May 2017 arrest). Married.

Met the victim in 2013 when he was a student at Rivera Middle School. In 2016, when the victim was 15 and had been kicked out of his family home, Culver obtained legal guardianship of the boy and took him into her home. Sexual abuse began thereafter. The victim told police the encounters were initially consensual but that Culver expressed anger when he asked her to stop.

Arrested May 2017; charges filed by Burlington County Prosecutor's Office. Indicted July 2018 on two counts of first-degree aggravated sexual assault, four counts of second-degree sexual assault, and two counts of second-degree child endangerment. Evidence included biological material and text messages exchanged between Culver and the victim. Teaching certification suspended September 2019 by NJ State Board of Examiners in a near-unanimous vote.

Sentenced May 1, 2020 by Judge Philip Haines. Conviction on count 7 only: third-degree abuse of child — cruelty/neglect (N.J.S.A. 9:6-3). Non-custodial sentence; 3 days jail, 1 year probation. Aggregate sentence.

NOTE: Defendant age approximately 42 back-calculated from age 43 at May 2017 arrest. Incident date approximate — guardianship obtained in 2016, exact start of abuse unclear. Massive charge reduction from first-degree aggravated sexual assault (10–20 years in NJ) to third-degree child cruelty/neglect. County convention note: Rivera Middle School is in Mercer County; Burlington County filed charges because offenses occurred at defendant's home. Obtaining legal guardianship of a vulnerable displaced student as access mechanism is a deliberate exploitation pattern (adoption-scheme). Defense attorney victim-blamed throughout, calling the victim 'a very troubled young man' who fabricated allegations.
