Marie-Jo Gordo #218 sentenced

StateFloridaCourtUS District Court, Middle District of Florida
JudgePaul G. ByronIncident Date2023-06-01
Defendant Age29Victim Age17
Prison Sentence5.0 yrs

Former art teacher at an Orange County charter school, Florida (school name not identified in charging documents). Also taught the victim as his 8th-grade teacher and religious ambassador in 2019. Female defendant, age 29 at arrest. One male student victim, age ~17 at time of offense (had not yet reached 18).

Relationship began June 2023 and continued through September 2023. Sexual activity occurred at hotels across Orange, Osceola, and Seminole Counties, and in Gordo's vehicle. Approximately 28 videos were recovered from the student's iPhone 14, documenting the sexual encounters. Gordo resigned from the charter school in June 2023 and subsequently obtained a position at a second K-8 school while the relationship continued.

Student's mother reported the relationship to the Orange County Sheriff's Office in September 2023; consent to search the student's phone was obtained at the same time. OCSO Digital Forensic Unit and an FBI task force officer reviewed the device. Seminole County Sheriff's Office Crimes Against Children task force also participated. Gordo was arrested in October 2023; complaint unsealed January 2024. She entered a not-guilty plea via signed waiver for the January 18, 2024 hearing.

Federal indictment charged three counts of sexually exploiting a minor (production, possession, receipt/distribution of child pornography and coercion/enticement). Gordo pleaded guilty to Count One of a Superseding Information: Distribution of Child Pornography (18 U.S.C. §2252(a)(2) and (b)(1)), offense concluded September 22, 2023. Original indictment Counts One through Three dismissed on government's motion.

Sentenced June 13, 2025, by Judge Paul G. Byron to 60 months federal prison; surrender to Bureau of Prisons by July 14, 2025. Court recommended placement near Central Florida, mental health counseling, and RDAP. 5 years supervised release; sex offender registration under SORNA and Florida statutes; no contact with minors; computer/internet use prohibited without probation officer approval; mental health and sex offender treatment program; polygraph testing; asset forfeiture ordered. Fine waived; no restitution ordered; $100 special assessment.

NOTE: Federal case (case 6:24-cr-3-PGB-NWH). State-level charges, if any, did not proceed to separate prosecution — the federal CSAM/distribution charge subsumed the conduct. The 2019 teacher-student relationship predates the 2023 sexual conduct by four years, suggesting a long-term grooming dynamic. CSAM production/distribution charge is rare in this DB (cf.
