StateFloridaCourtVolusia County Circuit Court
Incident Date2020-09-06

Prison Sentence5.42 yrs

Former teacher at United Brethren in Christ Academy (also cited as Brethren Christ Academy), Holly Hill, Florida. Age 31 at time of offense.

Victim was a former male student; age stated only as 'over 12 but younger than 16.' O'Brien had been his teacher approximately two years before the offense (circa 2018), then beginning in October 2019 pushed to befriend the family, initially visiting weekly and then every few days. The victim's mother noted feeling 'odd' by O'Brien's overt attempts to grow close. O'Brien also tutored the victim's younger brother and took him to McDonald's specifically to make the victim jealous.

On September 6, 2020, O'Brien and the victim were on a couch at his home covered by a blanket when the lewd act occurred. On September 27, the victim's mother found suspicious Instagram messages on his phone, posed as the victim to continue the exchange, then confronted O'Brien in a Steak and Shake parking lot. O'Brien admitted to an 'inappropriate emotional relationship,' said she had developed a crush and given the victim three options: pretend it never happened, stop talking, or 'do whatever they wanted and not worry about what is right.' She admitted the relationship became physical on September 6. O'Brien had quit her teaching job before her arrest.

Arrested October 2020; charged with lewd and lascivious battery. Posted $50,000 bail. Sentenced June 15, 2022 to 5 years and 5 months in prison plus 55 months probation; officially classified as a sex offender.

NOTE: Exact victim age not stated in any source; known only as 12–15. Grooming vector was sustained family infiltration combined with leveraging a younger sibling as a jealousy tool.
