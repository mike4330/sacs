Stacy Slamka #370 sentenced

State Florida
Court St. Johns County Circuit Court
Incident Date 2011-12-01
Defendant Age 47
Victim Age 16
Prison Sentence 0.82 yrs

Former substitute teacher in the St. Johns County School District, St. Augustine, Florida. Worked at multiple schools including Gamble Rogers Middle School, Pedro Menendez High School, St. Augustine High School, St. Johns Technical High School, Murray Middle School, and Gaines Alternative and Transition Schools; started substituting in 2010. Victim was a 16-year-old male, a Pedro Menendez High School student. Age 47 at time of offense. No prior disciplinary issues before this case.

Between December 2011 and January 2012, Slamka had sexual intercourse with the student six times. The two met and began communicating via phone and Facebook; she picked him up and drove him to Hastings, where the encounters occurred. In March 2012, school officials received copies of text messages between Slamka and the student, fired her immediately, and referred the case to the St. Johns County Sheriff's Office. Electronic forensic evidence from items seized in a search warrant took over a year to process. A warrant was issued and Slamka turned herself in to the Alachua County Sheriff's Office on March 20, 2013.

Initially charged with three counts of unlawful sexual activity with a certain minor (F.S. § 794.05). Sentenced (date approximate) to 300 days in jail (credited with one day served) and five years of probation on five felony battery charges. She was required to surrender her teaching certificate, is prohibited from St. Johns County school grounds, may not have isolated contact with minors (but may attend her own child's school functions unless the victim is present), and must continue counseling.

NOTE: The one-year delay between discovery (March 2012) and arrest (March 2013) was due to electronic forensic evidence processing. Charge count changed from 3 (unlawful sexual activity with a certain minor) at arrest to 5 (felony battery) at sentencing — the direction and nature of this change are unclear from available sources. Sentencing date is approximate. Sentence years = 0.82 (299 days continuous jail; no work-release or intermittent program mentioned).
