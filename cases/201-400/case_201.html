Jaime Corinna Melton #201 sentenced

StateFloridaCourtHillsborough County Circuit Court
Incident Date2022-09-01
Defendant Age42 Victim Age16
Prison Sentence3.75 yrs

Former English teacher (10th and 11th grade language arts) at Carrollwood Day School, a private institution in Tampa, Hillsborough County, Florida. Had previously worked at Bloomingdale High School. Female victim, age 16; described as a family friend of Melton who also attended the school where she taught. Defendant age 42 at arrest.

Incident occurred September 2022. A short-lived romantic relationship developed and culminated in a sexual encounter. Hillsborough County Sheriff's Office developed probable cause and obtained arrest warrant; Melton arrested Sunday, September 11, 2022 (approximately). Held without bond. Carrollwood Day School immediately suspended her; school head Sara Rubinstein noted she had an ''unblemished teaching record'' with no prior concerns. Sheriff Chad Chronister stated she had ''violated that trust'' as a trusted community member.

Pleaded guilty to Sexual Battery, Child Over 12 but Less than 18, by a Person in Familial or Custodial Authority (Florida statute). Sentenced August 23, 2024 by Hillsborough Circuit Judge Christine Marlewski to 45 months in prison plus 10 years sex offender probation (GPS ankle monitor, annual polygraphs, residency restrictions near schools). State sentencing guidelines recommended 10 years; significant downward departure based on acceptance of responsibility, genuine remorse, low recidivism risk per defense experts, and positive character record. Defense attorney Jason Mayberry; ASA Amber Turbeville prosecuted.

Defense psychosexual evaluator Jennifer Sager testified Melton showed no psychopathy, struggled with anxiety, depression, and some histrionic traits; was not generally attracted to minors but was attracted to this particular teen; low recidivism risk. Victim parents requested maximum sentence.

NOTE: Significant downward departure (45 months vs. 10-year guideline). Victim was a dual-relationship figure — both family friend and student. Private school. Melton will never teach again; teaching license effectively ended by conviction.
