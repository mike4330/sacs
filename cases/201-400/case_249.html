People v. Damyan #249 sentenced

StateIllinoisCourtCook County Circuit Court
JudgeCharles Burns Incident Date2019-01
Defendant Age32 Victim Age13
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former special education teacher at Alessandro Volta Elementary School, Albany Park, Chicago. Age 32 at time of offense.

Beginning in January 2019, engaged in sexual conduct with a 13-to-14-year-old male student she tutored after school hours. Damyan distributed her phone number to students including the victim; she and the victim exchanged approximately 12,000 text messages and phone calls. Damyan invited the student to her Logan Square home, where she gave him cannabis gummy bears and tea laced with marijuana before sexually assaulting him. The student returned the following day and similar conduct occurred. Damyan also smuggled marijuana vaping devices onto school property inside a stuffed animal and used them with the student on campus.

Damyan described the student as her boyfriend to an adult acquaintance who was a mandatory reporter. That individual reported the abuse to authorities in March 2019. CPS removed Damyan from classroom settings during the investigation; she left CPS employment in September 2019.

Arrested February 17, 2020, charged with criminal sexual assault. Pled guilty February 2021 to one count of criminal sexual abuse by force. Sentenced by Judge Charles Burns to two years probation.

NOTE: Cannabis-laced food used as grooming tool (compare Rogers #248). Victim age reported as both 13 and 14 across sources; abuse began January 2019 and the victim may have turned 14 during the offense period. Disclosure came through defendant's own admission to a mandatory reporter — unusual pathway. Charge reduced from sexual assault to sexual abuse; no prison.
