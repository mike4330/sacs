Former 7th and 8th grade math and science teacher at Bear Grass School, Martin County, North Carolina. Subsequently teaching at South Creek Middle School (also Martin County) at the time of arrest. ECU graduate. Defendant age 23 at time of offense. Victim was a 14-year-old male student. Two separate incidents occurred in May and June 2010 while the victim was her student at Bear Grass School; acts occurred in Martin County.

Arrested November 9, 2010 at her Greenville home; charged with sex offense with a student and statutory rape; initial bond $200,000. Bond reduced to $50,000 November 10, 2010; conditions: reside with mother in Salisbury NC, curfew, no contact with victim/family/Martin County students, no school property access, weekly check-in with Martin County Sheriff.

Pleaded guilty in 2013 to multiple counts of indecent liberties with a child (original charges: sex offense with a student and statutory rape). Sentenced to a maximum of 1 year 8 months; served 10 months; released March 2014. Sex offender registration required.

January 9, 2015: Charged with sex offender use of a social website (Facebook); later dismissed in Rowan County court. December 2015: Charged with felony failure to inform law enforcement of new/changed online identifier (Snapchat account, which she did not report as required).

NOTE: Charge reduction from statutory rape and sex offense with a student to indecent liberties. She continued teaching at a second Martin County school (South Creek Middle) between the offenses and her arrest — suggesting prior-warning-ignored or at minimum no administrative action until arrest. Repeat SORA violations post-release.
