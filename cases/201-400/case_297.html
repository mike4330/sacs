Former theater teacher at Irvin High School, El Paso, Texas (El Paso Independent School District). Age approximately 44 at time of offense (age 46 at February 2016 sentencing).

Victim was a 16-year-old male student. Schlesinger treated the student 'like a boyfriend' on and off campus. The two had sex in the theater classroom, in her van, and during a school-sponsored trip to San Antonio where the student stayed in her hotel room.

During the investigation, Schlesinger admitted to having photos of the boy's naked upper torso on her cellphone; EPISD police found additional photos of the boy's genitals on the same device.

A tip reached EPISD on May 1, 2014; Schlesinger was immediately removed from the classroom and resigned May 2. An arrest warrant was issued May 27 by EPISD police; she turned herself in to the County Jail on May 29, 2014.

Pleaded guilty to improper relationship between an educator and student and possession of child pornography. Sentenced February 12, 2016 to 3 years on each charge, served concurrently.
