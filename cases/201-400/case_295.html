Former English teacher and head cheerleading coach at Tishomingo High School, Tishomingo, Oklahoma. Also held a Pre-K-12 counselor certification. Married to Tishomingo Public Schools Superintendent Kevin Duncan. Prior career in juvenile justice and probation/parole (Oklahoma Office of Juvenile Affairs 1998–2006; Dept. of Corrections probation/parole officer, resigned 2012). Age 48 at time of arrest.

Victim was a 14-year-old male student. The relationship involved 7,425 text messages (May–September 2016) in which Duncan called the boy 'boo' and wrote 'I love you.' On at least one occasion, they kissed after school hours in a school building with clothing removed. Duncan also referenced performing a sex act on the victim via Snapchat.

Arrested September 16, 2016 by OSBI special agents on request of Tishomingo Police; booked into Johnston County Jail on complaint of lewd or indecent proposals or acts with a child under 16. Released on bail. First state trial ended in mistrial (hung jury). Venue for retrial changed to Norman. Convicted at retrial of lewd acts with a child; sentenced 2018 to 6 years in state prison.

State conviction vacated April 21, 2021 under McGirt v. Oklahoma, which held that Oklahoma lacks criminal jurisdiction over offenses committed by or against tribal members on Indian Country land. On the same day, the FBI filed a federal charge of Sexual Abuse of a Minor in Indian Country; U.S. Marshals took Duncan into federal custody.

Pleaded guilty to Sexual Abuse of a Minor in Indian Country in federal court. Sentenced December 15, 2022 by the Eastern District of Oklahoma to 3 years federal prison; projected release 2025.

NOTE: Defendant age is 48 at arrest (September 2016); offense age is approximately 47–48. McGirt transfer is analytically significant — the 6-year state sentence was nullified and replaced by a 3-year federal sentence for the same conduct. Husband's role as district superintendent raises questions about institutional oversight dynamics.
