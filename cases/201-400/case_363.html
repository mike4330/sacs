Former math teacher and cross-country coach at Smyrna High School, Smyrna School District, Kent County, Delaware. Victim was a 17-year-old male student. Age 37 at time of offense.

On February 11, 2014, after school hours, Suarez performed oral sex on the student inside her classroom while another student stood outside the closed door. School staff reported the allegations in February 2014. Suarez resigned at the April 16, 2014 Smyrna Board of Education meeting; she was arrested April 25, 2014 on charges of first-degree sexual abuse of a child by a person of trust. Bail was set at $20,000.

Pleaded guilty November 17-18, 2014 to rape in the fourth degree, one day before her scheduled trial. Sentenced February 25, 2015 in Kent County Superior Court to 10 years in prison, suspended after 6 months, followed by 1 year of probation. Ordered to have no contact with the victim, his family, or any child under 18 except her own children; required to undergo a mental health exam and complete a sexual disorders counseling program; must register as a Tier 2 sex offender.

NOTE: Single incident (February 11, 2014); the on-campus, in-classroom location with another student directly outside the door is notable. Cross-case: same school district, same county court, same charge (rape in the fourth degree), and same Tier 2 SORN requirement as Karen Brooks #362 (Smyrna Middle School, 2018) — two cases from Smyrna School District within three years.
