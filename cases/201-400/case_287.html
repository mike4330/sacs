Madeleine Schmaltz #287 sentenced

StateMinnesotaCourtSt. Louis County District Court
Incident Date2019-03
Defendant Age28 Victim Age16
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former science teacher and volleyball coach at Hermantown Middle School, Hermantown, Minnesota (hired 2014). Victim was a 16-year-old who had been Schmaltz's student approximately two years earlier and was now attending high school. Multiple sexual encounters occurred in spring 2019 at Schmaltz's home, framed as homework help and mentoring visits. Schmaltz had given the victim several gifts. Schmaltz admitted to sexual touching of the victim and to having the victim touch her. Age approximately 28 at time of offense.

Mandated reporter alerted law enforcement June 4, 2019. Victim's mother had overheard a phone conversation between her child and Schmaltz that was 'sexual in nature'; phone records confirmed Schmaltz's identity. The victim confirmed the sexual relationship and multiple visits to Schmaltz's home to law enforcement two days later. Schmaltz was interviewed the same day and acknowledged the relationship 'became sexual.' Schmaltz resigned from Hermantown School District; resignation accepted June 10, 2019. Charged by summons September 2, 2020 in St. Louis County District Court with fourth-degree criminal sexual conduct. Pleaded guilty in September 2020. Sentenced November 10, 2020 to three years of supervised probation.

NOTE: Victim was a former student — relationship maintained after the victim transitioned to high school, with Schmaltz sustaining contact under the pretext of academic mentoring. Gifts and in-home access are consistent with transactional grooming pattern. Fifteen months elapsed between the initial police report (June 2019) and formal charging (September 2020); defendant was not arrested, charged by summons only.
