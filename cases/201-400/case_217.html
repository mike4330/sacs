Tiffany Eichler #217 sentenced

Former physical education teacher at Canton McKinley High School, Canton, Stark County, Ohio. Female defendant, age 36 at time of offense. Three male student victims: one age 17 and two age 18. Atwater resident, married with children, no prior criminal record.

Sexual encounters occurred February 20–24, 2018 — the four days Canton City Schools were open that week. Snapchat was the primary communication platform. Eichler drove to one victim's home to pick him up, then drove from New Franklin to Canton at 2:30 a.m. for a second encounter with the same student. With a separate student, she called him to her classroom during non-school hours and locked the door. Detectives interviewed her March 9, 2018; she admitted to the relationships. Placed on unpaid leave the same day; resigned March 30, 2018.

Pleaded guilty May 2018 to three counts of sexual battery (each a felony carrying 1–5 years). Sentenced June 19, 2018, before Judge John Haas. Defense attorney Brad Iams argued no likelihood of reoffense; Stark County APA Christy Donnelly requested 4 years, citing lack of full accountability. At sentencing, Eichler claimed students initiated contact and that she felt pressured — a characterization prosecutors directly rebutted with evidence she drove to students' locations and locked a classroom door.

Sentenced to 30 days jail (immediately taken into custody) plus 30 half-days non-jail reporting program, 3 years probation, $1,000 fine, 300 hours community service, counseling, payment of victims' counseling costs, essay on the wrongfulness of her actions. Lifetime sex offender registration; teaching license revoked. Judge reserved 60 months prison for any probation violation.

NOTE: Prosecutor's sentencing filing explicitly raised gender-bias concern — that Eichler's supporters were seeking sympathy as a wife and mother that would not have been extended had the genders been reversed. Victim's mother called her a 'professional predator.' victim-blaming framing (claimed students pressured her) is documented and rebutted in the record.
