Heather Lee Robertson #325 sentenced

State Texas
Court 159th District Court, Angelina County
Judge Paul White
Incident Date 2016-11-01
Defendant Age 37
Prison Sentence 10.0 yrs

Former kindergarten teacher at Hudson ISD, Lufkin, Texas (Angelina County). Age 37-38 at time of offense; 39 at sentencing.

At least six male high school victims; all incidents occurred at Robertson's Lufkin apartment between November 2016 and February 2017. Robertson initiated contact via Snapchat, sending explicit messages, then invited students to her apartment for sex. In one incident she had sex with two students simultaneously. In another, she arranged for a student's parents to drop him off under the pretense of helping her move furniture. Other victims would sneak out of their homes at night and Robertson would pick them up. She admitted to sometimes having sex with students 'as many as four times.' Robertson stated she had become a heavy drinker and sometimes did not remember details of the encounters; she also did not require condom use.

Hudson ISD police began investigating April 20, 2017 following a tip; Robertson admitted to the conduct during questioning. The school accepted her resignation April 20, 2017. Arrested June/July 2017.

Indicted by Angelina County grand jury on six counts of sexual assault of a child and six counts of improper relationship between educator and student (second-degree felonies, each carrying 2-20 years). Pled guilty September 28, 2017 pursuant to a plea bargain to seven counts of sexual assault of a child and seven counts of improper relationship with a student before Judge Paul White, 159th District Court. Sentenced to 10 years TDCJ; lifetime sex offender registration; surrender of teaching certification. ADA Todd Dillon prosecuted.

NOTE: Victim ages not explicitly stated in available sources; 'sexual assault of a child' charges in Texas (§22.011) require victims under 17, placing all victims at high school age but under 17. Plea was to 14 counts (7+7) vs. 12 in the original indictment, suggesting at least one additional victim was documented after indictment. Among the highest victim counts in the DB.
