Florida v. Geliga #270 sentenced

StateFloridaCourtSt. Lucie County Circuit Court
JudgeWilliam L. RobyIncident Date2017-04-01
Defendant Age35Victim Age17
Prison Sentence4.22 yrs

Former chemistry teacher at a Port St. Lucie high school, hired by St. Lucie Public Schools in 2014. Age 35 at arrest (turned 36 on May 26, 2017).

Victim: 17-year-old male student, tutored by Geliga at Indian River State College (off-campus). In late April or early May 2017, the student and Geliga met twice off-campus and had sex in her car. Investigation began when another student's mother found texts between Geliga and her son; that student denied a sexual relationship but identified the 17-year-old victim. Detectives arranged for the victim to call Geliga while they listened in; she confirmed the sexual relationship. Arrested and booked into St. Lucie County jail, May 2017. Suspended by St. Lucie Public Schools following arrest; later dismissed.

Originally charged with two counts of sexual battery by person 24 or older on victim 16 or 17. Pleaded no contest to one count. Sentenced April 2019 to 66 months in prison plus 2 years sex offender probation. Appealed; Florida Fourth DCA reversed and remanded, finding the trial court erred by requiring the mental health mitigator (F.S. 921.0026(2)) to be connected to the criminal conduct.

Resentenced April 18, 2022 by Judge William L. Roby to 50 months 18 days on a reduced charge of unlawful sexual activity with a minor (794.05(1), F-2), nolo contendere. Jail credit of 50 months 18 days applied — released at the resentencing hearing. Followed by 2 years probation.

NOTE: A second student may have had text contact with Geliga but was not identified as a sexual victim. Resentencing capped total incarceration at approximately 50 months from May 2017 arrest.
