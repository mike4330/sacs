Florida v. Geliga #270 sentenced

State Florida
Court St. Lucie County Circuit Court
Judge William L. Roby
Incident Date 2017-04-01
Defendant Age 35
Victim Age 17
Prison Sentence 4.2 yrs

Former chemistry teacher at a Port St. Lucie high school, hired by St. Lucie Public Schools in 2014. Age 35 at arrest (turned 36 on May 26, 2017).

Victim: 17-year-old male student, tutored by Geliga at Indian River State College (off-campus). In late April or early May 2017, the student and Geliga met twice off-campus and had sex in her car. Investigation began when another student's mother found texts between Geliga and her son; that student denied a sexual relationship but identified the 17-year-old victim. Detectives arranged for the victim to call Geliga while they listened in; she confirmed the sexual relationship. Arrested and booked into St. Lucie County jail, May 2017. Suspended by St. Lucie Public Schools following arrest; later dismissed.

Originally charged with two counts of sexual battery by persons 24 or older on a victim 16 or 17 years old. Pleaded no contest in January (year approximate; likely 2019) to one count. Sentenced to 66 months (5.5 years) in prison plus 2 years sex offender probation.

Appealed sentence. The Florida Fourth DCA reversed and remanded for a new sentencing hearing, finding the trial court erred as a matter of law by requiring the mental health mitigator (F.S. 921.0026(2)) to be connected to the criminal conduct. Outcome of resentencing not available in current sources.

NOTE: Sentence_years = 5.5 reflects original sentence; resentencing pending per appellate remand. A second student may have had text contact with Geliga but was not identified as a sexual victim. Sentencing date approximate.
