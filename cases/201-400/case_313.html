Former physical education teacher at Grover Cleveland High School, Ridgewood, Queens, New York. Age approximately 38 at time of first offense (2013); 40 at plea.

Victim 1 was a 16-year-old male student; Morsi had sexual intercourse with him over a seven-week period in 2013 at the school. Victim 2 was also a 16-year-old male student; Morsi exposed herself and performed a sexual act on him around June 2014. Removed from the classroom immediately upon the school learning of allegations.

Charged June 2014 with 20 counts of rape in the third degree and 20 counts of criminal sexual act in the third degree. Arrested a second time the day after the initial arrest in connection with victim 2. If convicted of all charges, faced up to 8 years in prison. Has been attending a mental health program since July 2014; diagnosed with 'depressive disorder.'

Pleaded guilty January 16, 2015 to one count of rape in the third degree and one count of criminal sexual act in the third degree. Sentenced by Queens Supreme Court judge to complete one year of outpatient psychiatric counseling; upon successful completion, to receive 10 years probation. Also required to surrender New York teaching certificate and register as a sex offender.

NOTE: Defendant age approximately 38 at 2013 first offense (age 40 at January 2015 plea). Conditional sentence structure: 1-year treatment → 10yr probation. Incident with victim 2 appears to have occurred while the victim 1 investigation was already underway. Charge reduction from 40 total counts to 2.
