Former art teacher at Marked Tree High School, Marked Tree, Arkansas (Poinsett County). Lived in Jonesboro, Craighead County. Age 25 at time of offense. Married.

Accused of having sexual intercourse with four male students between January and April 2017. Goline initiated contact by sending increasingly sexual text messages; in at least one instance she sent an explicit photo of herself in a thong. She texted one victim her address while he was visiting Jonesboro and invited him to her apartment; she picked up another student in Marked Tree and drove him to her Jonesboro apartment where they had intercourse twice. Two students were present at her apartment on the same night on at least one occasion. She texted a fourth victim that she wanted to have sex with him but he was 'too young.'

Investigation began in April 2017 when a parent threatened to come to the school, telling administrators Goline was having sex with multiple students. Before being formally questioned, Goline walked into the principal's office crying; when asked if she needed to tell him something, she said 'I'm not going to lose my husband.' When asked if she had done anything inappropriate, she admitted: 'We had sex.' At the police station she said, 'I already admitted that I did it, how could I incriminate myself anymore,' then chose to remain silent.

Initially arrested late April 2017 on first-degree sexual assault (Class A felony; 10–40 years or life). After the DA determined the acts occurred in Craighead County rather than Poinsett County, Arkansas State Police re-investigated and re-arrested Goline in September 2017. Pleaded guilty March 2018 to sexual indecency with a child. Sentenced to 42 months probation, court costs and fines, and sex offender registration.

Charge reduction from Class A felony (10–40 years) to sexual indecency = no prison. In-office admission before formal questioning is an unusual pattern.
