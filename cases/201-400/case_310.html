Former art teacher at Marked Tree High School, Marked Tree, Arkansas (Poinsett County). Lived in Jonesboro, Craighead County. Age 25 at time of offense. Married.

Sexual intercourse with at least three male students between January and April 2016, and sexual conduct with a fourth. Grooming followed a consistent pattern: texting began through class-related contact or gaming apps, escalated to sexual messages, then to in-person meetups at her Jonesboro apartment.

Victim #1 was a student at East Poinsett County School (not Marked Tree HS); Goline texted him her address while he was visiting Jonesboro with a friend and he came to her apartment where they had intercourse. Victim #3 was in her class; she sent him a photograph for class, texting escalated, and she drove him from Marked Tree to her Jonesboro apartment for intercourse, then returned him that evening. Victim #4 was also in her class; contact began through a phone pool game, escalated to sexual texts; Goline picked him up and drove him to Jonesboro where they had intercourse twice. Victims #3 and #4 were both at her apartment on the same night; victim #4 had to persuade her to drive them back at 2:00 a.m. A fourth student told Goline he was 18 but she later learned he was significantly younger. Victim #2 received escalating sexual texts and a photo of Goline in a thong, but Goline texted him she wanted to have sex with him yet he was 'too young' — no physical contact with this victim.

Investigation began April 26, 2017, when a parent threatened to come to the school to harm a teacher, telling Coach Wilburn that Goline was having sex with multiple students. Principal Matt Wright alerted Superintendent Annesa Thompson. Before being formally questioned, Goline walked into the principal's office crying; when Wright asked if she needed to say something, she said 'I'm not going to lose my husband.' She admitted: 'We had sex' — four students total. At the Marked Tree Police Department, Chief Michael Matlock read Goline her rights; she said 'Yes, sir' when asked to confirm the statements.

DA Scott Ellington determined the acts occurred in Craighead County (her apartment), not Poinsett County. Arkansas State Police re-investigated. First arrested April 28, 2017; re-arrested September 2017 after investigation transferred to Craighead County. Charge filed: one count of sexual assault in the first degree pertaining to Victim #3 (under 18 at time of intercourse). Paschal v. State, 2012 Ark. 127, was cited as limiting additional charges.

Pleaded guilty March 2018 to sexual indecency with a child. Sentenced March 28, 2018 to 42 months probation, court costs and fines, and sex offender registration.
