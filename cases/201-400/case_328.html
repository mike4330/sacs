Rachel Duncan #328 sentenced

StateOhioCourtBelmont County Common Pleas Court
JudgeFrank Fregiato Incident Date2014-01-01
Defendant Age32
Prison Sentence1.5 yrs

Former teacher and softball coach at St. Clairsville High School, St. Clairsville-Richland City Schools, Belmont County, Ohio. Age approximately 32 at start of offense; 35 at sentencing.

Victim: female student and softball player on Duncan's team. The victim was a juvenile at the start of the charged conduct (January 2014) and became an adult at some point prior to May 2016. The victim's mother stated Duncan had groomed her daughter 'from a time before my daughter went through puberty.' Duncan and the victim exchanged over 10,000 text messages over the course of the relationship. Duncan threatened to kill herself if the victim disclosed the relationship.

Charged conduct: nine incidents from January 2014 through May 2016. Forensic analysis recovered in excess of 9,000 text messages from spring 2015 to spring 2016. Duncan placed on unpaid leave August 1, 2016. Arraigned December 15, 2016 on nine counts of sexual battery (third-degree felony, each up to 5 years; maximum 45 years); pled not guilty.

Pled guilty to nine amended counts of gross sexual imposition (fourth-degree felony). Sentenced May 30, 2017 by Judge Frank Fregiato to 1.5 years prison (all counts concurrent); Tier 1 sex offender designation. Joint recommendation from state and defense for concurrent sentences. Prosecutor Dan Fry.

Victim impact statement: 'The trauma and sexual abuse I experienced throughout my entire high school career... She sexually abused me; sexual advances and attacks increased over time... Coach Duncan told me I could not tell, or she would kill herself.' Victim's mother: 'Rachel Duncan stalked my daughter. Groomed her from a time before my daughter went through puberty.'

NOTE: Charge reduction from nine counts of third-degree sexual battery to nine counts of fourth-degree gross sexual imposition. Victim age at start of charged conduct unknown from available sources; victim was juvenile at the start (January 2014) and an adult before conclusion (May 2016), suggesting approximate age 16-17 in January 2014. Grooming preceded the charged conduct by an unspecified period.
