Lauren Harrington-Cooper #280 sentenced

StatePennsylvaniaCourtLuzerne County Court of Common Pleas
JudgeMichael T. Vough Incident Date2013-09
Defendant Age31 Victim Age17
Prison Sentence0.75 yrs

Former English teacher and hall monitor at Wyoming Valley West High School, Kingston, Pennsylvania. Two confirmed male victims: an 18-year-old senior (Victim 1) and a 17-year-old student (Victim 2). Harrington-Cooper had previously been employed at Wyoming Valley West Middle School, where administrative issues were documented — characterized by the superintendent as performance-related, not sexual conduct.

Grooming of Victim 2 followed a text-and-escalation pattern: after the student left his phone number on her desk with a heart drawn on it, she initiated texting, escalating to sexually explicit messages and instructing him not to tell anyone. After approximately two weeks, encounters moved to parked cars. In November 2013, she contacted him at 2:30 a.m. and performed oral sex. She repeatedly asked him to delete their messages. With Victim 1 (18-year-old senior), she performed oral sex three times and had intercourse twice. A third arrest while free on bail indicated possible additional victims. She was also observed continuing to contact students while on bail.

First arrested December 2013 for conduct with Victim 1 (institutional sexual assault). Second arrest January 9, 2014 on new charges involving Victim 2; bail set at $25,000. Third arrest followed as additional students came forward. Suspended without pay after first arrest; resigned January 22, 2014. Pleaded guilty in September 2014 to institutional sexual assault, corruption of minors, and unlawful contact with a minor. Judge Vough immediately sentenced her to 9 to 23 months in Luzerne County Correctional Facility. Parole first sought June 2015 after completing 9-month minimum; granted August 19, 2015 after approximately 11 months served. Required to register as sex offender; prohibited from being within 1,000 feet of areas where children congregate or 100 yards of schools; restricted to Luzerne and Lackawanna Counties.

NOTE: Serial grooming pattern across at least two (and possibly more) victims with consistent escalation methodology. Superintendent acknowledged prior issues at the middle school level but declined to specify; raises a possible prior-warning-ignored dynamic unconfirmed from public records. Continuing contact with victims while on bail resulted in bail conditions being tightened at second arrest. The 9–23 month sentence was the floor; approximately 11 months actually served.
