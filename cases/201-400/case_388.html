Lynn Burge #388 sentenced

State Texas
Court Cooke County District Court
Incident Date 2017-10-01
Defendant Age 31
Victim Age 16
Prison Sentence 0.0 yrs

Former teacher of culinary arts, child development, and principles of human services at Muenster High School, Muenster, Cooke County, Texas. Married with two children at time of offense. Age approximately 31 at time of first offense.

Victim 1 was a 16-year-old male student during the 2017-18 school year. The relationship began in fall 2017 when Burge sent nude photos via Snapchat. One night, Burge and the student drove rural backroads while drinking. They then went to Burge's home where she performed oral sex on him and they had sexual intercourse; the student fell asleep in her child's bedroom before she woke him and invited him into her bed. Her children were not home. Victim 2 was an 18-year-old male student (adult at time of encounter). In March 2018 — Burge's birthday — they had sex at her sister's home after another night of heavy drinking.

In September 2018, the Texas Department of Family and Protective Services received an anonymous tip. Texas Ranger Bruce Sherman conducted an informal interview with Burge at the State Fair of Texas in Dallas, where she was serving as a school field trip chaperone. Burge admitted having sex with the 18-year-old, exchanging nude photos with the 16-year-old, and drinking and backroading with the 16-year-old, but claimed she did not remember whether they had sex: 'I don't know. I hope not. I don't know.' Resigned from Muenster ISD days before her arrest. Arrested October 18, 2018; $125,000 bond.

Originally indicted on 2 counts improper relationship between educator and student (second-degree felony) and 1 count online solicitation of a minor (third-degree felony). Online solicitation charge dismissed per plea agreement. Pleaded guilty in late November 2019 to 2 counts improper relationship. Sentenced to 5 years deferred adjudication probation on each count (concurrent). Not required to register as a sex offender; teaching license surrendered. DA John Warren consulted with both victims' families before accepting the plea.

NOTE: Claimed intoxication-based amnesia for both incidents. Alcohol featured in both encounters. Texas deferred adjudication = no formal conviction if 5-year probation conditions are met. No sex offender registration is notably lenient — most TX improper relationship cases carry registration. Online solicitation (nude photo exchange with 16-year-old) dropped from plea. sentence_years = 0.
