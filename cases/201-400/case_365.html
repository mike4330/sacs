Laura Lynn Cross #365 sentenced

StateOhioCourtSummit County Common Pleas Court
JudgeTammy O'BrienIncident Date2013-08-01
Defendant Age32Victim Age14
Prison Sentence3.0 yrs

Former English teacher at Buchtel High School, Akron Public Schools, Summit County, Ohio. Victim was a male student she first knew as his 8th grade English teacher. Age approximately 30 at start of relationship; 32 at start of the charged offense period (August 2013). The relationship produced a child, born 2015.

The student's father raised concerns about Cross's behavior with his son to a Buchtel High School official and to Tallmadge Police as early as 2012, when the student was a freshman — no charges were filed and no meaningful institutional response followed. Cross resigned her teaching position in January 2015 while the relationship continued.

Cross persuaded the victim's mother (who had custody) to allow the teen to move into Cross's Tallmadge home under a court-approved 'partial parental custody' arrangement, on the pretext that she would continue to 'mentor' him. A child was born in 2015 and kept secret for approximately two years; charges were filed in August 2017 only after an anonymous tipster contacted the student's father and disclosed the child's existence. Akron Public Schools stated it was unaware of the case until a news investigation alerted them, suggesting the 2012 complaints were not preserved or communicated within the district.

Indicted on three counts of sexual battery (3rd degree felonies) covering August 1, 2013 through September 6, 2016. Pleaded guilty March 2018. Sentenced June 6, 2018 by Summit County Common Pleas Judge Tammy O'Brien to three concurrent terms of three years in prison. Designated a Tier 3 sex offender, requiring address registration every 90 days for life.

NOTE: prior-warning-ignored — the student's father reported concerns to both the school and police in 2012; neither acted. The court-approved 'partial parental custody' arrangement Cross engineered to move the victim into her home is one of the most elaborate victim-access schemes in the DB. The concealed child, born 2015, was undiscovered for two years until a third-party tipster disclosed it. Father's characterization: 'It was a straight failure from the system.'
