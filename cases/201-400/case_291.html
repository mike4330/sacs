Jennifer Dalton #291 sentenced

StateNevadaCourtCarson City District Court
Incident Date2011-03
Defendant Age33 Victim Age17
Prison Sentence1.0 yr

Former English teacher at Carson High School, Carson City, Nevada. One male victim, age 17. Sexual relationship occurred between March 4 and April 1, 2011. Age approximately 33 at time of offense.

The sheriff's office was called to the school August 31, 2011 to investigate 'inappropriate activities by a female teacher with a 17-year-old male student.' Dalton was placed on administrative leave with pay September 1, 2011 and resigned November 7, 2011. An arrest warrant was issued October 23, 2012; she turned herself in the following day. Arraigned April 2, 2013; entered not-guilty plea. Pleaded no contest August 20, 2013 to one felony count of sexual conduct between a school employee and a pupil. Sentenced October 15, 2013 by the Carson City District Court to a minimum of 12 months and a maximum of 30 months in state prison. No sex offender registration required under the Nevada statute.

At sentencing, defense argued for probation on grounds of no prior criminal history, and unusually argued that the conduct was 'less criminal' because it occurred on school grounds rather than 'different hotels throughout the area.' The judge rejected this framing and cited Dalton's position of authority over students. ADA Mark Krueger argued for a custodial sentence emphasizing the teacher's position of trust. DA Neil Rombardo issued a public statement: 'Far too often, the victim is forgotten in these types of cases. The impact this had on him and his family is unfair and should have never occurred.'

NOTE: No sex offender registration required — the Nevada statute under which she was charged (sexual conduct between school employee and pupil) does not trigger registration. Notable defense argument that on-campus conduct was a mitigating factor — the inverse of the usual prosecutorial framing. Approximately 17 months elapsed between offense end (April 2011) and warrant (October 2012).
