Kendahl Coode #284 sentenced

Also known as: Kendahl Erin Coode

State Tennessee
Court Montgomery County Circuit Court
Incident Date 2022-10
Defendant Age 26
Victim Age 16
Prison Sentence 0.0 yrs

Former English teacher at Northwest High School, Clarksville, Tennessee. Employed by Clarksville-Montgomery County School System (CMCSS) since August 2021; incident occurred in her second year. One victim, age 16 at time of offense (gender not specified). On two occasions in October 2022, Coode used online communications to expose the teenager to sexual material. Age approximately 26 at time of offense.

On November 1, 2022, CMCSS was informed of the investigation and immediately placed Coode on alternative worksite duty with a no-student-contact order. She resigned December 2, 2022. Indicted by the July 2023 term of the Montgomery County Grand Jury on two counts of solicitation of sexual exploitation of a minor; arrested July 17, 2023. A no-contact order was issued the same day by the Circuit Court, which found Coode 'a threat to the alleged victim.' Pleaded guilty to both counts January 26, 2024. Granted judicial diversion May 15, 2024 under T.C.A. §40-35-313; will remain on the Sexual Offender Registry despite diversion. Defense attorney Timothy Warren.

NOTE: Second CMCSS/Montgomery County case resolved via judicial diversion (cf. Alvarez #283); both involved online conduct and first-time-offender diversion arguments. Unlike Alvarez, Coode accepted Sex Offender Registry placement as part of the diversion terms rather than contesting it administratively. Conduct was entirely online (no confirmed in-person contact); indictment declined to specify the material involved.
