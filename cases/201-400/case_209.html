Former teacher at Mountain View School, Reedley, Kings Canyon Unified School District, Fresno County, California. Male victim, age 14, enrolled in independent study program; met with Jackson weekly at Dunlap Leadership Academy. Defendant age 39 at arrest (September 2021); approximately 38 at onset of conduct.

Fresno County Sheriff's Office received a tip about a sexual relationship between Jackson and the student. Sexual assault alleged to have occurred during one-on-one weekly meetings at Dunlap Leadership Academy. A photo of a pregnancy test was found on the victim's phone during investigation. Jackson wiped her phone on August 22, 2021 — days before her September 2021 arrest — and then restored it from a prior backup within minutes.

Arrested September 2021; originally charged with rape, continuous sexual abuse of a child, oral copulation with a child, and lewd acts with a child. Bail set at $215,000, later reported at $365,000; bonded out. Multiple defense continuances from 2024 through early 2026.

July 10, 2024: Fresno County Superior Court ruled Jackson to stand trial after investigators presented cell phone evidence. Multiple trial dates vacated on defense motions through October 2025.

January 16, 2026: Entered People v. West plea (California Alford equivalent) on Counts 1 and 2; remaining counts dismissed. Referred to probation for report, recommendation, and Prop 63 (Jessica's Law sex offender management) evaluation. Sentencing scheduled March 19, 2026.

Sentenced March 19, 2026, by Judge Raj S. Badhesha: 1 year in prison; sex offender registration for 20 years (per plea deal). Taken into custody immediately at sentencing. Victim's father addressed the court, stating the boy had not left his bedroom in a year.

NOTE: Phone-wiping days before arrest constitutes strong evidence of consciousness of guilt. Pregnancy test photo on victim's phone is notable. People v. West plea means defendant did not admit factual guilt. One of the longer case timelines in the DB — arrested 2021, sentenced 2026, ~4.5 years from arrest to sentencing.
