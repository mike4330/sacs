Former science teacher at Bottineau High School, Bottineau, North Dakota (Bottineau County). Age 24 at arrest and time of offense; born 1991.

Multiple male victims: at least two, ages 15 and 17; a separate complaint alleged contact with another 15-year-old in November 2015 (court records do not clarify whether the two 15-year-olds are the same person). Court documents did not confirm whether the victims were Bottineau High students.

Placed on administrative leave December 14, 2015. Charged December 15, 2015 with one count of Class C felony corruption of a minor; two additional Class C felony counts of sexual assault filed January 7, 2016. Bond set at $10,000 by Judge Michael Sturdevant. Resigned December 31, 2015; later surrendered teaching license at the request of the North Dakota Education Standards and Practices Board.

Under a plea agreement, the Class C felony sexual assault charges were dismissed and replaced with two Class B misdemeanor sexual assault counts; the corruption charge was replaced with two Class A misdemeanor contributing to deprivation of a minor counts. Sentenced September 15, 2016 by Judge Lee Christofferson to one year in jail, 315 days suspended, one day credit for time served — 49 days to serve in one-week increments at Heart of America Correctional and Treatment Center, Rugby, ND. Court fees of $325. No sex offender registration.
