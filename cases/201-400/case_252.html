State v. Valle-Tejeda #252 sentenced

StateFloridaCourtMiami-Dade Circuit Court, Eleventh Judicial Circuit, Div. F009
JudgeJoseph PerkinsIncident Date2007-09-01
Defendant Age27Victim Age11
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former reading teacher at Henry H. Filer Middle School and Hialeah Senior High School, Hialeah, Florida. Employed by Miami-Dade County Public Schools for 20 years. Age 27 at time of first known offense.

Engaged in sexual and lascivious relationships with at least three female students across approximately 12 years at multiple schools. Grooming method was consistently presenting herself as a mother figure, providing financial assistance and meeting victims' emotional needs.

V-2 (youngest known victim): Was 11 years old in 2007 when Valle-Tejeda was her 6th-grade reading teacher at Filer Middle. Relationship lasted approximately three years, with most inappropriate activity on school grounds. Conduct included kissing in the classroom, phone sex by text, and instructing the victim to masturbate via text — always instructing her to delete messages afterward.

V-1 (primary reporting victim): Was 14 in 2009 at Filer Middle School. Relationship lasted approximately four years through high school at Westland Hialeah Senior High. Conduct included showering together, kissing, oral sex, and digital penetration that caused bleeding and loss of virginity. Valle-Tejeda excused V-1 from other classes to be alone with her, signed her out of school without parental consent, and took her to the beach, restaurants, and her residence. Investigation found they were absent from school on 11 of the same days.

V-3: Junior-year student at Hialeah High School (age 17) who met Valle-Tejeda through Instagram. Regular contact by text with Valle-Tejeda insisting she delete messages. Met at Hooters, Flanigans, and nightclubs. V-3 stopped attending the offender's residence because Valle-Tejeda would become intoxicated.

In 2018, a parent complained to the Hialeah High School principal that Valle-Tejeda had invited students to her home and served them alcohol. Police received a summary of that 2018 conference showing no effective action was taken. V-1 reported the abuse to the Hialeah High School principal on February 25, 2021. Valle-Tejeda was arrested March 26, 2021 at a traffic stop on NW 27th Ave. Originally charged with 11 counts including offenses against students by authority figures, sexual battery on a minor by an adult, lewd and lascivious battery and molestation, tampering with physical evidence, luring or enticing a child, and child abuse.

Pled guilty to two counts of aggravated child abuse/great bodily harm (1st degree felony). Sentenced September 15, 2022 by Judge Joseph Perkins; adjudication of guilt WITHHELD. Placed on community control (no GPS) followed by probation. No prison, no sex offender registration.

NOTE: Adjudication withheld on aggravated child abuse charges rather than sexual offenses — Valle-Tejeda is technically not a convicted felon and likely does not face sex offender registration. Extraordinary outcome given scope: three female victims, 12+ years, multiple schools, original charges including capital-eligible sexual battery. The 2018 complaint about alcohol at her home generated a documented conference but no termination — a clear prior-warning-ignored pattern. Victim count may be higher; other students are mentioned as aware of or involved in the romantic relationship.
