Dance instructor at Dance Obsessions, a family-owned studio in Aston, Pennsylvania (Delaware County). Defendant age not stated in sources; per charge grade (statutory sexual assault, 4-8 years older than victim), was between 19 and 23 at time of offense.

Victim was a 15-year-old male dance student who had attended the studio since age 10. In spring 2018, Green had sex with the victim more than 50 times. The pair also exchanged nude images via Snapchat. A second sexual relationship occurred in 2019.

In November 2019, the victim's mother noticed declining grades, depression, alcohol use, and vaping; the teen eventually disclosed the relationship. Investigation began November 2019. Green surrendered to police September 4, 2020; charged with statutory sexual assault (18 Pa.C.S. § 3122.1(a), 4-8 years older) and institutional sexual assault of a child.

Pleaded guilty (non-negotiated) April 27, 2021. Sentenced July 13, 2021 by Judge James P. Bradley to 2-12 months confinement plus 3 years probation. Conditions: no contact with victim and family, DNA testing, sex offenders unit supervision, psychosexual evaluation.

NOTE: Defendant was a family friend who had known the victim since age 10; DA characterized the offense as exploitation of 'trust built over several years.'
