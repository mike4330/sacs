Beyonce Milagro Mass #321 sentenced

State Virginia
Court Augusta County Circuit Court
Judge Sean Workowski
Incident Date 2024-11-27
Defendant Age 23
Prison Sentence 1.0 yr

Former teacher's aide at Valley Academy Regional Alternative School, Fishersville, Virginia (Augusta County). A regional alternative school jointly operated by Augusta County Public Schools and Waynesboro Public Schools, opened 2019. Age 23 at time of offense and arrest; DOB August 3, 2001.

Multiple male victims (at least two in Augusta County; at least one in Rockbridge County). Investigation began December 2024 after Child Protective Services notified the Augusta County Sheriff's Office of sexual assault allegations. All inappropriate relationships alleged to have occurred off campus. First victim: male juvenile caught sneaking out of his home at night to meet Mass; alleged sexual abuse on at least two occasions. Second victim: alleged approximately 15 sexual encounters including once at a local truck stop; prosecutor William Sukovich described Mass as a 'groomer.'

Indicted by Augusta County grand jury May 27, 2025 on two counts of taking indecent liberties with a child by a custodian (18.2-370.1, Class 6 felony). Arrested May 28, 2025. Indicted on three additional identical charges in June 2025 (Augusta County). Separately indicted in Rockbridge County July 14, 2025 on one felony count of indecent liberties with child by custodian and one misdemeanor contributing to delinquency of a minor. Bond granted June 2, 2025 with electronic monitoring; revoked June 9, 2025 following second arrest; bond denied on Commonwealth's appeal before Judge Workowski.

Pled guilty October 9, 2025 in Augusta County Circuit Court (Case CR25000220-00). Sentenced to 5 years penitentiary, 4 years suspended, to run concurrently with another sentence; 3 years supervised probation after release. Costs $904. Prosecutor William Sukovich (ASA); defense attorney Scott Alleman.

NOTE: Victim ages not stated in available sources. The 'concurrent with another sentence' notation likely reflects concurrent resolution of the June 2025 Augusta County indictment (5 additional counts total across both Augusta County cases). Disposition of Rockbridge County charges unknown from available records.
