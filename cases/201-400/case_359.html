Nicole Souza #359 sentenced

State Connecticut
Court New London Superior Court
Incident Date 2018-06-01
Defendant Age 37
Victim Age 15
Prison Sentence 0.5 yrs

Former educator at Waterford Country School, a nonprofit serving special needs children, in Waterford, New London County, Connecticut. Employed there for 13 years before firing. Victim was a 15-year-old male in state Department of Children and Families (DCF) care, enrolled in a short-term life-skills program Souza supervised. Age 37 at time of offense.

In June 2018, the victim was taken by ambulance to a hospital with a blood-alcohol concentration of 0.202%; investigation revealed Souza had purchased liquor for him and had a sexual relationship with him. Inappropriate contact continued after the victim was discharged from the school program in June. School administrators met with Souza immediately upon learning of the allegations, determined she had violated school policy, and fired her. Waterford Country School offered full cooperation with the investigation and reached out to DCF for a review.

Arrested approximately October 8, 2018 and charged with sexual assault in the second degree, risk of injury to a child, and sale of liquor to a minor. Held on $50,000 bond.

Souza pleaded guilty to risk of injury to a minor (CGS 53-21). Sentenced September 9, 2019 to 6 months in prison and 5 years probation. She apologized in court.

NOTE: Behavioral-health and non-school setting — Waterford Country School is a nonprofit, not a traditional school. Victim was a vulnerable minor in state care. Charge reduction from sexual assault 2nd degree to risk of injury to a minor. Alcohol was the proximate trigger for discovery.
