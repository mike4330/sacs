Erica Gomez #381 sentenced

State Texas
Court Travis County District Court
Incident Date 2018-08-01
Defendant Age 23
Victim Age 15
Prison Sentence 0.0 yrs

Former math teacher at Bowie High School, Austin, Travis County, Texas. Had previously served as a private math tutor to the victim before being hired as a teacher at his school. Age 23 at time of offense.

Victim was a 15-year-old male student. Gomez admitted to police that she and the victim had sex four to five times, with encounters occurring in her car. They communicated via Snapchat using the screen name 'Special' (username 'Ericadinora'). In one message, Gomez wrote: 'Babe ... We've had car sex twice ... Like that blows my mind LOL.'

Discovered in early October 2018 when the victim's mother, awakened by the sound of the front door, found her son in bed fully clothed. She seized his phone and found explicit Snapchat messages. Clicking the 'Special' screen name revealed 'Ericadinora'; after finding a photo on Facebook, the mother recognized the woman as her son's former math tutor, who had since become his school teacher. Gomez was arrested October 8, 2018 and confessed to investigators. Bond set at $75,000. School principal sent a letter to parents and initiated termination.

Pleaded guilty November 12, 2019 to sexual assault of a child. Sentenced to 10 years deferred adjudication supervision, 200 community service hours, sex offender treatment, substance abuse assessment, no contact with victim, and barred from all minor children. Lifetime sex offender registration required. Improper relationship with a student charge dismissed per sentencing agreement.

NOTE: Deferred adjudication = no formal conviction entered if conditions met over 10-year supervision period; no prison time. Gomez was both the victim's private tutor and later his school teacher — access predated school employment. Travis County DA's office stated the guilty plea would result in teaching license revocation.
