Carrie Njoroge #351 sentenced

State Missouri
Court Jasper County Circuit Court
Judge David Mouton
Incident Date 2014-01-01
Defendant Age 29
Victim Age 18
Prison Sentence 0.0 yrs

Former choir teacher at Webb City High School, Webb City, Jasper County, Missouri. Age approximately 29 at time of offense (age 33 at sentencing October 2017). Victim was an 18-year-old male student — a legal adult. Sexual relationship occurred over approximately three months and included oral sex on multiple occasions and sexual intercourse on April 15, 2014 in her school office.

Webb City School District became aware of misconduct April 17, 2014 and placed Njoroge on paid administrative leave the same day. She resigned April 18. Warrant issued April 23; turned herself in to Christian County Sheriff's Office April 24 (she likely resided in Christian County), then bonded out. Waived preliminary hearing July 10, 2014 in Jasper County Circuit Court.

Defense attorneys Dee Wampler and Joseph Passinise filed motions to dismiss in October 2014 arguing the teacher-student sexual contact statute was unconstitutional, citing Lawrence v. Texas (2003): that the student had reached the age of consent and the parties were engaging in constitutionally-protected adult consensual sexual activity. Passinise argued the statute was overbroad. Motion to dismiss denied by Judge David Mouton (hearing October 30, 2014).

Entered an Alford plea October 24, 2017 in Jasper County Circuit Court before jury selection — admitting no guilt but acknowledging likelihood of conviction. Sentence: suspended imposition of sentence (SIS) plus supervised probation; sex offender registration; teaching certificate surrendered. Webb City Superintendent Anthony Rossetti issued statement expressing shock.

NOTE: Victim was 18 — an adult — but Missouri statute prohibits teacher-student sexual contact regardless of age. SIS in Missouri means no conviction is formally entered if probation completed successfully, similar to deferred adjudication. Defense's Lawrence v. Texas constitutional challenge is analytically significant and unusual. Approximately 3.5-year gap between arrest and sentencing. Defendant age and victim age unknown from initial sources; filled in from subsequent reporting.
