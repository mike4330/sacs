Alissa McCommon #203 sentenced

StateTennesseeCourtTipton County Circuit Court
Incident Date2019-01-01
Defendant Age34Victim Age12
Prison Sentence25.0 yrs

Former English Language Arts and Social Studies teacher at Charger Academy, Covington, Tipton County, Tennessee. Multiple male victims ages 12–17; 21 potential victims identified during investigation; five prosecuted. Defendant age 39 at sentencing (December 20, 2024); approximately 34 at onset of earliest known offense (~2019).

Covington Police began investigating in August 2023 after receiving information from Tennessee Department of Children Services about possible sexual misconduct involving a teacher and a former student. Multiple juveniles came forward stating McCommon befriended them as their former teacher, played video games (including Fortnite) with them, connected via mobile apps, sent inappropriate photos via Snapchat, and requested sexual relations. At least one victim was met at a party; another through Fortnite. McCommon used a code word to verify victims were alone before sending nude photos on Snapchat.

Primary victim (16 at time of investigation) stated that when he was 12, McCommon performed oral sex on him without consent. McCommon became pregnant; paternity of the child confirmed by DNA testing. Victim's mother has custody of the baby. In other texts with this victim, McCommon threatened to kill herself if he ended the relationship.

Arrested September 8, 2023; charged with rape of a child; posted $25,000 bond same day with no-contact conditions. On September 26, investigators received information that McCommon contacted the child via a previously unknown phone number using the same code word, sent text messages saying he would 'regret doing this,' and admitted to a sexual encounter. Re-arrested September 28, 2023 on charges of coercion of a witness, aggravated stalking, and harassment.

Originally indicted on 23 counts. Trial scheduled January 21, 2025; plea entered December 20, 2024 before Judge Blake Neill. Guilty plea to 12 counts: 1 count rape of a child (25 years at 100%); 2 counts statutory rape by an authority figure (8 years each at 100%); 3 counts aggravated statutory rape (2 years each at 100%); 3 counts sexual exploitation of a minor by electronic means (2 years each at 100%); 2 counts solicitation of a minor — aggravated statutory rape (2 years each at 100%); 1 count violation of the Child Protective Act (2 years at 100%). All concurrent: 25 years, no possibility of parole. Lifetime community supervision as violent sex offender on Tennessee Sex Offender Registry. No contact with any victim, including the infant child fathered by her 12-year-old victim. Teaching license revoked.

DA Mark Davidson confirmed victims supported the plea deal. One victim's mother in court: 'You took innocence from my son before puberty... Your mind is sick and twisted for looking at a child and being turned on. You will burn in hell.'

NOTE: Among the most severe cases in this DB — 25 years no parole, pregnancy from a 12-year-old victim, 21 potential victims, and post-arrest witness intimidation. Victim onset age (12) is among the youngest in this DB. Incident date is approximate; earliest known offense ~2019 based on victim's age at investigation. Multiple grooming vectors: gaming (Fortnite), Snapchat, in-person parties.
