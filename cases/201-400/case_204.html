Casey McGrath #204 sentenced

State Tennessee
Court Hamilton County Criminal Court
Incident Date 2022-05-01
Defendant Age 27
Prison Sentence 0.0 yrs

Former geometry and algebra teacher (10th grade) and assistant volleyball coach at Central High School, Chattanooga, Hamilton County, Tennessee. DOB September 24, 1994. Also taught at East Ridge Middle School in prior years. Was voted 'Teacher of the Month' in October 2021; held a mathematics degree from Lee University and a master's in secondary education from Vanderbilt University. Defendant age 27 at time of offense.

Suspended with pay in May 2022 after investigation into 'inappropriate physical contact with a student that does not result in harm' of a 'sexual nature.' Not rehired for 2022–23 school year; suspended without pay March 31, 2023. Grand jury indicted August 14, 2023 on aggravated statutory rape: sexual penetration with a person at least 13 but under 18, defendant at least 10 years older than victim. Arrested August 18, 2023 by Hamilton County Sheriff's Office (Officer Rominger, Shane); posted $10,000 bond.

Pleaded guilty to Statutory Rape (Class E felony, reduced from aggravated statutory rape Class D). Disposition April 10, 2025: Judicial Diversion — 4 years probation, suspended; state supervised probation; sex offender registration required; no contact with victim. If conditions satisfied, case dismissed from record after four years.

NOTE: Victim age not disclosed; indictment language (13–18, defendant at least 10 years older) places victim at most 17. Judicial Diversion is Tennessee's deferred adjudication equivalent — conviction expunged if probation completed. Charge reduction from Class D to Class E felony at plea. Approximately 14-month gap between suspension and arrest.
