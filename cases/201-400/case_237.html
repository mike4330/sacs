Rene Elena Carter #237 closed

StateMontanaCourtLewis and Clark County District Court
Incident Date2015-01-01
Victim Age17
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former teacher at Capital High School, Helena, Montana. Victim was a 17-year-old male student at the time of the relationship (2015). Defendant age not reported in available sources.

In 2015, the school principal brought a video showing Carter in inappropriate contact with the student to Helena police. The student denied sexual activity and the investigation stalled. In 2016, a family member of the student approached police having heard about the relationship; the investigation was reopened. The student subsequently admitted to 25 sexual encounters and stated they had planned to continue the relationship once he turned 18, but never did. Carter's husband told detectives the student had been abusive toward Carter — hitting her, pulling her hair, demanding her attention — calling him 'an unbelievable parasite' and stating Carter required counselling as a result.

Carter was charged in 2018 with three counts of felony sexual intercourse without consent. The case was delayed five times before reaching trial at Lewis and Clark County District Court.

On December 3, 2020, Carter entered an Alford plea — acknowledging the weight of evidence would likely lead to conviction without admitting guilt. Two rape charges were dropped; the third was amended to criminal endangerment. The plea explicitly stated the criminal endangerment was not for the sexual relationship but for conduct that 'created a substantial risk of death or serious bodily injury' to the victim.

Sentence: 3-year deferred sentence, $80 in costs. No prison time.

NOTE: The amended plea charge (criminal endangerment) is formally divorced from the sexual conduct — one of the most complete charge eliminations in the DB. The husband's active defense of Carter and characterization of the 17-year-old victim as an 'unbelievable parasite' is an extreme example of victim-blaming by proxy. Case delayed five times over two years before resolution. sentence_years=0 per no-prison convention.
