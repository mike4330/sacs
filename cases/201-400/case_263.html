Teacher at Lake Charles Charter Academy, Iowa, Louisiana (Calcasieu Parish). Age 35 at arrest. Iowa is a community near Lake Charles.

Three male victims, ages 13 and 14. Odom endeared herself to the victims' families, developing trusting relationships. Contact included texting at least one victim explicit nude photographs of herself and performing a sex act on him in a classroom. Contact occurred on multiple occasions.

A victim's mother reported to Lake Charles Police on approximately April 19, 2015. Arrested April 21, 2015. Originally charged with four counts of indecent behavior with juveniles, three counts of oral sexual battery, and one count of sexual battery. Bond set at $120,000.

Pled guilty to indecent behavior with juveniles charges in early 2018. Sentenced May 11, 2018 to five years in prison per count with all but 18 months suspended, concurrent; four years supervised probation after release. Taken into custody immediately at sentencing. Prosecutor Denisse Parrales; defense attorney Walt Sanchez.

Parrales at sentencing: 'She endeared herself to the families, developed what would be a trusting relationship and preyed upon that relationship... One of these victims did not attend high school. She really should not be around children.' After arrest, Odom was reportedly observed attending juvenile sports events; prosecutors cited this as lack of remorse. Defense said she attended to watch her own children play.
