Age approximately 27 at start of offense (age 29 at sentencing April 2023). Victim was a 15-year-old male student. Alleged relationship occurred between September 2021 and April 2022.

Case originated from a harassment protection order filed February 11, 2022 by the victim's mother, who had learned of an alleged relationship and contacted the school, which told her a criminal investigation was already underway. The mother documented that McPhillips continued to contact the victim via social media despite repeated requests to stop.
