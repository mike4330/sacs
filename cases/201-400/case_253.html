State v. Moser #253 sentenced

State North Dakota
Court Cass County District Court
Incident Date 2016-05
Defendant Age 35
Victim Age 14
Prison Sentence 10.0 yrs

Former teacher at Liberty Middle School, West Fargo, North Dakota, employed since the 2016-2017 school year. Age 35 at time of first offense. Age 37 at arrest.

Six victims total; sexual contact with two male victims — one age 15 and one age 14. The remaining four were victims of luring by computer. Moser maintained at least three separate Snapchat accounts, using them to send male students explicit photos — including bikini photos, photos of her legs, and photos from the shower — while keeping the accounts hidden from her husband. She told students she did not want her husband to know about the accounts. Former students described Moser asking to be invited to student parties, inquiring which boys attended, and exhibiting what they characterized as romantic interest in male students. Valley News Live obtained video showing Moser straddling a student in her classroom. Conduct documented as beginning as early as May 2016.

Former student from Park Christian High School (more than 10 years before charges) described the same pattern of conduct — unprofessional behavior, inappropriate focus on male students — suggesting a longstanding pattern predating her West Fargo employment.

Investigation began in June 2018 after the West Fargo Police Department received an anonymous tip about possible sexual contact between an adult female and a juvenile male. Moser resigned July 12, 2018. Bail set at $30,000.

Pled guilty August 27, 2018 to sexual assault, gross sexual imposition, and four counts of luring minors by computer. Two counts of use of a minor in a sexual performance were dismissed.

Sentenced November 5, 2018 in Cass County District Court to 15 years, 10 years to serve followed by 5 years supervised probation; sex offender registration required. Courtroom packed with family, friends, church members, and school faculty. Moser told the court she felt she did not deserve life or breath for her actions.

NOTE: Husband Brett Moser was pastor at River City Church, Fargo; placed on administrative leave after charges. Former students at a prior school described identical grooming-adjacent behavior more than a decade before West Fargo charges — suggesting institutional failure to document or act on earlier conduct. Six victims is a high count for the DB.
