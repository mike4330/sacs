Former special education teacher at Converse County School District #1 (Douglas High School), Douglas, Wyoming. Victim was a 17-year-old male student. In 2013, Marcus initiated contact with the student, exchanging picture messages before the relationship progressed to sex. The victim subsequently Snapchatted evidence of the encounter to his friends. Marcus's contract at Converse County SD #1 was terminated in July 2013 with no public reason given. She was subsequently hired as a SPED teacher at Park County School District #6 (Cody, WY).

Pleaded guilty May 20, 2016 to third-degree sexual molestation of a minor. Plea agreement called for prosecutors to recommend supervised probation up to five years; upon completion, conviction could be expunged. Age approximately 32 at time of offense.

NOTE: Contract terminated at originating district before arrest, with no public reason given — consistent with prior-warning-ignored pattern.
