Megan Batykefer #225 appeal

StatePennsylvaniaCourtAllegheny County Court of Common Pleas
JudgeEdward BorkowskiIncident Date2014-04-01
Defendant Age31Victim Age18
Prison Sentence0.67 yrs

Former rowing coach at North Allegheny Senior High School, Wexford (Pittsburgh suburb), Allegheny County, Pennsylvania. Female defendant, age 31. One male student victim, age 18 at time of sexual relationship; had been coaching him since 2012 (when he was ~15).

Victim himself said he deliberately waited until he turned 18 in April 2014 to tell Batykefer his feelings, recognizing it would have been inappropriate before then. Sexual relationship began after his 18th birthday and continued before and after his June 2014 graduation. Both parties described the relationship as consensual. The complaint notes the victim 'realized that it would've been inappropriate for him to be involved romantically with her when he was less than 18 years of age so he waited to let her know how he felt.'

Convicted August 13, 2015, by Judge Edward Borkowski after a bench trial. Defense challenged the 2012 Pennsylvania institutional sexual assault statute as unconstitutionally overbroad; Borkowski rejected the challenge: 'There is no constitutional protection for school employees to engage in sexual relations with their students... the purpose of the statute is to protect students from such undue and coercive influence.'

Sentenced November 10, 2015: 8 to 23 months jail. Borkowski stated: 'You cannot do this and not expect to go to jail.' However, Batykefer was allowed to remain out on bond while her attorney pursued an appeal of the constitutionality of the institutional sexual assault statute to the Pennsylvania Superior Court. Execution of sentence stayed pending appeal outcome.

NOTE: Adult-victim case (18-year-old; PA age of consent is 16). The 2012 PA statute criminalizes teacher-student sexual relationships regardless of student age. Defense made the same constitutional challenge raised in Plum teacher Jason Cooper's contemporaneous case. The victim's own statement — that he waited until 18 to initiate — is factually unusual in this corpus. Status as of filing: appeal pending, sentence not yet served.
