Former 9th-grade teacher at Griffin High School, Griffin-Spalding County School System, Griffin, Georgia (Spalding County). Age 41 at arrest and sentencing. Had taught at Griffin High School for one year prior to arrest; previously taught in other Georgia counties.

At least four male victims, all age 15 at the time of the offenses; two victims' families did not cooperate with the prosecution. The school launched an internal investigation after receiving information about possible inappropriate actions, then began working with Griffin Police Department. Placed on administrative leave and resigned during the investigation.

Arrested May 2017; originally charged with one count each of child molestation, sodomy, enticing a child, statutory rape, sexual assault, and sexual battery. Released on bond after a preliminary hearing. Subsequently arrested a second time after being found stopped in a vehicle with juveniles, marijuana, and a gun; District Attorney Ben Coker successfully sought and was granted bond revocation.

Pled guilty in Spalding County Superior Court to two counts of sexual assault and one count of child molestation; all other charges dismissed pursuant to plea. Sentenced to 30 years with 8 to serve in prison; sex offender registration; barred from teaching in Georgia. Victim impact statements delivered by the mothers of two victims. District Attorney Ben Coker.
