Former teacher at Corning-Painted Post Middle School, Painted Post, Steuben County, New York. Age approximately 36-37 at time of offense (age 43 at arrest April 2016). Victim was a male student under the age of 17; ongoing relationship during the 2009-2010 school year. Incidents did not occur at the school. Victim's mother stated in court: 'Our son was taken from us by one of the people we taught and pushed our son to trust. No longer did he want to be with us. No longer did he want to take our traditional late vacations or hunt with his dad. No longer did he want to talk or be a big brother to his sister.'

Following a 16-month investigation by the Painted Post Police Department, Conway was arrested April 2016 on one count of third-degree rape (felony) for sexual contact with a person under age 17. Arraigned on $25,000 bail. The student had since graduated from the district prior to the arrest.

Pleaded guilty to a misdemeanor count of endangering the welfare of a child. Sentenced April 11, 2017 in Village of Painted Post Court by Judge Annette Viselli Thorne: three years probation with 36 conditions; 90-day ankle bracelet; forfeit teaching license; no access to schools; no social media; $205 surcharge and $1,000 fine. No prison. DA: Brooks Baker (Steuben County). Defense: Joe Joch (Ithaca). Conway was still technically employed by the district (on administrative leave) at time of sentencing.

NOTE: Offense 2009-2010; arrest April 2016 — approximately six-year delay between offense and arrest. Victim age inferred as approximately 14-15 (described as under 17 and a middle school student at time of offense). Charge reduced from felony third-degree rape to misdemeanor endangering welfare of child. No sex offender registration noted in sources.
