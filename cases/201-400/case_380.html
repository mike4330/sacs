Former permanent substitute teacher in the Roxana School District (2020-2021 school year), then the Alton School District (2021-2022 school year), Madison County, Illinois. Edwardsville resident.

Victims were multiple male Roxana High School students between the ages of 13 and 17. Fischer admitted the abuse occurred between May and June 2021, near the end of her Roxana assignment. At least one offense occurred June 5, 2021, at the victims' home in Edwardsville. Age approximately 28-29 at time of offense.

In January 2022, Edwardsville Police received notification from Roxana Police that a former teacher had a sexual relationship with two male juveniles under 18. Edwardsville initiated its own investigation because the offenses occurred within city limits. A search warrant was executed February 1, 2022 at Fischer's Roanoke Drive residence; she was taken into custody that day. Bond set at $200,000.

A Madison County grand jury indicted Fischer in February 2022 on 12 counts. Initial charging documents listed 4 counts criminal sexual assault (Class 1 felony) and 3 counts aggravated criminal sexual abuse (Class 2 felony). Pleaded guilty to 3 counts of aggravated criminal sexual abuse. Sentenced in late October 2024 to 18 years in state prison.

NOTE: Multiple victims, at least 3; youngest was 13. Charge reduction from criminal sexual assault (Class 1) to aggravated criminal sexual abuse (Class 2) on all conviction counts. 18-year sentence despite pleading to Class 2 felonies — likely consecutive terms. Fischer was a substitute, not a permanent staff member. Offenses occurred at victims' home, not on school grounds.
