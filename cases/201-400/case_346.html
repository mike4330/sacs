Cali Heikes #346 sentenced

StateNebraskaCourtWayne County Court
Incident Date2022-09-01
Defendant Age25 Victim Age17
Prison Sentence1.0 yr

Former family and consumer sciences teacher at Winside High School, Winside, Wayne County, Nebraska. Age 25 at time of offense and arrest (age 26 at sentencing September 2023). Victim was a 17-year-old male student. Heikes is a graduate of Winside High School and Wayne State College.

Reported to law enforcement via HHS hotline tip, January 2023. A Wayne County Sheriff's deputy connected Heikes to the victim through a prior traffic stop: the victim was pulled over leaving the Winside Cemetery; a black Chevrolet SUV registered to Heikes was observed leaving the same area. The victim told the deputy he had been hanging out with his friend 'Cali.' The victim's guardians disclosed the victim had recently asked how they would feel about him dating a 25-year-old. The victim's sister told deputies that Heikes would sneak into the victim's house at night to have sexual intercourse, and that the pair were meeting approximately two to three times a week around town.

Arrested January 22, 2023; lodged in Antelope County Jail, subsequently posted bond. In March 2023, the court moved to revoke her bond after a letter from Heikes was found in the victim's bedroom between January 23 and February 25 — contact with the victim in violation of bond conditions.

Pleaded guilty to two counts of sexual abuse by a school employee. Sentenced September 6, 2023: 24 months jail; eligible for parole after 12 months; sex offender registration required.

NOTE: Second Winside school teacher arrested for an inappropriate relationship with a student within approximately one year; Rachel McPhillips (#347, former Winside art teacher) was charged in Wayne County for conduct in 2021-2022 with a different student. Post-arrest contact with victim via letter resulted in bond revocation. sentence_years = 1.0 (12-month minimum before parole eligibility; maximum 24 months).
