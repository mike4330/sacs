Emily Lehneis #354 sentenced

StatePennsylvaniaCourtYork County Court of Common Pleas
JudgeKathleen Prendergast Incident Date2022-09-01
Defendant Age29 Victim Age16
Prison Sentence1.0 yr

Former special education teacher (pre-K to 12th grade) at Susquehannock High School, Glen Rock, York County, Pennsylvania (Southern York County School District). Also coached for South Western School District. Age approximately 29 at start of offense (turned 30 in October 2022 during the relationship; age 31 at sentencing December 2023). Victim was a 16-17-year-old female student on the school soccer team.

Relationship began approximately September 2022. Lehneis gave the victim private soccer tutoring. The two communicated daily via text, calls, and social media including video calls where the victim was introduced to Lehneis's husband and children. Lehneis kissed the student in her classroom one afternoon between school hours and soccer practice. Around October 2022, around her 30th birthday, Lehneis asked to be the student's 'secret girlfriend.' The relationship — lasting a couple of weeks — involved exchanging explicit photos and videos. Lehneis used her marriage, family, and career as leverage to keep the victim silent: 'She manipulated me and had total control. She used her marriage, family and career to keep me from saying anything,' the victim said in a statement. The teen eventually ended the relationship; afterward was bullied.

Southern York County School District notified January 22, 2023; Lehneis placed on paid administrative leave and resignation letter obtained January 27. Childline and the Pennsylvania Department of Education notified. Charges filed May 2023. Pennsylvania Department of Education suspended Lehneis's teaching certificate August 25, 2023. Free on $150,000 unsecured bail.

Pleaded guilty September 20, 2023 to institutional sexual assault (felony, PA 18 Pa.C.S. § 3124.2) and corruption of minors (misdemeanor). Five other charges dismissed: depicting a sex act with a minor on a computer, sexual contact with a minor (two counts), disseminating explicit material of a minor, and unlawful contact with a minor. Sentenced December 26, 2023 by Judge Kathleen Prendergast: 1-2 years state prison; 5 years probation; sex offender conditions; $3,520 restitution to state victim compensation fund; approximately $5,000 additional restitution to victim's family (hearing pending). Chief Deputy DA Taylor Katherman. Defense: Robert O'Brien.

Lehneis was pregnant with her third child at sentencing; judge remarked it was 'a sad comment on today's society' that women's prisons have excellent prenatal care. Lehneis apologized in court: 'I have no excuse for what I did, and I cannot understand how I let myself do what I did.' Judge Prendergast placed blame entirely on Lehneis: 'Anything that happens to the defendant is based on the behavior she brought on herself, and is no way based on anything that the victim did or did not do.'

NOTE: sentence_years = 1.0 (1-year minimum before parole eligibility; PA indeterminate 1-to-2-year sentence). Victim is female. Lehneis also known as Emily Schindler (maiden name per charging documents). Lehneis was still married at time of sentencing; husband wept in court.
