Former special education teacher at New Haven Youth & Family Services, a residential school for at-risk boys with emotional and behavioral problems in Vista, California (San Diego County). Age 33 at time of offense; 34 at sentencing. Watson taught the victim in her class for several semesters from September 2010 to August 2011; he was released from the facility in January 2012. The sexual relationship began approximately two weeks after his release, when she allowed him to stay with her at her downtown San Diego apartment. Watson had also worked at Oceanside High School and school districts in San Diego and Kern counties; seven years' special education experience.

Victim: male student, age 16 when relationship began (turning 18 in April 2013). Watson wrote in a court letter: 'At some point I realized that he was still only 17 yrs old. But it was too late, our feelings were very strong by then... We would most likely be together at this time if it weren't for the age issue.'

A possible domestic violence incident at the apartment on July 28, 2012 — the victim told officers he had pushed Watson — led police to the relationship; investigators found 'I want to have a baby with you' written on the bathroom mirror. Police received a tip July 30, 2012; Watson arrested August 9, 2012. She was working at Oceanside High School at the time of arrest.

Pled guilty November 2012 to unlawful sexual intercourse with a minor more than three years younger (felony). Sentenced February 6, 2013 by Judge Laura Halgren to one year in county jail with early release authorized for childbirth and childcare; three years' felony probation; counseling; no contact with victim; no sex offender registration. Watson was seven months pregnant with the victim's child at sentencing (due April 5, 2013). Prosecutor Mary-Ellen Barrett (Deputy DA); defense attorney David R. Cohn.

NOTE: Pregnancy was central to the sentencing. No sex offender registration despite a felony conviction — judge cited non-predatory psychological profile. Victim's mother expressed sympathy for Watson; victim said he did not consider himself a victim. The victim's age at start of relationship (16) makes the 'three years younger' charge somewhat conservative given Watson was 33.
