Lacey Cunningham #385 sentenced

StateTexasCourtMontgomery County District Court
Incident Date2019-08-01
Defendant Age32 Victim Age18
Prison Sentence3.0 yrs

Former anatomy teacher and basketball coach at Woodlands Christian Academy, The Woodlands, Montgomery County, Texas (private school). Former counselor, unit coordinator, and basketball clinic lead at Kanakuk Kamps K-2 camp (at least 2010-2012); Kanakuk Institute graduate (2012). Age 32 at time of offense.

Victim was an 18-year-old female student. The relationship began in August 2019 when Cunningham initiated texting. She then asked to meet the student outside school for coffee, took her to church, and arranged for the student to stay overnight at her apartment. The relationship escalated to more than 20 sexual encounters. The victim described Cunningham as 'very manipulative, which has prevented her from coming forward sooner.' The student disclosed to her mother on approximately December 13, 2019.

Detectives interviewed Cunningham at her Conroe apartment (2443 FM 1488) on December 13, 2019. She cooperated and consented to the seizure of numerous electronic devices for forensic analysis. Arrest warrant obtained December 18, 2019. Cunningham turned herself in to Montgomery County Jail on December 19, 2019; bond set at $50,000.

Indicted twice on the charge of improper relationship between educator and student (Texas Penal Code 21.12), a second-degree felony. Sentenced to 3 years in the Texas Department of Criminal Justice. Released 2025.

NOTE: Same-sex offense — female defendant, female victim; relatively rare within this DB. Texas TPC 21.12 (improper relationship) applies regardless of student age; victim was 18 (legally adult) when the relationship began. Adult-victim tag applied. Grooming progression: texting → coffee → church → overnights → 20+ sexual encounters. Private school setting. Cunningham was identified through a tip from a former Kanakuk K-2 camper; no Kanakuk-specific victims have publicly come forward. Kanakuk Kamps has a broader documented abuse history (see FactsAboutKanakuk.com). Exact sentencing date unknown from available sources.
