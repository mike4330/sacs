English teacher at Beaumont High School, Beaumont Unified School District, Beaumont, California. Previously taught at La Quinta High School; joined Beaumont USD August 2015. Married with two children. Age 32 at sentencing.

Sexual relationship with a 16-year-old male student began approximately June 2017. The relationship became public through a Snapchat video allegedly showing Ciotta drinking alcohol with two shirtless teenagers. A relative of the victim texted the boy in June 2017 asking about rumors; replies suggested Ciotta's husband was using the boy's phone. The husband's text said he 'needed to know the extent of what happened.' Police investigated; the victim initially denied the relationship ('I'm not f------ talking!') and actively covered for Ciotta. He later told police they had sex at least five times and that he was usually intoxicated during encounters. He ended his silence after learning Ciotta had allegedly had sex with his 19-year-old friend and then said the student 'had ruined her life.' Beaumont police served at least seven search warrants, searching her home, swabbing her SUV for DNA, and examining her phone.

The victim's guardian and at least one other teacher reported concerns about the relationship to school administrators; no action was taken. The victim spent virtually the entire school day in Ciotta's classroom. Principal Christina Pierce resigned earlier in 2018. A civil lawsuit was filed against Beaumont USD, Principal Pierce, and Vice Principal Ian Young by the victim the week before sentencing.

Ciotta was arrested September 2017 and placed on administrative leave. Husband filed for divorce October 2017. She entered a not guilty plea in December 2017 in Banning. Pleaded guilty May 10, 2018 to three felony counts of unlawful intercourse with a minor. One count of oral copulation of a child was dismissed. Represented by Greenberg & Greenberg Law Firm.

Sentence: 180 days Riverside County Sheriff's work release, 4 years probation, 90 hours community service, 10-year no-contact order with victim, no social media contact with unrelated minors. No sex offender registration. Teaching license revoked.
