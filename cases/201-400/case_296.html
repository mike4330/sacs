Former graphic arts teacher and drama club supervisor at Fair Lawn High School, Fair Lawn, New Jersey. Worked at the school for 21 years. Age approximately 39 at time of offense (born c. 1977; age 44 at arrest in September 2021).

Victim was a 17-year-old female student who was a member of the drama club Knudsen oversaw; the victim later also enrolled in one of Knudsen's classes. The relationship became romantic and then sexual between January 13 and November 11, 2017. Knudsen also provided and ingested controlled dangerous substances with the student. The case came to light after police received tips from unidentified witnesses.

Arrested September 2021 on charges of second-degree sexual assault and two counts of second-degree endangering the welfare of a child.

Pleaded guilty approximately July 2023; plea deal allowed the second-degree crime to be sentenced as third-degree. Sentenced January 24, 2024 by Superior Court Judge Nina Remson to three years in New Jersey State Prison, suspended. Additional conditions: parole supervision for life (Megan's Law registration), no-contact restraining order (Nicole's Law) with victim, surrender of teaching certificate, and substance abuse evaluation.

NOTE: Victim was 17, at or above New Jersey's age of consent, but the teacher-student position-of-authority statute applies. Judge Remson explicitly acknowledged the relationship would have been technically legal outside a school context. No-prison outcome. Female-on-female offense. Defendant age 39 is approximate based on age 44 at September 2021 arrest; offense age could be 39 or 40 depending on birth month.
