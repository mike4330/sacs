Court Montgomery County Court of Common Pleas
Judge Joseph P. Walsh
Incident Date 2015-11-01

Former English teacher at Upper Dublin High School, Upper Dublin Township, Montgomery County, Pennsylvania. Also previously taught at Sandy Run Middle School. Age approximately 38 at time of offense (age 40 at sentencing June 2017). Victim was an 18-year-old male senior — legally an adult but protected under Pennsylvania's institutional sexual assault statute, which applies to school employees regardless of victim age.

Relationship began November/December 2015 and continued through April 2016. Victim testified LiMuli gave him rides home from school, stopping at local isolated locations to perform oral sex; approximately 10 such encounters. One instance of sexual intercourse at the victim's Dresher residence. LiMuli also fondled him in a private area of her classroom out of sight of other students. In exchange: clothes, sneakers, a dirt bike, athletic gear, alcohol, cash, and access to her credit card. Communication via phone calls, texts, and Snapchat.

Rumors circulated at the high school; school district reported relationship to police March 31, 2016. LiMuli placed on administrative leave April 2016. Charged with 12 counts of institutional sexual assault (11 indecent contact, 1 deviate sexual intercourse) approximately May 2, 2016; bail $50,000 unsecured. ADA James Price. Defense: Matthew Sedacca.

At trial LiMuli denied all sexual contact and claimed the student had accessed her devices and was blackmailing her over an extramarital relationship. Prosecution noted she never raised the blackmail allegation during the police investigation. Three-day jury trial (7 women, 5 men). Convicted February 2017 on 2 counts (1 deviate sexual intercourse, 1 indecent contact); acquitted on remaining 10 counts. Sentenced June 20, 2017: 2-12 months county jail (concurrent); 3 years probation following parole (4 years total supervision). Conviction affirmed on appeal by Pennsylvania Superior Court (J-S22028-18, 2018).

NOTE: Victim was 18 — an adult — but institutional sexual assault applies to school employees regardless of victim age in Pennsylvania. Blackmail defense raised only at trial, not during investigation; jury rejected it. LiMuli was a married mother of one child.
