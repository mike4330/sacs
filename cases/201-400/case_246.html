United States v. Meyer-Davis #246 sentenced

StateIowaCourtU.S. District Court, Northern District of Iowa
JudgeLeonard T. Strand Incident Date2022-05
Defendant Age29 Victim Age14
Prison Sentence20.0 yrs

Former middle school teacher at Humboldt Middle School, Humboldt, Iowa. Age 29 at time of offense.

From May 2022 through November 2023, engaged in sexual exploitation of a 14-year-old student, including exchange of over 130 photographs and more than 60 videos depicting child pornography, including sadistic and masochistic content. School administrators warned Meyer-Davis on multiple occasions and directed her to cease and desist any inappropriate relationship with the student. She denied the relationship to administrators and to law enforcement until confronted with photographic evidence of her kissing the victim, who was in a state of undress.

Arrested February 26, 2024 by Humboldt County Sheriff's Office on state charges: third-degree sexual abuse and sexual exploitation by a school employee. Posted $15,000 cash bond. Federal case 24-3028 filed in Northern District of Iowa; pled guilty April 30, 2025 to sexual exploitation of a child.

Sentenced October 6, 2025 by Judge Leonard T. Strand to 240 months (20 years) federal imprisonment, 5-year supervised release, and $13,243.67 restitution. No parole in federal system. Judge Strand described Meyer-Davis as every parent's worst nightmare and stated there was no indication the conduct would have stopped without law enforcement involvement.

NOTE: Classic prior-warning-ignored pattern with documented administrator interventions before arrest. Federal prosecution under Project Safe Childhood. CSAM volume (130+ photos, 60+ videos including S&M content) unusual even for federal cases.
