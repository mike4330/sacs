Dorothy Veracka #391 sentenced

Incident Date2018-09-01

Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former math teacher at Nashoba Regional High School, Bolton, Worcester County, Massachusetts. Mother of three; no prior criminal record. Age 41 at onset of offense.

Victim was a 15-year-old male student. Between September 2018 and March 2019, Veracka sent the student nude photos of herself via Snapchat and actively solicited nude photos in return. The student sent one photo before stopping. Veracka later admitted to investigators that she had received a nude photograph from the student. The student preserved screenshots of the Snapchat conversation and photos, which he provided to law enforcement during a Sexual Assault Intervention Network interview.

Placed on administrative leave September 30, 2019. Arrested October 2019; arraigned in Clinton District Court, released on personal recognizance. Fired by Nashoba Regional High School after her arrest.

Pleaded guilty to distributing obscene matter to a minor. Sentenced March 9, 2020 to 2.5 years in jail, suspended for 2 years with probation. Required to register as a sex offender. Charges of possession of child pornography and posing or exhibiting a child in nudity were dismissed.

Defense attorney Frank McNamara Jr. said the behavior was fueled by alcohol, late-night messaging, and compulsive behavior, and emphasized there was no physical contact. Police worked with Snapchat to complete forensic analysis of the exchanged content.

NOTE: No physical contact; offense was nude photo exchange via Snapchat only. Veracka solicited and received CSAM (student's nude photo), but possession of child pornography charge was dismissed per plea. No sex offender registration tier specified in sources. sentence_years = 0.
