State California
Court San Mateo County Superior Court
Incident Date 2015-10-01
Defendant Age 39
Victim Age 13
Prison Sentence 0.33 yrs

Former teacher at Taylor Middle School, Millbrae, San Mateo County, California (Millbrae School District). Age approximately 39 at start of offense (age 40 at arrest August 2016). Victim was a 13-year-old female student when the relationship began in October 2015; she was 15 at time of arrest.

Relationship discovered when San Bruno police received an anonymous report of a white van parked in front of a school with Butts and the victim kissing inside. Officers found Butts alone in the vehicle; the victim was found nearby. The victim initially denied anything inappropriate had occurred, then admitted the two had been kissing and said they were in love. Further investigation found explicit text messages and evidence of sexual conduct going back to October 2015.

Arrested August 17, 2016; posted $85,000 bail. Charged with felony lewd acts with a minor (CA PC 288). Millbrae School District placed Butts on administrative leave.

Pleaded no contest. Sentenced December 19, 2017 by San Mateo County Superior Court: 120 days jail; 3 years probation; sex offender registration; prohibited from associating with minors or frequenting places where children congregate (parks, schools, pizza parlors). DA: Steve Wagstaffe. Defense: Paula Canny.

At sentencing, Butts allegedly referred to the victim as a 'sexually sophisticated partner' and claimed she had saved the victim from suicide. DA Wagstaffe rejected both arguments: 'At 13, dealing with an adult, you can in no way attribute anything to the sexual sophistication of the victim. It's a non-factor, irrelevant in our minds.' The victim had told investigators she did not want Butts prosecuted.

NOTE: Victim initially non-cooperative with investigation; expressed that she and Butts were 'in love.' Victim-blaming argument ('sexually sophisticated partner') raised at sentencing and rejected. Suicide-intervention claim also advanced and rejected.
