Former teacher and cheer coach at Marshall High School, Marshall, Harrison County, Texas (Marshall ISD). Age approximately 23 at time of offense (age 25 at sentencing January 2026). Victim was a 16-year-old male student. Sexually explicit text messages and photos exchanged between Burris and the victim; discovered via search warrant executed on the student's cell phone. Burris was also a former Marshall High School student herself — a cheerleading champion and honor society member who graduated in 2018.

Reported by Marshall ISD Superintendent Richele Langley after she was informed by Brad Burris, Marshall ISD Board President and Burris's father-in-law at the time. Arrested February 8, 2024. Resigned from district April 2024; added to Texas Do Not Hire Registry June 2024 under her maiden name (Margaret Rizzo).

Harrison County DA Reid McCain recused due to his campaign and personal relationship with Brad Burris and with Brad's father Carlton Burris, longtime pastor of Immanuel Baptist Church in Marshall. Case transferred to Upshur County in May 2025. Plea hearing initially set December 2025 but delayed — sentencing terms were in dispute and victim's family had not been notified. Reset to January 8, 2026.

Charged with improper relationship between educator and student (TX PC 21.12, 2nd-degree felony, 2-20 years). Pleaded guilty January 8, 2026 before Visiting Judge Rebecca Simpson. Sentenced: 180 days county jail (day for day, no early release), release date July 7, 2026, at Upshur County Jail; 10 years probation. DA: Barry Wallace (Upshur County). Bond: $85,000.

NOTE: sentence_years = 0.5 (180 days day-for-day). Brad Burris, who reported Burris, was simultaneously the school board president and the defendant's father-in-law; institutional and familial conflict entangled. Locals suspected more victims; no additional charges brought. Some in the community expected probation only due to family connections — 180-day jail term was unexpected by some. Brent Burris (husband) is now engaged to remarry. Burris and Brent met when she was a senior and he was a sophomore at Marshall High School, both later attending East Texas Baptist University.
