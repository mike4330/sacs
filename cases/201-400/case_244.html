StateMissouriCourtChristian County Circuit Court
Incident Date2022-10-01

Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Woodworking and agricultural science teacher and FFA advisor at Nixa High School, Nixa, Missouri (Christian County). Employed by Nixa Public Schools since 2021. Age 26 at arrest; estimated 25 at time of offense.

Victim was a 16-year-old male student in Stewart's class. Sexual relationship began October 2022. Stewart would pick the student up in her car and drive to secluded areas for sexual acts. The victim told investigators he felt uncomfortable but felt pressured to continue because he received good grades for little classwork. The student's parents filed the initial complaint after growing concerned about the amount of time he was spending with Stewart.

Investigation began December 7, 2022. When initially questioned, Stewart admitted to occasional private messages with students but denied physical contact and claimed her phone auto-deletes text messages; a search of her phone found no messages. Further investigation uncovered a secret Instagram account Stewart used to communicate with the student. Arrested January 22, 2023 on $25,000 bond. Placed on administrative leave; fired by Nixa School District February 2023.

Originally charged with second-degree statutory rape, sexual misconduct with a minor, and first-degree sexual misconduct (all felonies). Pleaded guilty January 14, 2025 to one misdemeanor count of sexual abuse. Sentenced to 2 years supervised probation and sex offender registration.

NOTE: Three felony charges reduced to one misdemeanor at plea — among the most significant charge reductions in the DB. Grade-for-sex arrangement is an explicit quid pro quo grooming mechanic. Secret Instagram account discovered after Stewart falsely claimed no communication existed. sentence_years=0 per no-prison convention.
