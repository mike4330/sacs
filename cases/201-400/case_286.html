Reagan Gray #286 sentenced

State Arkansas
Court Pulaski County Circuit Court
Incident Date 2020-01
Defendant Age 23
Victim Age 15
Prison Sentence 0.0 yrs

Former teacher at Little Rock Christian Academy and later Sylvan Hills Middle School (Pulaski County Special School District), Little Rock, Arkansas. Volunteer youth group leader and music program participant at Immanuel Baptist Church, Little Rock. One male victim, age 15 at time of offense. The victim was a member of the church student ministry and was not a student at the schools where Gray taught. Gray obtained the victim's phone number through the church music program group chat. Age 23 at time of offense.

Beginning in 2020, Gray engaged in approximately a dozen sexual encounters with the victim, occurring in her car and apartment. Gray sent nude photos of herself to the victim daily and expected nude photos in return via Snapchat. The victim told investigators Gray performed oral sex on him but stated there was no intercourse so he could 'stay pure.' The victim's parents discovered text messages in 2020 and reported them to head pastor Steven Smith. Smith confronted Gray, who denied the relationship was physical. Gray was removed from the student ministry and required to attend counseling — but returned to the youth group shortly afterward. In Fall 2023, Gray admitted to Smith during a counseling session that the 2020 relationship was sexual. In February 2024, the FBI launched a federal investigation after learning Gray and the victim had continued communicating via Snapchat.

Placed on administrative leave by PCSSD in February 2024; arrested April 2024; fired by PCSSD in May 2024. Head pastor Steven Smith resigned from Immanuel Baptist Church amid the controversy. Charged with sexual assault in the first and fourth degree. Pleaded no contest to second-degree sexual assault. Sentenced May 19, 2025 to six years' probation, $1,000 fine, DNA sample, and placement on sex offender registry. No contact with victim ordered.

NOTE: Church-context case — victim was not Gray's school student but met her through church youth ministry where she volunteered; qualifies for this DB because Gray was a credentialed teacher and the relationship arose from a youth-authority context. Exceptionally clear prior-warning-ignored chain: parents reported → church confronted → Gray denied and was removed → Gray returned → Gray confessed to pastor three years later. Charge reduction from 1st/4th degree to 2nd degree. FBI investigation. The five-year delay between initial discovery (2020) and arrest (2024) is analytically notable.
