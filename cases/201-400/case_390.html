Former 4th and 5th grade math teacher at Grapeland Elementary School, Grapeland, Houston County, Texas (Grapeland ISD). Employed with the district for approximately 3 years at time of arrest. Age approximately 44 at time of offense.

Victim was under 14 years of age. Alleged abuse occurred in the Slocum area (Anderson County). The Anderson County Sheriff's Office did not confirm whether the victim was a student or family member of Singer.

Arrested October 9, 2018 after allegations made to the Anderson County Sheriff's Office. Grapeland ISD suspended Singer with pay; the school board later voted not to renew her contract. Indicted by an Anderson County grand jury on continuous sexual abuse and sexual assault charges.

Pleaded nolo contendere July 15, 2024 to count 1 (Continuous Sexual Abuse of a Child, Victim Under 14, TPC 21.02, First Degree Felony). Counts 2 and 3 (Sexual Assault of a Child, TPC 22.011(a)(2)) dismissed. Judge Michael Davis sentenced Singer to CSCD 6 months (community supervision/probation) with 100 hours community service; 3 days jail credit.

NOTE: Outcome is highly anomalous. Texas Penal Code 21.02 carries a mandatory minimum of 25 years to life with no parole eligibility for 25 years, and Texas law generally prohibits community supervision for this offense. A 6-month CSCD sentence on a nolo plea to a First Degree Felony is legally irregular and may reflect a judicial finding, reduced offense vehicle, or plea term not fully captured in available records.
