Former health and special education teacher at Lincoln Park Middle School, Duluth, St. Louis County, Minnesota. Employed in the Duluth school district since 2007, initially at Morgan Park Middle School (2007-2012) then Lincoln Park. Also taught health at the Area Learning Center's Academic Excellence Online program. Age 33 at time of offense.

Victim was a 15-year-old male former student. Between May 4 and May 19, 2018, Winterfeld engaged in multiple sexual acts with the victim at locations not specified in sources. Winterfeld confessed to investigators. A search warrant executed on both her and the victim's cellphones yielded video recordings, photographs, text messages, and other evidence supporting the allegations. Prosecutors noted she had engaged in grooming behavior before the first incident and had researched consent laws in advance.

Arrested and charged May 23, 2018; bail set at $100,000 by Judge Johnson at arraignment. Placed on paid administrative leave by Duluth school district; formally resigned August 2018 after 11 years of employment.

Pleaded guilty in October 2018 to one count of first-degree criminal sexual conduct with no plea agreement. Sentenced December 20, 2018 by Judge David Johnson to the presumptive guideline term of 12 years in prison. Must serve at least 8 years (two-thirds) before eligible for release, followed by 10 years of conditional release. Required to register as a predatory offender.

NOTE: Defense requested probation, presenting a psychosexual evaluator and therapist who testified Winterfeld was low-risk and treatment-engaged. Prosecutor Jon Holets explicitly argued against leniency based on sex, race, or socioeconomic status — Winterfeld had hired expert witnesses and received dozens of character letters, advantages unavailable to most defendants. Judge was unequivocal: 'We're here for sentencing related to your rape of an underage child.' Evidence included recordings and photographs of the acts. Winterfeld had received the 2016 Greg Irons Teacher Award and had no prior complaints. sentence_years set to 8 (mandatory minimum two-thirds of 12-year term).
