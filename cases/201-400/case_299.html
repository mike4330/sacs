Former teacher at Ripon High School, Ripon, Wisconsin (Ripon Area School District). Age approximately 27 at time of offense. Also known as Samantha Fitzpatrick (married name at time of offense; reverted to maiden name Marco by sentencing).

Victim was a male student, age 16 when the relationship began during the 2016–2017 school year, continuing until he was 17. Another teacher noticed the student frequently in Marco's classroom and that Marco would talk to him rather than teach; they were also absent or late on the same days. The relationship involved meetings at Marco's home, at the Comfort Inn and Suites in Ripon, and at the Welcome Inn Hotel. Marco later told investigators she rented the Welcome Inn room because the student was high on marijuana. Investigators found 67 calls, numerous deleted text messages, and Facebook messages of a 'sexually graphic nature' — including discussions of role-playing and sexual acts, explicit photos and videos, and declarations of love. Both parties initially denied any relationship.

Marco voluntarily resigned in May 2017 after school officials raised attendance concerns; the district's parent letter cited 'unreliable attendance issues.' Police learned of the relationship through a third party in spring 2017. Charges filed December 2017: child enticement, sexual assault of student by school staff (Wisconsin), exposing a child to harmful material, and obstructing an officer (35+ years combined exposure).

Pleaded guilty November 2018 to sexual assault of a student by school staff; remaining three charges dismissed. Sentenced February 11, 2019 by Judge Gary Sharpe to 3 years probation, 6 months imposed and stayed (activated only on treatment violation), and sex offender registration.
