Loryn Barclay #266 sentenced

StateMissouriCourtBarry County Circuit Court
JudgeDavid Boyce Mouton Incident Date2016-09-01
Defendant Age21 Victim Age17
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former substitute teacher and paraprofessional at Monett High School, Monett, Missouri (Barry County). Age approximately 21-22 at time of offense; 24 at arrest; 27 at sentencing. Hired August 2016 as a substitute teacher; promoted to full-time paraprofessional/teaching assistant in January 2017; removed April 27, 2017, the day the relationship was first reported to police.

Victim: 17-year-old male student; contact occurred after his 17th birthday in fall and winter of 2016. Sexual contact included oral sex in a car at a park in Lawrence County and sexual intercourse at the student's home in Barry County.

Anonymous tip received by Monett Police Department in April 2017. Monett City Police Officer Jay Jastal (school resource officer) investigated; the student confirmed the relationship on May 3, 2017; Barclay admitted an 'inappropriate relationship' the following day. Charged with multiple felony counts of sexual contact with a student in Barry County and Lawrence County. Arrested June 25, 2017.

Under a plea deal, pled guilty in February 2020 to one count of harassment of a person under age 17. All other charges dropped. Sentenced February 20, 2020 by Barry County Judge David Boyce Mouton to a suspended execution of sentence of 3 years in prison and 5 years probation. No active prison time.

NOTE: Reduction from multiple felony sexual contact with a student counts (two counties) to a single harassment charge is an extreme charge reduction. Over three years elapsed between allegations (April 2017) and plea (February 2020). Defendant was approximately 21-22 at time of offense — among the youngest defendants in the DB. In Missouri, the age of consent is 17; the offense was criminal solely due to the teacher-student relationship.
