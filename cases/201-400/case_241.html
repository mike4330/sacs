Former teacher's assistant at Onondaga-Cortland-Madison (OCM) BOCES, DeWitt, New York. Resided in Binghamton (Broome County) at time of charges. Age 30 at arrest and sentencing; estimated 28 at time of offense.

Engaged in sexual activity with a 16-year-old male student on multiple occasions in June 2009 and February 2010. No incidents occurred on school property; some took place at Hayes' home in Binghamton. The relationship came to light when the victim was arrested in Broome County following a separate incident at Hayes' home; Broome County officials notified the Onondaga County DA, who engaged the Onondaga County Sheriff's Office.

Charged January 27, 2011 following an investigation that began in October 2010: three counts third-degree rape, three counts third-degree criminal sexual act (all felonies), three counts third-degree sexual abuse, three counts endangering the welfare of a child, and one count unlawfully dealing with a child (all misdemeanors). Arraigned in DeWitt Town Court; posted $10,000 bail.

Pleaded guilty November 2011 to a single felony count of third-degree rape. Sentenced March 2, 2012 by State Supreme Court Justice John Brunetti. Apologized to the victim and his family at sentencing.

Sentence: 6 months jail (4-month required minimum; had already served approximately 2 months pre-sentencing at Onondaga County Correctional Facility in Jamesville), 10 years probation, sex offender registration upon release.
