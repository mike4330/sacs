Former student teacher at Lamar Middle School in Lamar, Missouri (Barton County), placed there as part of her senior-year teaching practicum at Missouri Southern State University. Resided in Golden City. Three known male victims, all 13-year-old students at the school; specific age recorded as 13. Age approximately 36 at time of offenses (2017); 39 at sentencing.

Conduct documented in 2017 included sending nude photos of herself to three different 13-year-old students; one victim reported that Edson asked him to have sex with her on multiple occasions and spoke to him sexually. A search warrant on her phone, executed by the Southwest Missouri Cyber Crimes Task Force, recovered images depicting two naked underage boys engaged in (or simulating) anal intercourse — i.e. CSAM separate from the self-photos sent to students.

Less than two months before her arrest, Edson had received the top award for middle-school education from MSSU's School of Arts and Sciences at the school's annual Academic Achievement Ceremony.

Charged with three counts of sexual misconduct with a child by indecent exposure, one count of promoting child pornography, one count of enticement of a child, and possession of child pornography. Filed waiver of preliminary hearing on March 5, 2019; case set for trial. Pleaded guilty to the three indecent-exposure counts and the one promoting-child-pornography count; the enticement and CSAM-possession counts were dismissed in exchange. On October 14, 2020 sentenced to four 4-year prison terms running concurrently (4 years total) plus lifetime DOC supervision after release.

In September 2025, Edson was charged in Dade County Circuit Court with two felony counts of violating her lifetime supervision after attending youth football games at Dykens Field in Greenfield on August 23 and August 25, 2025. She told investigators her nephew was playing tackle football and she watched from the parking lot with a chaperone, claiming she did not know she was prohibited; conditions of her supervision require advance Department of Corrections Command Center approval for any such attendance. A court date in that case was set for October 14, 2025.

NOTE: Three documented student victims plus a separate CSAM possession finding from the phone-search warrant. The MSSU teaching-award timing is striking — top recognition by her school of education weeks before arrest. Lifetime-supervision violation in 2025 is a rare follow-on data point for the parole-supervision-for-life cohort.
