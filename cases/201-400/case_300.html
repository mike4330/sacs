Former special education teacher / teacher's aide at Chapel Street School (Chapel Elementary School), Stratford, Connecticut. Employed there for four years. Married to a Danbury school music teacher. Age approximately 25 at start of offense (age 28 at October 2015 arrest).

Victim was a 12-year-old male boy she tutored privately at his Stratford home; she provided tutoring voluntarily without charging the family. The victim told police the two had sex 15–20 times over approximately two years beginning when he was 12. He came forward to authorities out of fear he might have fathered Sulzicki's child; DNA testing confirmed he was not the father of her daughter.

Surrendered to Stratford Police October 14, 2015; husband stood beside her at arraignment holding their infant daughter. Sulzicki initially denied any sexual contact, then admitted to sex once when the victim was in sixth grade. Charged with first-degree sexual assault, second-degree sexual assault, illegal sexual contact with a minor, and risk of injury to a minor.

Pleaded guilty August 2016 to second-degree sexual assault and risk of injury to a child; first-degree sexual assault and illegal sexual contact charges dismissed. Sentenced October 28, 2016 to 2 years in state prison, 10 years probation, and sex offender registration; no further contact with victim.

NOTE: Defendant age approximately 25 back-calculated from age 28 at October 2015 arrest; incident date is approximate. Victim disclosure was triggered by pregnancy fear rather than a third-party report. Free tutoring to the family provided the access mechanism. Sulzicki presented a 'slight smile' at arraignment.
