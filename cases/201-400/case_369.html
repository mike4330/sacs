Madalyn Arnett #369 sentenced

StateOhioCourtWarren County Common Pleas Court
JudgeTimothy N. Tepe Incident Date2018-03-23
Defendant Age25
Prison Sentence4.0 yrs

Former social studies teacher (grades 10-12) at Franklin High School, Franklin City Schools, Warren County, Ohio. Victim was a male student; exact age not reported (grades 10-12 context suggests approximately 15-18). Age 25 at time of offense.

A male student had approached Arnett at school about how he could improve his grades; she initially declined to assist. Arnett subsequently contacted the student on social media and began sending him sexual messages and nude images and videos of herself. On the evening of March 23, 2018, Arnett picked the student up down the street from his home, drove to a nearby parking lot, and had sexual intercourse with him in her vehicle. The student subsequently reported the incident, which was reported to school officials, who contacted police. Warren County Sheriff's detectives executed a search warrant at Arnett's West Carrollton residence and arrested her on March 27, 2018. Bond: $25,000 through Franklin Municipal Court. Jurisdiction was with Warren County Sheriff because the incident occurred in Franklin Township.

Pleaded guilty March 2019 to sexual battery (3rd degree felony, Ohio). Sentenced May 3, 2019 by Judge Timothy N. Tepe to four years in prison. Designated a Tier III sex offender (Ohio's highest registration tier).

NOTE: The grade-improvement request as the initial contact vector is notable — the student sought academic help and Arnett initially declined, then reversed course by initiating sexual contact via social media. Prosecutor David Fornshell: 'I continue to be amazed that teachers who engage in sexual activity with students believe that these teenagers will keep it a secret. Eventually, it gets out, and when it does, the price is usually a prison sentence.' Tier III is Ohio's highest SORN designation. Victim age not reported.
