Former fifth-grade teacher at Cable Elementary School, Northside Independent School District, San Antonio, Bexar County, Texas. Victim was a 12-year-old male student from her class. Age 38 at time of offense.

In late February 2010, three colleagues reported that Caldwell's behavior toward the boy appeared 'overly friendly.' She was counseled and reprimanded but not removed from the classroom. Shortly after, she conducted an unauthorized after-school tutoring session with the boy; the principal moved him out of her classroom on March 5, 2010. Contact continued through the summer after the school year ended.

In the early morning hours of late July 2010, the boy's father awoke to find his son missing. He spotted Caldwell's Ford Explorer outside the home; when he approached, she drove off. A roughly 30-mile chase followed during which it was revealed the boy had been driving her SUV. Caldwell had checked the boy into a motel at 1:15 a.m. and performed a sex act on him. She was arrested in Hondo, Texas. Bail was set at $75,000.

Originally charged with aggravated sexual assault of a child. Under a plea agreement, the charge was reduced to indecency with a child by exposure. Prosecutors remained silent at the probation hearing per the plea terms. Sentenced November 27, 2012 by Judge Lori Valenzuela to 10 years in prison; Valenzuela retained jurisdiction. On May 20, 2013 — within the Texas 180-day window — the sentence was converted to probation via shock probation. Actual time served: approximately 6 months. Caldwell had previously taught at districts in El Paso and Eagle Pass.
