Former teacher at Carver Middle School, Laurinburg, Scotland County, North Carolina. First-year teacher at the time of the offenses; NC educator license (Science 6-9, #1220444) effective February 15, 2018.

Victim was a 12-year-old student (under 13). Stanley was 23 at the time of offense. In late 2018, a parent notified the Scotland County Board of Education about an inappropriate relationship between Stanley and a student. Stanley submitted her resignation the day before her arrest, violating the state law requiring 30 days notice; the district was required to report the resignation to the NC State Board of Education. Removed from the classroom as soon as the investigation began.

Arrested by Scotland County Sheriff's Office approximately December 1, 2018 on a $2.5 million secured bond. Charged with 14 felonies: 3 counts statutory sex offense (victim under 13), 5 counts indecent liberties with a student by a teacher, 5 counts sexual activity by a teacher, and 1 count third-degree child exploitation (child pornography).

Accepted a plea deal and sentenced May 19, 2021 by Judge Dawn Layton. Five consecutive structured sentences: 18-31 months, 18-31 months, 17-30 months, 14-26 months, 14-26 months. Aggregate minimum 81 months / maximum approximately 12 years. Aggravating factor on all counts: defendant took advantage of a position of trust or confidence to commit the offense. Held at Anson Correctional Institute; NCDPS projected release date July 20, 2026.
