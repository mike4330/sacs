Former teacher and basketball coach at Jerling Junior High School, Orland Park, Illinois. Subsequently employed at Hufford Junior High School, Joliet. Age approximately 23 at start of offense (age 30 at January 2015 arrest; born c. 1984).

Two victims were sisters who were 14 years old when the abuse began in 2008; Labus was their basketball coach. Labus maintained contact with them after they graduated from Jerling Junior High. The initial charges covered December 2008 through February 2011; the sentencing article states the abuse continued until 2014 when the victims reported it. The abuse came to light when one victim told a friend; the two sisters then spoke with each other and police were notified in January 2015.

Arrested January 21, 2015; charged with four counts of aggravated criminal sexual abuse (Cook County). Bond set at 50,000; placed on electronic home monitoring. Orland Park administrators noted Labus had left Jerling in 2010; Joliet Public Schools District 86 suspended her from Hufford pending the case.

Pleaded guilty December 3, 2020 to aggravated battery (charge reduced from aggravated criminal sexual abuse). Sentenced by Judge Kerry Kennedy to 24 months probation; required to complete sex offender probation program; not required to register as a sex offender. Victims agreed to the plea deal because they did not believe Labus would receive prison time. Judge Kennedy called Labus a 'predator' at sentencing.

NOTE: Charge reduction from aggravated criminal sexual abuse to aggravated battery eliminated sex offender registration entirely — one of the most extreme reductions in this DB. Six-year interval from arrest to sentencing (2015–2020). Female-on-female offense; victims were sisters (multiple-victims, same family). Long-term abuse spanning approximately six years with a coach using her position of access.
