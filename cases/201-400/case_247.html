State v. Whiteside #247 sentenced

StateIowaCourtIowa District Court, Polk County
JudgePaul ScottIncident Date2015-09-01
Defendant Age26Victim Age15
Prison Sentence20.0 yrs

Former teacher and softball coach at Ankeny Northview Middle School, Ankeny, Iowa. Age 26 at time of offense.

Beginning in 2015, engaged in a pattern of sexual conduct with a 15-year-old female student over approximately seven years (2015-2022), using her role as teacher and coach to build intimacy. Conduct included on-campus abuse and electronic contact. Court documents describe Whiteside as methodical in her grooming. Victim reported the abuse to the Ankeny Community School District on January 24, 2024, approximately six years after the documented abuse period. School notified Ankeny Police, who searched Whiteside's work computer and personal phone. She was placed on administrative leave during investigation.

Arrested February 21, 2024, charged with third-degree sexual abuse (Iowa Code 709.4), four counts of sexual exploitation by a school employee (Iowa Code 709.15), and lascivious conduct with a minor (Iowa Code 709.14). Pled guilty to four counts of sexual exploitation by a school employee, pattern/practice/scheme of conduct (Class D felony).

Sentenced June 12, 2025 by District Judge Paul Scott to four consecutive 5-year terms (20 years maximum under Iowa indeterminate sentencing), 10-year Department of Corrections supervision, and sex offender registry. Defense sought 10 years; prosecution sought 20 consecutive years. Judge Scott stated he was struck by her lack of acceptance of responsibility and described the conduct as grooming from beginning to end. Victim told the court she was controlled, manipulated, and abused, and had attempted suicide multiple times due to the trauma.

NOTE: Seven-year relationship — among the longest documented in this DB. Female victim with multiple suicide attempts documented. Delayed reporting (~6 years post-abuse). Victim's statement alleged Whiteside may have been concurrently abusing a former Ankeny Centennial student; no additional charges filed. Iowa indeterminate sentencing means actual release is parole-board dependent.
