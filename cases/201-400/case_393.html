Lauren Debenedetta #393 sentenced

StateFloridaCourtCharlotte County Circuit Court
Incident Date2016-01-01
Defendant Age37 Victim Age15
Prison Sentence26.0 yrs

Former dance instructor and founder of Haven Dance Studio, North Port, Sarasota County, Florida (opened 2013). Studio shuttered shortly after her arrest. Age approximately 37-38 at time of offense.

Victim was a 15-year-old female student at Haven Dance Studio. The abuse began at the studio when Debenedetta called the student into her office during dance class, kissed her on the mouth, and touched her genitals. The relationship escalated to include sexual activity at Debenedetta's home and at the victim's home. Debenedetta purchased a prepaid cellphone for the victim so they could communicate covertly.

Discovered in 2016 when the victim's stepfather returned home unexpectedly and found Debenedetta hiding naked next to the victim's bed. Charged in 2016 in both Charlotte County and Sarasota County; cases consolidated to Charlotte County so the victim would only need to testify once.

Trial held in Charlotte County, July 2019. Found guilty on all six counts July 26, 2019: four counts of lewd or lascivious molestation (victim 12-15, offender 18+, F.S. 800.04(5)(c)(2)); one count of lewd or lascivious conduct; one count of lewd or lascivious battery. Sentenced September 20, 2019 to 26 years in prison, followed by 5 years sex offender probation, and lifetime sex offender registration. Prosecutors: ASA Kyle Stublen (Charlotte) and Special Appointed ASA Brooke Kernan (Sarasota).

NOTE: Same-sex offense — female defendant, female victim. Dance studio founder/instructor rather than traditional school teacher; position of authority is the institutional hook. Discovery: stepfather found defendant naked in victim's room. Prepaid phone provided to conceal communications. County field set to Sarasota (studio location, North Port); prosecution consolidated to Charlotte County where some offenses occurred.
