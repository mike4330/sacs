Former career and technical instruction teacher at Lowndes High School, Valdosta, Lowndes County, Georgia. Three confirmed male victims, all students at the high school at the time of the offenses. Age 42 at arrest (April 2015); age at time of offense approximate — many victims had graduated by arrest, suggesting offenses may have begun 1–2 years prior.

The Lowndes County Sheriff's Office received information in March 2015 about rumors of sexual contact between Chapman and male students. Investigators identified three male students who confirmed having had sexual relations with her. Sheriff Chris Prine noted that additional victims might be identified. Chapman surrendered to authorities on April 21, 2015, charged with three counts of sexual assault.

Chapman pleaded guilty to cruelty to children in the second degree (felony). Sentenced March 1, 2017 under Georgia's First Time Offender's Act to four years probation and a $2,000 fine. If she fulfills all requirements, she will be discharged without adjudication of guilt and 'completely exonerated of guilt of said offense charged.' Ordered to have no contact with victims or their families, and no contact with Lowndes County Schools except to inquire about her own children.
