Caroline Lawson #333 sentenced

StateFloridaCourtHillsborough County Circuit Court
Incident Date2017-11-01
Defendant Age29 Victim Age17
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former English teacher at Earl J. Lennard High School, Ruskin, Hillsborough County, Florida. Age 29 at time of offense. Victim was a 17-year-old male student. Sexual intercourse at Lawson's Tampa apartment on November 1, 2017. Lawson subsequently texted a third party stating she had slept with a student; victim provided detailed account to investigators. Lawson had been employed by the district since 2014 and resigned following her arrest.

Charged with 2 counts of unlawful sexual activity with a minor (sexual offenses against students by authority figure), second-degree felony, up to 15 years each. Arrested November 3, 2017; $30,000 bail.

Pleaded guilty to a lesser offense on February 6, 2019. Sentenced October 7, 2021: 5 years probation per count (concurrent); 50 hours community service (5 hrs/month); $1,500 restitution to victim; outpatient sex offender treatment; no contact with victim; no unsupervised contact with minors under 18 except biological infant child. No prison time. Prosecutor: Melissa Grajales. Defense: Paul Marx Sisco.

NOTE: Charge reduction from second-degree felony to lesser offense on plea. Sentencing occurred over two years after plea, unusual delay. No sex offender registration apparent from record.
