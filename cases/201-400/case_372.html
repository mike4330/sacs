Former business/vocation and 8th-grade English teacher at Dardanelle School District, Dardanelle, Yell County, Arkansas.

Victim was a 16-year-old male student. On September 6, 2018, the victim met Vega at a park in Russellville; he got into the passenger seat of her car and they engaged in sexual intercourse. The victim reported the relationship to the Russellville Police Department on September 11, 2018. Investigators obtained security footage from cameras at the park corroborating the victim's account of timing. Officers then contacted Vega, who denied sexual contact but admitted to a texting relationship with the victim and said some of their messages had crossed the line. She confirmed meeting the victim at the park. Age 40 at time of offense.

Arrested October 12, 2018. Placed on administrative leave, then fired by Dardanelle School District in October 2018.

Pleaded no contest to first-degree sexual assault in February 2019 in Pope County Circuit Court, Judge William Pearson. Sentenced to 6 years probation, 20 hours community service, and more than $2,500 in fines and court fees, with mandatory sex offender registration. A no-contact order bars any contact with the victim or his family. Banned from attending athletic or school-sponsored events at Dardanelle High School until June 2020, though permitted on campus to drop off or pick up her own children.

NOTE: No-prison outcome on first-degree sexual assault — max 30 years and a $15,000 fine. Offense location was Russellville (Pope County), prosecuted in Pope County; school was in Dardanelle (Yell County) per standard county convention. Park security footage was key corroboration.
