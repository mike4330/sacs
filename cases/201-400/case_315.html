Jennifer Levine #315 sentenced

State North Carolina
Court Pitt County Superior Court
Incident Date 2010-10-23
Defendant Age 32
Victim Age 15
Prison Sentence 1.33 yrs

Former administrative support associate in the School of Social Work at East Carolina University (ECU), Greenville, North Carolina. Not a classroom teacher — administrative role. Age 32 at time of offense.

Victims were teenage girls. Levine allegedly brought young girls to her apartment and provided them with alcohol; sex offenses followed. Investigation began October 2010; arrested November 4, 2010 by Pitt County Sheriff; initial bond 00,000.

Two cases filed in Pitt Superior Court (10CR058850 and 10CR058851). First degree sexual offense counts found no probable cause (January 2011) and were dismissed. Pleaded guilty May 4, 2011 to: (1) indecent liberties with a child (N.C.G.S. 14-202.1) — 16–20 months, 181 days credit for time served, 30 months supervised probation + 120 days special probation in custody; (2) sexual battery (N.C.G.S. 14-27.5A) — 75 days confinement, 30 months supervised probation. Both cases nominally discharged October 30, 2013. Sex offender registration required.

On September 1, 2014, Levine taped a note to the Pitt County sex offender compliance office stating she was moving out of the country. A SORA violation warrant was issued. U.S. Marshals found her working at a school near Rome, Italy, and monitored her location. Arrested September 5, 2015 at Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta airport upon return; transported to Clayton County, Georgia; faced 10–59 months in state prison for the SORA violation.
