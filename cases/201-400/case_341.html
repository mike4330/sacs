Amanda Ruth Schweitzer #341 sentenced

StateMissouriCourtU.S. District Court, Western District of Missouri
JudgeRoseann Ketchmark Incident Date2017-03-01
Defendant Age37 Victim Age13
Prison Sentence20.0 yrs

Former teacher at North Middle School, Joplin, Jasper County, Missouri. Age 37 at time of offense (age 42 at sentencing November 2021). Victim was a 13-year-old male student of Schweitzer's.

Beginning in March 2017, Schweitzer communicated with the victim primarily via Instagram. She sent him nude photos of herself; he sent one back. Sexual intercourse on at least two occasions in March 2017: at Schweitzer's Joplin home (Jasper County) and at a south Joplin campground (Newton County).

Initial investigation began when three male students reported Schweitzer had sent them nude photos. Arrested approximately August 1, 2017; released on $40,000 bond plus $10,000 cash. Held on state statutory rape charges in both Jasper County and Newton County.

Pleaded guilty June 16, 2020 to one count of enticing a minor for illegal sexual activity (18 U.S.C. § 2422(b)). Sentenced November 23, 2021 by U.S. District Judge Roseann Ketchmark: 20 years federal prison without parole; 15 years supervised release following incarceration. AUSA Ami Harshad Miller. Investigation: Southwest Missouri Cyber Crimes Task Force, Joplin PD, FBI. Case prosecuted under Project Safe Childhood.

NOTE: Three male students reported receiving nude photos in the initial investigation; federal plea covers one victim. State charges in Jasper and Newton counties appear to have resolved federally. 20-year sentence without parole (federal system; no early release) — among the longer sentences in the DB. Explicit image sent to and received from a 13-year-old. Victim was Schweitzer's own student.
