Former assistant principal at Stuart Cramer High School, Belmont, Gaston County, North Carolina. Previously taught exceptional children at North Gaston High, Springfield Elementary, Kiser Elementary, and Stanley Middle School. Employed by Gaston County Schools since 2009; became assistant principal in 2015 after earning a master's degree from Appalachian State University. Annual salary over $66,000. Age 34 at time of offenses.

Victim 1 was a 17-year-old male student who was struggling academically. His mother told the court that Rothwell had promised to take him under her wing when his mother approached her about his academic difficulties. Sexual activity with Victim 1 occurred in April 2019. Victim 2 was an 18-year-old male student; sexual activity occurred in September and October 2019 before Rothwell's arrest. Rothwell voluntarily disclosed the relationship with Victim 2 to her attorney after being arrested for the first.

On October 4, 2019, Gaston County Police went to Rothwell's home in Charlotte. She admitted her conduct to her estranged husband and then confessed to police that night. Arrested October 4; spent 7 days in jail on a $1 million bond before posting a reduced amount. Suspended with pay by Gaston County Schools.

Pleaded guilty October 28, 2019 to 2 counts of sexual activity with a student. Sentenced by Superior Court Judge Steven Warren to 2 years supervised probation; active prison sentence of 10-21 months suspended. Required to register as a sex offender for 30 years; teaching license surrendered. DA Locke Bell noted that NC sentencing guidelines would not have permitted active prison time for a first-time offender in this situation.

NOTE: Administrator rather than classroom teacher — access to Victim 1 came through an administrative counseling role with a struggling student. Multiple victims; Victim 1 was 17, Victim 2 was adult (18). Victim 1's mother told the court: 'I feel like my son got no justice at all.' Husband also taught in Gaston County Schools. NC guidelines precluded active incarceration. Adult-victim tag applies to Victim 2.
