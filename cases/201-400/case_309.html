Jessica Streeper #309 sentenced

State Pennsylvania
Court Montgomery County Court of Common Pleas
Incident Date 2014-01-01
Defendant Age 33
Victim Age 14
Prison Sentence 0.75 yrs

Former 8th grade math teacher at Eisenhower Middle School, Norristown, Pennsylvania. Age 33 at time of offense. Married with two children (ages 6 and 8 at sentencing).

Victim was a 14-year-old male student. Streeper and the boy communicated regularly by text message; forensic examination of the victim's phone confirmed the messages were sexual in nature. On several occasions Streeper picked the boy up at his Norristown home and they had sex in her car; on multiple occasions she brought him to her Buckingham Township home where they had sex.

Norristown Police received an anonymous tip on May 19, 2014; investigation led to Streeper. Arrested May 22, 2014; charged with institutional sexual assault, involuntary deviate sexual intercourse, corruption of minors, and related charges. Case prosecuted by ADA Jordan Friter under DA Risa Vetri Ferman.

Sentenced August 7, 2015 to 9–23 months in Montgomery County prison; began serving August 14, 2015. Judge granted one week to arrange childcare. In January 2016, Streeper petitioned for early release citing husband's health and financial difficulties; outcome not reported in available coverage.
