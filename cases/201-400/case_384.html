StateOklahomaCourtCuster County District Court
Incident Date2018-03-30
Defendant Age35 Victim Age15
Prison Sentence7.0 yrs

Former teacher at Clinton Public Schools, Clinton, Custer County, Oklahoma. Age 35 at time of first offense.

Victim was a 15-year-old male student. The sexual relationship began March 30, 2018. Hoffman used Facebook Messenger to communicate with the student and arrange meeting locations. They had sex numerous times in her car (Washita County) and in her father's detached garage (Custer County). In late April 2018, Hoffman took a group of students, including the victim, to the state Capitol for the statewide Oklahoma teacher walkout. She picked up the victim first and dropped him off last, having sex with him each time.

On April 7, 2018, Hoffman confessed to her husband. That same day, Hoffman and her husband drove together to the Clinton Police Department to report the relationship. She was arrested the following day by OSBI on complaints of second-degree rape, facilitating sexual conduct or communication with a minor by use of technology, and pattern of criminal offenses. Booked into Custer County Jail.

Pleaded guilty November 5, 2018 to all three charges. Sentenced December 17, 2018 to 15 years with 8 years suspended — 7 years actual Department of Corrections prison time.

NOTE: Husband-reported — Hoffman self-disclosed to her husband who accompanied her to the police. Teacher walkout used as an access opportunity: civic event provided cover to isolate the victim. Facebook Messenger used to coordinate meetings.
