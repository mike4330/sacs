Megan Connors #293 sentenced

State Florida
Court Hillsborough County Circuit Court
Judge Christopher Sabella
Incident Date 2015-01-01
Defendant Age 30
Victim Age 15
Prison Sentence 5.0 yrs

Former math teacher at Turkey Creek Middle School, Plant City, Florida. Age 30 at time of offense.

Victim was a 15-year-old male student. Relationship began January 2015 with kissing, then progressed to sexual intercourse during school hours in private rooms Connors arranged. At least once they left school in her car and had sex at the student's bedroom.

Arrested February 20, 2015 at her Brandon home, initially facing seven counts of sexual battery and lewd and lascivious battery; she invoked her right to remain silent. Charges were later consolidated to five counts. Connors rejected an initial 10-year plea offer; prosecutors then enhanced charges to carry potential life in prison. On the day jury selection began, she accepted a five-year deal.

Pleaded guilty August 1, 2016 to one count each of sexual battery and lewd and lascivious battery. Sentenced by Circuit Judge Christopher Sabella to 5 years in state prison, 2 years community control, and 15 years probation. Designated a sexual predator with lifetime sex offender registration.

Married with two minor children at sentencing; husband stood by her.

NOTE: Plea-day capitulation after rejecting 10 years is a notable pattern — charge enhancement to life-eligible triggered the deal. Victim initially denied the relationship then changed his account; cross-examination risk was cited by defense as a factor.
