Prison Sentence15.67 yrs

Former teacher at Motley County High School, Matador, Motley County, Texas. Age 47 at time of offense (age 48 at sentencing February 2018). Victim was a 16-year-old male sophomore at Motley County High School — not confirmed as her direct student.

In December 2016, Calaway used Snapchat to message the victim asking if he wanted to 'hang out,' followed by sexually explicit photos. She attempted to get him to send explicit photos in return. She then drove to a residence in Roaring Springs, Texas, picked him up, drove him to a secluded scenic location near Roaring Springs, and engaged in sexual conduct with him.

Taken into custody September 2017. Pleaded guilty October 2017 to one count of enticement of a minor (18 U.S.C. § 2422(b)). Sentenced February 9, 2018 by Senior U.S. District Judge Sam R. Cummings to 188 months in federal prison. AUSA Jeffrey Haag. Investigation: FBI, Lamb County Sheriff's Office, Motley County Sheriff's Office, Floyd County DA's Office, and Lamb County DA's Office. Case prosecuted under Project Safe Childhood.

NOTE: Federal case. Mandatory minimum 10 years, maximum life for 18 U.S.C. § 2422(b). 188 months (15.67 years) is substantially above the mandatory minimum — one of the longer sentences in the DB for a single offense with a 16-year-old victim. Calaway was 48 at sentencing; release would come in her mid-60s. Roaring Springs is the county seat of Motley County; jurisdiction involved multiple county and federal agencies.
