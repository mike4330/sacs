Corinna Carmen Crumpton #208 active

State Georgia
Court Hart County Superior Court
Incident Date 2021-08-01
Defendant Age 29

Former teacher at Hart County High School, Hartwell, Hart County, Georgia. Resident of Toccoa, Georgia (Stephens County). Victim: minor high school student; age not disclosed in available sources.

Crimes alleged to have occurred August through December 2021: electronically transmitted a photograph of her genitals to a minor student via Instagram. Hartwell Police Department requested GBI assistance. Arrested January 4, 2022 on felony charges of electronically furnishing obscene materials to a minor and sexual assault by a person with supervisory or disciplinary authority.

Indicted on multiple counts including Count 2: Improper Sexual Contact by an Employee in the First Degree (O.C.G.A. §16-6-5.1(b.2)). Trial court sustained a general demurrer to Count 2, ruling the statute required physical contact and that sending a photo did not qualify.

Georgia Court of Appeals, Case No. A23A0888 (decided October 12, 2023): reversed the trial court's dismissal of Count 2. Court held the plain language of §16-6-5.1(b.2) prohibits 'sexual contact' broadly, not limited to physical touching — that transmitting a lewd image ('lewd exhibition of the genitals') constitutes sexual contact intended to arouse or satisfy sexual desires. Case remanded for further proceedings. No final plea, conviction, or sentencing reported as of early 2026.

NOTE: Significant appellate case — Court of Appeals ruling established that digital transmission of explicit material from a teacher to a student constitutes 'sexual contact' under Georgia's educator misconduct statute even without physical touching. Case still pending post-remand. Defendant age at offense approximately 29 (30 at arrest January 2022).
