Emma Delaney Hancock #29 sentenced

StateOklahomaCourtLincoln County District Court
JudgeSheila KirkIncident Date2022-10-06
Defendant Age28Victim Age15
Prison Sentence4.0 yrs

Former substitute teacher at Wellston Public Schools, Wellston, Oklahoma (Lincoln County). Age 26 at time of offense.

Victim was a 15-year-old male sophomore at Wellston High School. Contact began October 6, 2022 when the student put his number in Hancock's phone for a class assignment; she sent him a Snapchat friend request that same night. Over the following weeks, conversations escalated from casual to nude pictures and videos. There were also two instances of physical contact: Hancock kissed and touched the student during lunch break while hiding in classroom closets or pantries on campus. She also let the student vape from her vape pen. Hancock also showed two 8th-grade students videos of herself kissing a woman and simulating oral sex at a Halloween party.

When confronted by another student who had heard about the relationship, Hancock initially denied it then admitted it, saying it "would not amount to anything because they were not going to get caught." OSBI began assisting the Lincoln County Sheriff's Office in November 2022. A search warrant on her Snapchat account found videos. She turned herself in April 13, 2023 and posted bond.

Trial began October 28, 2024 (~24 witnesses over 3 days). Jury found guilty on 2 counts of soliciting sexual conduct via technology, acquitted on the lewd/indecent acts and two additional soliciting counts. Jury recommended 3-year sentences on each guilty count. Judge Sheila Kirk sentenced to 4 years in prison on December 18, 2024, above the jury's recommendation. Defense: Billy Coyle. Civil lawsuit filed by student and father.

Husband at time of offense and trial was Alfred Hancock, Wellston police chief. Divorce filed August 12, 2025 (Lincoln County case FD-2025-00079).

NOTE: The lewd/indecent acts charge (acquitted) corresponds to real physical conduct (on-campus kissing and touching) documented in the affidavit — contact_type on conviction charges is 0 (non-contact soliciting only) but actual conduct was at minimum non-penetrative. Defense attorney Coyle referenced "another case in this exact area, within the same school district" with the same judge and prosecutor — likely the Alyssa Todd case (case #28). Cross-jurisdiction pattern worth tracking.
