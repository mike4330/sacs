Commonwealth v. Kristen McAllister #8 sentenced

Former English teacher at Tabb High School, York County, Virginia. Victim was an underage student; gender and exact age not reported. Age 31 at time of offense (November 1, 2021; birthday November 8).

An anonymous tip reached Tabb High School administrators on the evening of June 8, 2022, alleging unprofessional conduct involving a minor. Administrators immediately reported to the York-Poquoson Sheriff's Office. YPSO Major Ron Montgomery's team interviewed the tipster, the student, and McAllister by the following morning. McAllister was arrested at the school board office on June 9, 2022, and bonded out the same day. At the time of arrest, investigators noted the possibility of additional victims.

Indicted October 18, 2022 (case CR22001892-01) on one felony count of taking indecent liberties with a child by a person in a custodial or supervisory relationship (Va. Code §18.2-370.1). On June 24, 2025, pleaded guilty to an amended misdemeanor charge of contributing to the delinquency of a minor (Va. Code §18.2-371). All felony and remaining charges were dismissed. Sentenced to 12 months jail fully suspended, 12 months unsupervised probation, and $262 in costs.

NOTE: Case took nearly three years from indictment to resolution (October 2022 to June 2025). Felony amended to misdemeanor at plea — no prison time, and Virginia's misdemeanor contributing to delinquency charge (§18.2-371) does not carry a sex offender registration requirement. Possible additional victims flagged at arrest but no follow-up reporting found.
