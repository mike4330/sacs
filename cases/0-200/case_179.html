Amanda Hubble #179 sentenced

StateCaliforniaCourtFresno County Superior Court
JudgeJonathan Skiles Incident Date2021-11-01
Defendant Age32 Victim Age17
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former choral teacher at Clovis High School, Clovis Unified School District, Clovis, California. Had taught music for CUSD since 2016; 2021–22 was her first year at the high school level. Victim was a 17-year-old male student. Two documented sexual encounters: one in Hubble's car in November 2021, and one at Hubble's home in December 2021, two days before her arrest.

The relationship came to light when the victim tried to sneak home after the December encounter and found the door locked; his father confronted him. The next day the victim disclosed to a friend, and Hubble disclosed to another student. Snapchat records and phone records showed the two professing love and arranging meetups. Police sought a search warrant for bed sheets, comforter, and clothing.

Defendant age unknown. Arrested December 4, 2021. Originally charged with four felony sex counts. Placed on unpaid leave by CUSD following arrest; teaching credential suspended for misconduct.

Pleaded no contest to one count of unlawful sexual intercourse and one count of oral copulation of a person under 18. Sentenced July 27, 2022 by Judge Jonathan Skiles to two years probation and sex offender registration for a minimum of 10 years. Maximum exposure was 3 years 8 months. Credential subsequently revoked and employment terminated upon conviction.

Defense attorney W. Kent Hamlin; prosecutor Sandra Garcia. The victim's mother read a victim impact statement describing severe psychological harm: weight loss, antidepressant side effects, academic failure, job loss, loss of friends, and suicidal ideation. The family requested the maximum sentence.

NOTE: No-prison outcome over family's explicit objection for maximum. Victim impact statement among the most severe in the DB — suicidal ideation documented. Snapchat communication channel. Charge reduction from 4 felonies to 2.
