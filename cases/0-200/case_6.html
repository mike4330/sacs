Also known as: Katherine Ruth Harper

StateTexasCourtDenton County District Court
Incident Date2016-06-01
Defendant Age26Victim Age15
Prison Sentence6.0 yrs

7th grade English teacher and tennis coach at Tidwell Middle School (Northwest ISD), Roanoke, TX. Age 26–27 at time of offense (27 at arrest). The victim was a 15-year-old male former student who had since transferred to Byron Nelson High School; Harper was his former teacher and tennis coach at Tidwell.

Northwest ISD received an anonymous tip via the district website in December 2016 alleging Harper and the student were having sex and that she was pregnant. When the Byron Nelson principal questioned the student, he began crying and admitted to multiple sexual encounters. District officials placed Harper on administrative leave.

The defined incident period runs June 1–July 31, 2016, during which Harper and the victim communicated 76 times. Encounters included drinking alcohol, removing clothing, oral sex, and intercourse. Harper sent multiple nude photos via smartphone. At least one encounter included intercourse at Harper's initiation. Encounters took place at the boy's home and Harper's home.

Arrested March 21, 2017 by Trophy Club police; booked into Denton County jail; released on $15,000 bond. Harper was 8 months pregnant at arrest. The arrest warrant does not explicitly identify the 15-year-old as the father. Pleaded guilty; sentenced to 6 years prison and 10 years probation.

NOTE: Pregnancy at arrest is a defining feature of this case; paternity of child left ambiguous in public record. Former-student dynamic (victim no longer at her school at time of offense) is notable — relationship began during his time as her student. Cash communication pattern: 76 contacts in a 2-month window.
