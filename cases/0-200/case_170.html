Former physical education teacher at Las Plumas High School and Oroville High School, Butte County CA; taught in the district approximately 10 years. Age 32–33 at time of offense; 33 at arrest; 34 at probation violation sentencing. Two male victims (both 17), one a student at Las Plumas High School and one at Prospect High School. Brief sexual relationships with both students during summer 2015, away from school premises; included sharing nude photographs via electronic devices.

Arrested (date approximate, 2015–early 2016) on two counts of unlawful sexual intercourse with a minor, two counts of communicating with a minor for purposes of committing sexual acts, and two counts of communicating with a minor to commit a lewd or lascivious act. Sex offender registration was not required at sentencing, though Judge James Reilley (Butte County Superior Court) indicated it would be a strong possibility upon violation.

Sentencing on violation June 7, 2017: 60 days in jail, sex offender registration required, probation reinstated. Defense attorney Grady Davis argued registration was monitoring not punishment and the violation was non-sexual; judge disagreed.
