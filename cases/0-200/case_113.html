Former agricultural science teacher at Rockdale High School, Rockdale, Texas (Milam County). Age 24 at arrest. Male victim aged 17.

Despain engaged in a sexual relationship with the student during the 2022-23 school year and into fall 2023. Investigators found text messages on her phone in which the two professed love for each other, and a video on her Snapchat account showing them having sex. Surveillance footage showed the pair alone in her classroom on at least four occasions, including approximately three hours on Labor Day 2023 (when the school was closed) and approximately 90 minutes on Sunday, September 17, 2023. They were also alone at her home while her husband was away. Despain was pregnant; DNA testing confirmed the student, not her husband, was the father of the child.

Rockdale ISD reported potential misconduct to police on September 21, 2023. Despain was immediately suspended and resigned four days later. She turned herself in at the Milam County Jail on November 7, 2023 on two felony warrants: possession/promotion of child pornography (bond $200,000) and improper relationship between educator and student (bond $150,000). She pleaded guilty in November 2024 to the improper relationship charge (2nd degree felony, maximum 20 years). The child pornography charge was dismissed. Sentenced February 14, 2025 by Judge John W. Youngblood: 10 years felony probation, $3,000 fine, forfeiture of teaching license, sex offender evaluation and recommended treatment. No prison time; the offense as charged does not require sex offender registration.

NOTE: Milam County DA Brian Price explicitly argued for prison time and invoked the gender double standard at sentencing: 'My argument to the judge was she was in a position of power and trust over her students and she used that to abuse this young man, and that is going to affect him his entire life. People put in a position of authority and trust should be held to a higher standard, not a lower one.' He noted that a male teacher in the same circumstances would unquestionably receive prison. One of the few cases in this DB where a prosecutor publicly named the double standard on record.
