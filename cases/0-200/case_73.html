Prison Sentence15.0 yrs

Former substitute teacher at West Tallahatchie School District, Mississippi. Three victims (Minor 1, Minor 2, Minor 3), all exploited within a one-week window: Dec 13 (Minor 3), Dec 19 (Minor 1), Dec 20 (Minor 2), 2023. Resident of Southaven MS (DeSoto County — Memphis suburb area; Tallahatchie County is approximately 90 miles south, a notable geographic distance between residence and school district). State charges: sexual battery by a person in a position of trust and authority + child exploitation. Federal charge: production of child sexual abuse material (18 U.S.C. § 2251). State sentence: 25 years MDOC custody — 15 years to serve + 10 years post-release supervision; SORA registration; $2,000 assessment to Victims of Human Trafficking and Commercial Sexual Exploitation Fund. Federal sentence: 15 years (concurrent with state sexual battery charge). After release: 10 years state post-release supervision still applies. Sentenced November 3, 2025. Prosecuted by Mississippi AG's office (AG Lynn Fitch) in partnership with FBI. AG Fitch: 'Exploiting children that you are responsible to care for is a serious violation of trust.' NOTE: CSAM production charge (§ 2251) carries a federal mandatory minimum of 15 years — this is what drove the outcome. The production charge is substantively more serious than the sexual battery alone: it documents that Moore was not only abusing children but recording that abuse. Substitute teacher in a school district far from her home address raises access/targeting questions. Mississippi AG-level prosecution (not just county DA) and FBI involvement indicate this was treated as a significant case from early in the investigation. One of the harsher sentences in this DB; the federal mandatory minimum for CSAM production is non-negotiable unlike the state-court sentencing discretion seen in Alabama and California cases.
