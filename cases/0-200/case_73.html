Former substitute teacher at West Tallahatchie School District, Mississippi. Three victims exploited within a one-week window in December 2023. Resident of Southaven, Mississippi (DeSoto County — a Memphis suburb), approximately 90 miles from Tallahatchie County, a notable geographic distance between her residence and the school district.

Charged with state counts of sexual battery by a person in a position of trust and authority and child exploitation, and three federal counts of production of child sexual abuse material. Sentenced November 3, 2025. State sentence: 25 years in MDOC custody with 15 years to serve plus 10 years post-release supervision, sex offender registration, and a $2,000 assessment to the Victims of Human Trafficking and Commercial Sexual Exploitation Fund. Federal sentence: 15 years, concurrent with the state sexual battery charge. The 10-year state post-release supervision term applies after release regardless of the concurrent federal term. Prosecuted by the Mississippi Attorney General’s office (AG Lynn Fitch) in partnership with the FBI. AG Fitch: ‘Exploiting children that you are responsible to care for is a serious violation of trust.’

NOTE: The CSAM production charge carries a federal mandatory minimum of 15 years — this drove the outcome. Production charges are substantively more serious than sexual battery alone: they document that Moore was not only abusing children but recording the abuse. The geographic distance between her home in DeSoto County and the school district raises access and targeting questions. Mississippi AG-level prosecution and FBI involvement indicate this was treated as a significant case early in the investigation. One of the harsher sentences in this DB; the federal mandatory minimum for CSAM production is non-negotiable, unlike the sentencing discretion seen in Alabama and California state cases.
