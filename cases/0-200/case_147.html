Former physical education teacher at Ponaganset High School, Glocester, Rhode Island (Foster-Glocester Regional School District). Age approximately 37–39 during the offense; 39 at time of complaint. Married.

Crins began texting a male student during his sophomore year after allegedly finding his cell phone number online, initially asking him to do work for her while she was on vacation. The two continued communicating via text and social media. During his junior year (victim age 17), the relationship shifted — Crins sent texts telling him he "looked good," asked to wear his sweatshirts and his jersey at a school pep rally, and asked what he would give her for her birthday.

Crins then asked him for a kiss and gave him her home address in Cranston. The student drove to the address, parked on a nearby side street, and Crins walked over and sat next to him in his vehicle. She said "I can't believe I'm going to do this… You can't tell anybody," then kissed him for approximately 15 minutes. In the following days she sent texts saying she couldn't stop thinking about him, that he was "a good kisser," and that he had "impressed" her.

Several weeks later she invited him back; they kissed again in his vehicle and she engaged in sexual contact with him in the backseat. The student denied sexual intercourse occurred. Crins sent the student lingerie photos and videos while he was 17; she also sent nude photos and videos but claims only after he turned 18. She acknowledged making plans to have sex with him once he turned 18, which she claimed they never acted upon.

The student graduated spring 2025. He filed a formal complaint with Rhode Island State Police in October 2025. Superintendent Dr. Renee Palazzo confirmed the teacher "no longer works for the district." In a police interview Crins admitted to flirting, to kissing him once on a side street near her home, and to sending photos, but denied they were "ever physical" beyond one kiss. She said she told him to stop because she was his teacher and was married.

Indicted March 2026 on two counts of third-degree sexual assault (Rhode Island). Case active.
