Former assistant teacher at Eupora High School, Webster County MS. Also known as Kayla Murphy Tindall. Age 32 at arrest. Hired by Webster County Schools as assistant teacher at EHS during a special board meeting July 30 (2019) — approximately 2 months before the offense. Victim: 13-year-old child; incident approximately October 1, 2019; affidavit specified victim was 36 or more months younger than Tindall. Victim gender not specified in public records. EHS administrators made aware of alleged misconduct January 30, 2020; employee terminated or resigned the same day (superintendent Brian Jones did not clarify which). Mississippi Bureau of Investigation took over from Webster County Sheriff's Office; MBI Investigator Vernon Hathcock filed the affidavit February 4, 2020. Tindall surrendered on warrant February 4; released on $50,000 bond set by Justice Court Judge Rebecca Ellison. Preliminary hearing requested; continued to March 6. Pled guilty to statutory rape in Webster County Circuit Court. Sentenced approximately April 4, 2022: 6 years prison + 9 years probation upon release + SORA registration. Superintendent Jones: 'Our top priority is the safety of our students.' NOTE: Hired July 30, 2019; offense approximately October 1, 2019 — approximately 60 days into her employment. Raises the question of whether the relationship predated employment or began immediately upon hiring. MBI involvement (not just county sheriff) in a rural Mississippi case suggests the school-employee nature of the offense triggered state-level investigation protocols. 6 years is a meaningful sentence by Mississippi state standards; compare Moore (#73, 15 years) where federal CSAM production drove a much higher outcome.
