Ashley Wells #182 sentenced

StateKentuckyCourtChristian Circuit Court
Incident Date2016-12-01
Defendant Age34 Victim Age17
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former teacher at Trigg County High School, Trigg County, Kentucky. Victim was a 17-year-old male student from Christian County. Incident occurred in December 2016. Conduct described by the Commonwealth's Attorney as a passionate kiss lasting several minutes during which the victim's hands made sexual contact with Wells' body. No sexual intercourse alleged.

Age approximately 34 at time of offense (December 2016); age 40 at time of plea (November 2022). Indicted by a Christian County grand jury in April 2022 on one count of first-degree sexual abuse following a Kentucky State Police investigation. Arraigned in Christian Circuit Court.

Entered an Alford plea on November 17, 2022 to an amended charge of second-degree unlawful transaction with a minor (Class D felony), reduced from first-degree sexual abuse. Sentenced January 18, 2023 to a one-year sentence, felony pre-trial diverted for three years under supervision of probation and parole. No jail time and no sex offender registration required, provided no new crimes are committed during the diversion period.

On March 14, 2025 — while still within the three-year diversion period — Wells was arrested in Hopkinsville for second-degree wanton endangerment, DUI, and possession of an open alcohol container in a vehicle. She was found asleep in her car in the parking lot of Christian County Middle School with two child passengers and an open alcohol beverage. This arrest may trigger diversion revocation proceedings.

NOTE: No-prison, no-registration outcome on felony charge via Alford plea and pretrial diversion. Charge reduction from first-degree sexual abuse to second-degree unlawful transaction. Six-year gap between incident (December 2016) and indictment (April 2022) suggests delayed reporting or delayed investigation. Subsequent 2025 DUI arrest during diversion period with children present is a potentially significant development — diversion revocation possible.
