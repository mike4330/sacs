Former third-grade teacher at Sanborn Elementary School, Amarillo Independent School District. Hired September 2002. Age 34 at arrest. Male victims aged 15. Charges filed in two counties (Potter and Randall) reflecting separate incidents.

Beginning June 2011, Moore exchanged approximately 100 text messages with at least one 15-year-old Amarillo High School student — described as half "extremely sexually explicit" — over a three-month period. She offered oral sex in one message. In September or October 2011, Moore invited a 15-year-old boy to her car during halftime of an Amarillo High football game at Dick Bivins Stadium: she asked if he wanted to "see something she had in her car," led him to the vehicle where condoms were visible in the console, then drove him to a nearby apartment complex where they had sex in the back seat for 10 to 15 minutes. She returned him to the stadium before the second half. Randall County charges stem from Moore touching the genitals of a victim on or around July 15 and August 1, 2011. Affidavits reference three 15-year-old boys total. A DPS official observed Moore with a teen at a football game — hugging, sitting together, walking off privately — and reported this to investigators.

Texas Rangers began investigating in November 2011. On November 11, Rangers seized a Dell laptop, two USB flash drives, and an AT&T SIM card from Moore's Randall County home, and an iPhone 4 from Sanborn Elementary. Moore was placed on paid administrative leave November 14. She turned herself in at the Potter County Detention Center on December 6, 2011, after arrest warrant issued; released on $7,500 bond. A Randall County grand jury indicted on two counts of indecency with a child in March 2012. Moore resigned from AISD shortly after turning herself in.

Pleaded guilty January 31, 2013 to sexual assault of a child (Potter County) and indecency with a child (Randall County). Sentenced by 47th District Attorney Randall Sims: 10 years deferred probation, 180 days in jail, sex offender registration, forfeiture of teaching license.
