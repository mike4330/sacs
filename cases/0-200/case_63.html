Former girls' basketball coach and PE/computers/elementary teacher at Pickens Academy (private school), Carrollton, Alabama. Age 30 at arrest.

Two male student victims were confirmed at plea. Indicted on 13 counts of a school employee having sex with a student under 19. The primary victim alleged at least 11 incidents between October 2014 and March 2016, at her home and in a cemetery. A civil suit by one victim alleged additional incidents in her classroom during school hours, disputed by the defense. Arrested March 28, 2016. Pleaded guilty to improper sexual contact with two students and was sentenced to three years in prison. Prosecuted by DA Chris McCool of Pickens County.

An appeal was filed February 6, 2018 by attorney M. Virginia Buck of Northport on three grounds: equal protection (school employees are criminalized for sex with 16- to 18-year-olds while doctors, ministers, and attorneys are not); the Alabama school-employee statute did not cover private schools until a mid-2016 amendment, predating the charges; and a constitutional right to private consensual sexual intimacy. The Alabama Court of Criminal Appeals denied the appeal. A civil suit filed by the victim on the same day the appeal was denied alleged classroom incidents during school hours.

Husband James Franklin Parker, 32, a math teacher and basketball/football coach at the same school, was arrested March 30, 2016, two days after Charli, on the same charges involving a different female student in a separate and unrelated incident.

NOTE: This is the second cemetery-venue case in the DB (cf. Aldinger #60). The constitutional appeal is the most legally sophisticated challenge to school-employee statutes recorded here; the equal protection argument has theoretical merit but was rejected. The husband-and-wife simultaneous arrest at the same small private school is statistically extraordinary. The private-school statutory gap nearly created a complete legal defense; the court's rejection preserved the conviction.
