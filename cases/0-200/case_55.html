Former teacher's aide at Woodlawn Elementary School, Danville, Kentucky (Boyle County); previously at Lancaster Elementary School, Garrard County. Age 39 at time of sentencing (2024). Operating under married name Ellen Shell during offenses; legally reverting to maiden name Phillips as of January 2026. Mother of two.

Conducted sexual relationships with at least nine boys aged 14–16 across Boyle, Garrard, and Rockcastle counties, beginning approximately December 2021. Communicated via Snapchat, Facebook, and text messaging; met victims in person for sexual activity. Met multiple victims through her daughter (who was the same age as the boys) and through the schools where she worked; one victim had previously dated her daughter. Made "I ❤ Hot Moms" t-shirts and vehicle stickers for the boys as a grooming method. Bribed victims with alcohol and chewing tobacco. A source reported she took multiple boys to a parking lot during a dirt track race in Rockcastle in summer 2022 and performed sexual acts on several of them simultaneously. Investigation was triggered when video of Phillips performing a sexual act on an underage boy circulated online, prompting a mother to confront her teenage son. Ex-husband Giles Shell (brother of Kentucky Commissioner of Agriculture Jonathan Shell) filed for divorce August 2022, citing irreconcilable differences.

Initially arrested April 2023 after two minors alleged rape on three occasions between July 6 and August 15, 2022. Released; worked at a local gas station during investigation. Re-arrested December 2023 by Garrard County Sheriff's Department; held at Clark County Detention Centre. Investigation conducted by Garrard County Sheriff's Office, Homeland Security Investigations, Kentucky State Police, and Danville PD.

Pleaded guilty in federal court to one count of using the internet to entice a minor to engage in sexual conduct. As part of the federal plea, admitted to communicating with victims on Snapchat, Facebook, and text and meeting them for sex; admitted specifically to December 20, 2022 Facebook messages to a 15-year-old attempting to entice him to sneak out, offering alcohol and oral sex when he said he could not leave. Two counts of rape and three of sodomy in Garrard County were dropped following the federal plea. As of January 2024, still facing 5 counts rape, 5 counts sodomy, and 1 count tampering with evidence in Rockcastle and Boyle Counties.

Sentenced April 26, 2024 by Chief U.S. District Judge Danny Reeves to 188 months (15 years 8 months) plus lifetime supervised release. Must serve 85% under federal law (Project Safe Childhood). AUSA Erin Roth prosecuted.

Grooming via custom merchandise ("I ❤ Hot Moms" shirts/stickers) is distinctive. Husband-reported adjacent: discovery via circulated video, not husband directly. Teacher's aide rather than credentialed teacher. Federal enticement prosecution used despite primarily local predation pattern.
