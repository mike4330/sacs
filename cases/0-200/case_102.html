Former dance and social studies teacher at Landmark High School (alternative school, ~340 students, grades 10–12), Spanish Fork, Utah. Age 27 at time of offense. Male victim aged 17, a student she first met in her class in 2015 and again in 2016.

Lewis and the victim arranged to meet at her home on January 4, 2017, after the victim had expressed interest approximately two weeks prior. She supplied vodka; the victim recorded them kissing on his phone before they went to a bedroom and had intercourse. Lewis instructed the victim to deny the relationship if asked. She also purchased beer for the victim and another teenager on a subsequent occasion, funded by cash the victim gave her.

Charged January 31, 2017 with rape (1st degree felony), tampering with a witness (3rd degree felony), and two counts of supplying alcohol to a minor (class A misdemeanor). In June 2017, Lewis pleaded guilty to an amended charge of unlawful sexual activity with a minor, a 3rd degree felony. She received a suspended sentence of up to five years at Utah State Prison, with credit for 195 days pre-trial jail. Placed on the Utah Sex Offender Registry; ordered to remain drug- and alcohol-free and have no contact with students.

In July 2018, Lewis was arrested again after the Utah County Major Crimes Task Force found approximately 12 ounces of methamphetamine in her vehicle. She admitted to selling roughly one ounce of meth per week to juveniles to supplement her income and was additionally charged with drug distribution (2nd degree felony) and possession of paraphernalia. This followed earlier probation violations: a failed drug test in June 2017 and alcohol possession in October 2017. Outcome of the drug charges not known from available sources.

NOTE: Lewis's husband filed for divorce on the same day the original charges were filed (January 31, 2017). Charge reduction from rape (1st degree felony) to unlawful sexual activity (3rd degree felony) is significant. Victim-initiated contact noted in charging documents, though Lewis arranged the meeting and supplied alcohol. Subsequent drug distribution to minors is an unusual post-conviction development.
