Former teacher at Tomball ISD, Harris County, Texas. Victim was a 13-year-old male, a 6th grader in Bodine's class when abuse began. Defendant age not reported in available sources.

Bodine befriended the student by playing Fortnite with him and began texting him. Sexual intercourse began in April 2018, shortly after the student turned 13. After her divorce, Bodine moved into the same apartment complex where the student lived. When the student's family relocated to Houston's south side in July 2018, Bodine continued driving to his home two to four times a month until March 2021. Between April 2020 and February 2021, she sent 25 to 40 explicit photographs of herself to the teen.

Arrested April 2021 and charged with continuous sexual abuse of a child. The district attorney's office sought 20 to 40 years in prison. Harris County Judge George Glass sentenced Bodine to 60 days in jail, 10 years probation, sex offender registration, and therapy. The DA's office publicly objected to the outcome.

NOTE: Criminal defense attorney Christopher Downey, quoted by KTRK, stated that psychiatric testimony presented at sentencing and the defendant's gender likely contributed to the outcome — 'By all accounts, this is an unusual outcome. I am sure that the gender of the person on trial, the fact that this was a woman accused of having sex with a young boy, that's gonna factor into it as well.' The abuse spanned approximately three years (victim ages 13–15). Sending 25–40 explicit photographs to the teen constitutes CSAM-equivalent distribution.
