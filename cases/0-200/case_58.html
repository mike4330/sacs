Former world history teacher at Cypress Springs High School, Cy-Fair ISD, Cypress TX. Age 24 at time of sentencing. Victim: 15-year-old male student. Met with victim for sex at least twice in July 2017; also smoked marijuana with victim during encounters. Additionally sent topless photos via text to other students (plural) beyond the primary victim. Discovered when another student informed school officials; Schiffer admitted to the relationship after confronted. Removed from campus and placed on administrative leave. Charged November 2017: improper relationship with a student (Texas Penal Code § 21.12) + sexual assault of a child aged 14-17. Pled guilty May 2018 to one count of improper relationship with a student; assault charge dropped. Sentenced September 6, 2018: 8 years prison (faced up to 20). Defense attorney: Grant Scheiner. NOTE: Two distinct victim populations: primary victim (sex/marijuana) and secondary victims (topless photo recipients). The marijuana co-use suggests the relationship was framed as peer-level bonding rather than authority-figure grooming — a pattern that likely lowered the victim's perception of wrongdoing. At 24, Schiffer was unusually young for this DB — only ~9 years older than the victim. The student-tipster discovery mechanism (peer reported to school, not victim) is worth tracking.
