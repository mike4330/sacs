Former world history teacher at Cypress Springs High School, Cy-Fair ISD, Cypress, Texas. Age 24 at time of sentencing.

The victim was a 15-year-old male student. Schiffer met with the victim for sex at least twice in July 2017 and smoked marijuana with him during encounters. She also sent topless photos via text to other students beyond the primary victim. The conduct was discovered when another student informed school officials. Schiffer admitted to the relationship after being confronted, was removed from campus, and placed on administrative leave.

Charged in November 2017 with improper relationship with a student and sexual assault of a child aged 14 to 17. Pleaded guilty in May 2018 to one count of improper relationship with a student; the assault charge was dropped. Sentenced September 6, 2018 to 8 years TDCJ, having faced up to 20. Defense attorneys: Grant Scheiner at trial, Josh Schaffer on appeal. Remanded to TDCJ Crain Unit.

Appeal filed October 4, 2018, assigned to the 1st Court of Appeals. Opinion issued March 15, 2019, with mandate of dismissal May 17, 2019. The appeal was dismissed, likely as moot following shock probation.

A motion for shock probation was filed December 27, 2018. The shock probation hearing was held February 13 and 14, 2019, with a bench warrant issued to transport her from the Crain Unit. Shock probation was granted February 14, 2019, and the sentence was suspended and probated February 15, 2019, after approximately five months served in TDCJ. Early termination of community supervision was granted February 10, 2025, roughly six years into probation.
