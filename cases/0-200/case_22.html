State v. Madison Bergmann #22 sentenced

StateWisconsinCourtSt. Croix County Circuit Court
JudgeScott J. NordstrandIncident Date2024-01-08
Defendant Age25Victim Age11
Prison Sentence6.0 yrs

Elementary school 5th-grade teacher, Hudson, Wisconsin (St. Croix County). Defendant age not reported.

Male victim, age 11 (5th grade). Bergmann obtained the victim's phone number during a school skiing trip over winter break 2024 and began texting almost daily. Over approximately three months the pair exchanged over 35,000 text messages and approximately 100 handwritten love notes and artwork. The relationship was discovered when the victim's father found the texts, confronted Bergmann in her classroom with printed copies, and referred the matter to authorities. Arrested April 1, 2024.

Guilty plea entered November 2024 to one count of child enticement (sexual contact) and two counts of sexual misconduct by school staff or volunteer. Sentenced December 19, 2025 by Judge Scott J. Nordstrand to 6 years incarceration and 6 years extended supervision on the child enticement count; the two misconduct counts (1 year 6 months incarceration each) run concurrent. Prosecution sought 12 years; defense sought 1 year. Conditions of extended supervision: lifetime Wisconsin Sex Offender Registry; no contact with the victim or his family; no contact with anyone under 18 without prior agent approval; no internet or social media without prior agent approval; sex offender evaluation and treatment. Defense attorney: Joseph P. Tamburino. Prosecutor: Alysja Otten (same prosecutor as Faust #119).

NOTE: Cross-reference Abigail Faust (#119), a fellow 5th-grade teacher at the same Hudson school. Court documents in the Faust case show Bergmann and Faust were friends who texted each other about their respective relationships with students.
