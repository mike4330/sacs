State v. Madison Bergmann #22 sentenced

State Wisconsin
Court St. Croix County Circuit Court
Incident Date 2024-01-08
Defendant Age 25
Victim Age 11
Prison Sentence 6.0 yrs

Elementary school 5th-grade teacher, Hudson, Wisconsin (St. Croix County). Male victim aged 11 (5th grade).

35,000+ text messages and approximately 100 handwritten love notes and artwork exchanged over a three-month period in 2024. Relationship began after a skiing trip over winter break. Victim's father confronted Bergmann in her classroom with printed texts. Prosecution sought 12 years; defense sought 1 year; judge sentenced 6 years with 6 years supervision to follow.

NOTE: Cross-reference Abigail Faust (#119), a fellow 5th-grade teacher at the same Hudson school. Court documents in the Faust case show Bergmann and Faust were friends who texted each other about their respective relationships with students.
