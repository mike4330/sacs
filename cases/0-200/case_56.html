Heather Hare #56 sentenced

State Arkansas
Court U.S. District Court, Eastern District of Arkansas
Incident Date 2021-08-01
Defendant Age 33
Victim Age 17
Prison Sentence 13.0 yrs

Former Family Consumer Science teacher at Bryant High School, Conway AR. Met minor victim on first day of their senior year (2021-2022 school term). Began one-on-one counseling sessions; gave victim her personal phone number; communicated primarily via Instagram and Snapchat. Told victim she had dreamed of them having sex; gave victim her home address. Engaged in sexual activity 20-30 times during the school term: at her Conway residence, in her vehicle, and in her classroom and parking lots at Bryant High School. April 21-24, 2022: chaperoned school field trip to Washington D.C. as part of her extracurricular role; engaged in unlawful sexual activity with victim during the trip — this became the federal transportation charge. Indicted Aug 1, 2023; charged with (1) transportation of a minor with intent to engage in criminal sexual activity and (2) interstate travel for sexual activity by coercion. Pled guilty Jan 2024 to count 1; count 2 dismissed. Sentenced May 15, 2024 by U.S. District Judge Lee P. Rudofsky: 13 years federal prison + lifetime supervised release. Investigation: FBI, Saline County Sheriff's Office, Bryant PD. Prosecutor: AUSA Kristin Bryant. Notable: appeared on Good Morning America in 2020 after students made her a farewell video when her class was suspended during COVID-19 distance learning. NOTE: Federal nexus is the D.C. field trip — she was both the sponsor and chaperone, making her the sole adult authority on the trip during which the offense occurred. The GMA appearance pre-dates her offending window and is a striking contrast. Victim gender not specified in press materials. The dream disclosure ('I had a dream of us having sex') as a grooming opener is notable — a calculated erosion of the professional boundary framed as intimacy rather than proposition.
