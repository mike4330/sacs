Lindsey Rose Schneeberger #188 appeal

Also known as: Lindsey Schneeberger

StateMinnesotaCourtOlmsted County District Court
Incident Date2023-03-01
Defendant Age25 Victim Age13
Prison Sentence8.0 yrs

Former physical education teacher and volleyball coach at Pine Island Public Schools, Pine Island, Goodhue County, Minnesota. Victim was a 13-year-old female former student and volleyball coachee. Abuse occurred at Schneeberger's residence in Rochester between March 1 and May 15, 2023. Conduct included nude photo exchanges and sexual assault on multiple occasions. Discovered when the victim's mother found explicit text messages; Schneeberger had used pet names including "baby." Schneeberger was placed on leave March 6, 2023, and resigned March 16, 2023.

Age 25 at arrest (May 25, 2023); age 26 at sentencing (April 1, 2024). Arrested May 25, 2023 following a joint investigation by the Rochester Police Department and Goodhue County Sheriff's Office. Criminal complaint filed in Olmsted County (offense location).

Pleaded guilty February 6, 2024 to one count of first-degree criminal sexual conduct with a child under age 14 (Minn. Stat. § 609.342). Sentenced April 1, 2024 to 12 years in prison; minimum 8 years to serve per guidelines. Court rejected defense request for probationary dispositional departure. Sex offender registration required; lifetime supervision post-release.

Appealed sentence June 2024. Minnesota Court of Appeals denied the appeal March 3, 2025, ruling this was not a rare case warranting reversal and affirming the district court did not abuse its discretion. Schneeberger filed a petition for further review to the Minnesota Supreme Court on March 21, 2025; outcome pending.
