Former paraprofessional and long-term substitute at Dixon R-I School District, Dixon, Missouri (Pulaski County), approximately 100 miles northeast of Springfield. Substitute teaching period August 2022 – August 2024. Age 31 at offense. Married at time of offenses.

Multiple victims, at least one in middle school at time of offenses. Incidents occurred from August 2023 through September 2024. First victim was recruited by a friend who asked if they wanted to "do some work and make money"; the friend brought the victim to Smith's home, where Smith offered to pay for sex. Smith paid $130 for the first encounter and continued paying in cash or Cash App for approximately 6–7 subsequent encounters. She also took a victim to a field, offered $110 and marijuana, and performed sexual acts on them. Communicated with victims via Snapchat, including sending explicit photographs. Some sexual acts occurred at isolated outdoor locations (gravel roads, field) when her husband was away.

One victim had a video of Smith performing oral sex on him; Smith "smashed" the victim's phone when she found out. Smith subsequently told her husband the victims were blackmailing her with a marijuana video. Smith's husband threatened the minors with a baseball bat.

Someone reported Smith to school administrators in 2024 for possible misconduct with at least one student; the report was forwarded to authorities. Smith resigned August 2024. Initially arrested November 2024 on 19 counts including statutory rape, statutory sodomy, sexual trafficking, and patronizing prostitution. Bond set at $250,000.

In early September 2025, Smith was found at her home with one of the victims in violation of her no-contact order; bond was revoked September 10, 2025 and she was charged with tampering with a witness. Held at Pulaski County Jail on no bond.

Pleaded guilty (date: September 2025 per timeline) to 1 count endangering the welfare of a child and 2 counts sexual contact with a student. Sentenced November 19, 2025 by Judge Brendon Fox to 10 years: 6 years (endangering welfare) + 2 years each (two sexual contact counts), consecutive.
