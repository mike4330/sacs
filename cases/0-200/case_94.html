Elena Bardin #94 sentenced

State Kentucky
Court Adair Circuit Court
Judge Samuel Spalding
Incident Date 2024-09-01
Defendant Age 27
Victim Age 17
Prison Sentence 14.0 yrs

Former English teacher at Adair Youth Development Center (state juvenile detention facility), Columbia, Kentucky. Age 26-27 at time of offense; joined the facility in 2024. Male victim age 17, incarcerated juvenile facing an unrelated murder charge in Jefferson County.

Relationship developed over several months through letters and in-person contact. Security footage showed Bardin entering the victim's cell for extended periods in violation of facility rules. Evidence: 190+ handwritten letters and explicit photos developed at a local Walmart. Bardin repeatedly instructed the victim to hide her letters and photos. Colleagues grew suspicious and transferred the victim to a different housing unit. A building-wide security sweep on March 27, 2025 found the letters and photos in his new cell.

Arrested April 2, 2025. Initial charges: first-degree sexual abuse, distribution of obscene material to a minor, and solicitation for murder. Bardin had written in letters about wanting her husband Michael Bardin killed to avoid dividing marital assets and sharing custody of their 5-year-old daughter. Michael Bardin filed for divorce and sought sole custody on May 7, 2025.

Jury trial September 11-12, 2025, Adair Circuit Court. Verdict: guilty on first-degree sexual abuse, unlawful transaction with a minor, and distribution of obscene material; acquitted on solicitation to commit murder. The victim testified the relationship was an "amusing way to kill time" and that he never planned to act on her requests. Defense attacked his credibility, arguing "he was grooming her just as much." Bardin did not testify. Sentenced November 13, 2025 by Judge Samuel Spalding: 4 years (sexual abuse) + 10 years (unlawful transaction) consecutive = 14 years total; 12 months for distribution concurrent. Sex offender registration 20 years.

NOTE: Juvenile detention facility, not a school; victim is a court-ordered incarcerated juvenile. One of the more extreme cases in the DB: solicitation-to-murder charge (acquitted). Bardin wrote in one letter: "I can't be classified as an effing pedophile." Prosecution by Commonwealth's Attorney Brian Wright. Defense: Steve Romines.
