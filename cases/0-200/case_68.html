Alicia Gray #68 sentenced

Former math teacher (algebra, Math 9, honors pre-calculus) at Mary G. Montgomery High School, Semmes, Mobile County, Alabama. Had taught at the school for at least six years and was a graduate herself — she taught at her own alma mater. Age 28 at sentencing.

Victim: a 14-year-old student. Surrendered to the Mobile County Sheriff’s Office on February 25, 2013. Charged with second-degree sodomy, second-degree sexual abuse, and school employee sexual contact with a student under 19. Placed on paid administrative leave; resigned several months before the plea.

Pled guilty January 10, 2014 before Judge Michael Youngpeter. Sentence: 5-year split sentence with 6 months served plus 5 years probation; no employment with minors; surrender of teaching certificate; lifetime sex offender registration. Prosecutor Nicki Patterson: ‘A win-win for everyone… the primary consideration here was keeping her away from children.’ At the plea, Gray admitted she had harmed ‘the victim and others’ — suggesting additional individuals, though detectives interviewed former students and could not uncover others with improper contact. Superintendent Martha Peek: ‘When the allegations came out, we acted quickly.’

NOTE: The victim was 14 — below Alabama’s 16 age-of-consent threshold, making the school-employee statute constitutional challenge (raised in Parker #63 and Witt #65) inapplicable here; the more serious sodomy charge makes age-of-consent preemption arguments moot. The ‘harmed the victim and others’ language in Gray’s admission is unresolved — either additional informal victims who did not come forward, or deliberate ambiguity in the plea language. Alabama pattern: 6 months actual time for sex with a 14-year-old, including a sodomy charge.
