Westlake, Louisiana woman who met a 13-year-old male victim at a summer Bible school camp. Age 29 at time of offense. Not a school employee — access occurred through the faith-setting camp rather than institutional employment.

Multiple incidents at multiple locations across two parishes. Daughdrill sometimes picked the victim up after school without his parents' knowledge or permission. Two-parish prosecution: Calcasieu and Beauregard. Original Calcasieu charges included four counts of carnal knowledge of a juvenile, eight counts of sexual battery, three counts of second-degree kidnapping, one count of contributing to the delinquency of a juvenile, and one count of indecent behavior with a juvenile. Beauregard charges: one count of felony carnal knowledge of a juvenile plus three counts of second-degree kidnapping.

September 2012: pled guilty in Beauregard Parish (36th JDC) to felony carnal knowledge — five years LDOC, four suspended, five years supervised probation. October 9, 2012: pled guilty in Calcasieu Parish (14th JDC, Division H) to one count of felony carnal knowledge — five years LDOC, four suspended, concurrent with the Beauregard sentence; five years supervised probation; no contact with the victim or his family; no contact with minors under 17; SORA registration; restitution to victim's family. Effective custodial time: one year. ADA Cynthia Guillory (Calcasieu); defense attorney Fred Lebleu. Judge Ron Ware stated at sentencing: "You need to know you are getting a very lenient sentence. This affair was not a one-time thing and it happened at multiple locations."

NOTE: EDGE CASE — not a school employee; access was through a summer Bible school camp, the faith-setting analog to the school-employment pattern across the rest of the DB. The kidnapping charges document deliberate unsupervised access: picking up a 13-year-old from school without parental permission establishes premeditation. Judge Ware's explicit leniency acknowledgment is one of only two such judicial self-aware statements in this DB (cf. Kellenberger #53). Louisiana pattern (cf.
