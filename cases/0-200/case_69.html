StateLouisianaCourt—
Incident Date2011-06-01
Defendant Age29 Victim Age13
Prison Sentence1.0 yr

EDGE CASE — not a school employee. Westlake LA woman who met 13-year-old male victim at a summer Bible school camp. Multiple incidents at multiple locations; Daughdrill sometimes picked the victim up after school without his parents' knowledge or permission. Two-parish prosecution: Calcasieu and Beauregard. Calcasieu original charges: 4x carnal knowledge of a juvenile; 8x sexual battery; 3x second-degree kidnapping; 1x contributing to delinquency of a juvenile; 1x indecent behavior with a juvenile. Beauregard original charges: felony carnal knowledge of a juvenile + 3x second-degree kidnapping. September 2012: pled guilty in Beauregard (36th JDC) to felony carnal knowledge — 5 years LDOC, 4 suspended, 5 years supervised probation. October 9, 2012: pled guilty in Calcasieu (14th JDC, Division H) to one count felony carnal knowledge — 5 years LDOC, 4 suspended, concurrent with Beauregard sentence; 5 years supervised probation; no contact with victim or his family; no contact with minors under 17; SORA registration; restitution to victim's family. Effective custodial time: 1 year. ADA Cynthia Guillory (Calcasieu). Defense attorney Fred Lebleu. Judge Ron Ware told Daughdrill at sentencing: 'You need to know you are getting a very lenient sentence. This affair was not a one-time thing and it happened at multiple locations. It doesn't only affect the victim but his family and your family.' NOTE: The kidnapping charges (picking up a 13-year-old from school without parental permission) document deliberate unsupervised access and premeditation. Judge Ware's explicit leniency acknowledgment is one of only two judicial self-aware leniency statements in this DB (cf. Kellenberger #53 where victim asked for 3 years). The Bible school camp as access point is the faith-setting analog to the school settings across the rest of the DB. Louisiana pattern (cf. Dufresne #59): lenient state-court outcomes with judicial acknowledgment of that leniency.
