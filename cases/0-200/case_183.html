Former choir teacher at Oldham County High School, La Grange, Oldham County, Kentucky. Victim was an underage student (sex unknown; age not stated in sources; under 18). Sexual contact occurred after school hours on school property between April and June 2018 — eight instances of intercourse by Reed's own admission to police at the time of her June 2018 arrest.

Age approximately 36 at time of offense (2018); age 40 at sentencing (June 2022). Arrested June 2018. Originally charged with 30 offenses: 15 counts unlawful transaction with a minor, 10 counts third-degree rape, and 5 counts third-degree sodomy.

Pleaded guilty in March 2022 to one count of third-degree rape and one count of first-degree unlawful illicit sex acts with a minor under 18. All remaining 28 charges dismissed. Sentenced June 27, 2022 to five years in prison, not eligible for probation. Required to complete sex offender treatment. Sex offender registration for life.
