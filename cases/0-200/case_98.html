Nicole Renee Wooten #98 sentenced

StateArizonaCourtMaricopa County Superior Court
JudgeSherry StephensIncident Date2006-04-01

Prison Sentence20.0 yrs

Former physical education teacher at Herrera Elementary School, Phoenix, Arizona. Age 28 at time of offense. Female victim, age 14. Sexual conduct with the victim occurred between April and October 2006, during a period when the victim was transitioning from middle to high school.

The victim kept the abuse secret for more than six years, reporting to police in November 2012. Wooten was arrested February 2013. Pleaded guilty in 2014. Sentenced January 6, 2015: 20 years in prison plus lifetime probation — nearly nine years after the offense.

At sentencing the victim testified that Wooten "robbed my childhood away" and described Wooten as a former role model. Prosecutor Ryan Powell stated Wooten had turned the victim's vulnerable transition period "into a way for her to get her own sexual gratification from a child." Wooten did not speak at sentencing and submitted a written letter to the court. Defense argued for a mitigated sentence based on acceptance of responsibility, educational accomplishments, lack of criminal history, and an abusive childhood.

NOTE: Six-plus-year delayed disclosure is among the longer in the DB. Sentence of 20 years was imposed nearly a decade after the offense — relatively rare sentencing timeline. Female-on-female case.
