Former licensed social worker at the National Youth Advocate Program (NYAP), Columbus, Ohio. Age 24 at time of offense (September 2023). Resident of Mount Sterling, Ohio.

Victim: 13-year-old male client assigned to Shires for counseling services. Multiple sexual encounters occurred in September 2023 at various locations across Columbus. The encounters were captured on video on the victim's phone.

Discovered in October 2023 when the victim's mother found messages on her son's phone, including a message from Shires asking whether his mother "had seen the videos or messages." The phone was turned over to police. Detectives found video of Shires and the teen engaging in sex acts. Shires admitted to having sex with the teen on a three-way call between herself, the victim's mother, and detectives.

In November 2023, Shires was arrested a second time after appearing at the victim's home with a firearm. Police found her sitting on the front porch with the gun to her head and her finger on the trigger, yelling that she wanted to kill herself and the victim's mother. This conduct formed the basis for the inducing panic and intimidation charges.

Pleaded guilty May 2024 to four counts of unlawful sexual misconduct with a minor, one count of inducing panic with a firearm specification, and one count of intimidation of a victim or witness. A second count of intimidation and discharging of a firearm was dropped.

Sentenced to 4 years 9 months (4.75 years) in state prison with 253 days jail credit; Tier 2 sex offender registration for 25 years; 5 years parole after release. Shires apologized at sentencing: "I just hope [the family] can find peace from this, that they can find peace and trust with their social workers in the future."

In November 2023, the victim's mother filed a civil lawsuit against NYAP for negligent hiring, alleging Shires was not competent to perform her duties; seeking at least 0,000 in damages.
