State v. Stacy Schuler #20 sentenced

StateOhioCourtWarren County Common Pleas Court
JudgeRobert PeelerIncident Date2010
Defendant Age33Victim Age17
Prison Sentence4.0 yrs

Physical education teacher and athletic trainer at Mason High School (north of Cincinnati), Ohio, since 2000. Female defendant, age 33 at trial. Five male student victims, most ~age 17, several football players.

Sexual encounters with five students occurred in fall 2010 at her home in Springboro; alcohol was supplied to all five. Multiple incidents included group sex. Schuler resigned in February 2011 after an anonymous tip to school administration.

Four-day bench trial, October 24-27, 2011, before Judge Robert Peeler, Warren County Common Pleas Court. Insanity defense raised and rejected. Convicted on all 16 felony counts of sexual battery and 3 misdemeanor counts of providing alcohol to a minor.

Sentenced October 27, 2011, to 4 years in prison; judge stated she would be eligible to petition for release after 6 months. Granted early judicial release November 13, 2012, after serving approximately 13 months. Released to her parents home, having lost her own. Post-release: 5 years probation; drug, alcohol, and sex offender counseling; lifetime sex offender registration; prohibited from profiting from the case while on probation.

NOTE: One victim spoke at the release hearing and stated Schuler had ruined his life; another had written in support of her release. Prosecutor David Fornshell publicly criticized the early release, arguing the court misapplied the recidivism analysis. 13 months for 16 felony counts across 5 victims amounts to roughly 3 weeks per count. Cross-case note: alcohol-supplied grooming with multiple simultaneous victims is a recurring cluster pattern (cf. Smith #35).
