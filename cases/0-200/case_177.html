Ashley Nicole Anderson #177 sentenced

State Iowa
Court Scott County District Court
Incident Date 2018-09-01
Defendant Age 24
Victim Age 14
Prison Sentence 2.0 yrs

Former teacher at North Scott High School, North Scott Community School District, Eldridge, Scott County, Iowa. Victims were two male high school students, ages 14 and 15. Sexual contact occurred during the 2018–2019 school year. Anderson admitted to the misconduct during a police interview.

Age 24 at arrest (May 31, 2019); age 25 at sentencing (October 2020). Arrested May 31, 2019. Original charges included multiple counts of lascivious acts with a child, solicitation to commit a felony, and assault with intent to commit sexual abuse. Pleaded guilty in August 2020 to one count of sexual exploitation by a school employee. Sentenced October 2020 to 2 years in prison. Sex offender registration required.

Civil lawsuit filed by victims and families against North Scott Community School District for failure to supervise and protect.

NOTE: Charge reduction from multiple serious felonies to single exploitation count. Two male victims; youngest age 14 recorded per DB convention. Separate from case #176 (Ashley Nicole Anderson, Aplington-Parkersburg), a different individual with the same name. Civil district liability claim notable.
