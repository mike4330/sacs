Matarico first met the victim when he was 12 years old. Sexual abuse began in 2023 when the victim was 13 and continued until he was 14 in 2024. According to prosecutors, Matarico groomed the victim with special classroom attention and marijuana, then convinced him to have sexual intercourse in her vehicle. She told the boy "he is the only one she would go to jail for" and described him as her "crystal meth." She allegedly left her husband due to the relationship. After learning of the investigation, she drove to his new school to make contact and sent Instagram messages to the victim and his friends expressing romantic feelings.

Arrested November 7, 2024. Arraigned November 12, 2024; pleaded not guilty. Charged with 5 felony counts of lewd act upon a child under 14 (PC §288(a)), 2 felony counts of lewd act upon a child age 14 or 15 (PC §288(c)(1)), and 1 felony count of furnishing cannabis to a minor — 8 felony counts total.
