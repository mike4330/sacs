Brooke Dinkel #172 sentenced

StateKansasCourtSaline County District Court
Incident Date2012-12-26
Defendant Age32 Victim Age13
Prison Sentence6.8 yrs

Former counselor at Smoky Valley Middle School, Lindsborg, Saline County KS. Also identified as Brooke Dinkel. Age approximately 32 at time of offense; 33 at trial and conviction; approximately 41 at 2022 plea. Hired as counselor for the 2012–13 school year.

Victim K.H. was an 8th grader (age 13) whose aunt was a friend of Dinkel's family and asked her to check on him. K.H. was physically large for his age (6'2', ~170 lbs). Dinkel began counseling K.H. and rapidly escalated contact: repeatedly pulling him from class without cause, creating a boys' club to include him over teacher objections, sneaking food and drinks to him during detention, having him work at her home (wallpaper removal, painting), playing Xbox with him, buying meals together, and spending the night in a hotel room she rented. On multiple occasions faculty found K.H. in her locked office with paper covering the window. The principal warned her directly; other staff warned her she was crossing professional lines. She continued and enlisted a new teacher unaware of the warnings to help facilitate access. No faculty member ever observed Dinkel express fear of K.H.

Relationship turned sexual November/December 2012. Dinkel's own testimony placed first intercourse December 26, 2012. Throughout the relationship she bragged to friends about her 'young boyfriend,' claiming he was 19, called him 'the absolute best sex ever,' and described sexual encounters in graphic detail. She showed friends photos of them kissing and wearing each other's clothing; texted from a friend's phone 'this is ur gf bitch—love u.' She bought K.H. clothing, an iPad, Airsoft games, cash, and a credit card. K.H.'s aunt warned Dinkel to stay away or she would take action.

Discovery: March 9, 2013 — husband Jayson Dinkel found a love note addressed to K.H. under the couch. March 10, 2013 — Jayson came home and found K.H. on the couch with pants and underwear pulled to his ankles; Dinkel came downstairs and asked why he was home early. She later laughed with a friend about Jayson catching them. March 26, 2013 — Dinkel surreptitiously delivered pizza to K.H. in in-school suspension; when the principal investigated, Dinkel was standing outside the door. K.H. was expelled. That evening, K.H.'s aunt told the principal her concerns. March 27, 2013 — K.H. confirmed sexual acts in a meeting with school officials. On the same day, Dinkel texted K.H. warning that the school would try to turn them against each other and asking him to contact her afterward because she was 'freaking out'; she told him 'I know you love me and aren't going to say anything.' She texted friends that she had gotten into trouble but thought it would 'blow over because the police had not contacted her yet.'

Originally charged with rape and aggravated criminal sodomy. Jury convicted June 2014 on two counts of rape of a child under 14. Sentenced December 4, 2014 to 165 months (~13.75 years) per count, concurrent; sex offender registration imposed.

Kansas Court of Appeals affirmed March 23, 2018. Kansas Supreme Court vacated September 24, 2021 (State v. Dinkel, No. 113,705): trial counsel ineffective for failing to request a 'voluntary act' jury instruction, given defense claim that K.H. raped and blackmailed Dinkel. Released pending retrial. Pleaded no contest January 26, 2022 to contributing to child misconduct; no additional prison.

NOTE: sentence_years set to 6.8 (imputed time served December 4, 2014 – September 24, 2021 ≈ 6 years 9 months). Prior warnings from principal and multiple staff members documented and ignored — one of the clearest prior-warning-ignored patterns in the DB. Victim-blaming defense (13yo raped and blackmailed the adult counselor) directly contradicted by Dinkel's own texts, bragging, and demeanor evidence. Daylight Saving Time was the proximate cause of the husband's early arrival that led to discovery.
