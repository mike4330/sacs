Former counselor at Smoky Valley Middle School, Lindsborg, Saline County KS. Also known as Brooke Dinkel. Hired for the 2012–13 school year. Age ~32 at offense; 33 at trial; ~41 at 2022 plea.

Victim K.H. was an 8th grader (age 13) whose aunt asked Dinkel to check on him. Grooming escalated rapidly: repeatedly pulling him from class without cause, creating a boys' club to include him, sneaking food during detention, having him work at her home, playing Xbox, and spending the night together in a hotel. Faculty found K.H. in her locked office with paper covering the window on multiple occasions. The principal warned her directly; other staff warned her she was crossing professional lines. She continued and enlisted a new teacher to facilitate access.

Relationship turned sexual November/December 2012; Dinkel's own testimony placed first intercourse December 26, 2012. She bragged to friends about her "young boyfriend," called him "the absolute best sex ever," showed photos of them kissing, and texted from a friend's phone "this is ur gf bitch—love u." She bought K.H. clothing, an iPad, Airsoft games, cash, and a credit card.

Discovery sequence, March 2013: on the 9th, husband Jayson found a love note addressed to K.H.; on the 10th, he came home early (DST) and found K.H. on the couch with pants pulled down — Dinkel asked why he was home early, later laughed about it with a friend. On the 26th, Dinkel was caught delivering pizza to K.H. in in-school suspension; K.H. was expelled. That evening his aunt reported her concerns to the principal. On the 27th, K.H. confirmed sexual acts to school officials; Dinkel simultaneously texted him to stay quiet: "I know you love me and aren't going to say anything."

Originally charged with rape and aggravated criminal sodomy. Jury convicted June 2014 on two counts of rape of a child under 14. Sentenced December 4, 2014 to 165 months (~13.75 years) per count, concurrent; sex offender registration imposed.

Kansas Court of Appeals affirmed March 23, 2018. Kansas Supreme Court vacated September 24, 2021 (State v. Dinkel, No. 113,705): trial counsel ineffective for failing to request a voluntary-act instruction, given defense claim that K.H. raped and blackmailed Dinkel. Released pending retrial. Pleaded no contest January 26, 2022 to contributing to child misconduct; no additional prison.

NOTE: sentence_years=6.8 (imputed time served Dec 4, 2014 – Sep 24, 2021 ≈ 6 yr 9 mo). One of the clearest prior-warning-ignored patterns in the DB — principal and multiple staff warned her directly. Victim-blaming defense (13yo raped and blackmailed the adult counselor) directly contradicted by Dinkel's own texts and demeanor evidence.
