Culinary arts teacher at Plymouth High School, Plymouth, WI (Sheboygan County school; charged in Fond du Lac County). Resident of St. Cloud, WI. Age 41–42 at time of offense (43 at not-guilty plea December 2025).

In 2023, Zimmerman developed a relationship with a 16-year-old female student during a period when the victim was experiencing mental health struggles. They texted and talked at school and outside of school. The relationship turned sexual first through phone conversations, then escalated to in-person sexual contact in subsequent months. Zimmerman sent sexually charged texts and social media messages, then explicit photos and videos. The victim eventually spent nights at Zimmerman's home.

The victim's mother grew suspicious and reported concerns to the school district. The district kept Zimmerman employed and issued a no-contact order — which Zimmerman violated continuously. When the victim tried to end the relationship, Zimmerman made suicidal threats and threatened to make the relationship public to ruin both their reputations. After the victim told Zimmerman to never contact her again, Zimmerman began showing up at the victim's workplace. The victim came forward to police in July 2025.

Arrested August 25, 2025 (Fond du Lac County Sheriff, case 25SO-2170/25CF000779); bond set at $250,000 cash. Initially charged with 3 counts (September 2, 2025 formal charging): sexual assault of a student by school staff (x1), sexual intercourse with a child 16 or older (x1), exposing child to harmful material (x1). By not-guilty plea December 4, 2025, charges had expanded to 5 counts including 2 counts sexual assault of a student by school staff. Status conference January 6, 2026; no trial date set.
