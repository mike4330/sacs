Paraprofessional, teaching assistant, and former school nurse at Brady Exploration School in Lakewood, CO (Jefferson County Public Schools). Worked for JeffCo from 2015 to 2023 in various roles; at Brady from August to November 2023. Age 26–27 at time of offense (28 at sentencing February 2025).

In September 2023, Ewer began an inappropriate relationship with a 16-year-old male student with an undisclosed disability. The investigation was triggered in November 2023 when staff found inappropriate writing on the victim's worksheet; investigators also noted that staff had observed Ewer showing "favoritism" toward the student prior to the discovery. Evidence showed frequent sexual encounters, constant digital communication (thousands of text messages containing repeated derogatory remarks directed at the victim), and the introduction of fentanyl, alcohol, and weapons. Ewer directed the victim to bring a gun to their sexual encounters and instructed him "to bring the gun to school and shoot another faculty member in the leg." She also exploited the victim for money and manipulated him into believing she was emotionally invested.

Pleaded guilty in November 2024 to contributing to the delinquency of a minor in the first degree and sexual assault on a child by one in a position of trust. Sentenced January 31, 2025 by Judge Chantel Contiguglia: 4 years in prison followed by 10 years to life on Sex Offender Intensive Supervision (SOIS) probation. Prosecutor: Brynn Chase (special victims, First Judicial District DA's Office). While on bond in this case, Ewer was arrested for a drug offense in Douglas County.

Victim's mother at sentencing: "The defendant preyed on my son, leading him to believe she genuinely cared for him, all while exploiting him for money and manipulating him into thinking he was 'the one.'"

NOTE: Special-needs victim; disability not disclosed. Ewer's January 2024 arrest was described as the first of five similar educator misconduct cases in Jefferson County within the same year (others include Chloe Castro, JeffCo social worker, arrested November 2024 on similar charges — potential cross-reference case). Derogatory messaging pattern toward victim is unusual; most DB cases show affectionate/manipulative communication rather than degrading content. Drug (fentanyl) introduction as a grooming tool is rare in the DB. Prior staff observation of favoritism = prior-warning-ignored.
