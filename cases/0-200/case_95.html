Former teacher at Middle Township Elementary School, Middle Township, New Jersey. DOB 1990; age 27 at time of initial abuse (January 2018).

Victim was a male student whose family became friends with Caron. The children began visiting her home one to two nights per week, then in 2016 moved in with her permanently, living in her home through 2020. Caron was identified in court as a 'resource family parent' for the children — exploiting both her teacher role and her informal guardian role. The victim was 11 when the abuse began (~2018) and 13 when Caron, then 28, became pregnant. A child was born of the relationship in 2019 (approximately 5 years old as of January 2025).

The case came to light in December 2024 when the victim's father posted on social media noting that Caron's child resembled his son. The victim confirmed the sexual relationship in a police interview on January 3, 2025. Caron was arrested January 15, 2025. Middle Township School District placed her on paid administrative leave immediately upon learning of the allegations. SBI: 138119G.

Grand jury returned a true bill June 17, 2025 (indictment no. 25-06-00331-I). Counts: (1) sexual assault, victim under 13 (2C:14-2B, 2nd deg); (2) endangering welfare of a child — sexual conduct (2C:24-4A(1), 2nd deg); (3) aggravated sexual assault, victim ≥13/supervisory role (2C:14-2A(2)(C), 1st deg). Judge: Jeffrey J. Waldman, Superior Court. Defense: John W. Tumelty. Prosecutor: Dara Paley. Case review August 24, 2026; trial set September 8, 2026.

NOTE: Child born of the relationship constitutes biological evidence. Victim lived in Caron's home approximately four years (2016–2020). Case surfaced via social media paternity identification — unique mechanism in this DB.
