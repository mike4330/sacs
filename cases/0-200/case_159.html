Former paid tutor for athletes at Grand Rapids Catholic Central High School (and Grand Rapids Central High School), Grand Rapids MI. Also identified as Abigail Marie Simon. MDOC #951566. Age 31 at start of offense (April 2013); 33 at trial and conviction; 35 at sentencing. Ongoing sexual relationship with 15yo male student beginning April 26, 2013; multiple instances of sexual conduct. Grooming involved graphic texts, explicit photos, and romantic relationship framing.

Arrested/charged 2013. Tried November 2014; convicted by jury November 26, 2014 on three counts of first-degree criminal sexual conduct and one count of accosting a child for immoral purposes. Conviction upheld by Michigan Court of Appeals June 16, 2016 (COA #326149); further appeals denied. Lifetime sex offender registration required upon release.

Paroled November 22, 2022 after serving 8-year minimum. Fully discharged from parole supervision November 22, 2024 (MDOC OTIS profile).
