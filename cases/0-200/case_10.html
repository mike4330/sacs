Former teacher at Florida Aerospace Academy, Polk County, Florida. Age 30 at time of offense onset (August 2012).

Three male victims, all age 17. Abuse spanned August 2012 through April 2014 — approximately 20 months — with 37 or more incidents. Incidents occurred in multiple locations: her truck, hotels, and on the roof of Florida Aerospace Academy. Investigation began when the mother of one victim discovered text messages between her son and Fichter. Arrested April 22, 2014; simultaneously fired by the Polk County School Board.
