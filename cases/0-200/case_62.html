Former English teacher and club adviser at Citrus Valley High School and Redlands High School, Redlands Unified School District, San Bernardino County, California. Three male victims.

Earliest offense: 2007, victim aged 16 at Redlands High School; Whitehurst was 23. Primary victim (John BB Doe): relationship began summer 2012 when Doe was 16 and continued through June 2013. Whitehurst insisted on sex without birth control and became pregnant by Doe. She told him the pregnancy was "divine intervention," a "miracle baby," and "God's plan." She sent nude photos captioned "good night daddy, I love you so much" and called the school principal to excuse Doe's absences so he could attend prenatal appointments with her. She gave birth June 18, 2013.

Two weeks after the birth, Doe's parents reported to the school. Whitehurst was arrested July 1, 2013. Two additional victims came forward; she was re-arrested July 8. Originally charged with 41 felony counts (maximum 29 years). Pleaded guilty July 31, 2013 to 6 counts (4 unlawful sexual intercourse + 2 oral copulation with a minor under 18). Sentenced August 28, 2013 by Judge Arthur Harrison: 365 days county jail, 5 years probation, lifetime sex offender registration, teaching credential forfeiture, and counseling. Released January 5, 2014, approximately five months served.

Defense attorney James Gass: "She was not charged with doing anything against anyone's will or by force." Prosecutor: Deputy DA Melissa Rodriguez. A civil suit was filed by Doe against Whitehurst, Redlands USD, Citrus Valley High School, and teacher Sarah Seevers, alleging the district knew and helped conceal the abuse. Whitehurst's parents raised the child; Whitehurst sought custody after her release.

NOTE: The religious framing of a pregnancy caused by the sexual abuse of a 16-year-old ("God's plan," "miracle baby") is among the most calculated forms of psychological manipulation documented in this DB — weaponizing whatever spiritual framework the victim held. The 2007 offense predates the 2012-2013 primary relationship by five years, suggesting a persistent predatory pattern across two schools. Forty-one counts reduced to six with 365 days county jail (released at ~5 months) is the most favorable per-count outcome in this DB. The district's alleged concealment and alleged knowledge by co-teacher Sarah Seevers are significant institutional failure claims.
