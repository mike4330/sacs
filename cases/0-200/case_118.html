Former teacher at Bell Street Middle School, Clinton, South Carolina (Laurens County). Age 24 at arrest. Five male victims aged 14 and 15; some were students at Bell Street Middle, others from the broader area.

Sexual encounters with the five boys occurred at the school, a motel, a park, and behind a restaurant. The investigation began after school officials discovered a note believed to have been written by Ward to one of the boys. Ward was fired February 28, 2007.

Indicted March 30, 2007 on five counts of criminal sexual conduct with a minor and six counts of lewd acts on a minor. In September 2007, Ward pleaded guilty to three counts of second-degree criminal sexual conduct with a minor and three counts of lewd acts on a minor. Sentenced February 19, 2008: 15 years on each lewd act count suspended to 6 years, and 6 years on each criminal sexual conduct count, all sentences to run concurrently. Defense attorney Donald Hocker requested home imprisonment, citing Ward's vulnerability in prison and citing forensic psychiatric testimony that she was not a pedophile but suffered from personality disorders and a repressed childhood. At sentencing, Ward said: 'I apologize from the depths of my heart.' Solicitor Jerry Peace prosecuted.

NOTE: One of three Laurens County teachers charged with sexual crimes against students within 18 months: Wendie Schweikert (elementary school, 11-year-old victim, 10 years) preceded Ward; Karen Robbins (private school at time of offense, 15-year-old boy, charged September 2007) followed. All three prosecuted by Solicitor Jerry Peace. The cluster is notable as a jurisdictional pattern. Incident date is approximate — start of 2006-07 school year.
