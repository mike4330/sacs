Apryl Patterson #117 sentenced

State Ohio
Court Morgan County Common Pleas Court
Incident Date 2017-11-01
Defendant Age 26
Victim Age 17
Prison Sentence 2.0 yrs

Former intervention specialist at Foxfire Schools, McConnelsville, Ohio. Age 26 at time of offense. Male victim aged 17.

Patterson became sexually involved with the student in November 2017 and engaged in sexual activity with him on multiple occasions over approximately five months, through the end of April 2018. Sexual acts occurred at her home in St. Clairsville. She also purchased beer for herself and the student to consume at her apartment before sexual activity. Patterson maintained contact via phone and also had contact with the student during school hours.

The victim told his parents about the relationship; they filed a complaint on April 29, 2018. Patterson was placed on administrative leave the following day with instructions to have no contact with the victim, students, teachers, or school staff, and to stay off school property. She ignored that instruction, attempting to contact the victim through a coworker, and was terminated as a result. Investigators also discovered she had searched the internet for ways to delete Facebook and Snapchat interactions and researched other teacher-student sex cases.

Patterson attempted to represent herself as a sovereign citizen, but the court required her to accept appointed counsel. Prior to her plea hearing, she made comments to coworkers that 'her life was over and that she was going to prison.' She pleaded guilty May 8, 2019 to three third-degree felony counts of sexual battery. Sentenced June 10, 2019: two years in prison (state had recommended six years); Tier III sex offender registration for life; forfeiture of teaching license. Defense attorney Keith Edwards; Assistant Prosecuting Attorney Ron Welch.

NOTE: The internet search for ways to delete Snapchat/Facebook interactions is a documented evidence-tampering effort, unusual in its specificity. The sovereign citizen defense gambit is a rare procedural note in this DB. State sought 6 years; judge imposed 2 — a significant downward departure.
