Taylor Cruze #200 sentenced

StateTennesseeCourtRutherford County Circuit Court
Incident Date2021-08-01
Defendant Age22 Victim Age11
Prison Sentence10.0 yrs

Former 5th grade teacher at John Colemon Elementary School, Smyrna, Rutherford County, Tennessee. First-year teacher during the 2021-2022 school year. Multiple elementary-school-age victims, under age 13. Defendant age 22 at time of offense; 23 at arrest.

Alleged misconduct occurred in August 2021. Cruze posted inappropriate videos of students to TikTok. Conduct also included exploitation of minors by electronic means and solicitation of minors (rape of a child). Suspended without pay May 2, 2022; resigned May 22, 2022. Arrest warrant issued August 3, 2022; arrested August 10, 2022 by Criminal Investigator Marcy Gossett, Smyrna Police Department. Released on $100,000 bond. Charges filed in Rutherford County Circuit Court August 2022 under sealed indictment.

19-count indictment: 5 counts exploitation of a minor by electronic means (under 13); 5 counts exploitation of a minor by electronic means; 5 counts especially aggravated sexual exploitation of a minor; 3 counts solicitation of a minor – rape of a child; 1 count sexual battery by an authority figure.

Pleaded guilty before Judge James Turner; Sharon Reddick prosecuted; defense attorney Will Fraley. Sentenced to 10 years in Tennessee Department of Correction; commenced June 20, 2023; projected release June 20, 2033. Registered sex offender; classified 'Violent Against Children' on Tennessee Sex Offender Registry.

NOTE: TikTok as the initial exposure vector is unusual — public platform rather than direct messaging. First-year teacher; offenses occurred at start of school year. Multiple victims (elementary school age, under 13). Among the higher sentences in this DB for an exploitation case.
