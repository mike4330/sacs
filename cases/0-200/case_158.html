Kathryn Houghtaling #158 sentenced

State Michigan
Court Oakland County Circuit Court
Incident Date 2018-01-01
Defendant Age 25
Victim Age 16
Prison Sentence 4.25 yrs

Former special education teacher at Rochester High School, Rochester Community Schools, Rochester MI. Age 25 at approximate start of offenses; 26 at arrest; 27 at sentencing. Multiple sexual encounters with two male special education students (ages 16 and 17) at various locations including at the students' homes, which she allegedly entered without invitation. Also provided controlled substances to at least one victim.

Arrested January 2019; arraigned January 11, 2019. Pleaded no contest January 2020 to six counts of third-degree criminal sexual conduct and one count of delivery of a controlled substance to a minor (reduced from initial charges; two-victim case). Sentenced March 3, 2020 to 51 months (4 years 3 months) to 15 years in prison for the CSC counts, concurrent with 51 months to 8 years for drug delivery. Sex offender registration required.

Civil lawsuit filed by victims and families against Rochester Community Schools alleging deliberate indifference and failure to supervise.
