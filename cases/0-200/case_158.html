Former special education teacher at Rochester High School, Rochester Community Schools, Rochester, Michigan. Age 25 at approximate start of offenses. Multiple sexual encounters with two male special education students, ages 16 and 17, at various locations including the students' homes. Also provided controlled substances to at least one victim.

Arrested January 2019; arraigned January 11, 2019. Pleaded no contest January 2020 to six counts of third-degree criminal sexual conduct (student) and one count of delivery of a controlled substance to a minor — seven counts total, concurrent, all reduced from initial charges. Sentenced March 3, 2020 to 51 months (4 years 3 months) to 15 years on the CSC counts, concurrent with 51 months to 8 years on the drug count. Sex offender registration required.

Paroled June 4, 2024; assigned to Macomb County supervision; discharge date June 4, 2026.

A civil lawsuit was filed by the victims and families against Rochester Community Schools alleging deliberate indifference and failure to supervise.
