Rebecca L. Kilps #86 sentenced

State Wisconsin
Court Manitowoc County
Judge Robert Dewane
Incident Date 2022
Defendant Age 35
Victim Age 17
Prison Sentence 0.0 yrs

Former Spanish teacher at Two Rivers High School, Two Rivers, Wisconsin (Manitowoc County). Age 35 at sentencing. Victim: 17-year-old male student who met Kilps in her Spanish class.

Relationship began with occasional social media messages that escalated in frequency, became personal, and led to multiple sexual encounters. Discovery: Kilps' husband found suspicious messages on her phone showing the victim and Kilps "talking about a future together and saying they love each other" and called police. Kilps was arrested May 2022. She initially denied any sexual contact, then admitted it, acknowledging she knew it was illegal. Both Kilps and the victim gave separate statements confirming the relationship. She resigned from Two Rivers School District and relinquished her teaching license.

Originally charged with 3 felony counts of sexual assault of a student by school staff. Plea agreement: 2 felony counts reduced to 2 misdemeanor counts of sexual intercourse with a child age 16 or older (no contest). The remaining felony count was placed under a 5-year deferred prosecution agreement — no conviction is entered unless conditions are violated.

Sentenced January 25, 2023 by Judge Robert Dewane: sentence withheld on the misdemeanors; 3 years probation; 3 months jail as a probation condition (6 months stayed); no contact with anyone under 18 except immediate family; psychosexual evaluation; continued mental health treatment. At sentencing, Kilps described the victim as "an innocent bystander during a time in my life of deep sadness."

NOTE: Three felonies reduced to two misdemeanors plus one deferred prosecution is the most aggressive charge reduction in the DB — Kilps faces no felony conviction on record if she completes five years without violation. The "time of deep sadness" framing displaces moral agency onto her own emotional state, mirroring Altice's self-esteem deflection (#81). Husband-reported via phone discovery mirrors Bergmann (#22). No sex offender registration is mentioned in reporting; Wisconsin misdemeanor convictions may not trigger SORA depending on the specific charge.
