Kari L. Buchanan #145 closed

StateIndianaCourtOrange Superior Court
JudgeR. Michael Cloud Incident Date2018-12-01
Defendant Age42 Victim Age16
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former health teacher at Springs Valley High School, French Lick, Indiana. Resident of West Baden Springs. Age 42 at arrest. Investigation began January 15, 2019 when Indiana State Police were contacted by Indiana DCS regarding an inappropriate relationship between Buchanan and a male junior at Springs Valley at the time (age approximately 16–17). Buchanan turned herself in January 25, 2019; the school board held a special meeting the same day and accepted her resignation.

Case 1 (child pornography/dissemination): Originally charged with possession of child pornography and child seduction (Level 6 felonies) in addition to dissemination of matter harmful to a minor.

On June 5, 2019, Buchanan was arrested a second time on charges of obstruction of justice and invasion of privacy after authorities said she attempted to have the victim change his statement to the Orange County Prosecutor's Office. Isral Qualkenbush, 37, of French Lick, was also arrested for aiding Buchanan in communicating with the victim.

June 20, 2019: Buchanan entered guilty pleas in both cases before Judge R. Michael Cloud. Defense attorney: Melissa Campbell.

Case 1 plea: Guilty to Dissemination of Matter Harmful to a Minor (Level 6 felony). Dismissed: possession of child pornography and child seduction. Sentence: 730 days total — 365 days suspended (formal probation), 365 executed minus 32 days credit for time served = 333 days HOME INCARCERATION.

Case 2 plea: Guilty to Invasion of Privacy (Class A misdemeanor). Dismissed: obstruction of justice. Sentence: 365 days, all suspended, formal probation consecutive to Case 1 probation.

Additional conditions: counseling; limited school access (may drop off/pick up children but may not exit vehicle; may attend school sporting events unless victim is participating). Probation administered through Washington County (Orange County's chief probation officer previously worked with Buchanan; change made to avoid appearance of impropriety). Judge Cloud noted that if Buchanan successfully completes home incarceration, the state would not oppose petitioning to convert the Level 6 felony to a Class A misdemeanor.

NOTE: Victim-age inferred as 16–17 (junior in high school); DB records 16. No actual prison time — home incarceration only. Judge Cloud noted that much of the probable cause affidavit information "didn't actually pertain to the single victim in the prosecution's case" and would not be admissible at trial. Investigators heard rumors of sexual relationships between Buchanan and students going back approximately a decade — only one victim in the prosecution. Evidence-tampering: second arrest for attempting to suborn witness. Qualkenbush is a notable co-conspirator in the obstruction.
