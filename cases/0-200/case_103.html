Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former choir director at Wilmington Area High School, New Wilmington, Pennsylvania (Lawrence County). Age 26 at arrest. Female victim; age not specified in available sources.

Ortz's husband, who also worked for the Wilmington School District, discovered a love letter from the student to his wife after returning from a trip to Florida and reported the relationship to the high school principal in April 2022. Police were then contacted by the district. The student had visited Ortz's home on multiple occasions. Ortz and the victim continued to communicate through apps even after both were aware of the investigation. The student's father ultimately took the girl's phone, iPad, and laptop.

Charged May 2022 with institutional sexual assault, unlawful contact with a minor, and criminal use of a communication facility. Pleaded guilty in March 2023 to one count of criminal use of a communication facility, two counts of sexual contact with a student, and one count of unlawful contact with a minor. Sentenced July 6, 2023: five years probation with the first nine months under house arrest with electronic monitoring. Ordered to have no contact with the victim and to surrender her Pennsylvania teaching license.

NOTE: Ortz is the second music teacher at Wilmington Area High School charged with misconduct; Jonathan Priano (male, band teacher) was charged in June 2020 with 20+ counts involving female students — out of scope for this DB but context for district-level failure. Husband-reported; also notable that contact continued post-investigation-aware. The institutional sexual assault charge was not among the final plea counts — possible reduction.
