Christy Lee Smith #105 sentenced

Prison Sentence14.0 yrs

Former teacher at McCaskey High School, Lancaster, Pennsylvania. Age approximately 30 at time of offense. Two victims: a McCaskey sophomore aged approximately 15 in 2008, and a Solanco High School student aged 17. Offenses occurred between 2008 and 2010.

Originally convicted at trial in 2012 and sentenced to 20 to 40 years in prison by Lancaster County Judge Dennis Reinaker; that conviction was overturned on appeal. Retried in March 2014 and again convicted at trial on the McCaskey abuse charges. At the May 2014 sentencing hearing, Smith additionally pleaded guilty to sex abuse of the Solanco student. Judge Reinaker sentenced her to 14 to 30 years. Defense attorney Janice Longer requested a 10-year minimum; Reinaker declined, citing Smith's refusal to accept responsibility across two trials. When asked for comment or apology at sentencing, Smith responded only 'No.' Reinaker called her trial testimony 'laughable' and 'obviously such a lie.'

In August 2017, Smith received a resentencing after mandatory minimum sentences in such cases were ruled unconstitutional. Reinaker ordered 14 to 28 years — no reduction from the 14-year minimum. Smith appeared via video conference and offered no remorse.

NOTE: Three sentences in a single case: 20–40 years (2012, overturned on appeal), 14–30 years (May 2014, mandatory minimum later struck), 14–28 years (August 2017 resentencing). The 14-year minimum survived all three proceedings. Defendant age at offense is inferred from age 36 at 2014 sentencing. Victim 1 age inferred from 'sophomore in 2008.'
