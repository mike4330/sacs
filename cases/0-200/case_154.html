Allyson Moran #154 sentenced

State Michigan
Court Ingham County Circuit Court
Incident Date 2017-04-01
Defendant Age 26
Victim Age 15
Prison Sentence 2.08 yrs

Former long-term substitute chemistry teacher and girls soccer coach at Stockbridge High School, Stockbridge Community Schools, Stockbridge MI. Also identified as Allyson Brittany Moran. Age 26 at time of offense; 27 at sentencing. Married at time of offenses. Sexual assaults of 15yo male sophomore student (at least two incidents) at her home in Dansville MI in April 2017. Grooming included inviting student to her home and greeting him in an open robe with bra and underwear. A second former student reported a non-sexual but similarly inappropriate relationship.

Arrested approximately mid-September 2017. Pleaded guilty October 25, 2018 to one count of third-degree criminal sexual conduct (reduced from multiple original assault counts). At sentencing December 5, 2018, Moran initially attempted to shift blame to the student; judge and prosecutor explicitly criticized her for victim-blaming. Sentenced to 25 months to 15 years in prison (minimum 25 months before parole eligibility; maximum 15 years). Required to register as sex offender.

NOTE: sentence_years set to 2.08 (minimum 25 months before parole eligibility under Michigan indeterminate sentencing). Offenses occurred entirely off campus (defendant's home). Victim-blaming conduct at sentencing documented by multiple outlets. Second student's non-sexual relationship not charged but noted in reporting.
