Former special education teacher at Thunder Bay Junior High School, Alpena Public Schools, Alpena MI. Also identified as Heather Ellen Winfield. Age approximately 35 at start of offenses (2016); 38 at arrest; 41 at trial and sentencing. Alleged repeated sexual assaults of 11yo male student beginning in 2016 (victim age 11, prepubescent) through July 2018 (victim then 13–14). Victim reported over 100 instances of assault; conduct alleged at school and off-campus locations. Winfield served as the victim's special education teacher during this period.

Arrested January 11, 2019. Preliminary exam March 1, 2019 before Judge Thomas LaCross; bound over for trial on sufficient evidence finding. Trial commenced September 2021 (10-day trial). Jury deliberated approximately 16–17 hours before returning verdict September 23, 2021: acquitted on five counts of criminal sexual conduct (first, second, and third degree); guilty on one count of accosting a child for immoral purposes. Sentenced November 12, 2021 to 9 months in jail; required to register as sex offender for 25 years. Winfield maintained innocence throughout; defense alleged victim coercion or false accusations.

Civil lawsuit filed 2023 by victim (now adult) against Alpena Public Schools for failure to protect.
