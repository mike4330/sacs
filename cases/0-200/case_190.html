Former high school teacher at London High School, London Independent School District, Nueces County, Texas (employed since 2015). Victim was a male student (exact age not publicly specified). London ISD police initiated investigation in March 2021; Nueces County Sheriff's Office was called in. On March 29, 2021, Prince admitted to the improper relationship during questioning at her residence and was arrested. District placed her on administrative leave immediately and she subsequently resigned. Reported to State Board for Educator Certification and Texas Child Protective Services.

Age 40 at arrest (March 2021); age 41 at plea (February 2022); age 45 at final jail booking (February 2026). Charged with 10 counts of improper relationship between educator and student (Tex. Penal Code § 21.12, second-degree felony, punishable by 2–20 years).

Pleaded guilty in February 2022 to all 10 counts. Sentenced to 5 years deferred adjudication probation with a staggered 60-day jail component (12 days per year × 5 years). Conditions: surrender teaching certificate; stay away from victim; 120 hours community service; $1,500 fine; TAIP evaluation; felony victim impact panel; mental health evaluation; counseling; internet monitoring; zero-tolerance placement; standard probation conditions. Prince voluntarily surrendered her Texas teaching certificate and was added to the Do Not Hire Registry in March 2022.

Original judge was 214th District Judge Inna Klein. Klein was recused April 1, 2022 following a defense motion filed March 2, 2022 citing impartiality concerns; Senior Judge Rogelio Valdez ordered the recusal after a March 31 hearing. Case reassigned through Judge Missy Medary (5th Administrative Judicial Region).

On May 26, 2023, Prince was rearrested for probation violations: failure to report to CSCD (February 7), disrespect toward probation officer, non-payment of fines/court costs, curfew violation, failure to enroll in felony victim impact panel, failure to complete community service, and failure to comply with internet monitoring. CSCD recommended revocation; Judge Bobby Galvan imposed sanctions instead: 24-day jail sanction with time credit and 90-day GPS monitoring.

On February 24, 2026, Prince was booked into Nueces County Jail to serve the final 12-day installment of her agreed sentence.

NOTE: Some sources (including Naughty Teacher List) incorrectly attribute this case to Grand Prairie High School; all verified records confirm London ISD, Nueces County. Deferred adjudication means if probation conditions are met, conviction is removed from record. sentence_years = 0 (staggered 12-day annual jail segments are non-continuous per convention).
