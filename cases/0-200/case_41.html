Former teacher at Holly Hill Academy, a private school in Orangeburg County, South Carolina. Age 28 at time of offense.

The victim was a 16- or 17-year-old male student. Anderson sent nude photos of herself to the victim to lure him into a sexual relationship; the documented incidents occurred in October 2023 at a Pizza Hut in Santee and at a residence on Bunch Ford Road. Anderson was fired after the school head notified law enforcement following student disclosures. The victim's mother stated publicly that her son was not the only victim, suggesting additional unreported victims.

Arrested November 15, 2023 on two counts of sexual battery of a 16- or 17-year-old. The case was filed in the Orangeburg County Court of General Sessions on November 27, 2023 (Case No. 2023A3810701075) and indicted in 2024 (Indictment No. 2024GS3800088). Judge Valerie J. Lawrence was assigned. On April 10, 2025, before Disposition Judge Charles J. McCutchen, Anderson pleaded guilty to a reduced charge of second-degree assault and battery.

Sentenced to a 3-year South Carolina Department of Corrections term, suspended after one day of time served, plus five years of probation, sex offender counseling, and a permanent restraining order from the victim and family. Anderson was placed on the Central Registry of Child Abuse and Neglect. Sex offender registration applies only if she violates probation, in which case the unsuspended balance of the SCDC term and registration would attach.

NOTE: An unusually favorable disposition for the conduct described — the original two-count sexual battery charge was reduced to a single second-degree assault and battery charge, and the 3-year SCDC term was almost entirely suspended. CRCAN placement is a substitute civil consequence for sex offender registration in cases where the conduct fits the abuse-by-position-of-trust framework. The "not the only one" claim from the victim's mother was not pursued in the charging document. The penetrative-contact coding (3) reflects the conduct described in the original sexual-battery charge rather than the reduced assault-and-battery plea label, which understates the underlying act.
