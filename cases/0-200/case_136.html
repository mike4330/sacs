5th grade teacher at Woodsdale Elementary School, Abington, MA. Resident of Rockland, MA. Age 30 at arrest (January 2009); ~33 at sentencing March 2012. Husband: Scott McCallum.

McCallum was tutoring the victim's older brother when she began sexually abusing the younger boy at age 13. The abuse continued for approximately two to three years (sources differ: sentencing coverage states "nearly two years"; 2009 charging documents allege "more than 300 times over a three-year period") and took place in both Rockland and Abington. Conduct included rape, drugging the victim for sex, and providing alcohol to a minor. The abuse was discovered by the boy's father in early 2009.

Arrested January 8, 2009; fired from Woodsdale Elementary shortly after. Originally arraigned in Brockton District Court (Abington charges) and Hingham District Court (Rockland charges); indicted March 10, 2009 and transferred to Plymouth Superior Court. Ordered to stay away from victim, his family, and all children under 16; required to wear GPS tracking device. Released on $1,000 bail initially; pleaded not guilty at arraignment.

Pleaded guilty March 27, 2012 in Brockton Superior Court to 12 counts of child rape, 1 count of drugging a person for sex, and 1 count of providing alcohol to a minor. Sentenced to just under 2.5 years in prison (sentence_years=2.4) plus 10 years probation. Prosecutors: ADA Susan McDonough (charging phase). Defense: Kevin Mahoney.

Victim impact statement in court: McCallum "destroyed his life." Father's statement (read by prosecutor): called McCallum "a true predator and monster."

NOTE: 12 counts child rape is one of the heaviest charge counts in the DB; sentence of ~2.4 years against that charge volume is remarkably light. Dating-parent-for-access dynamic in reverse — McCallum accessed the victim through tutoring his older brother. Drugging charge (separate count) is one of only a handful in the DB. Charges span two jurisdictions (Rockland and Abington) requiring dual arraignments before superior court consolidation.
