Prosecution by Monmouth County Prosecutor's office (offense location controls jurisdiction, not school location). Arrested April 8, 2024; charged with sexual assault and endangering the welfare of a child. Defense attorney: Edward A. Cridge (private). Prosecutor: Keri C. Schaefer (Monmouth County). Plea to accusation (pre-indictment NJ plea mechanism — faster resolution, avoids grand jury) July 28, 2025 to 2C:14-2C(3)(b) — Sexual Assault by Supervisor, Victim 16–17 — second degree. Sentenced November 14, 2025 by Judge Jill G. O'Malley: 5 years incarceration (9 days jail credit); Parole Supervision for Life (PSL); standard sex offense fines (SCVT $1,000, SANE $800, VCCA $100, etc.). Original endangering-welfare-of-a-child complaint (2C:24-4A(1)) closed to accusation. No stated parole ineligibility period in record — NERA (85% rule, N.J.S.A. 2C:43-7.2) applicability unclear from record; if applied, minimum served ~4.25 years. NOTE: Accusation plea (vs. indictment) in NJ is a pre-trial resolution mechanism in which the defendant waives grand jury presentation — typical in cases with strong evidence where both sides want a negotiated outcome. NJ second-degree sexual assault carries 5–10 year range; 5-year floor suggests plea-negotiated minimum. Parole Supervision for Life is mandatory for second-degree sex offenses in NJ — permanent supervision condition regardless of sentence served. Off-campus wildlife area as meeting location is unusual; documents deliberate selection of a remote venue away from school and residential areas.
