Former teacher at Oakside Scholars Charter School (also reported as Oakside Prep Academy in early coverage), a charter campus in Waterford Township, Michigan. Age 26 at the time of the offense; 27 at sentencing. Male victim, age 16, a former student she was tutoring.

Sexual contact occurred in July 2023 at the victim's home in Pontiac while Sanroman was tutoring him. According to statements at sentencing, she recorded the sexual encounters. Sanroman disclosed the relationship to a fellow teacher, who reported it to charter school officials and triggered the police investigation. Charges were filed in June 2025 — approximately two years after the incidents. Oakland County Prosecutor Karen McDonald charged Sanroman with three counts of third-degree criminal sexual conduct (max 15 years per count under Michigan law).

Sanroman entered a guilty plea to all three counts before Oakland County Circuit Court Judge Cheryl Matthews on March 3, 2026. On April 28, 2026, Judge Matthews sentenced her to 4 to 15 years in prison and lifetime placement on the Michigan Sex Offender Registry. The defense attributed the conduct to unaddressed mental health issues. Asked at sentencing what she was thinking, Sanroman replied, 'Not my best thinking.' The judge characterized the conduct as 'disgusting.'

The victim's mother submitted a written impact statement read by the prosecutor describing her son's withdrawal from public schooling for online instruction and the family's ongoing fear and anxiety.

NOTE: Recording of sexual encounters with a minor — independently a CSAM exposure even where not separately charged. Case originated from the defendant's own self-disclosure to a colleague who reported the conduct; Prosecutor McDonald publicly praised that decision.
