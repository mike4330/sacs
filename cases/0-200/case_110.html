Former teacher at Oakside Prep Academy, a charter school in Waterford Township, Michigan. Age 26 at time of charges; 27 at guilty plea. Male victim aged 16.

Sexual contact occurred in July 2023 while Sanroman was tutoring the student in Pontiac. Sanroman allegedly disclosed the relationship to a fellow teacher, who then reported it to police. Charges were filed in June 2025 — approximately two years after the incidents. Oakland County Prosecutor Karen McDonald charged Sanroman with three counts of third-degree criminal sexual conduct (max 15 years per count under Michigan law).

Sanroman entered a guilty plea to all three counts before Oakland County Circuit Court Judge Cheryl Matthews on March 3, 2026. Sentencing scheduled April 28, 2026.
