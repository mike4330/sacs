Stacy Michelle Walker #54 sentenced

Former drama and theater tech teacher at Mount Carmel High School (Poway Unified School District, San Diego, California). Former student at the same school. Age 33 at time of offense.

Groomed a female student beginning when the victim was 15. Walker reportedly told the victim she had fallen in love with her the moment she first saw her in her office. The relationship continued until 2020 when the victim turned 18. Conduct during the 2018–2019 school year included special outings (restaurant and show trips), escalating sexual conversation, sexting via encrypted messaging apps, and sexual acts.

Victim reported in July 2023. Arrested March 2024; originally charged with 17 felonies. Pled guilty July 2024 to three sex crime counts; plea included an admission of abusing a position of trust or confidence. Sentenced August 14, 2024 to three years state prison plus lifetime sex offender registration.

The victim, then 22, delivered a detailed impact statement describing the grooming process, PTSD, depression, anxiety, and panic attacks. She described being forced to live a double life and lying to protect Walker, and said her heart was breaking for Walker rather than for herself at the time of reporting.

NOTE: Classic long-arc grooming case with encrypted-app sexting and controlling behavior. The victim's impact statement is among the most articulate in this DB on the mechanics of grooming psychology — the guilt-inversion dynamic (victim protecting abuser) is well documented here. Three-year sentence on 17 original felony counts reduced to 3 at plea is notably lenient.
