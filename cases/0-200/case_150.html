Former special education teacher and girls JV lacrosse coach at Sycamore High School, Sycamore Township, Hamilton County OH. Age 26 at time of offense; 27 at sentencing. Sexual relationship with 17yo female student during summer 2018 (May–August); victim was not under her direct supervision during the summer break period. Victim turned 18 by time of sentencing.

Originally indicted October 29, 2018 on two counts of sexual battery. Charge reduced to gross sexual imposition at initial plea, then further amended to interference with custody (a non-sex offense) at final plea in June 2019. Sentenced July 12, 2019 to one year community control (probation) and $100 fine; no prison. Judge noted no sexual predator finding. Judge advised that probation violation could result in up to one year prison.

Notable: victim delivered courtroom statement professing love for Walsh at sentencing. High-profile Cincinnati-area case; significant media attention due to charge reductions and probation-only outcome.
