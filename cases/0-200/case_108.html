Former middle school math and science teacher at Bakersfield School District, Missouri (2022–23 school year). Age 30 at arrest. Male victim aged 16.

Fowler began communicating with the victim via Snapchat and Messenger in early 2023 while he was her student; the relationship became 'flirty' by March. Fowler admitted sending the first nude photo; multiple images were exchanged. Physical contact occurred at a secluded property in Howell County — reached by meeting on a dirt road off the highway — and at Fowler's home. Fowler reported three sexual meetings; the victim reported approximately 15 between May and August 2023.

Investigation began August 30, 2023 when a parent provided screenshots of messages to an Ozark County sheriff's deputy. Fowler's smartphone was seized and she admitted to the relationship. Arrested and held 24 hours in Ozark County Jail August 31; released because the physical acts occurred in Howell County (separate jurisdiction). Case referred to Howell County Prosecutor; grand jury indicted September 13, 2023 on three counts of statutory rape and one count of statutory sodomy (max 7 years each). Fowler had transferred to the Lutie School District for the 2023–24 school year; terminated immediately when allegations became known. Pleaded guilty March 9, 2024 in Howell County Court to two counts of sexual contact with a student; statutory rape and sodomy charges dismissed. Sentenced March 15, 2024: suspended sentence, five years supervised probation, permanent surrender of teaching license, no contact with victim or school students, no unsupervised contact with minors under 18 (except own children), sex offender registration.

NOTE: Significant discrepancy in reported meeting frequency — Fowler said 3 times, victim said approximately 15. Parent discovered the relationship via screenshots rather than any school reporting mechanism. Fowler passed background check and had 'significant teaching experience and excellent references' per Lutie district statement. Charge reduction from statutory rape/sodomy to sexual contact with a student is substantial.
