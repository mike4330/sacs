State v. Ana D'Ettorre #3 sentenced

StateOhioCourtButler County Common Pleas Court
Incident Date2022-05-21
Defendant Age20 Victim Age14
Prison Sentence0.5 yrs

Former student teacher in the Lakota Local School District, Liberty Township, Butler County, Ohio. Grooming began while D'Ettorre was student teaching at Liberty Junior High, where she befriended a 14-year-old male 8th grader. Age 20 at time of offense.

After D'Ettorre left the district, she engaged in sexual conduct with the boy. She picked him up from his father's house in the middle of the night and drove to Monroe Park, where the offenses occurred. The crimes occurred between May 21 and August 30, 2022, while the victim was between 13 and 16 per the indictment (victim age at arrest was 14; broader indictment range reflects the charging period).

D'Ettorre pleaded guilty in February 2026 to unlawful sexual conduct with a minor (4th-degree felony) and disseminating matter harmful to juveniles (1st-degree misdemeanor); remaining charges were dismissed. Sentenced March 4, 2026 to 6 months in the Monday program at River City Corrections and Tier 2 sex offender registration for 25 years. She apologized in court.

NOTE: The victim's mother stated at sentencing that she believed D'Ettorre was remorseful and did not consider her a bad person, but said the offense would affect her son for the rest of his life. Grooming began in the school context but sexual conduct occurred post-employment. The 'Monday program' at River City Corrections is an intermittent reporting program, not continuous confinement — sentence_years may warrant correction to 0 if confirmed.
