Renee L. DeLeon #37 sentenced

StateConnecticutCourtMeriden GA 7 (Geographical Area Court)
Incident Date2008-04
Defendant Age36Victim Age14
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former teacher at James Moran Middle School, Wallingford, Connecticut (New Haven County). Age 36 at time of offense; 52 at arrest.

Victim: 14-year-old student. Offense period April 23–December 31, 2008. The relationship included numerous sex acts, including intercourse. Wallingford police received a report of suspected child abuse on April 15, 2024 — approximately 16 years after the offense — and opened an investigation. Detectives conducted multiple interviews and executed a search and seizure warrant.

DeLeon was no longer employed by the Wallingford school system at the time of the investigation. She was arrested August 8, 2024 on charges of second-degree sexual assault and risk of injury to or impairing the morals of a child. She pleaded guilty June 30, 2025 to illegal sexual contact with a victim (CGS § 53-21(a)(2), Class B felony). Remaining charges dismissed.

Sentenced August 25, 2025: 10 years jail execution suspended, 10 years probation with special conditions. Fine: $151. Defense attorney: EJ Gavin.

NOTE: sentence_years = 0 (full suspension). The 16-year reporting delay is the longest in this DB. Resolved under the same statute and identical $151 fine as the Maynard case (#44) — CGS 53-21(a)(2) appears to carry a mandatory $151 fine under this disposition type.
