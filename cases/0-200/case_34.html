Julie Rizzitello #34 sentenced

StateNew JerseyCourtMonmouth County Superior Court
JudgeJill G. O'MalleyIncident Date2017-11-01
Defendant Age37Victim Age16
Prison Sentence10.0 yrs

Former English teacher at Wall Township High School, Wall, New Jersey (Monmouth County). Ten years at the school; resigned July 2024 just before arrest. Age 29 at time of offenses; 37 at sentencing.

Two male victims. Victim 1 (17-year-old): groomed throughout his entire high school years. Unprotected sex at his family's home on his birthday in January 2018; Rizzitello told him she was unable to get pregnant. Weeks later she asked him to come over for a pregnancy test — confirmed pregnant, approximately 8 weeks, consistent with the birthday encounter. She had an abortion. The victim was not comfortable with this and struggled to come to terms with it. Charged with sexually assaulting this victim on various occasions between November 23, 2017 and January 21, 2018. Victim 2: over 18 but still her student; charged as a crime because of the teacher-student relationship. Locations included her Brick Township home, a Wall Township parking lot, and the Belmar bagel shop her family owned where she got both victims jobs.

Contacted both victims during the investigation to delete evidence. Arrested July 2024. Pleaded guilty. Sentenced February 11, 2026 by Judge Jill G. O'Malley (Monmouth County Superior Court, Freehold) to 10 years in state prison. Parole Supervision for Life after release; Megan's Law registration; no contact with victims; permanent teaching license forfeiture. Defense attorney Mitchell Ansell sought 5 years, citing Rizzitello's two young children (ages 4 and 6) and no prior criminal history.

At sentencing, Rizzitello read a letter to the court. Judge O'Malley noted it "was all about you — not mentioned the names of the victims, not once. You apologized to your friends, your family, the community. And you didn't apologize to the victims." O'Malley told Rizzitello: "You still don't see them as victims." Rizzitello twice interrupted with "I do," angering the judge. Earlier court appearance was before Judge Marc C. Lemiuex (July 16, 2024).

NOTE: Pregnancy and abortion caused by victim 1 is documented here from the presentence report as read by the judge. Contacting both victims mid-investigation to delete evidence likely contributed to the judge rejecting the defense's request for 5 years.
