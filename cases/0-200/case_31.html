Former guidance counselor at Pennridge South Middle School, Pennridge School District, Bucks County, Pennsylvania. Age 35 at time of arraignment (Pennsburg, Montgomery County).

Victim was a 14-year-old male student. Contact began fall 2022 after they sat next to each other on a class trip. Schutte subsequently began regularly calling the boy to her guidance office during class hours. Communication primarily through Canvas (the district's messaging app), then Snapchat after the school year ended. In June and July 2023, at least three incidents of sexual contact occurred in Montgomery and Bucks counties, including in a supermarket parking lot and Schutte's home. The boy described the activity as kissing and inappropriate touching. Discovery: a family member of Schutte's saw them kissing inside her home; the boy fled and hid on a nearby street and called his parents for a ride. The mother contacted Upper Perkiomen Police in July 2023. Schutte placed on administrative leave immediately.

Physical evidence included handwritten notes, Snapchat messages, surveillance footage, and an earring found at the victim's home.

Arraigned November 10, 2023 on three felony counts of institutional sexual assault, one felony count of corruption of minors, and three misdemeanor counts of indecent assault; released on $25,000 unsecured bail.

Pleaded guilty February 5, 2025 to one felony count of corruption of a minor of a sexual nature; all other charges dismissed. Sentenced May 15, 2025 to 7 years probation (no prison); 15-year SORNA registration; no contact with victim; no unsupervised contact with minors except biological children; psychosexual evaluation required. Showed no remorse at sentencing. Not classified as sexually violent predator.
