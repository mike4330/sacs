Kaitlyn Wilkes #109 sentenced

Former dance instructor at a dance academy in Cortland, Ohio (Trumbull County). Age 18 at time of offense; 20 at sentencing. Female victim aged 12. Not a school teacher — dance studio context; included for educator-role scope.

Wilkes had known the victim since the victim was 9 and Wilkes was 16, when both were students at the same dance academy. During the 2021-22 season, Wilkes became an instructor for the victim's class. Beginning in October 2021, Wilkes began isolating the victim from friends. In December 2021, sexual contact began at Wilkes' father's home in Champion; further incidents occurred at Wilkes' mother's home and at the dance studio — five incidents total at four separate sites between December 2021 and February 2022. Wilkes communicated with the victim via text, 'blowing up' the victim's phone and guilt-tripping her when she did not respond quickly enough. On one occasion, Wilkes caused the victim to believe she was harming herself in a bathroom: 'One time she had me thinking she was in the bathroom in the bathtub slitting her wrists.'

In January 2022, the mother of a 17-year-old witness discovered messages in which Wilkes admitted to the sexual abuse. Concerns were reported to a dance teacher at the studio in April 2022; Wilkes was let go from the studio around the same time. The situation escalated at a dance competition in Altoona, Pennsylvania in late April 2022, when Wilkes attempted to force her way into a hotel where the victim was staying. The victim was described as hysterical, saying 'make her go away' and 'she's the devil.' Police reports were subsequently filed with the Trumbull County Sheriff's Office and Bazetta Township police.

Jury trial May 2023 before Judge Cynthia Rice. Jury convicted on five counts of gross sexual imposition (three as lesser-included offenses on rape charges); not guilty on two rape counts; hung on six remaining rape counts. At sentencing July 10, 2023, the six hung rape counts were dismissed. Wilkes pleaded guilty via bill of information to six counts of sexual battery (2nd degree felony). Sentenced to 3 to 4.5 years in prison; Tier III sex offender designation with lifetime registration requirement.

NOTE: Wilkes was 18 at the time of the offense — among the youngest defendants in this DB. The pre-abuse relationship spans approximately three years (ages 9 to 12 for the victim), giving this case an unusually long grooming arc. The threatened self-harm is a notable coercive tactic. Prosecutors noted Wilkes admitted she 'knew it was wrong.'
