Former high school teacher with Cherokee County Schools, Hayesville, North Carolina. Age 25 at time of offense (age 26 at arrest).

Investigation by the Cherokee County Sheriff's Office began March 2018 with assistance from the HAVEN Child Advocacy Center. Sprinkles was served with indictments at the Mecklenburg County Detention Center on June 7, 2018, and released on a $25,000 unsecured bond. The grand jury indicted her June 4, 2018. The news article reported five counts of sex activity with a student, though case 18CR000524-190 shows two counts under NC GS §14-27.32(A) (offense date listed as 03/01/2017). The case continued through multiple continuances and four retained defense attorneys over five years.

On May 22, 2023, Sprinkles entered a guilty plea to one count of Sexual Act With a Student (NC GS §14-27.32(A)). The second count was dismissed per plea agreement. Judge William H. Coward imposed an intermediate sentence: confinement range 13–25 months (presumptive), suspended in favor of 24 months supervised probation. Additional conditions: sex offender registration (GS 14-208.6), no contact with victim Hannah West, psychiatric/psychological counseling, DNA sample, 50 hours community service, $9,255 restitution. On June 21, 2023, the court modified conditions to allow her to reside with her biological minor child and Landon Joseph Sprinkles.

Probation completed and final discharge issued May 21, 2025.

NOTE: Female victim (Hannah West) named in no-contact order. No prison time despite 13-25 month confinement range — intermediate probation disposition. The five-year gap from indictment (June 2018) to plea (May 2023) reflects extensive continuances and defense attorney turnover (Chappell → Mills → Roberts → Nielsen).
