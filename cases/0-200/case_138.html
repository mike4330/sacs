Former high school teacher with Cherokee County Schools, Hayesville, North Carolina. Age 25 at time of offense.

Investigation by the Cherokee County Sheriff's Office began March 2018 with assistance from the HAVEN Child Advocacy Center. Grand jury indicted June 4, 2018 on five counts of sexual activity with a student; Sprinkles was served with indictments at the Mecklenburg County Detention Center on June 7, 2018 and released on a $25,000 unsecured bond. Court records show two counts under the sexual act with a student statute (offense date March 1, 2017), though the arrest-era news article reported five counts. The case continued through multiple continuances and four retained defense attorneys over five years.

On May 22, 2023, Sprinkles entered a guilty plea to one count of sexual act with a student. The second count was dismissed per the plea agreement. Female victim.

Sentenced by Judge William H. Coward. North Carolina intermediate disposition: confinement range 13–25 months (presumptive), suspended in favor of 24 months supervised probation. Additional conditions: sex offender registration, no contact with the victim, psychiatric and psychological counseling, DNA sample, 50 hours community service, $9,255 restitution. On June 21, 2023, the court modified conditions to allow her to reside with her biological minor child. Probation completed and final discharge issued May 21, 2025.

NOTE: No prison time despite 13–25 month confinement range — intermediate probation disposition under North Carolina structured sentencing. The five-year gap from indictment (June 2018) to plea (May 2023) reflects extensive continuances and four-attorney defense turnover.
