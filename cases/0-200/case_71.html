Heather Whitten #71 sentenced

StateAlabamaCourt—
Incident Date2011-01-01

Prison Sentence1.0 yr

Former 5th-grade teacher (math and reading) at Williams Intermediate School, Pell City AL (St. Clair County). Had previously taught at Eden Elementary for 3 years. Age 38 at arrest. Background: 1992 Pell City High graduate; BS health administration Auburn 1997; MA early childhood/elementary education West Alabama 2011; began at Williams January 2012 — arrested one month later. Victim: 14-year-old male; met at school but was not her student. Prosecutors stated the sexual relationship occurred during and after the time Whitten had legal custody of the boy — she had taken on guardianship/custody while sexually abusing him. Indicted on three counts second-degree sexual abuse (victim between 12 and 16). Arrested February 2012; released on $5,000 bond; suspended without pay; later resigned. Pled guilty to second-degree rape. Sentenced October 9, 2012 by Judge Jim Hill: 5-year split sentence, 1 year and 1 day in prison at Tutwiler + remainder on supervised probation. DA: Richard Minor (St. Clair County). Superintendent: Bobby Hathcock. Character witnesses at sentencing: her brother and a longtime family friend, who described her as compassionate, caring toward children, active in church, and said the incidents were out of character. NOTE: Legal custody of the victim is the most severe access/power dynamic in this DB — she was not merely his teacher but his legal guardian while abusing him. The 'during and after' language from prosecutors implies the relationship may have predated or outlasted the formal custody arrangement. $5,000 bond for a rape charge is extremely low. Church activity cited in character testimony (again, Alabama church network as social legitimizer). 'Out of character' framing from family is common; the legal custody fact makes it structurally impossible — she could not have abused a child she had legal custody of 'out of character' for any extended period.
