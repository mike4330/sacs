Former Spanish teacher at Mayville High School, Village of Oakfield, Wisconsin (Fond du Lac County). Age 42 at time of first offense; 44 at sentencing.

The victim, a male student, began having lunch with Cardinal in her classroom in May 2011 during his sophomore year. In fall 2011 he enrolled in her Spanish class. In spring 2012 they began texting — initially about school, then personal matters, deepening into romantic communication. The first sexual encounter occurred December 8, 2011: Cardinal picked up the victim and drove him to her home in Oakfield. Subsequent encounters occurred at her residence; at least one occurred while Cardinal's 2-year-old child was present in the home. The victim eventually moved to Marion, Iowa in March 2012; Cardinal traveled to Iowa and the two had sex at his mother's house there.

Cardinal was placed on paid administrative leave April 23, 2012. She appeared in court April 27, 2012 on 12 felony charges: 6 counts of sexual assault of a student by school staff and 6 counts of child enticement through sexual contact. Bond: $5,000 cash. Fond du Lac County DA: Dan Kaminsky. Potential Iowa charges were discussed but not confirmed in public record.

Cardinal pleaded guilty to one count of sexual assault (felony) in a deal with prosecutors. At sentencing September 4, 2013, Judge Dale English exceeded the prosecution's recommendation of one year jail, sentencing Cardinal to 2 years prison plus 1 year extended supervision. English stated: "This isn't a probation case to me, just isn't. I think the offense is so aggravating that probation I don't think is appropriate." Cardinal made a tearful apology. Sex offender registration required. Asst. DA Devra Ayala prosecuted.

NOTE: Charge reduction from 12 counts to 1 at plea. Judge overrode prosecution's own recommendation and imposed prison over jail. Child present during at least one offense — an aggravating factor noted in press coverage. Cross-state contact (Wisconsin + Iowa) is unusual for this DB.
