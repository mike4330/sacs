Former special education teacher at Langham Creek High School, Cy-Fair Independent School District, Houston, Harris County, Texas. Victim was a 17-year-old male student. Sexual relationship included multiple instances at school and off-campus locations during the 2017–2018 school year.

Age 29 at arrest (July 25, 2018); age 30 at sentencing (March 2019). Arrested July 25, 2018 following investigation. Cy-Fair ISD terminated her employment in 2018. Original charges included indecency with a child and other sex offenses.

Pleaded guilty in March 2019 to improper relationship between educator and student (Tex. Penal Code § 21.12, second-degree felony). Sentenced March 2019 to 10 years deferred adjudication probation (community supervision). Conditions include no contact with victim, counseling, and restrictions on being around minors. Sex offender registration required. Probation violation could result in up to 20 years in prison. Texas Education Agency revoked teaching license in 2019.
