Former English teacher at Langham Creek High School, Cypress-Fairbanks Independent School District (CFISD), Houston, Harris County, Texas. Age 47 at time of offense. Employed with CFISD since August 2005. Male victim, age 15, a student in her English class. Married at time of offense.

Grooming began on Remind 101 (a school-sanctioned teacher-parent-student communication platform) shortly after the victim transferred into Suggs' English class around November 1, 2021. Communication moved to direct texts and then Snapchat, where Suggs sent explicit videos of herself. She texted as late as 1 a.m. on weekends and shared photos of her husband and family with the victim, telling him she could "lose everything" if the relationship became known.

The single confirmed sexual incident occurred December 10, 2021 in Suggs' vehicle on a residential street near the victim's home: kissing and oral sex performed by Suggs on the victim. Visible hickeys were noted on the victim's neck. Three days later, the victim's parents discovered the communications on his phone and met with school administrators December 13, 2021. CFISD placed Suggs on administrative leave immediately; she resigned January 10, 2022.

Complaint filed March 2, 2022. Arrested March 9, 2022; $40,000 surety bond. Grand jury indictment September 2, 2022. Pleaded guilty September 14, 2022 to Improper Relationship Between Educator/Student (F2), reduced from original charge of Sexual Assault of a Child; 6 years deferred adjudication community supervision before Judge Frank Aguilar, 228th District Court. No prison; no final conviction. Sex offender registration was initially ordered as a supervision condition but removed by amended order September 16, 2022. Prosecutor Janna Oswald; defense attorney Stephen A. Aslett.

Motion for early termination filed February 22, 2024. Deferred adjudication terminated early September 16, 2024 — approximately 2 of 6 years served. Case closed with no final conviction and no sex offender registration.

NOTE: Grooming initiated via Remind 101, a school-sanctioned platform — institutionally enabled access before moving to private channels. Charge reduced from sexual assault to improper relationship. Deferred adjudication terminated early; Suggs carries no criminal record or registration requirement.
