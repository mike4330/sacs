Dayna Chidester #121 sentenced

State Illinois
Court U.S. District Court, Northern District of Illinois
Judge Jorge L. Alonso
Incident Date 2018-09-01
Defendant Age 50
Victim Age 14
Prison Sentence 5.33 yrs

Former consumer and family science teacher at Reed-Custer High School in Braidwood, IL. Also previously taught at Thornwood High School in South Holland. Victim was a 14-year-old male non-student, also a resident of Manhattan, IL (Will County).

Chidester began communicating with the victim by text in September 2018. Contact escalated to sexting and exchange of nude photos before sexual contact began. She had sex with the victim 15-20 times between September and December 2018 at her home, in her vehicle, and at suburban motels. She purchased gifts including a robe and a longboard skateboard. She also directed the victim to photograph himself in sexually explicit conduct and send the images to her phone. More than 9,000 texts exchanged.

Authorities were alerted in December 2018 after a Reed-Custer student reported receiving texts from Chidester about her relationship with the victim. Will County investigators searched the victim's phone and found nude photos of Chidester. Chidester was arrested by state authorities January 3, 2019 on charges of aggravated criminal sexual abuse, indecent solicitation of a child, and unlawful grooming; bail set at $1 million.

FBI assumed jurisdiction; federal complaint filed September 25, 2019 charging sexual abuse of a minor (15-year mandatory minimum). State charges dropped. Chidester pleaded guilty to receiving child pornography. Sentenced December 8, 2021 by U.S. District Judge Jorge L. Alonso to 5 years and 4 months federal prison. AUSA Ashley A. Chung argued the offense was "not a one-time lapse in judgment, but an ongoing pattern of criminal activity."

NOTE: Federal prosecution produced significantly harsher outcome than state charges likely would have. Original federal charge carried 15-year mandatory minimum; plea to CP charge yielded 64 months. Chidester confided to a Reed-Custer student mid-offense: "It's horrible. (I) can go to jail." Prior-warning-ignored angle: a band teacher at the same school had been released on CSAM charges one month before Chidester's arrest.
