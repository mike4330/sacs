English teacher at Daniel Boone Area High School, Berks County, PA. Resident of Orchard View Road, Exeter Township. Age 31 at time of offense and arrest; 32 at sentencing.

Between January and April 2012, Risell engaged in sexual contact with a 15-year-old male student on multiple occasions at her Exeter Township home. The relationship began with text message communication, after which Risell arranged private meetings with the victim at her residence. In March 2012 she picked the boy up from his home and drove him to her house where they engaged in sexual activity.

Surrendered to Berks County detectives September 13, 2012; released on $20,000 unsecured bail. Initially charged with statutory sexual assault, institutional sexual assault, indecent assault, indecent exposure, and related offenses. Raised a potential insanity defense, but prosecutors (ADA Jonathan Kurland) did not believe it had merit; the matter was resolved quickly at the victim family's request to avoid months of additional litigation. Pleaded guilty to one felony count of institutional sexual assault.

Sentenced by Judge John A. Boccabella in Berks County Court: 5 years probation, 3 months home confinement (house arrest with electronic monitoring, permitted to leave only for approved purposes). Registered sex offender with 25-year address reporting requirement. Faced further evaluation by the Pennsylvania Sexual Offender Assessment Board for possible sexually violent predator classification (lifetime reporting, counseling, community notification); outcome of that evaluation not reported in available sources.

ADA Kurland: "This teacher took that trust and exploited it and used that child for whatever her selfish motivations or reasons were." Noted the plea was "a compromise to get that resolution" for the victim's family.
