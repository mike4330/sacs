Former substitute teacher at Harney Middle School, Las Vegas, Nevada (Clark County School District). Age 22–23 at time of offense; 23 at arrest. Gentile was also volunteering as director of a hip-hop club drawing students from both Harney Middle School and Las Vegas High School.

Beginning December 2015, Gentile developed a relationship with a 17-year-old male high school student who was a member of the hip-hop club. She would give him rides home after practice, during which they kissed four to five times. They exchanged approximately 800 text messages. The student told police he felt obligated to be in a relationship with Gentile so he would feel comfortable remaining in the club. Gentile had not taught since December 2015; she was arrested at her home March 1, 2016. The Clark County School District Police Department investigated; Capt. Ken Young stated police received information from numerous sources.

During her police interview, Gentile admitted she did not view the victim as a student. She acknowledged that they texted about relationship problems with other people. She also admitted feeling "powerful" and described the relationship as a form of revenge because her fiancé had been texting other women.

Charged with two counts of sex act by an adult school employee with a pupil. Bail set at $10,000. On January 18, 2017, Gentile was sentenced to 19–48 months (suspended); probation up to 5 years; sex offender registration required; fines and fees.

NOTE: No actual incarceration — suspended sentence with probation. The conduct appears limited to kissing and texting. Gentile's stated motivations (power, revenge against fiancé) are among the more explicitly documented psychological drivers in the DB. Student's account of feeling obligated to participate to maintain club access is a coercive-environment grooming pattern.
