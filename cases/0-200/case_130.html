Chloe Rose Castro #130 verdict

State Colorado
Court Jefferson County District Court (First Judicial District)
Judge Diego Hunt
Incident Date 2022-09-01
Defendant Age 26
Victim Age 12

School social worker and IEP case manager for Jefferson County Public Schools, Lakewood, CO. Rotated across six JeffCo schools 2021–2024; worked at Three Creeks K-8 (January 2022–May 2024), where she served as the victim's IEP case manager. Also worked at Columbine Hills Elementary, Leawood Elementary, Manning Middle, Drake Middle, and Wheat Ridge High. Age 26–27 at start of relationship; 28 at arrest. Resident of Lakewood, CO.

Castro met the victim at the end of his sixth grade year (approximately late 2022, when he was 12). She served as his IEP case manager, giving her regular one-on-one access. She asked him to be her "boyfriend" around age 12–13. Physical contact — cuddling, kissing, intimate touching, and exchange of nude photographs — occurred in her school office. Castro told the victim they "couldn't have sex until he turned 18" and discussed moving together to New Mexico or California when he turned 18 "to start a new life." Castro saved herself in the victim's contacts as "youth pastor" to avoid parental detection. Communication was conducted via Instagram over approximately two years.

Parents discovered "countless" inappropriate messages on the victim's phone on October 29, 2024. Arvada Police Department arrested Castro November 6, 2024. Bond set at $75,000; remanded into custody at plea. Charged with Sexual Assault on a Child by One in a Position of Trust — Victim Less Than 15 with Pattern of Abuse (F3) and Internet Luring of a Child (F4). Prosecutor: Deputy DA Tyra Forbes. Defense: Marshall Breit.

Pleaded guilty February 2, 2026 to Sexual Assault on a Child by One in a Position of Trust — Victim Less Than 15 (added Count 3, F3). Original counts dismissed at plea. No sentencing concessions by prosecution. Faces indeterminate sentence of minimum 4 years to life in DOC under Colorado's Sex Offender Lifetime Supervision Act (SOLSA); probation alternatively of minimum 20 years to life. Sentencing set April 2, 2026.

NOTE: Cross-reference Imagine Kay Ewer #129 — same JeffCo cluster; Ewer's January 2024 arrest was described as the first of five similar JeffCo educator cases in 2024, of which Castro was the last (November 2024). Both cases involve special-needs/IEP students. Jeffco Kids First advocacy group claimed a prior settlement with Castro and another family may have occurred, suggesting possible earlier unaddressed misconduct. Victim's mother (Lia Flynn) told Colorado Politics the case caused "lifelong issues and lifelong therapy." Behavioral health / social worker role — not a classroom teacher. "Youth pastor" alias is a notable concealment tactic.
