Conducted sexual contact with a 15-year-old male student between December 2023 and March 2024. Relationship escalated from kissing to sexting/lewd photos to planned sex. Used condom with both parties' DNA found in her car.

Initial arrest charges included sexual exploitation of a minor (CSAM) and sexual conduct with a minor — CSAM charge dropped at indictment. Northup, Deputy County Attorney.
