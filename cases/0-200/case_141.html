Former teacher and private tutor, Montgomery County, Texas. Age 39 at time of offense; 40 at sentencing.

In May 2019, Lowery was charged with online solicitation of a minor after parents of a 15-year-old boy she was tutoring discovered sexually explicit messages on his phone, including offers to perform sex acts and a nude photograph, and a request for an explicit photo in return. Bond was set at $10,000.

On November 1, 2019, while out on bond for the solicitation charge, Lowery picked up a 15-year-old boy and his 13-year-old girlfriend. She dropped the girl at a birthday party, returned to the boy's home, and performed oral sex on him and had intercourse with him on a couch. In a separate encounter, Lowery had both minors in her vehicle outside the boy's Montgomery County home and directed the 13-year-old girl to perform oral sex on the boy while Lowery watched. The girl complied. Sexually explicit messages were discovered by the boy's mother. Bond was set at $100,000 on the new charges.

Lowery pleaded guilty to Sexual Assault of a Child (2nd degree felony) and Sexual Performance by a Child (1st degree felony). In November 2020, she was sentenced to 20 years in prison. Assistant DA Laura Bond: "Lowery preyed on our community's most vulnerable... she violated children to satisfy her own selfish desires."
