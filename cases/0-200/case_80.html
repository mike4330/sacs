Former teacher at Pickens County high school, Clemson SC. With district since 2017. Age 33 at time of offense (35 at plea). Victim: student aged 16 or 17 (gender not specified in reporting). Single sexual encounter at Pelfrey's home December 29, 2021. Discovery: notification received by investigators and school district December 31, 2021 (two days after incident); Pelfrey confessed to the misconduct immediately and was promptly terminated. Arrested January 3, 2022 by Pickens County Sheriff's deputies. Grand jury indictment January 2023: (1) sexual battery of a student aged 16 or 17; (2) contributing to the delinquency of a minor. Solicitor: Walt Wilkins (13th Circuit). Plea March 2023: Alford plea to contributing to delinquency of a minor — sexual battery charge dropped. Sentence: 2 years probation + sex offender registration + lifetime teaching ban. No prior criminal record. NOTE: Alford plea means Pelfrey maintained her innocence while acknowledging sufficient evidence to convict — notable given she confessed to investigators at the time of discovery. The contradiction (immediate confession + later Alford plea) suggests attorney-driven strategy after indictment. Sexual battery dropped to delinquency-of-minor is a significant charge reduction producing a no-prison outcome; cf. Anderson #41 (SC, similar no-prison result). The FITS News coverage explicitly framed this as 'reviving discussion on criminalization of sex between consenting adults' — editorializing that minimizes the power differential and statutory context. South Carolina no-prison pattern holding (cf. #41).
