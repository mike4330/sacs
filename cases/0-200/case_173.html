Former special-needs teacher at Harding Middle School, Des Moines Public Schools, Des Moines IA. Age 37–38 at time of offense; 38 at arrest; 39 at sentencing. Victim was a 14yo male middle school student who was NOT in her classroom. Placed on administrative leave April 15, 2019 after district learned of investigation; resigned May 17, 2019; arrested May 30, 2019.

Multiple incidents November 2018 – May 2019. First incident: Johnson picked the boy up from a sporting event. Second incident: at her home while watching TV; her husband unexpectedly walked in during the attempt. She sent nude photographs to the victim via Snapchat. After the boy declined her advances, she berated him via text. Police Sgt. Paul Parizek: 'He tried to rebuff her advances and she continued on.' Bond set at $25,000 cash-only.

Charged with lascivious acts with a child, solicitation to commit a felony, assault with intent to commit sexual abuse, third-degree sex abuse, and dissemination/exhibition of obscene materials to a minor. Pleaded guilty January 2020 to one count of sexual exploitation by a school employee (substantially reduced). Sentenced August 21, 2020 to 2 years in prison; required to register as sex offender.

Civil lawsuit filed by victim's family against Des Moines Public Schools; district found not liable in 2021 ruling; settled 2023 (Des Moines Register, August 15, 2023).

NOTE: Victim was not in defendant's classroom — access was through school proximity and outside contact, including transportation. Husband-catches-them discovery pattern (cf. Baber #164, Dinkel #172). Victim's berating after refusal is documented in court records — unusual in that the victim actively declined and reported. Parent of nonverbal special-needs student in defendant's class noted she was never notified by the school, raising separate institutional communication concerns.
