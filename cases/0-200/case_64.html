Former special education teacher at Madison County High School, Madison County AL. Age 22 at arrest, 23 at sentencing. Hired start of 2018-19 school year; salary $39,305; passed criminal background check. Two male student victims, both at least 16; neither was in her special ed class. Victim 1: 17-year-old male; sex ~February 14, 2019 (Valentine's Day); also received nude photos. Victim 2: age not listed in court records; received nude photos on or about March 29, 2019 — the day Bates' resignation became effective (whether she was still a school employee on that date was raised as a potential issue). Resigned March 29, 2019; arrested April 12, 2019. Charged with 5 counts: (1) felony — school employee engaging in sex act with student under 19 (Ala. Code § 13A-6-81); (2) misdemeanor sexual contact with a student; (3-5) three misdemeanor counts distributing obscene material to a student. Pled guilty; sentenced to time served + 3 years probation; 10-year prison sentence suspended. Defense attorney: Robert Tuten. ADA: Tim Douthit. Family connection: daughter-in-law of Stacy Bates, chief deputy for Madison County Sheriff's Office — which reviewed the case. Sheriff's officials stated the relationship did not affect the investigation. NOTE: Second Alabama case (cf. Parker #63) with law enforcement/establishment family connection and an extraordinarily lenient outcome for a felony carrying up to 20 years and mandatory lifetime SORA on paper. Time served + probation with 10-year suspended sentence is the softest outcome for a felony conviction in this DB. The resignation-day photo adds a detail about the defendant's awareness: she sent explicit material to a student on the same day she formally left employment, possibly believing she was no longer subject to the school-employee statute. The Alabama pattern is cohering: § 13A-6-81 is serious legislation routinely producing non-custodial outcomes when defendants have local connections.
