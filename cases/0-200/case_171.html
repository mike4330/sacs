Former English teacher at Lawrence High School (LHS), Lawrence Public Schools, Lawrence, Douglas County KS. Also identified as Meredith L. Kane. Age 24 at arrest; 25 at sentencing. Sexual relationship with 15yo male student during fall 2006 through March 2007, her first year of teaching at LHS. Multiple acts in Douglas County.

Arrested March 16, 2007. Pleaded guilty July 2, 2007 before Judge Stephen Six to one count of aggravated indecent liberties with a child. Three remaining counts (second count of aggravated indecent liberties + two counts of criminal sodomy) dismissed. DA Charles Branson: 'She plead guilty to the most severe charge she could plea to.'

Bond violation: arrested August 27, 2007 for contacting victim in violation of release conditions; held until sentencing. Defense cited bipolar disorder as impairing judgment; judge rejected the argument and imposed prison time. Sentenced December 19, 2007 to 59 months (~4.92 years) in prison; required to register as sex offender.

NOTE: Bond violation (victim contact) is a notable pattern — cf. Yaranga #161 (contact violation re-arrest).
