Jennifer Collins McNeill #70 sentenced

StateAlabamaCourtChilton County Circuit Court
JudgeJohn B. BushIncident Date2012-04-01
Defendant Age39Victim Age14
Prison Sentence5.0 yrs

Former teacher and cheerleading coach at Thorsby School (K–12), Chilton County, Alabama. Age 39 at arrest.

Victim: a teenage male whose mother had been McNeill’s friend for a decade. The families attended the same church, where McNeill taught Sunday school. The victim was not her student. Offenses occurred April 2012 through June 2013. Arrested August 2013; charged with 6 counts of rape and 2 counts of sodomy of a juvenile male (victim under 16). Grand jury indictment filed February 21, 2014. Pled guilty May 2014 to second-degree rape and sodomy. Placed on administrative leave after arrest; resigned from the school system.

The original plea deal called for half of a 5-year sentence in prison and half under house arrest. At proceedings, McNeill claimed the victim and his mother had hacked her Facebook account to plant incriminating posts; her attorney later admitted she had ‘misrepresented herself.’ Judge John B. Bush mandated all 60 months in prison — no house arrest — citing the lie and her breach of moral responsibility to her community and church. McNeill cried and begged for mercy at sentencing.

NOTE: The Facebook lie modification is the only instance in this DB of a judge actively penalizing in-proceedings misconduct by revoking a plea-deal condition. It also validates the Facebook evidence as significant — she would not have risked a perjury-adjacent lie unless the posts were damaging. Access here was through church and long-term family friendship, not the school; the victim was not her student. Sunday school teacher at the same church as the victim’s family adds a faith-setting layer (cf. Daughdrill #69). At 5 years actual, the outcome is harsher than most Alabama state cases — likely a product of the judge’s adverse credibility finding.
