Jennifer Collins McNeill #70 sentenced

StateAlabamaCourt—
JudgeJohn B. BushIncident Date2012-04-01
Defendant Age39
Prison Sentence5.0 yrs

Former teacher and cheerleading coach/sponsor at Thorsby School (K-12 combined), Chilton County AL. Age 39 at arrest. Victim: teenage male son of McNeill's 10-year friend; not a student at Thorsby. McNeill and the victim's mother had been friends for a decade and attended the same church, where McNeill had taught Sunday school. Offenses: April 2012 – June 2013. Arrested August 2013; charged with 6 counts rape + 2 counts sodomy of a juvenile male (victim under 16). Grand jury indictment filed February 21, 2014. Plea: guilty May 2014 to second-degree rape and sodomy. Original plea deal: half of 5-year sentence in prison, half under house arrest. McNeill lied about incriminating Facebook posts — claimed victim and his mother had hacked her account; attorney later admitted she 'misrepresented herself.' Judge mandated all 60 months in prison (no house arrest) specifically citing the lie and her breach of moral responsibility to community and church. Placed on administrative leave after arrest; resigned from school system. Superintendent Dave Hayden. Chief Deputy DA: C.J. Robinson. McNeill cried and begged for mercy at sentencing. NOTE: The Facebook lie modification is the only instance in this DB of a judge actively penalizing in-proceedings misconduct by revoking the house-arrest portion of a plea deal. It also validates the Facebook evidence as significant — she wouldn't have risked a perjury-adjacent lie unless the posts were damaging. Access mechanism: church and long-term family friendship, not the school (victim was not her student). Sunday school teacher at the same church as victim's family adds a faith-setting layer (cf. Daughdrill #69). Outcome is harsher than other Alabama state cases — 5 years actual — likely a product of the judge's adverse credibility finding and the church/community trust framing.
