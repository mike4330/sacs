Tennille Whitaker #111 sentenced

StateNevadaCourtElko County District Court
Incident Date2015-09-01
Defendant Age38
Prison Sentence20.0 yrs

Former fourth-grade teacher at Wells Combined School (Wells Elementary School), Wells, Nevada (Elko County). Taught elementary school for approximately 11 years. Age approximately 38 at onset of offense; 40 at arrest. Multiple male victims — at least four high school boys serving as student teaching aides in her fourth-grade class; at least one had previously been a student in her class years earlier. Abuse period: September 2015 through June 2017.

Whitaker sexually harassed, molested, and abused the student teaching aides. She gave them gifts, provided at least one alcohol in exchange for sexual gratification, and drove victims to hotels. Conduct occurred both on and off campus. In February 2017, a custodian found Whitaker with a student in her locked, darkened classroom. In March 2017, a teacher discovered a Snapchat photo apparently showing Whitaker having sex with minor students.

School officials were first alerted by a parent in May or June 2016. In October 2016, another teacher reported concerns to Principal Christopher McAnany, which triggered an Elko County Sheriff's investigation. Despite the custodian and Snapchat discoveries in early 2017, Whitaker was not placed on administrative leave until her arrest on June 5, 2017; she resigned two months later.

Arrested June 5, 2017 on eight counts of sexual conduct between a school employee and a pupil. Pleaded no contest in April 2018 to four counts. Sentenced October 2018 to 20 years in prison. During sentencing, Whitaker tearfully apologized: 'I will be ashamed forever. I am truly sorry for betraying the trust of the parents and the victims.'

A civil lawsuit was filed March 9, 2019 in U.S. District Court in Reno on behalf of one victim and the estate of another. One victim moved from Wells after his 2018 graduation and died by suicide in August 2018. The lawsuit names the school district and Principal McAnany, alleging deliberate indifference to known grooming conduct over multiple years and suppression of information.

NOTE: Victim age not specified in sources; high school teaching aides suggests range of 15–18. One of the most extensively documented prior-warning-ignored cases in the DB: parent report (May/June 2016), teacher report to principal (October 2016), custodian observation (February 2017), Snapchat evidence (March 2017) — all preceding the June 2017 arrest. The victim suicide is the most severe secondary consequence in this DB. Civil lawsuit outcome unknown.
