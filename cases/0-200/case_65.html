Carrie Cabri Witt #65 sentenced

StateAlabamaCourtMorgan County Circuit Court
Incident Date2016-01-01
Defendant Age43 Victim Age17
Prison Sentence1.5 yrs

Former teacher at Decatur High School, Decatur AL (Decatur City Schools). Subjects: history, psychology, government, economics; also JV cheerleading coach and varsity girls' golf coach. Age ~43 at arrest, 48 at sentencing. Two male student victims ages 17 and 18. Arrested March 21, 2016 by Decatur PD Violent Crimes Unit on two counts of school employee engaging in sex act with student under 19 (Ala. Code § 13A-6-81); released on $10,000 bond. Superintendent Ed Nichols placed her on administrative leave. Constitutional challenge: lower court (Morgan County Circuit — the same court noted in Parker #63's coverage) ruled the statute unconstitutional, agreeing age-of-consent laws preempt school-employee restrictions. Alabama Supreme Court reversed and remanded. After five years of litigation, Witt pled guilty March 2021 to one count; second charge dropped. Sentenced July 6, 2021: 10 years with 18 months to serve in prison, then 18-month community corrections program, 7 years probation, lifetime SORA registration. ADA Courtney Schellack: 'Teachers are some of the most trusted and respected members of society. Ms. Witt violated that trust by her actions with one of her own students. That is exactly why this law is necessary.' NOTE: The Alabama § 13A-6-81 constitutional challenge (raised by both Parker #63 and Witt simultaneously, ~2016) is now clearly a coordinated defense strategy that got genuine traction — a Morgan County judge agreed with it at the trial level. The Alabama Supreme Court had to step in to preserve the statute. This 5-year delay between arrest and guilty plea is entirely attributable to that legal battle. Witt's case is the key precedent that foreclosed Parker's appeal. 18 months actual on a 10-year sentence continues the Alabama pattern of custodial sentences that are largely suspended, though Witt's outcome is slightly harsher than Bates (#64, time served) given the prolonged constitutional challenge.
