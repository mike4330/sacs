Former science teacher at Oak Ridge High School, Conroe ISD, Conroe, Texas. Was in her first semester at Oak Ridge when grooming began. Victim: male sophomore, under 17, severely disadvantaged background (father abandoned him, mother had been murdered); the complaint states school was his "safe haven" and he trusted his teachers.

Fall 2021: Mazock used her authority to keep the victim after class behind closed doors and obtained his Snapchat username. First contact October 19, 2021; her handle was "starshine bunny." She used Snapchat's disappearing-messages feature deliberately to send sexually explicit photos and solicitous messages. Multiple sexual encounters followed through fall and winter 2021. In December 2021 she picked the victim up in her vehicle and parked near his home to have sex; she also entered his home and had sex in his bedroom.

Drugs: Mazock provided cough syrup and Benadryl and encouraged drug use before sexual encounters. She also provided money or Percocet; a January 9, 2022 text read "don't forget the percs of th tutoring" (double entendre and drug solicitation). She communicated with another student, "M.P.," on Snapchat about the relationship. The relationship was an open secret discussed among students. Principal Anthony Livecchi failed to investigate rumors.

Discovery: On January 12, 2022, the victim overdosed on drugs provided by Mazock or purchased with money she gave him and required a lengthy hospital stay. Arrested January 25, 2022; charged with indecency with a child; posted $100,000 bond. Pleaded guilty to sexual assault of a child July 13, 2022. Sentenced October 3, 2022: 10 years prison. Civil lawsuit filed February 24, 2022: Doe ex rel. Roe v. Snap, Inc., et al., No. 4:22-CV-00590 (S.D. Tex.), against Snap Inc., Mazock, and Conroe ISD.
