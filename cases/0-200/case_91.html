Former kindergarten teacher, coach, athletic director, and music director at St. Peter Immanuel School (private, religious), Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Residence in Franklin, Wisconsin. Age 30–31 at time of offense.

Victim: a 16-year-old male former student, then in high school. Dillett had coached him in grade school and was a close family friend; she helped watch the children while his mother worked long hours for child protective services and his father was in prison. Sexual contact documented from at least June 2015; intercourse occurred at Dillett’s home in February 2016 and at the school in March 2016.

Charged April 8, 2016 — placed on paid leave the same day — with sexual assault of a child by a person who works with children and sexual intercourse with a child age 16 or older. An amended complaint added a more serious felony (second-degree sexual assault of a child). Pled guilty November 2016 to 2 counts of assault by someone who works with children; 6 other counts including the more serious felony were dismissed. Sentenced December 2, 2016 by Judge Joseph Donald: 2 years prison plus 3 years supervised release; sex offender registration. Defense requested probation with a suspended 18-month term; prosecution cited salacious texts as evidence of sexual intent.

Dillett admitted the offense to the victim’s parents and begged them not to report her. The boy initially tried to protect her by claiming he had raped her.

NOTE: Dillett exploited trusted family-friend status gained through coaching and childcare — access was not purely institutional but layered with personal trust. Private religious school. One of three Milwaukee-area teacher sex assault cases charged in the same period (cf. Novak #88, Domres #89). Judge Donald noted it may take the victim’s lifetime to overcome the experience.
