Elizabeth Dillett #91 sentenced

State Wisconsin
Court Milwaukee County Circuit Court
Judge Joseph Donald
Incident Date 2015-06-01
Defendant Age 30
Victim Age 16
Prison Sentence 2.0 yrs

Former kindergarten teacher, coach, athletic director, and music director at St. Peter Immanuel School (private/religious), 7801 W. Acacia St., Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Residence in Franklin, WI. Age 30-31 at time of offense. Male victim age 16, former St. Peter student (in high school at time). Dillett had coached the boy in grade school and was a close family friend; helped watch the children while his mother worked long hours for child protective services and his father was in prison. Sexual contact from at least June 2015; documented intercourse February 2016 at Dillett's home and March 2016 at the school. Amended charges included contact in June 2015 and January (dismissed at plea). Charged April 8, 2016 (put on paid leave same day) with sexual assault of a child by a person who works with children + sexual intercourse with a child age 16 or older. Amended complaint added more serious felony (second-degree sexual assault of a child). Pleaded guilty November 2016 to 2 counts assault by someone who works with children; 6 other counts including more serious felony dismissed. Sentenced December 2, 2016 by Judge Joseph Donald: 2 years prison + 3 years supervised release; sex offender registration. Defense (Schiro) requested probation with suspended 18-month term; prosecution cited salacious texts as evidence of sexual intent. Dillett admitted the offense to victim's parents and begged them not to report her; boy initially tried to protect her by claiming he had raped her. NOTE: Private school (St. Peter Immanuel); Dillett exploited trusted family-friend status gained through coaching and childcare. One of three Milwaukee-area teacher sex-assault cases charged in same period (see also Novak #88, Domres #89). Judge noted it may take victim's lifetime to overcome the experience.
