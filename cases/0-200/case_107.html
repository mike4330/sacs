Julie Hautzenroeder #107 sentenced

State Ohio
Court Hamilton County Common Pleas Court
Incident Date 2013-04-01
Defendant Age 36
Victim Age 16
Prison Sentence 2.0 yrs

Former science teacher at Colerain High School, Colerain Township, Ohio (Hamilton County, northwest of Cincinnati). Age 36 at time of offense. Two male victims, both aged 16. Single mother; employed by Northwest Local School District as substitute since 2003, full-time at Colerain since 2006.

In late April or mid-May 2013, Hautzenroeder invited the two boys to her home, supplied them with alcohol and marijuana, and had oral sex with one and intercourse with the other. A third adult present observed Hautzenroeder smoking marijuana with the boys but left before any sexual contact occurred.

Charged August 2013 with two counts of sexual battery; released on $1,000 bond. A jury convicted her of one count of sexual battery in November 2013. Sentenced to two years in prison; released early after approximately six months in July 2014. An Ohio appeals court upheld the conviction in December 2014. On appeal, Hautzenroeder argued that Colerain High School was not a 'school' as defined by the Ohio Revised Code, that evidence was insufficient, and that she had ineffective legal representation; all three grounds were rejected.

NOTE: Original two-count charge; jury convicted on one count only — likely reflecting split liability between the two victims. Sentenced to 2 years; served approximately 6 months before early release. Headline in one report references '18 months served' — inconsistent with the article body's stated 6 months; 6 months is used here. Assistant prosecutor Joshua Berkowitz handled the case.
