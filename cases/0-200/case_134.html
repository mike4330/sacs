Former middle school teacher at Stovall Middle School, Aldine ISD, Houston, Texas. Age 42 at time of offense.

On February 26, 2014, Smith performed a full-contact lap dance on a 15-year-old male student at the front of the classroom in front of other students, lasting approximately 3 minutes and 54 seconds. She rubbed her body against his, placed her head in his lap, and the student struck her buttocks multiple times. After the dance she said, "I love you, baby. Happy birthday." The performance was accompanied by music. A student recorded the incident on a cellphone, which led to police involvement. Smith told police that the class had persuaded her to do it.

Charged with the felony of improper relationship between educator and student. Bond was set at $30,000. Pleaded guilty and was sentenced approximately September 3 to 5, 2014 to three years of deferred adjudication probation. No prison time. Texas SBEC teaching license revocation was initiated.

NOTE: A single public classroom incident, not a sustained grooming relationship — analytically distinct from most DB cases. Spontaneous and witnessed by the entire class, with no prior evidence of individual targeting. The deferred adjudication outcome produces sentence_years = 0. The improper relationship statute was the same one used for ongoing sexual relationships; Texas law applies it to any sexual contact between educator and student regardless of whether a pattern exists.
