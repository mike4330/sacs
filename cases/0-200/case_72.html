Former special education inclusion support teacher and assistant cheer sponsor at Canyon High School, New Braunfels, Texas (Comal ISD). Hired 2020–21 school year. Age 34 at first arrest.

Male victim, age 15, student at Canyon High School. Sexual encounter occurred in a biology classroom closet in May 2021. Discovered when the victim's mother found sexually explicit texts on her son's phone, confirmed them with Anderson, and contacted police.

First arrested August 2021 on sexual assault of a child (second-degree felony); bond $50,000; placed on administrative leave. Police noted the possibility of additional victims at time of arrest. Second arrest December 2021 on improper relationship between educator and student.

Deferred adjudication granted December 19, 2022 by Judge R. Bruce Boyer on both counts. Terms: 10 years community supervision; 60 days jail distributed across the probation term (non-consecutive); sex offender registration; $2,000 fine; 500 hours community service; lifetime protective order. At the hearing the victim's mother addressed Anderson in court, describing her son's depression and anxiety and stating the ideal punishment would be for Anderson to see her own children endure the same pain. Principal Dustin Davisson notified parents.

NOTE: Deferred adjudication in Texas means no final conviction is entered if community supervision is completed successfully. The 60 days jail distributed over 10 years probation for sexual assault of a child — which normally carries 2–20 years — is one of the most favorable outcomes in this DB for serious physical contact conduct. Classroom closet as venue documents premeditation and deliberate use of institutional access.
