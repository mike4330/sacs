Former special education inclusion support teacher and assistant cheer sponsor at Canyon High School, New Braunfels TX (Comal ISD). Hired 2020-21 school year. Age 34 at first arrest. Victim: 15-year-old male student at Canyon High School. Sexual encounter in a biology classroom closet, May 2021. Discovery: victim's mother found sexually explicit texts on son's phone, confirmed from Anderson, contacted police. First arrest August 2021: sexual assault of a child (Texas Penal Code § 22.011, second-degree felony); bond $50,000; placed on administrative leave; will not return. Police believed additional victims possible at time of first arrest. Second arrest December 2021: improper relationship between educator and student (Texas Penal Code § 21.12). Plea: guilty to both charges. Sentence: 60 days jail distributed over the course of 10 years of probation (i.e., staggered, non-consecutive); SORA registration; $2,000 fine; 500 hours community service; lifetime protective order. Victim's mother addressed Anderson in court per New Braunfels Herald-Zeitung: described son's depression and anxiety; said the 'ideal punishment' would be for Anderson to see her own children endure that pain; revealed she had originally considered going to the school herself ('my instinct was to go and get you out of that school and deal with you myself'). Principal Dustin Davisson notified parents. NOTE: 60 days jail distributed over 10 years of probation for a second-degree felony (sexual assault of a child) is one of the most favorable outcomes in this DB for serious physical conduct — comparable to the weakest Alabama state sentences but for a more serious charged offense. Texas § 22.011 normally carries 2-20 years; this outcome is well below the floor of what the statute contemplates. Classroom closet as venue documents premeditation and use of institutional access. New Braunfels Herald-Zeitung ('zeitung' = German newspaper) — town has German colonial heritage dating to 1845 settlement.
