Former teacher at Andrew Jackson Middle School, Cross Lanes WV (Kanawha County). Also known as Ramsey Carpenter (maiden name). Age 29 at sentencing. Formerly crowned Miss Kentucky 2014; fiddle performance won preliminary talent competition at Miss America national pageant in Atlantic City. Victim: 15-year-old male former student who attended Andrew Jackson from 6th through 8th grade while Bearse was employed there. Sent at least 4 topless photos via Snapchat to the victim. Discovery: victim's parents found the photos on his phone and reported them to police. Arrested December 2018; originally charged with 4 counts sending obscene material to a minor. Pled guilty December 2019 to 1 count possession of material depicting minors in sexually explicit conduct (felony) — charge reduction from 4 counts to 1. At plea hearing, stated the first photo she sent was intended for her husband (claimed accidental initiation). Sentenced July 14, 2020 by Judge Louis H. Bloom: 2 years prison at Lakin Correctional Center (reported July 20, 2020) + 10 years supervised release + lifetime sex offender registration. Defense attorney: Timothy DiPiero. NOTE: Headline jurisdiction is KY (former Miss Kentucky) but case is West Virginia — Bearse competed as Miss Kentucky while residing/working in WV. The charge she pled to — CSAM possession — is technically a self-produced image possession charge, an unusual framing for what is functionally a sexting-to-minor case; likely used because it avoided the 'sending obscene material' counts while still being a registerable felony. 'First photo was intended for husband' defense is the cleanest example of the accidental-initiation claim in this DB — jury would have been skeptical given at least four photos were sent. Snapchat used as in Guess-Mazock #76 and Andrews #77.
