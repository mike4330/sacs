Former intervention prevention assistant at Billinghurst and Swope Middle Schools, Washoe County School District, Reno, Nevada. Age 47 at sentencing. Male victim aged 13 at onset of abuse. Married with two children at time of offense.

Rock began the relationship with the victim in December 2022 via text messages, which progressed to in-person meetings outside of school. Sexual acts occurred multiple times over approximately a year and a half. The victim's mother discovered the relationship by tracking her son's location after he snuck out, which led to a confession. The victim reported the abuse to police, leading to Rock's arrest on June 11, 2024. Rock's contract with the school district had ended the previous day (June 10). Bail was set at $2 million.

Jury trial in January 2026. After approximately six hours of deliberation, the jury returned guilty verdicts on all 19 felony counts: four counts of sexual assault against a child under 14, five counts of statutory sexual seduction, and ten counts of luring using a computer. Prosecutors presented testimony, DNA evidence, and records of repeated contact.

Sentenced April 17, 2026 by Judge Lynne Jones to 144 years to life in prison; no parole eligibility for 144 years. The initial announced sentence of 188 years was corrected to 144 by the DA's office. At sentencing, the victim read a statement: 'I was just a kid, and you knew exactly what you were doing.' The victim's mother also addressed the court. Judge Jones noted Rock had children nearly the same age as the victim.

NOTE: Role as intervention prevention assistant rather than classroom teacher; included for school-employee scope. Abuse onset December 2022 when victim was 13; year-and-a-half duration. 19-count conviction (4 sexual assault of child under 14, 5 statutory sexual seduction, 10 luring via computer) with consecutive sentences on the most serious counts drove the aggregate term. sentence_years = 144 (minimum parole eligibility; functional life sentence for a 47-year-old).
