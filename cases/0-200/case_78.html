Jenny Christine Hipp #78 sentenced

State Mississippi
Court Lamar County Circuit Court
Judge Prentiss G. Harrell
Incident Date 2017
Defendant Age 39
Victim Age 15
Prison Sentence 3.0 yrs

Former speech-language pathologist at Lamar County School District, MS. Employed since 2014; not a classroom teacher. Age 39 at time of offense. Victim: 15-year-old male student. None of the alleged incidents occurred on school grounds. Complaint received by district June 15, 2017; superintendent Tess Smith launched investigation and contacted Lamar County DA's office; Hipp was terminated June 21, 2017. Arrested June 2017 by Jones County authorities on a Lamar County warrant. Pled guilty November 12, 2019 to statutory rape. Sentenced November 21, 2019 by Circuit Court Judge Prentiss G. Harrell: 7 years MDOC — 3 years to serve + 4 years post-release supervision; $2,500 fine + court costs; SORA registration; credit for time served. Post-release: area restriction, 9 p.m.–5 a.m. curfew unless work requires, field officer–supervised program. Defense attorney: Tangi A. Carter (Hattiesburg). NOTE: Speech-language pathologist role is a specialist position with one-on-one student access — structural access opportunity without classroom oversight. ~2.5-year gap from arrest (June 2017) to plea (Nov 2019) typical for Mississippi state court timeline. Sentence is proportional to Tindall #74 (3yr on similar facts) and lighter than Turnage #75 (10yr, trial).
