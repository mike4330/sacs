Former substitute teacher in the Harbor Creek School District (building substitute; previously long-term substitute at Rolling Ridge Elementary School), Harborcreek Township, Erie County, Pennsylvania. Victim was a 17-year-old male student she had previously taught as a substitute; they also worked together at a McDonald's restaurant. Met at the YMCA in Harborcreek Township and exchanged Snapchat information in April 2021.

Age 27 at time of offenses (summer 2021). Alleged sexual contact occurred at Kedzierski's Harborcreek Township residence between June 1 and July 4, 2021 — no allegations of contact at school. Victim visited her residence three or four times; Kedzierski provided cases of beer (valued at $50–$80 each occasion) to the teen. Victim stated they had sex twice during the visits. Kedzierski admitted to oral sex but denied intercourse.

Investigation began June 2, 2022, when state police interviewed the victim at Harbor Creek Senior High School. Charges filed September 2, 2022. Arraigned September 13, 2022 by District Judge Lisa Ferrick; released on own recognizance. Harbor Creek School District removed Kedzierski from its substitute teacher list upon learning of the allegations (Superintendent Kelly Hess).

Original charges: third-degree felony institutional sexual assault of a minor; misdemeanor corruption of minors; furnishing alcohol to minors. Docket CP-25-CR-0002609-2022. All charges resolved to a single count of corruption of minors (M1, 18 Pa.C.S. § 6301(a)(1)(i)) via guilty plea. Sentenced July 31, 2023 by Judge Daniel J. Brabender Jr. to six months probation. Defense attorney: Justin Panighetti.

NOTE: No-prison outcome. Major charge reduction: felony institutional sexual assault dismissed; resolved on misdemeanor corruption of minors only. Off-campus conduct (her residence). Alcohol-for-access grooming pattern. Snapchat as initial communication channel. Victim was 17 — above Pennsylvania general age of consent but below threshold for institutional sexual assault statute.
