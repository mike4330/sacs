Former mathematics teacher at Broussard Middle School, Broussard, Louisiana (Lafayette Parish School System). An eighth-grade teacher and the 2025 Lafayette Parish Teacher of the Year. Age 37 at the time of the documented offense conduct in 2024; age 38 at arrest. The known victim is a juvenile male student of Oster's; specific age not publicly disclosed under Louisiana victim-privacy protections.

State charges. Oster was arrested on January 28, 2026 at Broussard Middle School by the Lafayette Parish Sheriff's Office and charged in Lafayette Parish District Court (15th Judicial District) with indecent behavior with juveniles and carnal knowledge of a juvenile, both Louisiana state felonies. The Lafayette Parish School System placed her on administrative leave the same day.

Federal indictment. On April 15, 2026, a federal grand jury in the Western District of Louisiana returned a one-count indictment charging Oster with enticement of a minor to engage in sexual activity under 18 U.S.C. § 2422(b). The charging instrument places the offense conduct from on or about April 2, 2024 through at least on or about July 23, 2024 — covering most of the spring and summer of 2024. The U.S. Attorney's Office alleges that during that period Oster used her cellphone to hold sexual discussions with the student and instructed the student on how to delete text messages to avoid detection. The case is captioned 6:26-CR-00158-RRS-DJA, assigned to U.S. District Judge Robert R. Summerhays with Magistrate Judge David J. Ayo, and prosecuted by U.S. Attorney Zachary A. Keller. The indictment includes a forfeiture notice covering any property used or derived from proceeds.

Detention. On April 23, 2026, following a detention hearing, Magistrate Judge Ayo ordered Oster detained pending trial. The Government invoked both the minor-victim ground under 18 U.S.C. § 3142(f)(1) and the flight- and obstruction-risk grounds under § 3142(f)(2). A rebuttable presumption arose under § 3142(e)(3) for offenses involving a minor victim under § 2422; the court found Oster rebutted that presumption but nevertheless concluded by clear and convincing evidence that no condition or combination of release conditions would reasonably assure community safety. The order's checked reasons cite the minor-victim element, the strong weight of evidence, the lengthy potential incarceration, and the serious danger her release would pose. Oster was remanded to the custody of the Attorney General.

Public-attention context. Oster's recognition as the 2025 Lafayette Parish Teacher of the Year drew significant local media coverage of the arrest and federal indictment. The federal indictment was reported in the regional press as part of a wave of seven recent Acadiana-area federal child-exploitation cases.
