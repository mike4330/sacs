Shelley Dufresne #59 sentenced

State Louisiana
Incident Date 2014-09-01
Defendant Age 32
Victim Age 16
Prison Sentence 0.0 yrs

Former English teacher at Destrehan High School, St. Charles Parish, Louisiana. Daughter of Judge Emile St. Pierre (29th Judicial District). Victim: 16-year-old male student.

Two separate incident jurisdictions. St. Charles Parish: unspecified date in September 2014, location disputed. Jefferson Parish: September 12, 2014 (Dufresne's birthday), group sex with the victim at co-teacher Rachel Respess's Kenner apartment. Respess, 24, was charged in Jefferson Parish with failure to report a crime; that charge was later dropped.

All three St. Charles Parish judges recused themselves. The Louisiana Supreme Court appointed retired Judge Anne Lennan Simon (16th JDC). Dufresne pleaded guilty in St. Charles on April 10, 2015 to a reduced charge of obscenity: $1,000 fine, 90 days in a mental health facility, 3 years probation, no jail, no sex offender registration. After leaving court she posted a smiling selfie on social media captioned "my mood today" with three smiling emojis.

In Jefferson Parish, Dufresne was charged with two counts of carnal knowledge of a juvenile. Judge Danyelle Taylor (24th JDC) acquitted in April 2017 on grounds of insufficient evidence that the acts occurred in Jefferson Parish — a jurisdictional finding, not a factual one. Jefferson Parish DA: Paul Connick (son of former Orleans Parish DA Harry Connick Sr., brother of entertainer Harry Connick Jr.). The Respess charge was then dropped.

NOTE: One of the most consequential plea deals in this DB — carnal knowledge (a felony) reduced to obscenity, with no incarceration and no SORA registration. The father's judicial position almost certainly contributed to the recusal cascade and may have influenced the plea offer. The social media post after the plea is remarkable and documented. The Jefferson Parish acquittal was purely jurisdictional — prosecution conceded the relationship occurred and only contested the location. Group sex on the defendant's own birthday with a co-worker and their shared student is unusual even by the standards of this DB.
