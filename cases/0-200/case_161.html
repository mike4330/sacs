Giovanna Yaranga #161 sentenced

StateVermontCourtChittenden Superior Court
JudgeKevin GriffinIncident Date2012-10-01
Defendant Age43Victim Age17
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former Spanish teacher at Burlington High School, Burlington School District, Burlington, Vermont. DOB November 19, 1968. Victim was a male high school student (initials R.B.). Sexual relationship involved four distinct incidents between October 2012 and March 2013, documented separately in the charging information.

Age 43 at time of first offense (October 2012); age 44 at arrest and plea (2013). Discovered April 29, 2013 when police found them together in a vehicle behind a building. Arrested April 29, 2013. Required to surrender passport at arraignment.

Arraigned May 6, 2013 before Judge Kevin Griffin on four felony counts of Sexual Exploitation of a Minor (13 V.S.A. § 3258(c)). Violated court-ordered no-contact conditions and was re-arrested May 27, 2013. On September 6, 2013, all four counts were amended to misdemeanor Sexual Exploitation of a Minor (13 V.S.A. § 3258(b)) and Yaranga pleaded guilty to all four. Judge Kevin Griffin sentenced her the same day: Counts 1–2 received 6–12 months all suspended; Counts 3–4 received 0–12 months all suspended. All counts concurrent. Four years probation expiring September 6, 2017. Sex offender registration required (Vermont VCIC/SOR). Probation conditions included no contact with R.B., no contact with minor males without PO permission (incidental public contact permissible), counseling, and school avoidance.

Yaranga filed three motions for early discharge from probation (October 2015, October 2016, July 2017), all denied. Her stated motivation was to return to Peru to visit family. Probation expired September 6, 2017 by natural term. Passport was returned September 20, 2013. Prosecutor: Susan Hardin. Defense attorney: James W. Murdoch (withdrew October 2015).

NOTE: Case resolved in approximately four months from arraignment — the 'five-year case' narrative in earlier press coverage was incorrect; the docket confirms guilty plea September 6, 2013. Felony-to-misdemeanor reduction (3258(c) to 3258(b)) was the key disposition. Victim age 17 is above Vermont age of consent (16) but the educator exploitation statute applies regardless. Three denied early discharge motions suggest active resistance from prosecution to any leniency. sentence_years = 0 (all suspended).
