Former English teacher at Dowling Catholic High School, West Des Moines, Iowa. Age 36 at time of offense.

Victim was a 17-year-old male student with a difficult home life who had confided in Gantt. Contact began December 2022 via personal email, school-issued email, and Instagram. Escalating messages across platforms led to physical contact on at least five occasions in Gantt's classroom after school hours, February–March 2023. Evidence included a shirtless photo from the student with Gantt's reply, messages scheduling outside-school meetings, and personal journal entries.

An anonymous tip led Dowling to place Gantt on administrative leave; school contacted West Des Moines PD. A search warrant yielded journals, computers, Fitbit, Kindle, letters, and school devices. Turned herself in April 14, 2023. Charged with felony sexual exploitation and aggravated misdemeanor sexual contact by school employee (Iowa Code § 709.15). Pled guilty February 8, 2024 to one count of sexual contact by school employee (aggravated misdemeanor). Sentenced February 28, 2024 to 2 years; no-contact order until February 28, 2029; sex offender registration; DNA sample required.

NOTE: Original felony reduced to aggravated misdemeanor at plea — a significant downgrade in Iowa's § 709.15 framework. Victim came to Gantt for support due to a difficult home life; pastoral exploitation pattern. Dual-channel communication (school email for plausible deniability; personal/social media for escalation) documented in search warrant evidence.
