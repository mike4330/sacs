Former math teacher at Tenaya Middle School, Fresno Unified School District, Fresno, California. Age approximately 30 at time of offense; 33 at trial. Male victim aged 14 at time of convicted offense (8th grade); victim alleged acts began at age 13 but jury acquitted on that count.

Incidents occurred March through May 2016. Nelson orally copulated the student in her classroom. She also sent the victim explicit text messages, nude photos, and discussed giving him money or clothing while making sure his mother and her husband did not find out. Nelson was married with a child. During police interviews, Nelson joked about 'hooking up with a 14-year-old.'

Arrested 2016; teaching credential suspended then revoked January 2019. At trial, Nelson admitted to the acts but claimed she was coerced: defense attorney Roger Nuttall argued the student was 'the real predator' who threatened to expose their relationship if she did not comply, and presented psychologist testimony that Nelson was not a sexual predator. The jury rejected the coercion argument on the age-14 count (guilty: oral copulation of a minor) but acquitted on the age-13 count (lewd or lascivious act with a minor). Sentence June 13, 2019: 1 year in jail, 4 years formal probation, lifetime sex offender registration. Judge Kristi Culver Kapetan: 'Parents entrust their children to teachers to guide them. Not to prey on them and corrupt them.' Prosecutor Liz Owen. Defense attorney Roger Nuttall sought probation with no jail time.

NOTE: The extortion-counter-claim defense — that the student threatened exposure to coerce the sex acts — was explicitly rejected by the prosecution: 'There is no evidence of a threat.' Judge noted the relationship trajectory: victim initially sought basketball conversation and validation; Nelson 'responded with sexy pictures, talk of partying and complaining about her marriage.' Civil lawsuit filed by victim's family. Victim age for purposes of conviction is 14; the 13-count acquittal prevented a mandatory felony strike and capped sentence at 3 years.
