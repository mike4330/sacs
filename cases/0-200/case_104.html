Former math teacher at Yucaipa High School, Yucaipa, California (San Bernardino County). Named Yucaipa High School "Teacher of the Year" in 2017. Age 38 at arrest. Male victim aged 16.

Arrested May 19, 2023 on suspicion of unlawful sexual intercourse with a 16-year-old male student. Charges filed April 14, 2023 included arranging a meeting with a minor for sexual interest (PC 288.4(b)) and unlawful sexual intercourse with a minor more than three years younger (PC 261.5(c)). Booked into Central Detention Center, San Bernardino, in lieu of $30,000 bail.

Pleaded not guilty on count 1 (June 4, 2024). On August 27, 2024, pleaded guilty to count 2 (unlawful sexual intercourse, PC 261.5(c)); count 1 dismissed pursuant to plea. Sentenced October 3, 2024: formal felony probation 3 years (to October 2, 2027); 180 days county jail, eligible for weekend/work release program, with 1 day credit. Sentence modified April 4, 2025 to reflect 50 days credit for time served. Change of venue entered October 24, 2025.

NOTE: Weekend/work release jail eligibility means no significant custody served. "Teacher of the Year" 2017 background is notable context. Investigation conducted jointly by San Bernardino County Sheriff's Department and Yucaipa-Calimesa Joint Unified School District.
