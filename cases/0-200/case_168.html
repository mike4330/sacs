Former physical education teacher at Plainfield North High School, Plainfield Community Consolidated School District 202, Plainfield IL. Employed there since 2006. Age 27 at arrest; 30 at sentencing. Had sex with 16yo male junior student in the back seat of a car in the parking lot of the Kohl's on Route 59, Plainfield, on January 4, 2011. The two had texted each other to arrange the rendezvous. Discovered when Plainfield police officers noticed two cars parked alone and investigated; found Blumenshine in the driver seat and the student in the back seat partially dressed. Both admitted to sex.

Arrested January 5, 2011 on charge of aggravated criminal sexual abuse. Prosecution later dropped that charge and replaced it with traveling to meet a minor (Class 3 felony). Sex offender evaluation showed 'very low risk' of reoffending. Pleaded guilty April 19, 2013; sentenced same day to 30 days in jail (reduced to 15 days by day-for-day credit under Illinois law; 4 days already served pre-bond, 11 days served at sentencing), 2 years sex offender probation, and placement on Illinois Sex Offender Registry for 10 years. Defense attorney George Lenard; prosecutor Assistant State's Attorney Frank Byers.
