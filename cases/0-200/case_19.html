State v. Stephanie Ragusa #19 sentenced

StateFloridaCourtHillsborough County Circuit Court
JudgeChet Tharpe Incident Date2008-01-01
Defendant Age31 Victim Age14
Prison Sentence10.0 yrs

Math teacher at Davidsen Middle School, Tampa, Florida. Also served as tutor to victim 2.

Two victims, both male students. Victim 1 was 14 years old; Ragusa had sex with him at least 3 times between January and May 2007. Victim 2 was 15 years old; relationship began February 15, 2007 at Ragusa's home and continued with sex more than 20 times through March 10, 2008 — three days before her first arrest. Ragusa initiated contact with victim 2 on March 10 via text message. Victim 2 identified Ragusa by describing tattoos only visible when she was undressed.

Arrested March 13, 2008, on 5 counts of lewd and lascivious battery (victim 1); posted $12,500 bail and released March 18. Arrested again April 15, 2008, on 2 counts of sex with a minor and 1 count of lewd and lascivious battery (victim 2), after investigators identified victim 2 from her seized cell phone.

Pleaded guilty April 2010 to 3 counts lewd and lascivious battery (victim 1) and 2 counts unlawful sex with a minor plus 1 count lewd and lascivious battery (victim 2). Sentenced June 28, 2010, by Judge Chet Tharpe: 10 years prison + 5 years sex offender probation. Designated sexual predator.

NOTE: Judge Tharpe cited Ragusa's arrogance in court appearances as an aggravating factor. District spokesman Steve Hegarty described the second arrest as "shocking — again." Both victims described as being from broken homes.
