Former reading specialist at Menomonee Falls High School, Menomonee Falls, Wisconsin. Age 30 at time of offense. Male victim age 16, nearly 17 at time of offense; described as 6-foot and 'very mature.' Relationship began October 2015, discovered December 11, 2015 when another teacher saw them kissing in her classroom; all sexual contact occurred in classroom during school day, up to ~12 times. Gifts to victim included shoes (transactional grooming). Originally charged December 2015 with 12 counts sexual assault of a student by school staff; bail reduced from $100k to $10k + $25k signature bond. Husband filed for legal separation at time of arrest. Pleaded no contest to 3 of 12 counts April 2017. Sentenced June 29, 2017 by Judge Ralph Ramirez: 2 years prison + 3 years supervised release; sex offender registration; prohibited from any school employment or volunteering. Bail jumping charge (harassment at ex-husband's residence) dismissed at sentencing. Defense argued Novak 'was the prey not the hunter'; treating psychiatrist testified she is not a pedophile; victim's grandmother disputed this, noting gifts-for-sex dynamic and that victim's family moved out of state. NOTE: All encounters occurred exclusively on campus in classroom; prior employment at Brown Deer School District (basketball coach). Novak showed suicidal ideation at initial arrest (protective vest at first appearance).
