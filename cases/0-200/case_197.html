Former physical education teacher and basketball coach at Frederick County Middle School, Frederick County, Virginia. Male victim, age 12 when contact began and 13 during the relationship. Defendant age 24 at arrest.

Texts numbered in the thousands and began when the boy was 12, escalating after his 13th birthday. Walker also coached the victim in basketball. Walker and the boy kissed on school property during the school day on February 18, 2023. Discussions in messages referenced potential future intercourse, touching of body parts, and Judge Iden noted his interpretation that they were considering exchanging pornographic videos. Walker urged the boy to delete the messages. The boy's mother became suspicious in early March 2023, took the boy's phone, read the messages, and notified the school on March 13, 2023. Frederick County Sheriff's Office contacted March 13; Walker turned herself in and was arrested March 23, 2023. Held without bond at Northwestern Regional Adult Detention Center.

Pleaded guilty January 2024 in Frederick County Circuit Court to felony taking indecent liberties with a minor under age 15. Sentenced by Judge Alexander Iden: 5 years with all but 15 months suspended. Two years supervised probation; barred from all FCPS property; no unsupervised contact with minors outside immediate family; no computer, smartphone, or social media without probation officer approval. Required to register as sex offender with Virginia State Police. Ordered to pay $872 restitution to victim's family. Credit for time served from March 23, 2023.
