Former teacher at Encore High School for the Arts, Riverside, California (Riverside County). Age 31 at time of offense; 32 at arrest. Resident of Corona at the time.

Female victim age 14, with a second female student involved on a misdemeanor count. The relationship lasted several months, with communication including text messages. Arrested February 6, 2017 at school on suspicion of lewd or lascivious acts with a minor and annoying or molesting a child under 18; the victim's mother had contacted police the prior weekend. School officials "fully cooperated" with the investigation.

Pleaded guilty May 17, 2019 — two years after arrest — to one felony count of contacting a child with intent to commit a crime plus two misdemeanor counts of child molestation. Sentenced same day to 120 days jail on weekends, sex offender registration, and sex offender classes. No prison time.

The victim's mother subsequently filed a civil suit against Riverside Unified School District alleging failure to protect, citing an unanswered email she had sent the school about her daughter's excessive tardies.

NOTE: Female-victim, female-defendant case. Weekend-only jail is effectively a no-prison outcome for a felony plus two misdemeanors. The two-year gap between arrest and plea is unusual and not explained in available coverage. The civil suit alleging the school ignored a tardy-notification signal makes this a notable institutional-failure case — the misdemeanor "child molestation" plea is to a non-contact statute (annoying/molesting) so contact_type is coded 999 despite the months-long relationship pattern.
