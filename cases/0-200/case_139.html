Former 5th-grade teacher at Farmersville Elementary School, Mount Vernon, Indiana (Posey County Schools). Age approximately 44 at time of offense; 45 at sentencing. Rodgers was not the victim's classroom teacher.

Rodgers had a roughly year-long sexual relationship with a 17-year-old female high school student. She was tutoring the victim in biology and assisted in coaching track while the girl was on the team. The victim's mother was friends with Rodgers, and Rodgers lived in the victim's home for approximately two months during the summer preceding the charges. The mother became aware of the relationship after seeing a photograph of Rodgers kissing her daughter. Rodgers had also written the victim a letter; investigating officer Candace Rueger testified that Rodgers knew the victim had lost someone close and believed she exploited that vulnerability.

Rodgers resigned from Farmersville Elementary in December 2017, approximately one month before a probable cause affidavit was filed in Posey County Circuit Court on January 5, 2018. Bond was set at $15,000 cash. She turned herself in to Posey County Jail before the sentencing hearing.

Rodgers pleaded guilty as charged to one count of child seduction (Level 5 felony) and one count of child seduction (Level 6 felony) under Indiana law. No plea agreement limited the sentence or required probation. On June 28, 2018, Judge James Redwine sentenced her to 3 years Indiana DOC: 1 year to serve in prison, 2 years probation. Sex offender registration required for 10 years. Redwine cited her duty as a teacher as an aggravating factor, but also her lack of criminal history and admission of responsibility. Prosecutor Travis Clowers sought immediate custody; Redwine instead allowed Rodgers several days to arrange her affairs before reporting Saturday.

NOTE: Female victim. Victim testified voluntarily that the relationship was mutual and that she did not want Rodgers incarcerated. Grooming vector: emotional exploitation of grief, shared household, tutoring/coaching access — a combination of trust, proximity, and emotional manipulation. Defense attorney Douglas Walton subpoenaed the victim to testify, which prosecutor Clowers objected to as unnecessary.
