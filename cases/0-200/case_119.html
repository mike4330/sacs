Former fifth-grade teacher at an elementary school in Hudson, Wisconsin (St. Croix County). Age 24 at charge. Male victim; age approximately 11 (5th grade, per CLAUDE.md inference).

Faust is accused of keeping the student after class, pushing him against the wall, and kissing him on the cheek. Court documents also show that Faust and fellow Hudson 5th-grade teacher Madison Bergmann (#22) were friends, and that the two had exchanged text messages about their respective relationships with students.

The Hudson School District learned of concerns in early May 2024. Faust resigned in early May 2024 and was immediately removed from the classroom; the district investigated her for alleged failure to report child abuse as required by state law. Charges were filed August 2025. Charged with four felonies: failure to protect a child, causing mental harm to a child, child enticement, and sexual misconduct by school staff. Released on a $10,000 signature bond. Next court date October 2025.

NOTE: The 'failure to protect a child' and 'failure to report' framing suggests Faust may have had knowledge of Bergmann's misconduct with a student and failed to report it as a mandated reporter, in addition to her own conduct. The text exchanges about their respective student relationships are a significant evidentiary link. Cross-reference Bergmann #22 (same school, same grade, same arrest period, friends).
