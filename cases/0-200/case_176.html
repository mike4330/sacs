Former math teacher (algebra, geometry, pre-calculus) and assistant track coach at Aplington-Parkersburg High School, Aplington-Parkersburg Community School District, Parkersburg, Butler County, Iowa. Began teaching in 2010. Age 24 at time of charges.

Four male student victims, ages 16 to 18, during the 2011 to 2012 school year. Conduct ranged from sending nude photographs and explicit texts to sexual intercourse with one victim. Anderson resigned before being charged in fall 2012.

Turned herself in to the Butler County Sheriff. Admitted to the relationships during a police interview, and admitted sending nude photographs and texts to all four victims. Charged with four counts of sexual exploitation by a school employee, a Class D felony. In April 2013, pleaded guilty to three counts; the count involving kissing and touching only was dismissed at plea, per the AG. Sentenced June 17, 2013 by Judge Christopher Foy in Butler County to 5 years in prison on each count, all concurrent. Sex offender registration was required upon release, plus 10 years of probation. Likely to serve no more than 2.5 years under Iowa's good-time statute (1.2 days credit per day served). Iowa AG Scott Brown prosecuted; superintendent Jon Thompson.
