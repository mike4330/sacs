Substitute teacher (via Source4Teachers staffing agency) at Easton Area High School, Northampton County, Pennsylvania. Resident of Pen Argyl, Pennsylvania. Age 49 at time of offense.

Male victim; the relationship began in approximately fall 2015 when the victim was 15. The victim's age was initially reported as 16–17 in early press coverage. The true starting age of 15 emerged only at a subsequent parole hearing, indicating the original investigation underestimated the relationship's duration or onset. Police discovered the relationship on May 18, 2016 when an officer on routine patrol encountered Aldinger and the victim together in a vehicle in Easton Cemetery. Aldinger admitted to multiple sexual encounters since the prior fall. No other victims were identified.

Released on $50,000 bail. Source4Teachers suspended her indefinitely. Charged with institutional sexual assault. Pled guilty; sentenced to 3–23 months in Northampton County Jail. In May 2017, Aldinger violated parole by contacting the victim after release; her defense attorney described the contact as a text sent "just to see if he was OK." Re-sentenced May 26, 2017 to 18 months to 3 years. Defense attorney: Philip Lauer.
