Emily Michelle Rogers #140 closed

StateTexasCourtComal County District Court
Incident Date2014-12-25
Defendant Age22Victim Age18
Prison Sentence0.0 yrs

Former social studies teacher and assistant coach for girls volleyball and soccer at Smithson Valley High School, Comal Independent School District, Spring Branch, Texas. Age 22 at time of offense; 25 at conviction. Rogers began at Smithson Valley in August 2014.

Rogers became romantically and sexually involved with an 18-year-old female student in late 2014 and early 2015. The victim testified to numerous sexual encounters including one on Christmas 2014. Drug use was a documented component: the victim testified they took Xanax at school, and smoked marijuana and snorted cocaine on off-campus excursions. The relationship was discovered when the victim began dating another student, who discovered the affair and notified police. Comal County deputies began investigating in April 2015; Rogers resigned from Comal ISD the same month. The relationship reportedly continued even after Rogers' April 2015 arrest.

Rogers was tried by jury in Comal County in November 2017. The jury deliberated approximately one hour before returning unanimous guilty verdicts on two counts of Improper Relationship Between Educator and Student (TX Penal Code §21.12, 2nd degree felony), each carrying up to 20 years. Defense attorney John Kuntz argued there was no proof of sex, only emotional attachment. Rogers did not testify. The former student testified she had loved Rogers and described the relationship. On November 3, 2017, Rogers was sentenced to 10 years probation on each count (concurrent), no prison, and a $2,500 fine per count.

NOTE: Adult victim (18 at offense) — TX §21.12 makes the educator-student relationship criminal regardless of victim age. Female victim. Drug use (Xanax, marijuana, cocaine) is an unusual documented grooming/relationship factor. Same county (Comal) as case #137 (Massey). Cross-case: no-prison outcome despite jury conviction on two 2nd-degree felonies.
