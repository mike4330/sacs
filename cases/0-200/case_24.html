State v. McKenna Kindred #24 sentenced

State Washington
Court Spokane County Superior Court
Incident Date 2022-06-01
Defendant Age 24
Victim Age 16
Prison Sentence 0.0 yrs

High school teacher at Central Valley High School, Spokane, Washington. Married to Kyle Kindred (later an associate attorney at Hart & Hart, Boise). Age 25 at sentencing; estimated 24 at time of offense.

Grooming began when the 16-year-old student discovered Kindred's Instagram account in June 2022, leading to exchanges of messages, photos, and videos. In mid-November 2022, while Kyle was on a hunting trip, Kindred invited the teen (now 17) to her home for a hours-long sexual encounter. She subsequently suggested using Snapchat to hide communications. Screenshots of Instagram messages — including 'when other girls talk about you in my class I could feel myself getting mad' and 'I really like being touched by you' — were shared with school officials by the teen's friends in December 2022. Kindred proactively reported to the principal, assistant principal, and school resource officer that she was being harassed online, before the investigation had begun. Placed on administrative leave; resigned 2023. Surrendered teaching license August 2024 amid an Idaho Standards Commission probe.

Charged with first-degree sexual misconduct and communication with a minor for immoral purposes. Pleaded guilty March 2024 to amended charges of second-degree sexual misconduct with a minor and communication with a minor for immoral purposes. Sentenced to 2 years probation, $700 fine, 10-year sex offender registration. No prison.

Couple relocated to Kuna, Idaho (suburb of Boise) in June 2024, purchasing a home for just under $500,000. Kyle Kindred attended Gonzaga University and SMU Dedman School of Law; remained with defendant throughout.

NOTE: Victim's mother stated grooming began when victim was 16. Defendant pre-empted investigation by self-reporting 'harassment' to school administrators. Charge reduced from first-degree to second-degree sexual misconduct at plea. sentence_years=0 per no-prison convention. Husband publicly supportive throughout proceedings.
