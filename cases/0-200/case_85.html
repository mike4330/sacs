Former teacher of anatomy, physiology, and environmental systems at Lockhart High School, Lockhart, Texas (Caldwell County, ~35 miles southeast of Austin). Previously taught at Plum Creek Elementary. Age 27 at arrest.

Victim was a 17-year-old male student. Arrested March 20, 2017 by Lockhart Police on a charge of engaging in sexual contact with a student. Lockhart ISD suspended her, and Lockhart PD stated this was their first investigation of teacher-student relationship allegations.

Her mugshot, in which she was smiling broadly, circulated internationally. Defense attorney Jason Nassour initially attributed the smile to her belief in her own innocence.

Pleaded guilty October 10, 2018 in a conditional plea submitted to Caldwell County District Court. Terms: 4 years deferred adjudication (probation) and teacher's license surrender. Judge Chris Schneider reviewed the proposed package; per available reporting, the plea was accepted.

NOTE: Deferred adjudication in Texas means no final conviction enters if probation is completed successfully — the most favorable possible legal outcome short of acquittal. The smiling mugshot became a recurring media reference throughout the case and is one of the most-circulated images associated with any case in this DB. The "smiling because she's innocent" framing followed by a guilty plea is a documented reversal. No-prison outcome (deferred adjudication = probation only) is consistent with the Texas pattern for first-offense plea cases involving 17-year-old victims.
