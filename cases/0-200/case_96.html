Jennifer Caswell #96 sentenced

Former middle school English teacher at Hollis Public Schools, Hollis, Oklahoma (Harmon County). Age 28 at time of offense. Male victim, age 15, eighth grade.

Sexual contact occurred across multiple locations: her classroom, the victim's home, her car, her home, and a Mississippi hotel room where police found them together at arrest. Two witnesses observed Caswell having sex with the victim inside her classroom. School officials declined to report to police and instead allowed her to resign in April 2014 while permitting her to retain her teaching credentials.

Arrested June 2014. Pleaded guilty June 2015 to three counts of second-degree rape, two counts of enticing a child, and one count of forcible sodomy. Sentenced August 13, 2015: 15 years with 5 suspended on each of 6 counts, concurrent — 10 years to serve; minimum 8.5 years before parole eligibility under statutory minimums. Defense attorney David Cummins stated the sentence was "at least twice" what was anticipated.

In May 2015, prior to sentencing, Caswell appeared on Dr. Phil and admitted to classroom sex with the victim. She described the relationship as "stupid," claimed it was consensual, and stated "I'm not a monster and I'm not a predator."

The victim's father filed a civil suit in June 2015 against the school district and Caswell. Hollis Public Schools settled in July 2017 for $125,000, denying wrongdoing.

NOTE: School officials had direct witness accounts of a classroom assault and chose cover-up over reporting — allowed resignation while preserving credentials.
