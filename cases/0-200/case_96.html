Jennifer Caswell #96 sentenced

StateOklahomaCourtHarmon County District Court
Incident Date2013-09-01
Defendant Age28 Victim Age15
Prison Sentence10.0 yrs

Former middle school English teacher at Hollis Public Schools, Hollis, Oklahoma (Harmon County, southwestern OK). Age 28 at time of offense. Male victim age 15, in eighth grade. Sexual contact occurred in her classroom, student's home, her car, her home, and a Mississippi hotel room where police found them together at arrest. Two witnesses saw Caswell having sex with victim inside her classroom; school officials declined to report to police and instead allowed her to resign in April 2014 while retaining her teaching credentials. Arrested June 2014. Pleaded guilty June 2015 to 3 counts second-degree rape, 2 counts enticing a child, 1 count forcible sodomy. Sentenced August 13, 2015 by Harmon County district judge: 15 years with 5 suspended on each of 6 counts concurrent = 10 years; minimum 8.5 years before parole eligibility per statutory minimums. Defense attorney David Cummins said sentence was 'at least twice' what was anticipated. Victim's father filed civil suit June 2015 against school district and Caswell; Hollis Public Schools settled July 2017 for $125,000; school denied wrongdoing. Caswell appeared on Dr. Phil in May 2015, admitted to classroom sex with student, called relationship 'stupid' but claimed it was consensual: 'I'm not a monster and I'm not a predator.' Blamed failing marriage. Incarcerated at Mabel Bassett Correctional Center (Oklahoma). NOTE: School administration covered up abuse after witnesses saw classroom assault — textbook prior-warning-ignored. Allowing resignation while preserving credentials is a recurring institutional failure pattern. Caswell's Dr. Phil appearance and public denial/minimization is notable.
