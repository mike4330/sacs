Brianne Altice #81 sentenced

StateUtahCourtDavis County
Incident Date2013
Defendant Age33Victim Age16
Prison Sentence4.0 yrs

Former English teacher at Davis High School, Draper, Utah (Davis County). Age 33 at arrest. Three teenage male victims: one age 16 and two age 17. One relationship continued while Altice was out on bail after her initial arrest.

The relationship was an open secret at school. Parents' civil lawsuits noted the running joke at Davis High was "Who is Ms. Altice sleeping with now?" — a documented prior-warning-ignored pattern. Access vector: students often confided personal problems to her, which she used as emotional entry points. Arrested December 2013. Pleaded guilty in 2015 to 3 counts of forcible sexual abuse of a student (Utah Code § 76-5-404). Sentenced July 2015 to an indeterminate 2-30 years in prison.

At sentencing: "If I could, I would personally apologize to each individual who has been negatively affected by this. I take responsibility for what I've done." The Davis County Attorney's Office said she had "layers of excuses" and was "not taking responsibility." Parole denied January 2017; at that hearing: "I had extreme self-esteem issues and they said things that made me feel good about myself. I just started to justify it all." Parole board vice chair Robert Yeates noted guidelines indicated she should remain until 2020. Granted parole April 2019; released September 10, 2019, approximately four years after sentencing. Conditions: 2-year sex offender treatment program; restitution via victim counseling; lifetime teaching ban. Civil lawsuits filed against Davis County School District.

NOTE: Continuing offenses while on bail is the most reckless post-arrest behavior in this DB. The contradiction between the 2015 sentencing statement ("I take responsibility") and the 2017 parole testimony ("low self-esteem, they made me feel good") is well-documented and mirrors the DA's "layers of excuses" characterization. The open-secret/running-joke pattern makes this a prior-warning-ignored case where the school environment itself normalized the behavior. Four years served on a 2-30 year sentence is lenient given three victims and a bail-period offense.
