Former English teacher at Kennedale High School, Kennedale, Texas (near Arlington, Tarrant County). Age 27-28 at time of offense.

Five male victims, all age 18 (legal adults but current students). Sexual encounters over approximately two months in 2011 at Colleps' Arlington home while her husband Christopher Colleps was serving in the military overseas. Encounters included group sex with multiple students simultaneously. The relationship began with sexually explicit text messages for some students; others were invited to her home for dinner before group sexual activity. A cellphone video of one multi-student encounter was recorded by a student, surfaced, and led to prosecution. Colleps was married with three children at the time.

Convicted August 17, 2012 by a jury in less than one hour on all 16 counts of improper relationship between educator and student (TX Penal Code § 21.12, 2nd-degree felony, 2-20 years per count). Sentenced the same day to 5 years in prison. Remained in Tarrant County Jail pending appeal; appeal denied May 2014; transferred to state prison. Parole-eligible as early as March 2013 under Texas law; the parole board reviewed the case approximately three months after her prison transfer and granted release December 2014 (roughly 2.5 years post-conviction). Mandatory supervised parole ran through August 2017 with no special parole restrictions. Not required to register as a sex offender because all victims were adults. Husband remained with her. Three students testified they did not consider themselves victims and opposed prosecution.

NOTE: The crime is the institutional teacher-student relationship, not age of consent — all five victims were 18. No sex-offender registration required because victims were of legal age. Group sex with multiple simultaneous students in a single encounter is highly unusual in this DB. Parole eligibility seven months post-conviction reflects TX law on non-violent offenders, not administrative leniency.
