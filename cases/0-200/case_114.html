Former special education teacher and volunteer soccer coach at Downers Grove South High School, Downers Grove, Illinois (Community High School District 99). Age approximately 27 at onset of abuse; 30 at arrest. Male victim aged approximately 13 at start of abuse (15 at time of reporting in March 2025). Victim was Formella's soccer coaching charge and tutoring student.

Abuse allegedly began in January 2023 and continued through August or September 2024 — approximately 50 sexual acts total, with at least 45 occurring at the school and additional incidents at Formella's home. Formella also sent multiple grooming text messages. The victim's mother discovered the abuse by looking through his cell phone; the victim and his parents reported to Downers Grove Police on March 15, 2025. Further disclosure occurred April 17, 2025 when the victim and parents returned to the DuPage County Children's Advocacy Center.

Formella was arrested during a traffic stop on March 16, 2025 and initially charged with two counts of aggravated criminal sexual abuse and one count of criminal sexual assault. The court denied the state's motion to detain her pretrial; she was released with conditions barring contact with the victim, minors, and the school. On May 20, 2025, a DuPage County grand jury returned an indictment adding 52 additional counts: 20 criminal sexual assault (Class 1), 20 aggravated criminal sexual abuse (Class 1), 6 indecent solicitation of a child (Class 3), and 6 grooming (Class 4). A second petition to deny pretrial release was again denied. Next court date: August 4, 2025.
