Daena Coreas Delgado #199 sentenced

StateTexasCourtMatagorda County District Court
Incident Date2021-09-11
Defendant Age30 Victim Age13
Prison Sentence8.0 yrs

Former teacher at Bay City Independent School District, Bay City, Matagorda County, Texas. Female victim (former student), age under 14. Defendant age 30 at time of offense; 31 at arrest.

Investigation began October 2021. Initial reports indicate defendant assaulted the victim and was initially investigated for injury to a child. Further investigation revealed an improper educator-student relationship and online solicitation of the same victim. Arrest warrant executed August 25, 2022 by Sgt. Minerva Pena at Municipal Court. Booked into Matagorda County Jail. Detectives identified one victim.

Both charges (TX §21.12 and TX §33.021(f)) carry second-degree felony level. Pleaded guilty to both counts May 29, 2025 before Judge Denise M. Fortenberry. Sentenced to 8 years Texas Department of Criminal Justice on each count, concurrent, commencing May 29, 2025. One day jail credit.

NOTE: 8-year actual TDCJ sentence distinguishes this from the Texas deferred adjudication pattern (cases #186, #187, #196). Online solicitation of minor under 14 (§33.021(f)) is a more serious charge than standard §21.12 — reflects victim age under 14. Victim age approximated as 13 (under 14 per statute).
