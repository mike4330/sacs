Former teacher at Huntington High School, Huntington ISD, Huntington TX. Also identified as Melissa Beth Williams. Resident of Conroe at time of revocation. Age approximately 40 at start of offense; 41 at arrest; 42 at initial sentencing; 46 at probation revocation. Improper sexual relationship with 17yo male student in 2007–2008; discovered after a tip to Child Protective Services, who notified Huntington Police Department. Though 17 is the age of consent in Texas, the 2003 educator sexual misconduct statute (Texas Penal Code §21.12) criminalizes any sexual contact between a teacher and student regardless of age.

Turned herself in April 11, 2008. Pleaded guilty January 2009 to improper relationship between educator and student (felony). Initial sentence: 6 months in jail (as probation condition) + 10 years probation + sex offender treatment.

Probation violations (2010): possessing pornography, having direct contact with a child under 17, associating with a known felon, possessing alcohol — addressed through additional probation terms rather than revocation.

Arrested again September 17, 2012 (turned herself in) after probation officer filed violation report. New violations included: discharged from sex offender treatment program for failing multiple polygraph tests; accessing internet without officer permission; and associating with Daniel McGinty, 26, a registered sex offender then wanted on felony revocation warrants for drug possession and failure to register. McGinty had been convicted at age 15 for aggravated sexual assault of a 9-year-old girl. Probation revoked October 18, 2012; sentenced to 8 years in prison.

Williams's 2010 violations — including contact with a child under 17 — were resolved without revocation; the 2012 violations led to full revocation.
