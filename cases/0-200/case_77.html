Annaleigh Andrews #77 sentenced

State Texas
Court 420th District Court, Nacogdoches County
Judge Malcolm Bales
Incident Date 2023
Defendant Age 26
Prison Sentence 2.0 yrs

Former teacher at McMichael Middle School, Nacogdoches ISD, Nacogdoches TX (deep East Texas). Victim: middle school child (gender not specified in reporting). Grooming: Snapchat messaging, marijuana use, took victim to a home the child described as 'a mansion.' Andrews asked victim to lie to their mother about whereabouts; separately lied to the mother herself, claiming she had taken the child to work on a project. Sexually assaulted the child multiple times. Arrested July 20, 2023; charged with 12 counts: 3x improper relationship between educator and student, 3x sexual assault of a child, 3x trafficking a child to engage in sexual conduct, 3x enticing a child with intent to commit a felony. Bench trial before Judge Malcolm Bales; convicted on 7 of 12 charges April 8, 2025. Sentence: two concurrent 2-year terms, $5,000 fine, lifetime sex offender registration. Received 3 months credit for time served in county jail. Self-surrendered April 24, 2025; booked into Gregg County Jail, Longview. On the stand, Andrews asked the court to excuse her behavior, saying her academy schooling left her sheltered and unprepared for the world. Also presented an autism diagnosis (defense-hired expert) as mitigation. DA Amy Wren (Nacogdoches County) issued statement after sentencing: office 'respectfully disagrees' with sentence and 'does not believe that it reflects the seriousness of the crime committed, the position of trust the defendant occupied during the commission of these offenses, or the harm suffered by the victim and the victim's family.' NOTE: Trafficking-a-child charge is rare in this DB — typically carries far heavier sentences. 2-year outcome on trafficking + sexual assault charges is among the most lenient in the DB. DA publicly breaking with the sentence is unusual and significant. Andrews's autism-mitigation argument and 'sheltered upbringing' defense are novel mitigation strategies recorded here. Bench trial (judge-only verdict), not jury.
