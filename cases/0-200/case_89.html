Former English teacher at New Berlin West High School, New Berlin, Wisconsin. Age 28 at time of first offense. Male victim age 16 (turned 17 during incident period); was in her sophomore English class 2014-15. Relationship developed through frequent texting (1,100+ messages April 2015–January 2016). First sexual encounter July 2015 in Domres' car at park-and-ride lot on Moorland Road, New Berlin (victim age 16). Second documented encounter October 2015 at Motel 6, Brookfield (victim age 17); Domres reserved room with single bed, paid cash. Evidence: victim's phone connected to motel WiFi matching room reservation date. More than a dozen total encounters. Charges filed April 2016; pleaded guilty August 2016 to 2 counts sexual assault of a student by school staff. Sentenced December 7, 2016 by Judge Lee S. Dreyfus Jr.: Count 1: 2 years prison + 3 years extended supervision; Count 2: 2 years prison + 2 years supervision suspended in favor of 3 years probation (re-exposure to Count 2 if probation violated). Sex offender registration. Domres married her high school sweetheart August 2015, mid-offense period. NOTE: Prosecutor noted teacher-victim relationship elevates what would otherwise be misdemeanor conduct to felony. Judge stated offense went 'well beyond a mistake in judgment' and was 'to meet your needs, and not those of a child.' Victim's family submitted no impact statement and did not appear at sentencing.
