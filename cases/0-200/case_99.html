Katerina Bardos #99 sentenced

StateColoradoCourtWeld County District Court
JudgeKopcow Incident Date2013-09-01
Defendant Age23 Victim Age12
Prison Sentence7.0 yrs

Former teacher at Brentwood Middle School (2013-2014 school year), then Frontier Academy, Greeley, Colorado. DOB 09/23/1990; age 23 when relationship began. Male victim age 12 when relationship began at Brentwood; relationship turned sexual June 2014 at victim's home while family was camping (victim age 13). Bardos fostered close relationship with victim's mother and sisters, described as 'like family.' After moving to Frontier Academy in 2014, continued relationship until arrest: 40-50 sexual encounters over course of a year at victim's home, Bardos' apartment, her car throughout Greeley (including Frontier Academy parking lot), and her parents' home. Also provided victim with marijuana. Arrested May 6, 2015 initially on contributing to delinquency of a minor (marijuana); sexual relationship discovered during investigation. Initially denied sexual relationship but admitted close bond, Xbox, homework, overnight stays. Charged with sexual assault on a child, sexual assault on a child by person in position of trust, contributing to delinquency of a minor. Pleaded guilty March 3, 2016 to Felony Child Abuse (F3) + Criminal Attempt to Commit Sexual Assault on a Child (F5); two counts sexual assault by person in position of trust dropped. Sentenced May 3, 2016 by Judge Kopcow: 7 years DOC + 5 years mandatory parole; sentencing range was 4-20 years; 2 days time served. Paroled September 11, 2018 (~2 years 5 months served). Prosecutor Chief Deputy DA Thea Carrasco. NOTE: Unusual plea — 'child abuse negligently causing death' used instrumentally to achieve appropriate sentencing range; nobody died. Relationship began at school then continued after Bardos moved schools. Early release after serving roughly one-third of sentence.
