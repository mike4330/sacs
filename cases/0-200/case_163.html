Denise Keesee #163 sentenced

State Oregon
Court Washington County Circuit Court
Incident Date 2012-01-01
Defendant Age 37
Victim Age 16
Prison Sentence 1.25 yrs

Former teacher at Sherwood High School, Sherwood School District, Sherwood OR. Age approximately 37 at start of offenses (2012); 38 at arrest; 39 at initial sentencing; 40 at probation revocation sentencing. Sexual contact with at least two male students (age 16 primary victim) including intercourse, occurring 2012–2013. Discovered during school misconduct investigation. Husband is a Sherwood Police Department officer who faced separate accusations of intimidation and cover-up related to the case; cleared in investigation.

Turned herself in June 17, 2013 following indictment. Pleaded guilty February 10, 2014 to two counts of second-degree sexual abuse (reduced from original six counts across two students). Initial sentence April 21, 2014: 30 days in jail, 5 years probation, sex offender registration. Arrested October 2014 for probation violation (failed to disclose her presence in an area where minors gather, per sex offender conditions). Probation revoked and sentenced October 24, 2014 to 14 months in prison.

Civil lawsuit filed by victim and family against Denise Keesee and Sherwood School District for $5.1–5.5 million, alleging failure to protect; case moved to federal court in 2015. Oregon TSPC (Teacher Standards and Practices Commission) final order also issued.
