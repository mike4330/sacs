Former seventh-grade math teacher at Miamisburg Middle School, Miamisburg, Ohio. Age 31 at time of offense. Male victim aged 14, an eighth-grader. Hired by Miamisburg City School District in 2008.

Beginning in early 2016, administrators documented a pattern of concerning behavior: male students eating lunch in Langford's locked classroom, Langford sitting on a student's lap, allowing inappropriate and sexual comments from students, and playing basketball with male students. A February 2016 memo outlined nine concerns; Langford was counseled to maintain professional distance and refrain from physical contact with students. In April 2017, an assistant principal again addressed appropriate boundaries after observing non-curriculum activities. In late May 2017, the assistant superintendent discussed transferring Langford to an elementary school; she resisted and raised the possibility of resignation.

On May 23, 2017, Langford was confronted with allegations of sexual contact with the 14-year-old victim in her locked, darkened classroom before the last day of school. Placed on administrative leave and resigned that day. Criminal complaint filed May 25. Police searched her home May 27, seizing underwear, a computer tower, an iPhone, and other items. Other students reported she gave them gifts and showed them an erotic photograph of herself and her husband having sex.

Indicted November 14, 2017 on three counts of sexual battery and three counts of unlawful sexual conduct with a minor. Jury trial April 10–13, 2018; victim testified to a 20–30 minute sexual encounter in Langford's locked classroom. Convicted on all six counts. Sentenced May 29, 2018 by Judge Timothy O'Connell: one year in prison, five years probation, Tier III sex offender designation, 32 restitution to victim. Defense sought community control with electronic monitoring; prosecution sought maximum incarceration, citing egregious nature of offense, age of victim, and lack of remorse.

NOTE: Multiple prior-warning-ignored instances documented in writing by administrators from January 2016 through April 2017 — roughly 16 months of red flags before the offense. The erotic photo shown to multiple students is a distinct grooming vector. One year on six felony counts is well below the prosecution's request and notable relative to the Tier III designation.
