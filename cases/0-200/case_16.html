State v. Kathryn Murray #16 sentenced

StateTexasCourtHarris County District Court
Incident Date2012-02-07
Defendant Age30 Victim Age15
Prison Sentence2.0 yrs

7th and 8th grade teacher at Memorial Middle School, Spring Branch ISD, Houston, TX. Age ~29–30 at time of offense; 31 at resentencing January 2014. Taught victim while also serving as his tutor.

Murray had multiple sexual encounters with a 15-year-old male student she was tutoring, including at the boy's home when his parents were not present. The relationship was discovered when the victim's brother informed the parents. Murray was arrested in January 2012 on charges of sexual assault of a child and released on $50,000 bail.

While on bond, Murray and the victim were found together at Murray's father's house in February 2013 — demonstrating that the criminal charges were insufficient to end contact. Bail was revoked; Murray remained in custody from January 2013 until sentencing. The victim's parents, faced with Murray's inability to stay away from their son, sent him to a school in Louisiana to physically separate them.

Pleaded guilty December 2013 to one count of improper relationship with a student and one count of sexual assault of a child (note: earlier charge records reflect two counts of sexual assault; plea charge composition may differ from original indictment). Initially sentenced January 21, 2014 to two years deferred adjudication and one year in jail; however, the court vacated this sentence because Texas law prohibits probation for sexual assault of a child. Resentenced January 31, 2014 by agreement of both parties: 2 years in prison, no probation, no contact with victim for at least two years, surrender of teaching license, lifetime sex offender registration.

Victim's father Alex Pallais: "She's a blonde pretty lady who abused a kid. A woman gets much lighter sentences, where the victim has the same reaction. They get depressed. They become very suicidal. So, why is it that a boy is different than a girl?"

NOTE: Original sentence was legally defective (probation not available for sexual assault of a child in Texas); resentencing yielded 2 years prison. With approximately 1 year of pre-sentence custody credit, Murray likely served ~1 additional year post-resentencing. Persistent contact through bond violation forced family to relocate victim out of state. sentence_years updated to 2.0 to reflect final imposed sentence.
