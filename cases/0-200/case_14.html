Former 6th-grade teacher at Las Brisas Academy, Goodyear, Arizona (Maricopa County). Age 26 at time of offense; 27 at sentencing (June 2019). Married.

Victim: 13-year-old male student. Zamora initially contacted the student via text messages over a school break in December 2017; a sexual relationship began shortly after. At least one encounter occurred on school grounds while another minor was present in the room. Zamora also texted sexually explicit content and material harmful to a minor.

Discovered March 21, 2018, when the victim's parents found evidence of the relationship on their son's phone via a parental monitoring app and alerted school staff, who contacted police. Zamora subsequently called the victim's family to try to convince them not to report her.

Originally charged with nine counts of sexual conduct with a minor, two counts of molestation of a child, and one count of furnishing harmful material to a minor. In April 2019, most charges were dismissed following a grand jury indictment and the case was narrowed. Zamora pleaded guilty to one count of sexual conduct with a minor, one count of attempted molestation of a child, and one count of public sexual indecency.

Sentenced June 10, 2019 to 20 years in the Arizona Department of Corrections. Maricopa County Attorney Bill Montgomery: "Today's sentencing is fitting for an individual who exploited her position as a teacher to groom and then abuse a young teen student."

NOTE: sentence_years = 20. Contact with another minor present in the room during on-campus encounter is the factual basis for the public sexual indecency charge. Defendant's attempt to persuade the family not to report constitutes obstruction. Monitoring-app discovery is a recurring pattern in this DB.
