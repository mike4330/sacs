Court Jefferson County Circuit Court
Incident Date 2013-03-01

Prison Sentence 12.0 yrs

Former teacher at Highland Middle School, Louisville, Kentucky (Jefferson County Public Schools). Hired full-time in October 2012; previously a substitute for less than a year. Age 24 at time of offense.

Victim was a 13-year-old male student she taught. The two had a six-week sexual relationship in spring 2013, meeting after school at her house, during spring break, and at Buechel Park. She was arrested April 12, 2013 when police were called to Buechel Park and found her having sex with the boy in her car. The boy told investigators Boyers was his girlfriend and she replied, 'I guess you could call it that.' The victim had saved a used condom; Boyers' DNA was found on the boy. Civil lawsuit filed by the family also alleged the two messaged throughout the school day and that teachers or administrators at Highland Middle School knew or should have known about the relationship.

Prosecuted in two counties: Jefferson County (Indictment 13-CR-1175) and Oldham County (Indictment 13-CR-00095). Pleaded guilty in both. Jefferson convictions (May 11, 2015): rape 2nd degree (10 yrs), sodomy 2nd degree (10 yrs), sexual abuse 1st degree (5 yrs). Oldham convictions (May 14, 2015): rape 2nd degree (10 yrs), sexual abuse 1st degree (2 yrs). Sentenced to 12 years total in Jefferson County on May 8, 2015; lifetime sex offender registration.

Released to parole supervision November 7, 2017; parole ends approximately May 5, 2027.

NOTE: Civil lawsuit filed by victim's family against Boyers and JCPS, alleging negligent training and supervision and that school personnel knew or should have known. Boyers initially denied intercourse; ultimately admitted to the relationship at sentencing. Parole record lists aliases: Jay Boyers, Jessica Cochran, Jessica Young (likely married names post-offense).
