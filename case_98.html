Former physical-education teacher at Herrera Elementary School, Phoenix, Arizona; 13-year teacher at the school. Female victim age 14. Sexual conduct with student between April and October 2006 — victim was transitioning from middle to high school. Victim kept incident secret for more than six years, reporting to police November 2012; Wooten arrested February 2013. Victim testified at sentencing that Wooten 'robbed my childhood away'; described Wooten as a former role model. Prosecutor Ryan Powell: Wooten turned the vulnerable transition period 'into a way for her to get her own sexual gratification from a child.' Pleaded guilty in 2014. Sentenced January 6, 2015 by Judge Sherry Stephens: 20 years prison + lifetime probation; sentencing occurred almost nine years after the offense. Defense argued for mitigated sentence based on acceptance of responsibility, higher education accomplishments, lack of criminal history, abusive childhood. Wooten did not speak at sentencing; submitted written letter to court. NOTE: Female victim; elementary school PE teacher. Six-plus-year delayed disclosure is among the longer in the DB.
