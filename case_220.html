Katie Rufener #220 sentenced

State Kansas
Court Finney County District Court
Judge Robert Frederick
Incident Date 2015-03-19
Defendant Age 26
Victim Age 18
Prison Sentence 0.0 yrs

Former science teacher, head cheerleading coach, and assistant dance team coach at Holcomb High School, Holcomb, Finney County, Kansas. Female defendant, age 26 at arrest. One male student victim, age 18.

Three incidents of sexual intercourse at Rufener's Garden City, Kansas residence: March 19, March 20, and April 10, 2015. Parents reported the relationship to the Finney County Sheriff's Office on June 18, 2015, nearly a month after the school year ended. Rufener had resigned from her teaching position in March 2015 and from her coaching positions in April 2015, but remained employed through the end of the school year. At the time charges were filed (October 2015), she had taken a position as a biology teacher at Southern Local Schools in Ohio and was placed on paid administrative leave pending resolution.

Originally charged with 3 felony counts of Unlawful Sexual Relations with a Student (K.S.A.) in Finney County District Court, each carrying 31–102 months potential. Rufener voluntarily returned to Garden City and was released on a recognizance bond.

Pleaded no contest to 3 reduced counts of Simple Battery (misdemeanor). Found guilty by District Judge Robert Frederick. Sentenced to 180 days jail per count, all suspended; 1 year non-reporting probation. Conditions: obey all laws, no contact with victim. No sex offender registration required.

NOTE: Charge reduction from felony Unlawful Sexual Relations to misdemeanor Simple Battery is dramatic — the original statute specifically covers consensual relations between a teacher and any student 16 or older. County Attorney Susan Richmeier had noted at filing that a supervised probation outcome was possible given Rufener's criminal history. Adult-victim case (18-year-old); no sex offender registration imposed. Victim's parents, not the victim, reported the relationship.
